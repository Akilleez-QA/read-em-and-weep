## What this slice does not absorb

The separate trace-corrected-square construction, equivariant splitting, weight-difference complete-intersection proof, weighted commutator/selection theorem, weighted Koszul exact sequence and sharp low-order ideal-membership result retain their own proofs and dependencies. This note assumes global σ and τ only when applying its general weight theorem; it does not claim to reproduce those constructions. The full-target collision theorem additionally requires its vanishing bounds and actual closed high-order representative. No whole weighted-selection or weighted-Koszul manuscript is superseded by this slice.
