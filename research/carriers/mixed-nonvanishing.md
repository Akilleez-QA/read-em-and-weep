# Nonvanishing of the mixed Atiyah class for the ribbon monad

## Conclusion and scope

For the explicit Hamiltonian ribbon monad, the Euler image of the mixed class is nonzero. More strongly, its four component classes `u beta_j` are linearly independent in `H2(P,End(F)(-2,-1))`. The same proof applies to any symplectic relative monad with the stated terms and a basepoint-free pair of cubic restrictions along its ruling lines. Application to a particular original Lange bundle still requires a proved identification with that monad class.

The proof uses the tracefree symmetric-square model to remove the possible higher-differential issue completely. It also checks the local Atiyah representative and the nonzero cubic-Jacobian map, including transition corrections.

## 1. A three-term model for the tracefree endomorphisms

Use the globally symplectic monad

`M=[A -> B -> C]`, in degrees `-1,0,1`,

`A=O(1,-2)`, `C=A*=O(-1,2)`,

`0 -> U=O(-2,1)^2 -> B -> W=U*=O(2,-1)^2 -> 0`.

The given normalized transition matrices preserve the alternating form on `B`, and `beta=(-h^T,a^T)` is dual to `alpha=(a,h)` for that form. Thus the cohomology `F` is symplectic of rank two. In characteristic zero,

`End(F)=O + End_0(F)`, and `End_0(F) is isomorphic to Sym^2(F)`.

The derived symmetric square of the locally free complex `M` computes `Sym^2(F)`. Because `A` and `C` occur in odd degrees and have rank one, its terms in degrees `-2` and `2` are zero. The only terms are

- degree `-1`: `A tensor B`,
- degree `0`: `Sym^2(B) + A tensor C`,
- degree `1`: `B tensor C`.

These are the graded-symmetric, rather than ungraded-symmetric, powers. This distinction is essential.

Twist by `O(0,-1)` and push along `p:P1 x P3 -> P1`. The only nonzero relative cohomology terms are

- `R3 p_*(A tensor B(-1)) = O(3)^2` in complex degree `-1`,
- `p_*(Sym^2(B)(-1) + A tensor C(-1)) = O(-4)^12` in degree `0`,
- `N=p_*(B tensor C(-1))` in degree `1`, where
  `0 -> O(-3)^20 -> N -> O(1)^2 -> 0`.

For example, the three graded pieces of `Sym^2(B)(-1)` have P3 degrees `1,-1,-3`; only the first contributes. The pieces of `A tensor B(-1)` have degrees `-2,-4`; only the latter contributes, in degree 3. All of these vanishings are ordinary projective-space line-bundle cohomology.

The q=0 differential is the inclusion

`m:O(-4)^12 -> O(-3)^20 subset N`,

represented fiberwise by a symmetric matrix of linear forms `L` sent to `L a`. It is injective: independent linear forms `a1,a2` have no nonzero symmetric linear matrix satisfying `L a=0`. Therefore, writing `R=coker(m)`,

`R1 p_* End(F)(0,-1)=R`,

`R2 p_* End(F)(0,-1)=O(3)^2`,

and `R0 p_* End(F)(0,-1)=0`.

There are no possible higher differentials: the only E1 positions are `(0,0)`, `(1,0)`, and `(-1,3)`. The scalar endomorphism summand contributes nothing because `O_P3(-1)` is acyclic. In particular `R` is the actual relative cohomology sheaf, not merely a subquotient of an E2 page.

## 2. The kernel bundle K

Put `K=coker[O(-4)^12 -> O(-3)^20]`. Then

`0 -> K -> R -> O(1)^2 -> 0`.

Here is a direct check of its useful filtration. The sheaf-level lower map is

`O(-4,1)^3 -> O(-3,2)^2`,

`(l11,l12,l22) -> (l11 a1+l12 a2, l12 a1+l22 a2)`.

Its kernel is generated by `(a2^2,-a1 a2,a1^2)` and is `O(-6,-1)`, which is relatively acyclic. Let T be its cokernel, supported on the first infinitesimal neighborhood of `Z=(a1=a2=0)`. In T the relations are

`a1 e1=0`, `a2 e1+a1 e2=0`, `a2 e2=0`.

The kernel of restriction to Z is generated by `a2 e1=-a1 e2`, is annihilated by the ideal of Z, and is `O_P(-4,1)|Z`. Hence

`0 -> O_P(-4,1)|Z -> T -> O_P(-3,2)|Z^2 -> 0`.

In intrinsic `Z=P1_base x P1_ruling` coordinates, `O_P(a,b)|Z=O_Z(a+b,b)`. Pushing this sequence gives exactly

`0 -> O(-3)^2 -> K -> O(-1)^6 -> 0`.

Thus `H0(K)=H0(K(-1))=0`.

## 3. The four Atiyah classes and the cubic Jacobian

Push the relative Atiyah class through `Omega^1_P3 -> O(-1)^4`, and call its four components `beta_j`. Over a base chart the monad is a genuine split-line monad on P3. The homogeneous-coordinate connection therefore represents the pushed Atiyah class by the derivatives of its differential. In the symplectic endomorphism model, this is the pair `(partial_j a,partial_j h)`, up to one uniform nonzero sign or normalization. The quotient `R -> O(1)^2` takes the `partial_j a` component.

For `a=(t x0-s x2,t x1-s x3)`, the four quotient sections are the columns of

`[ [t,0,-s,0], [0,t,0,-s] ]`.

They form a basis of `H0(O(1)^2)`. Since `H0(K)=0`, the classes `beta_j` form a basis of `H0(R)`, which has dimension four.

The kernel of this derivative-evaluation map `O^4 -> O(1)^2` is `O(-1)^2`, with columns

`(s,0,t,0)^T`, `(0,s,0,t)^T`.

These are tangent directions to the ruling line. Notice that this is the dual Euler sequence to the sequence of linear equations vanishing on Z; the two injections should not be confused.

Call the induced kernel map `gamma:O(-1)^2 -> K`. Its composite with `K -> O(-1)^6` is the cubic Jacobian along the ruling line. For the explicit chart `s=1`, the two kernel directions are `(1,0,z,0)` and `(0,1,0,z)`. They annihilate `a` exactly. With `h=(-x0^3,-x1^3)`, their `h` derivatives restrict to

`(-3 p^2,0)`, `(0,-3 q^2)`

on `Z_z`, parametrized by `(p,q,zp,zq)`. Hence the resulting constant map `C^2 -> C^6` is injective.

This calculation includes the necessary gluing correction. Changing a local monad trivialization by T changes its derivative representative by a commutator boundary `[partial_j T T^{-1},d]`. In the displayed symplectic transitions, the x-dependent part is the symmetric lower-left block. Such a boundary contributes `L a` in the lower symmetric model, which vanishes after restriction to Z. Thus the cubic-Jacobian map is an invariant of the actual Atiyah class, not an artifact of a local splitting. Equivalently, the calculation takes place in the quotient K already modded out by all those boundaries.

For a general fixed basepoint-free cubic pair `(f,g)`, the same map is

`w -> (partial_w f,partial_w g)`.

It is injective: if a nonzero constant direction annihilates both homogeneous binary cubics in characteristic zero, both are multiples of the cube of the complementary linear coordinate, giving a common zero. This proves the same conclusion for that monad class.

## 4. Cup product with u and actual cohomology

The evaluation diagram from

`0 -> O(-1)^2 -> O^4 -> O(1)^2 -> 0`

to

`0 -> K -> R -> O(1)^2 -> 0`

has left arrow gamma. Twist by `O(-1)`. The first sequence has connecting isomorphism `C^2 -> H1(O(-2)^2)`. After mapping further to `H1(O(-2)^6)`, naturality identifies the second connecting map with the injective cubic Jacobian. Thus `H0(R(-1))=0`.

The base Euler sequence tensored by R is

`0 -> R(-2) -> R(-1)^2 -> R -> 0`.

Its connecting map is cup product with `u=c1O_P1(1)`, up to the chosen nonzero normalization. Since `H0(R(-1))=0`,

`u:H0(R) -> H1(R(-2))`

is injective. All four `u beta_j` are therefore linearly independent. The base has dimension one, so Leray injects `H1(R(-2))` into the actual group `H2(P,End(F)(-2,-1))`. No differential or extension ambiguity can kill these classes.

As a dimension check, `rank R=10`, `deg R=-10`, and `H0(R(-2))=0`, giving `h1(R(-2))=20`. The remaining Leray contribution is `H0(O(1)^2)` of dimension four. Thus

`h2(P,End(F)(-2,-1))=24`.

Finally the Euler image of `Delta=A1(F) v-u A3(F)` is `-u(beta_0,...,beta_3)`, because the Euler image of v is zero. It is nonzero, so Delta is nonzero. Combined with [the rational reduction](mixed-reduction.md), the three actual mixed Atiyah evaluations on the symmetric elliptic pullback are all nonzero and linearly independent.


## Exact certificate and weak-kernel consequence

For the explicit k=0 seed of [PR23](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/pull/23), the independent [split-complex certificate](finite-ribbon-complex/README.md) constructs the actual four cocycles and a rational matrix W with Wd1=0 and WB=I4. Its boundary matrix has rank132; adjoining the four cocycles gives rank136. The only possible incoming higher differential has zero source. The executable certificate therefore agrees with the geometric proof in actual cohomology. The geometric argument above applies to the stated whole monad family, while this numerical certificate is for k=0.

For the degree-96 elliptic-product pullback E, the three mixed directions have distinct deck characters and are permuted by S3. Nonvanishing of one therefore gives three independent nonzero classes. Using the established 13+3 decomposition in [PR21](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/pull/21), the HH2 evaluation has rank15 and kernel dimension13, whereas the character annihilator has dimension16. Thus this seed fails the weak first-order kernel equality. This is not a nonexistence theorem for all rank-two carriers, nor a calculation of the HM ambient second-order obstruction. Application to every original Lange bundle requires a separate relative-monad identification.
