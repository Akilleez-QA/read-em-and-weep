# Conditional τ=2, U210, c=2 exclusion

Work on X=P¹×P³ over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, and write O(m,n) for its product line bundles. The following is an exclusion only under the explicit hypotheses below.

## Statement and dependencies

Assume the normalized block

    E=O(-5,1)⊕O(-3,1), T=O(-2,2)⊕O(-1,1),
    W=O(2,0)⊕O(-2,0), L=[[ell,h],[0,a]],

where ell is primitive, a is a nonzero quadratic base section, Δ=V(a) is the full length-two Cartier divisor, and ell,h are independent on every reduced exceptional fiber. Assume the actual primitive presentation

    0→O(-3) --ell→ O⁴→U→0, U≅O(2)⊕O(1)⊕O.

The central obstruction and exceptional rank input are the conditional criterion in [PR #54](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/pull/54), pinned to [commit 220853ab400944402396e210e7475a73901621e1, §§3–5](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/220853ab400944402396e210e7475a73901621e1/research/carriers/tau2-central-artin-gates/central-artin-gates.md). In particular, retain both first-stage torsion coordinates in each weighted column, the half-corrected central class, and the full-Artin exceptional square gate q±=−κ± h̄² on Δ. Here q=aQ_C−hQ_D modulo ell, and h̄ is the image of the actual ambient h in U(1). The square gate follows from Q_D being in the full-Artin span of ell,h, as proved in that input; it is not just a reduced-fiber condition.

**Additional direction hypotheses, explicitly assumed here:** in the splitting selected in §1, h_Z|Δ=0 and H_Y|Δ is a unit in O_Δ, where h̄=H_XX+H_YY+h_ZZ. Equivalently h̄ lies in the intrinsic positive subbundle U′(1) over Δ and its projection to the O(1)-quotient of U′ is a unit there. This note does not prove these hypotheses necessary for all U210,c2 data. They are not supplied by PR #54 alone.

**Conditional theorem.** Under these hypotheses there is no first-stage pair having both vanishing corrected central obstruction and the exceptional rank-two condition. The exclusion is uniform in the actual primitive presentation and every marked degree-two divisor, reduced or double. It uses no conductor alignment, monomial presentation, or τ=1 substitution. In particular it is not an unconditional exclusion of U210,c2, a classification of U210, or a classification of τ=2 families.

## 1. The actual presentation and the global Z gauge

For 0→O(-3)→O⁴→U→0, the canonical quotient U→O induces a nonzero constant map O⁴→O. Its constant kernel S is O³ and it admits a constant section. The image of that section in U selects a splitting U=U′⊕OZ with U′=O(2)⊕O(1), and Z has a global ambient lift. The actual sequence is therefore the direct sum of

    0→O(-3)→S=O³→U′→0

and O→O. This does not claim that an arbitrary automorphism of U lifts to a fixed O⁴ presentation. It chooses the quotient splitting using an actual ambient section. The positive rank-two subbundle U′ is intrinsic, so the condition h̄∈U′(1) survives this choice.

Since h_Z∈H⁰(O(1)) vanishes on all of the length-two divisor, h_Z=0 globally. Thus ell and h are Z-free in actual ambient coordinates. Choose normalized cocycles

    D_X=d₁/t+d₂/t²+d₃/t³+d₄/t⁴,
    D_Y=e₁/t+e₂/t²+e₃/t³, D_Z=0.

The connecting homomorphism obtained by twisting the actual rank-three sequence by O(-1) is an isomorphism H⁰(U′(-1))→H¹(O(-4)). In the bases (X,tX,Y) and (t⁻¹,t⁻²,t⁻³), its matrix is

    M = [[d₁,d₂,e₁],[d₂,d₃,e₂],[d₃,d₄,e₃]], det M≠0.

This proves the invertibility universally. Testing cusp and node representatives is an additional check, not its justification.

Write h̄=H_XX+H_YY, with degrees at most three and two. The actual ambient h on the finite chart is h=h̄+ell k₀. Its global lift condition and this k₀ are retained. Neither H_X nor H_Y is promoted to a freely chosen global quotient section. The proof only uses the supplied unit condition on H_Y.

## 2. Exact Z grading and the rank-forced scalar

A global q+∈H⁰(Sym²U(-2)) is

    q+=g₂X²+g₁XY+bY²+kXZ,
    deg g₂≤2, deg g₁≤1, b,k constants.

Because h̄ has no Z term, the full-Artin square gate makes k vanish on Δ. A nonzero constant cannot do so. Hence q+ is Z-free. The sheaf K=coker L is graded by actual Z-degree, and its entire vertical torsion is generated by (0,ell), of Z-degree zero. Every actual plus class with this q+ can consequently be represented by Z-free local columns and transitions without deleting either torsion coefficient.

The Z² coefficient of q− lies in H⁰(O(2)) and vanishes on Δ. It equals A a for a constant A. Its actual K-class is represented by (A Z²,0), globally. No L-gauge can alter its Z² coefficient: both ell and h are Z-free, whereas each gauge entry has relative degree one.

The actual fixed vertex Γ=P(OZ) has ell=h=0. At Γ the plus C-entry is zero and the minus C-entry is A. The necessary exceptional condition that the two C-row quadrics have no common zero on Λ therefore gives A≠0. This uses only geometric exceptional rank and works equally when Δ is nonreduced.

The central presentation is

    (r,d) modulo (ell s+h²k, a k),
    r∈O(-7,3), d∈O(-7,1),
    s∈O(-10,2), k∈O(-9,1).

Its Z² component has no d or k term and is exactly

    (O(-7)³ / ell O(-10))Z² = U′(-7)Z².

Thus it is an honest direct graded summand with H¹ dimension 4+5=9. The h²k relation cannot enter it, because k has relative degree one. Neither central torsion nor other Z-degrees can identify this component with zero.

## 3. Independent derivation of the corrected component

Choose infinity outside Δ, so a=a₂t²+a₁t+a₀ with a₂≠0. Use U₀=A¹ and U₁=P¹\(Δ∪{0}). These cover the base, U₁ contains infinity, and a is invertible on U₁. Restrict the ordinary infinity lifts to U₁. All formulas below are in common diagonal frames.

Put q=q+. Choose any polynomial representative κ of the unique Artin coefficient with q=−κh̄² modulo a. The full first-stage torsion is λ∈O_Δ(-6), represented by λ₀+λ₁t. Then legitimate local representatives are

    Q+,0=((q+κh̄²)/a, κh̄+ell λ)ᵀ,
    Q+,1=(q(X+ell D_X,Y+ell D_Y)/a,0)ᵀ,
    Q−,0=Q−,1=(A Z²,0)ᵀ

for the isolated Z² minus part. The first quotient is polynomial on U₀ by the full-Artin identity. No global degree bound on a local finite-chart coefficient is imposed.

Let D=D_X∂_X+D_Y∂_Y. Exact quadratic expansion yields the first-stage transition Q₁−Q₀=LB with

    B+,1=(Dq+(ell/2)D²q+κk₀h̄+h̄λ+ell k₀λ)/a,
    B+,2=−(κh̄+ell λ)/a,
    B−=0.

The local isotropy solutions for this component are

    R₀=A Z² [[0,0],[λ−κk₀,κ]], R₁=0.

The standalone script checks these equations directly before projecting to the final formula. For J=[[0,1],[-1,0]], V=JBᵀ, form the actual corrected cocycle

    C=R₁−R₀−Q₀V−(1/2)LVᵀJV.

It lies in ker(R↦RLᵀ−LRᵀ). Subtract the symmetric gauge with entries S₁₂=C₂₁/a and S₂₂=C₂₂/a; the bottom row and upper-right entry vanish. Reduce its remaining upper-left Z² coefficient modulo ell. The result is exactly

    η=A(Dq+2h̄λ)/a ∈ H¹(U′(-7)).

Both κ and the actual lift coefficient k₀ cancel. The sign and the coefficient 2 have been independently checked from the unprojected equations.

This computation respects the original weights. In particular B+ has weights (-7,1),(-5,1), B− has (-3,1),(-1,1), and V has corresponding rows (-3,1),(-1,1) and (-7,1),(-5,1). The central matrix weights are (-7,3),(-5,3),(-6,2),(-4,2). No c=0 transition spaces are used.

For the full pair, retain arbitrary lower-Z minus C and D entries and arbitrary minus transition linear forms in both transition entries. Their contribution to QV has Z-degree at most one except for the displayed A Z² term. Since the plus transition is Z-free, the full VᵀJV also has Z-degree at most one. Therefore the full half correction has no Z² contribution. Local lower-Z isotropy solutions likewise remain lower-Z; any extra homogeneous local Z² syzygy only changes η by a coboundary. A second symbolic full-pair calculation verifies these degree statements without setting minus torsion to zero.

## 4. Five decisive coordinates, including all two torsion coordinates

The Y coefficient is

    η_Y/A=((g₁,₀+g₁,₁t)D_X+2bD_Y+2H_Yλ)/a ∈ H¹(O(-6)).

Multiply by a through the genuine cohomology map H¹(O(-6))→H¹(O(-4)). The polynomial 2H_Yλ is a U₀-coboundary. The three surviving Laurent coefficients are exactly

    M (g₁,₀, g₁,₁, 2b)ᵀ.

Here the vector has three scalar entries. The symbols g₁,₀ and g₁,₁ are the constant and linear coefficients of g₁, not a five-entry vector with inserted zeroes and ones. Thus central vanishing and det M≠0 force g₁=b=0.

The remaining class is the boundary of 2H_Yλ in

    0→O(-6) --a→ O(-4)→O_Δ(-4)→0.

The boundary is injective because H⁰(O(-4))=0. Multiplication by the unit H_Y is an automorphism of the entire length-two Artin module. Hence λ=0, including a possible pure first-order nilpotent coefficient at a double point.

For an additional universal coefficient check, represent H¹(O(-6)) by the t⁻¹,…,t⁻⁵ coefficients at infinity. Let Y be the five-by-five matrix of η_Y/A on (g₁,₀,g₁,₁,b,λ₀,λ₁). Multiplication by a is

    P=[[a₀,a₁,a₂,0,0],[0,a₀,a₁,a₂,0],[0,0,a₀,a₁,a₂]].

Append the first two coordinate rows to P to obtain T. Exact calculation gives det T=a₂³ and

    P Y=[M diag(1,1,2) | 0].

The lower-right two-by-two block of TY is the first two principal-parts rows for 2H_Yλ/a, with determinant −4 Res(a,H_Y)/a₂⁴. Consequently

    det Y=−8 det(M) Res(a,H_Y)/a₂⁷ ≠ 0.

This independently certifies all five decisive coordinates at once. No factor involving the discriminant of a is required. Reduced and double divisors are handled by the same expression.

The cohomology quotient at infinity has been accounted for: on U₀ any polynomial is a coboundary, while an O(-6) section regular at infinity has expansion beginning at t⁻⁶. Allowing poles at the removed finite points on U₁ does not alter that bound. Thus the five displayed negative coefficients are the correct quotient coordinates, not an arbitrary truncation of a rational function.

## 5. Final full-Artin rank contradiction and stratum completeness

With g₁=b=0, the square gate becomes g₂X²=−κ(H_XX+H_YY)² on Δ. Its Y² coefficient is κH_Y²=0. Since H_Y is a unit, κ=0 in O_Δ. Therefore the actual plus D-row

    Q_D,+=κh̄+ell λ

vanishes on all of Δ. Equivalently, in the actual (ell,h) basis its coefficients are (λ−κk₀,κ), both zero. The necessary exceptional coefficient matrix cannot be invertible, even after reduction to one exceptional geometric fiber.

No division by κ, λ, b, g₁ or g₂ occurs. Thus zero, unit, one-component-supported and pure-nilpotent κ/λ strata are all covered. No condition on the minus torsion, the rest of q−, or the ordinary rank gate can repair this contradiction. The divisor may be any marked reduced pair or any marked double point, including points originally at infinity, because the proof only selects a chart containing its full support and transports the actual presentation.

## 6. Reproducible checks and scope

The standalone `audit_tau_two_u210_c2_exclusion.py` imports no earlier tensor or proof checker. It checks the raw first transition; both local isotropy identities; the corrected cocycle and symmetric elimination; all c=2 bidegrees; the arbitrary-minus Z² projection; the actual three-row coefficient matrix; the full five-by-two Artin boundary; and the block factorization of the universal five-by-five decisive matrix. Cusp/node and marked reduced/double examples are stress tests only. The geometric hypotheses and connecting-map argument are justified in the proof, not certified by finitely many samples.

See `README.md` for reproduction. `audit-tau-two-u210-c2-output.txt` records the checker output, and `audit-tau-two-u210-c2-exact-data.json` contains the small exact matrices generated by it.

The conclusion is conditional on the normalization, full-Artin gate, central criterion, and explicitly assumed direction hypotheses. Nothing here settles U210,c0,c1, the U111 cases, other directions in U210,c2, existence, stability, moduli, or any mixed-Atiyah obstruction. The proof contributes one bounded exclusion to [issue #52](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/issues/52); it does not close that issue.
