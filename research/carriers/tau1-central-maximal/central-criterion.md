# Central gluing and isotropy in the balanced τ=1, c=0 model

## 1. Conventions, twists, and local regular sequences

Let X=P¹×P³ over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, or interpret all rank tests geometrically after extending the ground field. Write

    E=O(-6,1)⊕O(-2,1),
    T=O(-2,2)⊕O(-1,1),
    W=O²,
    L=[[ell,h],[0,a]],
    J=[[0,1],[-1,0]].

Here ell, h, a have bidegrees (4,1), (0,1), (1,0). The section a has its simple zero at p₀. Primitivity of ell makes Y={ell=0} a P²-bundle. Independence of ell₀ and h makes Λ={a=ell=h=0} a line. The relevant regular sequences hold in every stalk meeting their common zero loci; outside those loci a generator is a unit and the corresponding Koszul complex is contractible.

For

    R=[[r,u],[v,w]] ∈ Hom(E*,T)=T⊗E,

the respective entry bundles are

    r: O(-8,3),  u: O(-4,3),
    v: O(-7,2),  w: O(-3,2).

The alternating part of R Lᵀ−L Rᵀ is determined by

    d(R)=a u−ell v−h w ∈ det T=O(-3,3).

Consequently the image sheaf of d is exactly I_Λ det T. It is not the full determinant line along Λ.

## 2. Primary vanishing is the complete local solvability condition

Set f_i=(Q_i J Q_iᵀ)₁₂ and M=ker d. Local isotropy is d(R_i)=f_i. Necessity is scheme-theoretic vanishing of f_i on Λ.

The syzygies of (a,−ell,−h) have the explicit parametrization

    u=ell b+h c,
    v=a b+h d₀,
    w=a c−ell d₀,

with ambiguity

    (b,c,d₀) ↦ (b+h k,c−ell k,d₀−a k).

The parameter bundles are

    b: O(-8,2), c: O(-4,2), d₀: O(-7,1), k: O(-8,1).

Thus M has the exact resolution

    0 → O(-8,1)
      → O(-8,3)⊕O(-8,2)⊕O(-4,2)⊕O(-7,1)
      → M → 0,

where k maps to (0,h k,−ell k,−a k). Every bundle in this resolution has nonnegative relative degree. On U×P³ for any affine base chart U, these bundles have no positive cohomology. The resolution gives Hʲ(U×P³,M)=0 for every j>0.

Applying this to

    0 → M → E⊗T → I_Λ det T → 0

shows that every f_i vanishing scheme-theoretically on Λ has a solution R_i over the entire affine-base chart, not merely stalkwise. This is the precise justification of local sufficiency.

On Λ, L is zero. Therefore replacing a first-stage lift Q_i by Q_i+L A_i leaves f_i|Λ unchanged. The first-stage vertical torsion changes Q_D by a multiple of ell and has the same property. The primary restriction lies in H⁰(Λ,O_Λ(3)), of dimension four. No independence of its four quadratic coordinate equations is asserted.

## 3. The quotient N and the doubled scheme

The map Sym²E→M, S↦LS, is injective as a sheaf map. Indeed L is invertible over the function field, and Sym²E is locally free on integral X. Put N=M/(L Sym²E).

Subtracting L[[0,b],[b,c]] from the syzygy matrix leaves

    [[r−h b,0],[h d₀,−ell d₀]].

Hence N is generated by pairs (r',d₀), of degrees (-8,3) and (-7,1). The exact relations are

    (r',d₀)=(ell s+h²k,a k),

with s of degree (-12,2), k of degree (-8,1). One can also check the absence of additional relations directly: if

    [[r',0],[h d₀,−ell d₀]]=L[[s,b],[b,c]],

then a b=h d₀. Since h is a nonzerodivisor modulo a, d₀=a k; cancellation gives b=h k and c=−ell k, followed by r'=ell s+h²k. The calculation applies where a is a unit as well.

Thus the presentation is genuinely exact:

    0 → O(-12,2)⊕O(-8,1)
      --[[ell,h²],[0,a]]-->
      O(-8,3)⊕O(-7,1) → N → 0.             (A)

Its first map is injective because its determinant is a ell. In particular this is a sheaf-local elimination; it does not globally split the stabilized middle bundle.

The homomorphism

    (r',d₀) ↦ a r'−h²d₀ mod ell

has image (a,h²)O_Y(-7,3). Let D⊂Y be the subscheme cut out by (a,h²). It is the doubled line in Y₀=P². Its ideal is exactly (a,h²), not its radical (a,h).

To compute the kernel, reduce modulo ell. Since (a,h²) is a regular sequence on Y, a r'=h²d₀ implies r'=h²k and d₀=a k modulo ell. Subtracting the second column of (A), and then its first column, leaves a unique class represented by (0,ell z).

The annihilator of this generator is exactly (a). For if

    (0,ell z)=(ell s+h²k,a k),

then a k=ell z, and regularity of (a,ell) forces z=a z' and k=ell z'. Conversely

    a(0,ell)=[[ell,h²],[0,a]](-h²,ell).

The degree of z is (-11,0). Therefore

    0 → i_*i*O(-11,0) → N
      → I_{D/Y}O_Y(-7,3) → 0,             (B)

where i:X₀=P³→X. The left term has relative degree zero. It is the entire torsion subsheaf relative to the base: the quotient is an ideal in O_Y and has no a-torsion. The torsion is supported on the full exceptional P³, not on Λ or D.

There is no contradiction between injectivity of this sheaf map and the vanishing of the displayed matrix representative along ell=0. Tensoring an injective sheaf map with the residue field need not remain injective; the quotient is not locally free there.

## 4. Half correction, exact sufficiency, and lift invariance

Use the symplectic matrix

    Ω=[[0,0,I],[0,J,0],[-I,0,0]].

For B(V)=½VᵀJV and symmetric S, the transition

    g(V,S)=[[I,VᵀJ,S+B(V)],[0,I,V],[0,0,I]]

satisfies gᵀΩg=Ω. For β=(L,Q,R), its isotropy equation βΩ⁻¹βᵀ=0 is exactly

    R Lᵀ−L Rᵀ−Q J Qᵀ=0.

Thus the transition and isotropy signs are mutually consistent. In particular, when Q_j=Q_i+L VᵀJ,

    d(Q_iV+L B(V))=f_j−f_i.

Choose arbitrary local isotropic solutions R_i⁰. Then

    C_ij=R_j⁰−R_i⁰−Q_iV−L B(V)

is a section of M on the overlap. Its image in N gives a Čech class ω∈H¹(X,N). Omitting B(V) leaves a nonzero quadratic error in general, and therefore does not give this obstruction class.

The two affine-base charts and their overlap are acyclic for M by §2. They are acyclic for Sym²E because its summands have relative degree two, and for N by (A). Consequently the Čech group for this cover really computes H¹(X,N).

Suppose ω=0. There are local sections n_i∈N with the image of C_ij equal to n_j−n_i. The map H⁰(M|U_i)→H⁰(N|U_i) is surjective because H¹(Sym²E|U_i)=0. Lift n_i to m_i∈M. Replacing R_i⁰ by R_i⁰−m_i makes the new C_ij lie in L Sym²E. Thus C_ij=L S_ij for a unique symmetric section S_ij on the overlap, producing precisely the required symplectic transition and isotropic local rows.

Conversely any completed central gluing has C_ij=L S_ij, so ω=0. There are only two distinct opens: choose the reverse transition to be the inverse, and all repeated-index cocycle conditions follow. There is no additional three-distinct-chart condition. This proves necessity and sufficiency, not just necessity.

Changing the initial R_i⁰ by sections of M changes the N-cocycle by a coboundary. More strongly, ω is invariant under arbitrary changes of the first-stage lifts, once the accompanying local R_i are transformed correctly. This follows from the following explicit calculation.

Write a general change of first-stage lift as Q_i'=Q_i+L U_iᵀJ and use the local symplectic gauge g(U_i,0). Then

    R_i'=R_i+Q_i U_i+L B(U_i),
    V'=V+U_j−U_i.

Define the symmetric bilinear matrix

    H(V,W)=½(VᵀJW−WᵀJV).

Direct multiplication yields

    g(V,S)g(W,T)=g(V+W,S+T+H(V,W)).

It follows, even before the obstruction vanishes, that

    C_ij'−C_ij
      =L[-H(U_i,V)+H(V,U_j)−H(U_i,U_j)].

The bracket is symmetric. Hence the two cocycles have identical images in N. This also shows explicitly why arbitrary choices of local first-stage lifts do not compromise the invariant criterion. Diagonal changes of the fixed bundle trivializations merely express the same sheaf maps in different frames.

## 5. Exact cohomology and independent matrix check

For U=p_*O_Y(0,1)=O(2)⊕O(1)², the direct image of O_Y(-7,3) is Sym³U(-7), whose degrees, with multiplicity, are

    -1 once; -2 twice; -3 three times; -4 four times.

Higher direct images vanish. Therefore

    h⁰(O_Y(-7,3))=0,
    h¹(O_Y(-7,3))=0+2+6+12=20.

On Y₀=P² the exact sequence

    0 → O_P²(1) --h²→ O_P²(3) → O_D(3) → 0

gives h⁰(O_D(3))=10−3=7 and h¹(O_D(3))=0. The ideal sequence consequently gives

    h⁰(I_D O_Y(-7,3))=0,
    h¹(I_D O_Y(-7,3))=7+20=27.

The torsion term in (B) has h⁰=1 and no higher cohomology. Hence

    h⁰(N)=1, h¹(N)=27, hʲ(N)=0 for j≥2.

Radicalizing D would change seven to four and produce the incorrect number 24. This is a genuine scheme-structure issue; the doubled exceptional line must be retained.

As a second proof-compatible check, (A) has source H¹ dimension

    11·10+7·4=138

and target H¹ dimension

    7·20+6·4=164.

Its H¹ map must have one-dimensional kernel and rank 137. The exact checker `verify_central.py` constructs this full rational 164×138 Čech multiplication matrix, without using either dimension assertion, for

    ell=s⁴x₀+s³t x₁+s t³x₂+t⁴x₃, a=t,

and separately h=x₁ and h=2x₀+3x₁+5x₂+7x₃. It verifies rank 137 in both cases and exhibits the torsion boundary in the kernel. The displayed ell has two linear and one quadratic syzygies with maximal minors equal to its coefficients, certifying the required splitting U=(2,1,1). These finite checks corroborate the universal sheaf proof; they are not substitutes for it.

For completeness, first-stage K=coker L has h⁰(K)=14: Sym²U(-1) has h⁰=16 and globally generated summands, so restriction to the exceptional line imposes three independent linear conditions; the vertical torsion contributes one. Thus the ordered first-stage pair has 28 parameters, including its two torsion scalars. Those scalars are retained throughout. Their absence from the primary restriction does not imply absence from ω.

## 6. Global residue on the common-zero scheme

On Y, the row (a,-h) annihilates L. Hence q=a Q_C-h Q_D is a global ordered pair of sections of O_Y(-1,2). Put Z=V_Y(q_1,q_2). In a local splitting define

    rho_i=a R_{C1,i}-h R_{D1,i}.

This is regular in O_Y(-7,3), including at the exceptional point. For a completed central gluing,

    R_j-R_i=Q_i V+L(S+1/2 V^T J V).

After left multiplication by (a,-h) and restriction to Y, the change of the first column is q times the first column of V. Thus rho_j-rho_i lies in the ideal generated by q_1,q_2. The local residues consequently glue to a global regular section of O_Y(-7,3)|_Z. This statement holds on the entire scheme Z and permits no pole at the exceptional fiber. The torsion completion parameter has zero image in this residue on Y.

## 7. Completion ambiguity and the exact rank gates

Fix the actual first-stage Q_i,V. Differences between two completed central solutions are local sections m_i∈M whose images in N glue. Conversely every section of H⁰(N) lifts locally to such a difference. Modulo local symmetric splitting changes, completed solutions therefore form a torsor under H⁰(N), hence an affine line if nonempty. This is a statement about framed completions with the first-stage data fixed, not a count of all monad isomorphism classes after further automorphisms.

The scalar generator has (r',d₀)=(0,ell), represented locally by

    ΔR=[[0,0],[h ell,−ell²]].

Where a is invertible,

    ΔR=L[[-h²/a,h ell/a],[h ell/a,−ell²/a]],

and the matrix in brackets is symmetric. It is therefore a pure splitting change on every ordinary fiber, preserving rank. On the exceptional fiber the representative vanishes at every point of the plane ell₀=0, which is stronger than vanishing only on Λ. Any alternate representative differs by a symmetric splitting change and yields the same conclusion about rank. The scalar can still matter away from that plane in the exceptional P³.

For a≠0, set

    q=a Q_C−h Q_D,
    rho=a R_C1−h R_D1.

Eliminate the pivot a in the positive second column. The isotropy relation expresses the remaining second negative entry in the ideal generated by ell and the two entries of q. Precisely, the ideal of 2×2 minors, after localizing at a, is

    (ell,q₊,q₋,rho).

The exact checker `verify_central.py` checks this determinantal ideal identity symbolically, not merely at generic numerical values.

Consequently the ordinary-fiber condition is exactly that rho be nowhere zero on V(q₊,q₋) in the plane Y_t. The quadrics must be a regular pair: if they had a positive-dimensional common zero locus, a cubic section would have a zero on a positive-dimensional projective component, forcing rank drop. For a regular pair their complete intersection has scheme length four, possibly nonreduced; “nowhere zero” means a unit in every local Artin ring after trivializing its line bundle, and checking support detects this.

On the exceptional fiber, no cancellation by a is available. One must compute all minors of the actual matrix [L,Q,R] and saturate by the entire irrelevant ideal of P³. The condition is that this saturation be the unit ideal, equivalently that the geometric projective zero scheme be empty. Saturating by a single chosen coordinate would test only one affine chart and is insufficient. Primary vanishing and checking Λ alone do not replace this test.

## Scope

Within the explicit first-stage hypotheses in [README.md](README.md), the primary restriction has a four-dimensional target, the corrected central obstruction has dimension 27, and the vertical completion ambiguity has dimension one. This proves the gluing/isotropy criterion; it does not produce a first-stage pair satisfying it or satisfying the additional rank conditions. The maximal-direction exclusion is proved separately in [maximal-direction.md](maximal-direction.md).
