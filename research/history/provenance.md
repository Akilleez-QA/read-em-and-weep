## Required reproducibility before a result is used as a lemma

For each new calculation publish:
- precise hypotheses, normalization, grading/sign conventions and source revision;
- full proof capsule or executable code plus exact inputs;
- exact output and whether it is symbolic, integer/rational or floating point;
- independent-check scope and remaining gaps;
- a narrowly worded conclusion.

Do not invent a missing run log, verification record or historical timestamp. If a calculation is rerun later, label its new date and compare with the recovered result.

## Deliberately excluded claims

- The withdrawn Floer action-order mismatch.
- The unverified same-sign graph-orientation/multidegree obstruction.
- A stand-alone FOOO-based surgery no-go without the recovered precise citation and full hypotheses.
- Any inference from h² exceeding a target dimension to an actual deformation obstruction without a separate evaluation calculation.
- Any claim of ambient all-order HM unobstructedness. The dated exact count now supports h¹=87 and ordinary fixed-X Yoneda-square vanishing.
- A repair for all imaginary quadratic fields from the squareclass-15 carrier.
- A solution, novelty priority or independent external validation.
