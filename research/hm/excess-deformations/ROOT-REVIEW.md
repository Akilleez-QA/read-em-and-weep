# Root mathematical review

The Hom-complex calculation was independently checked against the pure C resolution. In degree zero the first map has kernel of dimension one, hence rank twenty-four; the first extension space has dimension 126. The isolated pieces give dimensions seventy-five in internal degree minus one and five hundred in internal degree minus two. The diagonal Koszul bimodule resolution supplies the remaining duality directly: commutators are skew adjoint under trace, and the exterior factors contribute total degree minus five. This verifies every entry and every omitted zero in the self-Ext table.

The open Grassmannian family was checked without relying only on obstruction theory. Surjectivity in degree two kills all higher components, leaving vector bundles of ranks five and ten. Every Artinian graded flat deformation is of this form after choosing its degree-zero frame, by componentwise Nakayama. Smoothness therefore gives lifts. The effective frame group has dimension twenty-four, since the stabilizer is the scalar subgroup; the transverse formal slice has dimension 126. No global coarse quotient is inferred.

The finite-normalizer distinction was also checked. Its two degree-one summands have no common constituent, and the compatible multiplication has multiplicity one. Equivariant averaged frames exist over an Artinian base; the resulting scalar coefficient is a unit and can be absorbed in the degree-one frame. Thus the claimed equivariant rigidity is stronger than an infinitesimal dimension count and has the precise fixed-action hypotheses stated in the proof.

These are deformations of the auxiliary graded S-module C over fixed S. They do not settle realization by HM bundles, the original fixed pair or source, or any higher-order lifting problem for those objects. Root proposed the Ext and Grassmannian directions; author and separate auditor verified them independently. Mathematical review remains conditional on the pinned inputs, not formal proof-assistant checking.
