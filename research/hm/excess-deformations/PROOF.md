# Graded self-extensions and deformations of the excess factor

Work over k=C and fix S=Sym(T), dim(T)=5, with its standard grading. These are deformations of graded S-modules over the fixed ring S. No assertion about deformations of an HM bundle, a source instance, or a lift to those objects is made.

## Pinned inputs and conventions

PR498, commit 77018c2e6904a4ef9adfee5f2f48e49894b63fe9, defines the excess factor C and proves

- C_0=A has dimension 5, C_1=B has dimension 10, and all other pieces vanish;
- C is generated by A;
- End_S(C)_0=k, End_S(C)_1=Hom_k(A,B), of dimension 50, and there are no other homogeneous endomorphisms.

PR502, commit f8c9b1fb8b03a5721e4eca97cac4137f7ba955d0, proves its minimal resolution is

0 -> 10S(-6) -> 45S(-5) -> 75S(-4) -> 50S(-3) -> 15S(-1) -> 5S -> C -> 0.

Here a homogeneous map of internal degree d sends M_j into N_{j+d}. In particular Hom(S(-a),C)_d=C_{a+d}.

The only nonzero dimensions of Ext^i_S(C,C)_d are

| i | d | dimension |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0 | 1 |
| 0 | 1 | 50 |
| 1 | -1 | 75 |

First apply Hom_S(-,C) to the pinned minimal resolution. The nonzero cochain pieces are:

| cochain degree | internal degrees and dimensions |
|---|---|
| 0 | 0:25, 1:50 |
| 1 | -1:75, 0:150 |
| 2 | -3:250, -2:500 |
| 3 | -4:375, -3:750 |
| 4 | -5:225, -4:450 |
| 5 | -6:50, -5:100 |

The known scalar kernel in internal degree zero makes the first differential have rank 24. Thus Ext^1_0 has dimension 150-24=126. The isolated degree -1 piece gives Ext^1_{-1}=k^75. The isolated cochain-degree-two, internal-degree-minus-two piece gives Ext^2_{-2}=k^500.

For completeness, the duality used to determine every remaining entry can be proved directly here. The diagonal Koszul resolution of S as an S-bimodule, tensored on the right with C, is a free resolution of C. It has terms S tensor exterior^i(T) tensor C; its differential uses x_j acting on the first factor minus multiplication M_j on C. It is a resolution because the diagonal Koszul resolution is split exact as a complex of right S-modules off its augmentation (its augmentation target S is right S-free). Applying Hom_S(-,C) gives the finite cochain complex

K^i=End_k(C) tensor exterior^i(T*),
d(f tensor alpha)=sum_j [M_j,f] tensor (x_j* wedge alpha).

Assign each x_j* internal degree -1. Pair K^i and K^{5-i} by trace(fg) times exterior product into exterior^5(T*). The trace pairing on the full matrix algebra End_k(C) and the exterior-product pairing are perfect. Moreover trace([M,f]g)=-trace(f[M,g]); with the exterior sign this is a pairing of complexes. It induces perfect pairings on cohomology. Its total internal degree is -5: trace pairs endomorphisms of opposite internal degrees, and the exterior factors contribute -5. After choosing a volume form, this proves

(Ext^i_S(C,C)_d)* = Ext^{5-i}_S(C,C)_{-5-d}

as vector spaces. Without a volume choice retain the one-dimensional exterior^5(T*) target. This proof also fixes the grading shift, rather than importing an unspecified duality convention.

There is no cochain-degree-three piece of internal degree -2, so Ext^3_{-2}=0. Duality therefore kills the only other possible Ext^2 piece, Ext^2_{-3}. The displayed table follows, including all vanishings.

## 2. A smooth 150-dimensional framed family

Put E=T tensor A, of dimension 25, and let L be the 15-dimensional kernel of E -> C_1. Let G=Gr(15,E), and let U be the open subset where multiplication

T tensor L' -> Sym^2(T) tensor A

is surjective. This is an open condition on the universal subbundle. The point L lies in U because the pinned resolution says all relations of C are generated in degree one, whereas C_2=0.

For L' in U define C(L')=(S tensor A)/(S L'). Degree zero is A, degree one is E/L', and every degree at least two is zero. Indeed surjectivity kills degree two, and multiplication then kills all higher degrees. Over U these pieces are respectively a trivial rank-five bundle and the universal rank-ten quotient bundle. The family is therefore flat, and U is smooth of dimension 15(25-15)=150.

This family accounts for every sufficiently small graded flat deformation with a chosen degree-zero frame. More precisely, let R be a local Artinian k-algebra and let M be a finite graded S tensor R-module, flat over R, with special fiber C. Each graded piece is R-flat and finite; Nakayama shows the pieces outside degrees zero and one vanish, and the two remaining pieces are free of ranks five and ten. After choosing M_0=A tensor R, the multiplication E tensor R -> M_1 is surjective by Nakayama. Its kernel is a rank-fifteen direct summand L_R. The multiplication map T tensor L_R -> Sym^2(T) tensor A tensor R is surjective because its reduction is; hence M=C(L_R). Thus L_R is an R-point of U lifting L. Conversely every such point gives a deformation.

Since U is smooth, framed deformations lift across every small Artinian extension. Consequently unframed graded deformations also lift: choose and lift a degree-zero frame. This is a direct unobstructedness proof, independent of obstruction theory. It agrees with Ext^2_S(C,C)_0=0 from section 1.

## 3. The 126-dimensional formal transverse slice

Changing the degree-zero frame acts by GL(A) on U. The stabilizer at L equals Aut_S(C)_0=k*, since an automorphism is determined by its action on degree zero. Scalars act trivially on all of G. Thus the PGL(A) orbit has dimension 25-1=24 and trivial stabilizer at L.

Choose a Grassmannian affine chart around L and a 126-dimensional smooth slice through L whose tangent space complements the orbit tangent space. The action map PGL(A) times the slice -> U has invertible differential at (1,L). It is therefore etale there and is an isomorphism on the corresponding completed local neighborhoods. This gives a smooth formal versal slice of dimension 150-24=126 for graded deformations of C over fixed S, with tangent space Ext^1_S(C,C)_0. This is a local formal statement, not a claim that a global coarse quotient exists.

One can also read the tangent quotient directly: T_L G=Hom(L,E/L), of dimension 150, and infinitesimal frame changes have kernel the scalar endomorphisms, hence image dimension 24. The quotient has dimension 126, agreeing with the resolution calculation.

## 4. Rigidity with the pinned finite normalizer action

There is a sharp difference between ordinary graded deformations and equivariant ones. Keep fixed the pinned finite normalizer N, its action on S, and the compatible action on C. PR498 gives the multiplicity-one decomposition

T tensor A = (V tensor U') direct-sum (V tensor W),

where the first summand is L and the second is B. The two summands have no common N-irreducible constituent. Consequently

Hom_N(L,B)=0.

In degree zero the first Hom cochain group is Hom_k(L,B). Since taking invariants under a finite group in characteristic zero is exact, Ext^1_S(C,C)_0^N=0. Equivalently, the entire 150-dimensional Grassmannian tangent space has no N-fixed directions. The 126-dimensional ordinary tangent quotient therefore has none either.

This infinitesimal vanishing has a direct all-Artinian explanation. In an N-equivariant graded deformation over a local Artinian k-algebra R with trivial N-action on R, choose N-equivariant identifications M_0=A tensor R and M_1=B tensor R lifting the special-fiber ones. Such identifications exist: lift any frames, average the corresponding maps over N, and apply Nakayama to the averaged maps. All other graded pieces vanish as in section 2. The multiplication belongs to

Hom_N(T tensor A,B) tensor R = R mu,

the rank-one space supplied by multiplicity one. Its coefficient reduces to 1, so it is a unit. Rescaling the degree-one identification by this unit identifies the deformation with the constant module C tensor R, with its N-action. Thus every such equivariant graded deformation is trivial, not merely unobstructed.

## Contribution and limits

Purity and rigidity of degree-zero automorphisms do not make C rigid as a graded module. Its graded deformation problem is unobstructed with 126 transverse infinitesimal parameters, and there is a concrete flat Grassmannian family realizing them. The nonzero higher Ext groups occur in negative internal degrees and do not contradict degree-zero unobstructedness.

For the pinned finite normalizer action, section 4 proves that none of the ordinary parameters gives a nontrivial compatible equivariant graded deformation. This does not determine which ordinary deformations arise as determinantal excess modules or lift to HM/source data; those realization constraints remain separate. No selected HM/source instance, numerical matrix, rank checker, coefficient search, or large computation was evaluated. Claims here are mathematical derivations from the pinned inputs, not proof-assistant-checked statements.
