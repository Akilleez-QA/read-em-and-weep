## 2. Self-contained duality and its grading

Resolve the diagonal S as an S-bimodule by the Koszul complex on x_j tensor 1 minus 1 tensor x_j. Tensoring on the right with C produces a finite free left S-resolution of C. Exactness is preserved because the bimodule resolution is split exact as a resolution of the free right S-module S. Applying Hom_S(-,C) gives cochains

K^i=End_k(C) tensor exterior^i(T*),

with differential sum_j [x_j,f] tensor (x_j* wedge alpha). An underlying endomorphism of degree e in K^i has total internal degree e-i.

Choose a nonzero volume functional on exterior^5(T*). The trace pairing on End_k(C) and wedge pairing give a perfect pairing between K^i_d and K^(5-i)_(-5-d). Indeed the paired underlying endomorphism degrees sum to zero. The identity

trace([x,f]g)=-trace(f[x,g])

and the exterior signs make the differentials adjoint up to the required cochain sign. Thus the pairing descends to a perfect cohomology pairing, proving

(Ext^i_S(C,C)_d)* is isomorphic to Ext^(5-i)_S(C,C)_(-5-d)

as vector spaces after the choice of volume. No equivariant determinant twist is being suppressed in an equivariant assertion: only dimensions and nonequivariant duality are asserted here.

Combining this with the Hom support gives the complete nonzero list:

| i | internal degree: dimension |
|---|---|
| 0 | 0:1, 1:50 |
| 1 | -1:75, 0:126 |
| 2 | -2:500 |
| 3 | -3:500 |
| 4 | -5:126, -4:75 |
| 5 | -6:50, -5:1 |

For example Ext^2_(-3)=0 because its dual Ext^3_(-2) has no cochain term; Ext^3_(-4)=0 similarly. These exclusions are essential to determining the full table.

## 3. Elementary lifting of graded deformations

Let G=Gr(15,T tensor A). On the open subset U where T tensor L -> S_2 tensor A is onto, the universal quotient (S tensor A)/(S L) has graded pieces A, (T tensor A)/L, and zero in all degrees at least two. These pieces are locally free of ranks 5 and 10, so the family is flat. The PR502 linear presentation places C in U. The dimension of U is 15 times 10 =150.

For a finite flat graded deformation over a local Artin k-algebra R, its graded components are free of the original ranks by Nakayama and flatness. A chosen identification of degree zero with A tensor R gives a surjection S tensor A tensor R onto it, and its degree-one kernel is a rank-15 direct summand of T tensor A tensor R. Its reduction lies in U, hence the entire local Artin point lies in U. Conversely this subspace reconstructs the module. This identifies degree-zero-framed deformations with the formal neighborhood of U at the original relation subspace.

Every such Grassmannian point lifts across a local Artin small extension: in an affine Grassmann chart it is the graph of a matrix, whose entries can be lifted. The surjectivity condition remains valid by Nakayama. Hence these graded module deformations are unobstructed; this is an explicit lifting argument, not merely a dimension count.

Changing the degree-zero framing gives the GL(A) action. Its infinitesimal stabilizer is End_S(C)_0=k, so its infinitesimal orbit has dimension 25-1=24. The quotient of the framed tangent space by these infinitesimal changes has dimension 150-24=126, agreeing with Ext^1_0. This does not assert existence of a geometric quotient, a fine moduli scheme, or a universal family on such a quotient.

## Scope conclusion

The proposed full self-Ext table and unobstructed 126-dimensional graded tangent deformation problem are correct conditional on the pinned premises. These statements concern ordinary graded S-modules with S fixed, not normalizer-equivariant deformations, not ungraded deformations, and not the original HM bundle/source-instance higher-order lifting or algebraicity problem. In particular total Ext^2 has dimension 500 even though its degree-zero piece vanishes.

## 4. Audit of final authored additions

The complete accompanying authored proof was read, including its formal transverse slice and finite-normalizer rigidity sections.

The formal slice assertion is valid. Inside an affine Grassmann chart choose an affine linear subspace through the base point complementary to the 24-dimensional orbit tangent space. It is smooth of dimension 126. The action map from PGL(A) times this slice has invertible differential at the indicated point, hence is etale there. With equal residue fields it induces an isomorphism of completed local rings. This justifies a smooth formal versal slice; it does not assert a global quotient or remove scalar automorphisms from the module groupoid.

The finite-normalizer assertion is also valid, provided the pinned decomposition means the distinct irreducible multiplicity representations U' and W, as in PR498. Heisenberg Schur's lemma reduces Hom_N(V tensor U', V tensor W) to the corresponding intertwiners of the distinct multiplicity representations; these vanish. Thus Hom_N(L,B)=0, and exactness of finite-group invariants in characteristic zero gives Ext^1_0^N=0.

More decisively, for a compatible fixed N-action on S and trivial N-action on a local Artin base, equivariant frames on the two nonzero graded pieces exist by averaging lifts of the special-fiber frames and applying Nakayama. Multiplication then lies in the rank-one R-module Hom_N(T tensor A,B) tensor R. Its scalar coefficient is a unit because it specializes to the original nonzero multiplication. Rescaling the degree-one frame makes the family constant. This proves all-Artinian equivariant graded rigidity, not merely tangent vanishing. It makes no assertion about arbitrary changes of group action on S or about HM/source realization.
