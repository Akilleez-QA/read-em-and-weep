## Proposition (unnumbered), printed p.218

- h¹(P⁴, End(F)) = 24.
- h¹(P⁴, End(F)(−1)) = 5.
- h¹(P⁴, End(F)(k)) = 0 for every k ≤ −2.

## Scope and normalization

F is explicitly the classical Horrocks–Mumford bundle in the introduction (p.215) and section 2 (p.217). The proof on p.218 explicitly normalizes c₁(F)=−1. The introduction gives c₂(F)=4; section 1 explicitly uses S=C[x₀,…,xₙ]. Thus this verifies the complex case, not a positive-characteristic extension.

The proposition concerns F itself. Its application to finite pullbacks by projection formula immediately precedes it. End(F⊗L) is canonically End(F) for any line bundle L, so a change of HM normalization by a line-bundle twist leaves all these End-cohomology values unchanged. This is a standard tensor-dual identification, stated here separately from the proposition.

## Proof context, paraphrased

At zero the authors cite Decker [De1] and mention another approach in Manolache [Man]. The bibliography on p.220 identifies [De1] as Decker, Math. Z. 188 (1984), 101–110. For the other twists, p.218 uses the HM monad, End(F) ≅ F(1)⊗F, and exact sequences. Page 219 uses a generic section of F(3), its abelian-surface zero locus, Serre correspondence, and representations of the group generated by the Heisenberg group and an involution. This gives the value 5 at −1. Representation theory restricts the possibilities at −2; stability of F on every hyperplane and a bilinear-map lemma force zero. More negative twists follow by induction. These pages provide a mathematical proof rather than a computer calculation. No source, rank, matrix, model, or polynomial-instance evaluations were run for this retrieval.

The introduction's separate pullback theorem concerns finite morphisms P⁴→P⁴ of degree d⁴ and a smooth open part of a moduli component of dimension 5*binomial(d+4,4)−1. The verified dependency here is the proposition above.
