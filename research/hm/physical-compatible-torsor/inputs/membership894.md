## Result

positive U=Wprime, D5=Sym^4(U). The three-dimensional irreducibles A and
V=Sym^2(U) are distinct. Consider the FULL closed global source

    T(a,h)=a(V_h(a)-b(a,h) Z_a)-q W_h(a) in C^1_4.       (1)

All families in this note are supplied or chosen coherently and
G-equivariantly over the entire parameter space; V has bidegree (2,1),
b has (1,1), Z has (1,0), and W has (3,1). Merely having pointwise
primitives on a proper source locus is not a hypothesis here.
Z is the fixed canonical Atiyah representative, and V represents the
preceding FULL-FAMILY division q[v_h(a)]=a h zeta_a in M4, as in Section 4.
An arbitrary closed V of the same bidegree is not a substitute for this
prerequisite. No equation asserting the desired E or W is assumed.

For canonical, or independently compared canonical, b:

* The cohomological obstruction for a prescribed W lies in the exact
  27-dimensional space Hom_G(R,im(q:M2->M4)). It vanishes if and only if
  (1) admits a global equivariant polynomial degree-zero primitive.
* These are 6 V-type, 9 P4-type and 12 D5-type scalar multiplicity slots.
  They are NOT 27 output-vector dimensions or an actual evaluated rank.
* As the supplied closed cubic W varies, every element of this sector is
  attained. Its zero fiber is an affine seven-space at the COHOMOLOGY
  family level, inside the 34-dimensional space Hom_G(R,M2).

If b is allowed to be an arbitrary equivariant linear correction, the
sharp obstruction sector has dimension 28. Full membership first forces
b=b_can and then the same 27 compatibility conditions on W. In particular,
zero quotient-P4 obstruction alone does NOT validate a prescribed W.

The established canonical factorization already implies existence of
compatible polynomial choices. That existence is not claimed as new.
The new result is the exact, sharp separation between such existence and
the full-source membership certificate required for a supplied packet.

## 2. Why the coefficientwise cohomology test is sufficient

Write C^j_t=Gamma(X,D_M^j(t)) for the complete fixed-duality trace-free
global complex, Z^1_t=ker(d:C^1_t->C^2_t), and B^1_t=d C^0_t.
The actual G-action commutes with d and multiplication by the invariant q.
All these are finite-dimensional complex representations. The inputs
Z_a,V_h,W_h are closed, so T defines a map R->Z^1_4.

Positive-twist acyclicity identifies Z^1_4/B^1_4 with H1(B(4)). Since R
is central-even, any equivariant map from R has image in its positive
part M4. There is no discarded odd obstruction. The functor Hom_G(R,-)
is exact: ordinary linear splittings can be averaged over finite G in
characteristic zero. Therefore

    T belongs to Hom_G(R,d C^0_4)
      iff [T]=0 in Hom_G(R,M4)
      iff there is E in Hom_G(R,C^0_4) with dE=T.         (4)

This gives one global homogeneous cubic/linear family, not separate
pointwise choices followed by an unjustified gluing claim. A splitting
is taken only AFTER the entire coefficient map has zero obstruction.
It is not a claim to know the actual physical matrices of a primitive.

Likewise every element of Hom_G(R,M2) has a closed global-cochain
representative. Cohomologically distinct W-families are counted below;
actual cochain representatives have additional boundary freedom.

For fixed T, H0(B(4))=0 gives

    E'-E=dY,  Y in Hom_G(R,C^-1_4).                      (5)

Thus a primitive is unique modulo global degree-minus-one boundaries,
NOT literally unique as a degree-zero cochain. The full complex is
essential for (4)-(5); a supported middle-block quotient is insufficient.

## 4. Canonical correction leaves exactly 27 compatibility slots

First justify that the prerequisite V can be chosen over the full family
without presupposing a later primitive. The natural Z_a is linear in a;
the cohomology family a h zeta_a is quadratic in a and linear in h. The
actual D5 multiplication inclusion in input 2 puts its entire image in I.
An equivariant linear section I->M2 consequently produces a polynomial
v of that bidegree. A closed global cochain lift gives V; the same
positive-twist comparison supplies dC=a h Z-q V.

Any two such v differ by a family in K. Because a K lies in I, their
raw cubic quotient pi(a v) agrees. The canonical comparison (3) is
therefore not lost merely by choosing a different division representative.
An arbitrary change of the initial Atiyah probe or Schur normalization

Now fix V and the canonical/compared b. Set S=a(V-bZ). Equation (3) says

    [S] in Hom_G(R,I).                                 (9)

For a supplied closed cubic W, define the precise defect

    Omega(W)=[S]-q_*[W] in Hom_G(R,I).                  (10)

More strongly, (10) is an affine SURJECTION from Hom_G(R,M2) onto
Hom_G(R,I): q_* is onto this space by exactness. Its fibers are torsors

fixed. This uses actual cohomology and existence of global representatives;

In particular, all quotient-P4 tests pass for all these W. They cannot

## 5. An unnormalized linear correction adds exactly one slot

Let L=Hom_G(A tensor D5,A), the space of equivariant families b(a,h).
Since A tensor A=1+A+D5, dim L=1. Define the linear map

    c:L -> Hom_G(R,P),  c(b)(a,h)=pi(a b(a,h) Z_a).

It is injective. In sheaf form c(b)=beta2 composed with b; the injection
of beta2 as a SHEAF forces b=0 if this composition is zero. No assertion
of fiberwise injectivity at an exceptional axis is made or needed.
Thus ell=im c is a canonical one-dimensional subspace of the three
quotient channels. By (3), pi[a V]=c(b_can).

For arbitrary supplied equivariant b and closed cubic W, the residual

    Omega(b,W)=[a V]-[a b Z]-q_*[W]                    (11)

lies in the 28-dimensional preimage pi_*^-1(ell) in Hom_G(R,M4).
The affine map (11), on L plus Hom_G(R,M2), is onto that preimage:
variation of b covers ell and variation of W covers ker pi_*=Hom(R,I).
Its linear kernel has dimension seven, with zero b-component.

It follows that full membership is equivalent to BOTH

    b=b_can,
    Omega(b_can,W)=0 in the 27-dimensional sector.      (12)

The other two quotient channels are absent because of the established
global canonical raw-cubic theorem, not because symmetry forces them
away. This also explains why deleting b cannot be defended by passing
to a quotient: the input theorem proves b_can nonzero.
