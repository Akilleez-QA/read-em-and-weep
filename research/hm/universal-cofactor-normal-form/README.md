# Abstract cofactor model: normal form and failure divisor

First of three focused packets. Work over C with abstract dim V=5 and dim U=2 and define E=V⊗sl(U). The theorem concerns evaluation β_J:J⊗U→V⊗U on Gr(5,E), not an identification of E with any geometric module.

## Results

- Exact block normal form [P Q; R −P].
- Proper irreducible reduced normal rational divisor of Plücker degree two.
- Smooth locus exactly corank one and a cokernel-line incidence resolution.
- Rank-one output explanations form a codimension-five locus.
