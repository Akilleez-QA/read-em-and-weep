# Relative degree-three torsion controls the order-six jump

The full proof resolves the relative-cohomology boundary in PR442--443.
On the algebraic translated-root curve, localized at a valid parameter:

- Relative degree-two paired-character ideal cohomology is zero.
- The torsion in relative degree three is canonically two copies of Ext1(Q_A,A), not the ordinary dual of Q_A.
- Every fat-fiber degree-two group is Tor1 of that relative degree-three module.
- Its lengths stabilize by thickness two, but restriction maps multiply by the parameter. Two successive restrictions are zero. Thus no nonzero exceptional fiber class persists as a compatible formal sequence. More concretely, a nonzero closed-fiber coefficient class cannot lift to the third parameter thickening A/(z^3); at a simple controller root it cannot lift even to A/(z^2).

The fixed original quotient remains unevaluated. The result concerns coefficient geometry and auxiliary parameter thickenings, not source membership, the six pure conditions, quantization order, module lifting, or quantization algebraicity.
