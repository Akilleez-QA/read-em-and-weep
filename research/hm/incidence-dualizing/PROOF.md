# Dualizing the primary incidence: logarithmic bridge forms, trace, and nonsplitting

Status: authored structural proof, independently mathematically reviewed in REVIEW.md; not formally checked. Work over C. This argument uses only universal local algebra and the already proved incidence geometry; it performs no selected actual HM/source-instance evaluation.

## 1. Pinned inputs and theorem

Use PR575, commit 99529ca66e1c394e2b3fbe2f8786c71433755956 (https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/pull/575), and its dependencies. Its primary annihilator incidence has proper projection p:Z->S, with Z=X union E, where X=S(5) is the smooth normalization of S and E is a disjoint union of thirty P1 bridges. Each bridge meets X in two distinct reduced points. Put J=X intersection E, a reduced scheme of sixty points, and let j_X,j_E,j_J denote the closed inclusions into Z. At every attachment the completed local ring is

A=C[[a,b,t]]/(ta,tb), with X defined by t=0 and E by a=b=0.

There are no other component intersections. The natural unit O_S->Rp_*O_Z is an isomorphism. On X, p restricts to the finite normalization nu:X->S; each bridge is contracted to its corresponding node.

PR574, commit 3c92fdc469f6870520e7a9d905b5ab9199a5bf37 (https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/pull/574), proves that T=nu_*O_X/O_S has length thirty, one at each node, and that the absolute dualizing complex K_S has H^-2=nu_*omega_X and H^-1=T^D, where T^D is the dualizing (Matlis-dual) finite-length sheaf. Neither T nor its dual is canonically trivialized at each point.

Let K_Z denote the absolute dualizing complex, normalized so a smooth d-dimensional scheme has K=omega[d]. Then:

1. H^-2(K_Z)=j_X*omega_X, H^-1(K_Z)=j_E*omega_E(J), and all other cohomology sheaves vanish.
2. The natural injection j_E*omega_E->H^-1(K_Z) is the usual inclusion of regular into meromorphic differentials with at most simple poles at J. Its quotient is identified by residues, with signs fixed by the gluing convention.
3. Each bridge contributes H^0(P1,omega_P1(two endpoints)), a one-dimensional line, and H^1=0. The Grothendieck-duality trace Rp_*K_Z->K_S is an isomorphism; its degree-minus-one map identifies those thirty lines with T^D, not merely an abstract length-thirty sheaf.
4. K_Z is not the direct sum of its two cohomology sheaves with the indicated shifts. At every attachment its local Postnikov class is a nonzero element of a one-dimensional Ext^2 group.

## 2. Dualizing the gluing sequence

The reduced transverse attachment description gives

0 -> O_Z -> j_X*O_X direct-sum j_E*O_E -> j_J*O_J -> 0,

where the last map is the difference of the two restrictions. Applying RHom_Z(-,K_Z), and finite closed-immersion duality, gives the canonical triangle

j_J*K_J -> j_X*K_X direct-sum j_E*K_E -> K_Z -> j_J*K_J[1].

Here K_X=omega_X[2], K_E=omega_E[1], and K_J=omega_J in degree zero. The cohomology exact sequence therefore gives

H^-2(K_Z)=j_X*omega_X,
0 -> j_E*omega_E -> H^-1(K_Z) -> j_J*omega_J -> 0,

and no other cohomology. It remains essential to identify the middle sheaf rather than infer its geometry from this abstract extension alone.

## 3. Local resolution and the actual simple-pole inclusion

Set R=C[[a,b,t]]. A free resolution of A is

0 -> R --(-b,a)--> R^2 --(ta,tb)--> R -> A -> 0.

Exactness follows because R is a domain and a,b form a regular sequence: any relation ta*x+tb*y=0 is a multiple of (-b,a). Dualizing gives the cochain complex

R --(ta,tb)^T--> R^2 --(-b,a)--> R.

Its H^0 is zero. The kernel of the second map is generated by (a,b), so H^1=R/(t); its H^2=R/(a,b). Since K_A=RHom_R(A,R)[3] after a choice of ambient canonical generator, this proves that H^-1(K_A) is a free rank-one module over the bridge ring C[[t]], annihilated by (a,b). In particular there is no extension thickening off the bridge.

The morphism A->O_E=R/(a,b) is lifted by a map from the displayed resolution of A to the Koszul resolution of R/(a,b). In degrees 0,1,2 this map is respectively identity, diag(t,t), and multiplication by t. Dualizing, the natural map

Ext^2_R(R/(a,b),R) -> Ext^2_R(A,R)

is therefore multiplication by t. This is exactly the inclusion omega_E->H^-1(K_Z) at the attachment, and its cokernel has length one there. Away from J this map is an isomorphism on E.

These completed-local calculations descend to the coherent sheaves: completion is faithfully flat, so the ideal of E annihilates H^-1(K_Z), which is a line bundle on E. Call that line bundle L. The injection omega_E->L has precisely the reduced zero divisor J. There is a unique identification L=omega_E(J) which is the identity on regular differentials away from J and carries this injection to the natural one. Equivalently, embed L in the rational differential line using that injection; its local stalk at an endpoint is t^-1 omega_E. Thus the identification is intrinsic, not a noncanonical choice of a degree-zero line bundle.

The quotient is the residue sheaf in the exact sequence

0 -> omega_E -> omega_E(J) --res--> omega_J -> 0.

Locally dt/t maps to 1 after the standard identification of the dualizing line of a reduced point with C. Changing a parameter t to a unit times t leaves the residue unchanged. The sign in the quotient identification agrees with the chosen difference in the original gluing map; reversing that convention reverses the corresponding dual identification and does not change the intrinsic assertion.

Each component E_i is P1 and J_i has two points, so omega_Ei(J_i) has degree zero and is isomorphic to O_P1. This last isomorphism is not asserted to be canonically trivialized. The two residues of a global logarithmic differential on E_i are opposite, and residue at either chosen endpoint identifies its space of sections with C.

## 4. Proper pushforward and actual trace compatibility

All higher direct images of j_X*omega_X vanish because nu is finite. All higher direct images of j_E*omega_E(J) vanish because each bridge is contracted and H^1(P1,omega_P1(two points))=H^1(P1,O)=0. Consequently the cohomology-sheaf spectral sequence for Rp_*K_Z degenerates and gives

H^-2(Rp_*K_Z)=nu_*omega_X,
H^-1(Rp_*K_Z)=direct-sum over nodes q of H^0(E_q,omega_Eq(J_q)),

with all other cohomology zero.

This is not by itself a proof that the complexes or their trace maps agree. That agreement follows from proper Grothendieck duality: with K_Z=p^!K_S, the duality isomorphism is

Rp_*K_Z = RHom_S(Rp_*O_Z,K_S).

Under this isomorphism the trace Rp_*p^!K_S->K_S is the dual of the natural unit O_S->Rp_*O_Z. PR575 proves that very unit is an isomorphism. Its dual is therefore an isomorphism, proving the asserted trace statement with no choice of an abstract comparison of equal-dimensional groups.

For an explicit description at a node, label its normalization preimages + and -. The normalization quotient T_q is the quotient of C direct-sum C by the diagonal constants, with coordinate represented by f(+)-f(-). A global logarithmic differential on the bridge has residues (r,-r). Its pairing with this quotient is

[(c_+,c_-)] -> r*c_+ - r*c_-.

This descends precisely because the residues sum to zero. The residue exact sequence in Section 3 is dual to the endpoint restriction sequence, so functoriality of the dualized gluing triangle identifies this pairing with the degree-minus-one trace map to T_q^D. Swapping the two endpoint labels changes both difference and residue coordinate by a sign and leaves the pairing unchanged. Thus the thirty defect-dual lines have a canonical bridge realization, even though choosing a scalar generator in each is not canonical.

## 5. A further consequence: the dualizing complex does not split

At an attachment, choose an ambient canonical generator and put M=R/(t), N=R/(a,b). The truncation triangle of K_A is

M[2] -> K_A -> N[1] -> M[3].

Its connecting morphism is an element of Ext^2_R(N,M). The Koszul resolution on a,b computes this group as M/(a,b)M=C. We now prove that this element is nonzero, without choosing a potentially ambiguous sign for its generator.

Suppose K_A were isomorphic in the derived category of R-modules to M[2] direct-sum N[1]. Apply ambient duality D_R(-)=RHom_R(-,R)[3]. Biduality holds here because the displayed finite free resolutions are perfect over the regular ring R. Hence D_R(K_A)=A. The regular-quotient Koszul resolutions give D_R(M[2])=M and D_R(N[1])=N. The supposed splitting would imply an R-module isomorphism A=M direct-sum N. But A is cyclic, with dim_C(A/mA)=1, while dim_C((M direct-sum N)/m(M direct-sum N))=2. This contradiction proves nonsplitting. As Ext^2_R(N,M) is one-dimensional, the local Postnikov class is a generator up to a nonzero scalar.

In particular the global complex cannot split: a global splitting would induce this impossible completed-local splitting. This statement concerns the dualizing complex on the mixed-dimensional incidence, not an assertion that the incidence is Cohen--Macaulay, a smooth resolution, or a scheme with rational singularities.

### The surface dualizing complex also does not split

There is a separate global consequence on S. Regard its sheaves as sheaves on P4, put B=nu_*O_X, and use D_4(-)=RHom_P4(-,omega_P4)[4]. PR574 gives D_4(B)=nu_*omega_X[2] and D_4(T)=T^D in degree zero. If K_S split as nu_*omega_X[2] direct-sum T^D[1], ambient biduality would give

O_S = B direct-sum T[-1].

The right side has nonzero cohomology in degree +1 because T has length thirty, whereas the left side is concentrated in degree zero. Thus this splitting is impossible as well. This argument is independent of the mixed-dimensional local argument and uses the actual nonzero normalization defect. It upgrades PR574's cautious absence of a splitting claim to a rigorous exclusion.

## 6. Contribution and boundaries

The new step gives an explicit geometric realization of the primary surface's length-thirty dualizing defect by logarithmic forms on the thirty contracted bridges, proves compatibility with the actual proper trace, and proves nonsplitting of both the incidence and surface dualizing complexes. It refines the structure-sheaf bridge theorem of PR575 and the dualizing-defect theorem of PR574. It does not assert that omega_E(J) has a preferred scalar generator, that K_Z is pure-dimensional, or that a selected HM source-instance calculation, higher-order lifting problem, or algebraicity problem has been solved.
