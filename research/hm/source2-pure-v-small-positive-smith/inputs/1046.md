# Original-fourth composition preserves the entire nonic component ideal

## Theorem and scope

Retain the conditional pure-V gate and the normalized quadratic-lift pencil
R=alpha R1+beta R2. Let F=NLR be its four-component nonic row and put
H=FL, a five-component row of degree ten. Under the actual quotient-target
identifications of PR1025, H is the negative of the determinant-identified
original fourth on its surviving D summand, up to the fixed common nonzero
normalization. For every nonzero universal coefficient pair, the projective
ideal sheaves generated by the components satisfy

    (H)~ = (F)~.

In particular, at each of the ten order-three axes where L drops rank,
the original-fourth component ideal is exactly

    m      if 2alpha+beta != 0,
    m^2    if 2alpha+beta = 0.

There is no additional coefficient direction at which these ideals change.
Every scheme-theoretic nonic base-locus conclusion therefore transfers to
this determinant-identified original-fourth D row, not merely its support.
This does not concern the degeneracy scheme of the quadratic lift R, nor
additional specialized A-kernel classes at second-symbol rank-drop points.
It does not determine the actual gate or assert its nonvanishing.

Write a=p x^2+q xy+t y^2. In the fixed universal representation model,
B(h)=Pi(a^2 h) is a four-by-three quadratic matrix, L(d)=Pi(a d) is a
four-by-five linear matrix, and N is the row of signed maximal minors of B.
Thus NB=0 and N has degree six. The torsion-free cokernel of B is identified
by N with its rank-one ideal-sheaf image in O(6). Its canonical subsheaf
J is I_Z(6), and the actual quotient-third is

    s:P O(-3) -> J,        s=[LR].

The determinant image of s is precisely F=NLR, not an arbitrary row of
matching representation type. To compose with L, twist its source map:

    D O(-4) --L--> P O(-3) --s--> J -> coker B -> O(6).

The resulting degree-ten row is H=FL. PR1025's corrected-lift calculation
proves iota beta4,M|D=-sL in coker B. Hence the determinant image of that
original fourth is -H. The sign and nonzero target normalization do not
change a component ideal. These twists distinguish the original degree-four
sheaf map from its degree-ten determinant row and from the degree-nine
nonic row of s.

We compare the ideal sheaves of these rows in their ambient target line
bundle, using local trivializations. No fiberwise tensor-injectivity of
the ideal-sheaf target at its singular points is assumed. In particular,
this theorem is not a claim about an enlarged specialized fourth source.

## 2. Why only the C3 orbit needs a new proof

The components of H=FL are local linear combinations of those of F, so
(H) is always contained in (F). Off the ten C3 axes, L has rank four and
is locally a split surjection. A local right inverse S gives F=HS, hence
(F)=(H) there. This uses local surjectivity, not just generic surjectivity.

At a C3 axis, L has rank three and no such right inverse exists. Its local
normal form has three identity rows and a last row (u,v), by the reduced
maximal-minor scheme. That normal form alone need not preserve every row
ideal. We prove the necessary assertion for the actual universal pencil
by the whole-orbit identities below.

## 3. Whole-orbit finite quotients

On the entire affine chart p=1 let I be the ideal of the maximal minors of
L. PR1036 identifies its reduced Groebner basis in lexicographic order q>t:

    q^2+(27/2)t^5+(27sqrt5/10)t^4+6t^3-3sqrt5 t^2
        -(11/2)t-57sqrt5/10,
    q t^4-q,
    t^6+(2sqrt5/3)t^5+t^4-t^2-(2sqrt5/3)t-1.

The quotient has dimension ten and is the full reduced orbit, so no C3
axis is omitted at p=0. Ordinary affine powers of I localize to m powers
at each of these points. The dimensions of the quotients by I,I^2,I^3
are ten, thirty and sixty, respectively.

Form the complete universal rows

    Fi=N L Ri,        Hi=Fi L,          i=1,2.

No root, axis representative or actual source value is substituted.
All direction variables and both coefficient coordinates remain symbolic.
Exact arithmetic over Q(sqrt5) verifies the following identities:

    H1,H2 have all components in I;
    rem_I^2(H2) = (1/2) rem_I^2(H1);                       (1)
    (components of H1)+I^2 = I;                           (2)
    (components of H1-2H2)+I^3 = I^2.                     (3)

Each equality of ideals is checked by equality of the entire reduced
Groebner bases. The verifier checks componentwise membership in the
right-hand power as well. The first remainder is nonzero; a compact
witness in the first component, for the displayed monomial order, is

    [q^2 t^3] rem_I^2(H1)_0 = -1675264/27.

All coefficients in (1) are checked, not merely this witness. Quotients of
dimension at most sixty suffice. These are bounded whole-orbit certificates,
not point samples, generic rank calculations or global elimination.

## 4. Every coefficient direction and Nakayama

Let H=alpha H1+beta H2. Equation (1) gives

    H = (alpha+beta/2) H1 modulo I^2.

For every coefficient pair off ell3, its scalar alpha+beta/2 is nonzero.
Equation (2) therefore gives (H)+I^2=I. Localizing at any C3 axis gives
(H) subset m and (H)+m^2=m. Nakayama applied to m/(H) yields (H)=m.
This proof covers the full complement of the coefficient line at once;
it does not use chosen generic coefficient values.

On ell3 a nonzero pair has beta=-2alpha and alpha nonzero. Equation (3)
then gives (H)+I^3=I^2 and (H) subset I^2. At every C3 axis, Nakayama
applied to m^2/(H) gives (H)=m^2. Thus the statement includes all higher
local terms, not only the leading homogeneous order.

PR1036/PR1037 give exactly the same ideals m off ell3 and m^2 on ell3
for F. Combining these equalities on the C3 orbit with the local right
inverse off that orbit proves equality of the projective component ideal
sheaves everywhere. No equality of raw unsaturated homogeneous ideals of
different generator degrees is asserted.

## 5. Consequences and boundary

Every established nonic component scheme transfers unchanged to the
original-fourth D row in its determinant target. For example PR1037 gives
complete lengths46 and54 on ell5 and ell3, while PR1043 gives Z disjoint
union twelve reduced conic points, length46, on ellDelta. These lengths
are inherited consequences, not new nonic classifications.

The new content is the original-fourth component-ideal bridge at the
rank-drop orbit, closing the gap left by generic surjectivity of L.
Neither the rank of R nor a numerical rank of any actual source matrix
is used. The actual coefficient pair remains unselected. For the zero
universal pair both rows vanish identically; the finite-base interpretation
is reserved for nonzero pairs.
