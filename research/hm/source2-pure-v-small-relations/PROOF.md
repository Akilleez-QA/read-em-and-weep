# The nine-dimensional branch has a nonzero P relation and a level truncation

## Theorem and scope

Assume the actual source2 pure-V hypotheses and the nine-dimensional branch
of PR1059. Thus the specified submodule generated by im h has

    F2=I2 plus P_h,   F3=I3 plus (W tensor P_h),   F4=NC4,
    I=(D,T,P),       F4=P+D.

The direct sum at degree three is the actual first-multiplication image
from PR1054. All variables and embeddings are retained. Then:

1. The quadratic descendants of P_h have NONZERO P component in I4.
   Their P component is not an optional zero graph. The extension of
   negative modules 0->I->F->F/I->0 is not split.
2. In a presentation generated by F2, the linear relation representation
   is S=Sym³W. The minimal new quadratic relation representation is
   2V+P. The last P is a graph between the P quadratic descendants of
   I2 and of P_h, with both projections nonzero.
3. Make the EXPLICIT ARTIFICIAL TRUNCATION M with M_0=F2, M_1=F3,
   M_2=F4, and M_n=0 for every n>=3. Over R=Sym W it has minimal
   equivariant resolution

    0 -> (P+D) tensor R(-4)
      -> S tensor R(-1) plus (2V+P) tensor R(-2)
                         plus S tensor R(-3)
      -> (P+D) tensor R -> M -> 0.                     (1)

   Its socle is exactly M_2, and its Hilbert numerator is
   9-4t-10t²-4t³+9t⁴.
4. A nonzero graph coefficient cannot in general be normalized by a
   Q-preserving change of generators. With fixed reference intertwiners,
   the ratio between this coefficient and Q|P_h is invariant under
   equivariant changes of presentation respecting all arrows. No value
   of that ratio is computed or asserted universal.

Statement (1) concerns only the stated truncation, not the full actual
Rao module, its degree-five descendants, or a new vanishing theorem there.
The nine-dimensional branch remains conditional and unselected.

## 2. The polynomial P descendant cannot be the balanced endpoint

Let U be the nonzero actual W' subspace of W P16 in Sym17 W. Polynomial
multiplication W tensor U -> Sym18 W is nonzero: multiplication of a
nonzero polynomial by a nonzero variable is nonzero. Its domain is the
irreducible P, hence it is injective. Write P_desc for its image.

Associativity puts P_desc inside the image of Sym²W tensor P16. The
latter domain contains P with multiplicity one. Semisimplicity therefore
identifies P_desc as precisely the image of that P summand, and proves
that this polynomial P image is nonzero. This does not identify P_desc
with I4 or P_t.

The next multiplication of P_desc factors as

    W tensor W tensor U -> Sym²W tensor U -> Sym19 W.

The middle space is the irreducible T. Polynomial multiplication is
nonzero, so its last map is injective and its image has dimension six.
Consequently W tensor P_desc -> Sym19 W has rank six and kernel W'.
If P_desc=P_t, this kernel would lie in the kernel of (3), contradicting
balancedness. Thus P_desc and P_t are distinct actual polynomial
subspaces.

The P-isotypical subspace of H_t is exactly P_t. Therefore the quotient
Sym18 W -> NC4 kills P_desc if and only if P_desc is contained in P_t,
which, since both have dimension four, is equivalent to equality.
Equality was excluded. Its image is consequently the nonzero unique
P of NC4, namely I4. This proves the forced nonzero graph assertion.

An equivariant negative splitting F=I plus F/I would have to lift the
unique P generators of F/I to P_h, since Hom(P,I2)=0. But their P
quadratic descendants meet I4 nontrivially. Hence no such splitting
exists. A nonequivariant splitting would average to an equivariant one
in characteristic zero, so it also cannot exist.

## 3. Exact minimal relations in degrees one and two

Grade F2 as degree zero. The generator space is I2 plus P_h. Its first
map is the direct sum of Sym4 W multiplication to Sym5 W and the
injective free first step W tensor P_h. Thus its only linear relations
are the literal Koszul relations

    y e_i - x e_(i+1),   i=0,1,2,3,

where e_i denotes x^(4-i)y^i in the I2 factor; multiplication by f12 is
injective. These span S. Their linear multiples inject into
Sym²W tensor I2. Indeed the bidiagonal Koszul matrix is injective as
an R-module map (successive first nonzero coordinates prove this), so
no linear syzygy among these four relations exists. Their quadratic
span is W tensor S=A+D, of dimension eight.

After dividing out that span, the degree-two free presentation is

    Sym6 W plus (Sym²W tensor P_h)
      = (V_I+P_I) plus (V_h+P_h^(2)+D_h).              (4)

The first summand maps to I4 with kernel V_I, by the actual image
system (2). The V_h summand maps to zero since NC4 has no V. The
D_h summand is an isomorphism onto the unique D by PR1059. Both
P summands map isomorphically onto I4, by Section2 and (2).
Consequently the exact kernel of (4) is

    V_I plus V_h plus graph(P),                       (5)

with both graph projections nonzero. These are minimal quadratic
relations, representation 2V+P. The two V copies refer to specified
source summands of (4), not to arbitrary equal-character subspaces.

## 4. Socle and the artificial truncation resolution

The degree-zero socle is zero: the actual first multiplication has no
joint annihilator (multiplication by any nonzero variable is injective
on the polynomial generators).

The degree-one space is the actual direct sum

    I3=T_I,   W tensor P_h=W'_h plus T_h.

Its W' component is U from Section2. Its multiplication onto I4 is
nonzero, so it is not in the socle. Any remaining invariant socle
subspace is a T inside the two-copy T isotypical part. Project its
multiplication to D in F4. This projection kills T_I, since I ends in
I4=P. It is nonzero on T_h: the nonzero quadratic D image of P_h
cannot come through W tensor W'_h=P, so it comes through T_h.
Thus the adjoint map from the T multiplicity space to the D channel
has kernel precisely the T_I line. But T_I has nonzero multiplication
to I4, by (2). No nonzero T socle subspace remains. Semisimplicity
proves the entire degree-one socle is zero.

For the artificial module M, degree two is all socle. The two-variable
Koszul complex therefore gives Tor_2^R(M,C)=(P+D) in degree four only;
the determinant representation of W is trivial. A finite-length module
over the polynomial ring in two variables has projective dimension
two, so these are all second syzygies.

The representation-valued Hilbert numerator is obtained by multiplying
[M_0]+[M_1]t+[M_2]t² by 1-[W]t+t². Its coefficients are

    (P+D), -S, -(2V+P), -S, (P+D).

Together with the just-proved Tor_2 calculation and generation in degree
zero this gives exactly (1). All entries in its differentials have
positive degree, so the resolution is minimal. No artificial cubic
relation is asserted to hold in an untruncated actual degree five.

## 5. What a Q-preserving normalization can and cannot do

Fix reference intertwiners for the I arrows, the P descendant map to
I4 and the D descendant map. Write lambda for the nonzero coefficient
of the P descendant map, and q for Q|P_h:P_h->I4, also nonzero.
The coefficient lambda is not a selected actual value.

An equivariant generator change acts by b on I2=D and by a on P_h=P;
these two inequivalent summands cannot mix. The first arrows generate
F3, and the I arrows force the induced scalar on I4 to be b. The
nonzero D descendant forces the corresponding scalar on the other
endpoint D once the source P scalar is fixed. In particular there is
no extra repeated-T freedom at F3 compatible with all first arrows.

For two presentations with coefficients (lambda,q) and (lambda',q'),
intertwining the P descendants and Q respectively gives

    b lambda = a lambda',     b q = a q'.             (6)

Hence lambda/q=lambda'/q'. If the literal Q normalization is kept,
q'=q, then a=b and lambda'=lambda. One may rescale reference
intertwiners and thereby rename these coordinates, but that is not an
operation which eliminates the actual graph map while preserving the
specified arrows and Q. Section2 forces nonvanishing, not a universal
scalar value. No assertion about extending such presentation changes
to the whole source module is needed or made.
