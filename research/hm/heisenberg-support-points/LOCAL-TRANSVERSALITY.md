# Universal local transversality at the Heisenberg eigenlines

This lemma is about every equivariant isomorphism of the representation types below. It does not construct or evaluate a selected HM coefficient matrix, source instance, numerical rank, or experiment. Applied to the pinned actual exterior model, it proves that the thirty points in PROOF.md are reduced isolated points of the support-drop scheme.

## 1. Universal intertwiner structure

Let H be the exponent-five Heisenberg group. Let A be its five-dimensional irreducible representation with nontrivial central character zeta, and let V have central character zeta^2. Let H act trivially on the two-dimensional multiplicity space W. Consider any H-equivariant isomorphism

    phi: exterior^2 A -> V tensor W.

Fix one noncentral cyclic direction. Heisenberg representation theory permits bases indexed by i,j in F5 and generators g,h with

    g e_i=zeta^i e_i,   h e_i=e_(i+1),
    g v_j=zeta^j v_j,   h v_j=v_(j+2).

The central action is zeta on A and zeta^2 on V; these formulas encode that commutator relation. Rescaling along each five-cycle makes the displayed shift coefficients one because h^5=1. The choice is only a symbolic basis in the abstract representation, not a choice of an actual source parameter.

Weight preservation and shift equivariance imply

    phi(e_i wedge e_j)=v_(i+j) tensor w_(j-i),

for vectors w_d in W depending only on d=j-i. Alternation implies w_(-d)=-w_d, and w_0=0. For any fixed sum i+j, the two distinct unordered pairs have difference classes {+/-1} and {+/-2}. Since phi is an isomorphism on that two-dimensional weight space, w_1 and w_2 are linearly independent. No values for either vector are chosen or computed. In particular w_d and w_(2d) are independent for every nonzero d in F5.

## 2. Kernel, cokernel and the normal derivative

At the eigenline x=e_0, the support contraction has domain (A/<e_0>) tensor W* and is

    sigma_x(e_b tensor lambda)=v_b lambda(w_b),  b not equal to 0.

Its image is the sum of the four V-character lines v_b for b not equal to zero. Its cokernel is the line represented by v_0. Choose any nonzero lambda_b annihilating w_b. Its kernel is the direct sum of the four lines generated by e_b tensor lambda_b.

For a bundle map of rank four from rank eight to rank five, the first-order normal derivative to the rank-at-most-four locus is the canonical map

    T_x P(A) -> Hom(ker sigma_x, coker sigma_x).

This can be obtained by choosing a nonzero four-by-four minor and differentiating the remaining one-by-four Schur-complement row. Changing local frames only adds terms that vanish after restricting to the kernel and projecting to the cokernel.

Use tangent coordinates x=e_0+sum(t_c e_c), c not equal to zero. Differentiating the universal wedge contraction in direction e_c and applying it to e_b tensor lambda_b gives

    v_(b+c) lambda_b(w_(b-c)).

Projection to the cokernel kills this unless b+c=0. For b=-c the coefficient is

    lambda_(-c)(w_(-2c)),

which is nonzero: lambda_(-c) annihilates precisely the line spanned by w_(-c), and w_(-2c) is independent of w_(-c). Thus the normal derivative pairs the four tangent-character lines with the four kernel-character lines by a permutation and four nonzero scalars. It is an isomorphism.

Consequently the four local Schur-complement equations have independent differentials in the regular local ring of P4. They generate its maximal ideal locally, so D4(sigma) is reduced and zero-dimensional in a neighborhood of x. Equivariance, or the same universal argument for each direction, proves the claim at all thirty eigenlines.

## 3. Exact strength and limitation

Unconditionally, the canonical thirty points are reduced isolated components of the actual support-drop scheme, and primary evaluation has rank exactly eight there by PROOF.md. This is not merely a statement about its underlying set.

The argument does not exclude an additional positive-dimensional component elsewhere. If the entire support-drop scheme is finite, PROOF.md's length-thirty argument proves that these thirty reduced points are the entire scheme. No expected-codimension assumption is used in the local transversality calculation itself.

The only representation input beyond PROOF.md is the standard Schrödinger model of the exponent-five Heisenberg group, described in Horrocks-Mumford (1973), Section 1. All algebra here holds for every H-equivariant isomorphism phi of the indicated type, so it does not depend on determining actual intertwiner coefficients. This is a structural mathematical proof, not formal verification and not a selected-instance computation.
