## 2. Universal tangent formula on the blowup

Fix an arbitrary character direction and nonzero u in L_i, v in L_j with i!=j. Complete v to the four character directions away from L_i. A blowup chart around the exceptional mark (i,j) has parameters t and a_m for m not in {i,j}, and its map to the affine chart of P(A) is represented by

    u + t v(a),    v(a)=v + sum_(m not in {i,j}) a_m w_m,

where w_m is any nonzero vector of L_m. The exceptional mark is t=0, a=0. Because [u] is a base node, q(u^3)=0. Universal polarization, followed by division by the exceptional factor, gives a local lift of Phi to W*:

    q((u+t v(a))^3)/(3t)
      = q(u^2 v(a)) + t q(u v(a)^2) + (t^2/3)q(v(a)^3).

No cubic coefficient or actual point is evaluated here. This is the defining polarization identity applied to the abstract quotient map.

Let ell_y=C q(u^2v). The vector space generated by this value and the first derivatives is

    U_(i,j) = span{ q(L_i^2 L_m): m!=i } + C q(L_i L_j^2).       (1)

The projective differential image is naturally Hom(ell_y,U_(i,j)/ell_y), viewed inside Hom(ell_y,W*/ell_y)=T_y P(W*). In particular it is not necessary to identify projective tangent vectors with vectors of W* without quotienting by their common value line.

Every generator in (1) is a repeated-index tensor with unequal indices; PR610 Section 5 therefore proves it is nonzero. This includes the normal derivative q(L_i L_j^2)=q(L_j^2 L_i). Nonvanishing does not require a new actual coefficient computation.

## 3. Supports intersect only at the common value

Under D the joint weight of L_a L_b L_c is (a+b+c,a^2+b^2+c^2) in F5^2. PR610 proves every repeated-index weight has exactly two tensor multisets in Sym^3 A and multiplicity one in W*. Its partner operation on the twenty ordered pairs (i,j), i!=j, is fixed-point-free and involutive. Consequently there are ten distinct such weights. Since dim W*=10, these exhaust W*; all other weights vanish in W*.

To compare an arbitrary paired fiber, relabel characters by t -> (t-i)/(j-i). This is a change of abstract labels, not an asserted geometric symmetry of the actual surface. Under t -> at+b, the joint labels transform by

    (S,Q) -> (aS+3b, a^2 Q+2abS+3b^2),

an invertible transformation. It preserves equality and intersections of character supports. Thus it suffices universally to write (i,j)=(0,1), with partner (k,l)=(4,3).

The four exceptional generators of U_(0,1), followed by its normal generator, have weights

    (1,1), (2,4), (3,4), (4,1); (2,2).

The four exceptional generators of U_(4,3), with remaining index m=0,1,2,3, followed by its normal generator, have weights

    (3,2), (4,3), (0,1), (1,1); (0,4).

These five weights in each row are distinct, so the nonzero generators are independent. The only shared weight is (1,1), which is exactly the common value line ell_y. Since every such weight space is one-dimensional,

    dim U_(i,j)=dim U_(k,l)=5,
    U_(i,j) intersect U_(k,l)=ell_y.                         (2)

Taking the quotient by ell_y proves that the two ambient branch tangent images intersect in zero. This independently recovers injectivity of the differential at these marks. Because Y embeds smoothly in M, its differential images are subspaces of the respective ambient branch tangent images. The surface tangent images therefore also intersect in zero. The argument applies at every bad value by PR610's exhaustive pairing description.

Here “transverse” means zero intersection of the two branch tangent spaces. Their sum has dimension eight in the nine-dimensional ambient tangent space, so it does not mean that their sum is all of T_y P9.

## 4. From tangent separation to the completed image ring

We give a general algebraic lemma to avoid a tangent-only inference. Let f:N->Q be a finite normalization of a reduced complex variety embedded in a smooth ambient variety P. Assume f^(-1)(y)={p1,p2}, N is smooth of dimension d at these points, and the composite maps to P have injective differentials with zero intersection of their images. Put

    A=O_Q,y,   B=(f_*O_N)_y,   R=O_P,y^,
    B_i=O_N,pi^ = C[[z_i1,...,z_id]].

The normalization inclusion A->B is injective and finite. Flatness of Noetherian completion preserves that injection. Completion of the finite semilocal algebra B at the maximal ideal of A splits into its two completed local factors: the radical of that ideal in B is the intersection of its two maximal ideals, the two adic topologies agree, and the Chinese remainder theorem gives the product decomposition. Thus

    A^ -> B^ = B_1 x B_2                                             (3)

is injective. This step in particular rules out extra infinitesimal structure in the completed image.

The composite R->B_i is surjective. Indeed injectivity on tangent spaces means surjectivity on cotangent spaces. Lift a basis of the cotangent space of B_i to elements of R. Their images form a regular system of parameters of the complete smooth ring B_i. Every power series in these parameters lifts to the same convergent formal expression in R, proving surjectivity. Write I_i for its kernel. Because R surjects onto A^ and (3) is injective, its kernel on A^ is exactly I_1 intersect I_2. Hence

    A^ = R/(I_1 intersect I_2).                                     (4)

Let m be the maximal ideal of R. The tangent spaces to R/I_i are the annihilators of (I_i+m^2)/m^2 in (m/m^2)*. Their zero intersection says

    I_1+I_2+m^2=m.

Apply Nakayama to the finite R-module m/(I_1+I_2): it equals its product with m, so it is zero. Therefore I_1+I_2=m. The usual exact sequence for two ideals now gives

    R/(I_1 intersect I_2)
       = (R/I_1) x_(R/(I_1+I_2)) (R/I_2)
       = B_1 x_C B_2.                                             (5)

This proves the asserted completed rings for d=4 and d=2. It does not assume a simultaneous linearization of both branches, an algebraization of formal coordinates, or a normal-form statement not supplied by (3)–(5).

## 5. Conductor, depth, and multiplicity

For A_d=C[[u1,...,ud]] x_C C[[v1,...,vd]] and its normalization B_d, the residue-difference map gives

    0 -> A_d -> B_d -> C -> 0,
    (f,g) -> f(0)-g(0).

The quotient has length one. Multiplication by an element of A_d on this quotient is multiplication by its common residue. Thus its annihilator, the conductor, is

    m_A = m_u direct-sum m_v,

also an ideal of B_d. Completion is faithfully flat; it detects equality of finite ideals, annihilation, and the length of finite-length modules. These computations therefore descend to the actual algebraic stalks. Globally, for either h:N->Q among Phi and g, there is an exact sequence

    0 -> O_Q -> h_*O_N -> K_Q -> 0,                            (6)

where K_Q is supported on Sigma with one-dimensional fiber at each point. Thus K_Q is noncanonically isomorphic to direct-sum_(y in Sigma) C_y and has length sixty. The conductor is exactly I_(Sigma/Q), and

    I_(Sigma/Q) = h_* I_(Omega/N),
    I_(Sigma/Q) O_N = I_(Omega/N).

The first equality uses the natural inclusion into h_*O_N. The 120-point ideal upstairs is not an invertible ideal or a divisor.

For d>=2, H^0_m(B_d)=H^1_m(B_d)=0 because both regular branches have depth d. The exact sequence above gives H^0_m(A_d)=0 and H^1_m(A_d)=C, so depth(A_d)=1. Also

    m_A^n = m_u^n direct-sum m_v^n   for n>=1,
    length(A_d/m_A^(n+1)) = 2 binom(n+d,d)-1.

Consequently the Hilbert–Samuel multiplicity is two and the embedding dimension is 2d. These completed invariants equal the corresponding invariants of the algebraic local rings. In particular these are not Cohen–Macaulay singularities for d=2 or4, nor ordinary hypersurface double points in those dimensions.

## 6. Global structure-sheaf cohomology

Both normalizations are connected smooth projective varieties, with global functions C, as are their integral images. In (6), the map from global normalization functions to K_Q is zero: a global constant has equal residue on both branches. Since a finite morphism has no higher coherent direct images, the long exact cohomology sequence yields

    0 -> C^60 -> H^1(Q,O_Q) -> H^1(N,O_N) -> 0,
    H^i(Q,O_Q) = H^i(N,O_N),  i>=2.

Blowing up smooth points leaves structure-sheaf cohomology unchanged. For M=Bl_30(P4), h^i(O_M)=(1,0,0,0,0). For Y=Bl_60(X), h^i(O_Y)=(1,0,4) by PR574. This proves the displayed cohomology tuples and

    chi(O_V)=1-60=-59,
    chi(O_T)=5-60=-55.

## 7. Exact Hilbert polynomials

Twisting (6) by O_Q(n) and using the projection formula shows

    chi(O_Q(n))=chi(O_N(nD_N))-60.                            (7)

For the surface, K_Y=b*K_X+E_Y. Pullbacks from X have zero intersection with the exceptional curves, and E_Y^2=-60. Therefore

    D_Y^2=75,
    D_Y.K_Y=(3b*H_X-E_Y).(b*K_X+E_Y)=3(15)+60=105.

Smooth surface Riemann–Roch gives chi(O_Y(nD_Y))=5+(75n^2-105n)/2. Equation (7) proves

    P_T(n)=(75n^2-105n)/2-55.

For the ambient blowup, let E_M be the sum of its thirty exceptional P3s. For n>=0, the filtration by O_M(3nH_M-jE_M), j=0,...,n, has successive exceptional quotients O_(E_M)(j), because H_M restricts trivially and O_M(-E_M)|E_z=O_P3(1). Hence

    chi(O_M(nD_M))
      = chi(O_P4(3n)) - 30 sum_(j=0)^(n-1) chi(O_P3(j))
      = binom(3n+4,4) - 30 binom(n+3,4).

Here chi(O_M(3nH_M))=chi(O_P4(3n)) follows from the blowup pushforward of O_M and vanishing higher direct images. This also follows from the standard affine blowup charts and the vanishing of H^i(P3,O(j)) for j>=0. No independence of global fat-point conditions is asserted or needed: Euler characteristics suffice. Polynomiality extends the identity to all integer n. Subtracting sixty by (7) proves the asserted P_V. Its leading coefficient is (81-30)/24=51/24, agreeing with degree51.
