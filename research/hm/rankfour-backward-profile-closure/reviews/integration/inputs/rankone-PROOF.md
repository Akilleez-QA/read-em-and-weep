# Exclusion of the rank-one restricted dual cubic image in the ordered (3,1) rank-four plateau

## Statement and assumptions

Retain the actual geometric premises and fixed physical SL2(F5) marking in the six complete original sources of the lower-image packet. Suppose the actual second fundamental form has generic rank four and maximal ordered plateau (3,1). A chosen T6 copy in H0(Q*(3)) cannot have restricted derivative image of rank one.

This is a new conditional rank-one-image exclusion. Combined with separately checked exclusions of ranks two, three and four and the nonzero-image lemma, it would exclude this ordered plateau. No generic SF>=5, square surjectivity, full-adjoint invertibility or original proof repair is asserted here. No actual coefficients, parameter values, jets, matrices or instance ranks are evaluated.

All constant-subspace and saturated-transform hypotheses are retained, not inferred from numerical data.

Let a=degF0,b=degF4 and ci=degLi. The actual determinant ledger is

 a<=-20, b<=-36; c(i+1)=ci+8+delta_i;
 a+3b=-168+delta1+2delta2+3delta3;

## Rank one forces a split rank-three kernel, not a full-annihilator identification

Since the saturation of O(9) in E*(1) is a line of degree at least9 and at most9, J is saturated. The full rank-four dual derivative image saturates to B*(1); J is a saturated subline of that bundle. Dualization gives the actual exact sequence

 0 -> N -> B -> O(-8) -> 0,

with rankN=3 and degN=c3+8. The derivative embeds B(2) as its locally free image subsheaf in Q, without asserting saturation there. Thus N(2) embeds in Q and maxdegN<=6. Since degN>=6, every ordinary degree of N is at least degN-12>=-6.

Consequently Ext1(O(-8),N)=H1(N(8))=0 even as an ordinary vector space: all degrees of N(8) are at least2. Therefore B=N+O(-8) splits equivariantly. This splitting is proved, never declared horizontal, and J is not identified with the full rank-four annihilator.

## A complete finite kernel domain

Every equivariant block R O(e) of N has dimension at most3, -6<=e<=6, and coefficient parity e modulo2. Its total rank is3 and its degree is one of6,8,10,12,14,16,18. Enumerating all unordered blocks gives53 abstract kernels. Independently enumerating each rank-three total-degree domain by ordinary degree partitions gives exactly the same set.

The actual derivative kernel sequence and H0(F0(3))=0 imply

 H0(F2(3)) <= H0(B(1))+H0(B(-1)).

## Seven low-degree cases have no endpoint

In each c3=-2 or0 case the lower two determinant defects vanish by the exact ledger. Hence L2=B(-2),L1=B(-4), and

 chi(F0(t))=chi(E(t))-chi(B(t))-chi(B(t-2))-chi(B(t-4)).

For c3=-2 the rank-five endpoint has degree-24 and slopes in[-8,-4]; the complete equivariant domain has13 elements. For c3=0 it has degree-30 and slopes in[-14,-4]; there are46 elements. In all seven cases no endpoint satisfies both exact Euler identities at twists3 and4. Thus these cases are impossible. The possible upper defect12 for c3=-2 has not been assumed zero: it does not enter this lower-filtration contradiction.

## Nine positive-degree cases violate the actual upper endpoint map

In each of the remaining nine cases delta3=0. Put Z=F4 perpendicular in H*. Then

 0 -> Z -> Q* -> L4* -> 0,
 L4=B(2), maxdegZ<=-4.

The actual endpoint derivative is Z -> L4*(-2)=B*(-4), so after twisting4 it induces H0(Z(4)) -> H0(B*). Exact Euler additivity gives

 chi(Z(4))=chi(Q*(4))-chi(B*(2)).

For every one of the nine B shapes the physical V multiplicity of the last character is1, while its multiplicity in H0(B*) is0. This uses V distinct from A; a dimension test would miss it. Nonnegative H1 can only increase the forced V multiplicity in H0(Z(4)). Thus the kernel on sections contains a V copy.

Since maxdegZ<=-4, all sections of Z(4) come from its degree minus-four top summand, and a V section copy evaluates to a saturated subbundle K=V O(-4) of Z and of the constant ambient. The actual derivative of K has zero outer component by construction, hence factors through (Z/K)(-2). Every ordinary degree of that quotient twist is at most-6, so Hom(K,(Z/K)(-2))=0. The full second fundamental form of K vanishes.

In characteristic zero its Grassmannian classifying map is therefore constant, which would force degreeK=0. But degreeK=-12. This contradiction excludes all nine remaining kernels, without imposing delta1=delta2=0. It completes the rank-one-image exclusion.
