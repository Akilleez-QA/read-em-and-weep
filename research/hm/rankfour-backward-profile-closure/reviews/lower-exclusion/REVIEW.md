## 3. Independent audit of the top-section lemma

Because S is saturated in the constant ambient bundle, K is saturated there too: the quotient is an extension of two locally free quotients. If the derivative section vanishes, beta_S vanishes on K. The derivative of K therefore factors through (S/K)(-2). Removing a line from the top coefficient space leaves all degrees of S/K at most -d, so every target degree is at most -d-2. Consequently Hom(O(-d),(S/K)(-2))=0.

Thus K has zero second fundamental form in the original constant bundle. In characteristic zero its classifying map is constant, contradicting deg K=-d<0. This does not assume a horizontal splitting of S. The proof works for a non-equivariant kernel line, so it establishes ordinary injectivity; equivariance of the map then supplies injectivity as a representation map.
