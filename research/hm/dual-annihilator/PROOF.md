# The scalar annihilator algebra of dual HM cohomology

Work over C with the normalized Horrocks–Mumford bundle F on P(V)=P^4, B=End_0(F), T=V*, S=Sym(T), and the actual graded module H_j=H^2(B(j-5)). Write I=Ann_S(H). This is the scalar polynomial annihilator, not the module of relations in a two-generator presentation of H.

We prove that S/I has Hilbert function

(1,5,15,25,15,3),

is level of socle degree five and type three, and has no quadratic relations. Its cubic annihilator space is exactly the normalizer summand A tensor W' inside Sym^3 T. In particular I requires at least five minimal quartic generators. These statements do not choose the remaining cubic Betti parameter of H or its scalar middle ghost.

## 1. Pinned structural inputs

PR482 at 30c2f32418bbe68ad5b2635ee424d698f329fe8d proves that H is generated in degree zero and has graded pieces

W', T tensor W', A* tensor Q_4, A tensor Q_4, V tensor W', W'

in degrees zero through five, and zero elsewhere. Here A=V_1 is five-dimensional, W' is the two-dimensional quotient representation, and Q_4 is the four-dimensional spin representation. The normalizer representations used below are irreducible when stated as such; the notation Q_4 is not the ordinary four-dimensional section representation.

The symmetric trace pairing on B and Serre duality give an S-compatible perfect pairing

b: H_j tensor H_(5-j) -> C.

This pairing is symmetric upon exchange of its two arguments: both cohomological degrees are two, so the cup-product sign is (-1)^(2 times 2)=+1, and trace is symmetric. Polynomial multiplication satisfies b(qh,h')=b(h,qh') whenever the degrees match. These are statements about the actual cohomological pairing, not only a symmetric Hilbert function.

Use the normalizer decompositions already employed in PR479 and PR483:

Sym^2 T=A* tensor U',
Sym^3 T=A tensor (J_5+W'),
W' tensor Q_4=U'+J_5,
Sym^2 W'=U'.

Here U' has dimension three and J_5 dimension five; W' is self-dual. In particular A* tensor U', A tensor J_5, V tensor U', and U' are irreducible. Different displayed quotient labels at the same nontrivial Heisenberg weight are inequivalent.

## 2. Scalar annihilators through degree three

Because H is generated by H_0, the kernel of the actual action

rho_d: S_d -> Hom(H_0,H_d)

is precisely I_d. Indeed an element killing H_0 kills every polynomial multiple of H_0. Conversely an annihilator kills H_0.

In degree zero the action is nonzero. In degrees one and two the source is irreducible, and the action is nonzero because generation implies that S_d tensor H_0 surjects onto the nonzero space H_d. Thus I_0=I_1=I_2=0.

In degree three the source and target have decompositions

S_3=A tensor (J_5+W'),
Hom(H_0,H_3)=A tensor (U'+J_5).

Equivariance forces the A tensor W' source summand into the kernel. The remaining A tensor J_5 summand acts nontrivially, because the total action cannot vanish on the nonzero H_3 generated from H_0. Irreducibility makes its kernel zero. Consequently

I_3=A tensor W', dim I_3=10,
dim (S/I)_3=25.

This conclusion determines a scalar annihilator subspace, not the optional fifteen-dimensional cubic relation space in the presentation of H.

## 3. Symmetry determines the last two action spaces

For q in S_4, define the trilinear form

f_q(x,w,w')=b(qw,xw'), for x in T and w,w' in H_0.

Commutativity and compatibility with b give

b(qw,xw')=b(xqw,w')=b(w',xqw)=b(qw',xw).

Thus f_q is symmetric in w,w'. The map q -> f_q is equivariant and takes values in

T* tensor Sym^2(H_0*) = V tensor U'.

Its kernel is I_4: if f_q=0, then qw is orthogonal to every xw', and T tensor H_0 -> H_1 is onto. The perfect pairing H_4 tensor H_1 then gives qw=0 for all w. The converse is immediate. The map is nonzero because H_4 is generated from H_0 and is nonzero. Its irreducible target V tensor U' therefore makes it onto. Hence dim(S/I)_4=15.

Similarly q in S_5 defines

g_q(w,w')=b(qw,w').

The same calculation shows that g_q is symmetric, so it lies in Sym^2(H_0*)=U'. Its kernel is I_5 by the perfect pairing H_5 tensor H_0. Generation and nonvanishing of H_5 imply that this map is nonzero. Irreducibility of U' then gives dim(S/I)_5=3.

For d>=6, S_d kills H_0, since H_d=0, and therefore kills H. Together with section 2, this proves the entire stated Hilbert function. No decomposition of the full space Sym^5 T, numerical matrix, or evaluation at a selected HM instance is needed.

## 4. A canonical inverse system and levelness

Let U be the subspace of S_5* spanned by the functionals

p -> b(pw,w'), for w,w' in H_0.

The preceding section gives dim U=3 and identifies it with the dual of U', hence with U'. For q in S_d, with 0<=d<=5, membership in the homogeneous inverse-system annihilator of U means

b(pqw,w')=0 for all p in S_(5-d) and all w,w' in H_0.

By compatibility, this is b(qw,pw')=0. Since S_(5-d)H_0=H_(5-d) and the complementary pairing is perfect, it is equivalent to qw=0 for every w. This is precisely q in I_d. Degrees above five are automatically annihilators. Thus I is the homogeneous apolar annihilator of this canonical three-dimensional space of quintic functionals.

For completeness, levelness also follows directly without any inverse-system classification theorem. If a homogeneous q is nonzero in (S/I)_d for d<5, there exist w,w' and p in S_(5-d) with b(pqw,w') nonzero. Expressing p as a linear combination of products of linear forms, at least one product has this property. Its first factor x has xq nonzero in S/I. Therefore q is not in the socle. All of (S/I)_5 lies in the socle and has dimension three. Hence S/I is level of socle degree five and type three.

## 5. A necessary quartic generator space

There are no annihilators below degree three, while dim I_3=10 and

dim I_4=dim S_4-15=70-15=55.

The space of degree-four consequences of lower-degree annihilators is T I_3, whose dimension is at most dim(T tensor I_3)=50. Thus

dim (I/S_+I)_4=dim I_4-dim T I_3 >=5.

In particular the ten cubic scalar annihilators cannot generate I in degree four. This is an intrinsic obstruction to replacing the full scalar annihilator by its cubic part. It does not assert an exact quartic generator count, absence of higher annihilator generators, or a presentation of I.
