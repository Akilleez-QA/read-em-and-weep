# The residual P projection reduces the generated endpoint to nine or sixteen

## Theorem and boundary

Retain the actual source2 hypotheses and the conditional pure-V canonical
homotopy h. Let F be the negative submodule generated by im h, and P_h its
canonical degree-two P summand, as in PR1054. Fix any genuine constant
separation N=N0 direct-sum NR from PR790. Retain the ACTUAL exact sequence
of fixed-variable pencils from PR795,

    0 -> NC -> NR --pi--> N8 -> 0.                         (1)

There are precisely the following necessary alternatives for the generated
endpoint; no alternative is asserted realized by an actual source:

- If P_h is contained in NC2, then F4=NC4=P+D and dim F4=9.
- If its N0 projection is nonzero, or if that projection is zero but
  pi2(P_h) is nonzero, then dim F4=16 and
  F=I direct-sum Free_<=2(P_h) as NEGATIVE modules.

Thus, for any hypothetical actual source satisfying the hypotheses,

    dim F4 belongs to {9,16},
    dim F4=9 if and only if P_h is contained in NC2.         (2)

The nine-dimensional branch is not excluded by the exact middle dimension
fourteen. In that branch F/I is the negative system

    (P_h, W tensor P_h, D_C),

whose quadratic map is the nonzero unique D quotient of A tensor P_h,
where A=Sym²W and D_C=NC4/I4. Its quadratic relation module is exactly
V+P. The possible graph of the P relation into I4 remains unselected.
Also h kills all of NC4 in this branch, since F4 is contained in ker h.

These statements are conditional on actual projection maps. Neither
projection value, endpoint parameter nor actual HM coefficient is chosen.
The splitting in the sixteen-dimensional case is negative-module only:
Q maps P_h isomorphically across to I4. No residual horizontal splitting,
Q-stable direct sum, or source exclusion follows.

## 2. The endpoint-contained branch has a forced D descendant

Suppose P_h is contained in NC2. It must be the unique P16 constituent
of Sym16 W. Let a:N0->NC be any nonzero A-channel radical map of PR794,
factored through its actual image. Its first map is injective on
N0,2=Sym8 W=P0+D0. Therefore its restriction P0->P16=P_h is an
isomorphism, by the actual injection and the target's multiplicity one.
No source D copy is identified with I2 in this step.

PR1058 proves injectivity of the universal map

    theta0:A tensor P0 -> N0,4.

Its domain is the multiplicity-free V+P+D, while N0,4=V+2P+D has a
unique D. Hence its D restriction is nonzero and maps ONTO that unique
D. PR794 proves the fourth A-channel map a4 is an isomorphism on that
same actual D constituent. Consequently a4 theta0 has a nonzero D
component in NC4.

Because a is a map of the fixed pencils, its square with every quadratic
commutes. Thus a4 theta0 is precisely the quadratic multiplication of
the embedded P_h, after the isomorphism a2|P0. Its D image therefore
fills the unique D in NC4. Meanwhile F already contains I4, the unique
P in NC4. All descendants remain in NC since it is a submodule. Hence

    F4=NC4=P+D.                                          (5)

This is a nonvanishing argument through a specified pencil map; matching
P or D characters alone would not prove it. In particular the smaller
alternative F4=I4 cannot occur in this branch.

Modulo I4, the actual quadratic map A tensor P_h->F4/I4 is the surjection
onto D_C. Since A tensor P=V+P+D, its kernel is V+P and its D restriction
is an isomorphism. With the first two spaces from PR1054, associativity
identifies F/I with the system stated in the theorem. The V relation
cannot map to I4 by equivariance; its P relation may have an actual graph
component into I4. No value or vanishing of that scalar is asserted.

The middle space is still W'+2T of dimension fourteen, inside NC3 of
dimension eighteen. There is no contradiction to the fixed first-arrow
geometry: PR1054 proves injectivity of W tensor P_h and disjointness
from I3 under this hypothesis as well. We do not convert this compatibility
into an assertion that the branch is actually realized.

## 3. A universal injectivity certificate in the degree-eight quotient

We now prove that the unique P in N8,2 generates ALL of N8,4 by quadratic
multiplication. Use the monomial bases x^(n-i)y^i and K=Q(sqrt5).
Let Pi6:Sym6 W->P be the pinned four-row projection, and let G6 be the
apolar Gram matrix with antidiagonal entries (-1)^i/binom(6,i).
Then E6=G6^(-1) Pi6^t embeds P* into Sym6 W with image its unique P.
The checker verifies Pi6 E6 invertible, all pinned group intertwining
identities, and invariance of G6. Self-duality identifies the image type
with P; no actual P_h is identified with this coordinate space yet.

Let Delta_2,n:Sym(n+2) W->Sym2 W tensor Symn W denote complete
polarization. On x^(n+2-k)y^k its coefficient at tensor row
j*(n+1)+(k-j) is

    binom(n+2-k,2-j) binom(k,j),

whenever 0<=k-j<=n. Multiplication composed with Delta_2,n is
binom(n+2,2) times the identity. In particular Delta_2,4 E6 embeds the
unique P constituent of A tensor Sym4 W: the Clebsch decomposition is
Sym6+Sym4+Sym2, and only Sym6 contains P.

The quotient in (4) is identified with Sym6 W plus Sym4 W by

    C6(a tensor f)=((a,f)_1,(a,f)_2),                     (6)

where the two ordinary transvectants are

    (a,f)_k=sum_j (-1)^j binom(k,j)
        (partial_x^(k-j) partial_y^j a)
        (partial_x^j partial_y^(k-j) f).

The checker proves C6 Delta_2,6=0 and multiplication times Delta_2,6
is28 Id_9. The matrix C6 has rank twelve. Its kernel is therefore exactly
the nine-dimensional polarization image, so (6) is an actual quotient
identification, not merely a representation isomorphism of the same size.

Form the twelve-by-twelve matrix M as follows. Its columns are indexed
by a negative quadratic b in x²,xy,y² and by a column of E6. Apply
Delta_2,4 E6, multiply its SECOND polynomial factor by b, and then apply
C6. Rows are the seven degree-six coefficients followed by the five
degree-four coefficients. Exact arithmetic gives

    det M = -427490120879308800000000000000000 != 0.        (7)

Thus the genuine universal map

    theta8:A tensor P8 -> N8,4

is an isomorphism, with P8 the unique P in N8,2. This is a bounded
universal representation calculation, not an evaluated rank of an actual
source matrix. The two A factors in its construction have different
roles: one is the fixed coefficient factor in Q8=A O(6); the other is
the negative quadratic direction. They are not silently identified in

## 4. Transport through the genuine residual quotient

Assume the N0 projection of P_h is zero, so P_h is contained in NR2,
but pi2|P_h is nonzero. The unique P in N8,2 makes this map an isomorphism
onto P8. Naturality of the actual exact sequence (1) gives

    pi4 T_a = T_a^8 pi2.

Hence A tensor P_h->F4->N8,4 is theta8 composed with an isomorphism
on its source factor, and is an isomorphism. Since I4 is contained in
NC4, pi4 kills I4. Therefore the surjection of PR1054

    A tensor P_h -> F4/I4

is also injective. It is an isomorphism, and dim F4=4+12=16.
The actual quadratic descendants of P_h meet I4 trivially. Together
with PR1054's exact first two spaces, associativity proves
F=I direct-sum Free_<=2(P_h) as negative modules, just as in PR1058.
This argument uses only the quotient maps in (1), not a choice of a
horizontal splitting NR=NC plus N8.

If pi2|P_h is zero, exactness instead gives P_h contained in NC2, so
Section2 applies. These are exhaustive alternatives because P_h is
irreducible. Adding the nonzero-N0 branch of PR1058 proves (2).

## 5. Consequences and unresolved boundary

The eight necessary endpoint dimensions in PR1054 reduce to9 or16.
Equivalently any generated endpoint smaller than sixteen is exactly
NC4 and has dimension nine; it is not the smallest P-only endpoint.
In that branch h(NC4)=0 because F4 is contained in ker h. The conditional
formal-rank36 conclusion in PR1054 that required F4=I4 cannot be invoked:
its antecedent is now ruled out under these source2 hypotheses.

This does NOT assert that the original generic rank is different from36,
or that formal D persistence is impossible by another mechanism. Neither
remaining branch is selected or excluded. No actual projection value,
P graph relation, endpoint parameter or invariant Q restriction is
computed. Repeated D representations remain their specified actual
subspaces. The degree-one W-generated string is not used.

The pure-V radical j4 acts from the separated N0 block into NC and
kills NR, as in PR792/PR795. It is not a nonzero row on the N8 quotient.
Thus Qh=j4 supplies no automatic vanishing of the P component of the
N8 descendants in Section4. In fact j4 T_a P_h=0 already follows from
negative intertwining and j2(P_h)=0. No stronger projection exclusion
is inferred from this row.
