# The actual mixed obstruction commutes with every background KS direction

## Statement and scope

Assume precisely the universal homogeneous primitive/contraction, determinant normalization, covering, rational-denotation and raw-transgression premises of the inherited compatible-family, source-domain and structural raw-closure proofs listed below. Let E_s be the accepted background translation family on the fixed X=C^4, with x_d=z_d+s_d and fixed root data in z,y. Let R_s be its SAME literal mixed second-star residual, in the source's first-index row-End convention. Then for each background tangent c, with KS cocycle a^c, there is a regular degree-two cochain

    B^c_ijk = dot R^c_ijk - R_ijk a^c_ik

satisfying

    δ B^c = a^c cup R - R cup a^c.

Consequently

    [κ(c), [R]]_graded = 0 in Ext^3_X(E_0,E_0).

The degrees are one and two, so the graded sign is plus in the exponent and the commutator is the difference of the two products. Under the accepted injectivity theorem for the translation KS map, [R] therefore commutes with four linearly independent Ext^1 classes. This is a necessary structural constraint on the mixed obstruction class under the stated premises. It proves neither [R]=0 nor [R]≠0, no detector value or response, and no independent H24 freedom. It does not claim a flat connection on cohomology.

If the identification of row-End matrices with the chosen column model uses transposition, transport that identification and its opposite multiplication consistently. Vanishing of this commutator survives the resulting uniform convention/sign change; the explicit formula below is for the source row convention.

## 1. The actual family is closed, not only its central fiber

The source-domain theorem proves uniform cleared regularity of the actual source formula over the one-factor translation family. It explicitly does NOT by itself assert differentiated closure. Closure follows instead by combining its regularity with the parameterwise identities in the compatible-family and raw-closure proofs.

Use fixed coordinates z_d,y_d on X and the tangent family x_d=z_d+t c_d. The physical V_d and the root frames are independent of t, and [∂_t,V_d]=0. The coefficient map v=e(x) varies. Every primitive, transition, raw block, raw scalar and physical derivative uses this varying coefficient map. Universal section/contraction/determinant identities pull back along this family. The actual moving carrier/legal cover is retained.

In particular the inherited raw-transgression identity holds over the parameter, and gives BOTH first-star lifts U_p(t),U_q(t), for p=(0,1),q=(2,3), obeying

    U_r,jk g_ij + g_jk U_r,ij + μ_r(g_jk,g_ij)
      = U_r,ik + b_r,ijk g_ik.

The composite-edge term U_r,ik is essential and is included. Here

    b_(a,b),ijk = (15/4) q_a,ij q_b,jk,
    q_d,ij = (m_j,d-m_i,d)y_d/z_d.

These q and b are t-independent in the fixed-root coordinates. They retain their own-factor support, so V_2 b_p=V_3 b_p=V_0 b_q=V_1 b_q=0 throughout the family.

The structural raw-closure proof, §§7–8, is an algebraic identity for arbitrary such transitions, first lifts and commuting derivations, before the final support substitution. In its notation the actual residual satisfies

    R_t = tf(M_t) + δ_t W_t,
    δ_t R_t = tf(g_il(t)^-1 A_ijkl(t)),

where

    A_ijkl = μ_p(g_kl,b_q,ijk)g_ik + μ_q(g_kl,b_p,ijk)g_ik
             -g_jl μ_p(b_q,jkl,g_ij) - g_jl μ_q(b_p,jkl,g_ij).

Every term of A differentiates a gerbe in its complementary physical pair. Thus A=0 identically over t and δ_t R_t=0. This is not an inference from closure at t=0. It retains the full mixed defect, both raw scalars, both Fk terms, all E terms, and the ordinary scalar-gerbe convention. It is restricted to the stated disjoint mixed sector; no same-pair conclusion follows.

For all four tangents, repeat the factorwise compatible construction or substitute x_d=z_d+t c_d directly. The proof uses only the separate fixed root factors, homogeneous pullback, the common split arrows and commuting V_d. No numerical bound for a newly enlarged multivariable source is needed: regularity on the actual opens follows directly from those regular local ingredients and quotient-rule differentiation. The one-factor owned bounds are stronger evidence of regularity where stated, but are not silently asserted as new four-parameter bounds.

## 2. A genuine regular fixed cover for differentiation

Restrict the actual family to Spec k[t]/(t^2). Choose a finite affine refinement of its central carrier/legal cover, retaining owner labels and each actual frame. Since a nilpotent thickening has the same underlying topological space, the thickening of each such affine open lies in the corresponding family open. Every denominator used there has invertible central reduction and hence is invertible on the thickening. The family matrices and residual coefficients are therefore regular over the dual numbers on these fixed opens and their overlaps.

The vector bundle is trivialized there by its actual family quotient frames. The coefficient derivative means the t coefficient in those frames, with underlying z,y coordinates fixed. Thus dot R and dot g are regular sections in the central local frames. This is a local coefficient derivative, not an assumed globally defined derivative End(E_t)→End(E_0), and not a connection on H^2. Changes of transition and source frames are exactly accounted for below.

Since X is separated, finite intersections of this affine cover are affine. Its Cech complex computes H^*(X,End(E_0)), and local freeness identifies this with Ext^*(E_0,E_0). Products of regular coefficients remain regular. No meromorphic global primitive or unproved product neighborhood of the reduced cover is used.

## 3. Source conventions and the derivative of closure

Write G_ij=g_ij(0), and define

    T_ij(M)=G_ij^-1 M G_ij,
    a_ij=G_ij^-1 dot g_ij.

The source convention is g_jk g_ij=g_ik. Differentiating this relation gives

    a_ik=a_ij+T_ij a_jk,

so δa=0. Transport satisfies T_ij T_jk=T_ik. For a degree-two cochain,

    (δR)_ijkl=T_ij R_jkl-R_ikl+R_ijl-R_ijk.

All cochains are expressed in their first vertex's frame. Direct differentiation of conjugation gives

    ∂_t(g_ij^-1 M(t)g_ij)|_0
      = T_ij dot M + (T_ij M)a_ij-a_ij(T_ij M).

Differentiating the parameterwise identity δ_t R_t=0 therefore gives

    (δ dot R)_ijkl
      = a_ij(T_ij R_jkl)-(T_ij R_jkl)a_ij.          (1)

Equation (1) alone is NOT the standard cup commutator. Its second term has the wrong endpoint placement for R cup a.

The derivative in (1) is the derivative of the entire actual source. It includes moving primitives, normalization inverses/adjugates, all raw scalars and blocks, every physical derivative, and moving chart/frame coefficients. In inverse-x coordinates the horizontal derivative and odd-basis derivative must both be used as in the source-domain theorem. The fixed-z formulation gives a simple definition of that same complete derivative; it does not freeze any unproved parameter-dependent leaf.

## 4. Exact endpoint correction

Define a regular degree-two cochain h_ijk=R_ijk a_ik, using the composite edge from the first to the last vertex. Then

    (δh)_ijkl
      = (T_ij R_jkl)(T_ij a_jl)-R_ikl a_il
        +R_ijl a_il-R_ijk a_ik.

The cocycle identities give

    T_ij a_jl=a_il-a_ij,
    -R_ikl+R_ijl=R_ijk-T_ij R_jkl,
    a_il-a_ik=T_ik a_kl.

Substitution, with matrix order unchanged, yields

    (δh)_ijkl
      = -(T_ij R_jkl)a_ij+R_ijk(T_ik a_kl).        (2)

Subtract (2) from (1). With B=dot R-h,

    (δB)_ijkl
      = a_ij(T_ij R_jkl)-R_ijk(T_ik a_kl).        (3)

The transported Alexander–Whitney product is

    (u cup v)_(i0...i(p+q))
      = u_(i0...ip) T_(i0 ip)(v_(ip...i(p+q))).

Thus (3) is exactly δB=a cup R-R cup a. This product satisfies the differential Leibniz rule and represents the Yoneda/composition product under the locally free Cech model. Hence the claimed Ext^3 commutator vanishes.

Although R is tracefree, B need not be tracefree: the product R_ijk a_ik can have scalar part. This does not affect the asserted Ext^3(End) result. Replacing B by its tracefree part proves only the tracefree projection of (3); one must not present that projected formula as the unprojected identity without its scalar term.

## 5. Limits and source ledger

A regular extension of a cohomology representative along a bundle deformation is substantive input. The theorem does not state that every class has such an extension. Nor does it assert δ dot R=0: equation (1) states its exact failure. The endpoint correction converts that failure to the genuine graded commutator boundary. No cohomology parallel transport or constancy of [R_t] is assumed or established.

Four independent KS classes do not by themselves force their degree-two common graded centralizer to vanish. There is no supplied computation of that centralizer, of Ext multiplication, or of [R]. Thus this centralizer argument alone does not decide a mixed second-star lifting question.

## 6. Serre-pairing consequence, without identifying any concrete detector

Since X is a product of four smooth elliptic curves, its canonical bundle is trivial. Fix any nonzero volume form to identify the Serre trace H^4(O_X) with k. Serre duality gives the perfect pairing

    Ext^2(E,E) × Ext^2(E,E) → k,  (u,v) ↦ tr(u v).

For any b in Ext^1(E,E), multiply the proved relation aR−Ra=0 by b and take trace. Graded cyclicity, with degrees |a|=|b|=1 and |R|=2, gives

    tr(a R b) = (-1)^(1·3) tr(R b a) = -tr(R b a),
    0 = tr((aR−Ra)b) = -tr(R(ba+ab)).

Therefore [R] is orthogonal to every polarized Yoneda product ab+ba where a is a background KS class and b is any Ext^1 class. Equivalently, if W=im κ and

    P_W=span{ab+ba : a∈W, b∈Ext^1(E,E)} ⊂ Ext^2(E,E),

then [R] lies in the Serre annihilator P_W^⊥. This statement is unchanged by rescaling the chosen volume form. It asserts neither the dimension nor nontriviality of P_W, and identifies none of the concrete retained scalar tests with this Yoneda image. In particular it does not eliminate a named detector or imply an evaluated pairing. Products with b also in W may already vanish by the ordinary polarized background-family relations; no additional dimension is inferred from those pairs.

Thus the cohomological commutator vanishing in this note is not an additional restriction on [R]. Its distinct content is the regular literal-family cochain B=dot R−R a_endpoint, including actual parameterwise closure and frame variation.

In particular P_W=0. Therefore P_W^⊥=Ext²(E,E), and Section 6 imposes no detector restriction at all. Neither the dimension of W nor the number of retained detector labels changes that conclusion. For a full-sign class the relevant Serre partners are invariant, as the all-character theorem already covers. This is a root-cause exclusion of this proposed test, not evaluation of a negative detector or a new mixed-vanishing proof.
