For the exact frozen `raw_engine.PhysicalModel.leaf` and `compiler.Lift.homotopy`, the undifferentiated End homotopy adds at most **carrier 6, legal 0**, per selected owner occurrence. The former legal allowance 3 is unnecessary for this operator. This is an all-chart theorem in the actual product/root-line model, including infinity, rather than an inference from a legal-mask-free finite formula.

    J0 <= 36/20, J1 <= 42/20, J2 <= 48/20, J3 <= 54/20.

Using also the accepted whole-projector contraction identity, the operator has the stronger bound **carrier 4, legal 0**, and the sequence becomes

    J0 <= 36/20, J1 <= 40/20, J2 <= 44/20, J3 <= 48/20.

This stronger step bounds the whole E=IP using its proved equal expression; it does not assign carrier order 2 to the uncombined product's intermediate arithmetic.

The previously chosen clearing weights 54/29 and standard-factor exponent 5251 remain unchanged. They still dominate these improved proof bounds. The same cleared-suffix implication follows with those unchanged weights; an optional smaller mathematical clearing certificate is stated below without changing any partition or program.

No actual matrix product, primitive polynomial expansion, physical-source evaluation, source import is used. ## 2. Why the unweighted h is the actual geometric homotopy

The accepted product-root theorem fixes the actual geometry, not just numerical powers. On X=C^4, let N be the exterior product of O_C(O); let f be the product x-map followed by the coefficient morphism to P4. Its normalized isomorphism N^2=f*O(1) identifies the actual four-term complex with

    K=f*L tensor N,
    L^j=O(j)^(d_j),  j=-1,0,1,2,
    (d_-1,d_0,d_1,d_2)=(5,15,10,2).

In particular K^j=N^(2j+1)^(d_j). The accepted homogeneous primitive theorem gives degree -1 to H0,H1,eta, degree 0 to W,L, column degrees (0,1) to I, and row degrees (0,-1) to P. Its homogeneous contraction is therefore a contraction of L, and pulling back and tensoring N gives its actual contraction of K.

Let A be any of the 16 standard product charts, with infinity parameters t_i=1/x_i on its infinity factors. Write

    tau_A=product_(i in A)t_i,
    ell_A=tau_A^-1 ell_ref,    v_A=tau_A v_ref.

Here ell_A is the regular square-line frame of N^2 on the entire pulled-back standard chart. Let e_m=nu_m e_ref be the actual legal frame of N, and put

    c_(m,A)=e_m^2/ell_A=nu_m^2 tau_A.                          (3)

The accepted bridge proves that c_(m,A) is an actual regular unit on each legal/standard intersection. Factorwise its values are

    finite chart:   bit 0 -> 1,       bit 1 -> (x^2-1)/x;
    infinity chart: bit 0 -> t,       bit 1 -> 1-t^2.

The infinity/bit-0 legal open excludes t=0; the infinity/bit-1 unit remains nonzero at the origin. Thus this comparison includes all permitted branch and infinity points, without adjoining a square root of tau_A.

In the actual e_m-power frames of K, the homotopy coefficient is

    h_(m,A)=c_(m,A) h_c(v_A),                                 (4)

for each of H0,H1,eta. This is the correct coefficient of a map N^(2j+1) to N^(2j-1). In the common rational e_ref-power comparison, it becomes

    nu_m^(2j-1) [c_(m,A) h_c(v_A)] nu_m^-(2j+1)
      =nu_m^-2 c_(m,A) h_c(v_A)
      =tau_A h_c(v_A)
      =h_c(v_ref).                                           (5)

The last equality is the accepted degree -1 homogeneous chart law. This proves that the frozen unweighted leaf is exactly the same homotopy in the original rational resolution frame. The calculation works for all three arrows; j has canceled.

Likewise the actual e_m-frame inclusion and projection have coefficients

    I_(m,A)=I_c(v_A)diag(1,c_(m,A)^-1),
    P_(m,A)=diag(1,c_(m,A))P_c(v_A).

Their composite is I_c(v_A)P_c(v_A), of homogeneous degree zero, and agrees with (1). Thus the formal D cancellation is geometrically the cancellation of the intermediate quotient basis, not an unsupported renormalization of the physical construction.

## 3. Global extension across legal divisors and root-twist cancellation

The common root twist disappears canonically from strict endomorphisms:

    Hom(f*L^u tensor N, f*L^v tensor N)
        =Hom(f*L^u,f*L^v).

Every degree-r block consequently has line factor f*O(r)=N^(2r), independently of the common N. In particular h has the square-line inverse factor N^-2, and E has degree zero. A coefficient of h relative to ell_A^-1 is exactly h_c(v_A); (4) is only its representation using the legal e_m^-2 line frame.

For each carrier, the accepted homogeneous primitive theorem supplies explicit global homogeneous clearance: its own carrier H_c squared clears every H0,H1,I,P entry, and H_c clears eta. This theorem includes the auxiliary v0=0 divisor. Pulling back those polynomial cleared sections by the coefficient morphism proves regularity on all standard charts. Therefore H_c^2 h extends over the entire product beyond the legal chart, as a section of its correct line bundle. No legal factor is needed. The same argument gives H_c^4 E regular globally in its degree-zero End bundle.

This is the decisive extra argument beyond finite legal-mask independence. A finite formula alone would not exclude an omitted infinity pole. Here the exact source is identified with an already defined homogeneous section and that section has an explicit all-chart carrier-only clearance. Any v0 pole is owned by the carrier according to that theorem. Powers of t pull back with their actual ramification at O; no order-one valuation for t on C is asserted.

More explicitly, for any two standard charts A,B let lambda=tau_B/tau_A on their overlap. Then

    h_B=lambda^-1 h_A,   E_B=E_A.

Both terms of (2), applied to a degree-r coefficient F_B=lambda^r F_A, have the same law lambda^(r-1). In the actual legal frame, a degree-r input has coefficient F_m=c_(m,A)^-r F_A. Since h_m=c_(m,A)h_A and E_m=E_A, the full operator satisfies

    H_m(F_m)=c_(m,A)^(1-r) H_A(F_A),

which is exactly the degree-(r-1) Hom-frame rule. Thus bare H_A is not being mistaken for its actual e_m-frame coefficient. These are identities of rational bundle maps, and the cleared maps just constructed are regular. Hence the agreement extends to every overlap. There is no extra legal-frame factor left to add to the bound.

For full-sign coefficients on Y=(P1)^4, the accepted deck-line dictionary gives b_B=lambda^(r-2)b_A, after removing the four odd factors y_i/u_i. Multiplication by h multiplies this by lambda^-1, so (2) gives lambda^(r-3), exactly the full-sign degree-(r-1) line. The primitives and E depend only on the x-coordinate coefficient vector and hence preserve every deck character. The h operation does not create an odd factor or divide by one. The full-sign assertion applies when the input's full-sign membership is already established; no membership claim for a new residual is made here.

## 4. Old squared frames and connections introduce no correction

The old squared legal frame is ell_m=product ell_(m_i), where ell_0=1 and ell_1=x. It differs from the actual root square by

    e_m^2=h_m ell_m,    h_m=product(1-x_i^-2)^(m_i).

The accepted bridge retains this factor. The old connection in the actual e_m frame is alpha_m=(1/2)dlog h_m, and its common-reference coefficient is alpha_m-dlog nu_m. These facts are essential for differentiated source blocks.

The present operator is undifferentiated. Changing the frame of a bundle map uses only the tensor/Hom transition ratios; no derivative of a ratio is taken. Thus (4)–(5) and the canonical End identification already account for the old/actual square-line discrepancy. There is no dlog nu_m, alpha_m, q, or derivative of h_m in (1)–(2). An old-flat-frame expression and an actual-legal-frame expression must not be compared without their line factor, but the actual factor is precisely (3)–(5), and it cancels as stated.

Accordingly this result does not justify assigning legal order zero to a derivative of h in a specified nontrivial connection, a differentiated HPL kernel, the B blocks, or a Ward divergence. Those are different operators. The absence of derivative syntax is verified in the frozen `Lift.homotopy`, but syntax alone is not the proof of the geometric comparison; §§2–3 supply it.

    h_o <= (2,0),    E_o <= (4,0).

The first branch of (2) adds at most (2,0); the second at most (4,0)+(2,0)=(6,0), all charged to that same occurrence o. The accepted structural-A theorem proves, and the accepted homogeneous extension places on every chart, the identity

    gamma H0 + IP + H1 delta = Id_(K^0).

Consequently the exact E returned by the compiler also equals

    E=Id_(K^0)-gamma H0-H1 delta.                              (6)

The homogeneous gamma and delta maps are polynomial and need no carrier or legal denominator. H0 and H1 are each cleared by the square of the same owner's carrier. Substituting this bound for the WHOLE E in the second branch of (2) gives at most (2,0)+(2,0)=(4,0). The first branch remains at most (2,0). Thus the actual returned homotopy adds at most **(4,0)**. This uses a specific, already proved all-chart contraction identity, not an assumed unknown cancellation, a finite evaluation, or a changed source.
