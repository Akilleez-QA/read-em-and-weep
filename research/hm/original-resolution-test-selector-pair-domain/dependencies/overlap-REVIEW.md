## 2. Exact frame and scalar calculation

For complex degree u, the line weight is k_u=2u+1. The accepted actual split-frame identification gives

    Gamma_i,k = B_i,k + k beta_i,
    beta_i = -m_i,d y_d/(2x_d).

Consequently

    DeltaGamma_ij,k = B_j,k-B_i,k-(k/2)q_ij,d.

On F:K^u -> K^v, the target contribution minus the source contribution has scalar coefficient

    -(k_v/2)q F + (k_u/2)Fq = -(v-u)q F = -r q F.

This is an ordinary degree-zero connection action. A Koszul sign in its right action would be incorrect. At r=-1 the coefficient is +q, and at r=0 it is zero. The producer keeps both raw-block endpoint actions; it does not replace their difference by a scalar commutator.

Under one common change of ambient coefficient frame T_k, every Gamma_i,k receives the same derivative term -(V T_k)T_k^-1. This term cancels before any estimate is applied. The difference therefore transforms tensorially, and on the globally defined even Hom line its action obeys

    A_B(lambda^r F_A)=lambda^r A_A(F_A).

No square root of lambda is required. The accepted raw B theorem already retains and cancels the common ambient-row derivative in the split-minus-old comparison; the present subtraction does not drop the old connection or reset it at infinity.

The conclusion is specific to the difference. For example beta_i/z=-m_i/(2t) in an infinity representation, whereas the bit-one legal section is 1-t^2. That legal section cannot pay a t pole by itself. An overlap with nonzero q contains an actual bit-zero endpoint, which supplies the missing legal section. This does not establish, or need, an identical own-owner certificate for every individual Gamma_i.

## 3. All-chart q ownership and the operator bound

The local legal representatives are

    finite:   bit0 -> 1, bit1 -> x(x^2-1),
    infinity: bit0 -> t, bit1 -> 1-t^2.

For q=z kappa,

    kappa_finite=(m_j-m_i)/x,
    kappa_infinity=(m_j-m_i)/t.

Equal bits give q=0. Unequal bits give exactly one bit-one endpoint and one bit-zero endpoint. The finite bit-one factor clears kappa to (m_j-m_i)(x^2-1); the infinity bit-zero factor clears it to m_j-m_i. These are the actual endpoints of this overlap. At infinity the factor t is the bit-zero endpoint's legal section, not a new independently borrowed standard divisor. Its ramification multiplicity on C is not changed.

Each B_i F or F B_i term adds at most 5/1 at i alone, and the corresponding j terms add at j alone. The scalar q term needs carrier zero and legal one at the indicated endpoint. A common certificate for the sum thus adds 5/1 at each endpoint. These are five additive summands; multiplying their allowances together would be unnecessary. Conversely, powers from the new operator and an existing input factor at the same occurrence must add. The proof does this correctly.

The accepted B statement is coefficientwise on the entire standard chart, including its odd-factor coefficient. The q calculation is likewise a direct coefficient calculation. Thus the all-chart conclusion is stronger than regularity merely on the open where the legal equations are invertible.

## 4. Characters, branch points and missing-axis input

For each chart, the cover algebra is free over its invariant base with squarefree basis z_S. Its character projection remains base-linear after invariant clearing. This validates coefficient extraction at branch points without division by z_S.

Multiplication by the overlap's odd factor z_d changes S to S XOR {d}. If d is already present, it also contributes the polynomial branch equation f_d=z_d^2. The latter has the chart law f_infinity=t^4 f_finite. Together with b_infinity=t^-2 b_finite for the stripped raw B and q coefficients, this supplies the required +2 coefficient-line shift when a bit is removed, as well as the -2 shift when a bit is added. The operator therefore works on arbitrary admitted pure-character inputs; union of characters would be wrong.

For the actual endpoint input, the direction convention is decisive. With complementary pairs (a,b) and q,

    Z_a=(L_b s_q;jk - s_q;ij U_b)/2,
    Z_b=-(L_a s_q;jk - s_q;ij U_a)/2.

The current direction is the other member of the pair. The two currents have that direction's character, and the scalar has character q. Hence Z_d is missing exactly d. This typing holds in the fixed physical row frames across standard representation charts, not just in an unidentified affine array.

The inclusion j contains one transpose and preserves this character, its physical diagonal factors canceling in the degree-zero ambient frame law. H is deck invariant and lowers internal degree by one. Restrictions preserve character and the Hom-line transformation. Exactly one C_d and two H factors therefore turn either insertion into a pure full-character, degree-minus-two map. Its coefficient line is the claimed multiplicity copy of

    O_Y(-4,-4,-4,-4).

No derivative of Z is part of either insertion; no quotient-rule increment is appropriate for Z here.

Nonidentity ambient transports must still be applied before subtraction; no additional row conjugation is inserted into the common ambient delta.
