## 2. A universal non-composition lemma

We first prove a small universal statement, independent of any selected actual map:

    If j:W(-9)->P(-4) and q:P(-4)->O(4)
    are nonzero equivariant morphisms, then q j is nonzero.  (5)

### 2.1 Every such j is an everywhere saturated rank-two inclusion

Suppose first that the generic rank of j were one. Its saturated image in P(-4) would be an equivariant line O(e), with e<=-4 by the ordinary top-degree bound. The whole ambient P(-4) has trivial central action, so e is even; there are no characters with which to alter that parity.

For the source map W(-9)->O(e), the only relevant symmetric powers are

    Sym1 W=W, Sym3 W=S4, Sym5 W=T6.

For even e<=-4, these show that a nonzero source map is possible only when e=-8: the other candidates e=-4,-6 have no W, and e<=-10 has negative Hom degree. But the inclusion O(-8)->P(-4) would lie in

    Hom_G(P,Sym4 W)=Hom_G(P,D)=0.

This contradiction rules out generic rank one. Since j is nonzero and its source has rank two, it has generic rank two.

Every maximal minor of j has degree ten. The common zero divisor of these minors is G-invariant and has degree at most ten, bounded by any one nonzero minor. The invariant-divisor minimum twelve therefore forces that common divisor to be empty. Thus j has rank two at every point, and its image is a saturated subbundle of P(-4).

### 2.2 Every such q is an everywhere surjection

The entries of a nonzero q have degree eight. Their common zero divisor is invariant and has degree at most eight, again strictly less than twelve. It is empty, so q is everywhere onto O(4). Its kernel B is a rank-three subbundle with

    deg B=4(-4)-4=-20.

### 2.3 A zero composition would create a forbidden top line

If q j=0, the saturated inclusion from Section 2.1 lies in B. Its quotient there is a line bundle: indeed B/j(W(-9)) is the kernel of the induced surjection P(-4)/j(W(-9))->O(4) between vector bundles. Its degree is

    -20-2(-9)=-2.

Thus there is an equivariant exact sequence

    0 -> W(-9) -> B -> O(-2) -> 0.                   (6)

Its equivariant extension group vanishes:

    Ext^1_G(O(-2),W(-9))
      =(W tensor H1(O(-7)))^G
      =(W tensor Sym5 W*)^G
      =0.                                             (7)

The last equality follows from the fixed identity

    W tensor T6=D+V_alt+P,

which contains no trivial representation. Serre duality uses det W=1, and G-invariants are exact in characteristic zero. Equation (7) is equivariant Ext vanishing; ordinary Ext is not claimed to vanish.

The exact sequence (6) therefore splits equivariantly. In particular it would inject O(-2) into B and then into P(-4). But even ordinary Hom(O(-2),P(-4)) is zero by degree. This is impossible and proves (5).

This proof of (5) uses neither representatives of the two universal maps nor an evaluation of their composition. Its nonvanishing follows from their source/target types, saturation, and the small Ext identity.

## 3. The cubic projection of the actual fifth jets is forced

Suppose K has either of the two asserted cubic types. Choose an equivariant Birkhoff splitting solely to describe its morphisms:

    K=S4(-3)+R(-4)+N,
    N=W(-9) or O(-8)+O(-10).                           (8)

No summand is declared horizontal. The actual inclusion i:Zhat_5->K from (4) has zero component to N by ordinary degree.

Its component s:Zhat_5->S4(-3) must be nonzero. Otherwise it would land entirely in R(-4). The obstruction to extending this actual map across the actual jet sequence in (4) lies in

    Ext^1_G(O(2),R(-4))
       =(R tensor Sym4 W*)^G
       =Hom_G(D,R)=0.                                  (9)

Therefore it would extend to a regular equivariant map a:Zhat_6->K. Compare its composite into the original constant C O with the actual sixth-jet inclusion. The two maps agree on Zhat_5, so their difference factors through O(2). Since Hom(O(2),C O)=0, they agree everywhere. This would put the actual Zhat_6 inside K, contrary to (4). This is precisely the positive-quotient uniqueness argument of the frozen proof, now applied to the R block of (8).

The fixed Hom space for s is one-dimensional:

    Hom_G(T6(-5),S4(-3))
       =Hom_G(T6,S4 tensor Sym2 W)=C,
    S4 tensor Sym2 W=T6+S4+W.                         (10)

It is generated by the universal surjection

    0 -> W(-9) -> T6(-5) -> S4(-3) -> 0.             (11)

For completeness, (11) is the standard complete-jet quotient. In the complete degree-ten coefficient space, the fiber of Zhat_j at a line ell is ell^(10-j) Symj W. Inside the fiber ell^5 Sym5 W of Zhat_5, the actual Zhat_1 is ell^9 W=ell^5(ell^4 W). The order-three evaluation of the complete degree-five system has kernel ell^4 W and supplies the globally surjective map Sym5 W O -> Sym3 W(2). Twisting by -5 gives (11), whose kernel is exactly the intrinsic actual inclusion Zhat_1->Zhat_5. This is a universal fiber formula in a variable line, not an evaluation at a selected point.

By (10), the nonzero actual s is a nonzero scalar multiple of that universal surjection. Hence its actual kernel is precisely Zhat_1. Since the N component of i is zero, this proves an actual inclusion

    Zhat_1=W(-9) subset R(-4) subset K.                (12)

It is saturated: Zhat_1 is an actual saturated jet bundle in C O, and R(-4) is a subbundle of that same ambient. In particular it is not merely a morphism into an abstractly isomorphic block. Moreover beta_K kills it because Zhat_1 subset Zhat_4 subset K^-.

## 4. The P component is nonzero, and its positive quotient must vanish

Write the components of the actual inclusion (12) as

    j_V:W(-9)->V_alt(-4),
    j_P:W(-9)->P(-4).

The component j_P is nonzero. If it were zero, the identity beta_K|Zhat_1=0 and the exact actual V_alt row (3) would force j_V to factor through O(-14). But

    Hom(W(-9),O(-14))=0

    q_P j_P=0.

Since j_P is nonzero, the universal non-composition lemma (5) proves

    pi beta_K|P(-4)=0.

Together with (3), the actual near-top block consequently has

    im(beta_K|R(-4))=W(1) subset A(2).                (13)

The equality, not just containment, uses the already-surjective V_alt row. This is the substantive actual compatibility forced by the lifted jet flag.

## 5. Every remaining row has a defective positive quotient

The cubic row also has zero O(4) projection, because

    Hom_G(S4(-3),O(4))
       =Hom_G(S4,Sym7 W)=0,
    Sym7 W=T6+W'.                                     (14)

Thus only N in (8) can contribute to pi beta_K:K->O(4).

For K4, the remaining row is W(-9)->O(4). The fixed decomposition

    Sym13 W=W+S4+T6+W'

contains W with multiplicity one. Multiplication by the nonzero universal invariant f12 identifies Sym1 W=W with a nonzero W submodule of Sym13 W. Therefore every equivariant row W(-9)->O(4) factors through

    W(-9) -> O(-8) --f12--> O(4).                    (15)

In particular its coherent image is contained in O(4)(-D12), whether the row is zero or nonzero.

For K5, the row O(-8)->O(4) is a scalar multiple of f12, since its invariant degree is twelve. The other row O(-10)->O(4) vanishes, since there is no invariant of degree fourteen: fourteen is not a nonnegative combination of the possible orbit sizes 12,20,30,60. In either case, (13)-(15) prove that the entire actual composite pi beta_K factors through the proper subsheaf

    O(4)(-D12) subsetneq O(4).                        (16)

This contradicts the fact that beta_K in (1) and pi in (2) are both everywhere surjective, so their composite must be everywhere surjective. Hence neither K4 nor K5 occurs under the retained residual simultaneous hypotheses.
