## 5. Full prism, not the prefix-only prism

For alternating degree-q c, the operator under consideration is

    (Qdot_d c)_(e0,...,e_(q-1))
      = sum_(k=0)^(q-1) (-1)^k sum_(a0,...,a_(q-1),b)
        (product_(j=0)^(q-1) rho_(ej,aj)) (V_d rho_(ek,b))
        c_(a0,...,ak,b,a_(k+1),...,a_(q-1)).

Every source occurrence in this coefficient receives its own clearing weight. In particular the duplicated pivot receives BOTH rho_(ek,ak) and V_d rho_(ek,b), on separate occurrences. There is no unweighted suffix. For Qdot c_d there are five weighted occurrences; for Qdot delta c_d there are six. The full output target intersection is U_e0 intersect ... intersect U_e(q-1).

For a summand let T be its source occurrences with multiplicity and put

    C'_v=H_v^35 product_i ell_vi^15,
    D'_v=C'_v product_i Z_(r(v),i)^5251.

The ledger and homogeneous-frame premises make N=(product_(v in T) D'_v)c a regular cleared section in F_d, where c here denotes the missing-d character component of c_d (or the corresponding face term). Each weight (or differentiated normalized weight at b) divided by C'_v is regular on its own standard chart, by section 4. The differentiated normalized weight is a local section of the d-character O(-2) factor, whereas the other weights are scalars. Consequently the normalized summand, interpreted by this sheaf multiplication, is

    N * product_(v in T)(weight_v/C'_v)
      / product_(v in T,i) Z_(r(v),i)^5251.

All standard factors in the denominator are units on the FULL output target intersection: r(a_j)=e_j and r(b)=e_k. This correspondence includes every owner selected before an internal HPL deletion. Thus every summand extends regularly to that target intersection as a section of F. The identical argument applies to restrictions and to each face term in delta c_d. Normality supplies extension across codimension at least two once the stated homogeneous divisorial certificates are imposed.

This stronger full-weight conclusion does not contradict the older prefix-only H_rho theorem: that different operator has genuinely unweighted suffix owners and requires suffix clearing. One cannot drop suffix clearing there merely because it is absent here.

Repeated target tags AMONG SAMPLED SOURCE OCCURRENCES are permitted because the inserted pivot has the same target tag as its neighbor. Such repeated tags do not by themselves kill an individual source summand: distinct common-source vertices sharing that tag remain distinct and must be retained with the exact sorting sign. This statement does not concern repeated output target indices, on which an alternating output cochain can vanish. If the common-source vertices themselves coincide, the alternating value is zero by definition. Equal physical carrier/mask labels at different tags need not vanish. The frozen terminal sorted_source implementation is certified only on distinct-staircase tags; this theorem makes no assertion that its repeated-key branch computes this mathematical extension.

## 6. Serre boundary and exact remaining term

The regularity just proved makes S Qdot_d c_d a genuine tensor degree-three cochain in F. The staircase map is a regular cochain map. The formal differentiated-prism identity therefore holds in the appropriate sheaf Cech complexes, not just in a common function field. After full-sign extraction, the first term of the displayed identity is an actual Cech boundary.

For a retained test p interpreted as a global section of O(2,2,2,2), multiplication takes F to the canonical bundle K_Y=O(-2,-2,-2,-2). In finite coordinates this includes the authenticated ID0 polynomial (x0 x1+x2 x3)^2; its degree in each coordinate is at most two. p is not asserted to be a globally regular scalar function on the projective variety. Its homogeneous section and its compatible restrictions are what commute with the Cech differential. Serre trace annihilates this K_Y-valued boundary. The same conclusion applies to other authenticated tests only with their correct dual-section interpretation.

It follows that the outer derivative family's Serre pairing equals the pairing of

    -p sum_d S Qdot_d delta c_d.

No supplied theorem sets this remaining cochain to zero. Complete-source closure concerns a different input, the complete residual, and cannot replace this defect. Other portions of B_p, including derivatives of p and all inner/current kernels, remain untouched. This proof supplies neither a rational coordinate-divergence primitive for the boundary nor the complete B_p certificate; those require their own comparison or identity.
