# The actual ideal-valued fifth norm test is faithful

## Statement and exact square sheaf

Work over C on the actual integral quadric cone Q, with B=End_0(F),
fixed physical-even marking, and the classified source data of the fifth
boundary ladder. Fix nonzero a,h and the canonical b,U_a. Put K=aJ4,
f=a^4h, so K is a section of B_(4Q)(12) and K|Q=fN.

The square sheaf in this theorem is

    Qsq = pi_*(I_Z^2(6)),

where pi is the tame coarse map from the smooth weighted stack
X=P_stack(1,1,2,2), and I_Z is the ideal of its actual regular Serre
section. This is equivalently the exact image of

    tau=tr(N-): B_Q(4) -> O_Q(6).

It is NOT the square of the coarse Ext-annihilator ideal. The trace-image
notation in the norm construction is interpreted as this exact image.
In particular no coarse tensor identity involving a noninvertible spinor
is silently substituted for the stack square.

The ideal-valued norm coset from [PR769 at
1599ab33ec5392433da49fcfbd0bddd5ba1482d3](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/1599ab33ec5392433da49fcfbd0bddd5ba1482d3/research/hm/ideal-valued-fifth-norm/PROOF.md)
is denoted nu(K). Under its canonical connecting identification it belongs
to H1(Qsq)_+ and equals tau_*(delta_5 K).

**Theorem.** The map

    tau_*: H1(B_Q(4))_+ -> H1(Qsq)_+

is an isomorphism. Both spaces are the four-dimensional representation P4.
For V_a=im(beta2_a), the induced map

    H1(B_Q(4))_+/V_a -> H1(Qsq)_+/tau_*(V_a)             (1)

is therefore an isomorphism, not merely a necessary obstruction map.
Consequently

    beta4_a(h)=0  iff  nu(K) belongs to tau_*(V_a).      (2)

This resolves the trace-kernel faithfulness dependency in the norm
construction. It does not compute nu(K), decide either side of (2), or
identify the nonzero fourth-square scalar with this fifth-boundary value.

## 1. The exact kernel was already established

Let S be the actual rank-one reflexive spinor on the coarse cone and
S_hat its invertible stack counterpart. Put E=F_Q(2) and
G=pi*E tensor S_hat^(-1). The pinned source
[WEIGHTED-STACK-SQUARE-SHADOW-AND-PRIMARY-SCALAR.md, sections 1--4](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/8d8b5be4ce62c3055bb53be9e16d29cc63072f04/research/hm/order-four-quadratic-load/WEIGHTED-STACK-SQUARE-SHADOW-AND-PRIMARY-SCALAR.md)
proves the actual section sigma of G is regular on every stack chart,
including charts over the vertex. Its symmetric-square sequence is

    0 -> G(2) -> pi*B_Q(4) -> I_Z^2(6) -> 0.

The quotient is the trace against the nilpotent, with the fixed determinant
identifications. Local regular-pair formulas give its image as the ordinary
square generated by f0^2,f0g0,g0^2; here f0,g0 denote local components,
not the source multiplier f=a^4h.

Tame coarse pushforward is exact in characteristic zero. Projection
formula and the actual spinor identities identify the kernel as

    P=pi_*G(2)=F_Q tensor S(3).

Thus the established exact sequence is

    0 -> P -> B_Q(4) --tau--> Qsq -> 0.                 (3)

This includes the singular vertex; it is not obtained by replacing S
with an invertible sheaf there. The sequence and its kernel are prior
results, not new claims of this note. Their application to the fifth norm
criterion is the missing dependency resolved here.

## 2. Why the kernel has no H1 or H2

The same pinned source proves these vanishings from actual primary
cohomology and the independently established cone closure. We recall the
argument to distinguish it from an invalid positivity inference.
Tensor the ambient spinor resolution by F(3):

    0 -> W' tensor F(2) -> W' tensor F(3) -> i_*P -> 0.

The stated primary groups are

    H0(F(2))=0, H0(F(3))=P4,
    H1(F(2))=1+A+T6, H1(F(3))=W,

with no higher cohomology in those twists. Abstract characters leave just
one possible P4 scalar lambda in the relevant H1 map, giving

    H0(P)_+=A+D5+ker(lambda:P4->P4),
    H1(P)=coker(lambda:P4->P4),
    H2(P)=0.                                         (4)

The actual cone closure supplies H0(B_Q(4))_+=A+D5.
It is independently proved by
[NO-PRESERVING-POSITIVE-CONE-EXCESS.md](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/8d8b5be4ce62c3055bb53be9e16d29cc63072f04/research/hm/order-four-quadratic-load/NO-PRESERVING-POSITIVE-CONE-EXCESS.md)
and
[POSITIVE-CONE-MOVING-LINE-OBSTRUCTION-VANISHES.md](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/8d8b5be4ce62c3055bb53be9e16d29cc63072f04/research/hm/order-four-quadratic-load/POSITIVE-CONE-MOVING-LINE-OBSTRUCTION-VANISHES.md).
The injection H0(P)_+ into this eight-dimensional adjoint space leaves
no room for ker(lambda). Hence lambda is nonzero, H1(P)=0, and (4) gives
H2(P)=0. No scalar is evaluated and no selected rank is computed.

The long exact sequence of (3) now proves that tau_* is an isomorphism.
The actual cone target is P4. Its compatible square-quotient description
is also explicit in
[POSITIVE-SECOND-BOCKSTEIN-SQUARE-FILTRATION.md, sections 1--2](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/8d8b5be4ce62c3055bb53be9e16d29cc63072f04/research/hm/order-four-quadratic-load/POSITIVE-SECOND-BOCKSTEIN-SQUARE-FILTRATION.md):

    H1(Qsq)_+ = H0(Q4(6))/H0(Sym8(W') tensor O_L(2))
              = H1(P4 tensor O_L(-2)) = P4.            (5)

Here Q4 is the vertex bundle in the established exact sequence

    0 -> P4 tensor O_L(-8) -> Sym8(W') tensor O_L(-4)
      -> Q4 -> 0.

Thus this is the same actual square target and map, not an unrelated
four-dimensional space matched by a dimension count. Extra Serre-involution
characters are not identified with physical parity in this argument.

## 3. Exactness of the quotient criterion

An isomorphism T induces an isomorphism of its source modulo any subspace V
with its target modulo T(V). Apply this to tau_* and V_a to obtain (1).
The identity nu(K)=tau_*(delta_5 K) from PR769 then proves (2).

No rank assumption or inverse for beta2_a is needed. Thus the criterion
also applies on its rank-drop directions. Classified changes retain the
fixed a,h,b,U_a scope: their raw boundaries differ by V_a, and their norm
classes differ by tau_*(V_a). No assertion that this latter image is zero
is made or needed.

In particular the potential residual kernel

    (im H1(ker tau)+V_a)/V_a

from the abstract norm-shadow construction vanishes in this actual cone
problem. This follows from the exact kernel vanishing above, rather than
from the mere sheaf-surjectivity of tau.

## 4. Constructive sufficiency for a fixed fourth lift

Before taking the second-symbol quotient, one obtains the stronger fixed-K
statement

    K extends to a global trace-free section on 5Q
    iff nu(K)=0 in H1(Qsq)_+.                          (6)

There is a direct gluing proof. Choose a global scalar lift P0 of n(K)
and local adjoint lifts K_i, as in PR769. Write

    n(K_i)-P0=q^4 w_i.

If nu(K)=0, its defining quotient section is the image of some global
r in H0(O_Q(16)). Replace P0 by P0+q^4r. Now every w_i belongs to f Qsq.
After refining the cover, sheaf-surjectivity of tau gives D_i in B_Q(4)
with w_i=f tau(D_i). Replacing K_i by K_i-q^4D_i makes all their norms
equal to the chosen common global scalar P0. Their overlap differences
are q^4 c_ij with f tau(c_ij)=0.

Since Q is integral and f is nonzero, multiplication by f is injective on
Qsq. Therefore c_ij lies in P=ker tau. The cocycle is a coboundary because
H1(P)=0. Subtracting the corresponding local q^4P corrections glues the
adjoint lifts globally and does not change their common norm. Conversely
a global adjoint lift makes its boundary, and hence its norm coset, zero.
This proves (6) without choosing or evaluating any actual source entries.

All divisions by f here mean membership in its image sheaf, not inversion
across its divisor. The construction retains the source divisors and the
vertex. Taking the physical-even summand is exact, so the choices can be
made in that sector.

## Contribution and remaining problem

The fifth norm test is complete for the actual adjoint boundary, both for
a fixed fourth lift and modulo the classified second-symbol changes.
The remaining problem is the value of its P4 class modulo tau_*(V_a), not
an additional unexamined trace-kernel cohomology obstruction. The q^3
fourth-square theorem supplies no such q^4 value by itself.

This is a new application and constructive dependency resolution using
prior exact cone geometry. It is not a new proof of the older kernel
sequence or rank33 theorem. No selected actual coefficient, point,
derivative, matrix, source or rank evaluation, and no large computation,
is used. Independent review is recorded separately; no proof-assistant
certification or algebraicity conclusion is asserted.
