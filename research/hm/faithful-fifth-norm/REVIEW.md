## Checks and findings

### 1. The square sheaf and trace map are the actual ones

The proof consistently uses `Qsq = pi_*(I_Z^2(6))`, with the ordinary
ideal square formed on the smooth weighted stack. It expressly excludes
the square of the coarse Ext-annihilator. The first pinned source proves
regularity over every stack chart, the symmetric-square sequence, and
the exact coarse kernel `P = F_Q tensor S(3)`. Its local lower-component
formula has the image generated by the ordinary square of the regular
pair. The amended PR769 explicitly identifies that same image with
`tau = tr(N-)` using the fixed nilpotent normalization. Consequently the
new proof is not matching unrelated four-dimensional targets by dimension.

The use of the coarse spinor is legitimate: projection formula uses the
locally free bundle F, and the argument does not pull the noninvertible
coarse spinor back as though it were invertible at the vertex.

### 2. Kernel vanishings are prior inputs, not a new computation

Sections 3-4 of the weighted-stack source already establish
`H1(P)=H2(P)=0`. The new proof accurately recalls their derivation:
the primary resolution leaves a single possible P4 scalar, while the
independently established `H0(B_Q(4))_+ = A + D5` excludes its kernel.
No positivity-only inference or selected rank evaluation is substituted.

The long exact sequence of
`0 -> P -> B_Q(4) -> Qsq -> 0` therefore gives the asserted canonical
H1 isomorphism. Sections 1-2 of the second pinned source separately state
the same isomorphism and the exact vertex-relation quotient identifying

twisting by six agree with the proof's displayed formulas.

### 3. The quotient criterion keeps the full second-symbol image

An isomorphism induces an isomorphism after quotienting its source by
`V_a` and its target by its exact image `tau_*(V_a)`. Together with the
amended PR769 connecting-class identity and the pinned fifth-boundary

The proof neither discards `tau_*(V_a)` nor assumes it vanishes. It uses

### 4. The constructive gluing proof is sufficient

The fixed-K statement is stronger than quotient vanishing and is correctly
kept separate from it. A zero norm coset supplies one global scalar
correction r, so replacing P0 by `P0 + q^4 r` makes all local errors lie
in `f Qsq`. Surjectivity of tau gives local adjoint corrections D_i.
The rank-two polarization identity and `q^8=0` on 5Q show that subtracting
`q^4 D_i` makes every local norm equal to this same global scalar P0.

The remaining overlap differences have the form `q^4 c_ij` and satisfy
`f tau(c_ij)=0`. Because Q is integral, f is nonzero, and Qsq is a
subsheaf of `O_Q(6)`, multiplication by f is injective there. Hence this
is a P-valued cocycle. Refining to a suitable affine cover and using
`H1(P)=0` removes it by P-valued local corrections. Such corrections
preserve the common norm since their trace under tau is zero. This gives
a global trace-free extension, including over the source divisors and
singular vertex. No inversion of f on its zero divisor occurs.

The converse follows directly from the vanishing of the boundary of a
global extension. No separate scalar or adjoint lifting hypothesis is
silently added: the scalar lift exists by the line-bundle vanishing
already proved in amended PR769.
