# Higher-twist structural exclusions

For k=2,3,4, a nonzero trace-free map F→F(k) cannot have zero or square determinant. Its zero scheme is nonempty and at least one-dimensional, and lies scheme-theoretically in the determinant hypersurface's singular scheme. The spectral rank-one MCM module is nonfree exactly above this zero set. Normal candidate covers must be non-locally-factorial along a positive-dimensional locus.

These statements do not determine H⁰End₀(F)(k). PROOF.md contains the proof and a separate primary-source verification that Pic(X)=Z for the global spectral covers; this Picard result does not make the spectral module invertible. AUDIT.md records independent review.
