- In p,q-degree zero and one the primary quotient is D0 plus ((a,b)D0)^4; in higher degrees the full quotient has only pure-p and pure-q tails, annihilated respectively by b and a. This proves the injection into the three claimed primary quotients and the irredundant decomposition. The three radicals are incomparable, so they are the minimal associated primes and there is no embedded center component.

Its associated prime P has dimension two, forcing equality. With the five smooth base parameters, depth is seven; dimension ten, embedding dimension fifteen, and twenty independent minimal quadrics follow.

The whole-fiber support forces both outer lines of any all-nonzero non-rank-one zero into the determinant zero scheme set-theoretically.
