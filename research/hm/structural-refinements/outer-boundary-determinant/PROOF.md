# Outer-surviving two-zero boundary: a conditional exact model and a global degree-fifteen obstruction

## Theorem and the unresolved nonemptiness issue

Work over C with dim V=5, dim J=20, and the stipulated trilinear operation f:V^3→J. Import the identities

    f(x,y,z)=f(z,y,x),
    f(x,y,z)+f(y,z,x)+f(z,x,y)=0,

and the locally split rank-four repeated-input injection v↦f(u,u,v) modulo Cu, for u≠0. 

On X=P(V), put L=O_X(−1), E=(V⊗O_X)/L, and A=L²⊗E⊂J⊗O_X. Let U_10 be the open where the middle quadratic linearization

    Q:L⊗Sym²E → (J⊗O_X)/A

has rank ten. On this open let G be the rank-fourteen subbundle generated by A and the middle symmetric image, and put R=(J⊗O)/G, a vector bundle of rank six. There is a canonical alternating quotient map

    α:L⊗Λ²E → R,
    u⊗(p̄∧q̄) ↦ [f(p,q,u)].

Let Ω⊂U_10 be its isomorphism locus. Then:

1. At a two-zero point with nonzero third input u and [u]∈Ω, choose exact coordinates

       (x,y,z)=(au+p,bu+q,u),   p,q∈H,   dim H=4.

   After shrinking the smooth five-dimensional u-base S, the full original zero ideal is exactly

       K=(b p_i−2a q_i, p_iq_j for all 1≤i,j≤4).

   This is an equality of full ideals at the center, not merely of leading terms or isolated components. In fact it holds over the entire (x,y)-fiber above this u-base. The nonzero-first-input case follows by outer symmetry.
2. Write B=O(S), T=B[a,b,p,q], P_x=(a,p), P_y=(b,q), P=(p,q), and

       Q_P=(P²,bp−2aq).

   There is an irredundant primary decomposition

       K=P_x∩P_y∩Q_P.

   The first two ideals are prime, Q_P is P-primary, and these three minimal primes are all the associated primes. In particular there is no embedded component or nonzero submodule supported only on the two-zero center.
3. The nilradical of T/K is square-zero and is free of rank four on D=Spec B[a,b], the reduced rank-one component. The three component dimensions are 10,10,7, with generic multiplicities 1,1,5. At a closed point of the center, the full local ring has dimension 10, embedding dimension 15, depth 7, and exactly twenty minimal quadratic equations. The chart is a product over S, and its full closed-point tangent cone is the product of T_uS with the fixed transverse quadratic cone.
4. Globally there is a rank-twenty vector bundle C and a map Φ:C→J⊗O_X, defined by the exact sequence

       0 → L²⊗V → L⊗V⊗V → C → 0,
       κ(u²⊗v)=u⊗(u⊗v+2v⊗u),

   through which u⊗p⊗q↦f(p,q,u) factors. Its determinant line has c₁(C)=−15h. Thus det Φ is a section of O_X(15), up to the fixed one-dimensional determinant factors of V and J.
5. The nonvanishing locus of det Φ is exactly Ω; in particular it automatically lies in U_10. If det Φ is not identically zero, its zero scheme is a nonempty effective Cartier divisor of class 15h, hence degree fifteen. On U_10 this is exactly the determinant degeneracy scheme of α. It is the global obstruction to the sufficient outer-boundary criterion above.
6. Every zero triple with all three inputs nonzero that is not rank one has both outer projective lines [x] and [z] in V(det Φ). Thus, conditionally on det Φ≢0, both outer lines lie on the same degree-fifteen hypersurface. This restriction is vacuous if det Φ≡0.

NONEMPTINESS IS NOT PROVED. Neither Ω≠∅ nor det Φ≢0 follows from the argument. Even nonemptiness of U_10 alone does not imply nonemptiness of Ω. If det Φ is identically zero, there is no degree-fifteen Cartier divisor defined by this section: its zero scheme is all of X. No theorem here asserts that the conditional outer model occurs for the actual operation.

## 1. Cyclic signs and the alternating quotient

Put A_u(v)=f(u,u,v). Outer symmetry and cyclic zero give

    f(v,u,u)=A_u(v),
    f(u,v,u)=−2A_u(v),
    f(u,u,u)=0.

For F_u(p,q)=f(p,q,u) and B_u(p,q)=f(p,u,q), the cyclic identity and outer symmetry give

    F_u(p,q)+F_u(q,p)=−B_u(p,q).

Thus the symmetric part of F_u is −B_u/2. Its alternating part is

    D_u(p,q)=(F_u(p,q)−F_u(q,p))/2,
    F_u=−B_u/2+D_u.

The alternating part is genuinely additional information: the rank-ten statement about B_u does not determine its rank modulo the middle image.

On U_10 the subbundle G is the image of the middle symmetric map L⊗Sym²V→J⊗O; its image contains A. Modulo G the symmetric part of F_u vanishes, so [F_u(p,q)] is alternating. Replacing p by p+cu changes F_u by −2cA_u(q), and replacing q by q+du changes it by dA_u(p); replacing both introduces only a zero f(u,u,u) term. Hence the quotient class depends only on p̄,q̄∈E. Its linear dependence on u gives the twist L, proving the stated intrinsic definition of α. No extra dual or positive twist occurs.

## 2. Exact recovery of the full outer ideal

Choose a linear functional ℓ nonzero at u and H=ker ℓ. On ℓ(z)≠0 the coordinate change

    u=z,  a=ℓ(x)/ℓ(u),  b=ℓ(y)/ℓ(u),
    p=x−au,  q=y−bu

is algebraically invertible. Trilinearity gives the exact identity

    f(au+p,bu+q,u)=A_u(bp−2aq)+F_u(p,q).             (1)

Work over a sufficiently small open S lying above Ω, so all the vector-bundle inclusions and quotient maps under discussion split algebraically. Project (1) to R. The result is α_u(p̄∧q̄). Since α is invertible, the ideal of these six projected coordinates is exactly

    Alt=(p_iq_j−p_jq_i : i<j).

Every coordinate of D_u(p,q) belongs to Alt. Removing this part of (1), and then projecting modulo A, leaves −B_u(p,q)/2. The rank-ten hypothesis gives a left inverse to Sym²H→J/A, so these remaining equations recover

    Sym=(p_iq_j+p_jq_i : i≤j).

Over C the sum Alt+Sym is exactly M=(p_iq_j:all i,j). The remaining A component of (1) is bp−2aq plus terms already in M. Since A_u is a split injection, its four coordinates recover bp−2aq. Thus all the generators of K belong to the full operation ideal.

Conversely every coordinate of F_u(p,q) lies in M by bilinearity, so (1) shows that every operation equation belongs to K. This proves exact equality. No division by a or b occurs. In particular the equality holds at a=b=p=q=0 itself.

All splittings and inverses used here depend only on u. There is no further localization in a,b,p,q, so the equality holds for the entire other-input fiber above S. Its reduced support, computed below, consists only of x=0, y=0, and x,y∈Cu. Consequently, if x,y,z are all nonzero and [z]∈Ω, the zero equation forces the triple to have rank one. Applying outer symmetry gives the same conclusion if [x]∈Ω. This proves assertion 6 without using any additional rank-two classification. It is a necessary condition on actual zero triples, not a sufficiency statement about points of the determinant hypersurface.

## 3. The three primary components

Set D_0=B[a,b] and give p,q degree one while a,b have degree zero. The quotient by Q_P is the split square-zero algebra

    T/Q_P ≅ D_0 ⊕ ((a,b)D_0)^4.

An explicit presentation sends p_i↦2a t_i and q_i↦b t_i into D_0[t_1,…,t_4]/(t)^2. The relations on the pair (2a,b) are generated by (b,−2a), since a,b form a regular sequence. These are exactly the four displayed linear module relations. Because D_0 is a domain and (a,b)D_0 is torsion-free, any element with nonzero D_0-part is a nonzerodivisor. All elements with zero D_0-part are square-zero. Hence Q_P is P-primary.

Clearly K⊂P_x∩P_y∩Q_P. For the reverse inclusion the p,q-degree decomposition of T/K is

    degree 0: D_0;
    degree 1: D_0^8/(bp_i−2aq_i) ≅ ((a,b)D_0)^4;
    degree d≥2: (D_0/(b))⊗_B Sym^d(B^4_p)
                 ⊕ (D_0/(a))⊗_B Sym^d(B^4_q).

Indeed all mixed products vanish. Multiplication of bp_i−2aq_i by p_j imposes b p_i p_j=0, and multiplication by q_j imposes a q_i q_j=0; higher-degree relations are exactly their multiples. There are no relations between the displayed pure-p and pure-q pieces.

The map to T/Q_P is the identity on degrees zero and one and kills the higher pieces. The remaining pure-p tail injects into T/P_y=B[a,p], and the remaining pure-q tail injects into T/P_x=B[b,q]. Therefore

    T/K → T/P_x ⊕ T/P_y ⊕ T/Q_P

is injective, proving the decomposition. Its radicals are pairwise incomparable, so it is irredundant and all three primes are minimal. There are no other associated primes.

Let m=(a,b,p,q), the ideal of the relative two-zero center. For each displayed primary quotient, its radical omits an element of m. That element is a nonzerodivisor on that quotient. The displayed injection consequently implies

    H⁰_m(T/K)=0.

This is an intrinsic absence of center-supported torsion, stronger than merely failing to exhibit an embedded primary component.

## 4. Reduction, nilpotents, and component geometry

Intersecting the three radicals gives

    √K=(b p_i,a q_i,p_iq_j).

Modulo K write h_i=b p_i=2a q_i. The nilradical N is generated by these four classes. Both p and q annihilate h_i: the cross products vanish, and b p_i p_j=a q_i q_j=0. Also h_i h_j=b²p_i p_j=0. Thus N²=0 and its action factors through D_0.

There are no D_0-linear relations among h_i. In T/Q_P they map to 2ab t_i, which are D_0-linearly independent. Therefore

    N ≅ D_0^4,

with each displayed generator in total degree two. In particular N is supported on all of the reduced rank-one component D, and has no summand supported only at a=b=0. This is a module statement, not a claim that the full reducible algebra is split over its reduction.

The component P_x is x=0, P_y is y=0, and P is the rank-one component x=au,y=bu,z=u. Over S their relative dimensions are 5,5,2. The coordinate components meet each other precisely along S. The rank-one component meets each coordinate component along its corresponding scalar axis, of total dimension six; the common intersection of all three is S. At the generic point of either coordinate component the other two components disappear and the scheme is reduced. At the generic point of D, Q_P has square-zero algebra of dimension 1+4 over its residue field. This gives multiplicities 1,1,5.

The full nilradical D_0^4 must not be confused with the nilradical ((a,b)D_0)^4 of the isolated primary quotient T/Q_P. These are different algebras. Their depths at the two-zero center are accordingly different.

## 5. Depth, flatness, and the full tangent cone

The coefficient-free ideal yields an exact product

    T/K ≅ B⊗_C A_*,
    A_*=C[a,b,p,q]/(bp−2aq,p_iq_j).

It is B-flat, with every total graded piece finite free over B. At a closed center point the five regular base parameters form a regular sequence, reducing the depth question to the vertex of A_*.

Here is a direct depth computation, retaining the reducible scheme. Write S_0=C[a,b,p,q], localized at its homogeneous maximal ideal, and continue to denote the three primes by P_x,P_y,P. First the standard intersection sequence

    0 → S_0/(P_x∩P) → S_0/P_x ⊕ S_0/P
      → S_0/(P_x+P) → 0

has middle depth min(5,2)=2 and last depth 1. Hence S_0/(P_x∩P) has depth 2. Since P_x∩P=(p,a q), one has

    S_0/((P_x∩P)+P_y) ≅ C[a]_(a).

The second intersection sequence, adjoining P_y, therefore proves that S_0/√K has depth 2. Finally N≅C[a,b]^4 has depth 2 and gives

    0 → N → A_* → S_0/√K → 0.

The depth lemma implies depth A_*≥2. The associated prime P has quotient dimension 2, so the standard associated-prime bound gives depth A_*≤2. Thus the transverse depth is exactly 2 and the full depth is 5+2=7. The isolated primary quotient has transverse depth 1, hence full depth 6; one cannot substitute that depth for the depth of the full scheme.

The four bp_i−2aq_i and sixteen p_iq_j are linearly independent quadratic forms: the former involve scalar variables and the latter use distinct cross monomials. They are twenty minimal generators at the vertex. There are no linear equations, so the full embedding dimension is 5+10=15. The local dimension is 5+5=10.

Because the product is exact and the equations are homogeneous, the maximal-ideal tangent cone at a closed center point is

    T_uS × Spec A_*.

As an optional consistency check, its transverse Hilbert series is

    H_(A_*)(t)=2/(1−t)^5+5/(1−t)^2−10/(1−t)+4,

with initial graded dimensions 1,10,35,80. In particular 55−35=20 agrees with the quadratic count. In the middle-surviving model of the cited proof, six extra center-supported degree-two classes change 35 to 41 and lower the transverse depth to zero; those classes are absent here under the additional alternating-invertibility hypothesis.

## 6. The global quotient bundle and the order of its canonical relation

Define F_0:L⊗V⊗V→J⊗O_X by

    F_0(u⊗p⊗q)=f(p,q,u).

The morphism κ:L²⊗V→L⊗V⊗V is canonically

    κ(u²⊗v)=u⊗(u⊗v+2v⊗u).

Its composition with F_0 is f(u,v,u)+2f(v,u,u)=0. The coefficient order matters: with this slot convention the swapped expression 2u⊗v+v⊗u would map to −3A_u(v), which is generally not zero. Only an overall nonzero scalar can be changed harmlessly.

The map κ is a subbundle injection. Locally split V⊗O=L⊕E. Writing v=λu+w, its inner two-factor tensor is

    3λ u⊗u+u⊗w+2w⊗u.

Its three displayed components lie in distinct direct summands, and vanishing forces λ=w=0. Local frames therefore split the injection. Its quotient C is locally free of rank 25−5=20, and F_0 descends to Φ:C→J⊗O.

This identifies a canonical rank-five subbundle contained in ker F_0. It does NOT assert that this is the entire actual kernel at every line. Equality of the actual fiber kernel with this subbundle holds precisely where Φ is invertible.

Taking determinants gives

    det C = det(L⊗V⊗V) ⊗ det(L²⊗V)^−1
          = L^15 ⊗ (det V)^9.

In ordinary Chern-class notation, c₁(C)=−25h−(−10h)=−15h. Consequently

    det Φ ∈ H⁰(X,L^−15 ⊗ det J ⊗ (det V)^−9).

The last two factors are fixed one-dimensional vector spaces. After choosing their trivializations this is H⁰(X,O_X(15)); its zero scheme and divisor class do not depend on these choices. Thus the notation O(15) suppresses only constant determinant factors, not a variable twist.

## 7. A canonical filtration and the determinant comparison

Embed Sym²V in V⊗V by symmetrization with the factor 1/2. Its image in C has kernel L³: indeed κ(u²⊗v) is symmetric in its last two factors if and only if u∧v=0, and κ(u³)=3u⊗u⊗u. Define

    D_sym=(L⊗Sym²V)/L³.

It is a rank-fourteen subbundle of C, and there is a canonical exact sequence

    0 → D_sym → C → L⊗Λ²E → 0.                 (2)

The last map sends u⊗p⊗q to u⊗(p̄∧q̄). Its kernel is exactly D_sym: after quotienting V⊗V by symmetric tensors, κ has image −u∧V; quotienting this image leaves Λ²(V/Cu). All these quotients are vector bundles. Also

    0 → L²⊗E → D_sym → L⊗Sym²E → 0.           (3)

The restriction Φ|_(D_sym) is −1/2 times the middle symmetric operation. Therefore its rank is 4+rank Q, and on U_10 it identifies D_sym with G. The quotient morphism in (2) is then precisely α:L⊗Λ²E→R. We obtain a diagram with exact rows

    0 → D_sym → C       → L⊗Λ²E → 0
          ↓≅       ↓Φ          ↓α
    0 → G     → J⊗O    → R      → 0.

Thus on U_10 the determinant of Φ differs from the determinant of α only by an invertible local factor. Their determinant zero schemes coincide there, with multiplicities, not merely as sets.

Conversely if Φ is invertible at a point, its restriction to the rank-fourteen subbundle D_sym is injective there. Hence rank Q=10, and the quotient α is an isomorphism. This proves globally

    D(det Φ)=Ω={u∈U_10 : α_u is an isomorphism}.

The twists can also be checked entirely within (2). One has

    det D_sym=L^12⊗(det V)^6,
    det(L⊗Λ²E)=L³⊗(det V)^3.

Thus c₁(D_sym)=−12h and c₁(L⊗Λ²E)=−3h. On U_10, det R=det J⊗L^−12⊗(det V)^−6, so the determinant line of α is

    det R⊗det(L⊗Λ²E)^−1
      =L^−15⊗det J⊗(det V)^−9,

exactly the global determinant line above. In particular the alternating source is L⊗Λ²E and has degree −3, while its target determinant has degree 12; their difference is 15.

## 8. The conditional degree-fifteen obstruction locus

Let δ=det Φ. If δ≢0, its zero scheme Δ is an effective Cartier divisor because X is smooth and integral. Its divisor class is

    [Δ]=c₁(J⊗O_X)−c₁(C)=15h.

Its intersection with three general hyperplanes has degree 15, counting multiplicities. It is necessarily nonempty: a nonzero section of O_P4(15) cannot be nowhere vanishing, since that would trivialize this nontrivial line bundle. No reducedness, irreducibility, smoothness, or multiplicity-one assertion is implied.

The middle rank-drop locus is contained in Δ: if rank Q≤9, then the restriction to D_sym has rank at most thirteen, and the rank-six quotient source can contribute at most six additional dimensions, so rank Φ≤19. Locally, expanding det Φ along the first fourteen columns of a splitting of (2) also shows that δ belongs to the ideal generated by the fourteen-by-fourteen minors of Φ|_(D_sym). Thus the containment holds for the usual maximal-minor rank-drop scheme as well as on its support.

If one additionally imports the conditional global rank-ten package in [quintic-normalizer-exclusion](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/feb2038a34949d8eddd670cde5871b639c26187d/research/hm/normalizer-rank-five/quintic-normalizer-exclusion/COROLLARY.md) and [rank-ten-fitting-strata](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/057792d7c964d56929f4252b2e2e3ce9f634a862/research/hm/middle-surface/rank-ten-fitting-strata/PROOF.md), then X\U_10 has codimension at least two. In that situation Δ is the scheme-theoretic closure of its alternating-determinant restriction Δ∩U_10: a Cartier divisor on the smooth X has no associated component confined to that codimension-at-least-two complement. This additional observation is not needed for the construction of δ or for its class.

Vanishing of δ is an obstruction to the sufficient criterion, not a classification of the full outer germ on Δ. The proof does not determine which additional components, weaker exact models, or embedded structures occur there. It also makes no claim about the all-zero vertex.
