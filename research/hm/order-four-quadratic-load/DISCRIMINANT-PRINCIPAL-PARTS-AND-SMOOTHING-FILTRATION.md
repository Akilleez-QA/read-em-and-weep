# The discriminant carries the actual first-principal-parts bundles

## Result

Let T=P(W*) be the negative hyperplane pencil, P=P(Sym^2 W) the negative quadratic plane, and d:T->P the discriminant embedding t |-> 2t. Retain the actual root correspondence eta:Z->T, nu:Z->P. Put

    K=nu_*eta*O_T(-5),
    C_lin=coker[44 O_T(-1) -> 46 O_T],
    C_quad=coker[44 O_P(-1) -> 48 O_P].

All arrows here are the established actual multiplication arrows, with their stated inversion characters. The special-hyperplane square theorem, together with the theorem away from its twelve parameters, now proves that C_lin is a vector bundle of rank five and degree 26. In particular its previously possible torsion is zero. The exact root-transform identity gives

    C_quad=nu_*eta*(C_lin(1)),

so C_quad is a vector bundle of rank ten on ALL of P. Every negative quadratic, including a double hyperplane and every exceptional root, has actual odd kernel dimension six and cokernel dimension ten.

For a vector bundle E on T, write J^1(E) for its bundle of first principal parts, with its standard projection to E. There are canonical identifications

    d*K = J^1(O_T(-5)),
    d*C_quad = J^1(C_lin(1)).                         (1)

Their canonical filtrations are

    0 -> O_T(-7) -> d*K -> O_T(-5) -> 0,              (2)
    0 -> C_lin(-1) -> d*C_quad -> C_lin(1) -> 0.      (3)

If C_lin is written, noncanonically, as a direct sum of line bundles O_T(d_i), then global generation gives d_i>=0 and sum d_i=26. Without choosing or determining these five degrees, (1) implies

    d*K is isomorphic to O_T(-6) directsum O_T(-6),
    d*C_quad is isomorphic to directsum_i O_T(d_i)^2. (4)

These splitting statements do not split the canonical filtrations (2)-(3). In fact every line-bundle principal-parts extension used in (4) is nonsplit.

The smoothing operator does NOT automatically preserve these jet filtrations. Its off-filtration block is an actual diagonal restriction map, described in Section 4 below. Thus (4) cannot be used to replace the Hermite target by two unrelated constant targets or to infer a kernel.

## 1. Scheme-theoretic restriction of the root correspondence

On T_root x T_parameter, the pullback of the universal root equation along d is the square of the diagonal equation. Locally, if z is the root coordinate and t is the parameter coordinate, it is exactly

    (z-t)^2=0.

Globally the diagonal divisor has bidegree (1,1), and the pulled-back universal root divisor has bidegree (2,2). The equation is the square, with no residual component. Consequently

    Z x_P T = 2 Delta_T subset T x T,

as schemes, not just on closed points. Its projections will be denoted p1,p2. Since nu is finite, its pushforward commutes with this base change. For any vector bundle E on T,

    d*(nu_*eta*E)
       = p2*(p1*E tensor O_(2 Delta_T))
       = J^1(E).                                    (5)

The exact sequence of the doubled diagonal has square-zero ideal Omega_T^1. Tensoring by p1*E and taking the finite pushforward gives

    0 -> E tensor Omega_T^1 -> J^1(E) -> E -> 0.

Here Omega_T^1=O_T(-2), with its usual SL2 linearization. This proves (1)-(3), including their parameter line factors. In particular d*O_P(1)=O_T(2), which must be retained when restricting the smoothing direction bundle.

## 2. Why all the cokernels are now locally free

The exact hyperplane section bundle is A tensor O_T(-6), where A=Sym^2 W. At every parameter the actual fiber section space is exactly its three independent squares, all even. The actual ambient sequence therefore has constant kernel dimension three and constant cokernel dimension five. Equivalently the earlier base-change torsion term is zero at every parameter. Thus C_lin is locally free.

The theorem at the twelve previously exceptional parameters is [SPECIAL-HYPERPLANE-ADJOINTS-ARE-EXACTLY-SQUARES.md](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/5ce98e6bef1fd849353f563b1ccfc62f4d28b8d9/research/hm/order-four-quadratic-load/SPECIAL-HYPERPLANE-ADJOINTS-ARE-EXACTLY-SQUARES.md); it retains the ordinary ribbon ideal square and proves that its sextic section space is generated by the determinant square. The theorem away from those parameters is HYPERPLANE-ADJOINT-EXHAUSTION-AND-SIX-TO-TEN.md. No claim about the rank of a quadric smoothing operator is used in these inputs.

The established exact identity C_quad=nu_*eta*(C_lin(1)) then makes C_quad locally free because nu is finite flat of degree two. The quadratic multiplication complex has kernel A tensor K, rank six, and locally free cokernel of rank ten, so it commutes with every fiber and has rank 38 everywhere on P. This includes the discriminant. This is a constant-rank statement about the already proved negative-quadratic restriction, not an assertion about adding a positive block.

## 3. Principal parts of a line bundle on P1

For any integer n there is the canonical exact sequence

    0 -> O(n-2) -> J^1(O(n)) -> O(n) -> 0.            (6)

Its extension class in H1(O(-2)) is n times the generator, up to the harmless common sign convention for the connecting map. This follows directly by differentiating the transition z^n: modulo the square of the diagonal ideal,

    (t+h)^n = t^n + n t^(n-1) h,      h^2=0.

If n!=0, (6) is the unique nonzero extension up to rescaling. The Euler sequence, twisted by O(n-1), gives such an extension, and hence

    J^1(O(n)) is isomorphic to O(n-1)^2,   n!=0.      (7)

For n=0 the extension is split and J^1(O)=O directsum O(-2); that exceptional case is not used in (4).

Now n=-5 for the source. For a summand O(d_i) of C_lin, n=d_i+1>=1 for the target. Thus (7) proves (4). The functor J^1 preserves direct sums, but the decomposition of C_lin and the resulting splitting in (4) are not canonical.

As degree checks, d*K has degree -12, equal to twice c1(K)=-6. The target has degree

    2 deg(C_lin(1)) + 5 deg(O(-2)) = 62-10=52,

equal to twice c1(C_quad)=26. These checks are consequences, not a substitute for (5).

## 4. The actual Hermite operator and its off-filtration block

Write m_qplus for positive ambient multiplication by q_+ on the quadratic kernel, followed by the actual cokernel projection. For the deformation q_-+s q_+=0, the earlier gluing convention gives d_gl=-m_qplus. This is the sign convention fixed in TWO-ROOT-DIVIDED-DIFFERENCE-SMOOTHING-FORMULA.md.

Restricting the global normal-direction map to the discriminant gives

    [A tensor J^1(O_T(-5))] tensor Sym^2 V* tensor O_T(2)
       -> J^1(C_lin(1)).                              (8)

For a fixed q_+, this is equivalently a map from A tensor J^1(O_T(-5)) to J^1(C_lin(1)) tensor O_T(-2). The O_T(2) is the pullback of the normal parameter line O_P(1), not an optional normalization.

On an affine pencil ell(z)=u+zv, let a(z):A->H0(B_R(3)) be the actual degree-six moving square inclusion, and k(z) its actual degree-five boundary syzygy. At q_-=ell(t)^2, write root data as c(z)=c0+c1(z-t) modulo (z-t)^2. The exact Hermite calculation gives the ambient source boundary

    -k'(t)c0-k(t)c1,

and the quotient plane jet

    H(z) = a''(t)c0/2+a'(t)c1
       +(z-t)[a'''(t)c0/6+a''(t)c1/2].                (9)

The map (8) is -delta_z(q_+H(z)) on the full dual-number root scheme, where delta_z is the actual relative plane-restriction boundary. The signed Bockstein is its negative. In particular the second output jet includes the variation of delta_z itself.

The source cotangent subbundle in (2) is locally c0=0. Projecting its image in (8) to the target value quotient in (3) gives

    c1 |-> -delta_t(q_+ a'(t)c1)                     (10)

for m_qplus, with opposite sign for d_gl. Globally this is a map

    A tensor O_T(-5) tensor Sym^2 V* -> C_lin(1),

because the source cotangent factor O_T(-7) has been tensored by the normal-direction line O_T(2). In local compatible frames, (10) is also -rho_t(mu_qplus(k(t)c1)), the actual diagonal hyperplane block. This follows by differentiating the genuine moving-section boundary identity, not by assigning an arbitrary derivative to a fixed matrix.

The composite defining (10) is canonical: source filtration inclusion, actual map (8), and target filtration quotient. Its local derivative expression does not require a connection. A change of moving section frame adds a multiple of a(t), whose image under delta_t after q_+ is zero. The root-coordinate and parameter-line changes are already included in (8).

No vanishing of (10) is established. Thus the actual smoothing map need not carry the source cotangent piece into the target cotangent piece. Even if a future argument proved that vanishing, the remaining diagonal pieces and the full Hermite variation would still need analysis. Neither the nonsplit jet extensions nor their noncanonical balanced splittings justify a rank assertion.

## Scope

The new statements are the exact discriminant principal-parts identity, its canonical filtrations, its splitting consequences without knowing the five individual degrees, and the actual off-filtration diagonal block. They retain both the nonreduced root scheme and the normal parameter line. No actual HM coefficient, chosen evaluation, rank scan, or new splitting enumeration is used.
