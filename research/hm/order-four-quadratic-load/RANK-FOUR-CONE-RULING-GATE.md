# A rank-four quadric cone gives a rank-30 balancedness gate

This is a new theoretical degeneration route, not a vanishing claim. It concerns the actual normalized HM bundle F and B=End_0(F). No source matrix, selected coefficients, or evaluated rank is used. The product-quadric obstruction published at 04ae00f0134c15d63dc39f84ce9a07b8ed81515d does not apply automatically to integral rank-four cones.

## Sources and current ownership

Current coordination was read through PR1 comment 6317634851. Null's full-adjoint gate remains open; Mr. Slop's primary Picard work is separate. Boralevi, *The Horrocks–Mumford bundle restricted to planes*, https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0602002, Main Theorem (printed pp.2–3) and Corollary 2.2 (printed p.5), gives the six plane-resolution strata and their dimensions. We use only this classification, not its displayed coefficient matrix. The established adjoint cohomology is chi(B(2))=chi(B(4))=-85.

## 1. All ruling planes of a general cone have acyclic twist-four adjoint

A rank-four quadric Q in P4 has one vertex v and two rulings by planes. Each ruling is a P1 in the Grassmannian of planes. The strata below the generic and divisor strata have dimension at most four, whereas the Grassmannian has dimension six. A general projective translate of either fixed ruling curve avoids their closures: the incidence of translates meeting a fixed codimension-two locus has dimension at most dim(PGL5)-1, because PGL5 acts transitively on the Grassmannian. Intersect the two resulting nonempty open conditions. Thus every plane in either ruling of a general Q has one of the two resolutions

    0 -> 3O(-3) -> 5O(-2) -> F_P -> 0,
    0 -> O(-4) -> O(-1) + 2O(-2) -> F_P -> 0.

Here B_P(4)=Sym^2(F_P)(5), since det(F_P)=O(-1). The standard symmetric-square complex of a locally split short exact bundle sequence is exact. For the first resolution it gives

    0 -> 3O(-1) -> 15O -> 15O(1) -> B_P(4) -> 0.

For the second it gives

    0 -> O + 2O(-1) -> O(3) + 2O(2) + 3O(1)
      -> B_P(4) -> 0.

Plane line-bundle cohomology therefore proves, in both cases,

    h0(B_P(4))=30,  H1(B_P(4))=H2(B_P(4))=0.

Both complexes also show global generation, since the last bundle before B_P(4) is globally generated. In particular the divisor-stratum encounters do not contribute cohomology jumps; one must not assume that every ruling plane is in the generic Steiner stratum.

### Exactly two nongeneric Steiner fibers

Boralevi gives the divisor-stratum closure degree five in the Plücker embedding. The Grassmannian Gr(3,5) itself has degree five and Picard group generated by its Plücker class. Therefore this effective divisor has class one Plücker hyperplane, not five. The ruling curve has universal plane subbundle O + O(-1)^2, hence Plücker degree two. A general translate meets the reduced divisor transversely in its smooth locus, and avoids the lower-dimensional strata. This follows from generic transversality for the transitive PGL5 action; alternatively the tangent incidence gives the same open condition in characteristic zero. Consequently each ruling has exactly two distinct divisor-stratum fibers, and every other fiber has the generic Steiner resolution. This count alone does not impose balancedness on the adjoint pushforward.

## 2. The exact ruling pushforward

Choose either small resolution pi:Y->Q. In quotient convention

    Y=P_{P1}(O + O(1) + O(1)),  p:Y->P1,
    H=O_Y(1)=pi^*O_Q(1).

The exceptional section maps to v and has normal bundle O(-1)^2. Put W=pi^*(B|Q(4)). The plane calculation and cohomology/base change show

    E_Q=p_*W is locally free of rank 30,
    R^i p_*W=0 for i>0,
    p^*E_Q -> W is surjective.

Also pi_*O_Y=O_Q and R^i pi_*O_Y=0 for i>0. For completeness, normality gives the first assertion. For the only possible higher direct image R1, formal functions at the vertex reduce to neighborhoods of the exceptional P1. Their graded structure sheaves are Sym^n(O(1)^2)=O(n)^{n+1}, all with vanishing H1. Induction on neighborhoods and formal functions give R1=0; fiber dimension excludes higher terms. Projection formula thus identifies H^i(Y,W)=H^i(Q,B_Q(4)).

The hypersurface restriction sequence gives chi(B_Q(4))=chi(B(4))-chi(B(2))=0. Consequently

    rank E_Q=30, deg E_Q=-30,
    H0(B_Q(4))=H0(P1,E_Q).

By splitting on P1 this proves the exact criterion

    H0(B_Q(4))=0  if and only if  E_Q=O(-1)^30.

Indeed all summands must have negative degree, and thirty negative integers summing to -30 must all equal -1. The two rulings supply two a priori different rank-30 bundles, but this criterion holds for each and their H0 and H1 agree with the same groups on Q. No equality of their splittings is asserted.

## 3. Vertex evaluation and a finite necessary splitting list

Restriction to the exceptional section gives a surjective bundle map

    E_Q -> B_v tensor O_{P1},

where the constant factor O_Q(4)|v has been absorbed into B_v. Surjectivity follows fiber by fiber from global generation of B_P(4). Let N_Q be its kernel. Then

    0 -> N_Q -> E_Q -> O^3 -> 0,
    rank N_Q=27, deg N_Q=-30, chi(N_Q)=-3.

Thus cone vanishing is equivalently BOTH H0(N_Q)=0 and the actual connecting map C^3->H1(N_Q) being an isomorphism. This is an exact extension criterion, not generic-position reasoning. If cone vanishing holds, the negative splitting of N_Q must be one of

    O(-4) + O(-1)^26,
    O(-3) + O(-2) + O(-1)^25,
    O(-2)^3 + O(-1)^24.

These are all partitions of the three excess negative degrees beyond rank 27. The finite list is necessary, not a proof that any listed bundle occurs for HM. Conversely a negative N_Q in this list is not sufficient: its three-dimensional H1 must be hit isomorphically by the actual vertex boundary.

## 4. A relative primary presentation of the adjoint gate

Put J_Q=p_*pi^*(F_Q(2)). For both plane resolutions above, F_P(2) is globally generated with h0=5 and higher cohomology zero. Thus J_Q is locally free of rank five and has no higher-direct-image corrections. The established primary Euler characteristics chi(F(2))=-10 and chi(F)=-5 give chi(F_Q(2))=-5, hence deg(J_Q)=-10.

In fact the actual multiplication map

    Sym^2(J_Q) -> p_*pi^*(B_Q(3))

is an isomorphism. On a generic plane, the symmetric-square resolution of 3O(-1)->5O gives h0(Sym^2(F_P(2)))=15 with higher cohomology zero and identifies its sections with Sym^2(C^5). On the divisor-stratum plane, use O(-2)->O(1)+2O. Its symmetric square has left term O(-1)+2O(-2) and right term O(2)+2O(1)+3O, again giving h0=15 and no higher cohomology. Products of the three linear sections and the two constant sections span precisely all these right-hand sections. The fiberwise isomorphisms prove the bundle assertion by base change.

Let T=p_*O_Y(H)=O+O(1)^2. Actual multiplication of plane sections further yields an exact sequence

    0 -> M_Q -> Sym^2(J_Q) tensor T -> E_Q -> 0,
    rank M_Q=15, deg M_Q=-120.

Surjectivity follows on generic planes from the symmetric-square Steiner complex. On a divisor-stratum plane, polynomial multiplication spans every summand of O(3)+2O(2)+3O(1) before quotienting by O+2O(-1), hence is also onto. The numerical calculation is deg(Sym^2 J_Q)=6 deg J_Q=-60, so the rank45 source has degree 3(-60)+15(2)=-150; subtracting deg E_Q=-30 gives -120. These are actual relative multiplication maps, not maps chosen with those ranks and degrees. Their transition data remain essential.

## 5. General cones have no twist-three adjoint section

The previously proved generic-product primary injection supplies a rank-two quadric q with H0(F_Q(2))=0. Primary rank-deficiency is closed on the irreducible variety of symmetric quadrics of rank at most four. It therefore cannot contain this entire variety. Its complement meets the dense rank-four locus, and a general rank-four cone is primary-good. Intersect this open condition with the ruling open used above.

Consequently H0(J_Q)=0, so all five summands of J_Q are negative. Section 4 then gives

    H0(B_Q(3))=H0(Sym^2 J_Q)=0.

This distinguishes general rank-four cones from the hyperplanes used in the product obstruction. It does not imply twist-four vanishing.

The source V_Q=Sym^2(J_Q) tensor (O+O(1)^2) has every summand of degree at most -1, and hence H0(V_Q)=0. Since M_Q is a subbundle, H0(M_Q)=0 as well. Both have Euler characteristic -105, so the exact twist-four gate becomes

    H0(B_Q(4)) = ker[H1(M_Q) -> H1(V_Q)],
    dim H1(M_Q)=dim H1(V_Q)=105.

This is the canonical map from the actual relative multiplication sequence, not a chosen square matrix. Its invertibility is not established. The generic-product primary-injection source is the PR543 file GENERIC-PRODUCT-PRIMARY-INJECTION.md, already used in the published product-obstruction note 04ae00f0134c15d63dc39f84ce9a07b8ed81515d.

## Remaining precise gate

The published plane resolutions control every fiber cohomology and remove all higher-direct-image corrections for a general rank-four cone. They do not determine the transition maps of E_Q, or the vertex extension class. Establishing the balanced splitting O(-1)^30 for even one such actual cone would give a full-adjoint vanishing witness and hence an open smooth-quadric vanishing locus by semicontinuity. An arbitrary rank-30 degree-minus-30 bundle cannot be substituted for this actual pushforward. No unavoidable nonzero section has been proved on rank-four cones.
