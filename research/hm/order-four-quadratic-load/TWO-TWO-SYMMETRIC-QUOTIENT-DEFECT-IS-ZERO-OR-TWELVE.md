# The (2,2) full derivative defect is zero or twelve

## Result

For a hypothetical maximal rank-three Gauss profile (2,2), put

    K = E^-, L = (Q*)^-, A = E/K, B = Q*/L.

These are the FULL derivative quotients; no identification with a quadratic or cubic subimage is made. The injective negative-transpose map

    A -> B*(-2)

has torsion cokernel of length either zero or twelve. Consequently either it is an isomorphism, or its determinant vanishes simply at the unique orbit of twelve points of the icosahedral action on P1. In the latter case the cokernel has length one at every point of that orbit, and the induced rank-three map has fiber rank two there. This concerns the induced quotient map, not a new computation of the actual HM maps.

A reproducible exhaustive representation calculation gives a finite necessary list of ordered splitting pairs (A,B), together with compatible primal and dual formal splitting types. The list is an overlist: no listed map or profile is asserted to exist. It does not exclude (2,2), prove SF>=4, or improve the square-rank bound 27.

## 1. Actual inputs and both negative towers

Use the actual splitting/persistence source and the strict top-slope lemma in ACTUAL-EQUIVARIANT-SPLITTING-AND-GAUSS-PERSISTENCE.md and NEGATIVE-GAUSS-KERNELS-STRICTLY-LOWER-THE-TOP-SLOPE.md. The primal has rank17, degree-54, all degrees at most-2. For k=2 dual persistence gives H0(Q*(1))=0, so Q* has rank13, degree-54 and all degrees at most-2 as well.

The published exhaustive lists are the 28 E types and the 37 Q* types of verify_adjacent_rank_three_gauss.py. Their individual degrees are at least-10 and at least-28, respectively. These minima are used only as safe bounds.

Strict decrease of the highest negative degree, applied on both sides, gives

    maxdeg K <= -3, maxdeg E^{--} <= -4,
    maxdeg L <= -3, maxdeg (Q*)^{--} <= -4.

In particular rank K=14 and rank L=10 imply

    deg A >= -12, deg B >= -24.                    (1)

The intrinsic derivatives K -> A(-2) and L -> B(-2) have the respective second backward transforms as kernels. Twisting by3 thus gives injections

    H0(K(3)) -> H0(A(1)), H0(L(3)) -> H0(B(1)).    (2)

No endpoint rank-one or rank-two degree estimate is used.

## 2. The full negative-transpose factorization

Dualizing the actual SF factorization E -> Q(-2), its transpose has kernel L, and its annihilator of the source kernel is A*. Hence it factors through an injective equal-rank map

    B -> A*(-2).

Equivalently, A -> B*(-2) is injective with the same determinant zero divisor. This is a sheaf injection, not necessarily a subbundle inclusion at its ramification points. Set t to the torsion length. Then

    t = -6 - deg A - deg B >= 0.

Its invariant effective determinant divisor has degree in the semigroup generated by12,20,30. By (1), t<=30, so initially

    t in {0,12,20,24,30}.                          (3)

It follows also that deg A<=18 and deg B<=6. Source global generation and the known target degrees give the exhaustive individual degree windows

    A: -10 <= e <=26; B: -28 <= e <=8.             (4)

For example A is a quotient of E and E(10) is globally generated, while A embeds into Q(-2), whose largest degree is at most26. The argument for B is symmetric.

## 3. Exact character envelopes

Write inequalities of characters to mean nonnegative multiplicities of every irreducible. The frozen Euler identities are

    chi(E(2)) = -V,
    chi(E(3)) = U = Wprime+2T6,
    chi(Q*(3)) = T6-2W-S4.

The Q* twist-two Euler character is calculated from its actual presentation in the verifier. From the exact quotient sequences and (2), necessary inequalities are

    chi(A(3))+H0(A(1)) >= U,
    chi(B(3))+H0(B(1)) >= T6-2W-S4.                (5)

The vanishings H0(K(2))=H0(L(2))=0 additionally give

    chi(A(2)) >= chi(E(2)),
    chi(B(2)) >= chi(Q*(2)),                      (6)

and inject H0(E(2)) into H0(A(2)), and H0(Q*(2)) into H0(B(2)). These last conditions depend on the chosen formal primal and dual type and are tested separately.

Every quotient block must receive a nonzero equivariant projection from its source. Every block of an injected bundle must have a nonzero map into its target. We impose these necessary Hom conditions for A from E into Q(-2), B from Q* into E*(-2), and between A and B*(-2) in both transpose directions. These conditions are not regarded as sufficient for fiberwise rank three. We also impose the ordinary necessary injection inequality: sorted ordinary degrees of A are componentwise at most those of B*(-2). If t=0, the injection is an isomorphism and the splitting must agree exactly.

## 4. Exhaustiveness and conclusion

A and B are center-even of rank three. Since the only irreducibles of dimension at most three are 1,W,Wprime,A,V, every splitting has exactly one of the forms:

- three even-degree trivial-coefficient lines;
- an odd-degree W or Wprime block and an even-degree line;
- one even-degree A or V block.

The verifier exhausts precisely these shapes within (1),(4), tests (5),(6), then the 28 by37 source/target combinations and (3). It evaluates only formal representation characters and integer bundle degrees. There are no selected coefficients, polynomial-point evaluations, actual-instance matrices, rank experiments or scans. The output JSON records every surviving necessary tuple.

All surviving records have t=0 or12. This proves the asserted restriction, since each filter above is necessary and the finite enumeration covers all splitting possibilities. For t=12 the only possible invariant divisor is the reduced orbit of size12: every other orbit has size20,30 or60, and a repeated twelve-orbit has degree at least24. The local structure theorem over a discrete valuation ring then gives elementary divisors (1,1,z), up to units, at each point of that orbit. This last statement is a universal local consequence of determinant order one, not an instance-matrix experiment.
