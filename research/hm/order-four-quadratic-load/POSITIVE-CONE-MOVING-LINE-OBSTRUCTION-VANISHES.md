# Actual positive moving-line adjoints vanish on the invariant cone

## The new conclusion

Every inversion-even adjoint section in H0(Q0,B(4)) preserves the actual saturated spinor S in E=F_Q0(2). This is stronger than imposing the candidate condition r in Hom(S,J(4)): the actual lower component of an endomorphism obeys a square-ideal condition.

Combined with the separately checked absence of positive filtration-preserving excess, this would give h0(B_Q0(4))_+=8 and positive q0 multiplication rank33. The argument below proves the moving-line assertion; it does not itself replace the separate preserving-line proof or prove the later smoothing scalar nonzero.

## 1. The actual lower component lies in a square ideal

For a tracefree map phi:E->E(4), put r=p(4) phi i. Embed J(4) into S(6). Then r is a global ordinary sextic, since Hom(S,S(6))=O_Q0(6). It is even if phi is even.

At the generic point of the negative vertex L, use the transverse cover B=k[[a,b]], R=B_even, with spinor M=B_odd. The actual quotient J is generated by two odd functions f,g, since E is locally free of rank two. The saturated exact sequence identifies its kernel with the odd spinor via

    h -> (-g h, f h),

up to an invertible factor. Thus in local trivializations, writing phi=[[alpha,beta],[gamma,-alpha]],

    r = beta f² - 2alpha f g - gamma g²

up to a unit. In particular r lies in (f,g)²_even. Merely belonging to Ann(T_L) would not imply this stronger condition.

The same local calculation at the smooth reduced conic C0 shows that r lies in I_C0² there. The spinor is invertible near C0, and its inclusion is a regular rank-two section with zero scheme exactly C0.

## 2. Parity and the two vanishing orders isolate one homogeneous piece

The unique actual vertex module gives generic odd Loewy dimensions 2,3,2. Accordingly one may choose f to begin with a nonzero cubic f3, and replace the second generator so that g has order at least five. Every generator has odd cover degree. Therefore the square-ideal condition forces r to have cover order at least six, or positive-coordinate order at least three, at the generic vertex.

On the quadric cone, ordinary sextics have a direct decomposition by positive-coordinate degree p and negative-coordinate degree 6-p; the equation q0 preserves this bigrading. Inversion-even sextics have p even. The vertex order condition excludes p=0 and p=2. At the conic, the condition I_C0² excludes the negative-degree-zero piece p=6. Consequently an even actual lower component can have only

    positive-coordinate degree4, negative-coordinate degree2.

In the spinor cover it is a binary form of degree8 whose coefficients are quadratic forms on the negative base. Equivalently it is a section of Sym8(W-prime) tensor O_P(W)(2).

## 3. Its remaining term must contain the cubic relation

Write f=f3+f5+..., g=g5+..., and write each coefficient alpha,beta,gamma as an even cover series. The cover-degree-eight term of the expression in Section 1 is a sum of terms of the forms

    beta2 f3², 2beta0 f3 f5, and -2alpha0 f3 g5.

A unit factor can add another multiple of f3². Every such term is divisible by f3. This reasoning does not require f or g to be globally homogeneous, nor does it discard higher-order corrections. It establishes divisibility of the only possible homogeneous part of r at the generic base point.

The actual first vertex relation is the everywhere nonzero cubic line

    K3=O_P(W)(-3) -> Sym3(W-prime) tensor O.

This follows from the actual first coordinate action Sym3(W-prime) O(1)->A O(2): its kernel is O(-2), or O(-3) after untwisting. Equivalently it is the universal cubic relation already determined by the unique vertex module. The relation has no exceptional zero fiber.

Multiplication by this line gives a subbundle

    Sym5(W-prime) tensor O(-3)
      -> Sym8(W-prime) tensor O.

It is a subbundle because multiplication by a nonzero binary cubic is injective at every base point, with constant rank. The quotient is therefore locally free. A section of the target whose quotient vanishes at the generic point has zero quotient everywhere.

Twisting by O(2), the generically established divisibility forces r to be a global section of

    Sym5(W-prime) tensor O(-1).

This has no global sections. Hence r=0.

## 4. Scope and dependencies

The actual even adjoint therefore preserves S. This eliminates the entire moving-line contribution without computing either stage of the candidate Hom/Ext lifting maps individually.

Inputs are the saturated spinor extension, the reduced conic first-jet result, the unique vertex module and its actual cubic relation. Generic two-generation of J comes from the actual free middle term, not from a Hilbert-function guess. The argument retains the non-Cartier spinor and does not identify Ann(T) with the extension-annihilator. All computations are universal local algebra and line-bundle degrees; no actual coefficient selection or matrix rank evaluation is used.
