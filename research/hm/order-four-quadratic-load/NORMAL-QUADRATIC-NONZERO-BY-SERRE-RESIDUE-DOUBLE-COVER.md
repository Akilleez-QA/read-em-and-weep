# Nonzero normal quadratic coefficient from an actual Serre-residue double cover

## Theorem

For the actual invariant cone Q and the fixed-determinant first-normal extension torsor, the quadratic coefficient d is NONZERO. Consequently the exact first obstruction c s+d s^2, with c!=0, has two distinct roots. Both roots come from actual ambient bundles, so the two later formal extension gates vanish at both roots. There are exactly two marked equivariant fixed-determinant formal-extension ISOMORPHISM CLASSES of the fixed cone bundle, not just at most two. All conclusions use the cited actual geometric and cohomological inputs; the companion verifier checks the displayed universal identities, not those entire geometric inputs or the original repair.

This does NOT prove the positive cubic Bockstein is nonzero. It determines the auxiliary normal quadratic coefficient and, by NORMAL-QUADRATIC-COEFFICIENT-UNIVERSAL-B-SQUARE.md, proves its universal constant kappa is nonzero without calculating a numerical value.

The geometric construction is the order-four block coordinate transformation which fixes positive coordinates and multiplies negative coordinates by a scalar mu with mu^2=-1. It preserves the actual zero SCHEME on the weighted stack, not just its support. Its pullback has the same cone bundle but a nonisomorphic ambient bundle. Formal restriction distinguishes these two ambient bundles, so they occupy the two different first-normal points.

## 1. The actual vertex zero scheme is homogeneous

Write X=P_stack(1,1,2,2), with positive cover coordinates a,b of weight1 and negative coordinates u,v of weight2. Let Z be the regular spinor zero scheme of the actual rank-two bundle G on X:

    0 -> O_X -> G -> I_Z(2) -> 0.

Physical O(1) means weighted O(2). The zero scheme has disjoint components: the reduced conic C and a vertex component supported on L=P(W). The latter lies in the negative-projection open X minus C.

The established actual residual odd quotient pushes to

    D=(A + Wprime tensor M) O_L(2), dim M=2.

Its even-coordinate module structure has unique nonzero channels determined by a vector a0 in M and a covector b0 in M*, with b0(a0)=0. In an abstract multiplicity basis, write B1=Wprime a0 and choose B2 complementary. The module action is

    B2 -> A -> B1 -> 0.

This is the already proved universal module normal form, not an evaluation of actual coefficients. Its source odd-spinor module is generated by Wprime O_L(2) in its lowest relative degree. Every G-equivariant map from these generators to D has the form v in M, because Hom_G(Wprime,A)=0 and all displayed bundles have the same O_L(2) twist. The map is a surjection, so b0(v)!=0: otherwise its image is contained in the killed B1 summand.

In the basis a0=e1, b0=e2*, module automorphisms act by

    A |-> k A,
    M |-> [[k,r],[0,k]] M,   k!=0.

More explicitly, the two action maps are a0 tensor U: V tensor A -> (Wprime tensor M) tensor W and b0 tensor Vmap: V tensor (Wprime tensor M) -> A tensor W. The displayed automorphism satisfies U_M a0=k a0 and b0 U_M=k b0. Hence it commutes with these maps including their base W factor, not only with a symbolic three-vertex diagram. In particular its upper-right entry shifts B2 into the annihilated B1 summand without changing either channel. If v=v1 e1+v2 e2 with v2!=0, take k=1/v2 and r=-v1/v2^2. This automorphism sends v to e2. Therefore EVERY actual generator-surjection has the same kernel as the homogeneous one sending its generators into B2.

Give B2,A,B1 relative degrees0,1,2. Even positive-coordinate multiplication raises degree by one, and the source has its natural positive-coordinate grading. The normalized surjection is graded, so its kernel is homogeneous. The negative projection on the COARSE open Q minus C is affine, so its module functor recovers the actual kernel inside the odd-spinor source, not only an abstract quotient isomorphism class. On each stack chart this is precisely the odd part of I_Z tensor O_wt(5); the stack projection itself is not being claimed representably affine. Thus homogeneity of the recovered coarse odd module means homogeneity of the odd ideal on the stack cover charts.

Locally at the vertex the ideal is generated by a regular pair of odd functions. Hence it is generated, as an ideal in the full cover ring, by its odd part. Homogeneity of that odd part proves homogeneity of the ENTIRE actual ideal I_Z at the vertex. This is the extra step needed to pass from the odd module to a scheme statement. The conic component is reduced and fixed under block scaling. Thus the entire Z is fixed as a scheme under scaling either coordinate block.

The homogeneous vertex ideal has generator degrees3 and5 in positive cover coordinates, by the established constant Loewy-layer theorem. A homogeneous local quintic lift is sufficient here; no global splitting of the nonsplit quintic-generator extension is used.

## 2. All invariant nilpotent units are accounted for

The regular-section Koszul sequence is

    0 -> O_X(-2) -> G* -> I_Z -> 0.

Here G*=F_hat tensor S_hat(-1). The ambient spinor presentation gives

    0 -> Wprime F(-2) -> Wprime F(-1) -> i_*pi_*G* -> 0.

The primary groups satisfy H^j(F(-1))=0 for all j, and H^j(F(-2)) is zero except H2(F(-2))=Wprime. Thus

    H1(G*)=Wprime tensor Wprime=1+V,
    H2(G*)=0,

and its H0 is zero. O_X(-2) is acyclic, as follows from the quadric hypersurface sequence and exact tame coarse pushforward. Consequently

    H0(O_Z)=2·1+V,   H1(O_Z)=0.                         (1)

Restriction to the two reduced components maps the two-dimensional invariant part isomorphically onto C plus C: the constants on each disjoint component are already two independent invariant sections. The kernel of restriction is the V summand and is nilpotent. There is no invariant nilpotent section. In fact its square is zero because Sym^2 V=1+D5 has no map to V, while a product of nilpotents cannot have a nonzero reduced constant component.

In particular

    H0(O_Z)^{G,units} = C* times C*.                     (2)

This argument explicitly retains the nilpotent structure rather than replacing Z by its reduced support.

## 3. The invariant Serre residue parameter

Let

    Lres = sheaf Ext^1(I_Z(2),O_X)
         = det(N_(Z/X)) tensor O_Z(-2).

This intrinsic line is also omega_Z tensor omega_X^{-1} tensor O_Z(-2). Local-to-global Ext, together with H1(O_X(-2))=H2(O_X(-2))=0, gives

    Ext^1_X(I_Z(2),O_X) = H0(Z,Lres).

The actual locally free extension supplies a nowhere-zero invariant residue, identifying Lres with O_Z equivariantly for G. The extension is locally free exactly when its residue is a unit at every point of Z. Therefore its invariant locally free classes form (C*)^2. Rescaling the two outer rank-one terms changes both residues by one common nonzero factor, so isomorphism classes with a distinguished section line are parametrized by their residue ratio in C*.

There are no extra global ideal automorphisms: Hom(I_Z,I_Z)=O_X because X is normal and I_Z agrees with O_X away from codimension two. Its global units are C*. Equality of residue ratios is therefore sufficient to give an equivariant isomorphism of the middle bundles, allowing the section to be scaled.

## 4. Intrinsic relative torus weights are four

Our convention is pullback on coordinate functions: phi_mu^*u=mu u and phi_mu^*v=mu v. The natural tautological linearization therefore gives weight mu^{-2} to the physical O(-2) frame u^{-2}. On sheaf Ext, conormal generators transform contravariantly, giving the inverse determinant character. Equivalently one can make all computations after the twofold torus cover mu=t^2; then every chart character below is integral, and the final characters descend.

Let phi_mu scale the negative coordinates u,v by mu and fix a,b. It commutes with G and fixes Z by Section1. We compute its action on Lres with its intrinsic normal and determinant factors.

At a point of C choose a positive coordinate chart. Its local ideal is generated by the two negative coordinates, each of weight mu. Thus det(N_(Z/X)) has weight mu^{-2} for the pullback action on extension residues. O_Z(-2) has weight1 there, since the positive chart frame is fixed. The conic residue weight is mu^{-2}.

At a point of L choose u!=0. On the covering chart the positive local coordinates are a/sqrt(u), b/sqrt(u), each of weight mu^{-1/2}. The homogeneous regular ideal generators have degrees3 and5, so their determinant has weight mu^{-4}; its dual det(N_(Z/X)) has weight mu^4. The physical O(-2) frame u^{-2} has weight mu^{-2}. Therefore the vertex residue weight is mu^2.

The resulting characters are integral and independent of the temporary square-root chart. The nonsplit global quintic relation does not change them: another homogeneous quintic generator differs by a degree-two multiple of the cubic generator and transforms with the same weight.

Since each invariant residue component is one-dimensional, the reduced-fiber weights determine its full character; Section2 excludes hidden invariant nilpotent directions. Thus the ratio of vertex to conic residue transforms by

    rho |-> mu^4 rho.                                  (3)

If mu^2=-1, both residues acquire the SAME scalar -1. Hence the actual G and phi_mu^*G are isomorphic on X. Descending and undoing the spinor twist gives

    F|Q is isomorphic to (phi_mu^*F)|Q.                  (4)

The natural equivariant structure of the spinor line is preserved by block scaling. The isomorphism may be multiplied by a constant square root to respect any fixed determinant framing. Thus (4) genuinely supplies a marking by the fixed cone bundle, not a new unspecified determinant line.

## 5. The two ambient bundles are nonisomorphic

The universal monad parameters are nonzero. Source gauges preserve the ratio r=a/b, while phi_mu acts by

    r |-> mu^{-2} r.

For mu^2=-1 this gives -r, which differs from r over C.

To see that this distinguishes actual bundles, use the ordered strong exceptional triple

    O(-1), K=Omega^2(2), O.

All positive Ext groups between its terms vanish. The reverse Hom groups vanish, and each term has scalar endomorphisms. These are the standard Bott identities: K and K*(-1)=Omega^2(2) tensor Delta^{-1} are acyclic; K(1) and K*=Omega^2(3) tensor Delta^{-1} have only H0; line-bundle assertions are immediate; K is an exceptional exterior-Euler bundle. Thus the derived Hom between two such monads is computed by their global chain-Hom complex. Its degree-minus-one terms vanish. Every isomorphism of their bundle cohomologies consequently lifts uniquely to a degree-zero chain isomorphism, which is diagonal in monad degree.

For G-equivariant maps, the middle coefficient Wprime has scalar endomorphisms, while the left coefficient N=A+Wprime permits only its two block scalars. Commutation with the left arrow rescales a and b equally. Thus a/b is invariant under every such chain isomorphism, proving F and phi_mu^*F are not G-equivariantly isomorphic.

They are not isomorphic even after forgetting equivariance. Both are stable and hence simple. Any ordinary isomorphism between two G-linearized simple bundles differs from equivariance by a character of G. The perfect group G has no nontrivial character, so an ordinary isomorphism would be equivariant, contrary to the preceding argument.

## 6. Formal restriction really distinguishes the two bundles

Let F'=phi_mu^*F. If F and F' were isomorphic on the formal completion along Q, they would be isomorphic on every Q_n. Set H=Hom(F,F'). For sufficiently large n,

    H1(H(-2n))=H3(H*(2n-5))*=0

by Serre duality and ordinary Serre vanishing. The restriction sequence therefore lifts an isomorphism on Q_n to a global homomorphism f:F->F'. Since det(F)=det(F')=O(-1), det(f) is a global constant, and it is nonzero on Q_n. It is a nonzero constant everywhere, so f is a global isomorphism. This contradicts Section5.

Thus these two ambient bundles give distinct marked formal extensions of the fixed cone bundle. They also give distinct marked first-normal points: the finite-normal-tower theorem proves every lift from 2Q onward is unique up to a unique marked isomorphism. An isomorphism between their marked 2Q restrictions would therefore extend compatibly to a formal isomorphism, which was just ruled out.

No algebraization theorem is being invoked. Both formal objects are already furnished by actual ambient bundles; the argument only proves injectivity of restriction for isomorphisms between these two bundles.

## 7. The quadratic coefficient cannot vanish

The earlier normal-derivative theorem gives the EXACT obstruction polynomial

    ob(s)=c s+d s^2,  c!=0,

and at most two liftable first-normal points. Sections4-6 construct two distinct points which lift to every order. Therefore d!=0. There are exactly two first-normal points extending to 3Q, each has a unique formal continuation, and both later scalar gates vanish on both points. The second root is -c/d in the coordinate based at the actual point.

Combined with d=kappa b^2 and the established b!=0, this also proves the universal cup constant kappa is nonzero. This is a geometric evaluation of its vanishing status, not a multiplication of two known nonzero legs.
