# Hyperplane adjoints are exactly squares, and the generic odd bound is six

## Main result

For the actual normalized HM bundle F and B=End_0 F, let H_lambda be a hyperplane containing the positive fixed plane R, parameterized by the negative line T. Outside the twelve order-five parameters,

    H0(H_lambda,B(3)) = Sym^2 H0(H_lambda,F(2)),
    h0(H_lambda,B(3)) = 3.                              (1)

All three sections are inversion-even. There are no other hyperplane adjoints at these parameters, of either parity.

Consequently, for a general smooth inversion-even quadric Q,

    h0(Q,B_Q(4))_- <= 6.                                (2)

For a negative quadratic q_- with two distinct roots outside the twelve exceptional parameters, the reducible quadric D=(q_-=0) has precisely six odd adjoint sections. Its actual first global smoothing obstruction in the family D_s=(q_-+s q_+=0) is a map

    d_gl: C^6 -> C^10.                                 (3)

This is the existing global smoothing map with its now-proved exact source and target dimensions. No injectivity or other rank assertion about (3) is made. In particular (2) is an upper bound, not generic odd vanishing. No maximal-rank assertion about the earlier quartic normal map beta is needed.

## 1. Actual geometric inputs

The checked primary-source application is [REDUCED-DETERMINANT-THREE-CONIC-PENCIL.md, pin35fc96](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/35fc96bebf9c771a4de481c753bb527e510cd8c1/research/hm/order-four-quadratic-load/REDUCED-DETERMINANT-THREE-CONIC-PENCIL.md). For a stable rank-two E0 on P3 with c1=-1,c2=4,h0(E0(1))=0,h0(E0(2))=2 and reduced determinant cubic, it proves that the determinant is three distinct planes and that a general section of E0(2) vanishes on three disjoint reduced plane conics, one on each plane. A reduced conic may be two meeting lines; smoothness is not required.

The actual hyperplane theorem in HYPERPLANE-SQUARE-SURVIVORS.md gives, for every lambda,

    h0(F_Hlambda(1))=0,   h0(F_Hlambda(2))=2,

and both sections in twist two are even. The actual determinant linearization identifies

    B_Hlambda(3) = Sym^2(F_Hlambda(2))                  (4)

equivariantly.

THREE-LINE-FACTOR-COVER-GEOMETRY.md proves that the actual degree-three line-factor cover has genus four and that its line-factor polarization L satisfies pi_*L=V tensor O_T. It follows that every length-three factor fiber spans the full dual plane. There are exactly twelve nonreduced determinant members, at the order-five parameters. At every other parameter its three line factors are distinct and linearly independent. Thus the three associated planes in H_lambda are nonconcurrent along a line: they meet only at the negative vertex.

Fix any lambda outside those twelve points. In the rest of Sections 2-4 write

    E=F_Hlambda(2),  det E=O_P3(3),
    S=h1 h2 h3,
    C=C1 disjoint-union C2 disjoint-union C3,
    Ci subset Pi=(hi=0),

where C is the zero scheme of a suitable general section s of E. Choose a second section t completing a basis. The regular-section sequence gives

    0 -> O -> E -> I_C(3) -> 0,

so H0(I_C(3)) is one-dimensional and generated by S=s wedge t. Each Ci is a reduced plane conic, with plane equation qi=0.

## 2. A length-two plane intersection imposes a unique double quadratic

Let Z be the length-two intersection of a plane conic with a line ell, where ell is not a component of the conic. Its homogeneous ideal in that plane is the complete intersection

    I_Z=(ell,q),      deg ell=1, deg q=2.

Its square has the standard resolution

    0 -> O(-4) directsum O(-5)
      -> O(-2) directsum O(-3) directsum O(-4)
      -> I_Z^2 -> 0.                                  (5)

The generators are ell^2,ell q,q^2, and the two syzygies have degrees four and five. Since H1(O_P2(k))=0 for every k, twisting (5) by two gives

    H0(I_Z^2(2))=C ell^2.                              (6)

This concerns the actual ordinary ideal square, not merely pointwise double vanishing. It also applies when Z is a tangent length-two scheme supported at one point. The complete-intersection resolution verifies saturation in this calculation; no reducedness of Z is assumed.

## 3. The only sextic double along C is S squared

We claim

    H0(P3,I_C^2(6))=C S^2.                            (7)

Take F6 in the space on the left. Restrict it to Pi. Double vanishing along Ci gives

    F6|Pi = qi^2 ri,

where ri is a quadratic on Pi. For j different from i, let

    Zj=Cj intersect Pi.

This is a length-two complete intersection on the line ell_ij=Pi intersect Pj. That line cannot be a component of Cj: otherwise it would meet Ci, contradicting the disjointness of the conics. Moreover Zj is disjoint from Ci, so qi is a unit in a neighborhood of Zj.

The restriction of the ordinary ideal square I_C^2 to Pi has image I_Zj^2 near Zj. Hence qi^2 ri belongs to I_Zj^2 there, and division by the unit qi^2 gives ri in I_Zj^2. Equation (6) forces

    ri in C ell_ij^2.

Applying this to both j different from i places ri in the intersection of the two lines C ell_ij^2 and C ell_ik^2. The two plane-intersection lines are distinct by nonconcurrence. Therefore ri=0.

It follows that F6 vanishes identically on each Pi. Their distinct equations divide F6, so

    F6=S G3

for a cubic G3. At the generic point of every irreducible component of Ci, the other two plane equations are units, and (I_C^2:S)=I_C there: locally this is the elementary identity ((h,q)^2:h)=(h,q) for a regular sequence. Therefore G3 vanishes generically on every component of C. The curve C is reduced, so G3 belongs to I_C globally. The one-dimensionality of H0(I_C(3)) gives G3 proportional to S. This proves (7).

This last cancellation is precisely where the reducedness supplied by the classification is used. No assertion that arbitrary nonreduced curves behave similarly is needed.

## 4. Exhaustion of the adjoint space

For the regular section s of E there is an exact symmetric-square sequence

    0 -> E --(multiply by s)--> Sym^2 E
      -> I_C^2 tensor (det E)^2 -> 0.                  (8)

Locally, if s=(f,g) is a regular sequence, the right-hand map is generated by g^2,-fg,f^2, and its syzygies are exactly multiplication by s. Thus (8) has the ordinary ideal square I_C^2, not an unverified replacement by a symbolic square.

By (7), the right-hand global-section space in (8) is C S^2. The section t^2 maps to (s wedge t)^2=S^2, which is nonzero. Hence (8) gives an exact sequence on global sections

    0 -> H0(E) -> H0(Sym^2 E) -> C S^2 -> 0.

Since h0(E)=2, the middle space has dimension three and is spanned by s^2,st,t^2. Equation (4) proves (1). Both s and t are even, so all three products are even. This holds for every lambda outside the twelve nonreduced determinant parameters, not just for an unspecified generic hyperplane.

The conclusion identifies the terminal global hyperplane kernel with the forced squares. It does not assert that every earlier normal-obstruction kernel already has dimension three; beta may have extra classes later removed by the next obstruction.

## 5. The reducible quadric has exactly six odd sections

Choose two distinct good parameters lambda_1,lambda_2 and let

    D=H_lambda1 union H_lambda2=(q_-=0).

The normalization exact sequence is equivariant:

    0 -> B_D(4) -> B_Hlambda1(4) directsum B_Hlambda2(4)
      -> B_R(4) -> 0.

The fiber action on B_R(4) is even. On either hyperplane, multiplication by its negative normal coordinate identifies the odd sections of B_H(4) with the even sections of B_H(3), since the odd restriction to R is zero. Keeping the corresponding one-dimensional coordinate factors, this gives

    H0(B_D(4))_- =
      H0(B_Hlambda1(3))_+ tensor lambda_1^*
      directsum H0(B_Hlambda2(3))_+ tensor lambda_2^*.

By (1), its dimension is exactly six. These are precisely the previously identified two-root square survivors; there are no additional global hyperplane contributions at good roots.

## 6. Semicontinuity and the exact first smoothing dimensions

Take a nondegenerate positive quadratic q_+ and form the flat quadric family

    D_s=(q_-+s q_+=0).

Its nonzero fibers are smooth inversion-even quadrics. The restriction of B(4) is a flat vector-bundle family on this flat proper family of hypersurfaces. Taking the odd direct summand commutes with cohomology and base change because the involution has order two in characteristic zero. Upper semicontinuity and the six-dimensional central fiber give

    h0(B_Ds(4))_- <= 6

on a nonempty open set of the parameter line. Upper semicontinuity in the full irreducible smooth even-quadric parameter space then proves the generic bound (2).

For the precise obstruction target, the established actual ambient cohomology gives

    H1(B(2))_- of dimension44,
    H1(B(4))_- of dimension48,
    H2(B(2))=0,

and H0(B(2))=H0(B(4))=0. The actual restriction sequence therefore identifies

    H0(B_D(4))_- = ker mu_q-,
    H1(B_D(4))_- = coker mu_q-,
    mu_q-: C^44 -> C^48.

Its kernel has dimension six, so its rank is38 and its cokernel has dimension ten. The actual positive multiplication map induced on the kernel and cokernel is

    m_qplus: ker mu_q- -> coker mu_q-,
    a |-> [mu_q+(a)].                                 (9)

For exact operator comparisons, retain the gluing convention in GLOBAL-SMOOTHING-TO-WEIGHTED-THIRD.md, Section 3: T_ij a_j-a_i=q_- c_ij, and substitution q_-=-s q_+ gives the deformation obstruction -q_+ c_ij. With that connecting identification the named global smoothing map is d_gl=-m_qplus, not m_qplus. Thus the signed first deformation/Bockstein operator is the negative of (9); changing an overall cochain convention changes both compared operators together. This agrees with TWO-ROOT-DIVIDED-DIFFERENCE-SMOOTHING-FORMULA.md: m_qplus=-delta(q_+H) and d_gl=+delta(q_+H). The two maps have identical kernels, ranks, and six-to-ten dimensions. No dimension or vanishing conclusion changes. This is the actual global gate, not a matrix chosen from its dimensions.

The ambient H2 vanishing used here is the actual PR167 result at [pin063da90](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/063da90fc4543304e87e701cdefcc08225412f17/research/conductor-normalization/sharper-hm-regularity/SHARPEST-BOUND.md); its proof uses the primary HM resolution. The ambient parities and the functorial comparison with weighted third-normal obstructions are recorded in GLOBAL-SMOOTHING-TO-WEIGHTED-THIRD.md, [pin f8725aa](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/f8725aa205bb400d9431d33049c2ffa117f90126/research/hm/order-four-quadratic-load/GLOBAL-SMOOTHING-TO-WEIGHTED-THIRD.md).
