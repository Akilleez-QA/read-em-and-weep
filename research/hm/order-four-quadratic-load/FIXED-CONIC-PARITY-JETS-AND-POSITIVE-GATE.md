# Fixed-conic parity jets and the actual positive excess gate

## Results and scope

Let Q be a smooth inversion-even quadric, let C=Q intersect P(V_+) be its fixed conic, and put I=I_(C/Q), B=End_0(F), G=B_Q(4). In this note C is the fixed conic, not the25-line evaluation locus.

1. Every even section of G vanishing on C vanishes on the doubled conic defined by I^2. Every odd section vanishes on C; if its first normal jet vanishes, it lies in I^3 G. These are scheme-theoretic ideal-power statements.
2. Every smooth fixed conic satisfies h0(B_C(4))=27 and H1(B_C(4))=0. Its doubled neighborhood has a canonical equivariant splitting, with section parities(27,42) and H1=0; the odd summand is the first normal-jet space.
3. On the nonempty smooth-even open where H0(B_Q(3))=0, there is an actual map

       alpha_Q:H1(B_Q(1))_-^2 -> H1(B_Q(2))_+^3,
                       64 -> 87,

   and a canonical exact sequence

       0 -> ker alpha_Q -> H0(B_Q(4))_+ -> C^27
         --partial_Q--> coker alpha_Q -> H1(B_Q(4))_+ -> 0.

4. Restriction to O_Q/I^7 is injective for every smooth even Q; odd sections are detected already modulo I^6. Thus even leading normal orders are at most6 and odd orders at most5.

The positive excess vanishes exactly when the actual boundary partial_Q is onto. Neither this surjectivity nor any maximal-rank assertion for alpha_Q follows from the dimensions. In particular H1(B_Q(1)) is retained, not discarded.

## 1. Canonical parity of every associated-graded piece

The ideal I is generated by the actual two odd ambient linear forms u,v. They are a regular sequence on Q. If W=span(u,v), then its character is negative and

       I/I^2 = W tensor O_C(-1).

The involution fixes C pointwise, and O_C(1) has positive fiber character because C lies in P(V_+). Evaluation from the scalar representation U is surjective on the positive fixed plane, so F(3) has scalar fiber action there. Therefore End_0(F) and G|C have positive fiber character. No equivariant trivialization of G in a neighborhood is used.

Regular immersion gives the canonical equivariant identities

       I^m G/I^(m+1)G
         = G|C tensor Sym^m(I/I^2)
         = B_C(4-m) tensor Sym^m W.

Every such graded piece has pure character(-1)^m. Consequently, for sigma different from(-1)^m, the map

       H^j(Q,I^(m+1)G)_sigma -> H^j(Q,I^m G)_sigma

is an isomorphism for every j. This follows from the exact quotient sequence: the quotient and all its cohomology have only the opposite character, and eigenspace projection is exact in characteristic zero. The same assertion on invariant open sets proves the scheme-theoretic section statement, rather than only a numerical global vanishing.

In particular,

       H0(IG)_+ = H0(I^2G)_+,
       H0(G)_- = H0(IG)_-,
       H0(I^2G)_- = H0(I^3G)_-.

The first normal jet of an odd section is its canonical class in IG/I^2G. Its vanishing therefore forces order at least three. More generally, positive sections can have only even leading normal order, and negative sections only odd leading normal order. This does not assert that any prescribed jet lifts.

## 2. The actual conic splitting and its cohomology

The fixed-plane theorem in INVERSION-EVEN-TWIST-THREE-VANISHING.md proves

       0 -> 3O_R(-3) -> 5O_R(-2) -> F_R -> 0,
       R=P(V_+).

Twisting by2 proves that F_R(2) is globally generated, hence so is F_C(2). Identify the smooth conic with P1, so O_C(1)=O_P1(2). Birkhoff-Grothendieck and deg(F_C)=-2 give

       F_C = O_P1(a) directsum O_P1(-a-2),  a>=-1.

Global generation of F_C(2)=O(a+4) directsum O(2-a) forces a<=2. Thus a is one of-1,0,1,2, without any assertion that all four possibilities occur. It follows that

       B_C(4)=O(8) directsum O(2a+10) directsum O(6-2a).

All three degrees are at least2. Hence H1=0 and h0=27, uniformly for every smooth fixed conic.

The first normal graded piece is B_C(3) tensor W. Its three underlying degrees are6,2a+8,4-2a, all nonnegative. It therefore has H1=0, h0=2*21=42, and pure odd parity. For comparison, the second normal graded piece has h0=45 and H1=0 for a<=1, but h0=48 and h1=3 if a=2. Thus acyclicity of higher conic neighborhoods must not be inferred from the reduced conic calculation.

### Canonical splitting on the first infinitesimal neighborhood

Write 2C for the subscheme defined by I^2. Its underlying topological space is C, so the involution acts on its structure sheaf as an algebra automorphism over that space. There is an equivariant square-zero sequence

       0 -> N* -> O_(2C) -> O_C -> 0,
       N*=I/I^2,

with N* pure odd and O_C pure even. Exactness of the invariant functor gives a canonical isomorphism of algebras

       O_(2C)^+ -> O_C.

The invariant subsheaf is a subalgebra, so its inverse supplies a canonical algebra section and retraction 2C -> C. Thus O_(2C)=O_C directsum N* as a split square-zero algebra, canonically and equivariantly. This uses the actual involution, not a choice of a splitting of an extension class.

For M=G|2C, flatness gives

       0 -> N* tensor G_C -> M -> G_C -> 0.

The kernel is pure odd and the quotient pure even. Hence M^+ maps isomorphically to G_C, while M^- is exactly N* tensor G_C. Multiplication defines the canonical equivariant isomorphism

       O_(2C) tensor_(O_C) G_C -> M.

It is an isomorphism on both eigensummands; the tensor product uses the canonical algebra section above. In particular

       H0(G|2C) = H0(G_C) directsum H0(N* tensor G_C),
       parity dimensions=(27,42),       H1(G|2C)=0.

The odd summand is the genuine first normal-jet space. This does not provide a splitting on any higher neighborhood, or lift these69 first-neighborhood sections to Q.

## 3. Actual resolutions of the ideal powers

For the regular sequence(u,v), the equivariant power resolution is

       0 -> Sym^(m-1)W tensor det W tensor O_Q(-m-1)
         -> Sym^m W tensor O_Q(-m) -> I^m -> 0.

The first representation has sign(-1)^(m-1), the second sign(-1)^m; the maps use the actual odd generators. Tensoring by G yields

       0 -> B_Q(3-m) tensor Sym^(m-1)W tensor det W
         -> B_Q(4-m) tensor Sym^m W -> I^mG -> 0.

For m=2 this is

       0 -> B_Q(1) tensor W tensor det W
         -> B_Q(2) tensor Sym^2 W -> I^2G -> 0.

Thus the left multiplicity two has odd character and the right multiplicity three has even character. The actual positive cohomology map is exactly alpha_Q above, not a chosen map between vector spaces of the same dimensions.

## 4. The missing twist-one group is32-dimensional in odd parity

The uniform twist-one index can be checked directly. In the ambient exterior-symmetric resolution for B(-1), the left O(-11) has H4 dimension210 and trace10, the middle wedge^2 U O(-6) has H4 dimension30 and trace6, the primary U F(-3) is acyclic, and the25-line tail has H1 dimension175 and trace7. Consequently

       chi(B(-1))=5,    chi^iota(B(-1))=-3.

PR455 proves H3(B(-1))=0. Also H4(B(-1))=0, for example by Serre duality and stability: its dual is H0(B(-4))=0. The proved H2(B(1))=0 and the restriction sequence B(-1) --q--> B(1) give H2(B_Q(1))=H3(B_Q(1))=0.

Since chi(B(1))=-55 and chi^iota(B(1))=1, for even q one obtains

       chi(B_Q(1))=-60,   chi^iota(B_Q(1))=4,
       (chi_+,chi_-)=(-28,-32).

On the actual lower-good open H0(B_Q(3))=0, multiplication by nonzero forms on integral Q forces H0(B_Q(2))=H0(B_Q(1))=0. The lower indices therefore give

       H1(B_Q(1)) parity=(28,32),
       H1(B_Q(2)) parity=(29,34).

This proves the source64 and target87 of alpha_Q. Taking cohomology of the m=2 resolution, and retaining the already proved higher vanishings, gives

       0 -> H0(I^2G)_+ -> C^64 --alpha_Q--> C^87
         -> H1(I^2G)_+ -> 0,
       H^j(I^2G)_+=0 for j>=2.

No vanishing of H1(B_Q(1)) was assumed; it supplies the entire64-dimensional source.

## 5. Positive restriction and the actual excess criterion

Section1 identifies the positive cohomology of IG and I^2G in every degree. Apply positive cohomology to

       0 -> IG -> G -> G|C -> 0.

Using Section2 and the preceding exact presentation gives precisely

       0 -> ker alpha_Q -> H0(G)_+ -> H0(G|C)
         --partial_Q--> coker alpha_Q -> H1(G)_+ -> 0,
       dim H0(G|C)=27.

The boundary is the one induced by this actual restriction sequence. It is not an independently chosen map to the cokernel. The known invariant Euler index is4, so H0(G)_+ has dimension4+dim coker(partial_Q).

For explicit bounds, write k=dim ker alpha_Q and r=rank partial_Q. Then

       dim coker alpha_Q=23+k,
       h1(G)_+=23+k-r >= max(0,k-4),
       h0(G)_+=27+k-r >= max(4,k).

Thus k>=5 already forces positive excess. If alpha_Q is injective, the remaining positive gate is the surjectivity of the actual27-to23 boundary. Injectivity of alpha_Q is sufficient to reduce the size of that gate, but is not necessary for exact total corank four.

Away from the lower-good open the same ideal-power and conic restriction sequences remain valid. One must retain the lower H0 terms and use the actual H1 groups in alpha_Q; their dimensions are then not fixed64 and87. The equivalence H1(G)_+=0 iff partial_Q is onto remains valid with those actual groups, since H2(B_Q(1))=0 still holds.

## 6. Odd first jets and the retained third-order dependency

Odd sections have a canonical first normal-jet map

       H0(G)_- -> H0(B_C(3) tensor W),

whose target has dimension42. Its kernel is exactly H0(I^3G)_- by Section1. On the lower-good open the m=3 power resolution gives

       H0(I^3G)_-
         =ker[H1(B_Q)_-^3 -> H1(B_Q(1))_+^4],

with right-hand target dimension112. The source H1(B_Q)_- is retained as an actual cohomology group; it is not declared zero. Consequently the42-dimensional jet space by itself does not prove detection of every odd section.

These results complement the separate actual34-to38 odd Koszul gate. They give a new positive restriction-boundary criterion and exact parity orders, not a rank computation, an extension theorem for arbitrary conic jets, or generic twist-four vanishing.

## 7. Uniform finite normal-jet detection

The seventh-power resolution has the form

       0 -> B_Q(-4) tensor Sym^6 W tensor det W
         -> B_Q(-3) tensor Sym^7 W -> I^7G -> 0.

Both cohomology terms that could supply H0(I^7G) vanish, without any lower-good or quadric-restriction stability assumption.

First, Q is a smooth quadric threefold, so self-duality of B_Q and Serre duality give

       H1(B_Q(-4)) = H2(B_Q(1))* = 0.

The last vanishing was established uniformly in Section4. Second, the ambient restriction sequence

       0 -> B(-5) -> B(-3) -> B_Q(-3) -> 0

shows H0(B_Q(-3))=0. Indeed the negative ambient section groups vanish by stability of F, and H1(B(-5)) is dual to H3(B)=0, established in the exact ambient cohomology theorem. Hence

       H0(Q,I^7 B_Q(4))=0.

Therefore restriction to O_Q/I^7 is injective on all adjoint sections. This means normal jets through degree6; it is the subscheme defined by the seventh power of the ideal, not a claim of first-jet injectivity. The argument actually applies to any plane complete-intersection conic on a smooth quadric with these ambient cohomology inputs. Inversion is needed only for the following refinement.

The parity identities show H0(I^6G)_-=H0(I^7G)_-=0. Consequently odd sections are detected already by O_Q/I^6, namely jets through normal degree5. Every nonzero even section has leading normal order among0,2,4,6, and every nonzero odd section has leading order among1,3,5.

The actual splitting gives a further bounded refinement without asserting existence of any splitting stratum. If a<=0, the sixth normal graded piece has no sections, so a nonzero even section has normal order at most4. If a=-1, the fifth normal graded piece also has no sections, so a nonzero odd section has normal order at most3. For a=1 or2 the uniform bounds6 and5 remain; no prescribed leading jet is asserted to occur.

This is a finite filtration of the actual section space. It does not prove surjectivity onto the allowed jet spaces, the64-to87 map's injectivity, the27-to-cokernel boundary's surjectivity, or the separate34-to38 odd gate.
