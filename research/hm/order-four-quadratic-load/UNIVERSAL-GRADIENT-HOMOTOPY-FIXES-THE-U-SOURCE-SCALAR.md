## 1. The source coefficient has only its scalar channel

## 3. Proof of the compression identity

Let p,q be arbitrary U vectors and write L_p=L(p). Define the U-symplectic contraction of two V-valued forms by epsilon_U. The marked Clifford commutator formula implies

    epsilon_U(L_p wedge L_q) wedge r(v)
        =-(g_L/(2chi)) epsilon_U(p,M_v q) tau.        (5)

Here is an explicit basis-free check of the coefficient in (5). In any dual pair of bases, the coefficient of the wedge v_i wedge v_j is

    epsilon_U(M_i p,M_j q)-epsilon_U(M_j p,M_i q)
       =-epsilon_U(p,[M_i,M_j]q).

The marked commutator identity is [M_i,M_j]=(g_L/(2chi))M_(e_i cross_q e_j). Contracting with the polar vector r(v)=b_q(v,-) cancels the inverse polar form in the cross product and yields M_v. This proves (5), with no coordinate values selected.

Now

    B0(p)=M_v p omega_W-L_p wedge j.

Using (3),(4), the V^3 W component of the wedge pairing of B0(p) with R_Da B0(q) is

    -2a epsilon_U(L_p wedge L_q) wedge r(v) wedge j.

The V^3 W component of i_x Omega is -tau wedge j. Substituting (5) identifies the scalar pairing as

    -(g_L/chi) a epsilon_U(p,M_v q).

Both vectors lie in K, so their determinant-valued wedge pairing lies in the line generated by i_x Omega. Thus this coefficient comparison proves the full identity (2), not only its displayed component. The verifier also checks all exterior components directly.

## 4. The two-arrow skewness forces the source scalar

Use the two remaining U-column equations, with Q_a=0:

    M_a B0+M_b A0=A0 Q_b+C0 S,

    -B0* M_a B0-Q_b* M-M Q_b=2a M.                  (6)
