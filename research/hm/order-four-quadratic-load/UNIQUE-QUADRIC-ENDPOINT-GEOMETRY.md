# The endpoint a=1, b=6 forces an integral genus-two curve and constant evaluation kernel

Assume the generic endpoint a=1,b=6 in UNIQUE-QUADRIC-CUBIC-BOUND.md. Its fixed curve D on the unique integral quadric Q has degree five, contains all 25 section points Z, and has reduced positive-degree image with no line component. We prove two further necessary constraints, not a contradiction.

## 1. The fixed curve is geometrically integral

A general quintic combination from the six ideal generators avoids every component of D. Its intersection with D has total length 25, exhausted by the 25 distinct points Z. Therefore each intersection is reduced, and Z avoids singular points of D, in particular intersections of distinct components. Each positive-degree irreducible component D_i receives exactly 5 deg(D_i) distinct labels.

There are no line components and the total degree is five. Thus if D were geometrically reducible it would consist of one degree-two and one degree-three component. Their disjoint label blocks would have sizes ten and fifteen.

This partition is canonical: D is the fixed curve of the intrinsic cubic system on the unique quadric. To express equivariance precisely, work over the function field of the hyperplane parameter space. The 25 labeled points are rational over that field because they arise by intersecting the fixed lines with the generic hyperplane. The normalizer acts semilinearly on the field and permutes those labels, preserving the fixed-curve construction. The two components have different degrees, so the normalizer cannot interchange their ten- and fifteen-label blocks. This contradicts transitivity on the labels. Thus D is geometrically integral.

The same reasoning is compatible with the cone resolution: exceptional components have hyperplane degree zero, carry no Z points, and are omitted from the image curve D.

## 2. Its arithmetic genus is two

On a smooth quadric the residual ruling pencil leaves D of type (2,3) or (3,2), so its arithmetic genus is (2-1)(3-1)=2.

On a quadric cone one must not assume that its strict transform is always 2E+5f. The full fixed divisor on F2 is 3E+5f, but more than one copy of E may occur. The arithmetic genus downstairs can be calculated without that assumption. The divisor class group of the normal cone is generated by a ruling line L, with hyperplane class H=2L. This follows equally from Pic(F2) modulo its exceptional class E. A degree-five prime divisor has class 5L=2H+L. Hence its divisorial ideal satisfies

    I_(D,Q) isomorphic to I_(L,Q)(-2).

Using the exact sequence for the ruling line gives

    chi(O_D(t))
      =chi(O_Q(t))-chi(O_Q(t-2))+chi(O_L(t-2))
      =(t+1)^2-(t-1)^2+(t-1)=5t-1.

Thus p_a(D)=2 in the cone case as well. This is arithmetic genus, not a smoothness assertion. In particular singular behavior at the cone vertex can change the genus of a resolution of D.

## 3. All six quintic restrictions are proportional

Choose a quintic generator combination s not vanishing identically on D. The preceding intersection count shows that its zero divisor on D is exactly the reduced scheme Z. Since Z is disjoint from singular points of D, it is an effective Cartier divisor. Therefore

    O_D(5)(-Z) isomorphic to O_D.

Every restricted quintic generator vanishes on Z. Since D is geometrically integral and projective, h^0(O_D)=1. It follows that the image of the six-dimensional quintic generator space in H^0(O_D(5)) is exactly one-dimensional. It is nonzero by the choice of s.

## 4. A fixed two-dimensional global-section subspace vanishes on D

Let E=F(3) and U_E=H^0(E), of dimension four. The six quintic generators are the actual exterior minors of the evaluation U_E tensor O -> E. On D minus Z this evaluation has rank two, because its rank-drop scheme is the HM line arrangement C and C intersect H=Z.

The projective vector of its exterior minors is constant along D minus Z by Section 3. Equivalently the quotient U_E -> E_p has constant Plucker point there. Its two-dimensional kernel is therefore a fixed subspace S_H of U_E, independent of p in this dense open of D. Each section in S_H vanishes on that open, hence on all of the integral curve D. Consequently

    dim S_H=2,  S_H contained in H^0(E tensor I_D).

Here I_D is the ideal of the curve in P4. The subspace may vary with the hyperplane H. This is an actual evaluation-kernel consequence, not an assertion that S_H equals an original prescribed source subspace or that D lifts to a fixed surface.

## Boundary

The endpoint now forces a geometrically integral degree-five arithmetic-genus-two curve and a two-dimensional subspace of global E-sections vanishing along it. Neither condition is shown impossible. No smoothness, source identification, global lifting, selected coefficient evaluation, or full Q4 vanishing is asserted.

The companion EXCLUDE-UNIQUE-QUADRIC-ENDPOINT.md subsequently proves that this entire generic endpoint is impossible, using the actual evaluation geometry and Heisenberg-invariant surface argument. The constraints here are its intermediate input, not a still-viable endpoint.
