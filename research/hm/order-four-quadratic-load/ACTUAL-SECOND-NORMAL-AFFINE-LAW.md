# Actual affine dependence of the second-normal HM class

## Results

Fix a balanced smooth conic C=(q_+=0) in the positive fixed plane R=P(V_+) of HM inversion. Put W=V_-*, B_R=End_0(F_R), and

    V_C=H1(C,B_C(-2)),          dim V_C=9.

Here the twist(-2) is the plane hyperplane twist: on C=P1 it is O_P1(-4). For a negative quadratic block q_- in Sym^2 W, the even quadric has equation q_++q_-=0. Its intrinsic second-normal projective class satisfies the ACTUAL affine dependence law

    xi(q_-)=zeta_C-eta_C tensor q_-.

The two fixed classes have intrinsic geometric meanings:

* eta_C in V_C is the projective extension class of F_R on the doubled plane conic. It is exactly the unique conormal lift of the restricted trace-free Atiyah class of F_R.
* zeta_C in V_C tensor Sym^2 W is the restriction of the ambient second-normal class zeta of F along the positive plane, using the canonical projective projection onto that plane.

The ambient class lies in

    zeta in (Sym^2 W tensor H1(R,B_R(-2)))^A5,

and this invariant space is one-dimensional. This constrains zeta to one scalar parameter but does not prove that parameter nonzero.

The top-jet commutator tensor c_xi is therefore polynomial of degree at most two in the negative-block coefficients. If the coefficient span of eta_C in sl_2 has dimension at least two, a nonempty open of nondegenerate negative blocks has injective actual 15-to-63 top-jet factor, hence no global even adjoint section with leading normal order four. This last implication is conditional: no actual noncommutativity of eta_C is proved here.

## 1. A canonical weighted neighborhood of C

Let J be the ideal of the positive plane in P4, generated by the actual two negative linear forms. In a neighborhood of C consider the homogeneous ideal

    K=J^3 + q_+ J + (q_+^2).

The degrees of the three types of generators are3,3,4. The corresponding subscheme Z is independent of local coordinate choices; it retains normal weight at most two if q_+ has weight two and the generators of J have weight one. Its underlying reduced scheme is C. Inversion acts on it and gives the invariant structure algebra R_Z=(O_Z)^+.

Its exact square-zero extension is

    0 -> L tensor (1 directsum Sym^2 W) -> R_Z -> O_C -> 0,
    L=O_C(-2).

The first kernel summand is the plane conormal direction generated by q_+. The second is the quadratic negative-normal direction. The direct sum is intrinsic: projection P4 minus P(V_-) -> R preserves q_+ and separates these directions. Its geometric extension class is (kappa_(C in R),0); the plane-conic summand is nonsplit, while the ambient negative-normal direction has the canonical projection splitting.

The invariant algebra (End F|Z)^+ is Azumaya over R_Z. The local proof is the same averaging and Nakayama argument as in the second-normal theorem: lift a scalar-weight basis on C, average it into that weight, and use Nakayama to obtain a basis on Z. In that basis the invariant endomorphism algebra is a matrix algebra over R_Z.

Since B_C is trivial on the balanced conic, the canonical evaluation algebra H0(End F_C) tensor_C R_Z gives the reference algebra. The torsor of comparison isomorphisms reducing to evaluation has additive kernel

    B_C tensor L tensor (1 directsum Sym^2 W).

Its class decomposes canonically as

    (eta_C,zeta_C) in V_C directsum (V_C tensor Sym^2 W).

This definition does not assume a splitting of R_Z over O_C.

## 2. Pushout to the actual quadric gives the affine law

On Q(q_-), the positive conic equation satisfies the exact identity q_+=-q_-. Modulo the third power of its conic ideal, this defines a map from the weighted neighborhood to the actual third conic neighborhood. On the square-zero kernel it is precisely

    L directsum (L tensor Sym^2 W) -> L tensor Sym^2 W,
    (a,s) -> s-a tensor q_-.

The resulting invariant algebra extension is the pushout by this map. Pulling back an Azumaya comparison torsor pushes forward its additive class by the same map. Equivalently, this follows directly from the Čech inner automorphisms 1+ad(eta_ij,zeta_ij). Therefore

    xi(q_-)=zeta_C-eta_C tensor q_-.

The sign is fixed by q_+=-q_-. A simultaneous opposite Čech convention changes the named classes but not the identity or any vanishing criterion.

The construction is algebraic in the negative block. It works on the entire affine space Sym^2 W of blocks, including the singular cone q_-=0, because the relevant neighborhoods are along C, away from the negative vertex. Smooth even quadrics form the nonempty open of nondegenerate blocks. The square-zero kernel remains the same vector bundle on C throughout. The full neighborhood has successive graded pieces O_C, N*, and Sym^2 N*, each pulled back from C, so the family is flat and the fixed class spaces commute with base change. Thus the class is taken in the fixed vector space V_C tensor Sym^2 W. No identification between cohomology of unrelated moving curves is needed.

In particular the actual dependence is constant if eta_C=0 and has an injective three-dimensional linear part if eta_C is nonzero. This is a dichotomy, not a determination of the actual case.

## 3. eta_C is the actual conormal projective Atiyah class

Setting J=0 in the weighted neighborhood gives the doubled conic in R. Thus eta_C is exactly the projective extension class of End(F_R) on that doubled conic, relative to the constant evaluation algebra on C.

Let at_0(F_R) be the trace-free part of the Atiyah class, obtained by subtracting half its trace. It belongs to H1(R,B_R tensor Ω_R). Restrict it to C. Naturality of the Atiyah class sends it to at_0(F_C) under Ω_R|C -> Ω_C. Balance gives F_C=O_P1(-1) tensor a constant two-space, so its projective Atiyah class vanishes. Hence the restricted class lies in the kernel of

    H1(B_C tensor Ω_R|C) -> H1(B_C tensor Ω_C).

The conormal sequence is

    0 -> O_C(-2) -> Ω_R|C -> Ω_C -> 0.

Because B_C is trivial and Ω_C=O_P1(-2), H0(B_C tensor Ω_C)=0. Its cohomology sequence therefore identifies H1(B_C(-2)) injectively with that kernel. There is a UNIQUE class lifting the restricted projective Atiyah class.

This class is eta_C, up the already fixed common sign convention. To check the identification, use local frames of F_R whose reductions on C are a scalar line transition times the fixed projective evaluation frame. On the doubled conic the projective transition is 1+t eta_ij, where t is its local conormal equation. Differentiating and restricting to C gives eta_ij dt; the scalar line transition disappears on taking the trace-free part. This is exactly the conormal image of the torsor cocycle defining eta_C.

Thus the affine slope is tied to the actual HM plane restriction and therefore to its actual Steiner bundle. It is not an independent nine-dimensional parameter. Nevertheless a nonzero projective Chern class on the whole plane does not by itself prove nonzero or noncommuting conormal restriction on this particular C; no such inference is used.

## 4. The ambient class and its one-scalar normalizer constraint

The open P4 minus P(V_-) is the total space of V_- tensor O_R(1), with its canonical projection p to R and zero section R. Its third zero-section neighborhood has invariant algebra

    O_R directsum (Sym^2 W tensor O_R(-2)),

with square-zero second summand. Compare the actual invariant End(F) there with the pullback of End(F_R) along p. The additive comparison torsor defines

    zeta in Sym^2 W tensor H1(R,B_R(-2)).

Restricting this torsor to C gives the zeta_C of the weighted construction. The positive-plane normalizer preserves the projection and actual bundle, so zeta is invariant under its genuine A5 action.

The actual Steiner presentation is

    0 -> A tensor O_R(-3) -> D tensor O_R(-2) -> F_R -> 0,
    dim A=3, dim D=5,

where A is an irreducible three-dimensional A5 representation. The five-dimensional coefficient representation D is irreducible. One can see this without a coefficient evaluation: if D were reducible, its possible decompositions have a trivial summand or only lower-dimensional constituents. Since A tensor V_+* is either 1+3+5 or4+5, every such decomposition of D has a constituent, or excess multiplicity, not hit by an equivariant linear presentation.     0 -> End(F_R)(-2) -> D* tensor F_R
      -> A* tensor F_R(1) -> 0.

The twist-one Steiner sequence shows that F_R(1) is acyclic, while H1(F_R)=A and its other cohomology vanishes. Since O_R(-2) is acyclic, removing the trace gives the canonical representation isomorphism

    H1(R,B_R(-2)) = D* tensor A,

of dimension15. For either irreducible three-dimensional A5 module,

    5 tensor 3 = 3 directsum 3' directsum 4 directsum 5.

The negative two-space W is a faithful binary-icosahedral module. Its symmetric square is a genuine irreducible three-dimensional A5 module: it cannot be three trivial representations, since that would make its projective A5 action trivial. Either choice of the three-dimensional constituent occurs once in D* tensor A. Self-duality of these A5 representations therefore gives

    dim (Sym^2 W tensor H1(R,B_R(-2)))^A5 = 1.

This proves the one-scalar constraint on the actual ambient zeta. It does not prove that scalar nonzero or that its restriction to C is nonzero.

## 5. An actual-family commutator criterion

Let T=H1(O_P1(-4)). Use a basis of T only to display components:

    eta_C = sum_i eta_i tensor t_i,
    zeta_C = sum_i zeta_i(w) tensor t_i,

where eta_i are constant sl_2 elements and zeta_i(w) are sl_2-valued binary quadratics. The dependence law reads

    xi_i(q;w)=zeta_i(w)-q(w) eta_i.

Hence every component of the intrinsic top commutator tensor is

    [xi_i,xi_j]
      = [zeta_i,zeta_j]
        -q([eta_i,zeta_j]+[zeta_i,eta_j])
        +q^2[eta_i,eta_j].

This is polynomial of degree at most two in the actual negative-block coefficients. It is a tensor identity, independent of the chosen display basis of T.

If the coefficient span of eta_C has dimension at least two, some [eta_i,eta_j] is nonzero. Along any nonzero fixed negative quadratic direction q_0, the coefficient of t^2 in c_xi(t q_0) is q_0^2[eta_i,eta_j], which is nonzero. Thus c_xi does not vanish identically on the three-dimensional space of negative blocks. Its nonvanishing open meets the open of nondegenerate blocks. By the proved top-factor criterion, a general smooth even Q with this fixed positive conic then has injective ell_4, and hence injective d_4. This rules out leading normal order four for its global even adjoint sections, but says nothing by itself about lower-order positive excess or odd sections.

There is an exact version of the remaining obstacle. The commutator tensor vanishes identically for ALL negative blocks if and only if all three fixed tensors vanish:

    [eta_i,eta_j],
    [eta_i,zeta_j]+[zeta_i,eta_j],
    [zeta_i,zeta_j],

for every i,j. Necessity follows by replacing q with t q_0 and comparing powers of t; multiplication by a nonzero binary quadratic is injective in the polynomial ring. Sufficiency is the displayed expansion. If any one of these fixed actual tensors is nonzero, generic nondegenerate negative blocks have injective ell_4.

No nonvanishing of these tensors has been established here. The conic cohomology-pencil isomorphism controls the reduced restriction and its splitting type; it is not by itself an evaluation of this conormal Atiyah class or of the ambient second-normal scalar. Proving one of the indicated tensor nonvanishings is the precise remaining local obstruction test.
