# The actual third value as a midpoint dualizing operator defect

## Exact new comparison

The actual fixed-probe secondary tensor can be computed at the midpoint first-normal bundle, because its variation along the marked normal torsor vanishes. Let F_tilde denote that midpoint bundle on 2Q, and let N_tilde be its unique invariant square-zero lift of N in B_Q(2).

Retain the fixed nonzero scalar k from the proved actual identity zeta_a=k At_F(D_a). No change of the actual probe normalization is made below.

The corrected cone operator constructed from the actual Serre residue has symbol k a h D_a and twist4. Multiply it by a. Its symbol is k a^2 h D_a and its twist is6. The obstruction to lifting THIS specified multiplied operator across 2Q is exactly the unquotiented secondary value Psi(a,h,t(a)), up to a fixed sign convention.

The surrounding commutator complex gives a canonical way to compute that obstruction from an actual global section

    r(a,h) in H0(omega_(2Z)(5))_+.

Here 2Z is the ordinary square thickening of the regular spinor zero scheme on the weighted stack, not the ambient thickening 2Q. There is a canonical connecting map

    delta:H0(omega_(2Z)(5))_+ -> H1(B_Q(4))_+=P4

such that

    Psi(a,h,t(a)) = delta(r(a,h)),                       (1)

up to that fixed sign. In particular

    Psi(a,h,t(a))=0  iff  r(a,h) lies in im H0(G(4))_+.   (2)

This identifies the absolute operator obstruction, rather than only a variation. It does NOT determine whether the actual section satisfies (2). Substituting an arbitrary Hessian correction for N_tilde would require a comparison with the actual midpoint lift and is not licensed by this theorem.

## 1. The complete commutator sequence and its cohomology

Write M=omega_(2Z)(1) for the global tangent-modulo-conjugation module. The intrinsic description proved in GLOBAL-NORMAL-ADJOINT-DEFECT-DUALIZING-TWIST.md supplies

    B_Q -> G -> M -> 0.

Fix the tangent identification tau(v)=sigma v^t J+v sigma^t J, with N=sigma sigma^t J. Then [K,N]=tau(K sigma) and tau(sigma)=2N. With this convention the first map is exactly X->X sigma. Its kernel is O_Q(-2), generated by N. Locally, a trace-free endomorphism commuting with N is a rational multiple of N; regularity of its three entries forces the multiplier to be regular across the codimension-two regular ideal. Equivalently Hom(I_Z^2,O)=O on the smooth stack. This proves the kernel statement also at Z.

After twisting by4, set J=im(B_Q(4)->G(4)). There are exact sequences

    0 -> O_Q(2) -> B_Q(4) -> J -> 0,
    0 -> J -> G(4) -> M(4)=omega_(2Z)(5) -> 0.          (3)

These are understood on the weighted stack and pushed to Q when a coarse bundle is displayed. Tame coarse pushforward is exact and preserves cohomology, so no extension of the spinor line to 2Q is being assumed.

Since H1(O_Q(2))=H2(O_Q(2))=0, the first sequence canonically identifies H1(B_Q(4)) with H1(J). Composing its inverse with the boundary from the second sequence defines delta. Exactness proves

    ker delta = im H0(G(4)).                            (4)

No vanishing of H1(G(4)) is needed, and surjectivity of delta is not asserted merely from (3).

## 2. Constructing the actual section r

Let O be any positive corrected cone operator of twist4 and symbol k a h D_a from POSITIVE-SERRE-RESIDUE-AND-CANONICAL-CONE-OPERATOR-CORRECTION.md. Transfer from G to F_Q using the natural D_a action on the spinor twist. This is only a cone operation. Set O0=a O, of twist6.

The operator preserves the section line and hence the nilpotent line. With the natural action of its symbol on the physical twist O_Q(2), there is a global scalar lambda of twist6 satisfying

    O0(N)=lambda N.

Choose a lift lambda_tilde to 2Q. Such a lift exists by H1(O_Q(4))=0. The symbol k a^2 h D_a is an actual ambient twisted derivation and annihilates q0, so it acts on the fixed first ambient thickening. Choose local fixed-determinant operator lifts O_i on F_tilde with this symbol and reduction O0. Here fixed determinant means that the determinant operator is the natural symbol action on det(F_tilde), with its induced action on O(2) included when differentiating N_tilde. Local existence follows from local freeness of F_tilde.

Their errors are divisible by q0:

    O_i(N_tilde)-lambda_tilde N_tilde=q0 R_i,
       R_i in B_Q(6).

Differentiate N_tilde^2=0. The scalar term also has zero anticommutator with N_tilde. After dividing by q0 and restricting to Q, this gives tr(N R_i)=0. Consequently R_i belongs to the tangent kernel, identified with G(4).

On an overlap, O_j-O_i=q0 K_ij for a fixed-determinant adjoint K_ij in B_Q(4). Thus

    R_j-R_i=[K_ij,N].                                  (5)

The classes of R_i in M(4) agree. They define the claimed actual global section r. Physical parity is positive throughout.

Equation (5) says that the boundary of r in H1(J) is the image of the Cech cocycle K_ij. The latter is exactly the obstruction to gluing local lifts of O0. The first sequence in (3) therefore proves that delta(r) equals that operator-lifting obstruction. This includes the centralizer obstruction: its H1 and H2 vanish, rather than being silently discarded.

## 3. Identification with the fixed-probe third value

The canonical lift of t(a)=a zeta_a=k a At_F(D_a)|_Q to the midpoint normal bundle is k a At_(F_tilde)(D_a), since D_a is tangent to 2Q. Multiplying by h and dividing by q0 gives the obstruction to lifting the chosen cone operator O with symbol k a h D_a. More explicitly, if zeta_tilde=k At_(F_tilde)(D_a) and b is the divided normal obstruction class of O, then

    q0 b=a h zeta_tilde,    q0(a b)=a^2 h zeta_tilde.

Multiplying the SPECIFIED Cech obstruction b by a gives the obstruction to lifting a O, namely O0. This does not infer uniqueness from the second displayed equality alone: the corresponding normal multiplication map can have a kernel, and the operator O fixes the required divided class. The resulting class is a b, exactly the intrinsic definition of Psi in POSITIVE-SECONDARY-TENSOR-NORMAL-VARIATION.md. A uniform choice of the Cech boundary sign makes (1) literal; its vanishing is independent of that choice.

The fixed actual Atiyah probe is used here, not a separately normalized probe from another ambient extension. The proved zero normal variation identifies its midpoint value with the actual ambient value. The eventual third Bockstein is still obtained by quotienting (1) by the actual second-symbol image.

## 4. Choice checks and what remains to compute

Changing local O_i by q0 K_i changes R_i by a commutator and does not change r in M(4). Changing lambda_tilde by q0 rho, with rho a global section of O_Q(4), changes R_i by -rho N. This is the image of the global section -(rho/2) sigma of G(4), so it leaves delta(r) unchanged.

Changing the corrected cone O by a positive section of B_Q(4) changes it by N times a quadratic, by the proved cone section theorem. Every such section lifts at the midpoint because N_tilde exists and quadratics lift. Hence the changed O0 has the same lifting obstruction, agreeing with the choice independence of Psi. No flatness of ker N_tilde is used.

The actual section r retains both the ambient deformation of the coordinate algebra and the actual normal bundle/lift. A chosen local even-polynomial Hessian formula can be used to calculate it only after matching its normal frame and N_tilde with this actual data. The quotient in (2) is not known to vanish on r. This is the exact remaining absolute computation.

## Additional intrinsic filtration of the defect

There is a canonical exact sequence

    0 -> O_Z -> M=omega_(2Z)(1) -> G|_Z -> 0.           (6)

The first arrow is the class of N, which under the fixed tau convention is the class of sigma/2 in the presentation M=coker(B->G). Adding scalar endomorphisms makes the evaluation image End(G) sigma=I_Z G, so the quotient of M by this arrow is G|_Z. Locally the class of N corresponds to f g in I/(f^2,g^2), whose annihilator is I (the factor1/2 in the G-presentation has no effect); hence the first term really is O_Z. This filtration can help separate rescaling a nilpotent from moving its line, but neither quotient alone replaces the full operator comparison above.
