# The inversion quotient and an exact lower-twist parity gate

## Scope and input

This note concerns smooth inversion-even quadrics only. It uses the actual involution, scalar action on U=H0(F(3)), disjointness of the twenty-five HM lines, and the equivariant Euler calculation in INVERSION-EVEN-QUADRICS-ARE-ADJOINT-BAD.md. Write B=End_0(F). No actual matrix is evaluated, and no exact generic corank is claimed.

## 1. Explicit quotient geometry

Split ambient coordinates into the three positive coordinates x and two negative coordinates. Smoothness of an even quadric means both diagonal quadratic blocks are nondegenerate. Over the complex numbers change bases within these blocks and write

    Q: q_+(x)+uv=0,
    iota(x,u,v)=(x,-u,-v).

The invariant graded ring is generated by x of weight one and a=u^2, c=v^2 of weight two. Since uv=-q_+(x), the quotient is

    Y: ac=q_+(x)^2 in P(1,1,1,2,2).

This follows by taking invariants of the homogeneous coordinate ring, and so describes the projective quotient, not just a birational model. The fixed locus upstairs consists of the smooth conic C_+=Q intersect P(V_+) and the two points p_1,p_2=Q intersect P(V_-).

At the conic the involution has one positive tangent direction and two negative normal directions, giving a transverse A1 quotient. At each isolated fixed point all three tangent directions are negative, giving the quotient singularity 1/2(1,1,1). There are no other stabilizers.

Blow up the fixed conic and the two isolated points in Q, with exceptional divisors E_C,E_1,E_2. The lifted involution fixes each exceptional divisor pointwise and acts by minus one in its normal direction. The quotient of this blowup is smooth: locally it is the quotient of the total space of the tautological line bundle by fiberwise negation, hence the total space of its square. Denote this smooth quotient by Ytilde and its double-cover map by rho.

The blowup canonical formula and the ramification formula give

    K_Qtilde=-3H+E_C+2E_1+2E_2,
    rho* K_Ytilde=K_Qtilde-(E_C+E_1+E_2)
                  =-3H+E_1+E_2.

This explicit resolution does not supply a Kodaira-type vanishing for the adjoint eigensheaves: they are not the canonical bundle tensored with an established ample line bundle.

## 2. Odd adjoint sections must vanish on the entire fixed conic

The conic C_+ is disjoint from every HM line. It is disjoint from the fixed line C00=P(V_-). If a point of C_+ lay on any other line, its invariance would place it on the exchanged partner as well, contrary to disjointness of those lines.

Consequently the actual evaluation U tensor O->F(3) has rank two everywhere along C_+. Since iota acts on U by one scalar, it acts by the same scalar on each fiber of F(3) there. It follows that its action on B is trivial there. Ambient O(4) has positive character on C_+, so B_Q(4)|C_+ has only positive fiber character. Therefore every odd section of B_Q(4) vanishes scheme-theoretically on C_+.

The ideal of C_+ in Q is generated by the two odd linear forms u,v, a regular sequence. If W_- denotes their two-dimensional sign representation, the equivariant Koszul resolution is

    0 -> B_Q(2) tensor det(W_-)
      -> B_Q(3) tensor W_-
      -> B_Q(4) I_C+ -> 0.

Here det(W_-) has positive character. Taking the odd part of cohomology, and using that the restriction to C_+ has no odd global sections, gives the exact sequence

    H0(B_Q(3))_+ tensor W_-
      -> H0(B_Q(4))_-
      -> H1(B_Q(2))_-
      -> H1(B_Q(3))_+ tensor W_-.

After suppressing the fixed sign label, the outer multiplicities are two. In particular, if H0(B_Q(3))_+=0, then the excess odd sections are exactly the kernel of the actual Koszul multiplication map

    H1(B_Q(2))_- -> H1(B_Q(3))_+^2.

Without that hypothesis there is still the exact sequence above; no generic lower-twist vanishing is imported into the even subsystem.

## 3. Exactness of the four-dimensional lower bound

The established equivariant Euler calculation states

    h0(B_Q(4))_+ - h1(B_Q(4))_+ = 4,
    h0(B_Q(4))_- - h1(B_Q(4))_- = -4,

with no higher cohomology. Hence the actual total corank is exactly four if and only if both H1(B_Q(4))_+ and H0(B_Q(4))_- vanish. Section2 identifies a concrete actual lower-twist obstruction to the second vanishing. The first remains a separate cohomology condition.

Equivalently, for the finite quotient p:Q->Y, decompose p_*B_Q(4)=G_+ directsum G_-. Exactness of invariants in characteristic zero gives H^i(Y,G_+)=H^i(Q,B_Q(4))_+ and similarly for the negative part. Thus the missing conditions are H1(Y,G_+)=0 and H0(Y,G_-)=0. The explicit quotient alone proves neither; a vanishing theorem for line bundles on its resolution cannot be substituted for one for these actual rank-three eigensheaves.

## Outcome

The new deductions are an explicit smooth quotient resolution with its canonical class, forced conic vanishing of every odd adjoint section, and an exact Koszul cohomology gate for those sections. The outstanding issue is to prove the actual parity multiplication injectivity (with its possible H0 contribution) and positive-parity H1 vanishing uniformly on a dense open of the even family. No evaluated coefficients, arbitrary generic-map hypothesis, or full-solution claim is used.
