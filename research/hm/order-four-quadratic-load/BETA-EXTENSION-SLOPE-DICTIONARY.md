# The complete on-curve beta extension space

## Fixed data and statement

Fix an integral projective Cohen–Macaulay curve D of arithmetic genus three, a reduced Cartier divisor Z of 25 smooth points, and a line bundle L of degree five with L=O_D(5)(-Z). Assume L is generated by its three-dimensional complete space B=H0(L), and its associated map spans the beta plane as in BETA-SPLIT-IMAGE-BOUNDARY.md at d755d22d3bf460fe314dd846bda1e893d682b71c in PR543. These are fixed on-curve data; no ambient HM realization is asserted.

Every element of Ext1(O_D(Z),L) is realized by an on-curve rank-two vector bundle with a four-dimensional evaluation subsystem whose image is L plus O_D, rank drops precisely at Z, and beta kernel map agrees with that determined by B. The extension vector space has dimension 22 and is explicitly the quotient of 25 local slope parameters by three global splitting changes. Thus these on-curve conditions alone do not further restrict this extension vector space. This is not a claim that every class is the restriction of the HM bundle.

## 1. Exact quotient of slope parameters

The exact sequence

0 -> L(-Z) -> L -> L|Z -> 0

gives

0 -> H0(L) -> H0(L|Z) -> H1(L(-Z)) -> 0.

Indeed deg L(-Z)=-20 gives H0=0, while H1(L)=0 by duality: the rank-one torsion-free dualizing sheaf has degree four, so omega_D tensor L^-1 has negative degree. Hence

Ext1(O_D(Z),L) = H1(L(-Z)) = H0(L|Z)/H0(L).

The dimensions are 25-3=22. Endpoint identifications are fixed: this is the vector space classifying extensions with their specified inclusion and quotient, not the moduli space after all automorphisms.

Put J=L plus O_D. At p in Z an inverse elementary transform E containing J is described by adjoining t^-1 v to J, where t is a local parameter and the residue of v spans a line in J_p. Transversality to L_p permits unique normalization of its O-coordinate to one, so that line is spanned by (a_p,1), with a_p in L_p. These lines, independent of a chosen parameter, are precisely the points of the affine space H0(L|Z). Changing the splitting of J by (l,c) -> (l+b c,c), b in H0(L), translates all slopes by b|Z. The cohomological connecting map identifies the resulting quotient with the extension space above (an overall sign depends only on the gluing convention).

## 2. Every extension gives the same evaluation geometry

Take any extension

0 -> L -> E_e -> O_D(Z) -> 0.

Both endpoints are line bundles. Locally the extension splits, so E_e is locally free even if D has singularities away from Z. Pull it back along the canonical inclusion O_D -> O_D(Z). The resulting extension of O_D by L splits because H1(L)=0. Choose a splitting and write its preimage as J=L plus O_D inside E_e. Its quotient is O_D(Z)/O_D, a sheaf of length 25. Locally at each p in Z the inclusion J -> E_e has elementary divisors (1,t), with L a saturated subbundle. Thus all marked points are active.

The map (B plus C) tensor O_D -> J is evaluation on L and the identity on O_D. It is surjective. Its composition into E_e has rank two off Z and rank exactly one at every point of Z. Its kernel off Z, and its intrinsic extension as the kernel of evaluation to J, is the rank-two evaluation kernel of B -> L; in particular it lies in the fixed B. This kernel map is independent of e and is the same beta map for every extension class. The four sections are linearly independent: H0(J) -> H0(E_e) is injective.

Since deg O_D(Z)=25, both endpoint bundles have H1=0. Therefore every E_e has H1(E_e)=0 and h0(E_e)=3+23=26. The principal-part boundary has rank three for every member of this constructed family, not merely for a generic extension class. This does not say that every principal part lifts: its kernel has dimension 22.

## Consequence and boundary

The full 22-dimensional vector space survives all the fixed on-curve conditions used here. Any further exclusion or identification of the actual HM restriction must use additional ambient information, variation in H, equivariance across the family, or constraints not imposed in this construction. In particular, a successful on-curve extension construction is not evidence of an ambient HM realization or a solution of Q4.

This is a symbolic classification deduction. No selected HM coefficients, numerical ranks, or consumed experiments are used.
