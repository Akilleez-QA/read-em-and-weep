# At most one quadric through a general HM line section

## Result

For the 25 disjoint HM lines C in P4 and a general hyperplane H, put Z=C∩H. Then

    a=h0(I_(Z,H)(2))<=1.

If a=1, its unique quadric is irreducible over C, and

    b=h0(I_(Z,H)(3))>=4.

Together with the binary relation-bundle bound in RAO-BINARY-PENCIL-BOUNDS.md, the remaining generic alternatives are

    a=0 and 0<=b<=7, or a=1 and 4<=b<=7.

No assertion a=0, b=0, product-quadratic injectivity, or generic Q4 vanishing is made.

## Inputs and exact pins

1. C consists of 25 disjoint lines and its ideal sheaf is generated by the six quintic maximal minors of the actual four-section evaluation of F(3). The six-dimensional saturation and evaluation construction are in PR538 at `3d0b833b1a22680a4ac1ea793fdeec378730de21`, explicitly cited in PR543 `PROOF.md` at `f4e61e37e8be9ad32936baa7c69a3433efdc8c2a`.
2. The standard projective normalizer acts 2-transitively on the 25 line labels. PR541 at `9ce30d8d783da881be68548d158ebcb48e122850`, `research/hm/generic-cubic-primitive-closure/PROOF.md`, Section 3: the translation group F5² acts regularly and SL2(F5) acts transitively on nonzero differences.
3. Every proper hyperplane section spans H. Same PR541 commit, `research/hm/generic-cubic-primitive-closure/EXACT-LINEAR-DEFECT.md`, Section 1, or its direct proper-section argument in `EXACT-LINEAR-FAILURE.md`.

Only these geometric/equivariant statements, not any older residual map Q, are used below. No uniform-position theorem for integral curves is imported.

## 1. A general section has no three collinear points

Fix three distinct component lines L1,L2,L3. Choose p1∈L1. The plane spanned by p1 and L3 cannot contain L2, since two lines in a projective plane meet whereas L2,L3 are disjoint. Choose p2∈L2 outside that plane. The line M=p1p2 misses L3: otherwise p2 would lie in that plane. A general hyperplane containing M contains none of the finitely many component lines. Its section points on L1,L2 are p1,p2, while its point on L3 is not on M. Thus this triple is not universally collinear.

On the open parameter space of proper hyperplanes, collinearity of this fixed triple is closed. Its complement is therefore a nonempty open. Intersect these opens over the finitely many triples. A general section has no three collinear points.

## 2. Two independent section quadrics force at least 23 coplanar points

If two quadrics f,g through Z were coprime, their complete intersection in H=P3 would be a degree-four curve. Choose a linear combination s of the six restricted quintic generators avoiding each of its finitely many irreducible curve components; this is possible because their common base scheme is the finite scheme Z. Then f,g,s is a regular sequence and its complete intersection has length 2·2·5=20. It cannot contain the 25 distinct points Z. Thus no pair of section quadrics is coprime.

Suppose the entire section-quadric system has dimension at least two. It must have a nonconstant gcd: otherwise fix a nonzero f and choose a member g outside the finitely many subspaces of members divisible by each irreducible factor of f, producing a coprime pair. The gcd has degree exactly one, since degree two would force the system to be one-dimensional. Write the system as h times a space of linear forms of dimension a>=2.

All points of Z outside the plane h=0 lie in the common zero set of those linear forms, which has dimension at most one. Since no three section points are collinear, at most two points lie outside h=0. Therefore at least 23 section points lie in a plane.

## 3. Two-transitivity excludes that generic coplanarity

For each fixed 23-element subset S of the line labels, coplanarity of its section points is closed on the proper-hyperplane parameter space. If a>=2 generically, Section 2 says that the union of these finitely many closed conditions contains a dense open subset. Irreducibility of the parameter space forces one fixed subset S to be coplanar generically (indeed throughout the proper open).

Applying a normalizer element transports this generic property to its translate of S: its projective action is an automorphism of the hyperplane parameter space. Two-transitivity on the complementary pair implies transitivity on all 23-element subsets. Hence every 23-element subset is coplanar for a general hyperplane, simultaneously since there are only finitely many subsets.

But a section spanning P3 has four points forming a spanning basis. Those four labels are contained in some 23-element subset, which therefore cannot be planar. This contradiction proves a<=1.

## 4. The unique generic quadric, when present, is irreducible

Suppose a=1. On a dense open hyperplane parameter space the unique equation varies algebraically as a line subbundle of the space of quadrics. If it were generically reducible, it would be a union of two planes; a double plane is immediately excluded by the spanning property.

For two distinct component planes, record the set of point labels in their intersection and the unordered pair of exclusive label sets. On a dense open these data are constant: there are finitely many possible label data, their factor-incidence conditions are algebraic, and the unique quadric makes the unordered data intrinsic. Equivalently one may pass to the finite double cover ordering the factors; monodromy can only exchange the two sets. Shrinking the base removes every special incidence where a label moves onto the intersection.

Naturality and uniqueness make the generic intersection-label set and unordered partition invariant under the normalizer. The intersection contains at most two labels by Section 1. Transitivity on 25 labels forces that invariant set to be empty. Neither exclusive block can be empty, since otherwise all section points would lie in one plane. The two nonempty blocks would give an invariant two-block system on a transitive set of odd size 25. A transitive group must make the blocks equal in size, an impossibility. Thus the unique quadric is irreducible.

Multiplication of its nonzero equation by the four independent linear forms on H injects a four-dimensional space into the cubic ideal, so b>=4. Irreducibility is not smoothness: an integral quadric cone is not excluded.

## Boundary

The sole remaining quadric possibility is an irreducible unique generic quadric. This does not establish a lifting theorem for the disconnected union of lines, or eliminate the possibility. When a=0, the cubic trace intersection from the earlier plane-trace criterion still requires control. All work is symbolic; no selected/source matrices, coefficients, or hyperplanes were evaluated, and no public write was made.
