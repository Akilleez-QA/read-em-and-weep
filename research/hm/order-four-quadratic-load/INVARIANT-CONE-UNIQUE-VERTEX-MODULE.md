# The actual residual defect has a unique three-step vertex module

## Result

Let D=p_*T'=(A plus 2W-prime) O_P(W)(2) be the exact residual pushforward. Its positive-coordinate module action has a unique equivariant isomorphism class compatible with the actual spinor quotient. The residual support is therefore on the negative vertex L, and its ideal satisfies

    I_L^3 T'=0, but I_L^2 T' is nonzero.

At the generic point of L its successive Loewy dimensions are 2,3,2. It is not a reduced sheaf on L and cannot be replaced by one. No actual multiplication coefficient or matrix rank is evaluated.

## 1. The two universal channels

Put M=C² with trivial G action and R=A direct-sum (W-prime tensor M). Positive coordinate functions act on D through

    V tensor R -> R tensor W.

Here V O(-1) is the relative coordinate-generator bundle for the affine negative projection. Character multiplication shows that both diagonal blocks vanish and each off-diagonal representation channel has dimension one. Choose nonzero universal maps U:V tensor A -> W-prime tensor W and Vmap:V tensor W-prime -> A tensor W. Every action is therefore determined by

    a in M, b in M*,
    A --a tensor U--> W-prime tensor M,
    W-prime tensor M --b tensor Vmap--> A.

These are abstract multiplicity coordinates, not evaluated coefficients of the actual HM bundle.

## 2. Exact universal relation calculation

An exact model uses the standard quaternionic binary-icosahedral W, its sqrt(5)-Galois conjugate W-prime, A=Sym²W and V=Sym²W-prime. Write r=sqrt(5), and x,y for the negative base sections. In the standard binary-square bases one universal map has the three matrices

    U0 = [[-r*y, x, -y], [-r*x, r*y, 3*x]],
    U1 = [[-x, y, -r*x], [r*y, -x, y]],
    U2 = [[-3*y, -r*x, r*y], [x, -y, r*x]].

Let

    Q = [[0,0,1],[0,-1/2,0],[1,0,0]],
    J = [[0,1],[-1,0]],
    Vi = Q^(-1) Ui^transpose J.

The verifier checks equivariance on generators of the binary icosahedral group, not merely on one torus. It also checks exactly

    Ui Vj = Uj Vi,
    (U0 V2+U2 V0)/2 - U1 V1 = 0.

Thus the W-prime multiplicity block imposes neither a commutator nor a cone relation. On the A block the corresponding expressions are nonzero. For example

    (V1 U0-V0 U1)[0,0] = 2x²+2r y²,

and the cone contraction is

    (V0 U2+V2 U0)/2 - V1 U1
       = [[8xy,4y²,0],[-8x²,0,8y²],[0,-4x²,-8xy]].

In the full action every A-block expression is multiplied by the single scalar b(a). Therefore commutativity and the positive cone equation are equivalent to exactly

    b(a)=0.

Sufficiency follows because all remaining blocks vanish identically, as displayed. Necessity follows from the nonzero universal commutator, or independently from the nonzero cone contraction. This is a polynomial identity computation in universal representation spaces, not an actual bundle rank test.

## 3. Actual quotient generators exclude either zero channel

If b(a)=0, every triple product of positive-coordinate actions vanishes: alternating block paths contain b(a), or terminate in the subspace W-prime tensor Ca annihilated by b. Hence I_L^3 T'=0 and the support is on L, without needing a separate invariant-curve classification.

At the generic point of L the transverse ring is the A1 ring k[[a0²,a0b0,b0²]], and the spinor is its odd module k[[a0,b0]]_odd, minimally generated by two elements. Since T' is an actual quotient of this spinor (up to an invertible twist), its minimal generator number is at most two.

If a=0, all positive-coordinate images lie in A, of dimension3, leaving at least four generators in the seven-dimensional module. If b=0, all images lie in W-prime tensor Ca, of dimension at most2, leaving at least five generators. Both contradict the actual two-generator bound. Thus a and b are nonzero.

For a two-dimensional multiplicity space, GL(M) acts transitively on pairs a,b with both nonzero and b(a)=0. Choose a=e1 and b=e2*. Ordering the three pieces as A,B1=W-prime e1,B2=W-prime e2, the action is exactly

    B2 -> A -> B1 -> 0.

Its total image is contained in A plus B1, of dimension5. The two-generator bound forces that image to have dimension at least5, hence exactly5; the collective maps onto A and B1 are surjective. Applying the ideal again gives precisely B1, dimension2, and the third power is zero. This proves the generic Loewy dimensions 2,3,2 and the nonzero second power.

## Scope and evidence

The resulting action is an actual globally unique equivariant module structure on D, up to isomorphism, even though neither multiplicity coordinate was selected. It does not yet compute the Ext-annihilator of the spinor extension or settle the positive rank33 condition. In particular Ann(T') must not silently replace the extension-annihilator.
