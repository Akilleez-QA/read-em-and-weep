# Exclusion of the eleven single-Wprime cases in the last rank-two quotient

## Result and scope

All eleven a=1 candidates in EXTREMAL-31-LAST-REMAINING-QUOTIENT-REDUCTION.md are impossible. With EXTREMAL-31-DOUBLED-GAUSS-EXCLUSION.md (PR543, 229e621948e9f6d80902080ad227993da3f0dced), this excludes the entire (8,2) quotient and therefore all five rank-two dual-image quotients for the hypothetical (k,l)=(3,1) plateau. The rank-one dual-image branch is still open. No stronger SF or positive-square rank bound is asserted.

Write Wp=Wprime, P=P4, T=T6, and C(-j)=C tensor O(-j). Put L=O(-8)+O(-10) or W(-9). The actual map is

    delta: K -> O(4)+Wp(-3),

of generic rank three, with K of rank14 and degree -58. Its actual kernel Q has rank11, H0(Q(2))=0 and H0(Q(3)) containing no Wp. The actual next derivative beta_Q has generic rank three: this is the last known rank-three plateau step, with kernel of rank8. No later derivative is assumed to have rank three. These are the persistence inputs of EXTREMAL-31-FIVE-QUOTIENT-SECTION-ENVELOPES.md. Actual quotients, not saturated images in a displayed target, determine every next derivative target.

We use the four already excluded kernels

    Qalpha=(V+P)(-4)+Wp(-5)+L,
    Qbeta=T(-3)+Wp(-5)+L+O(-10),

with actual quotient K/Q=O(4)+Wp(-3), from the doubled-sector proof. The present proof reduces to those kernels or contradicts the rank-three next derivative directly. It includes the branch where the low scalar on Wp(-3) is ZERO; the a=2 low-isomorphism argument cannot simply be reused.

## 1. Universal evaluations and extensions

Besides the established sextic and septic kernels, two evaluations are useful:

    ker(T(-3)->O(4))=V(-4)+Wp(-5),
    ker(P(-4)->O(4))=Wp(-5)+O(-10).

Indeed T=V tensor W and P=Wp tensor W. Tensor Euler gives surjections T(-3)->V(-2) and P(-4)->Wp(-3), with kernels V(-4) and Wp(-5). Composing with the irreducible sextic V evaluation or septic Wp evaluation gives the unique respective septic T and octic P series. The compositions are nonzero because polynomial multiplication is nonzero, and uniqueness follows from the representation multiplicities. The resulting kernel extensions split: their line factors in Ext1 are H1(O(1)) and H1(O(5)). The underlying sextic/septic series are basepoint-free, by the invariant-divisor bound twelve; these compositions therefore are surjective as sheaf maps.

Every P(-4) octic map lifts through the septic Wp(-3) evaluation. The obstruction is (P tensor Sym4 W)^G=0. Equivalently it is the product through tensor Euler P(-4)->Wp(-3). For extensions of L by O(-10), the only possible nonzero class occurs on the O(-8) summand, in H1(O(-2)). Its middle bundle is W(-9). Thus an extension of L by O(-10) is L'+O(-10), for L' again one of the two choices of L. An extension of O(-8) alone by O(-10) is L. All extensions involving the additional Wp(-5) below split by H1 vanishing.

One high map must not be mistaken for a basepoint-free series: W(-9)->O(4) has degree thirteen. Its one-dimensional equivariant Hom is generated by complete linear evaluation W(-9)->O(-8), followed by multiplication by the nonzero invariant of degree twelve. Its actual image sheaf is O(-8) and its kernel is O(-10). We retain that actual image at its zeros.

## 2. A common low-map lemma

Suppose the high projection K->O(4) is surjective and its kernel is either

    Jalpha=(V+2P)(-4)+L,
    Jbeta=T(-3)+P(-4)+L+O(-10).

The remaining low map J->Wp(-3) must have generic rank two. Its only possible contributors are P(-4), O(-10), and W(-9). The low Hom from each P is the tensor Euler surjection, with kernel Wp(-5).

If a P component is nonzero, a multiplicity change leaves one active P. All deeper low maps lift through it: Ext1(C(-j),Wp(-5))=0 for j>=8. Subtracting these lifts gives Qalpha or Qbeta respectively. Both components of delta are then sheaf-surjective, so the actual quotient is the full O(4)+Wp(-3), as required to invoke the previous exclusion.

Suppose all P components vanish. For split L, all low components are proportional maps O(-10)->Wp(-3), so the generic rank is at most one. For L=W(-9), rank two forces the W(-9) component to have generic rank two. In Jbeta, its O(-10) component lifts through W(-9). To see this without assuming a determinant value, the unique injection O(-10)->W(-9) followed by the nonzero map to Wp(-3) cannot vanish: otherwise it would factor through O(-8), but Hom_G(O(-8),Wp(-3))=0. The composition therefore spans the unique O(-10) low Hom.

The kernels in this nonsplit, no-P case are consequently

    (V+2P)(-4), or T(-3)+P(-4)+O(-10).

In each application below the high-generating block has zero low map in the no-P case. It therefore supplies the entire O(4) factor inside the image of delta. The actual quotient is O(4)+W(-9), including any zeros of its embedding into the original target. The next derivative target is O(2)+W(-11). Neither displayed kernel has ANY Hom to W(-11), so its derivative has rank at most one, contradicting the remaining rank-three plateau.

The high-core low-zero condition is verified explicitly below; it is not an automatic statement about an abstract splitting of J.

## 3. The six a=1 rows

The six rows below represent eleven candidates, since the last five each have two choices of L. Let c denote the low scalar on a Wp(-3) block, when present.

### Row I: Wp(-3)+(V+2P)(-4)+O(-8)

If c is nonzero, eliminate the low Wp block by source/target graph changes. The remaining high source is (V+2P)(-4)+O(-8). If a P octic acts, its kernel and the one possible O(-8) extension give Qalpha. If no P acts, only O(-8) can act; rank three forces it nonzero. The kernel is (V+2P)(-4) and the actual quotient is Wp(-3)+O(-8). Its next target Wp(-5)+O(-10) has zero Hom from the kernel, impossible.

If c=0, the high Wp septic is nonzero, since otherwise Wp survives in H0(Q(3)). Remove the P octic high graphs using Section1. The high kernel is Jalpha, where the O(-8) extension by the septic kernel O(-10) gives L. Its high core Wp(-3) has low map zero by c=0. Section2 applies. In fact its L low map is already zero because both original blocks have zero low map; the general lemma still covers it.

### Row II: (Wp+T)(-3)+P(-4)+L

If c is nonzero, eliminate Wp. The high source is T(-3)+P(-4)+L. A nonzero T septic yields Qalpha by Section1; the P and L high maps lift through it since the obstruction line factors have degrees at least -1. If T is zero but the P octic is nonzero, Section1 gives Qbeta, with the possible extension absorbed into the choice of L'.

If both T and P high components vanish, rank three forces the L high map nonzero. Whether L is split or W(-9), its actual high image is O(-8) and its kernel is O(-10), by Section1. Thus Q=T(-3)+P(-4)+O(-10), with actual quotient Wp(-3)+O(-8). Its next target is Wp(-5)+O(-10); the low Hom is zero and only the O(-10) scalar high component can act. Rank is at most one, impossible.

If c=0, the high Wp septic is forced nonzero. If the T high component is also nonzero, complete septic evaluation has kernel (V+P)(-4); including the remaining P and L yields Jalpha. If the T high component is zero, the septic pair has kernel O(-10), the P high graph lifts, and the L extension yields Jbeta with L replaced if necessary by L'. The high core has low map zero in both cases. Section2 excludes them.

### Row III: P(-2)+2P(-4)+L

H0(Q(2))=0 forces the P sextic nonzero. Its kernel is V(-4), and all deeper high maps lift. Thus the high kernel is Jalpha. The degree -2 high core has no low map by degree. Section2 applies.

### Row IV: V(-2)+Wp(-3)+(V+P)(-4)+L

The V sextic is forced nonzero. If the high Wp septic is zero, its low scalar c is nonzero, or Wp would survive in H0(Q(3)). Eliminating this low isomorphism and taking the V sextic kernel gives Qalpha; all deeper high maps lift through V(-2).

If the high Wp septic is nonzero, the combined V sextic and Wp septic have kernel P(-4), by the previously proved unique nonsplit tensor-Euler extension of Wp(-3) by Wp(-5). Including the other blocks and lifting their high maps gives Jalpha. For Section2's no-P alternative, the map on this new P(-4) kernel must vanish. Its projection onto Wp(-3) is the tensor-Euler surjection, while V(-2) has no low map. Consequently c=0. This proves that the combined high core has low map zero in precisely the branch where that condition is needed.

### Row V: V(-2)+(Wp+T)(-3)+L+O(-10)

The V sextic is again forced nonzero. The T septic high map lifts through it: its only possible obstruction has invariant factor (T tensor Wp)^G=0 times H1(O(-2)). Removing this high graph does not change its zero low map.

If the Wp high map is zero, c is nonzero, and low elimination gives Qbeta. If the Wp high map is nonzero, combine V and Wp as in RowIV, giving Jbeta. If the low map on its P(-4) block vanishes, the same surjective projection proves c=0; the high core is then low-zero. Section2 applies.

### Row VI: (V+P)(-2)+P(-4)+L+O(-10)

Both sextic components are forced nonzero by H0(Q(2))=0. They form complete sextic evaluation, with kernel T(-3). All remaining high maps lift, giving Jbeta. The degree -2 high core has zero low map. Section2 applies.

These exhaust all eleven candidates, including every zero/nonzero branch of the relevant low scalar and leading high components. Thus (8,2) is excluded in the rank-two dual-image branch. The separate rank-one branch remains unresolved.
