# The special fibers determine the sextic polarization sign

## Theorem

The actual determinant-cubic family has three-line factorization, by the checked classification application cited below. Use the factor-cover notation

    pi:B -> P(W*),       M=pi*O(1),
    N=the other canonical ruling,       tau=M-N,
    K_B=M+N,       L=f*O_P(V*)(1).

Then exactly the following possibilities remain:

1. The line-factor image is the invariant dual conic. Here L=K_B-tau. At every one of the twelve special parameters the determinant cubic is a doubled tangent line to the invariant conic together with its other order-five tangent line. Its associated nonisotropic axis is singular on the cubic.
2. The line-factor image is the six-nodal invariant sextic. Its twelve node-preimages are the parameter-ramification orbit R_M, not the other orbit R_N. In particular

       L=K_B+2tau,

   and the previously possible class K_B-2tau is excluded. At every special parameter the determinant cubic is the double polar line of its associated nonisotropic axis together with one order-five tangent line. The associated axis is a smooth point of this cubic.

This determines the sign in the actual sextic alternative. It does not choose between the conic and sextic alternatives.

## Proof

An order-five subgroup fixes every point in a degree-three invariant fiber: its permutation action on at most three points is trivial. Its three fixed points in the dual plane are the nonisotropic axis, representing the polar line x=0, and the two isotropic points, representing y=0 and z=0. The fiber embedding keeps the images of its two support points r,s distinct.

In the conic-image case, f(r) and f(s) are both isotropic. They must be the two distinct isotropic fixed points. The norm cubic is therefore y^2 z, after interchanging y and z if necessary. This proves assertion 1 and its claimed geometry.

Suppose instead that the image is the nodal sextic. At a fixed parameter, exactly one of r and s belongs to the node-preimage orbit. Its factor is the polar line x=0. The other factor is one of y=0,z=0, because it is fixed by the same subgroup and is distinct from the polar factor.

If the node-preimage orbit were R_N, the simple point s would supply the polar line and the double point r would supply a tangent line. The resulting cubic would be x y^2 or x z^2. Neither belongs to the actual specialization eigenspace span{x^2 y,y^2 z}, or to the version obtained by exchanging y,z. Hence this case is impossible.

The node-preimage orbit is therefore R_M. The double factor is the polar x=0 and the simple factor is a tangent, so the cubic is x^2 y, up to the allowed coordinate interchange. The associated point p belongs only to the simple component and is smooth on the cubic.

Finally normalized plane adjunction and Riemann-Hurwitz give

    K_B=3L-R_M,       R_M=K_B+2M=2K_B+tau.

It follows that 3(L-K_B)=tau. Since L-K_B belongs to the cyclic order-five group generated by tau, multiplication by the inverse of three modulo five gives

    L-K_B=2tau.

This proves assertion 2.
