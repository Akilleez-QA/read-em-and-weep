# The normal quadratic coefficient is a universal constant times the b-channel squared

## Statement

Retain the universal three-channel monad parameters (a,b,c) from UNIVERSAL-THREE-CHANNEL-MONAD-ISOTROPY.md. On the subbundle locus abc!=0, normalize the invariant class beta in H2(B(-4)) using the leftmost symmetric-monad cohomology line, and normalize eta by

    delta_q0(eta)=beta.

The normal quadratic cup coefficient is defined by

    [eta,eta]/2 = d(a,b,c) beta|Q.

After one fixed choice of the irreducible-channel identifications and generators, there is a SINGLE universal constant kappa such that

    d(a,b,c)=kappa b^2.                                  (1)

In particular the quadratic normal obstruction vanishes for every subbundle monad in this family, or for none of them. No exceptional locus inside abc!=0 is possible. This theorem DOES NOT determine kappa. The already established b!=0 does not imply kappa!=0.

The determinant character in the proof is indispensable. Dropping it gives weights inconsistent even with scalar coordinate changes, which act trivially on projective space.

## 1. Retain the determinant line in the symplectic monad

Write Delta=det(H), H=V+W, and K=Omega^2(2). Natural linearizations give

    K tensor K -> Omega^4(4)=Delta O(-1).

The wedge pairing on K is symmetric. Tensor with the alternating pairing on Wprime to obtain the alternating form on Wprime K, valued in

    L=Delta O(-1).

Thus the natural self-dual monad, with determinant characters retained, is

    N O(-1) -> Wprime K -> N* Delta.

Indeed the adjoint of the left arrow has precisely the indicated target. Its rank-two cohomology has determinant L. Consequently the adjoint complex is

    Sym^2(monad) tensor L^{-1},

not the expression with Delta silently removed. It is the symplectic endomorphism differential graded Lie complex, and on the actual subbundle locus its cohomology is End_0(F). Twisting by t, its leftmost term, in complex degree -2, is

    Lambda^2 N tensor Delta^{-1} O(t-1).                (2)

All these constant lines restrict trivially to the perfect finite group G; they are nevertheless NONTRIVIAL for the block coordinate rescaling used below.

## 2. Canonical invariant beta and eta lines

At t=-4, the invariant H2(B(-4)) line is the hypercohomology edge contribution from (2):

    (Lambda^2 N)^G tensor Delta^{-1} H4(O(-5))
      = Lambda^2(Wprime) tensor Delta^{-2}.             (3)

Here H4(O(-5))=Delta^{-1}. The same invariant edge computation follows directly from the negative symmetric-monad calculation: other contributing characters have no invariant, so there is no unresolved differential or splitting in this invariant line.

At Q, the analogous leftmost term for B_Q(-2) is Lambda^2 N Delta^{-1} O_Q(-3). If Rq denotes the one-dimensional equation representation generated by q0, the equivariant hypersurface sequence gives

    H3(O_Q(-3))=Rq tensor Delta^{-1}.

The connecting isomorphism delta_q0 identifies its invariant line with (3) after retaining Rq. Equivalently, the normalized eta line transforms as Rq times the beta line. The negative-monad b-channel theorem guarantees the relevant invariant connecting map is an isomorphism on abc!=0. This is why the normalized generator is unambiguous there.

## 3. Source gauges determine one weight

Rescale the A source coefficient by r and the Wprime source coefficient by s. The universal parameters transform as

    (a,b,c) -> (s a,s b,r c).

The associated monad isomorphism is g^{-1} on the left, identity in the middle, and g^t on the right. Thus beta and eta both transport with factor s^{-2}, because their line comes from Lambda^2(Wprime) on the left. Functoriality of the bracket gives

    d(s a,s b,r c)=s^2 d(a,b,c).                         (4)

In particular d is independent of c under its multiplicative gauge; no assertion about individual cochain formulas is used.

## 4. Block coordinate changes determine the remaining weights

Let a coordinate change scale V by lambda and W by mu. The three channel coefficients transform as

    (a,b,c) -> (lambda^3 a,lambda mu^2 b,lambda^2 mu c).

Also Delta has weight lambda^3 mu^2 and Rq has weight lambda^2. Therefore the transport factors in the chosen cohomology generators are

    beta:lambda^{-6} mu^{-4},
    eta: lambda^{-4} mu^{-4}.

Comparing the transported bracket with its target beta yields

    d(lambda^3 a,lambda mu^2 b,lambda^2 mu c)
      =lambda^2 mu^4 d(a,b,c).                         (5)

As a consistency check, lambda=mu=t sends every alpha channel to t^3 times itself. Formula (5) gives t^6, exactly as the common source gauge s=r=t^3 in (4). Omitting Delta^{-1} from (2) fails this check.

The function b^2 satisfies (4) and (5). Hence d/b^2 is invariant under all source gauges and block coordinate changes. The preceding universal-three-channel theorem proves that these transformations act transitively on abc!=0. Thus d/b^2 is constant, proving (1). One may alternatively use that cup products vary algebraically on this constant-cohomology family and solve the Laurent-character equations; the only allowable monomial is b^2.

## 5. What this proves and what remains

This removes all dependence on the other two monad channels from the NORMAL quadratic coefficient after canonical edge normalization. It rules out cancellation loci involving a or c, but it does NOT rule out a universal cancellation giving kappa=0. In particular, the negative-monad proof only shows that its own natural Bott multiplier is nonzero; it does not identify the twofold cup/primitive composition defining kappa with that multiplier or its square.

A determination of kappa must keep the symplectic dg Lie product and the hypercohomology correction terms, or supply another geometric identification. Neither multiplying the two known nonzero b-channel legs nor the weight computation evaluates this constant. No actual monad coefficients have been selected, and no actual-instance matrix or rank computation is involved.
