# Vanishing in six axis fibers kills the residual third channel

## Precise theorem

Let beta2:A O_P(A)(-2)->P4 O_P(A) be the established actual second symbol, with cokernel I_Sigma(6), where Sigma has length24 and misses the discriminant conic Delta. Let Psi:D5 O(-3)->I_Sigma(6) be any equivariant cubic third gate.

If (i) Psi restricts to zero on Delta, and (ii) its map to the ACTUAL COKERNEL FIBER is zero at each of the six order-five axes, then Psi=0 globally.

Condition(ii) means zero in I_Sigma/m I_Sigma. Ordinary value zero in O at a point of Sigma is automatic and is NOT sufficient. This note proves the geometric implication independently of any proposed cochain calculation.

## 1. Six semisimple axes in general position

Each of the six C5 subgroups of A5 has one fixed semisimple axis in P(A), represented in stabilizer eigenlines by st. The two other eigenlines s^2,t^2 lie on the discriminant. These six semisimple axes form one orbit and miss Delta.

No three are collinear. If a line contained three of them, its orbit would contain at most five lines: distinct lines account for disjoint sets of pairs among the fifteen pairs of axes, and each such line accounts for at least three pairs. But there is no orbit of at most five lines in P(A). An orbit of size2,3,4 would give an impossible nontrivial injection of simple A5 into S4; size1 contradicts irreducibility. A size5 orbit would have stabilizer A4. The restriction of the three-dimensional representation A (and its dual) to A4 is irreducible, so A4 has no projectively fixed line. For example its character is3,-1,0 on identity, the three involutions, and the eight elements of order3, and pairs to zero with every one-dimensional A4 character.

There is no conic through all six axes. Five points with no three collinear determine a unique conic. If it contained the sixth, it would be G-stable; perfectness makes its equation invariant. The only invariant quadratic on A is the discriminant, which contains none of the axes. This is a contradiction.

It follows that the six conics C_i through all but the ith axis are distinct and irreducible. A reducible conic containing five points would have a line containing at least three.

## 2. The actual second symbol has rank two and no linear ideal terms there

At an axis a=st, the binary lift of C5 gives input weights8,0,2 and target weights2,4,6,8. The O(-2) fiber has weight0. Therefore the second symbol can have nonzero columns only on weights2 and8. The normalizer interchanges those two columns and their target lines, so either both are nonzero or both zero. Thus its rank is2 or0, not1.

Rank0 is impossible. If all entries vanished at one axis, equivariance would give the same at all six. Every maximal three-by-three minor would then vanish to order at least three. The local defining ideal of Sigma would lie in m^3, giving local length at least dim C[x,y]/(x,y)^3=6 at each axis. This contradicts length(Sigma)=24<36. Hence the rank is2 at every axis.

Reduce locally by its invertible rank-two block. The residual presentation is a two-by-one column presenting the same ideal of maximal minors. The derivative of that residual column is the natural map

    T_p P(A) -> Hom(ker beta2_p,coker beta2_p).

The tangent weights are2,8, the kernel weight is0, and the cokernel weights are4,6. There is no equivariant map between these representations. Therefore both residual entries have zero first jet, proving

    I_Sigma,p is contained in m_p^2.                    (1)

This uses an actual geometric differential and representation weights, not a map evaluation.

There is a useful sharper consequence. The local ideal is generated by the two residual Schur entries and lies in m^2, so its colength is at least four: colength three would force I=m^2, which requires three minimal generators. Six such points exhaust the known total length24. Thus Sigma is supported EXACTLY at these six axes, with local length four at each.

In equivariant affine tangent coordinates x,y, the two quadratic initial entries have opposite weights4,6. They are therefore proportional to x^2 and y^2, respectively; xy has weight0 and cannot occur. Neither coefficient can vanish, since then the degree-two quotient alone would force local length at least five. Consequently each local ideal is (x^2,y^2): the generators have those initial terms, all cubic terms already lie in that ideal, and equality follows from the equal colength four. In particular these are six actual length-four complete intersections, not six reduced points and not ordinary double-point schemes m^2. The weaker inclusion(1) is sufficient for the argument below.

## 3. A fiber zero is a third-order zero after discriminant division

The square restriction hypothesis and disjointness of Sigma from Delta give, from the established exact sequence,

    0 -> I_Sigma(7) --delta--> I_Sigma(9)
      -> O_Delta(9) -> 0,

an equivariant factorization Psi=delta Psi1, where Psi1 is a D5-valued family of degree-seven forms in I_Sigma.

At each axis delta is a unit in the local ring. Condition(ii) therefore puts every component of Psi1 in m_p I_Sigma,p. By(1), every such septic vanishes to order at least three at all six axes.

This is where the ideal-sheaf fiber matters: ordinary vanishing under I_Sigma->O would provide only membership in I_Sigma and would not imply a third-order zero.

## 4. Six conics exclude every such septic

Let F be any degree-seven form vanishing to order at least three at all six axes. For each irreducible conic C_i, local intersection multiplicities at its five specified axes are at least three, so their sum is at least15. Bezout gives total intersection degree14 unless C_i is a component. Therefore all six distinct conics C_i divide F.

Their product has degree12, larger than7. Hence F=0. Apply this to every component of Psi1 to conclude Psi1=0 and Psi=0.

## Scope

The theorem is conditional on square restriction vanishing and actual cokernel-fiber vanishing. The new source-change/two-chart calculations are candidate verifications of those hypotheses and require their own comparison audits. This geometric theorem does not evaluate those cochains, assume generic surjectivity, or settle later obstruction orders.

If both hypotheses are established for the actual third gate, only then the last finite Smith valuation lower bound advances from3 to4. The upper bound31 and the unresolved generic section alternatives4 or5 remain unchanged.

Inputs: actual second symbol and length24 Hilbert--Burch scheme; THIRD-BOCKSTEIN-POLARIZATION-AND-SQUARE-DIRECTION-GATE.md for the exact discriminant sequence; standard explicit A5/A4 character arithmetic included above. The companion verifier performs only weights, finite counts, and intersection inequalities, not selected coefficients or matrix ranks.
