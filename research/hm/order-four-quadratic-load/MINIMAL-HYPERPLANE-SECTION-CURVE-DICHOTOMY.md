# Minimal hyperplane sections: reduced curves force the two-section branch

## Actual input and open set

Let F be the normalized HM bundle, and G=F|H for a general hyperplane H≅P3. The existing GOOD-PLANE-PRIMARY-TRACE.md proves H0(G(1))=0 on a nonempty open of hyperplanes, by the actual good-plane Koszul isomorphism. GENERIC-PRODUCT-PRIMARY-INJECTION.md proves that the generic value b=h0(G(2)) is either one or two. The section nonvanishing uses the separately pinned Banica–Manolache theorem and Boralevi hyperplane stability, with the source roles specified in EVERY-PRODUCT-QUADRATIC-IS-ADJOINT-BAD.md, published at 04ae00f0134c15d63dc39f84ce9a07b8ed81515d.

The following conclusion uses these actual inputs without a selected hyperplane or a coefficient/rank experiment.

## 1. Regularity is proved, not assumed

Take any nonzero s∈H0(G(2)) on this hyperplane open. A divisorial zero of degree d≥1 would factor s through a nonzero section of G(2−d). Every such section vanishes: H0(G(1))=0 and multiplication by a nonzero complementary polynomial injects each lower-twist section into that zero space. Thus s has no divisorial zeros.

Its zero scheme C cannot be empty, because c2(G(2))=4+2(−1)+4=6 is nonzero. Locally the ideal of a section of a rank-two bundle is generated by two elements. With no codimension-one component it has height two wherever nontrivial; in a regular local ring those two elements form a regular sequence. Hence C is a pure locally complete-intersection curve, without isolated or embedded components, of degree six.

Adjunction gives

ω_C = (ω_P3⊗det G(2))|C = O_C(−1),

because det G(2)=O(3). Consequently p_a(C)=−2. Negative arithmetic genus is allowed because connectedness and reducedness have not been assumed.

The section sequence is

0→O_P3→G(2)→I_C(3)→0,

and H1(O_P3)=0 gives the exact numerical identity

h0(I_C(3))=b−1.

In particular the b=1 branch has no cubic containing C scheme-theoretically.

## 2. Reduced subcanonical sextics are three disjoint plane conics

Assume C is reduced. Decompose it into its connected components C_j, which are open-and-closed Gorenstein curves and inherit ω_Cj=O_Cj(−1). A proper reduced connected curve over C has H0(O)=C, so p_a(C_j)=h1(O_Cj)≥0.

If d_j>0 is its degree, curve Riemann–Roch and duality give

−d_j=deg ω_Cj=2p_a(C_j)−2.

Therefore d_j≤2, and the same equality makes d_j even. Thus each d_j=2 and p_a(C_j)=0. A reduced connected degree-two curve in projective space is a plane conic: an integral component is a smooth plane conic, or the curve is two distinct meeting lines in their common plane. A nonplanar pair of lines would be disjoint, contrary to connectedness.

Since the total degree is six, C has exactly three pairwise disjoint connected components, each a plane conic, possibly reducible. Let L_j be an equation of a plane containing C_j. Then the nonzero cubic L_1 L_2 L_3 vanishes on the reduced curve C, hence belongs to H0(I_C(3)). The section identity above gives b≥2.

On the generic b≤2 open this proves b=2 and h0(I_C(3))=1. In that case this product of plane equations spans the entire cubic ideal system of C.

## 3. Exact remaining geometric alternative

If the generic value b is one, then for every H in its generic open the unique projective section of G(2) has a necessarily nonreduced lci sextic zero scheme, with ω_C=O_C(−1), arithmetic genus−2, and no containing cubic. This is stronger than merely leaving an unspecified one-section possibility.

Conversely, a reduced minimal section curve on the generic b≤2 locus forces b=2 at that H. To conclude that the generic value is two one still needs such reduced curves on a dense family, or an argument excluding the generic nonreduced sextic alternative. A reduced curve on an exceptional hyperplane alone is not enough: h0 may jump there.

No reducedness or connectedness assertion for the actual generic section has been proved here. In particular neither Bertini nor the global generation of the different twist F(3) may be used to assume the minimal section curve reduced.

## Consequence for the adjoint product bounds

If the reduced generic alternative is established, the published Hopf bound h0(B_H(3))≥2b−1 gives at least three adjoint sections in twist three. Products then have corank at least six, and the actual rank-twenty lift boundary has rank at most fourteen on its generic product-good flank open. Until reducedness or another b=2 proof is supplied, the unconditional product corank bound remains two.

This note provides a new geometric necessary condition on the unresolved b=1 branch, not a proof that this branch is impossible. No source coefficients, ranks, experiments, or external posts are used.
