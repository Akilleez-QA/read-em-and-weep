# The remaining special D5 octic does not extend: exclusion of scalar a=12

## Statement and scope

At an order-five hyperplane, the actual Cartan boundary is nonzero on its source weight-zero line. Together with the previously proved weight-one nonvanishing, this excludes the remaining scalar a=12 possibility. It follows, using the established classification and no-off-diagonal-divisor theorem, that the actual residual map has generic rank four in every remaining target type.

This is a universal ideal-sheaf proof with all nonzero geometric parameters retained. It does not choose actual HM coefficients, evaluate a selected matrix or rank, or assert completion of the original proof repair.

## 1. Formal normalization and actual ideals

The established special curve is C=Q disjoint-union Y. The nonzero parameters permit rescaling the equations q,q_c and the coordinate x to write

    q=y²+B u², q_c=y²+D u²,
    g=xz²-yq,
    I_Q=(z,q_c), I_Y=(x²,xq,q²,g),
    q0=x²+eta yz.

Here B,D,eta are nonzero, and B!=D. These remain indeterminate parameters. The normalization follows by dividing the original conic equations by their nonzero y² coefficients and replacing x by kappa*x/(c*A) in the original g=kappa*x*z²-c*y*(A*y²+B0*u²). It is a change of coordinates and generator scales, not a specialization of their actual values. Rescaling q0 then gives the indicated eta!=0.

Set

    alpha=D/B, beta=1-alpha, so q_c=alpha q+beta y²,
    S=x²z, G=xz²-y³, J=xyz, K=xy².

Polynomial function weights modulo five are x:0, y:4, z:1, u:4. Inversion fixes x,y,z and negates u. The octic q0 G² is even and has weight4.

We use two established exact ideal-sheaf images on the plane (x=0):

    I_Y²|x=0 = q² (y,q)²,
    (I_Y²:x)|x=0 = q² (y,q).                       (1)

These are proved by the regular-pair calculations in SPECIAL-HYPERPLANE-ADJOINTS-ARE-EXACTLY-SQUARES.md, sections 5 and 3, and retain the nonreduced complete intersection (y,q). No polynomial-square saturation assumption is made.

## 2. The first forced trace

Suppose F in H0(I_C²(8)) restricted to R=(u=0) is q0 G². Averaging over the finite stabilizer and inversion, take F of weight4 and even parity.

On Pi=(z=0), the ordinary-square trace argument gives

    F|Pi in q_c² span{x⁴,x²q,q²}.

The weights of these three directions are respectively 1,4,2. Thus only x²q q_c² can occur. Restriction to u=0 fixes its coefficient to one, since q0 G²|z=0=x²y⁶. Consequently

    F|Pi=x²q q_c².

There is an explicit section with this trace in I_C²:

    F0=x²q q_c²-beta² x³y³z²
      =alpha² x²q³+2alpha beta x²y²q²-beta² x²y³g. (2)

The second expression lies in I_Y² term by term; the first lies in I_Q² term by term. Hence F-F0=zH, where

    H in H0(I_Y²(7) intersect I_Q(7)),
    H has weight3 and even parity.                 (3)

Here (I_Y²:z)=I_Y² because z is a nonzerodivisor on the Cohen-Macaulay ordinary thickening; and (I_Q²:z)=I_Q.

Writing H_R for restriction to u=0, direct subtraction gives

    H_R=x⁴z³+(beta²-2)x³y³z
         +eta x²yz⁴-2eta xy⁴z²+eta y⁷.            (4)

## 3. The second forced trace and its exact lift

By (1), H|x=0 belongs to q² H0((y,q)²(3)). The degree-three section space of (y,q)² is generated by y³,y²z,y²u,yq. After requiring weight3 for H (and even parity), its trace must have the form

    H|x=0 = q² y (a y²+b q).

Membership H in I_Q says that this trace belongs to (z,q_c). It contains no z, and q_c is coprime to yq. Therefore a y²+bq is a scalar multiple of q_c. Equation (4), at x=0, fixes that scalar to eta. Thus

    H|x=0=eta y q² q_c.                            (5)

There is an explicit lift of (5) lying in both ideals required in (3):

    H1=eta[-alpha q²g+beta y g²].                  (6)

Both terms lie in I_Y². Modulo z, expression (6) is eta yq²q_c, so H1 is in I_Q as well. Its restriction to R is

    (H1)_R=eta[beta x²yz⁴-(alpha+2beta)xy⁴z²+y⁷].  (7)

## 4. The colon forces an impossible final trace

By (5), H-H1=xL. Equation (3) and (6) imply

    L in H0((I_Y²:x)(6)),
    L has weight3 and even parity.

The second identity in (1) puts its trace in q² H0((y,q)(2)). The degree-two section space of (y,q) is generated by y²,yz,yu,q. Their weights are respectively 3,0,3,3, while q² has weight1. None can produce weight3. Therefore

    L|x=0=0.                                      (8)

But (4) minus (7), divided by x and restricted to x=0, is

    L|x=u=0 = eta(beta-1)y⁴z²
             = -eta alpha y⁴z² != 0.             (9)

Both eta and alpha=D/B are nonzero by the actual geometric hypotheses. Equations (8)-(9) contradict each other. Hence q0 G² does not extend to H0(I_C²(8)).

The section spaces used in both trace steps are those of complete-intersection ideal sheaves (y,q) and their ordinary square. Their degree-two and degree-three descriptions follow from the Koszul and square resolutions on P2; H1 of the relevant line bundles vanishes. Thus the argument does not lose a possible extra saturated section.

## 5. Actual Cartan and residual consequences

The source weight-zero line of Sym⁸W is the monomial X⁴Y⁴. Cartan polarization followed by wedge with w=X³Y sends it to a combination of SK and G² with a nonzero coefficient on G². The coefficient is universally nonzero (the symmetric polarization coefficient is binomial(4,2)²/binomial(8,4)); the two nonzero wedge normalization factors do not change this conclusion.

The shadow q0 SK extends: K belongs to I_Z(3), and H1(I_C(4))=0 gives a lift of q0K to H0(I_C(5)); multiplying by S supplies the octic lift. Thus the actual weight-zero Cartan boundary is nonzero precisely as forced by the G² nonextension above.

At ell=X, the scalar a=12 target O_T(14) has stabilizer weight -14=1 modulo five, whereas this source line has weight0. Equivariance rules out its nonzero image in that scalar line. Therefore scalar a=12 is impossible.

The earlier weight-one nonextension already excludes scalar a=14 and a=18. Types IV and VII are impossible, and the remaining types I, II, III, V and VI all have generic residual rank four: types I, II and V by the unconditional no-off-diagonal-divisor theorem; type III because its low V O(4) coupling cannot vanish now that scalar12 is excluded; and type VI because its low W-prime O(5) coupling cannot vanish now that scalar14 is excluded.

This conclusion concerns the established residual map and classification. Any implication for the original global proof must be made through its separately checked reduction, not inferred merely from this note's title.
