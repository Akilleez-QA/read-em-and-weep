# The Serre involution splits the actual lower cohomology into dimensions nine and twenty-one

## Statement and scope

Let phi multiply the negative coordinate block by mu, where mu^2=-1, and use the cone marking furnished by the Serre double-cover theorem. On physical inversion-positive cohomology phi is an involution. For

    C=H1(B_Q(2))_+=V+3P4+3D5

its actual eigenspaces are

    C^(phi=+1)=P4+D5,
    C^(phi=-1)=V+2P4+2D5.                         (1)

These have dimensions nine and twenty-one. The negative quadratic space A has phi character -1, and the actual target P=H1(B_Q(4))_+ has phi character +1. Consequently multiplication by A annihilates the entire nine-dimensional positive eigenspace. The already proved affine secondary-tensor variation vanishes on the entire twenty-one-dimensional negative eigenspace.

This computes actual symmetry sectors, not the unknown absolute cubic value. It does not improve the generic four-or-five section count.

## 1. Keep the Serre marking in the exact sequences

Work on the smooth weighted stack X=P(1,1,2,2), with exact tame coarse pushforward understood. Let G be the spinor-twisted rank-two bundle, sigma its invariant regular section, and Z its actual zero scheme. The Serre theorem proves that Z is homogeneous for block scaling. Choose the cone marking so that the subline generated by sigma has phi weight +1. Its quotient I_Z(2) then has the EXTRA weight -1 relative to its intrinsic coordinate linearization. The determinant likewise has extra weight -1 relative to O(2).

Let epsilon be the one-dimensional representation trivial on G and of phi character -1. The genuine equivariant K-theory identities are

    [G]=[O]+epsilon[I_Z(2)],
    [B_Q(2)]=epsilon[G]+epsilon[I_Z^2(4)].        (2)

Indeed Sym^2 G has kernel G through multiplication by sigma and quotient I_Z^2(4), whose quotient weight is (-1)^2=+1. Dividing by det G and twisting by O(2) contributes epsilon in (2). Only after taking the phi trace is epsilon replaced by the scalar -1; epsilon is not the additive inverse of the trivial representation in genuine equivariant K-theory.

The known cone section theorem gives H0(B_Q(2))_+=CN with phi weight -1. The restriction sequence and the established ambient vanishings give H2(B_Q(2))_+=0 and H3(B_Q(2))=0: H2(B(2))=0, H3(B)=H3(B(2))=0, and H4(B)=0 suffice. The first vanishing is part of the established uniform ambient bounds; the restriction sequence is B -> B(2) -> B_Q(2). Therefore (2) yields

    Tr_phi C = chi_phi O_Q(4)-chi_phi O_Q(2)
               +chi_phi O_Z(2)-chi_phi O_(2Z)(4).       (3)

Here chi_phi is the virtual G-character obtained by taking the phi trace on the physical-positive part of the Euler characteristic. The group action commutes with phi, so this character retains each irreducible multiplicity, not just its dimension.

## 2. The four Euler characters

The actual homogeneous zero scheme is the disjoint union of the reduced positive conic and the vertex component. At the vertex a cover-degree d term in physical twist t has intrinsic block weight mu^(t-d/2). For even t, the physical-positive terms have d divisible by four. On the conic the positive-coordinate action is trivial; its first normal terms in the double conic are physical-negative and do not enter the table.

The needed table is

| Sheaf, physical-positive part | Ordinary Euler character | Phi-trace Euler character |
|---|---|---|
| O_Q(2) | A+D5 | D5-A |
| O_Q(4) | A+V+2P4+3D5 | -A-V+D5 |
| O_Z(2) | A+P4+2D5 | -A+P4+2D5 |
| O_(2Z)(4) | A+2V+4P4+5D5 | -A+2P4+3D5 |

Here are the individual contributions, including the nonreduced vertex pieces.

For O_Q(2), the positive quadratics modulo q0 give D5 with weight +1; the negative quadratics give A with weight -1. For O_Q(4), the negative-coordinate degrees zero, two, and four give respectively

    Sym^8 W'=P4+D5,
    D5 tensor A=A+V+P4+D5,
    Sym^4 W=D5,

with signs +,-,+.

For O_Z(2), the reduced conic contributes Sym^4 W'=D5 with sign +. The vertex degrees zero and four contribute A with sign - and V tensor A=P4+D5 with sign +. The latter uses the actual cubic even quotient V tensor O_L: the exact sequence is

    0 -> W' O_L(-5) -> D5 O_L(-2) -> V O_L -> 0.

For O_(2Z)(4), the conic contributes Sym^8 W'=P4+D5 with sign +. Its vertex degrees zero and four give D5 and D5 tensor A, with signs + and -. At degree eight the proved square-relation sequence is

    0 -> P4 O_L(-4) -> Sym^8 W' O_L -> Q4(4) -> 0.

Consequently its Euler character is

    chi Q4(4)=Sym^8 W'+P4 tensor A
              =V+2P4+2D5,

with sign +. This uses H1(O_L(-4))=Sym^2 W=A and retains the square-ideal relation rather than replacing the vertex by its support. There are no further physical-positive vertex pieces: the ordinary square of the homogeneous (3,5) complete intersection has no component of cover degree twelve or higher. The established square filtration supplies this same termination.

All signs in the table are intrinsic coordinate signs. The additional Serre-marking factors are applied only in (2), so they are not counted twice.

## 3. Recover the two eigenspaces

Substituting the last column into (3) gives

    Tr_phi C=-V-P4-D5.

The ordinary character is the independently established V+3P4+3D5. As a cross-check, applying the ordinary, unsigned Serre sequences to the middle column recovers exactly this character. The twice-composed cone marking differs from the central-inversion marking only by a scalar, since End_G(F_Q)=C. Scalar conjugation is invisible on B. Thus phi squares to physical inversion on C, and consequently squares to one on its physical-positive part. Taking half the sum and difference proves (1).

## 4. Consequences for actual multiplication and normal variation

Multiplication A tensor C -> P is natural for the cone marking. Its factors have signs -1 and +1 on C^(phi=+1), while P has sign +1. Hence it is zero on that entire eigenspace.

The earlier actual perfect-action theorem identifies the full multiplication kernel with 2P4+2D5 and the active quotient with P4 tensor A*=V+P4+D5. The active quotient must lie in the negative eigenspace. Its kernel there is therefore one P4+D5; the other P4+D5 is exactly the positive eigenspace.

For the secondary tensor, the affine normal reflection changes the tangent direction by -1. Naturality, together with the signs of A, D5, C and P, forces its variation to vanish whenever the input in C has sign -1. Thus its possible nonzero normal variation is confined to the nine-dimensional P4+D5 input, not all thirty dimensions. In particular the actual fixed quadratic probe t(a), already proved to have negative sign, remains in the zero-variation sector. No absolute value is selected by this observation.
