    G5=ker[H0(B_R(5)) O_T -> C_lin(2)].

In that case it factors through G5. After twisting by four, its obstruction to lifting is H1(A O_T)=0. Ordinary lifts differ by H0(A O_T)=A; they are NOT unique. There is, however, a unique equivariant lift because A^G=0 and invariants are exact in characteristic zero.

Hence H0(I_R^5 B(9))^G=H0(I_R^6 B(9))^G. The actual square-bundle theorem gives the latter space A^G=0. Thus no invariant lift can be changed by a higher-normal invariant section.

## 3. Geometric exclusion of the ordinary product line

Suppose the prescribed invariant lift belonged to the first term in (2). Its fourth normal coefficient is then the restriction of the unique D5 summand of the ambient H0(B(5)), paired with Sym^4 W=D5. Equality with the prescribed q0 low-D5 Veronese would force this whole ambient five-dimensional summand to restrict to zero on C0=(q0=0) in R.

But U=H0(F(3)) is the actual genuine four-space and generates F_C0(3). The associated ruled surface in P(U*)=P3 is integral and nondegenerate, hence its homogeneous ideal has at most one quadratic equation. Under the actual identification H0(B(5))=Sym^2 U this gives

    dim ker[H0(B(5))->H0(B_C0(5))] <= 1.

It cannot contain the five-dimensional D5. This contradiction excludes the ordinary product line.

Therefore any prescribed lift has a NONZERO image in ker(mu) under the connecting map (2). This is a genuine necessary non-product condition, not a proof of scalar nonvanishing.

    sigma4 restricted to C0 = 0

## Remaining precise gap

The argument has reduced the proposed scalar-vanishing lift to a nonzero invariant Koszul class in ker(mu) with a specified leading normal tensor. Its existence is not settled by the dimensions four-to-one or by the distinguished t6 family. Determining the actual leading-normal image of those non-product classes, or a geometric obstruction to that specified tensor, remains open here. No selected coefficient, multiplication rank, or consumed experiment is used.

The new progress is the geometric exclusion of the entire ordinary product line, the exact invariant connecting space, the injective leading-normal map, and the necessary conic-vanishing test for the t6 candidate. None is relabeled as resolution of the leading scalar.
