# The intrinsic plane quotient gives an upper bound of 46 for the normal defect

## Scope

Fix a general parameter B in a two-dimensional residual beta image. By BETA-QUOTIENT-POSITIVITY.md, B lies outside κ(C); X=X_B is integral, locally a complete intersection, of degree 15; and A=O_X(5H−C) is globally generated. The associated morphism h:X→P² is generically finite of degree 100. The morphism need not be finite everywhere; X need not be normal or smooth. On every active residual sextic D, A|D=L has degree five and h|D is finite birational onto an integral plane quintic Γ. The curve D has arithmetic genus three, but is not assumed smooth or Gorenstein.

Let D be a general member of a positive-dimensional algebraic family of distinct such curves on this fixed X. The previously established normal-defect sequence is

0→ω_D⊗(ω_X|D)^−1→N_{D/X}→F_D→0,

where N is rank-one torsion-free, e=length F_D, and deg N=e−21. For a family of dimension at least two, the independently proved lower bound is e≥23.

## 1. A general moving D is generically in the étale locus

Because h is dominant and generically finite in characteristic zero, its function-field extension is finite separable. Consequently there is a dense open subset of X on which h is étale. One way to see this is to restrict first to the quasi-finite locus, then to a finite neighborhood of the generic point, and invert the discriminant of the finite separable function-field extension. The resulting étale neighborhood is nonempty. The maximal étale locus is open, so its complement is a fixed proper closed subset of the integral surface X and has only finitely many curve components.

A positive-dimensional family of distinct integral curves cannot have its general member equal to one of those finitely many components. Thus the generic point of a general D belongs to the étale locus of h. In particular X is smooth there, since P² is smooth. The plane curve Γ is also smooth at its generic point, as every integral curve in characteristic zero is generically smooth.

The reduced equation of Γ pulls back to a local parameter transverse to D at this generic point. Equivalently, D occurs with multiplicity one in the effective Cartier divisor h*Γ. This is a generic statement; it asserts neither that h is étale everywhere along D nor that h*Γ has no other components.

## 2. The normal-sheaf injection

Let f_Γ be the homogeneous quintic equation of Γ. Its pullback is a section of A^5 which vanishes scheme-theoretically on D, since h|D factors through Γ. It therefore gives a morphism

A^(−5)→I_{D/X}.

Precomposition gives a morphism of O_D-modules

j:N_{D/X}=Hom_X(I_{D/X},O_D)→Hom_X(A^(−5),O_D)=A^5|D.

At the generic point of D, I_{D/X} is generated by a parameter t, and the preceding multiplicity-one assertion says h*f_Γ=u t for a unit u. Hence j is multiplication by a unit there and is nonzero generically. Since N is torsion-free of rank one on the integral curve D, its kernel is zero. The cokernel G_D has finite length. We obtain the exact sequence

0→N_{D/X}→O_D(5A)→G_D→0.

This argument uses only generic smoothness/étaleness, the scheme-theoretic factorization through Γ, and torsion-freeness of N. It does not use normality of X or Gorensteinness of D.

## 3. Exact numerical budget

Since deg(A|D)=5, the target line bundle has degree 25. Additivity of degree in a finite-length quotient gives

length G_D=25−deg N=25−(e−21)=46−e.

Therefore e≤46. Combining with the lower bound for a family of dimension at least two gives

23≤e(D)≤46,
length G_D=46−e(D).

More generally the same upper bound applies to any positive-dimensional family of distinct residual sextics on an integral X_B outside κ(C), whenever the same quotient and defect hypotheses hold. The stronger lower bounds 25 or 27 for family dimensions at least three or four may then be combined with it as applicable.

Equivalently, composing the two canonical injections provides a filtration of a length-46 quotient:

0→F_D→O_D(5A)/(ω_D⊗(ω_X|D)^−1)→G_D→0.

The middle quotient has length 46 because its source has degree −21 and its target degree 25. The two summands in its length are precisely e and 46−e. Neither is identified with a classical singularity invariant here.

## Boundary

This produces a finite necessary obstruction budget, not a contradiction or a complete repair. In particular, it does not establish an upper bound below 23. Γ varies with D, whereas the non-étale locus used to justify generic multiplicity one is fixed on X. Contracted curves do not invalidate that argument: they are contained in this fixed proper closed complement and cannot contain the general member of a moving family.
