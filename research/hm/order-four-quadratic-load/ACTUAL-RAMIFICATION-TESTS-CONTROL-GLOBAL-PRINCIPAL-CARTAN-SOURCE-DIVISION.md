# Actual ramification tests control global principal Cartan source division

## Scope

The twelve actual ramification tests are sufficient for one PRECISE global
lifting problem: division of the Cartan-VV component through the two
principal source maps. They are not sufficient for division of the whole
monad cochain by q. In particular, a source correction through the a-channel
also introduces its actual b-channel companion, which remains to be handled.

The argument below is an exact sheaf calculation on the product of the two
spinor lines. It uses the actual smooth bicubic and its reduced ramification
divisor, not an evaluated source matrix or a pointwise rank experiment.

## 1. The actual principal source map

Let S=P(U) times P(W), with O(i,j) denoting the two usual line-bundle
factors. The positive null covector is v=r^2. In the fixed actual Cartan
space P, consider

    Phi: U O(-2,0) plus A O(0,-1) --> P O,
    Phi(Q,P0)=Abar(r^2,Q)+p_C(w,P0).                    (1)

Nonzero channel scalars can be included in the two columns throughout;
they do not change the exact sequences or the following conclusion. The
full source arrow is NOT (1): its U column also contains the actual b-channel.
Here (1) means only its Cartan-VV projection.

The c-column is the actual marked cubic multiplication map transported
through f. It is fiberwise injective, with the standard cubic evaluation
quotient

    0 --> A O(0,-1) --> P O --> O(0,3) --> 0.           (2)

The quotient has its fixed determinant/evaluation marking. Composing the
first column of (1) with (2) gives the two positive polar derivatives of the
actual bicubic F(r,w), up to a common nonzero marked constant:

    U O(-2,0) --> O(0,3).                              (3)

This follows by polarization of lambda_w(Abar(r^2,u)); its cubic primitive
is lambda_w(Abar(r^2,r)), namely the actual (3,3) equation.

## 2. Exact support and the line kernel

The two sections in (3) are of bidegree (2,3). Their simultaneous zero
scheme R is the ramification scheme of the actual smooth cubic projection
D --> P(W). Euler's identity puts it on F=0. The previously proved
ramification theorem says R consists of twelve reduced points. In
particular these two polar sections form a regular sequence; no common
curve or nonreduced contribution has been suppressed.

The Koszul sequence is

    0 --> O(-4,-3) --> U O(-2,0)
      --> I_R(0,3) --> 0.                              (4)

Combining (2) and (4), the complete principal map (1) has the exact sequence

    0 --> O(-4,-3)
      --> U O(-2,0) plus A O(0,-1)
      --> P O --> O_R(0,3) --> 0.                     (5)

One can check (5) without appealing to determinant ranks: its image is the
inverse image of I_R(0,3) under (2). A section in the kernel of (3) has a
unique c-source component cancelling its image, because the first arrow
of (2) is injective. Thus the kernel is exactly the line in (4).

Sequence (5) is exact as sheaves. At R the line-kernel inclusion is not a subbundle: its fiber kernel has dimension two. Locally the remaining row is (x,y), with kernel generated by (-y,x).

At each reduced point of R, the last map in (5) is precisely the actual
functional lambda annihilating both principal source images. Its line
factor is retained in O_R(0,3).

## 3. The relevant homogeneous degree has no global lifting obstruction

Twist (5) by O(4,3). Its kernel becomes O, and its source and target are

    U O(2,3) plus A O(4,2) --> P O(4,3).               (6)

Let I be the image sheaf in (6). From

    0 --> O --> U O(2,3) plus A O(4,2) --> I --> 0

and the elementary product-line cohomology, H^1(I)=0 and H^0 of the source
surjects onto H^0(I), with one-dimensional kernel. Therefore

    image H^0(Phi(4,3))
      =ker[H^0(P O(4,3)) --> H^0(O_R(4,6))].           (7)

The last map is surjective. Its dimensions are

    source 69, kernel 1, image 68,
    target 80, quotient 12.

Thus the twelve functional conditions are independent in exactly this
homogeneous degree. This proves sufficiency, rather than inferring it from
the existence of twelve tests or a dimension count alone.

## 4. Application and its boundary

Any actual Cartan-VV residual of positive-coordinate degree two and
negative-coordinate degree three is a section of P O(4,3). If its actual
lambda value vanishes at all twelve ramification pairs, (7) supplies global
principal source corrections Q and P0 of respective spinor bidegrees
(2,3) and (4,2). The positive degrees are even, so these are polynomial
coefficients in the original positive v variables: respectively degree one
and degree two. Their total ordinary coefficient degrees are both four.

The construction may be chosen equivariantly and linearly in all prescribed
input parameters, by splitting the finite-dimensional surjection onto its
image over characteristic zero and averaging over the finite group. The
space of choices for each target section is the one-dimensional kernel in
(7); no canonical choice is asserted without additional marking. This one-dimensional ambiguity is on the positive cone; ambient polynomial representatives additionally have q0-multiple ambiguity.

In particular this statement applies to any calibrated sole-c residual
whose full calculation establishes the stated bidegree and all twelve
vanishings. It does NOT assert these vanishings for an uncomputed residual. For a
residual linear in the c-source, the condition must hold for EVERY c-input
at every point of R. Vanishing only on the distinguished matched square
input at each ramification pair is insufficient for this application.
For such a residual, correcting (6) removes its ENTIRE Cartan-VV component
on the positive null cone, not just its twelve values.

Restoring the full monad source U arrow introduces b_m B Q as well as
 a_m A Q. That companion generally has a different positive/negative
bidegree and must not be discarded. The spin-VV, mixed and WW equations,
the full fixed-duality comparison, and the remaining actual q division are
not solved by (7). No fourth Bockstein value follows from this theorem.
