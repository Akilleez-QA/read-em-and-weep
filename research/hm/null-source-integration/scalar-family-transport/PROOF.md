## 2. Scalar chain-map identity, uniformly

    L_(i,b)(X)=X13[i,b]-sum_(a=0..4,k=0..4) gamma[i,a,k] X02[a,5b+k].

Here gamma[i,a,k] are CONSTANT coefficients of gamma[i,a]=sum_k gamma[i,a,k] v_k. They are not the polynomial gamma[i,a]. Confusing the two inserts an erroneous extra v_k.

For the bottom End differential dW=(W ev,gamma W), ev[b,5b+k]=v_k. Substitution gives, term by term,

    L_(i,b)(dW)=sum_(a,k) gamma[i,a,k] W[a,b]v_k
               -sum_(a,k) gamma[i,a,k] W[a,b]v_k=0.

Extending L by zero in other internal degrees is therefore a sheaf chain map to the complex having F in internal degree -2 and zero elsewhere, where after deck-product removal F=O(-4,-4,-4,-4). Its total-degree-two image is in Cech degree4. All entries are first in the original frame; the degree -2 conversion is tau^-4. This identity holds for all30 maps and hence the selected20, and adds neither denominators nor frame changes.

## 7. Implementation obligations and exact generalization boundary

The following obligations suffice; each should be attached to the operator or source adapter, not presumed from a name/hash:

1. Exact P-model constant selector coefficients, row-transpose convention, full-sign/deck and original-frame identification, and requested L sign.
2. One coherent fresh source ordering and alternating extension. A historical nonlinear source evaluated in a different order needs a comparison witness. The physical F pullback vanishes on repeated physical labels; Rsharp and T generally only have common-cover alternation. Never give Rsharp the stronger physical repetition rule for free.

4. Exact numerator/domain witnesses for the whole regularized terms and their owned54/29/5251 factors; imported lemmas apply only with their stated input clearing identities and physical frames. Actual source adapters must realize those identities.
5. Fixed polynomial partitions, same r tags, regular extension, S/A/prism signs, full J0..J3 and top closure equation.
6. Formula(B)/(C), all signed Cech faces and retained prefix owners; derivative nodes only after certified cancellation, with full h branch predicates.
7. Finite coefficient convolution realization and static resource review before actual arithmetic. This proof leaves PR83 execution/value flags unchanged.

What generalizes: the regular-cover/domain theorem and scalar chain-map naturality apply to all30 maps, so in particular the20 selected maps, with one common domain and no old78 detector binding.

What does not generalize: the old special first-carrier merger, its v0^1/h_j^13/legal1 allocation, old directional zeros, source-specific four-tail cancellation,781 features, sparse selected kernels, or historical source comparison. The old identities I_0[10,:]=I_*[10,:]=0 and H0[0,:]=e_10^t/v0 prove one special row, not a family theorem. Classification of30 constant rows proves no such identities.

The domain theorem is therefore enough to specify a valid target for Codex's family compiler, but not to certify its current implementation or supply any of the32 values. The rational-sector completeness theorem is a separate premise; no conclusion about other sectors/characteristics follows here.
