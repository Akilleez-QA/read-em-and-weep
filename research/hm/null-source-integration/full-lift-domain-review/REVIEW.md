## 4. Whole-standard extension and the precise prism statement

For each actual owner v let

`D_v=H_v^54 product_i ell_vi^29 Z_(r(v),i)^5251`.

For an internal-r coefficient the product-cleared numerator has coordinate degree `sum_v(54d_v+29deg ell_vi+5251)+r-2`. The actual homogeneous pole theorem removes every possible divisorial pole, including those in infinity frames. Since `(P1)^4` is smooth and normal, that rational section extends regularly. This use of normality is valid only after all actual poles and homogeneous transitions have been accounted for; metadata alone cannot establish them.

For Pi_out every sampled owner carries a matching outer weight. Its carrier54/legal29 local quotients cancel all corresponding H/ell factors. Each retained Z has the sampled owner's designated standard tag and is already a unit on the target standard intersection. Therefore the whole weighted projection term extends there.

For H_rho, write a prism input as inserted owners B and unchanged suffix A. After multiplying by the inserted weights its denominator may still contain `product_(a in A) H_a^54 product_i ell_ai^29`, as well as Z units. Multiplying by the allowed **output suffix clearing factors** yields the full-standard regular extension. Thus H_rho returns a cochain in the permitted common-cover pole domain, not generally a standard-regular cochain before clearing. The outer prism takes no new derivatives; prefix owners remain attached throughout restrictions. This is precisely [regular-cover transport §6](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/10810ee877fdaf291399198ad60d7dd0c316a42d/research/hm/null-proof-foundations/hm-regular-cover-transport.md): inserted poles are cleared and original vertex denominators remain allowed.

Suggested clarification after JC §5's prism sentence:

> For an outer prism, only inserted carrier/legal factors cancel against weights. Original suffix factors remain in the allowed output common-cover domain; after their prescribed clearing, the whole prism coefficient extends to the standard target. All standard Z factors are already target units.

This qualification suffices for the D-stable regular comparison and its seventh-owner boundary. The smaller J2 allowance alone would not establish that full comparison.
