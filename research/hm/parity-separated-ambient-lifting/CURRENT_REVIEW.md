# Independent mathematical review of parity-separated ambient lifting

Verdict: PASS for the stated conditional ambient lifting theorem, explicit fourth scalar coefficient, and fixed-lower-map fifth-order exclusion. This is direct proof review, not formal checking or evaluation of a selected source instance.

The reversal anti-involution is distinct from geometric inversion. The norm tau(A)*A is itself fixed by tau. A leading odd defect therefore vanishes, whereas an even coefficient changes it by twice the added scalar. This proves the stated unique even normalization. Products of reversal-unitary elements remain unitary in a noncommutative algebra because tau reverses order; commutativity of the factors is not used. Their adjoints commute with tau, so have only even operator coefficients. A ratio of two such units cannot first differ in even degree.

At an even lifting step, lower multiplicativity plus associativity makes the leading map defect a Hochschild cocycle; parity makes it symmetric. Differential HKR on smooth affine intersections supplies a normalized differential primitive, and finite averaging preserves it. After unit normalization, both sides of the pentagon are unitary and agree below the even order, so equality follows directly. The remaining inner defect is a derivation. Its Cech closedness follows from triple composition; lower pentagon alone already suffices because the scalar adjoint begins one order later. Vanishing of invariant H2(T) supplies the derivation correction. That correction cannot alter the top pentagon since it annihilates the unit constant. No H3(O) vanishing is used in this even step.

At odd order the leading map cocycle is alternating. Combining its three cyclic Hochschild equations proves the two Leibniz rules, so it is an actual biderivation. A Hochschild differential of a linear operator is symmetric, making nonzero R impossible to repair by a new top map. Once R is zero and the odd map coefficient is fixed to zero, the inner equation follows from evenness of both operators. The remaining scalar class is precisely the pentagon obstruction for these fixed reversal-compatible maps, removed by delta e=P when invariant H3(O)=0. This statement does not assert that all potential changes of lower data have been exhausted.

The fourth-order norm is 2e+a^2-2bd-mu2(b,b), because a=b^2/2 and the mu1 cross terms vanish by the Poisson Leibniz rule. Substituting d=b^3/6+c gives e=b^4/24+bc+mu2(b,b)/2. This agrees with an independent expansion. It is explicit in the already supplied c, not a computation of c. It preserves literal lower data and differs from an arbitrary earlier solution by a scalar Cech2 cocycle; no unwarranted unique primitive is claimed.

The fifth homomorphism coefficient contains exactly Delta mu5, T mu3_j, the two negative mu3_i/T terms, L_U mu1 and -mu1(Tf,Tg). There are no mu2 or mu4 contributions because map degrees are even. Changing U by a derivation gives L_v mu1 and preserves the prior inner identity when delta v=0. The chartwise addition of a translation-invariant bivector to mu5 is a genuine associative truncated product: its Hochschild differential is zero and its higher interactions occur beyond degree five. This furnishes the asserted counterexample for fixed lower maps, while leaving possible lower-map or product changes outside the exclusion.

The result does not construct arbitrary odd local products, remove the odd gate, prove module liftability, evaluate the HM source, settle the six pure diagonal conditions, or establish algebraicity.

## Separate review of the odd-bivector repair addendum

PASS. The crucial noncircular step is valid: both sides of the inner identity are even linear operators once the lifted triple unit is reversal-unitary, independently of whether the even maps are already multiplicative in top odd degree. Since their lower coefficients agree, their odd leading difference is zero. Composition of leading multiplicativity defects adds, whereas composing with the genuine inner automorphism leaves that leading defect unchanged. Thus delta R=0 as a literal bivector-valued Cech cochain, not merely after HKR.

Adding a top biderivation changes the local associator only by its zero ordinary Hochschild differential, preserves odd reversal parity, and shifts the map defect by B_j-B_i. It does not alter lower data or top unit normalization. The necessary and sufficient condition for this precise product repair is therefore vanishing of [R] in invariant H1(wedge2 T). On an abelian variety geometric inversion acts as minus one on wedge2 V tensor W, so that invariant group vanishes. The remaining scalar pentagon is solved separately using invariant H3(O)=0. No local associative odd extension is supplied by this argument: its existence remains a hypothesis and the next construction problem. The addendum correctly distinguishes adjustable top products from immutable prescribed products.
