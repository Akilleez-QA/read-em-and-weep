# Full Koszul-homotopy operator

The actual full degree-three operator Theta=X(Id tensor h)delta has rank12 and image exactly the two T copies of F3. Its image on kerQ3 is a canonical T complement to I3.

On odd cohomology, j3 and Theta are independent square-zero operators whose pairwise products vanish. Their T multiplicity module has the regular three-dimensional type C[epsilon,eta]/(epsilon,eta)^2. The operator images are canonical; a cyclic generator and the resulting normal-form isomorphism are not.

The old terminal factorization through Q3 holds only on kerj3. It does not extend to the whole middle space. No global module endomorphism, actual source value or gate incidence is asserted.
