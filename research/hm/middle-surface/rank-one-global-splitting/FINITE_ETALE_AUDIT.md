# Independent audit: persistence under finite étale covers

Verdict: PASS. Reviewed FINITE_ETALE.md and independently opened the cited Stacks Project sections https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/04DY and https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BL6.

Theorem 59.45.2 and Remark 59.45.3 give the étale equivalence and affine compatibility for the nilpotent immersion. Finiteness can also be checked directly: over an affine chart of T the lifted cover is affine, and its algebra modulo the nilpotent base ideal is finite. Lift a finite set of module generators. Repeated substitution through the nilpotent ideal shows that the lifted algebra is generated by the same finite set. Hence the lift is finite as well as étale. Full faithfulness gives uniqueness with the specified reduction identification.

Flatness identifies the nilradical with p*N; its quotient is the reduced smooth scheme U'. On a locally split chart, the finite étale cover base-changes the split thickening. Formal étaleness uniquely extends the overlap derivations. The tangent isomorphism T_U'=p*T_U therefore identifies the resulting extension class with p*kappa. No nonexistent global map T→U is used.

Since U is connected, a nonempty finite étale cover has positive constant degree n and is surjective. The unit O_U→p_*O_U splits by trace divided by n, including for disconnected covers. Projection formula and affine cohomology then split the degree-one pullback map for the vector bundle T_U tensor N. Thus the nonzero kappa stays nonzero, excluding all global retractions and all split identifications of the lifted thickening, even with a reduction automorphism.

The conclusion is correctly restricted to finite étale covers. The trace argument alone would not handle ramification, because the tangent identification and extension-class pullback are also essential.  No outstanding correction was found.
