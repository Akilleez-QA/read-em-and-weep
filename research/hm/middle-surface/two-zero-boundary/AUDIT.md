# Independent audit: relative two-zero cone and its blowup

Verdict: PASS for the final PROOF.md Sections 1–6. All stated hypotheses and limits are retained.

1. The two permutation types expand separately to bA(p)-2aA(q)+f(p,q,u) and cA(p)+aA(q)+f(p,u,q). Outer symmetry alone exchanges the outer nonzero-input cases; it does not identify the two mixed bilinear terms. The coordinates are algebraically independent and all ten fiber variables vanish on the center. Thus the ideal is exactly homogeneous quadratic relative to the five-dimensional base. Standard grading gives K^n as the sum of degrees at least n and gr_K A=A, proving the exact relative normal cone. This does not prove a product description of the full closed-point tangent cone when the base is also filtered.

2. Independently checked the blowup charts with possible zero divisors and irrelevant torsion. Localizing at t_i first gives the strongly graded ring B_i[r,r^-1]. The degree-zero Rees chart image is exactly B_i[r]. Any t_i-torsion has already been killed; no division inside the original ring is assumed. The dehomogenized ambient ideal is independent of r, so saturation of r²(F) by r is exactly (F), even when its quotient is nonreduced. Thus the intrinsic blowup and ambient strict transform agree and are the total tautological line bundle over Proj A. The free polynomial line variable makes the exceptional divisor Cartier with its full scheme structure.

3. Each normalized projective chart is truly the independent-coordinate slice t=1 of the earlier one-zero normal form. Checked the changes v=u+p or v=u+q in the nonzero-outer-input case and the required outer swap in the nonzero-middle-input case. The induced four residual coordinates may differ from the initial p,q, as the proof states. The old determinant inverses and rank-ten Nakayama argument remain valid near the chosen rank-one direction. The new exceptional variable r is independent of all equations.

4. The rank-one directions form the P1-bundle of the two scalar coefficients. Their endpoints lie in disjoint projectivized complementary coordinate subspaces. The endpoint model eP² and interior model P² imply exactly the stated components near this P1-bundle, multiplicities one/five, and a square-zero nilradical locally free of rank four over the reduced rank-one component. No other component meets those directions, but this does not exclude components elsewhere in the projective cone. The reduced rank-one strict transform is its ordinary blowup along the zero section.

5. The added depth and tangent conclusions check. At closed points over the rank-one directions, the strict transform has tangent dimension eleven; interior dimension/depth seven; endpoint dimension ten and depth seven. The exceptional divisor removes the independent regular parameter r, lowering all corresponding dimensions/depths by one and tangent dimension to ten. Thus this blowup does not resolve the displayed singularities. Its clean local decomposition cannot exclude center-supported embedded structure in the original germ.

The theorem gives an exact relative cone and a controlled neighborhood of its rank-one exceptional directions. It does not classify the entire two-zero germ, impose flatness over the base, compute the full closed-point tangent cone, or handle the all-zero vertex.  No outstanding correction was found.
