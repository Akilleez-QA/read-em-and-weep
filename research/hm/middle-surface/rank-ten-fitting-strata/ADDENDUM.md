# A finite degree list for the rank-ten singularity cycle

Retain the conditional rank-ten hypotheses of PROOF.md, and the established normalizer equivariance and irreducibility of the twenty-dimensional representation J. Let

    S=Ext^1(R,O),    D=R**/R,
    [S]_2=delta h^2,

where [S]_2 means its two-dimensional support cycle, and is zero when its support has dimension at most one. Then

    delta belongs to {0,5,10,15,20,25,30,35,40,45,50,55,60,65,70},
    c2(R**)=75-delta belongs to {75,70,65,...,5}.

Moreover delta=0 if and only if R is reflexive. Thus any nonreflexive rank-ten quotient has a codimension-two singularity cycle of degree at least five and at most seventy. Reflexivity does not imply local freeness here: codimension-three or codimension-four rank drops remain possible.

## Proof

The codimension-two local-duality calculation in PROOF.md gives

    [D]_2=[S]_2=delta h^2,

with delta>=0. Since R and R** agree in codimension one, c1(R**)=c1(R)=12h. Taking the degree-two Chern character in 0 -> R -> R** -> D -> 0 gives

    c2(R**)=c2(R)-delta h^2=(75-delta)h^2.

The reflexive sheaf R** is generated by the sections coming from the globally generated R outside a subset of codimension at least two. The positivity lemma in `/research/hm/local-defect/rank-nine-defect-two-sided/PROOF.md`, Section 1, therefore gives c2(R**)>=0.

Equality is impossible. By that proof's reflexive zero-c2 splitting lemma, equality would force

    R** = O^5 direct-sum O(12).

Duals of torsion-free sheaves agree with the duals of their reflexive hulls, so this would give

    R* = O^5 direct-sum O(-12),
    dim H^0(R*)=5.

The dual of the quotient J tensor O -> R is the equivariant inclusion R* -> J* tensor O. Taking global sections embeds H^0(R*) as a five-dimensional invariant subspace of the irreducible twenty-dimensional representation J*, which is impossible. Hence c2(R**) is a strictly positive integer, and

    0<=delta<=74.

The map defining R is equivariant, and the functorial induced action on S=Ext^1(R,O) makes S an H5-equivariant coherent sheaf. Its support is Z, whose dimension is at most two. Apply the proper-support Hilbert congruence theorem of `/research/hm/local-defect/heisenberg-defect-hilbert-congruence/PROOF.md`. If S has dimension two, its top-dimensional cycle has degree divisible by five. If S has smaller dimension, delta=0. Therefore in all cases

    5 divides delta.

Combining this with the interval above proves the stated finite list.

Finally, an effective two-dimensional cycle on P^4 has degree zero exactly when it is zero. Thus delta=0 exactly when S has no codimension-two support. PROOF.md shows this is equivalent to R being reflexive, by its projective-dimension-one presentation and the depth criterion for reflexivity. In this case D=0 entirely, even though S itself can remain nonzero on a curve or a finite set.

## Scope

This is a necessary numerical condition under generic rank ten. It does not establish the generic rank, identify a singularity component, or rule out its remaining finite degree possibilities. The argument uses only the established exact sequences, equivariance, Hilbert congruences, and positivity and splitting lemmas.
