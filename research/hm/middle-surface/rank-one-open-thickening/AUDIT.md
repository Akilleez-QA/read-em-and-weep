# Independent audit: rank-one open square-zero thickening

Verdict: PASS for PROOF.md, including the separately checked converse when stated with local reduction equal to D.

1. The exact rational elimination is essential and valid on a Zariski neighborhood of every all-nonzero rank-one point: the four repeated-input equations have invertible coefficient 1+LB along p=0. The sixteen residual generators belong to P² and have quadratic classes -pi F. Full rank ten means these classes generate P²/P³. Applying Nakayama first on D if needed and then to the finite module P²/J gives J=P² in the actual local ring; coherence spreads equality to a neighborhood. No completion or flatness premise is needed.

2. The full ideal is the four eliminated equations plus the squared residual ideal, not the square of the rank-eight ambient conormal ideal. The local equalities imply I_D²⊂I_X, so the nilradical is canonically the cokernel of the first derivative map on the ambient conormal bundle. Its rank is four, and its square vanishes. The neighborhoods may be combined after removing D\U to obtain W∩D=U; the reduced scheme there is exactly U. A distinct irreducible component meeting U would have an open piece contained in D and hence itself be contained in D, which is impossible.

3. The inverse-image Fitting description agrees with rank-ten-fitting-strata/PROOF.md: the transpose of the residual rank-ten-to-sixteen presentation presents the relevant Ext1 sheaf, so its Fitt0 is the maximal-minor rank-drop scheme. The intersection assertion is restricted to R. Boundary triples with a zero input are correctly excluded from the automatic conclusion.

4. Explicit base coordinates yield local algebraic retractions and a free rank-five algebra O_U plus its rank-four square-zero module. The proof does not infer a global retraction from a quotient algebra sheaf. For smooth U, local splittings differ by derivations into the nilradical; their obstruction and torsor lie in the stated H1 and H0 groups. The general extension criterion only gives local square-zero structure, not a Zariski ambient tubular neighborhood; its formal-smoothness argument for splitting the abstract thickening is valid.

5. The local split algebra is free over the regular base. Base parameters form a regular sequence, its Artinian reduction has socle dimension four, and it is therefore Cohen–Macaulay of type four, non-Gorenstein and non-lci. Ten residual quadratic generators together with the four independent linear-normal equations give fourteen minimal ambient generators, height eight and closed-point tangent dimension eleven. Generic length five gives the component cycle 5[U].

6. The converse characterization also holds precisely as proposed: if, near a point of R, the reduced local scheme equals D and its nilradical is square-zero, then in the exact eliminated presentation that nilradical is P/J. Its square-zero property forces P²⊂J; the prior J⊂P² gives equality and hence full quadratic rank. No statement of this converse omitting the local-reduction hypothesis is justified or needed.

 The full-rank locus is characterized structurally, not enumerated. No outstanding mathematical correction was found.
