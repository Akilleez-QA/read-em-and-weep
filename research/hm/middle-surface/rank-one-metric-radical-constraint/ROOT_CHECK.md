# Root verification of metric extension and radical identity

19 October 2026.

The intrinsic map to J/A_H(E) is well-defined: changing outer lifts changes the numerator by supported repeated-image terms. Generic rank one forces all second minors to vanish on the integral base. Rank zero at a point would provide a quadratic self-map of a three-dimensional vector space with no eigenline, contrary to c2(Q(2))=7. A locally unit entry then puts the map in constant-rank-one normal form, giving its line image and kernel subbundle even over a singular integral base. Thus no metric graph blowup is needed after H is regular. This does not eliminate complement indeterminacy.

Independently checked the ruled-surface Chern calculation for the radical R: with the lines convention, ξ²+c1(R)ξ=0; the rank-two section has c2=(3e+5u)ξ+7ξ². Its nonvanishing gives 3e+5u−7r=0 in Pic(T). Substituting determinant relations gives (det P*)^7=(U*)^9 N^3, hence 7p=9a+3n. The value-line degree m=2(p−n) and determinant-line degree 7 degΔ=40a−10n follow. No positivity of the discriminant restricted to its own component is presumed.

The extension theorem allows generically rank-one or rank-two metrics. An effective determinant divisor is asserted only on the generically nondegenerate branch. Support extension is an imported hypothesis, not proved again here. The rank-one curve identity is a necessary condition, not an exclusion of all moving ternary-support surfaces.
