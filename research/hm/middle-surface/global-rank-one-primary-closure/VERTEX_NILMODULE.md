# Nilmodule degeneration at the original vertex

Use the independently reviewed global primary ideal Q and rank-one determinantal prime P in R=C[V^3], dim V=5. Put C=R/P and N=P/Q, the square-zero nilradical of R/Q.

N is a torsion-free C-module of generic rank four. Indeed its square is zero, so the action factors through C. If a nonzero class c in C annihilated a nonzero element of N, any lift of c outside P would be a zero divisor in the P-primary ring R/Q, a contradiction. Generic rank four is the established length-five transverse calculation.

At the original vertex, N requires exactly thirty generators. The prime P is generated by the thirty 2-by-2 minors of a 5-by-3 matrix, hence their images generate N. The global theorem gives Q_2=0. These thirty minors are linearly independent in degree two, and N has no pieces of degree below two. Thus (R_+N)_2=0 and dim_C N/R_+N=30: all generators lie in degree two, so no higher-degree quotient classes remain. Localizing at the homogeneous vertex gives the same minimal generator number by Nakayama.

Consequently the generic rank-four nilmodule is not locally free at the vertex; its fiber there has dimension thirty. On every nonzero two-zero-input stratum its minimal generator number is eight, by the separate exact ideal-module model. Away from those strata and the vertex, on the primary component, it is locally free of rank four. This last assertion uses the explicit nonzero-column chart, in which the nilmodule is (b,c)^4 and one of b,c is a unit whenever at least two inputs are nonzero. It does not require residual rank ten at that boundary line, because it concerns the already identified global primary closure, not the whole cubic zero scheme.

No vertex depth, full primary decomposition of the cubic ideal, or absence of embedded components of the full zero scheme is inferred. All statements use homogeneous universal polynomial identities and the proved primaryness; no selected operation values are computed.
