## 3. Actual differential generation excludes the O(-16) form

## 4. The top D(-2) has a genuine full seven-dimensional constant span

This target has top degree -5, whereas U3 has pure degree -3. The ordinary Hom is zero. Therefore U3 is contained in G_D^-. Conversely G_D^- is contained in G_D intersect E0^-=U3. Hence

    G_D^-=U3.                                     (10)

Continue actual derivative kernels. Since U3=G_D^-, its second fundamental form lands in

    (G_D/U3)(-2)=O.

Applying the same reasoning successively gives the actual flag

    G_D^-=S4(-3),

    next kernel=W(-5),
    next kernel=O(-6),

## 6. Both O(-18) forms force an impossible degree-minus-one subbundle
