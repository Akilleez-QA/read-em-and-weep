# The determinantal resolution and its forced sextic syzygies

Work over the complex numbers. Put S=Sym(T), T=V*, dim V=5, D=Fitt_0(H), Q=S/D and R=S/Ann(H). All claims below are conditional on the pinned module and representation identifications in PR498/501, the scalar resolution of PR500 (including PR499), and the C purity proved in PR502 for the final refinement. No source-instance evaluation is used.

The final result is the exhaustive free-module family displayed in section 3, with k in {15,25,35,45}. In particular the penultimate module is exactly 110S(-8), and at least fifteen degree-six third syzygies are forced. No particular value of k is selected.

## 1. An exterior-contraction exclusion

PR498 identifies Z=Ann(H)/D with C(-3) tensor W', where dim W'=2, C_0=A, C_1=V tensor W_0*, and C vanishes in other degrees. Here dim A=5, dim W_0=2 and p:A tensor W_0 -> exterior^2 V is an isomorphism. The multiplication is, up to a nonzero scalar,

mu(t,a)(w)=i_t p(a tensor w).

Let a_4=beta_(4,4)(C). Since C has no negative graded pieces, this is the kernel dimension of its linear Koszul differential

d_4: exterior^4 T tensor A -> exterior^3 T tensor V tensor W_0*.

Tensor this map with W_0 and contract W_0* against W_0. After using p on the source, the composite is the universal map

Delta: exterior^4 T tensor exterior^2 V -> exterior^3 T tensor V,
Delta((t_1 wedge ... wedge t_4) tensor beta)
 =sum_i (-1)^(i-1)(t_1 wedge ... omit t_i ... wedge t_4) tensor i_(t_i) beta.

This map is invertible. To see this without a matrix, choose a volume form identifying exterior^4 T with V and exterior^3 T with exterior^2 V. Up to an overall sign, Delta becomes

v tensor (w wedge z) -> (v wedge w) tensor z - (v wedge z) tensor w.

After interchanging the output tensor factors, this is J=h Alt-id, where

Alt(v tensor(w wedge z))=v wedge w wedge z,
h(v wedge w wedge z)=v tensor(w wedge z)-w tensor(v wedge z)+z tensor(v wedge w).

Since Alt h=3id, the inverse of J is (1/2)h Alt-id. Thus Delta is invertible. An element of ker(d_4) tensored with any w lies in ker(Delta), so ker(d_4)=0. Consequently

beta_(4,4)(C)=0.

This is a universal exterior-tensor identity. In particular it does not assume that an equivariant multiplicity-one map is nonzero merely because it exists.

## 2. The remaining C terms

C has regularity one, is generated in degree zero, and has socle dimension ten in degree one. Put b=beta_(2,2)(C)=beta_(1,2)(C), and d=beta_(3,3)(C). Its Hilbert numerator is

5-15t+50t^3-75t^4+45t^5-10t^6.

Using section 1 gives the exhaustive possible free modules

F_0(C)=5S,
F_1(C)=15S(-1) plus bS(-2),
F_2(C)=bS(-2) plus (50+d)S(-3),
F_3(C)=dS(-3) plus 75S(-4),
F_4(C)=45S(-5),
F_5(C)=10S(-6).

PR501 proves b is either 0 or 24. Section 1 does not select b or d.

## 3. The determinantal resolution

Use the exact sequence 0 -> Z -> Q -> R -> 0. PR497 proves D is generated by exactly 35 quartics. PR500 gives

F_1(R)=10S(-3) plus 15S(-4),
F_2(R)=10S(-4) plus 52S(-5),
F_3(R)=45S(-6) plus 10S(-7),
F_4(R)=20S(-8),
F_5(R)=3S(-10).

The Tor long exact sequence, degree by degree, determines every possible Betti entry of Q except a single numerical parameter. Let rho be the rank of the connecting map

Tor_3(R,C)_6 -> Tor_2(Z,C)_6.

Here the second C in Tor denotes the residue field of S, not the module C above; equivalently write Tor^S_i(-,S/m). The source has dimension45, so 0 <= rho <=45. Put k=45+2d-rho. Then

F_0(Q)=S,
F_1(Q)=35S(-4),
F_2(Q)=52S(-5) plus (55+k)S(-6),
F_3(Q)=kS(-6) plus 160S(-7),
F_4(Q)=110S(-8),
F_5(Q)=20S(-9) plus 3S(-10).

In particular k >=2d and k <=45+2d. No other shifts occur.

For completeness, the decisive degree pieces are as follows. In degree five, Tor_2(Z)_5 has dimension2b and injects into Tor_2(Q)_5. The map Tor_2(R)_5 -> Tor_1(Z)_5 is onto because Q has no quintic generators. Thus beta_(2,5)(Q)=2b+52-2b=52. In degree six the same long exact sequence gives beta_(3,6)(Q)=2d+45-rho and beta_(2,6)(Q)=100+2d-rho. In degree seven, Tor_3(Z)_7 of dimension150 injects into Tor_3(Q)_7, whose quotient is Tor_3(R)_7 of dimension10. Section 1 makes Tor_4(Z)_7 zero, hence beta_(4,7)(Q)=0. In degree eight, Tor_4(Z)_8 of dimension90 injects into Tor_4(Q)_8 with quotient Tor_4(R)_8 of dimension20. Finally the degree-nine and degree-ten terms agree with the socle of Q established in PR495. All remaining degrees vanish by the same exact sequence and the displayed supports.

The pure penultimate term 110S(-8), the exact 160 generators in homological degree three and internal degree seven, and the exhaustive one-parameter family are new structural conclusions. This does not yet determine k or the full maps of the resolution.

## 4. Forced channel after the pure C theorem

This section uses PR502's additional proof of b=0; it does not follow from PR501 alone. Then d=0 by minimality, as in PR501, and the degree-three Koszul complex is exact on its left. Its representation difference gives

Tor_2(C)_3 = [exterior^2 T tensor V tensor W_0] - [exterior^3 T tensor A]
 = T tensor (U' plus W_0 plus J_5).

Indeed exterior^2 T=E tensor W_0, exterior^3 T=A tensor W_0, V tensor E=T tensor(U' plus W_0), and A tensor A=T tensor(U plus W'). Using W_0 tensor W_0=1 plus U, U tensor U'=T_4 plus J_5, U tensor W_0=W_0 plus Qspin, and W_0 tensor W'=T_4, the first expression expands as T tensor(U' plus 2W_0 plus T_4 plus J_5 plus Qspin), and the subtracted expression is T tensor(W_0 plus Qspin plus T_4). The stated difference follows.

Consequently Tor_2(Z)_6=T tensor[(U' plus W_0 plus J_5) tensor W']. PR492 identifies Tor_2(N)_4=V tensor(U plus W' plus Qspin), and the duality used there identifies

Tor_3(R)_6=T tensor(U plus W' plus Qspin).

The target of the connecting map contains no T tensor U. To see this it is unnecessary to decompose J_5 tensor W': the quotient central element -I acts evenly on U', J_5 and U, and oddly on W_0 and W'. Hence the sole even constituent in (U' plus W_0 plus J_5) tensor W' is W_0 tensor W'=T_4, an irreducible four-dimensional representation inequivalent to U. The T tensor U source channel therefore maps to zero.

The other two source channels T tensor W' and T tensor Qspin are inequivalent irreducibles of dimensions10 and20. On each the kernel is either zero or the entire channel. Thus

rho is in {0,10,20,30}, and k is in {15,25,35,45}.

In particular beta_(3,6)(S/D) is at least15: a sextic third-syzygy contribution is forced, unlike the corresponding excluded ghost for the scalar algebra. This is an equivariant exclusion, not an evaluation of either remaining map. The proof uses the normalizer conventions and irreducibility recorded in PR489/492; PR492's source formula is essential.
