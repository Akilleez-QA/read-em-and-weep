# Public inputs and dependency map

1. **Twist-one vanishing.** [PR #91](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/pull/91), pinned to [commit ee7c4d4627c2efa07ea167a535cf1582002ef335](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/commit/ee7c4d4627c2efa07ea167a535cf1582002ef335), supplies H⁰End₀(F)(1)=0. The result remains a review dependency, not an assertion of upstream acceptance. The public sibling [proof](../twist-one-vanishing/PROOF.md) is available on the stacked base.

2. **Section resolution and cohomology-module shifts.** Wolfram Decker, *Monads and cohomology modules of rank 2 vector bundles*, Compositio Mathematica 76 (1990), 7–17, printed pp.14–16. [Primary PDF](https://www.numdam.org/article/CM_1990__76_1-2_7_0.pdf). The section-module resolution is reconstructed by comparing the section-resolution notation on p.14, its relationship with the H¹-module resolution on p.15, and the explicit shifts on p.16. The full sheafified resolution is reproduced in PROOF.md, not imported through an unpublished regularity claim.

3. **HM bundle, cohomology, and finite symmetry.** G. Horrocks and D. Mumford, *A rank 2 vector bundle on P⁴ with 15,000 symmetries*, Topology 12 (1973), 63–81. [Primary PDF](https://www.dam.brown.edu/people/mumford/alg_geom/papers/1973a--Rk2BdleP%5E4-NC.pdf). Use the finite normalizer and Heisenberg representations in §2, especially the nontrivial-central-character isotypic equivalence (2.2); the invariant quintics in Theorem 3.5; and the cohomology calculations in §4. Their bundle is F(3) in the normalization here. No refined label for H⁰End₀(F)(2) is required.

4. **Bogomolov inequality in characteristic zero.** Adrian Langer, *On boundedness of semistable sheaves*, Documenta Mathematica 27 (2022), 1–16, Theorem 3.13, printed p.11. [Primary PDF](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/26645). Apply to the H-semistable torsion-free rank-two kernel normalization with all polarizations equal to H.

5. **Reflexive equality case and flatness.** Wenhao Ou, *Foliations whose first Chern class is nef*, DOI 10.4171/JEMS/1426, Theorem 3.1 (PDF p.5) and Theorem 3.5 (PDF p.7). [Primary PDF](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/34043?nt=1). Theorem 3.1 concludes that a semistable reflexive sheaf with the indicated three Chern-number vanishings is a numerically flat vector bundle. Theorem 3.5 gives a compatible flat connection. Reflexivity, rather than mere torsion-freeness, is essential and is verified in PROOF.md.

The other ingredients are the standard trace splitting in characteristic zero, Serre duality on P⁴, the cohomology spectral sequence of a locally free resolution, the depth criterion for reflexivity on a smooth variety, Harder–Narasimhan uniqueness, and the effective leading support cycle of a torsion sheaf. Their actual uses and numerical signs are written out in the proof.
