# Independent check: undifferentiated scalar terms

Let p and q denote complementary pairs. Write x_p=s_p I+k_p, with k_p traceless, delta s_p=F_p=b_p-c_p, c_p=tr(C_p)/2 and C_p^tf=-delta k_p. On an increasing triple, put A=k_p,ij, B=Ad(g_ij^-1)k_p,jk, D=k_p,ik, a=s_q,ij, b=s_q,jk, d=s_q,ik.

The complete undifferentiated q-scalar/p-traceless contribution, INCLUDING the gerbe term, is

U=b A+a B+(a+b)(D-A-B)-b_q D
 =-b B-a A+(a+b-b_q)D
 =-delta(s_q k_p)+(F_q-b_q)D
 =-delta(s_q k_p)-c_q D.

If instead computing the DIFFERENCE M(s)-M(0), the -b_q D term is already present in M(0) and cancels out of that difference. Then the corresponding formula has +F_q D. These are consistent statements with different baselines; do not mix them.

This is formal matrix/cochain algebra, with no physical polynomial evaluation and no claim about the final detector value. Complementary derivatives of s still remain. The proposed removal of the central part requires closedness of the full residual, trace splitting, and vanishing of full-sign scalar H^2; it cannot be justified term by term by centrality alone.
