# Proof-note index and dependency scope

This collection contains twenty-one mathematical proof and audit notes. It preserves the distinctions between a formal identity, a full-domain existence theorem, a conditional compression, an abstract counterexample, and an evaluated geometric obstruction. None of these notes supplies the complete actual obstruction value.

## Reading order and selected inventory

### 1. Corrected scalar elimination and exact adjoint/domain rules

- `direct-scalar-free-pairing-reduction.md`: complete baseline/gerbe convention, the closed trace-free representative Rsharp, and its linear adjoint pairing.
- `scalar-reduction-root-check.md`: independent check distinguishing the full expression from the difference M(s)-M(0).
- `hm-scalar-pairing-elimination-audit.md`: boundary identity, central splitting, and the derivative terms that remain.
- `transposed-scalar-demand-grammar.md`: chart/exponent filters, signs, finite-cover tuple rules, and rational-pending demands.
- `scalar-homotopy-adjoint-controls.md`: independent formal coefficient-map and derivative controls.
- `scalar-cover-and-domain-audit.md`: ordered-cover checks, owned pole ledger, and homogeneous domain bounds.
- `refined-tracefree-pole-domain.md`: complete outer comparison ledger and section 6 homogeneous numerator/Laurent envelope.

The adjoint grammar is an earlier stage of the construction. Its statements about an outer domain still to be supplied must be read with the later domain audit and finite-domain certificate. Finite branching or a pole envelope does not establish occupied support, implementation cost, or an actual detector value.

### 2. Direct dual filling and the full finite domain

- `direct-dual-scalar-fill-contract.md`: graded cup identities, kernel-annihilation condition, and the exact pivot-minor dual formula.
- `direct-dual-scalar-fill-independent-audit.md`: sign and domain audit of that contract.
- `owned-denominator-finite-domain-certificate.md`: acyclic sheaf Koszul model with full homogeneous numerator spaces; bounded total primitive proves detector boundary annihilation.
- `owned-denominator-finite-domain-independent-audit.md`: independent check of the cover, exactness, ownership, and primitive comparison.

The first two notes predate the last two. The later theorem closes the finite-H1 and primitive-domain gaps for its stated full model. Its conclusion is proof-level existence. Physical coefficient rows, an instantiated pivot or factored inverse, and an exact physical filling residual remain absent. The omitted-column counterexample explains why arbitrary clipping does not inherit the theorem.

### 3. Conditional tensor-jet compression

- `jet-quotient-direct-dual-certificate.md`: exact quotient chain map, finite-algebra Koszul contraction, and dual filling under explicit factorization and descent hypotheses.
- `jet-dual-compression-independent-audit.md`: frame, surjectivity, factorization, and adjoint-sign checks.

The actual detector must factor through the chosen quotient. Full-domain exactness, a pole envelope, or a successful small example does not supply that factorization. Quotient chain maps are not asserted to preserve the full model's cohomology.

### 4. Full 4038-to-78 selector pruning

- `source-independent-selector-pruning.md`: same-owner split identities, graded/terminal pruning, exact counts, and conditional prefix-mask merging.
- `root-selector-pruning-audit.md`: independent formal path/sign audit.
The support-certificate companion supplies [refined-prefix-pruning-audit.md](../null-support-certificates/refined-prefix-pruning-audit.md), the common-frame carrier-prefix proof and obstruction to an unproved four-tail merger.

All retained owners, weights, standard tags, masks, and tail labels remain part of the expression. The counts are formal path counts, not physical polynomial-product counts. Repeated tail carriers are not automatically zero.

### 5. Quartic remainder and second-jet limitations

- `m0-baseline-independent-audit.md`: the complete gerbe-corrected baseline, selected trace-free entries, and formal controls.
- `m0-current-selected-kernels.md`: complete current-coordinate baseline and the preferred Q cancellation used by the quartic decomposition.
- `rsharp-quartic-current-remainder.md`: exact quartic-current decomposition and all fourteen mixed assignment sectors, with H/J and E(TF) kept opaque.
- `m0-second-jet-survivor.md`: trace reduction and the universal transition-local two-jet boundary no-go argument.
- `second-jet-no-go-independent-audit.md`: independent factor, sign, and theorem-scope audit.
The support-certificate companion supplies [source-second-jet-degeneracy-certificate.md](../null-support-certificates/source-second-jet-degeneracy-certificate.md): the exact three-equation cross-Gram criterion, sufficient conditions, and abstract failures of common shortcuts.

These are formal identities and universal counterexamples. They do not prove nonvanishing for the pinned source, survival under the complete detector, or nonvanishing of the complete Rsharp obstruction. The source-second-jet control is intentionally repeated from the support-certificate companion for a self-contained replay. Its note remains in that companion; the repeated control is shared provenance, not independent evidence.

### 6. Inference failure analysis

- `obstruction-inference-fmea.md`: failure modes from replacing complete by selected, physical by formal, cohomological by local, full domain by clipping, or actual support by an envelope.

Its combined historical regression refers to tests outside this focused collection. Use this collection's README and `run_checks.py` for the exact local replay scope; do not treat the historical command as an additional completed replay of absent tests.
