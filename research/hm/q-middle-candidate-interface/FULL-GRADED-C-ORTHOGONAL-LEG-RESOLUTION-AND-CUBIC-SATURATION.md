# Full graded C-orthogonal legs and saturated cubic lower middles

## Result and boundaries

Over the actual localized marking ring, the entire C-orthogonal mixed-leg
module has a constructive Koszul resolution, not merely a degree-one
calculation. After the actual C and A source legs are removed, it has the
exact presentation

    0 -> A tensor S(-2) -> D5 tensor S(-1) -> Q -> 0.       (1)

The ten exterior-cube coefficient polynomials of its relation map generate
exactly the ideal (x,y,z)^3. Thus the projective quotient is locally free;
this is a polynomial ideal certificate, not an evaluated rank test.

There is a real symmetric-kernel saturation defect in degree one. It is
annihilated by (x,y,z), and does not survive in degree three. Source
quotienting introduces no missing degree-three global section either.
Consequently every **exactly C-annihilating mixed-mixed positive-cubic
lower middle**, modulo its A/C source terms, is generated by positive-linear
coefficients on pairs of the five previously constructed D5 legs. This is
proved constructively and with global-section checks, not inferred from
the degree-one leg dimension.

This does NOT prove that all lower middles satisfying only a q0-divisible
C equation have this form, nor that the complete source problem is covered.
It does NOT identify different full-U completions of the same lower middle.
In fact the three polynomial relations in (1) have explicit non-source
full-U remainders. Those remainders are retained below and are **not claimed
to be independent of the already retained eighteen completed columns**.
Actual exterior presentation relations can further identify lower classes;
no independence of actual middle operators is inferred from (1).

## 1. Ring, physical pairing, and constant splitting

Let

    R = Q[FB^±1, FC^±1, FD^±1],  S=R[x,y,z],  m=(x,y,z).

All other physical scales, including am,bm,cm, remain formal nonzero units
when used. Work in the fixed determinant frame of
`universal_physical_mixed_leg_foundation.py`. For coefficient blocks
c0,c1 in U tensor V, let m(c0,c1) be the complete contracted VVW form,
including both its VV and VW components. The fixed inverse determinant,
not a new scalar pairing normalization, is used throughout.

Put G=Gmix=P4+A+D5. The pairing with the three actual marked C columns is

    lambda(v):G tensor S -> A tensor S(1),
    lambda(v)y = (id_A tensor [x y z]) D y,                (2)

where D:G->A tensor V is a constant R-linear map. Its entries are obtained
by taking all coefficients of x,y,z in the literal exterior pairing.
The constructor solves against nine independent formal RHS coefficients
using Laurent-unit pivots, and exports physical maps

    C:R^3->G,  Zeta:G->R^3,  Rsplit:R^9->G

satisfying the complete identities

    D C=0,  Zeta C=I,  D Rsplit=I,  Zeta Rsplit=0,
    Rsplit D+C Zeta=I.                                   (3)

Thus the constant map is onto with **exactly** the C-source kernel, over
R itself. No claim rests on a field dimension or a generic specialization.
The literal all-scale matrices are reconstructed and checked by
`graded_module()`; its C projection is the same marked Cartan source
projection as in the audited degree-one construction.

## 2. Complete graded kernel and an all-degree right inverse

Set N=ker([x y z]:S^3->S(1)). Use the Koszul maps

    d2 = [ -y -z  0 ]       d3 = [ z ]
         [  x  0 -z ]            [-y ]
         [  0  x  y ],           [ x ].                 (4)

Equation (3) gives, literally,

    K=ker lambda = C tensor S direct-sum Rsplit(A tensor N).

It follows that

    0 -> A tensor S(-2)
      -> (Csource tensor S) + (A tensor Lambda²V tensor S(-1))
      -> K -> 0,                                        (5)

where the first map has zero C component and is id_A tensor d3.
This is exact in all degrees. Here is a constructive proof avoiding any
additional elimination. If k=(k0,k1,k2) is homogeneous of degree d and
x k0+y k1+z k2=0, define

    h_ij=(partial_i k_j-partial_j k_i)/(d+1).

Then d2 h=k. For h homogeneous of degree e with d2 h=0,

    t=(partial_x h12-partial_y h02+partial_z h01)/(e+2)

satisfies d3 t=h. These identities follow from the Koszul/de Rham
anticommutator i_v d+d i_v=(polynomial degree+exterior degree)id.
They hold over R because every positive integer is invertible.
The verifier checks the full identities, including the off-kernel
correction, on complete formal monomial spans; the displayed
anticommutator is the proof for arbitrary degree.

For a C-orthogonal y, first set c=Zeta y and k=D y. Apply the preceding
homotopy independently to the three A blocks. Then

    y=C c+Rsplit (id_A tensor d2) h.                      (6)

This is the requested all-degree constructive right inverse.

## 3. Literal source quotient and its three physical relations

Let Acoeff be the twelve-by-four matrix whose column (r,a) is v tensor e_a
in W block r and zero in the other block. Then

    m(Acoeff e_(r,a))=A(w_r e_a),  (w0,w1)=(u,w).

Let Y be the twelve-by-five matrix of the normalized five physical legs
from the audited degree-one constructor, before the optional quartic-label
rescaling. Both lie in K. Apply the homotopy in section 2 to D Acoeff and
D Y, and concatenate the constant results into P. The verifier proves

    det P=2 FB^4 FC^3 FD^2/9,
    Rsplit d2 P=[Acoeff-C Zeta Acoeff, Y].               (7)

The determinant is a Laurent unit. This is a verified coordinate
isomorphism, not an instance-rank argument. Put

    P^-1 (id_A tensor d3) = [H; Rel],

with H having four rows and Rel five. Then

    Y Rel=-Acoeff H+C Zeta Acoeff H.                     (8)

The complete relation matrix, in the existing normalized Y order, is

    Rel = [ z/FB          0                    -3x/FD ]
          [ x/(6FB)      -FC y/(2FB FD)        0      ]
          [ 0            -z/FC                 0      ]
          [-y/FB          0                     3z/FD ]
          [ 0            -3x/FC                -9y/FD ].

There are no other relations modulo sources. Indeed every relation lifts
through the isomorphism P to a Koszul relation; conversely (8) supplies
all three. The map Rel is injective as an S-module map: if Rel t=0, (8)
says an Acoeff combination is a C combination. C has zero Clifford spin,
whereas the A combination has M(v) times its source spinor in each W
block. Since M(v)^2=(xy+z²)I and S is a domain, that source combination
is zero. Koszul exactness then gives t=0. This proves (1).

For arbitrary homogeneous y in K, `reconstruct_leg(y,d)` returns explicit
coefficients c,a,b with

    y=C c+Acoeff a+Y b.

In particular it records the C coefficient adjustment caused by projecting
Acoeff, rather than silently forgetting that source shift.

## 4. Polynomial subbundle certificate

Let M_I denote the exterior cube of Rel in its three rows I. Multiply the
ten M_I by the Laurent units in `fitting_certificate()`. In lexicographic
row-triple order the resulting polynomials are

    n012=x²z,                n013=xy²-yz²,
    n014=FB FD x³+3FC²y²z,  n023=xyz-z³,
    n024=yz²,                n034=x²y-xz²,
    n123=xz²,                n124=xyz,
    n134=FB FD x²z+3FC²y³,  n234=y²z.

These generate every cubic, by the checked triangular identities

    x³=(n014-3FC²n234)/(FB FD),
    x²y=n034+n123,    xy²=n013+n024,
    y³=(n134-FB FD n012)/(3FC²),    z³=n124-n023,

and the five immediately displayed remaining monomials. Conversely every
M_I is cubic. Hence the Fitting ideal is exactly m³. On Proj(S), Rel is a
subbundle inclusion and Q~ is a rank-two vector bundle. Equivalently, the
projectivized P4 source inclusion in A tensor N is a subbundle. This
certificate neither selects a positive point nor substitutes values for
FB,FC,FD.

## 5. Symmetric kernel, its real defect, and constructive cubic generation

Use the constant splitting (3) to reduce a symmetric tensor to its C/C,
C/(A tensor V), and (A tensor V)/(A tensor V) blocks. Exact C annihilation
forces each mixed C block into A tensor N. In the last block set

    Hsym={T in Sym²(A tensor S³): T(id_A tensor v)=0}.

In characteristic zero, separate symmetric and alternating A channels:

    Hsym = Sym²A tensor Sym²N
           + Lambda²A tensor S(-1).                    (9)

The product image of Sym²(A tensor N) is

    Sym²A tensor Sym²N + Lambda²A tensor m S(-1).

Therefore its cokernel in Hsym is exactly

    Lambda²A tensor (S/m)(-1).                          (10)

This is the actual saturation defect. It is real in degree one, but is
zero in every degree at least two, including the desired degree three.
No inference from degree-one dimensions is being made.

There is a direct factorization, not merely a depth argument. Let C_v be
the three-by-three cross-product matrix, (C_v)_ia=epsilon_iua v_u.
For a symmetric homogeneous matrix M of degree d>=2 with M v=0, put

    B_ab = sum epsilon_aki epsilon_blj
                   partial_k partial_l M_ij / [d(d+1)].

Then B is symmetric of degree d-2 and M=C_v B C_v^t. The identity follows
by differentiating M v=0 twice and using Euler's identity. For the
alternating sector, every annihilating matrix is f C_v. If its degree is
d>=2, Euler writes f=sum v_i partial_i f/(d-1); each v_i C_v is a wedge
of two Koszul columns. Thus this sector too factors constructively into
kernel-leg products. The accompanying algebra note and verifier give the
signed formulas and check the complete free-coefficient identities.

Combining this factorization, (6), and (7), every degree-three tensor in
the exact C kernel is a sum of products involving C or Acoeff, plus

    sum_(i,j) K_ij(v) Y_i Y_j*,  K=K^t, deg K=1.        (11)

Stars use the fixed physical determinant pairing, not an unmarked
transpose. Optional quartic-label changes are Laurent-unit changes of
basis, so (11) uses exactly the previously constructed D5-square family.

## 6. Source quotient and global sections introduce no cubic defect

The source-ideal intersection is the literal product ideal: after C is
removed, source terms meeting Hsym form P4*(A tensor N). For example contraction of
sym(P4,H) is the injective P4 inclusion times the contraction of H, so
annihilation forces the other leg into A tensor N. This also follows locally from
the subbundle splitting established in section 4.

Consequently (10) implies that the degree-three source quotient agrees
with (Sym²Q)_3. It is not enough to stop there: the sheafified symmetric
resolution and its global sections must also be checked. On P²_R, (1)
gives the exact symmetric resolution

    0 -> Lambda²A O(-4) -> A tensor D5 O(-3)
      -> Sym²D5 O(-2) -> Sym²Q~ -> 0.                   (12)

Twisting by three leaves O(-1), O, O(1), all with vanishing higher
cohomology. Splitting the length-two complex into short exact sequences
therefore gives exactly

    0 -> A tensor D5
      -> Sym²D5 tensor S1 -> H0(Sym²Q~(3)) -> 0.         (13)

There is no extra global section, source-lifting obstruction, or
saturation class in degree three. The formal quotient in (13) has thirty
coefficients. Its finite-group character is 2A+2V+2P4+2D5, giving twelve
harmonic external intertwiners and two parameter-trace intertwiners.
These are statements about the proved lower source-quotient presentation,
not independent actual full-U middle operators.

For comparison, twisting (12) by one has a nonzero H²(O(-3)) term, giving
three extra saturated degree-one sections. Their existence is consistent
with (10). This is why an unsaturated presentation alone would not have
justified a global claim.

For actual global middles the existing global Koszul-square presentation
already supplies cubic polynomial representatives after full source
normalization. A normalized weight-zero representative has mixed-mixed
legs and positive cubic coefficients. Its exact equation T0 C=0 is
precisely the polynomial coefficient condition used above: mixed
contraction is injective on coefficients with no u,w dependence, since
its VV part is u kappa(c0)+w kappa(c1). Thus (11) proves generation of
actual exactly C-annihilating lower representatives. Possible further
exterior relations can reduce this span, not create missing generators.
This argument does not normalize a merely q0-divisible C equation to an
exactly zero one.

## 7. Full source homotopies and full-U completion must remain attached

Replacing an A-source leg A(s) by zero is not a full monad normalization.
With kappa=bm/am one has

    A(s)=alpha_U(s)/am-kappa B(s).

A C-source leg similarly equals alpha_C(t)/cm. For a symmetric product,
replace each leg p by its remaining representative p'; write p-p'=alpha(s)
and q-q'=alpha(t). The complete homotopy is

    H=f delta_D [s b(q,-)+t b(p',-)],
    P=H alpha,   D_H=alpha H-H^sharp beta,   right=-P^vee.

It retains every U/C source block and preserves both arrow residuals.
Thus eliminating A/C terms from (11) creates definite higher-weight middle
terms and source changes. They are not zeroed by a lower-weight argument.

More sharply, let Z be the six-by-five matrix of the canonical degree-one
VWW partners and L=Y-kappa p(Z). For the columns of Rel define

    sigma_j=-sum_(r,a) w_r e_a H_(r,a),j,
    zeta_j=(Zeta Acoeff H)_j,
    W_j=p(Z Rel_j)+B(sigma_j).                          (14)

The verifier checks the complete exterior identities

    W_j*A=0,   W_j*B=0,
    L Rel_j=C(zeta_j)+alpha_U(sigma_j)/am-kappa W_j.      (15)

Every W_j is nonzero and has nonzero C pairing, so it is not an actual
full-source leg. The constructor exports every W coefficient and C row;
the reproduction log prints all three with FB,FC,FD unassigned.
Therefore the degree-one completion does not extend to an S-linear map
on Q without these correction terms. Dropping them would be an error.

This does not manufacture extra independent directions beyond the eighteen
completed harmonic columns. Multiplying (15) by another completed leg
already yields a positive-linear coefficient on completed D5 squares,
plus a full-source boundary. In particular the lower coefficient relations
need not be removed from the completed eighteen-column family. Nor does
(15) classify arbitrary higher-weight companions. The complete weight-five
C equation, its forced source terms, and lower q0 divisions remain
separate requirements.
