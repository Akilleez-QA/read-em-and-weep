# q-divisible cubic middles have exact-C lower representatives

## Checked result and essential limitation

Every symmetric mixed-mixed positive-cubic coefficient tensor T0 with the
**literal** condition T0 C=0 modulo q0 admits an explicit polynomial
identity

    T0=T_exact+q0 B,
    T_exact C=0,   B=B^t positive-linear.                (1)

All determinant and marking factors stay physical. This has been checked
with all 108 independent coefficients of a symmetric positive-linear
8-by-8 matrix on the **existing raw eight q-orthogonal legs**, not only on
a balanced model or a different lift. The raw-eight source changes and
all their VWW partners are also checked literally.

Thus, after allowing lower q0-middle corrections and actual lower A/C
source terms, these q-orthogonal cubic lower middles introduce no new
lower generators beyond the exact-C D5 squares. This does NOT collapse
the full completed families. Full-U completion differentiates the mixed
legs and need not commute with multiplication by q0 or by a coefficient.
The precise gradient/product-rule anomaly is given in section 6. An
explicit full-U completion of q0 B is provided and checked with all 234
symmetric-linear physical coefficients free; its higher terms generally
are not q0 multiples.

Nothing here removes the existing divided-arrow correction N, its C,C
anomaly, or any source endpoint. Nothing here turns a source-valued low-C
equation T0 C=A Q1+C Y0+q0 V into literal q0 annihilation without an
additional justified normalization. No completed-family equivalence,
source solution, or obstruction value is claimed.

## 1. Exact polynomial q-orthogonal leg module

Use the frozen constant physical split from
`FULL-GRADED-C-ORTHOGONAL-LEG-RESOLUTION-AND-CUBIC-SATURATION.md`:

    lambda(v)=(id_A tensor v^t) D,
    Rsplit D+C Zeta=I,  D Rsplit=I,  Zeta Rsplit=0.

Let q=xy+z² and g=(y/2,x/2,z)^t, so v^t g=q. Define the three marked
mixed legs

    Ggrad=Rsplit(id_A tensor g).                         (2)

Then lambda(Ggrad)=q I, with no direct C-source projection. For any
homogeneous y with lambda(y)=q rho,

    y-Ggrad rho belongs to the exact-C kernel.

Apply the checked all-degree exact reconstruction to obtain

    y=C c+Acoeff a+Y b+Ggrad rho.                        (3)

All coefficient vectors, including the C shift caused by A projection,
are returned by `reconstruct_q_leg`. Over S itself, the q-orthogonal module
is the direct sum of the exact-C module and the free three-generator
Ggrad summand: an element of Ggrad meeting the exact kernel has q rho=0,
hence rho=0. Thus its quotient by the stated A/C sources has the exact
presentation Q direct-sum A tensor S(-1), where Q is the five-generator
exact quotient. Its degree-one quotient is A+D5, as expected.

The actual quadratic is q0=q/theta. The routine does not set theta to one:
its actual q-pairing legs have rho=R0/theta when compared with (2), and
an identity qB=q0(theta B) transports the correction exactly.

## 2. The conic module is not a naively restricted Koszul module

Set barS=S/(q). The kernel of v^t:barS³->barS(1) is generated by the three
linear columns of the cross-product matrix C_v and the fourth column g.
There is a complete linear periodic resolution. With block sizes shown,

    L=[C_v,g],
    Dconic=[ v  -C_g ],       Econic=[ g^t  0 ],
           [ 0   v^t ],              [ C_v  g ],

one has the polynomial identities

    L Dconic=[0,q I3],  Dconic Econic=Econic Dconic=q I4.

Consequently

    ... -> barS(-3)^4 -> barS(-2)^4 -> barS(-1)^4
        -> ker(v^t over barS) -> 0                     (4)

is exact, alternating Econic and Dconic. The independent companion note
proves every exactness by polynomial lifting and cancellation of q, not
by an evaluated rank test. Tensoring with A and adjoining the free
constant C summand gives the full physical conic-leg module.

A conic relation is not an exact physical source relation before reducing
modulo q. After the physical source conversion it has the form

    Y R_new+Ggrad a
      =-Acoeff H_new+C Zeta Acoeff H_new+q Rsplit(t).     (5)

Both the C shift and the q-leg remainder are mandatory.

## 3. Universal differential inverse for cubic tensors

Let r be a completely free homogeneous quadratic three-vector. Write
J(r)_ij=partial_j r_i and f=v^t r. Euler's identity gives

    Gamma_plus(r)=J(r)+J(r)^t-Hess(f)/2,
    Gamma_plus(r)^t=Gamma_plus(r),
    Gamma_plus(r)v=r.                                   (6)

This solves all eighteen independent quadratic RHS coefficients without
any coefficient-fitting solve. For v^t r=0 one also has

    Gamma_minus(r)=(J(r)-J(r)^t)/3,
    Gamma_minus(r)^t=-Gamma_minus(r),
    Gamma_minus(r)v=r.                                  (7)

The full off-kernel identity is Gamma_minus(r)v=r-grad(v^t r)/3.

For a symmetric tensor on A tensor V, its A blocks satisfy T_ba=T_ab^t.
If T_ab v=q r_ab, put

    r_plus=(r_ab+r_ba)/2, r_minus=(r_ab-r_ba)/2,
    B_ab=Gamma_plus(r_plus)+Gamma_minus(r_minus).

Skew symmetry forces v^t r_minus=0. Thus B_ba=B_ab^t and B_ab v=r_ab,
so T-qB is exactly annihilating. This proves (1) constructively.

For the constant physical C-source summand, a cross block has a scalar
quadratic defect r; the correction grad(r)/2 gives contraction r. The C/C
block may be left zero. Conjugating by the literal constant basis
[C,Rsplit], with inverse [Zeta;D], produces the physical twelve-by-twelve
B. `q_cubic_lift` checks every equality against the original exterior
pairing map before returning it. Its optional supplied defect is verified,
not trusted as an assumption.

## 4. Conic saturation and global sections are checked independently

Let H be the exact symmetric kernel on A tensor S³ and barH its literal
kernel over barS. Both are saturated. For barH, write it as the kernel of
a map between finite free barS modules. The image is torsion-free and
barS has irrelevant depth two, so H_m^0(barH)=H_m^1(barH)=0. This proves
that every graded kernel equals the corresponding conic global sections;
no unsaturated-module shortcut is used.

On P², local splitting of v^t identifies the kernel sheaf as
Sym²(A tensor Omega¹(1)); its restriction is the conic kernel sheaf.
The previously proved resolution

    0 -> O(-3)^18 -> O(-2)^36+O(-1)^3 -> H~ -> 0

implies H¹(H~(1))=0, since the twist has only O(-2),O(-1),O. Restricting
to the conic therefore gives the exact sequence

    0 -> q H1 -> H3 -> barH3 -> 0.                       (8)

Its relative coefficient ranks are 3,108,105 as consequences of exactness,
not a rank experiment. The exact cubic representative is not unique;
its ambiguity is precisely q times the three degree-one alternating-A
exact-kernel tensors. This also explains why restricting the degree-one
leg dimensions alone would have been inadequate.

Combining (1) with the frozen exact-C cubic factorization gives generation
by the five D5 legs modulo lower q0 middles and lower A/C source products.
The constant C-source blocks introduce no additional obstruction.

## 6. The precise derivative/product-rule anomaly

For a homogeneous mixed coefficient y of positive degree d, set

    r_y=b(m(y),B)/det,
    Z_d(y)=p(J grad_v(r_y)/d),
    L_d(y)=m(y)-(bm/am)Z_d(y).                           (9)

Here p denotes the full VWW contraction, and all its VW and WW outputs
are retained. Euler gives Z_d(y)*A=r_y and Z_d(y)*B=0; also m(y)*A=0.
Therefore L_d(y) annihilates the entire U arrow exactly.

For a homogeneous scalar p0 of degree e, differentiation gives

    Z_(d+e)(p0 y)-p0 Z_d(y)
      =[p(J grad(p0) r_y)-e p0 Z_d(y)]/(d+e).            (10)

In particular multiplication by q produces grad(q) terms that need not be
q multiples. The verifier checks (10) with all 36 coefficients of a free
positive-linear mixed leg and all six coefficients of a free quadratic
multiplier. It also checks the complete U columns with independent am,bm.

A particularly transparent completion of qB can be given without any
factorization ambiguity in B. Let g_j be the twelve constant mixed basis
legs, and write the symmetric linear matrix as B(v). Define

    b_j=sum_i B_ij(v) g_i,
    c_j=q g_j.

Then b_j has positive degree one and c_j degree two. The operator

    T_qB=(1/2) sum_j [L_1(b_j)L_2(c_j)*
                       +L_2(c_j)L_1(b_j)*]             (11)

has lower term exactly qB and annihilates the full U arrow. Its partners
are explicitly

    Z_1(b_j)=p(J sum_i grad(B_ij) r_(g_i)),
    Z_2(c_j)=(1/2)p(J grad(q) r_(g_j)).                 (12)

Thus both grad(q) and **every derivative of B_ij** occur. If B came from
the free K(v) reduction, those derivatives include the induced coefficient
and K derivatives. No scalar coefficient is held artificially constant.
The exported constructor differentiates the actual polynomials literally.

The verifier exercises (11) on all 234 independent coefficients of a
symmetric positive-linear twelve-by-twelve B and checks both entire U
columns. Formula (12) yields nonzero positive-linear partners for the
q times constant legs, so these partners cannot be q-divisible. The T2
and T4 terms, and their complete C residuals, remain part of (11).
Consequently a lower identity (1) is not a claim that the difference of
two full completed families is q times a full cochain or a source boundary.
Such a claim still requires the explicit higher comparison, source ends,
and divided-arrow/anomaly transport.

## 8. A complete C interface for the arbitrary qB completion

There is a stronger checked result than merely retaining an unprocessed
C residual. Write E=lambda_C, so E has three rows and twelve columns.
For the twelve b_j and c_j of section 6, collect their partners in ZB,ZQ
and their C pairings in the twelve-by-three matrices RB,RQ. Thus

    RB_ja=b(Z_1(b_j),C_a)/det,
    RQ_ja=b(Z_2(c_j),C_a)/det.

Define the three degree-two mixed legs and their degree-two Euler partners

    N1=B E^t,   ZN=Z_2(N1),   N=N1-kappa p(ZN).          (13)

Crucially, N annihilates the entire U arrow. It is NOT a naive isolated
lower qC1 subtraction. The complete residual of (11), scaled exactly like
the other source families, is

    (kAt/kappa) T_qB (cm C)
      =kAt cm [q/kappa N+D3+kappa p(G5)],                (14)

where the unconverted weight-three terms are

    D3_mixed=-(B RQ+q RB)/2,
    D3_vww=q ZN-(ZQ N1+q ZB E^t)/2,
    G5=(ZB RQ+ZQ RB)/2.                                 (15)

Every WW coefficient of D3_vww is identically zero. This is not assumed:
Euler and symmetry of B give cancellation of q ZN_WW against the two
half-terms. For each spin channel, if g is its positive-degree-three
three-vector, set h=-curl(g)/4. Then v cross h=g and the literal identity

    p(g)=mixed(-w h,u h)                                (16)

converts the remaining pure-VW contribution to mixed coefficients. Its
VV part is zero because the two terms cancel. Thus D3 is in the same
no-WW mixed target as the existing source extension, while G5 remains a
full VWW coefficient. The exported adapter checks (16) on the complete
exterior forms.

The residual D3+kappa p(G5), as well as N, annihilates the whole U arrow.
But it is generally not independently a closed C-source cochain. Put

    S_ab=b(p(ZN_a),C_b)/det-b(p(ZN_b),C_a)/det.

The exact remaining C,C anomaly is

    skew_C(D3+kappa p(G5))=q S,
    skew_C(q/kappa N)=-q S.                             (17)

These identities retain the anomaly rather than silently passing to a
quotient. They have been checked with all 234 coefficient symbols free.

`universal_q_middle_C_interface.py::q_middle_C_interface` returns all
terms in (13)-(17), including the raw VWW term, its converted mixed
coefficient, and the anomaly. `extend_q_middle_C_source` uses the actual
q0=q/theta, calls the existing `extend_C_source(D3,G5)`, and returns its
unconditional candidates and errors. `restore_q_middle_companion` uses
the identical checked all-scale restoration interface, including the
fixed negative determinant-dual right arrow. In particular the additive
actual divided left C arrow is

    kAt cm [Vnorm-kappa^(-1) N1+p(ZN)].                  (18)

No extension error is asserted to vanish. No new restricted source solve
has been performed.

## 9. The C-source quotient is valid for the entire chosen completion

For every constant physical C-source leg c, b(c,Bsource)=0. Hence all
Euler partners of p0(v)c vanish for every homogeneous positive p0. Let
g be any constant mixed leg, ell a positive-linear scalar, and
B=ell sym(c,g), with sym(c,g)=(c g^t+g c^t)/2. The whole chosen completion,
not just its lower coefficient, has the exact factorization

    T_qB=sym(c,H),
    H=[ell L_2(qg)+q L_1(ell g)]/2.                     (19)

Every derivative and both higher weights occur in H. Thus this is a
literal full-source middle factor. The existing global fifteen-generator
source-homotopy formula transports it with BOTH source endpoints. A
lower-only C projection is not being substituted for that homotopy.

The complete qB construction consequently descends, modulo those actual
full-source homotopies, from positive-linear Sym²(Gmix) to positive-linear
Sym²(Gmix/Csource). The latter has 3*45=135 free coefficients, compared
with 3*78=234 before quotienting. The source quotient is the constant
nine-dimensional P4+D5 module; this claim does not require assigning a
point or testing an evaluated rank. `verify_q_middle_C_source_factor.py`
checks (19) with completely free c,g,ell on every exterior tensor
component.

This reduction concerns the specified qB completion. It does NOT identify
an arbitrary q8 completion with it or discard the difference of their
higher companions. That comparison remains an explicit higher/source
problem. A complementary regular-middle cochain subtraction, removing
q sym(g,Lh) with all its U and C divided outputs, is documented separately
in `Q-MULTIPLIED-LOWER-MIDDLE-COMPLETION-HAS-A-HIGHER-WEIGHT-NORMAL-FORM.md`
and is not replaced by the canonical N choice
in (13).
