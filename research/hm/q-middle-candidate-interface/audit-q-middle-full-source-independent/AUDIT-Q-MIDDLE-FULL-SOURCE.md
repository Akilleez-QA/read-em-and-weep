# Independent audit of the prescribed qB completion and full source quotient

## Verdict

The universal qB interface passes the independent checks below. The bounded
necessity-and-sufficiency argument transfers from the audited q8 theorem to
this added family, for exactly the old supported middle, its arbitrary
negative-quartic Euler companion, fixed closed target/trace, and allowed
bounded source blocks. This is a criterion, not a source solution, an
operator-independence assertion, completeness among arbitrary higher
completions, or an obstruction value.

One factor-of-two error was found in the unpublished C-source companion:
its S is an unhalved symplectic sum, while the input coefficient sym is
halved. Its displayed factor was 1/2 where it had to be 1/4. The author
corrected that companion and added a regression check. The main frozen
qB bundle was unchanged. The audit below applies to the corrected companion.

## Complete physical residual and canonical subtraction

Write q0=(xy+z^2)/theta, kappa=b_m/a_m, E=lambda_C, and let B be any
symmetric positive-linear twelve-by-twelve coefficient matrix. The paired
homogeneous completion of q0 B annihilates both complete U columns, not
only the lowest U term. Every derivative of B is retained.

Independently construct N1=B E^t, ZN=Z_2(N1), and N=N1-kappa p(ZN).
The exact C action is

    (kAt/kappa) T_qB(c_m C)
      = kAt c_m [q0/kappa N + D3 + kappa p(G5)].

Both N and D3+kappa p(G5) annihilate the complete U arrow. Each exterior
column is closed. The independently evaluated D3 has no WW component and
agrees with the API's literal mixed reconstruction.

There is also a short direct explanation for the WW cancellation. For
homogeneous degree-d mixed coefficients, contraction of the Euler partner
with the positive vector equals J times its B-source pairing. Thus in

    D3_vww = q0 ZN - (ZQ N1 + q0 ZB E^t)/2,

the three WW contractions are q0 J r(N1), one half of q0 J r(N1), and
one half of q0 J r(N1). The latter two exhaust the first. Symmetry of B
identifies the two lower tensor contractions. This is a polynomial Euler
identity, not a vanishing inferred from a pointwise rank.

For each homogeneous cubic three-vector g with v.g=0,

    v cross curl(g) = -4g.

Consequently h=-curl(g)/4 gives the global polynomial Koszul conversion
p(g)=mixed(-w h,u h). Both the no-WW property and this full exterior-form
conversion were independently compared, including the canceled VV terms.

The independent checker retains the exact C,C anomaly:

    skew_C(D3+kappa p(G5)) = q0 (ZN_C-ZN_C^t),
    skew_C(q0/kappa N)    =-q0 (ZN_C-ZN_C^t).

Neither piece is declared separately a monad cycle.

## Why the bounded converse transfers

The old q8 audit proved the high source inverse on its entire free solution
domain, without assuming C,C cycle closedness. The qB input meets the same
hypotheses; the old theorem is not applied directly to a nonzero-C1 middle.

1. The added full U action is zero. All old U source gauges and the
   arbitrary negative-quartic Euler reconstruction therefore apply to the
   old residual exactly as before. No T_new A=0 shortcut is used.
2. Subtract q0/kappa N on the complete fixed-dual pair. Divisibility is
   equivalent before and after subtraction because N is regular. Its
   exterior closure and U,C compatibility are preserved, although C,C
   compatibility changes by the explicit anomaly above.
3. The reduced lowest C equation is precisely the old A Q1+C Y0 equation.
   The old conic (A,C) injectivity therefore removes the same q-source
   gauges, including the B Q1 companion and negative-dual source end.
4. The subtraction changes no weight-five component. Its pure-WW possible
   quotient is still killed by exterior closure; the old VV/source-end
   conditions and full VWW splitter therefore force the joint Pc and LC
   conditions. Old and new terms must be combined, not separately screened.
5. Exact U,C compatibility survives. Together with exact U and weight five,
   it yields the old high-spin compatibility condition. No C,C condition
   on the reduced C arrow is needed for this argument.
6. The remaining high input is exterior-closed, no-WW, and has the same
   bidegrees as the previously audited complete high inverse. That inverse
   recovers every possible high source/quotient modulo q-source gauge.
   Thus its joint saved errors are necessary, not merely sufficient.
7. Conversely vanishing all joint errors gives literal polynomial division
   by the displayed restoration. Original middle symplecticity, source
   isotropy, and the fixed closed target imply full quotient closure after
   cancellation of the regular q0. Both source endpoints are retained.

This proves necessary and sufficient error criteria within the stated bounded
added family. It does not prove that any coefficients satisfy those criteria.

## Full C-source boundaries and normalized coefficient quotient

The independently rebuilt C-source columns pair identically to zero with B_U.
Thus every homogeneous p(v)c has zero Euler partner. For constant C-source
c, constant mixed g, and positive-linear ell, the actual coefficient input
B=ell(cg^t+gc^t)/2 has the full completion

    T_qB = S(c, ell L_2(q0 g)+q0 L_1(ell g))/4,

where S(x,y)=x[y,-]+y[x,-] is the UNHALVED operator sum. The 1/4 is essential
for the numerical middle/source representative, although any nonzero common
factor would leave the qualitative boundary conclusion unchanged.

Put h=(ell L_2(q0 g)+q0 L_1(ell g))/4 and c=alpha_C(csrc)/c_m.
The all-scale homotopy uses

    H=(kAt/(kappa c_m)) csrc [h,-],
    Hd=(kAt/(kappa c_m)) h csrc^t,
    P=H alpha, Pd=beta Hd=-P^t,
    T=alpha H+Hd beta.

Before isotropy the exact remainders are

    T alpha-alpha P = Hd (beta alpha),
    beta T-Pd beta = (beta alpha) H.

Therefore this is a full regular source homotopy boundary, not a middle
C factor erased while leaving its endpoints zero. Since h annihilates U,
its U source columns vanish; its remaining homogeneous pieces have the
allowed lower/high C-source degrees.

The prescribed construction therefore factors modulo full homotopies through
V tensor Sym^2(Gmix/Csrc)=V tensor Sym^2(P4+D5), of dimension135 rather
than234. The exact removed coefficient module is a useful additional check:

    V tensor (Sym^2(A) + A tensor (P4+D5))
      = 2*1 + 5A + 6V + 6P4 + 8D5,

of dimension99. Hence

    234=135+99,
    harmonic: 96=55+41,
    trace:    18=10+8.

This yields 55 harmonic and10 trace coefficient channels for this family
modulo those boundaries. It asserts neither independence of their supported
operators nor absence of further A-source/higher relations, and adds no
fixed-trace freedom to the actual source problem.

## Higher-weight normal form

For any complete pair Lq=qg-kappa Zq, Lh=h-kappa Zh, the independent free
matrix calculation gives

    S(Lq,Lh)=q S(g,Lh)-kappa S(Zq,Lh).

The regular twist-two middle B0=S(g,Lh) has the complete boundary
(B0 alpha,-beta B0). With arbitrary fixed source ends and targets, the
higher normal form changes BOTH differential components by -q d(B0).
If the original residual is divisible, the divided class changes by the
regular boundary -d(B0). If division has not been proved, only the polynomial
identity is available. The generally nonzero divisible U component must
not be discarded. This comparison does not identify arbitrary completions
and does not replace the canonical full-U qN subtraction above.
