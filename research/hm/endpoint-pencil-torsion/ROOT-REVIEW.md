# Second independent review of the endpoint pencil

20 October 2026. Scoped mathematical verdict: PASS. This is a structural mathematical review, not a selected HM/source evaluation or formal verification.

A two-dimensional domain subspace on which the good direction is an isomorphism reduces the restricted pencil to tI+C. Polynomial reduction to constants is surjective onto its cokernel, and the leading-degree argument shows constants inject. Thus its length is exactly two, not the weaker bound four from two generators and a quadratic annihilator. The full primary cokernel is a quotient. Localization at a characteristic root gives the stated multiplicity bound and annihilator exponent.

The natural section-resolution square transfers this as a surjection from fifteen copies of the primary cokernel onto the endpoint quotient family. The cubic and other resolution relations are retained through further quotienting. The total length bound thirty, at most two possible exceptional parameters, and the local bounds follow. Cokernel formation commutes with arbitrary specialization by right exactness, so the original quotient really is the zero fiber; flatness is not assumed. Generic zero does not imply zero at that fiber.

The primary corank-two case exhausts the total length bound and is reduced torsion of length two at that point. A one-dimensional primary fiber gives a cyclic local module of length at most two, so the asserted conditional dichotomy is correct. No fiber dimension is evaluated.

For the formal realization, D=U0^{-1}U1 is self-adjoint for U0. Its formal square root H satisfies H^T U0 H=U0+tU1; g=H^{-1} therefore has precisely the required pullback orientation. Transforming the cubic as well gives a genuine target-automorphism family through the original geometry. Its quotient is controlled by the same primary module, but is not identified with the completion of the fixed-cubic family.

At zero the quadratic annihilator is t^e times a unit with e≤2. Thus the explicitly defined formal quotient is annihilated by t², and right-exact reduction reconstructs it over dual numbers. The first derivative of v0(H(t)x) is the displayed universal directional derivative. This is a parameter-jet statement about the presentation, not a computation of an actual source jet.

The generic and special geometric cohomology assertions are made separately over their fields. No relative R² identification, ordinary duality of torsion modules, or unproved cohomology base-change statement is used. The fixed quotient, actual source membership, six pure conditions, module lifting and algebraicity remain unresolved.
