# Independent review: cubic canonical relations

The argument was checked independently against the pinned proofs of PR479, PR483, PR489 and PR492. It is valid conditional on their representation, presentation and exact-sequence inputs. This is mathematical review, not formal proof-assistant verification.

The critical inference is coefficient extraction, not a dimension estimate. If the antisymmetric inclusion of a cubic annihilator becomes zero in (L/mL)_3 tensor W, then its two coefficients ae_1 and ae_2 both lie in (mL)_3. Consequently the actual injection I_3 tensor W into L_3 would have image in T L_2. When r=15, the exact quadratic-shadow sequence has source A tensor (R_6 plus 1 plus U') and kernel A tensor U', so its image is A tensor (R_6 plus 1). This image cannot contain the A tensor U' constituent of I_3 tensor W=A tensor (1 plus U'). The contradiction proves the required nonzero map. Since I_3 is irreducible, it also proves injectivity. It follows that q=20, not 30, in this case.

The degree-three Tor sequence and its zero term were checked separately. Tor_1(I,C)_3 vanishes by minimality because all generators of I have degree at least three. Thus when r=15 the injection on cubic generators gives Tor_2(N,C)_3=Tor_2(H,C)_3 tensor W, with dimension thirty. When r=0 it instead gives Tor_2(N,C)_3=I_3, with dimension ten. The complementary internal degree for R/N duality is ten, so these are the asserted third-syzygy dimensions of R in degree seven. No higher shift is silently inferred from the cubic argument.

The additional implication r=15 implies v=0 was proposed during this review and separately checked by the proof author. Its argument uses the minimal cover of M=L tensor W, not an assumed nonzero equivariant scalar. A nonzero degree-four minimal syzygy of I has a nonzero vector of linear coefficients on I_3. The cubic-generator projection of its image is id_T tensor alpha applied to that vector, hence is nonzero. By contrast every element of (mZ)_4=T Z_3, where Z is the kernel of the minimal cover of M, has zero cubic-generator projection: a degree-three syzygy cannot have a constant coefficient on a minimal cubic generator. This proves injectivity on Tor_1 in degree four, so the kernel parameter v vanishes. The refined Betti table is exactly PR492 with q=20 and v=0; the r=0 branch is not changed. This portion is a jointly developed argument with reciprocal mathematical checking, rather than an independent rediscovery by this reviewer.

This review does not choose r or the scalar middle parameter s. It uses no selected HM/source-instance evaluation, numerical multiplication matrix or rank calculation, coefficient search, or large computation. The original fixed-pair/source questions, higher-order lifting and algebraicity are not resolved by this result.
