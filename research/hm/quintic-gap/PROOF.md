# Structural exclusion of actual primary quintics

Status: independently mathematically reviewed (PASS); see REVIEW.md. Not formally checked. Work over C. No selected actual HM/source coefficients, evaluation matrices, rank tests, numerical search, or large computation are used.

## 1. Statement and notation

Let S⊂P⁴ be the actual primary surface, P its five-variable homogeneous polynomial ring, I its saturated prime ideal, and ν:X→S its normalization. Write B=Γ_*O_S, N=Γ_*O_X, M=N/B, and C=B/(P/I). The finite normalizer is denoted 𝒩, its Heisenberg subgroup H₅, its scalar center Z₅, and K=𝒩/H₅≅SL₂(F₅). In K, let j be the central involution. The notation V_a means the standard five-dimensional 𝒩-extension with central weight ε^a, using additive weights a modulo five; it is not the Galois-index notation of the original paper.

**Theorem. I₅=0 and C₅=0.** Thus actual ambient restriction P_d→H⁰(S,O_S(d)) is an isomorphism for every 0≤d≤5.

Inputs already established independently:

1. S is reduced integral and invariant under 𝒩, of degree 15 (global-primary-equality, PR573, 6c2dbd48edea0577acf063a60e1edbbbe92ced5c; actual equivariance in degree-five-invariants, PR590, eb236dac3af8adcbc7f2f660b55e9d07904858c6).
2. I_d=C_d=0 for 0≤d≤4 (low-equation-exclusion, PR595, 1107ece91d6ef00a097bfc35cb32c463ee82bc87, research/hm/low-equation-exclusion/PROOF.md). Here C_d=H¹(P⁴,I_S(d)).
3. The actual minimal P-resolution of N is
   0→4P(−5)→10P(−3)→P⊕5P(−2)→N→0,
   with the P summand mapping by the unit (normalization-resolution, PR592, 1a2822304e0e76d985f8a083fb36548127b881a1).
4. The degree-two conductor image Q₂=M₂ has dimension five and is V₂ as a 𝒩-module. Multiplication P₁⊗Q₂→M₃ has image dimension 15 and kernel V₃⊗W, where W is a two-dimensional K-representation on which j acts by −1 (lower-conductor-ranks, 80f302ddd7e0dd19f1c54b725f56e5f81bb47c69, Section 5). Also dim B₅=126=dim P₅.

Input 4 follows from the actual conductor multiplication, not from a hypothetical primary resolution. Its representation identity is P₁⊗Q₂=V₃⊗(U⊕W), with U three-dimensional and j-even, while the actual conductor image is V₃⊗U.

## 2. Actual equivariant cubic relations

By Input 2, P₂→B₂ is an isomorphism. The sequence

0→P₂→N₂→M₂→0

is therefore a short exact sequence of finite-dimensional 𝒩-representations. Choose a 𝒩-equivariant splitting s:Q₂→N₂, possible by averaging over the finite group. Extend by P-linearity to the graded equivariant surjection

π:F₀=P⊕(P(−2)⊗Q₂)→N,    (p,q)↦p·1+s(q).

It is surjective and minimal by Input 3: its degree-zero and degree-two generators map isomorphically to the minimal-generator quotient of N. Equivalently, the degree-two quotient by P₂ is exactly Q₂ and the resolution states that there are no other generator degrees. Let L=ker π. Input 3 implies that L is generated by L₃, with dim L₃=10, and that there are no relations of smaller degree. This conclusion is invariant under choice of minimal presentation; it does not suppose that the original displayed resolution had specified equivariant matrices.

Projection from (F₀)₃=P₃⊕(P₁⊗Q₂) onto its second summand identifies L₃ equivariantly with ker[P₁⊗Q₂→M₃]. Indeed:

- Its kernel on L₃ is ker[P₃→N₃]=I₃=0.
- Given q whose conductor image vanishes, π(0,q) belongs to B₃. By Input 2, B₃ is exactly the image of P₃, and this image is injective. There is therefore a unique p with π(p,q)=0.

Thus the **actual** cubic relation representation is

L₃≅V₃⊗W.                                               (1)

As L is generated by L₃, multiplication is a surjective equivariant map P₂⊗L₃→L₅. Consequently averaging supplies a surjection

(P₂⊗L₃)^{H₅}→L₅^{H₅}.                                 (2)

No assertion about the primary ideal's higher syzygies is used here.

## 3. Parity of invariant relations versus invariant quintics

The representation facts needed are universal, independent of the actual surface:

P₂≅V₂⊗U₂,  dim U₂=3,  j|U₂=+1;
P₅^{H₅}≅U₃⊕U₃',       j|P₅^{H₅}=+1.                    (3)

Here U₂ may be either of the two three-dimensional labels under Galois/dual convention. Both are j-even. These identities are Horrocks–Mumford (1973), Lemma 3.3(v) and Theorem 3.5 (the latter following from Lemma 3.3(viii)), printed pp70–71. The dual/Galois conventions only permute the labels and preserve parity. The standard extensions satisfy V₃≅V₂* as full 𝒩-modules, by the duality established in that paper, Section 1, printed p65. Source:
https://www.dam.brown.edu/people/mumford/alg_geom/papers/1973a--Rk2BdleP%5E4-NC.pdf

For clarity, the H₅-invariant line in V₂⊗V₂* is the identity endomorphism, and the induced K-action on it is trivial, since it is conjugation. Combining (1) and (3) gives

(P₂⊗L₃)^{H₅}
 ≅(V₂⊗V₂*)^{H₅}⊗U₂⊗W
 ≅U₂⊗W.                                                (4)

Thus j acts by −1 on the whole space (4), and by (2) also on L₅^{H₅}. On the other hand, projection onto the first summand defines an equivariant map L₅→P₅. On H₅-invariants its source is j-odd and its target j-even by (3), so it is zero.

Every f∈I₅^{H₅} gives the relation (f,0)∈L₅^{H₅}; its first projection is f. The preceding zero-map statement therefore forces

I₅^{H₅}=0.                                             (5)

This does not confuse L₅ with I₅. Only the inclusion f↦(f,0) and the projection L₅→P₅ are needed; there can be other degree-five relations. It also does not infer (5) from equal dimensions or from merely Heisenberg-isotypic free-module ranks.

## 4. A small geometric upper bound

Choose a general hyperplane H⊂P⁴ such that D=S∩H is integral; Bertini irreducibility for the hyperplane system on the integral surface gives this. One may also avoid the finite node set. Let J be the saturated homogeneous ideal of D in H≅P³ and Q=P/(ℓ) its polynomial ring.

Because ℓ is a nonzerodivisor on O_S, the ideal restriction sequence is exact:

0→I_S(d−1) --ℓ--> I_S(d)→I_{D/H}(d)→0.

Input 2 applied at d=4 gives J₄=0, using H¹(I_S(3))=0. Applied at d=5 it gives J₅≅I₅, using I₄=0 and H¹(I_S(4))=0. These are statements about actual sheaf restriction, not an assumption that P/I is Cohen–Macaulay or projectively normal in all degrees.

The homogeneous coordinate ring Q/J is a domain, since D is integral. Multiplication by any nonzero degree-one element is injective from degree four to degree five. Therefore

35=dim(Q/J)₄≤dim(Q/J)₅=56−dim I₅,

so

 dim I₅≤21.                                             (6)

A weaker bound below 24 would already suffice. No degree-five evaluation on selected points is involved.

## 5. The normalizer orbit contradiction

The center Z₅ acts trivially on P₅, hence on I₅. Restrict I₅ to G=H₅/Z₅≅F₅² and decompose into its character spaces. The quotient K acts transitively on the twenty-four nontrivial characters. Since I₅ is an actual 𝒩-submodule, all those character spaces have one common dimension m. Thus

dim I₅=dim I₅^{H₅}+24m=24m

by (5). Bound (6) forces m=0 and therefore I₅=0. Finally the exact ambient restriction sequence and dim B₅=dim P₅=126 give C₅=0. This proves the theorem.

## 6. Scope

The conclusion closes the degree-five actual ambient-map gap. It does not assert C=0 in every higher degree, determine a full primary ideal resolution, or settle the unrelated full-adjoint/source-lifting/algebraicity questions. The only new geometric ingredient beyond the pinned inputs is the elementary Hilbert-function monotonicity of an integral hyperplane-section curve. The key representation step identifies the actual cubic relation module through the already proved ambient isomorphism in degree three.
