# Exact recovery from three compressed quintic quotients

## Inputs and conventions

Work over C. Let S be a standard graded polynomial ring in five variables, m its positive-degree ideal, H generated by W=H_0 of dimension two, I=Ann_S(H), and R=S/I. We use the original-module results, not a selected source-instance evaluation:

- [PR491, commit e9ed26058f69f3b96bdcdf920cb638e9dd68def8](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/e9ed26058f69f3b96bdcdf920cb638e9dd68def8/research/hm/endomorphism-rigidity/INDEPENDENT-DERIVATION.md) identifies N=Sym²_S H with D_5(R), where D_5(M)_d=Hom_C(M_(5-d),C), by the balanced pairing. N is generated by N_0=Sym²W.
- [PR514, commit d915ed6a7f0c679343447106707692eb7bdee6f7](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/d915ed6a7f0c679343447106707692eb7bdee6f7/research/hm/cyclic-submodule-duality/PROOF.md) proves that T_w=Ann_S(w² in N) gives a compressed Gorenstein quotient G_w=S/T_w with Hilbert function (1,5,15,15,5,1), uniformly for every nonzero w. Moreover R has Hilbert function (1,5,15,25,15,3).
- [PR494, commit 72ab9a7524aef8c9d1c1fd989f8b0e83826dc916](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/72ab9a7524aef8c9d1c1fd989f8b0e83826dc916/research/hm/higher-symmetric-powers/PROOF.md) identifies S_2=E⊗Sym²W and N_2=E⊗Sym⁴W, with dim E=5. This is the actual universal multiplication quotient: the action of S_2 on w² has image E⊗w²Sym²W. Also N_1=S_1⊗Sym²W.

All Hilbert functions below start in degree zero unless explicitly stated otherwise. Write U_w=S w²⊂N. Under evaluation R_d×N_(5-d)→C, the annihilator of U_w is exactly T_w/I. Indeed vanishing of evaluation against every multiple of w² is equivalent, by nondegeneracy and S-balance, to annihilating w². Consequently U_w is canonically D_5(G_w), not merely a vector space with its Hilbert function.

## 1. Two quotients recover a length-58 algebra, with an exact six-dimensional kernel

Let x,y be a basis of W. The cyclic submodules U_x,U_y each have Hilbert function (1,5,15,15,5,1). Their degree-zero and degree-one intersections are zero. In degree two their intersection is

E⊗(x²Sym²W ∩ y²Sym²W)=E⊗C x²y²,

of dimension five. In degrees three, four and five each cyclic submodule equals N, since their respective dimensions are 15,5,1, the dimensions of N in those degrees. Thus

HF(U_x+U_y)=(2,10,25,15,5,1),
HF(U_x∩U_y)=(0,0,5,15,5,1).

More strongly N/(U_x+U_y) is isomorphic to S/m², generated by the class xy. Its degree-zero part is one-dimensional, its degree-one part is freely S_1 times that class, and its degree-two part is zero; degree-zero generation proves the assertion in every higher degree. This establishes the S-module identification, not only equality of Hilbert functions.

Put J_i=T_i/I inside R. Dualizing the preceding sum and intersection gives

J_x∩J_y ≅ D_5(S/m²), supported in degrees 4,5 with dimensions 5,1;
HF(S/(T_x+T_y))=(1,5,15,5);
HF(S/(T_x∩T_y))=(1,5,15,25,10,2).

The canonical two-ideal exact sequence identifies the latter algebra as

B_xy := G_x ×_{S/(T_x+T_y)} G_y = S/(T_x∩T_y).

Its length is 58; the common quotient has length 26. The map R→B_xy has the explicit length-six square-zero kernel D_5(S/m²).

## 2. Three distinct directions recover R as an algebra

Let w_1,w_2,w_3 represent three distinct points of P(W). Their squares form a basis of Sym²W: this is the elementary nondegeneracy of three distinct points on the binary Veronese conic. Since N is generated in degree zero,

U_1+U_2+U_3=N.

Therefore

T_1∩T_2∩T_3=Ann_S(N)=I.

The last equality also follows from N=D_5(R), since a finite module and its dual have the same annihilator. In particular the diagonal map R→G_1×G_2×G_3 is injective. Two distinct directions never suffice: their common kernel has length six by Section 1. Thus three is the exact minimum among distinct pure-square quotients.

There is a precise iterated fiber-product reconstruction, including its gluing quotient. Put A=T_1∩T_2 and C=S/(A+T_3). Then

R ≅ (G_1 ×_{S/(T_1+T_2)} G_2) ×_C G_3.

All maps are the canonical quotient maps from S. Inside R, A/I has Hilbert function supported in degrees 4,5 with dimensions 5,1; T_3/I has Hilbert function (0,0,0,10,10,2). Their intersection is zero. Their sum consequently has Hilbert function (0,0,0,10,15,3), so

HF(C)=(1,5,15,15), length(C)=36.

The image of A in G_3 is all of (G_3)_4⊕(G_3)_5, hence exactly m⁴G_3. Thus the gluing quotient is canonically C=G_3/m⁴G_3. This description determines the required map from B_12 as well: it is induced from S→S/(A+T_3).

## 3. Pairwise compatibility alone leaves five spurious cubic parameters

Define the algebra of pairwise-compatible triples

P={ (g_1,g_2,g_3) in G_1×G_2×G_3 : g_i=g_j in S/(T_i+T_j) for every i<j }.

There is an injective algebra map R→P. It is not surjective. To compute the discrepancy, use U_i=D_5(G_i). Dualizing each graded piece identifies P_d with functionals on ⊕_i(U_i)_(5-d) that vanish on all pairwise relations (u,-u,0), and their permutations, for u in U_i∩U_j. The image of R_d consists of functionals vanishing on the entire kernel of the summation map ⊕U_i→N.

In N-degrees zero and one, the three square lines, respectively their tensors with S_1, are independent and exhaust N. In N-degrees three through five, all U_i equal N, and all relations are generated by pairwise differences. Only N-degree two can contribute a discrepancy.

Here write L_i=w_i²Sym²W⊂Sym⁴W. Each L_i has dimension three, their sum has dimension five, each pairwise intersection has dimension one, and their triple intersection is zero. These facts follow from divisibility by the distinct linear forms w_i. The full relation space ker(⊕L_i→Sym⁴W) has dimension four. The three pairwise relation lines are independent: a dependence would force its components into L_1∩L_2∩L_3=0. Thus their span has dimension three and leaves exactly one additional relation. Tensoring with E leaves five additional relations. Therefore

HF(P)=(1,5,15,30,15,3), length(P)=69,

and there is an exact sequence of graded S-modules

0 → R → P → k^5(-3) → 0, where k=S/m is the ground field.

Here k^5(-3) denotes five copies of the residue field placed in degree three; no multiplicative quotient algebra P/R is asserted, since R is a unital subalgebra, not an ideal. The positive-degree ideal of S kills this module cokernel by its degree support.

## 4. Explicit five-component compatibility test

Rescale representatives of the three directions so that w_1=x, w_2=y, w_3=x+y. Such rescaling does not change any T_i. In ⊕L_i the relation

(-x²y(x+y), -xy²(x+y), xy(x+y)²)

has sum zero. It is not a sum of pairwise relations: the third component of a sum of pairwise relations is a linear combination of (x+y)²x² and (x+y)²y², whereas its third component here is (x+y)²xy. These are independent after division by (x+y)². Thus this relation spans the missing one-dimensional quotient of relation spaces.

A degree-three element g_i∈(G_i)_3 is naturally a functional f_i on (U_i)_2=E⊗L_i, by the pairing with the generator w_i². A pairwise-compatible triple belongs to the image of R_3 if and only if, for every e∈E,

-f_1(e⊗x²y(x+y)) - f_2(e⊗xy²(x+y)) + f_3(e⊗xy(x+y)²)=0.

These are exactly five independent linear conditions. Together with pairwise compatibility they give a complete membership test for the image R⊂G_1×G_2×G_3. No additional conditions occur in other degrees. For inhomogeneous tuples, apply this test to their homogeneous degree-three components.

This test and the iterated fiber product are equivalent exact reconstructions. The former exposes the obstruction to naive pairwise gluing; the latter packages the correction as an ordinary quotient-algebra gluing map.

## 5. Contribution and distinction from column quotients

[PR512, commit 26811b53c198879d0c55a2fb9ba75187bee38d9c](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/26811b53c198879d0c55a2fb9ba75187bee38d9c/research/hm/column-family-intersections/PROOF.md) proves that three distinct column ideals intersect in m³, so even the entire column family only reconstructs S/m³ as a common-image quotient. In contrast the three compressed quintic annihilators here intersect in I, reconstructing all of R, including its degrees three through five. This is a change of quotients, not a recovery claim for the original column family.

The reconstruction uses the quotients as S-algebras with their canonical maps. Three unrelated abstract length-42 Gorenstein algebras, or three Hilbert functions alone, do not specify these gluing maps or recover R. This work is an algebraic consequence of the pinned first-order module structure; it makes no new claim about higher-order deformation obstructions, algebraicity, or the unresolved Betti parameters. The proof uses universal binary multiplication and exact module duality, not numerical rank tests or selected actual HM coefficients.
