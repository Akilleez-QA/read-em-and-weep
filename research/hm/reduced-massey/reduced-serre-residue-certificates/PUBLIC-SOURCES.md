# Public sources

- [Luigi Lunardon, Some remarks on Dupont contraction](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1807.02517). Definition 2.1; Construction 3.6; Theorem 3.8; Remark 3.9: polynomial simplex contraction, naturality and signs.
- [Fiorenza–Iacono–Martinengo, Differential graded Lie algebras controlling infinitesimal deformations of coherent sheaves](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/31821). §3: Thom–Whitney integration, extension and semicosimplicial homotopy.
- [Stacks Project, Duality for proper schemes over fields](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0FVU). Remarks 48.27.2 and 48.27.6: perfect cup/evaluation pairing.
- [Stacks Project, Cohomology of projective space](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01XS). Lemma 30.8.1: Laurent standard-cover cohomology; integral coefficient residue.
