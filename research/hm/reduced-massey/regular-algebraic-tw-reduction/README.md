# Construct the algebraic TW reduction

A full regular algebraic orbit-reduced representative with determinant-normalized local Atiyah lifts, scalar primitive T=K+WhI, the exact elimination identity, genuine multiplicative comparison and a finite-expression statement.

## Reused context

The precise TW model, the opening definition of I,W,K, and the multiplicative comparison already appear in the ordinary-cubic context. They are retained for a self-contained proof and are not new contributions. The algebraic local lifts, T construction, full reduced identity and relative effectiveness statement are additional.
