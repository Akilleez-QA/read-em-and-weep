# A first-polar nonannihilation forces a nonzero Type I P row

## Result and exact scope

For every Type I source126 constant inclusion in the pinned normal form, the joint radical P-row map

    R_P:J→Hom_G(2P,P)

is NONZERO. This conclusion uses the actual constant inclusion and its balanced degree-twenty-eight block; it needs no Q-compatibility hypothesis and no selected instance evaluation.

Combined with the preceding joint-annihilator determinant gate, hypothetical full-J compatibility with the canonical invariant Q forces rank R_P=1 exactly. The rank-zero 36→32 annihilator reduction is therefore impossible. Only the rank-one 32→28 reduction remains under that hypothesis. This does not yet exclude full-J compatibility: it supplies no rank-two determinant theorem. It does not exclude Type I itself, any source-dual pair, or any frontier label.

G is the binary icosahedral group with W its defining symplectic two-space; A=Sym²W, S=Sym³W, D=Sym⁴W, T=Sym⁵W. The relevant decompositions are

    Sym⁴W=D, Sym⁷W=Wp+T, Sym⁸W=P+D, Sym⁹W=S+T,
    D⊗W=S+T.

In particular scalar invariants in degrees4 and8 vanish. All one-dimensional G representations are trivial. The Type I constant configuration is

    F=F_P⊕F_D=(P O(-2))⊕(2D O(-2)) ⊂ C O,
    C=A+V+P+D, C O/F=O(28),
    Y=W O(-9), span Y=A+V+D.

The radical J has dimension two, and every j comes from a constant map whose P coefficient is zero, with j(Y)⊂F. Its restriction J→Hom_G(Y,F) is injective because Y spans the support A+V+D. Natural equivariant Serre duality identifies its P bundle component with the P row in R_P.

## 2. The degree-eight D system has no linear syzygies

Choose the unique nonzero D-valued homogeneous degree-eight covariant r(w), up to scalar. This is a formal covariant, not a selected point or an HM coefficient. Its coefficient map D*→Sym⁸W identifies the unique D constituent. Multiplication gives

    μ:D*⊗W*→Sym⁹W.

Both source and target decompose as S+T. We prove that BOTH channels are nonzero, rather than infer this from equal characters.

Here is a polynomial proof that avoids any unproved channel identification. Identify D*=Sym⁴W* and use formal coordinates x,y. Let f_i=x^(4−i)y^i and let r_i be its image under the injective coefficient map D*→Sym⁸W*. The highest-weight tensor x⊗x⁴ lies in the T=Sym⁵W* summand, and its image x r_0 is nonzero. Thus the T channel does not vanish.

The S=Sym³W* summand is generated by the four Koszul tensors y⊗f_i−x⊗f_(i+1), for i=0,1,2,3. If the S channel vanished, we would have y r_i=x r_(i+1) for every i. Divisibility in C[x,y] then forces r_i=h x^(4−i)y^i with a single homogeneous polynomial h of degree4. In other words, the coefficient embedding is multiplication by h. Equivariance forces h to be G-invariant: for every g and every quartic f, equivariance says h(gf)=(gh)(gf), and cancellation gives gh=h. But no scalar invariant of degree4 exists. Thus the S channel is also nonzero.

Schur's lemma now makes μ an isomorphism. Equivalently, if a linear polynomial covector ℓ(w) on D satisfies ℓ(w)r(w)=0, then ℓ=0. This is an exact universal no-linear-syzygy theorem for this D system.

## 3. No quadratic covector kills its first polar

The first partial derivatives of r define a nonzero equivariant map

    dr:W O(-7)→D O.

The equivariant Hom space has dimension one: it is the invariant part of D⊗W⊗Sym⁷W, and D⊗W=S+T whereas Sym⁷W=Wp+T. Thus, after twisting by -2, dr spans the unique space Hom_G(Y,D O(-2)). Denote a nonzero generator by φ.

Let p(w):D→U be ANY homogeneous quadratic polynomial matrix into a constant vector space U, and suppose p(w)dr(w)=0 identically. Euler's identity gives p(w)r(w)=0. Differentiating the latter gives

    (∂p/∂w_i)r+p(∂r/∂w_i)=0.

The second term vanishes by hypothesis. Every row of ∂p/∂w_i is a linear syzygy of r, hence zero by Section2. Characteristic zero then makes p constant, and its homogeneous degree2 forces p=0. Thus composition with φ is injective on quadratic polynomial covectors, including equivariant maps D O(-2)→P O.

This argument does not assert that arbitrary first polars have this property: the no-linear-syzygy statement in Section2 is essential.

## 4. A zero joint P row contradicts the balanced top

Suppose R_P=0. Then every j:Y→F factors through F_D=2D O(-2). The dimension of Hom_G(Y,F_D) is two by Section3. Since J has dimension two and restriction is injective, its restrictions fill this entire Hom space.

Let p:F_D→P O be the P constant projection of the given inclusion F_D⊂C O. Every constant j has zero P coefficient, so p∘j|Y=0. Because these restrictions fill the Hom space, p kills the map φ into each D copy separately. Section3 forces each component of p to vanish. Therefore

    F_D ⊂ (A+V+D) O=:H0 O.

F_D is saturated in C O: it is a direct summand of the subbundle F and C O/F is locally free. It is consequently saturated in H0 O as well. Its rank is10 and degree−20, so H0 O/F_D is a line bundle O(20), with its standard G linearization. Indeed determinant characters are trivial, since G has no nontrivial one-dimensional character.

The quotient q:C O→O(28) kills F_D, so its restriction to H0 O factors through an equivariant map

    O(20)→O(28).

This map is nonzero: otherwise H0 O⊂F, impossible because H0(F)=0 while H0 is a nonzero constant space. But its space of equivariant maps is H0(O(8))^G=0. This contradiction proves R_P≠0.

## 5. Conditional sharper reduction and limits

The prior joint-annihilator theorem proves rank R_P≤1 under J⊂D_Q, using the actual second Bockstein. Combining it with Section4 gives rank R_P=1. Its only surviving common-annihilator spaces are

    U=A+2V+2P+3D, dimension32;
    Z=2V+3P+2D, dimension28.

The induced quotient Q is an isomorphism on P+D, and the prior explicit filtered contraction therefore retains exactly U→Z for Q+tT_a. No selected value of a is taken. The reduced Q still has kernel A+D and cokernel P and retains the nonzero second symbol.

The remaining task is to decide whether rank-one joint P action can actually be Q-compatible, or to prove rank R_P=2 on the appropriate actual top. Nothing here supplies that determinant nonvanishing. The six preliminary Type II footprint alternatives are a separate unfinished analysis; none is used in this theorem.
