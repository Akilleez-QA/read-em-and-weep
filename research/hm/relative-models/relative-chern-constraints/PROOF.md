# Global relative Chern constraints and exceptional corrections

## Result and scope

Over C let X=P²_z×P²_h, s:X→P²_h, and m:X→P⁴ be multiplication of binary quadratics. Put A=m*End₀(HM), K=A⊗O(−3,−3), E=R¹s_*K, T=R²s_*K. Write y=c₁(O_P²_h(1)). Then E is a globally locally free sheaf of rank 12, including across the unstable-plane parameters, whereas T is torsion supported exactly on those parameters. There is an exact integral K-class identity

    [E]−[T] = −18[O]−21[O(−1)]+51[O(−2)],

and hence

    ch(E)−ch(T)=12−81y+(183/2)y²,
    χ(E(t))−χ(T(t))=6t²−63t−18.

These are global constraints on the recipient sheaves. They neither evaluate any specified obstruction class nor give a thirty-to-twelve reduction.

## Sources and imported inputs

The current source is https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/pull/1#issuecomment-6260407782, created and last updated 2026-10-19T05:31:48Z. Its relative formulation and total H²(K) dimension 32+30=62 are used below, the latter explicitly as an imported cohomology input.

The normalized Chern classes c₁(HM)=−H and c₂(HM)=4H², and the exhaustive stable/unstable plane classification, are from Ada Boralevi, *The Horrocks–Mumford bundle restricted to planes*, https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0602002, Corollary 2.2. The previously checked unstable-plane calculation gives

    h⁰(End₀ E_plane)=3,
    h⁰(End₀ E_plane(−3))=0.

For stable planes these dimensions are both zero. One proof in the unstable case uses 0→O→E_plane→I_Z(−1)→0, with H⁰(E_plane(t))=0 for t<0: a map E_plane→E_plane(−3) kills O and factors through I_Z(−1), giving H⁰(E_plane(−2))=0. No entries of a HM monad are used. The stable-plane parameter set is nonempty and dense; this follows from the prior degree-nine family versus degree-25 unstable-surface argument. The exceptional set is not assumed finite or nonempty.

## 1. Why the rank-12 sheaf extends locally freely

Set D=R¹s_*A. Serre duality on each plane and the negative-endomorphism vanishing above give H²(A_h)=0 for every h. Thus R²s_*A=0, and Rs_*A is locally represented by a two-term complex of vector bundles in degrees 0 and 1. This is the standard perfect-complex/base-change description for a vector bundle on a smooth proper relative surface, with the top term removed using fiber H²-vanishing.

Furthermore s_*A is torsion-free: multiplication by a nonzero base regular function is injective on A and hence on its direct image. It vanishes generically because stable plane bundles are simple, so s_*A=0. Consequently

    Rs_*A ≅ D[−1],

and D has local projective dimension at most one. It need not be torsion-free; no such assumption is made.

The trace pairing gives A≅A∨. Relative duality for s yields

    Rs_*(A⊗ω_s) ≅ RHom(Rs_*A,O)[−2]
                      ≅ RHom(D,O)[−1].

Therefore, globally,

    E = D∨⊗O(−3),
    T = Ext¹(D,O)⊗O(−3).

At a local regular ring of dimension at most two, a presentation 0→F₀→F₁→D→0 gives 0→D∨→F₁∨→F₀∨→Ext¹(D,O)→0. Thus D∨ is a second syzygy; the depth lemma and Auslander–Buchsbaum imply it is free. Equivalently, duals of coherent sheaves on a smooth surface are reflexive and therefore locally free. This proves the claim for E. Its generic rank is 12, obtained from the plane Euler characteristic.

Top-degree cohomology and base change gives T⊗k(h)≅H²(K_h). This is zero at stable parameters and three-dimensional at every unstable parameter. It determines support and number of generators, but not length or divisorial multiplicity.

## 2. GRR computation, including normalization and signs

For a rank-two bundle with formal roots a,b, End₀ has character 1+exp(a−b)+exp(b−a). Since (a−b)²=c₁²−4c₂=−15H²,

    ch(End₀ HM)=3−15H²+(75/4)H⁴.

Let x be the hyperplane class from P²_z. Then m*H=x+y, x³=y³=0, td(T_s)=1+(3/2)x+x², and

    ch(Rs_*K)= coefficient of x² in
      [3−15(x+y)²+(75/4)(x+y)⁴]
      exp(−3x−3y)[1+(3/2)x+x²].

Since exp(−3x)td(T_s)=1−(3/2)x+x², the coefficient before multiplying exp(−3y) is

    −12+45y+(195/2)y².

Multiplying exp(−3y) gives

    ch(Rs_*K)=−12+81y−(183/2)y².

Because R⁰s_*K=0, this equals −ch(E)+ch(T), establishing the result. R⁰s_*K=0 follows also directly from H⁰(End₀ E_plane(−3))=0 on every fiber.

The displayed integral K-class has this character. Since K₀(P²) is freely generated by O,O(−1),O(−2), with injective rational Chern character and no torsion, it is an integral equality, not just a numerical approximation.

As additional checks,

    ch(D)=12+45y−(195/2)y²,
    ch(E(3))−ch(T(3))=12−45y−(195/2)y².

The globally extended rank-12 V is E(3)=D∨. The source's End-based V on the stable open agrees with this End₀-based bundle because R¹s_*ω_s=0. Globally the End-based top direct image would additionally contain the scalar O; that scalar must not be inserted into T here.

## 3. Finite and divisorial corrections are different

Write ch(T)=d y+e y². Here d is the effective degree of the one-dimensional support cycle, weighted by generic module lengths; d≥0. No positivity is asserted for e. Then

    rank(E)=12,
    c₁(E)=(d−81)y,
    ch₂(E)=(183/2+e)y²,
    c₂(E)=((d−81)²/2−183/2−e)y²,
    χ(E)=χ(T)−18,      χ(T)=(3/2)d+e.

If the exceptional support is finite, let ℓ=length(T). Then d=0, e=ℓ and

    c₁(E)=−81y,       c₂(E)=(3189−ℓ)y²,
    c₁(V)=−45y,       c₂(V)=(1110−ℓ)y²,
    χ(E)=ℓ−18,        χ(V)=ℓ−153.

If there are n distinct exceptional points, ℓ≥3n, because each local module has three-dimensional residue fiber. Equality and actual ℓ are not determined by those fibers. These formulas do not themselves impose an upper bound on ℓ: no stability or Chern-class positivity theorem for E or V has been established.

If exceptional curves occur, d can be positive, determinant degrees change, and χ(T) is not a length. Replacing T by a finite skyscraper correction in this case is incorrect.

## 4. Exact base-change defect despite local freeness

At a base point h, the truncation triangle E[−1]→Rs_*K→T[−2], after derived restriction to k(h), gives

    0 → Tor₂(T,k(h)) → E⊗k(h) → H¹(K_h)
      → Tor₁(T,k(h)) → 0,
    T⊗k(h) ≅ H²(K_h).

The initial zero uses H⁰(K_h)=0. Consequently, at an unstable parameter,

    dim Tor₁(T,k(h)) − dim Tor₂(T,k(h)) = 3.

Thus E is a rank-12 vector bundle even though H¹(K_h) has dimension 15 there. If T has finite nonzero support at h, it has projective dimension two over the regular local surface ring, so Tor₂ is nonzero; the E-fiber-to-cohomology map has both a nonzero kernel and a nonzero cokernel. At the generic point of an exceptional divisor the local ring is a DVR, Tor₂ vanishes, and the cokernel dimension is three over the residue field. For finite support the injection also bounds dim Tor₂(T,k(h))≤12; over a regular local surface this is the socle dimension of T_h. Thus each finite local exceptional module necessarily has three generators, socle dimension at most 12, and first Betti number equal to three plus its socle dimension. These statements still do not determine the torsion lengths or the derived extension class.

## 5. Global Euler and gluing consequences

Let r be the rank of the single potentially relevant Leray differential

    d₂:H⁰(T)→H²(E).

Since T has support dimension at most one, the complete relevant exact sequence is

    0→H¹(E)→H²(K)→H⁰(T)→H²(E)→H³(K)→H¹(T)→0,

and H¹(K)≅H⁰(E). Also H⁰(K)=0 and H⁴(K)=0. The latter follows from Serre duality on X and H⁰(A)=H⁰(s_*A)=0. GRR gives χ(K)=18, hence, using the source's imported h²(K)=62,

    h¹(K)+h³(K)=44,
    h¹(E)+h⁰(T)−r=62,
    h⁰(E)+h²(E)−r+h¹(T)=44.

In particular, the total recipient's non-middle cohomology cannot vanish in both degrees 1 and 3. This does not determine either dimension separately.

For finite support, h⁰(T)=ℓ and h¹(T)=0, so

    h¹(E)=62−ℓ+r,
    h⁰(E)+h²(E)−r=44,
    0≤r≤min(ℓ,h²(E)).

If the exceptional set is empty, these specialize to h¹(E)=62 and h⁰(E)+h²(E)=44. Thus there is no inference from rank 12 to cohomology dimension 12, even in the absence of exceptional support.

The exceptional contribution to H²(K) remains ker(d₂), not automatically H⁰(T). The K-class does not determine d₂. Block exchange swaps the projections, so these s-relative identities cannot be projected termwise into block-even, block-odd, or the retained thirty-dimensional summand. All cohomology counts here refer to the full 62-dimensional H²(K), unless explicitly labeled otherwise.

No actual obstruction values, physical source inputs, matrices, jets, determinants, or detectors were evaluated.
