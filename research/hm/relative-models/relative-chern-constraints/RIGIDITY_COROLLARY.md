# Global section and cohomology consequences of the pure-boundary rigidity input

Use the notation and all hypotheses of PROOF.md: A=m*End0(HM), K=A⊗O(-3,-3), D=R1s_*A, V=D*, E=V(-3), and T=R2s_*K. Import the weight-two Ext1 vanishing and finite-pushdown identification explicitly stated in https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/pull/1#issuecomment-6260553958 . These are imported certified cohomology inputs, not physical evaluations performed here.

The weight-two input gives H1(X,End(m*HM)⊗O(-3,0))=0. Its tracefree summand therefore vanishes too. The relative duality calculation in PROOF.md gives R0s_*(A⊗O(-3,0))=0 and R1s_*(A⊗O(-3,0))=V. The degree-one Leray edge is an isomorphism, so H0(V)=0. Consequently H0(E)=0: multiplication by any nonzero cubic gives an injective sheaf map E→E(3)=V, since E is locally free on the integral base.

As proved in PROOF.md, H1(K)=H0(E), H0(K)=H4(K)=0, chi(K)=18, and the imported total h2(K)=62. Thus

    h1(K)=0,       h3(K)=44.

Serre duality on X, together with the trace pairing A*=A, identifies H3(K)*=H1(A). Since s_*A=0 and Rs_*A=D[-1], H1(A)=H0(D). Therefore

    h0(D)=44,      h0(D*)=0.

This does not assert that D is generated by its global sections, nor that a restriction or Kodaira–Spencer map has any specified rank. D may have torsion and is not silently identified with its double dual.

If T has finite support of length ell and r is the rank of d2:H0(T)→H2(E), the prior exact sequence now gives the sharper identities

    h1(E)=62−ell+r,       h2(E)=44+r.

In particular h2(E)≥44. For V=E(3), the finite-support Riemann–Roch identity chi(V)=ell−153 and H0(V)=0 give

    h1(V)=h2(V)+153−ell ≥153−ell.

The lower bound may be vacuous when ell is large. None of these dimensions determines the actual retained class, d2, ell, or exceptional-locus nonemptiness. If exceptional curves occur, use the divisorial formulas and h1(T) term from PROOF.md rather than these finite-length specializations.

These deductions resolve the individual non-middle cohomology dimensions previously known only through h1(K)+h3(K)=44. They are statements about the full recipient, and cannot be projected termwise to the block-even retained thirty-space because block exchange interchanges the two projections. No actual source operation, matrix, input, jet, residue or detector value is evaluated.
