# Independent audit

Status: PASS, Sections 1–5.

The twisted nilpotent filtration has factors L,L(1),L(2), so the trivial determinant on P¹ forces L=O(−1). Its inclusion into the trivial three-bundle is a linear map of parameter spaces with no projective zero; thus it is injective. After normalizing the kernel vector to (s,t,0), the polynomial syzygy forces the first columns to be ta and −sa. The argument excluding a from the kernel-image plane is valid: otherwise a is a fixed eigenvector with eigenvalue a1t−a2s, which nilpotency forces identically zero.

With a as third basis vector, trace and quadratic trace give C=0 and tA−sB=0. Therefore A=αs and B=αt; rank two excludes α=0. The simultaneous basis change and parameter change normalize α over C and preserve conjugacy of the subspace. The canonical form has rank two and nilpotency index three at every nonzero parameter. The stated kernel and kernel-square planes, and their intrinsic line/plane descriptions, follow directly.

For a hypothetical three-dimensional all-regular-nilpotent space, the same filtration on P² gives c(E)=1−h², contradicting triviality. Thus the dimension bound is correct. Conjugacy of individual regular nilpotents gives extension of every one-dimensional such space to a canonical pencil.

The final example establishes only an abstract fixed-three-plane obstruction: its spectral open is empty while its regular-nilpotent candidate locus is nonempty. It proves neither realization as an actual kernel of the original operation nor exclusion of degenerations with varying three-plane. These limitations are stated correctly.

This audit used universal algebra only. No actual source or operation instance was evaluated. No corrections remain.
