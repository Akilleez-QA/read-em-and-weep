# Exceptional fiber cohomology: alternative proof and review

The full dimension theorem is already public in [PR116's exceptional-surface result](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/e906fb83ca5ae4f5c75e7ca7a61863b984f15a0d/research/hm/null-factor-surfaces/exceptional-surface-0514/RESULT.md). This supplement preserves a second complete proof, using extension of Hom maps across codimension two, and the historical independent review of that proof and its geometric sources.

## Exact contribution and overlap

- The total triple (4,128,4), deck triples, retained both-odd/swap-odd triple (0,15,3), and top-direct-image support statement are already proved in PR116, Sections 1–7. They are not claimed as new results.
- The alternative Hom argument obtains Hom(I_Z(−1),E(t))=H⁰(E(t+1)) directly by codimension-two extension, rather than by comparing the two scalar actions on a nontrivial extension class.
- The two unstable resolution types are explicitly linked to Boralevi's Corollary 2.2. The historical source review checks the exact pushforward, generic stable locus and signs.
- [PR247](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/01c96a7e1b24685eacd5d1dc52ed0ea026490146/research/hm/relative-models/relative-chern-constraints/PROOF.md), Sections 1 and 4, already incorporates the retained three-dimensional top fiber, the 15-dimensional degree-one fiber cohomology, and the distinction from a globally locally free rank-12 direct image.
