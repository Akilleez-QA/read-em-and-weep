Status: PASS, Sections 1–4. No corrections remain; this audit is frozen.

The recovered tautological three-plane has line quotients with Chern roots −h,−t,h−2t. Independent expansion of its inverse Chern class gives c2(Q)=h²−2ht+7t² and c3(Q)=5h²t−10ht²+15t³. Since Q has rank two, the claimed rational operational relation follows.

On an integral nilpotent threefold, t cannot be numerically zero: it is one third of the pulled-back ample Grassmannian class, so numerical triviality would force the projection to be constant. Every fiber has dimension at most one by the imported exclusion of a projective surface consisting entirely of regular nilpotents. If h=lambda t numerically, nefness gives lambda≥0 and ampleness of 2h+t gives positive t³. Substitution in the Chern identity contradicts ((lambda−1)²+2)t³>0. Thus h and t are numerically independent without any assumed nefness of 2t−h.

The tensor-line transition gives R=L⊗M² on the nilpotent incidence, hence r=h+2t and H=3t. The universal spectral endomorphism has constant rank two on reduced X; the invertible-minor argument therefore gives its kernel as a genuine line subbundle. The claimed extension of h is valid. On a four-dimensional integral spectral component, q must have a nonempty Cartier zero divisor because an identically zero q puts the component inside N and an everywhere-nonzero q forces dimension at most two. Each boundary component is a nilpotent threefold. Restriction of r,H to it is independent, proving the stated image-rank and Picard-rank obstructions. Numerical triviality restricts along a closed subvariety because its curves are also curves in the ambient component.

For the final intersection refinement, the Grassmannian image of a nilpotent threefold has dimension at least two, since all fibers have dimension at most one. Thus the semiample class t has positive intersection t²A with any ample class A; take A=2h+t to obtain 2b+c>0. If b=0, nonnegativity and a=2b−3c force a=c=0, a contradiction. This verifies b>0. Image dimension three gives c>0 by generic finiteness; image dimension two gives c=0. The Khovanskii–Teissier inequality is used in the correct direction and supplies no further contradiction here.

These are necessary constraints on hypothetical extremal components, not an exclusion of those components or an improved total dimension bound. No actual operation matrix, input, value, or jet was evaluated.
