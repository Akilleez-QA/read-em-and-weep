The universal twist is M=O_P(E)(1)⊗det S. Thus q and h are sections of M² and M³. On a projective integral spectral subvariety avoiding q=0, q trivializes M². The square-root cover is finite étale and supplies a nowhere-zero eigenvalue lambda. On an integral component dominating the base, normalization of A by lambda gives B³=B and the three polynomial eigenprojectors. These are line subbundles even over a singular base, by their idempotent splitting and constant fiber ranks.

The eigenline duals are globally generated, hence nef. The projection to G is proper and quasi-finite because any positive-dimensional projective fiber would carry the ample restriction O(1) as a torsion line bundle, impossible. It is consequently finite, and its pulled-back Plücker class H is ample. The quotient of the trivial five-bundle by the split tautological rank-three bundle has rank two. Its third Chern class is exactly the complete homogeneous polynomial h3 in the three nef eigenline-dual classes. Intersecting with H^(d−3) gives a sum of nonnegative terms; their vanishing contradicts H^d>0 for d≥3. Hence every nilpotent-free projective spectral family has dimension at most two.

For an integral component of the closed reduced spectral incidence, q is either identically zero, nowhere zero, or has a nonempty effective Cartier zero divisor. The first case is in the three-dimensional nilpotent incidence, the second has dimension at most two, and the third has dimension at most four because its divisor is contained in the nilpotent incidence. This proves dim X≤4 across all kernel rank jumps.

Hence D2 is contained in the proper image of X and has dimension at most four.
