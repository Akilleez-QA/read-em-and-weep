This contains the full (2,2) weight selection and regular commutator proof, its complete mathematical review, and the scoped transported-remainder corollary. The same-type square observation is included; the earlier filtration, cubic theorem and weight-ideal geometry are referenced rather than republished.

Read PROOF.md, REVIEW.md and DEPENDENCIES.md.

The companion equivariant splitting argument is an alternative proof, not a prerequisite: the committed cubic construction already supplies the coefficient complement.

These are mathematical proofs and historical mathematical reviews, not source-instance evaluations or proof-assistant certificates.
