# Root mathematical review

With L contained in A direct-sum B, the two off-diagonal sl2 images are B direct-sum A. The remaining diagonal image has intersection dimension exactly d=dim(L intersect (A intersect B)^2), giving dim(sl2 L)=s+4-d. The cases s>=6, s=5, s=4 are exhaustive. In the last case a value below6 forces A=B of dimension2 and L=A tensor W. Thus rank5 cannot occur for any four-plane, and the pinned no-full-fiber condition excludes the only lower-rank alternative.

The abstract rank-six example was checked directly: its first-coordinate-only and second-coordinate-only vectors have disjoint one-dimensional supports, so no full fiber is present. It establishes sharpness of the universal bound, not an actual HM example.

The incidence bound uses only the pinned rank<=6 locus of dimension at most2 and generic rank10. Combining the new pointwise lower bound gives dimension at most10 for the kernel incidence and hence primary-bad codimension at least4. General P3 avoidance and projection from a prescribed point then yield the two stated three-space existence results. Neither selects a parameter nor transfers goodness to the prescribed point.

Root and independent mathematical review are conditional on the exact actual exterior/no-full-fiber and generic-rank inputs, not formal certification. The result does not settle full adjoint quadratic invertibility, the residual map, lift boundary, Q4, or higher geometric conclusions.
