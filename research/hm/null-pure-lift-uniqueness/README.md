## Open boundary and corrections

Uniqueness is for a fixed corrected abstract ambient and fixed classical reduction. It is not a physical ambient identification, canonical Čech representative, parity theorem for an actual mixed defect or evaluation of any mixed coordinate. Distinguish total 32+30 from the further 2+30 decomposition inside the block-even space.
