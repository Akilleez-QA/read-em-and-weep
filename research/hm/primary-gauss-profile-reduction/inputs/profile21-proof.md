# The (2,1) profile has thirteen necessary quotient pairs

## Result and limits

For a hypothetical maximal rank-three Gauss plateau of profile (k,l)=(2,1), let

    K=E^-, M=K^-, A=E/K, J=K/M,
    L=(Q*)^-, B=Q*/L, a=deg A, b=deg B.

Then a>=0. The original negative-transpose injection A -> B*(-2) has determinant length t in {0,12}. There are at most thirteen ordered equivariant splitting pairs (A,B), listed below. They have 877 compatible formal (E,Q*) records, involving 22 of the 28 E types and 24 of the 37 Q* types.

If t=12, there are only two pairs:

    A=O(-8)+O(4)+O(6), or A=O(-8)+W O(5),
    B=O(-18)+O(-8)+O(6).

Both original cokernels are O(6) restricted to the reduced twelve-point orbit. Both have a=2, b=-20, and the separate second primal injection J -> A(-2) is an isomorphism. Its endpoint kernel M has rank eleven and degree -52.

These are necessary conditions, not realizability statements. Neither the (2,1) profile nor generic rank three is excluded. No SF or square-rank bound changes. This result does not assume the (1,2) saturation result, does not swap E with Q*, and does not assert that either endpoint has derivative rank three.

## 1. Orientation, degrees, and proved windows

The convention is Section 4 of ACTUAL-EQUIVARIANT-SPLITTING-AND-GAUSS-PERSISTENCE.md. The chain is F0=M, F1=K, F2=E, F3=E^+. Thus K and E are interior rank-three stages; M is an endpoint. In the reversed dual chain Q* is at index one, so L is an endpoint, not another rank-three interior stage. The actual ranks/degrees are

    (rank E,deg E)=(17,-54), (rank Q*,deg Q*)=(13,-54),
    rank K=14, rank M=11, rank L=10, rank A=rank B=rank J=3.

The actual E degrees lie between -10 and -2. Because k=2, dual section persistence gives H0(Q*(1))=0. The proved 37-type dual list in verify_adjacent_rank_three_gauss.py is therefore applicable: Q* degrees lie between -28 and -2. This list follows from the two actual identities H1(Q*(-1))=M3 and H1(Q*)=M2 with central parity; it does not require l=2. E's 28-type list is the one in the actual splitting note.

Strict top-slope decrease, proved in NEGATIVE-GAUSS-KERNELS-STRICTLY-LOWER-THE-TOP-SLOPE.md, gives

    maxdeg K,maxdeg L <= -3,
    maxdeg M,maxdeg L^- <= -4,
    maxdeg M^- <= -5.

All these kernels are saturated in the original constant ambient bundle. These slope bounds do not require a specified rank for an endpoint derivative.

Let T=coker(J -> A(-2)) and s=length T. Since K is interior, this is an equal-rank injection with torsion cokernel. Degree additivity gives

    deg J=a-6-s,
    deg M=-48-2a+s <= -44.

Hence a>=-2 and 0<=s<=4+2a. On the dual side, rank L=10 and top degree at most -3 give b>=-24. The original equal-rank negative-transpose injection gives

    t=-6-a-b>=0, so a<=18, b<=-4, t<=20.

All determinant degrees are even: these are center-even bundles and the binary icosahedral group is perfect. Every nonempty invariant effective divisor on its projective line has degree in the semigroup generated by 12,20,30 (ordinary orbits of size60 add no generator). Thus initially t is 0,12,20.

The individual ordinary degree windows are exhaustive:

    A: [-10,26], B: [-28,8].

A is a quotient of E, and embeds into Q(-2); B is a quotient of Q*, and embeds into E*(-2). Source global generation gives the lower bounds, and target splitting gives the upper bounds. The unequal actual source degrees are retained throughout.

## 2. Two primal Euler envelopes, including the endpoint

Write a character as genuine if all irreducible multiplicities are nonnegative. For any bundle F, write chi(F)=H0(F)-H1(F). The actual presentations give

    chi(E(2))=-V,
    chi(E(3))=U=Wprime+2T,
    chi(E(4))=M3 tensor Sym^3 W - M4 tensor Sym^4 W,
    chi(Q*(3))=T-2W-S.

First, twisting the two exact sequences for K,J,M,T by three and using H0(M(3))=0 gives

    chi(A(3))+chi(A(1))-chi(E(3))
      = H0(T(3))+H1(M(3)).                    (1)

This is genuine. Unlike a top-section-only estimate, it retains the negative H1(A(1)) terms. It is valid because K, not L, is the second rank-three interior stage in this profile.

Second, the derivative of M factors through J(-2). Since H0(M^-(4))=0, the induced map H0(M(4)) -> H0(J(2)) is injective. The injection J(2) -> A gives

    H0(M(4)) -> H0(A) injectively.

Let C be the genuine cokernel representation of this injection. The two Euler sequences now give the exact identity

    chi(A(4))+chi(A(2))+H0(A)-chi(E(4))
      = H0(T(4))+H1(M(4))+C.                  (2)

Thus (2) is genuine, even with nonzero s. No rank-three claim about the endpoint M, or about its next kernel, was used. In particular this argument is not an application of a rank-three interior theorem to an endpoint.

## 3. Other necessary filters and finite exhaustion

The derivative L -> B(-2) has kernel L^- regardless of its endpoint rank. Strict slope therefore gives H0(L(3)) -> H0(B(1)) injectively and the genuine envelope

    chi(B(3))+H0(B(1))-chi(Q*(3)).            (3)

The vanishings H0(K(2))=H0(L(2))=0 also give the genuine characters chi(A(2))-chi(E(2)) and chi(B(2))-chi(Q*(2)), and the injections H0(E(2)) -> H0(A(2)), H0(Q*(2)) -> H0(B(2)). For each candidate source F=E or Q*, the bundle quotient F -> C=A or B induces a surjection H1(F(r)) -> H1(C(r)). The verifier tests these latter inequalities for -2<=r<=8; this is a selected finite collection of necessary tests, not a claim that every possible cohomological constraint is tested.

Finally, every quotient block must receive a nonzero equivariant projection from its source, and every injected block must have a nonzero map into its target. These tests are applied to A from E into Q(-2), B from Q* into E*(-2), and the two transpose injections between A and B*(-2). The ordinary sorted degree inequality for the rank-three injection is imposed. If t=0, A=B*(-2) as equivariant bundles. None of the nonzero-Hom tests is treated as sufficient for generic fiber rank.

The only center-even rank-three splitting shapes are three even-degree trivial lines, one odd-degree W or Wprime block plus one even-degree trivial line, or one even-degree A_std or V block. This follows from the complete irreducible dimension list and central parity. Enumerating exactly these shapes in the proved windows, (1)-(2) and the twist-two test leave 340 primal shapes; (3) and its twist-two test leave 188 dual shapes. Testing the 28 by37 proven source types and the remaining conditions gives the thirteen pairs below. The resulting a>=0 and t!=20 conclusions are finite-character deductions, not assertions from the initial degree inequalities alone.

## 4. Full necessary pair list

Eleven pairs have t=0 and B=A*(-2). Their A are:

1. O(-10)+2O(6), a=2.
2. O(-8)+O(2)+O(6), a=0.
3. O(-8)+O(2)+O(16), a=10.
4. O(-8)+O(4)+O(6), a=2.
5. O(-8)+O(4)+O(16), a=12.
6. O(-8)+W O(5), a=2.
7. O(-8)+2O(6), a=4.
8. O(-8)+O(6)+O(16), a=14.
9. O(-8)+Wprime O(9), a=10.
10. Wprime O(-5)+O(16), a=6.
11. Wprime O(-1)+O(6), a=4.

The two t=12 pairs are those stated in the result. There are 837 records of defect zero and 40 of defect twelve. The original twelve-defect divisor is the reduced twelve-point orbit, with cokernel length one at each point, by invariance and determinant degree. This says nothing stronger about the separate second determinant until its own degree bound is used.

For every pair, the remaining second primal defect obeys s<=4+2a and the invariant-orbit condition. Therefore the necessary list is

    a=0 or2: s=0;
    a=4 or6: s=0,12;
    a=10 or12: s=0,12,20,24;
    a=14: s=0,12,20,24,30,32.

Here deg M=-48-2a+s. In particular both original t=12 branches have s=0 and deg M=-52, as claimed. No entry in these lists is asserted realizable.

## 5. The original twelve-defect line is pinned

Let D12 be the reduced twelve-point orbit. At each point the original cokernel has length one, so it is the pushforward of an equivariant line bundle on D12. The stabilizer in the binary group is cyclic of order ten, the inverse image of the projective order-five stabilizer. The original source and target are center-even. Thus its stabilizer character is one of the five even powers, represented by O(k)|D12 for k=0,2,4,6,8. The tautological fiber character generates the cyclic character group; changing its sign only permutes the list.

The invariant degree-twelve defining section gives an equivariant exact sequence

    0 -> O(k-12) -> O(k) -> O(k)|D12 -> 0.

Consequently the original quotient character at twist r must be

    chi(B*(-2)(r))-chi(A(r))
      = chi(O(k+r))-chi(O(k+r-12)).

For each of the two t=12 pairs, exact character comparison at r=0 and1 leaves uniquely k=6. Because the actual cokernel must be one of the five classified orbit lines, this pins it to O(6)|D12; no sufficiency inference from arbitrary Euler data is needed. The verifier additionally checks equality for -2<=r<=8. This original torsion line is not the second primal torsion T, which is zero in both these branches.

## Reproduction and provenance

Run python3 verify_two_one_primal_interior_quotients.py. Add --full to regenerate the separately named full record file. The default writes two-one-primal-interior-quotient-summary.json with all thirteen pairs, record counts, defect lists and dependency hashes. Its source-type enumeration is read from the existing abstract verifier, but its rank-three quotient enumeration and all filters are regenerated from the proved windows, not taken on trust from an existing (2,2) record file.

The preliminary constraints are adapted from TWO-TWO-SYMMETRIC-QUOTIENT-DEFECT-IS-ZERO-OR-TWELVE.md: its Section1 through Section3 only need k>=2, not l=2. Equation (1) uses the primal half of the second-interior calculation; equation (2) is the additional endpoint-top-slope envelope used here. The result is separate from PR674's general a>=-4 and original (1,2) saturation theorem; neither is used in this calculation.

All computations are bounded exact character arithmetic and integer degrees. There are no selected actual coefficients or points, actual-instance matrices or rank evaluations, actual-map scans, model spikes, or consumed experiments. Earlier files are unmodified. The geometric proofs justify the filters; character arithmetic does not substitute for those proofs.
