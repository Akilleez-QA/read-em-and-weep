# Independent mathematical verification

The polar matrix over the full local Artin ring has an invertible two-minor and vanishing three-minors, hence split rank two. The value at h and the value at u form a basis of the image, so the image line L is a direct summand. Consequently H, E, K and the symmetric beta are defined over the actual Artin base with no reduction shortcut.

Three distinct closed joint eigenvalue tuples can be separated by one scalar functional. Simple characteristic roots lift uniquely over the Artin ring; their pairwise differences are units. Polynomial interpolation and Cayley-Hamilton produce rank-one orthogonal projectors. On each eigenline chart the equations have coefficients rho_j-rho_i that are units, forcing all transverse coordinates to zero over arbitrary test rings. The eigenline scheme is exactly three copies of the base.

The cross ideals J_ij are invariantly generated by evaluation of beta on L_i tensor L_j against K*. Therefore the branch equations are exactly I_i=J_ij+J_ik. Their pairwise sums all equal Q. Any two labelled branch lifts over the same Artin test ring annihilate Q and yield the unique third lift. This does not prove lifts over A itself unless Q=0.

For the function-gluing step, pairwise sum equality alone is insufficient. Here J_12 is contained in I_1 intersect I_2, while I_3=J_13+J_23, so (I_1 intersect I_2)+I_3=Q. This kills the precise triple Chinese-remainder defect. The exact module sequence then gives length(B_A)=length(A/(intersection I_i))+2 length(A/Q). The scheme-theoretic union of the branch images and their disjoint union are correctly distinguished.

Linearization of the exact coefficient ideals yields the asserted branch tangent kernels. The cross coefficients vanish under both the metric functional and separating-operator functional identically, so their first derivatives land in the common kernel of two independent closed functionals. Their independence follows because a proportional functional would make the separating operator scalar. No first-order vanishing is promoted to full-Artin existence.

All conclusions concern the three-choice closed locus and the stated tensor hypotheses. No actual physical/source coefficients were evaluated.
