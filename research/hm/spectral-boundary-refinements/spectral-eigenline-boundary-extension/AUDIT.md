Status: PASS for Sections 1–3 and scope. No corrections remain.

The finite locally free cover algebra injects into its separable generic algebra over integral Y, proving reducedness even when the cover is reducible. Since lambda²=q/2 and q is a nonzerodivisor, lambda is a nonzerodivisor. Its zero scheme is Cartier with line bundle M. All three shifted endomorphisms have constant geometric rank two, so on this reduced cover their kernels are genuine line subbundles of the trivial five-bundle.

A local cyclic vector exists at every fiber because both the semisimple and the nilpotent endomorphisms are regular. Lifting and inverting its cyclic determinant gives the claimed local basis. The three eigenvectors are primitive in that basis and satisfy their eigenvalue equations. Their matrix is equivalent over the local ring to diag(1,lambda,lambda²): after the unit pivot, subtract half of the second column from the first in the displayed two-by-two block. No principal-ideal-domain assumption is used. Injectivity, the two torsion summands, and Fitting ideals (lambda³),(lambda),O follow exactly.

The determinant map has Cartier zero divisor 3D_lambda, giving det S=(L−⊗L0⊗L+)⊗M³. The twists and signs then give the sum of eigenline-dual Chern classes as H+3(r−H)=3r−2H. The eigenline duals are globally generated, so their sum is nef. Finite-surjective descent proves nefness on Y. On components inside N the same class is 3h, also nef. Testing curves on components proves the assertion on all reduced X.

The statements are local module and divisor identities on the eigenvalue cover. They do not imply a reduced boundary, a normal or smooth cover, or a numerical multiplicity for the separate rank-one component. No actual operation instance was evaluated.
