# Full K-theory correction for the eigenline collision

## Statement

Retain the eigenvalue cover Y' and notation of ../spectral-eigenline-boundary-extension/PROOF.md, for any integral spectral component not contained in the nilpotent locus. Thus lambda is a regular section of M, D=V(lambda) is Cartier, and L_-, L_0, L_+ are the extended eigenlines. No reducedness of D is needed. In the Grothendieck group of perfect complexes on Y',

    [S] = [L_-]+[L_+]+[L_0 tensor M]+[L_0 tensor M^2]-[L_0].

Put x_i=c1(L_i*), x=x_0, m=c1(M), and let s_j be the elementary symmetric polynomials in the three x_i. The following are operational Chow identities:

    m(x_--x)=m(x_+-x)=0,
    c1(S)=-s1+3m,
    c2(S)=s2-6mx+2m^2,
    c3(S)=-s3+3mx^2-2m^2x.

Since Q=(V tensor O)/S has rank two, they imply the boundary-corrected eigenline identity

    h3(x_-,x_0,x_+) = 30mx^2-35m^2x+15m^3.

Here h3 is the complete homogeneous polynomial of degree three. These statements hold in every dimension, in particular on a four-dimensional spectral component's cover. They do not exclude such a component: m is not known nef, so the right side has no established intersection sign.

## 1. A canonical filtration of the cokernel

Let C=coker(L_- direct-sum L_0 direct-sum L_+ -> S). The imported cyclic presentation, with local basis v,Av,A^2v and local parameter lambda, presents this cokernel by

    A^2v=0, lambda Av=0, lambda^2 v=0.

In particular lambda^2 C=0. Multiplication by the global section lambda of M is a map C tensor M^{-1} -> C. Let C_1 be its image. There is a canonical exact sequence

    0 -> C_1 -> C -> C/C_1 -> 0.

Both end terms are annihilated by the ideal of D. Write i:D -> Y'. Because all three eigenlines restrict to the same primitive line L=L_0|D in S_D, reduction modulo lambda gives

    C/C_1 = i_*(S_D/L).

The map S_D tensor M_D^{-1} -> C_1 has kernel (ker A_D^2) tensor M_D^{-1}: in the displayed presentation this kernel is generated by Av and A^2v. Thus

    C_1 = i_*((S_D/ker A_D^2) tensor M_D^{-1}).

All these identifications hold over the possibly nonreduced D. The same cyclic basis shows that A_D is a length-three regular nilpotent, with locally free kernel flag. Its successive quotients are

    L, L tensor M_D, L tensor M_D^2.

Indeed A_D induces isomorphisms from each successive quotient to its predecessor tensored by M_D; this can be checked directly in the cyclic basis. Therefore

    [C] = i_*(2[L tensor M_D]+[L tensor M_D^2]).

The pushforward notation here is legitimate in perfect K-theory: a vector bundle on an effective Cartier divisor is perfect on the ambient scheme, locally possessing a length-one locally free resolution. The displayed exact sequences therefore give identities of perfect classes.

## 2. Eliminating the divisor pushforwards

The bundles L tensor M_D^j extend as L_0 tensor M^j. The Cartier sequence gives

    [i_*(L tensor M_D^j)] = [L_0 tensor M^j]-[L_0 tensor M^{j-1}].

Consequently

    [C]=[L_0 tensor M]+[L_0 tensor M^2]-2[L_0].

The exact sequence defining C now proves the stated K-theory formula. This establishes a global correction, rather than only a local list of invariant factors or a determinant identity.

## 3. Chern classes and the rank-two quotient constraint

Multiplicativity of Chern classes in perfect K-theory first gives the full generating-series formula

    c_z(S) = ((1-x_- z)(1-x_+ z)(1+(m-x)z)(1+(2m-x)z))/(1-xz).

The eigenlines are canonically equal after restriction to D. Hence the Cartier intersection formula gives m(x_i-x)=0 for i=-,+. This is an operational identity, not just an equality after taking degrees: for every base change, intersect first with the Cartier class, then restrict the line-bundle class, which is zero on the resulting divisor. The same identity follows from the bivariant formula for a regular Cartier immersion.

Expanding the preceding generating series and using those two relations yields the three stated Chern classes. Alternatively, in every term containing m one may replace each x_i by x; the numerator then has the three nilpotent roots -x, m-x, 2m-x, while its m-free terms are the original elementary symmetric polynomials.

From c(S)c(Q)=1 and rank Q=2 one has

    0=c3(Q)=-c1(S)^3+2c1(S)c2(S)-c3(S).

Substitution yields

    0=h3(x_-,x_0,x_+)-30mx^2+35m^2x-15m^3,

as asserted. The lower quotient class is also explicitly

    c2(Q)=s1^2-s2-12mx+7m^2.

As a consistency check, on D the previous nilpotent notation is x=h and m=h-t. The new c3(Q) identity restricts to

    5t(h^2-2ht+3t^2)=0,

five times the imported nilpotent flag relation. No division by five in integral Chow groups is needed or asserted.

## 4. Scope and obstacle

These identities control all Chern classes of S on the eigenvalue cover using the extended eigenlines and the one boundary line bundle. They require no normalization, smoothness, transversality, or additional evaluation of any source tensor. They can be intersected with arbitrary Cartier classes on fourfold components. In contrast with the nilpotent-free proof, h3 of the nef eigenline classes is no longer zero: its explicit correction is supported on the Cartier boundary. Although 3r-2H=x_-+x_0+x_+ is nef, m=r-H has no proved nefness, and the alternating correction does not itself establish a dimension contradiction. A further numerical or geometric restriction on that boundary is needed to exclude the extremal branch.
