# The quadratic normal obstruction is nonzero at every line

For every nonzero u∈V, the linearized quadratic normal map

    Q_u:Sym²(V/Cu)→J/f(u,u,V)

is nonzero. Equivalently its rank is at least one at every geometric point of P(V), including the exceptional quadratic-basepoint locus.

Indeed the projectivized common zero scheme of Q_u is the fiber over [u] of the quadratic flag incidence B. Its reduced structure is identified with the corresponding kernel-line fiber of the global reduced nilpotent incidence N. The preceding proof bounds every such fiber by dimension two. If Q_u were identically zero, this fiber would be all P(V/Cu)≅P³, contradicting that bound. The argument is unchanged after algebraically closed field extension.

This is a universal nonvanishing statement, not a calculation of Q_u at any chosen input. It neither determines the generic rank nor asserts that Q_u has empty projective zero locus at every line.
