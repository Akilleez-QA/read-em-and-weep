# Independent audit

Status: PASS, Sections 1–4. No corrections remain.

The ordered independent eigenlines reconstruct both the three-plane and the projective tensor, and the positive eigenvalue is intrinsically a homomorphism from its tensor line to det W. Thus the eigenvalue-cover point is reconstructed without a choice of scale. The inverse is algebraic on the independent-triple open. On each integral cover component this proves birationality and the asserted open isomorphism. Every component dominates the integral base, so lambda remains a nonzero regular section and defines a Cartier divisor there.

The class B is the pullback of the ample Segre polarization under this generically degree-one morphism. It is nef with positive top self-intersection, hence big, and these properties descend along the finite dominant map to the original component. Its contracted curves are exactly those mapping to a point in the product; the open isomorphism and the diagonal boundary description give the precise criterion in the theorem.

The nilpotent kernel-line fibers have dimension at most two. On a hypothetical integral threefold in such a fiber, h is trivial and 2h+t restricts to the ample class t, while the imported cubic relation forces 3t³=0. This is impossible. Finiteness of the boundary cover preserves the fiber dimension bound. No absence of one- or two-dimensional contracted fibers is inferred.

Independent expansion of the degree-four quotient Chern series gives the correction −60mx³+105m²x²−90m³x+31m⁴. Passing to the Cartier boundary cycle and substituting m=h−t gives respectively 10d+5a+15b and 14d+18a−3b+31c. Using a=2b−3c yields the two stated simplified formulas. The argument for strict positivity is sound: 6h3−B³ is a nonnegative linear combination of nef monomials, so h3 B has positive degree when B⁴>0. The three h3 x_i degrees agree by the boundary relations and are therefore all positive.

All intersection formulas retain the Cartier divisor multiplicities and work on singular integral components. These results do not imply ampleness, exclude spectral fourfolds, or evaluate any actual operation instance.
