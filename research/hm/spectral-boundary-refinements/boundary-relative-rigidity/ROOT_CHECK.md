# Independent mathematical verification

The determinant equation is essential over nilpotents. Inverting a four-minor in the addition matrix reduces it to diag(I4,s), and the actual boundary equation sets s to zero. Likewise the rectangular minors, together with a unit value f(h,u,h), give an actual image line subbundle. The polar sends the addition image into that line; with one complementary source generator it has rank at most two as a module map. Thus the map to the determinantal polar scheme is valid, not merely a map of reduced supports.

The scheme fiber at a fixed polar pair has fixed H=P^-1(L): the bundle inclusion of rank four is an isomorphism by Nakayama. Taking quotients by h and inverse images in H are inverse on arbitrary test schemes. A nondegenerate scalar q identifies the plane uniquely as the orthogonal complement of the line. Its q pairing makes the common-eigenline zero equations identical to the beta-orthogonality equations over rings.

For a tangent w orthogonal to eigenvector v, pairing the linearized eigenline equations with v kills the eigenvalue derivative. A nonzero w would then have the identical joint eigenvalue tuple. Its span with v contains a nonzero isotropic vector, contradicting the diagonal-zero exclusion for beta. Consequently all geometric fibers have zero tangent spaces and are reduced of length zero, one or three.

The relative differential sheaf vanishes by fiberwise Nakayama, so the finite morphism is unramified. For a finite local factor C over local Artin A, reduced closed fiber C/m_A C=C_field says the element 1 generates the residue module. Nakayama gives surjectivity A to C, hence C=A/I. At most three such factors yield length(boundary)<=3 length(polar scheme). This strengthens only the boundary bound and cannot be substituted for a length bound on all of R. No flatness, etaleness, total reducedness, or physical obstruction calculation is established.
