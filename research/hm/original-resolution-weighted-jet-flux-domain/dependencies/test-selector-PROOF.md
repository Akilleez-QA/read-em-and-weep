# The retained-test / selector-connection pair has a regular canonical-line domain

## Result and scope

For every frozen constant scalar selector L, every supplied missing-direction endpoint Z_d, and every authenticated retained test p in H^0(Y,O_Y(2,2,2,2)), the exact source-reference family

    Q_d = p[-L G_ref,d J2(Z_d)] + (V_d p)[L J2(Z_d)]             (1)

has a genuine all-standard-chart, full-character canonical-line coefficient domain after the full ordinary partition comparison. The two terms in (1) must use the SAME original-reference derivative convention. In a regular standard representation chart A their exact sum is

    Q_d,A = -p_A L G_A,d J2,A(Z_d) + (V_d p_A)LJ2,A(Z_d).        (2)

Here G_A is the correctly transported local connection, not the reference expression with coordinates merely substituted. The opposite frame anomalies cancel exactly. The frozen scalar row is a global regular degree-compatible morphism, as proved below; no opaque regular-selector premise is needed for this step.

A direct sufficient five-occurrence certificate for (2) is

    carrier (26,17,14,14,5), legal (6,7,7,7,3),                 (3)

hence uniform **26/7**. An independent product-rule certificate for the SAME grouped family is

    carrier (22,22,18,15,6), legal (6,8,8,8,4),                 (4)

hence uniform **22/8**. Neither dominates the other coordinatewise; either is cleared by the unchanged 54/29 ordinary weights. The source-reference term -L G_ref J2 alone has no such unconditional own-owner claim. At an infinity divisor with every available direction-d legal bit equal to one, its unowned principal coefficient is -2 f_d/t_d; a separately regular reference term requires the appropriate divisibility of f_d. We do not assert that an actual source value violates or satisfies that condition.

The product-rule identity retaining all other terms is

    Q_d = V_d[p L J2(Z_d)]
          -p L(H C_d J1+H delta H C_d j)(Z_d)
          -p L J2(V_d Z_d).                                  (5)

The two overlap terms, current/input derivative, and derivatives of outer weights are retained, not set to zero. This is a regularity and exact-grouping theorem. It proves no zero pairing, total divergence, complete remainder cancellation, Ext basis identification, obstruction value.

## 2. The actual frozen scalar row is regular and constant

Write g_iak for the fixed integer coefficient tensor of the homogeneous linear map gamma(v). These g_iak are constants, not the owner transition matrices g_ij. The full frozen scalar_terms function gives, for each admissible (i,b),

    L_(i,b)(M)=M_(3,1)[i,b]
                - sum_(a=0..4,k=0..4) g_iak M_(2,0)[a,5b+k]. (7)

Zero coefficients may be omitted without changing the row. The key (u,v) means a map K^(u-1)->K^(v-1), of degree v-u. Both (3,1) and (2,0) have degree -2. Every entry in both blocks is therefore a section of the SAME line N^-4=q^*O_Y(-2,-2,-2,-2). Constant integer entry selection is a regular global morphism from their multiplicity sum to N^-4. Consequently

    L(M_A)=tau_A^-2 L(M_ref),   L_A=L_ref,   V_d L_A=0.       (8)

It adds no pole, preserves character, and commutes with the admitted ambient restrictions. This is the needed selector attribution, not a statement that these rows form a basis of an Ext or Serre-dual space.

For completeness, the chain-row sign can be checked without any actual matrix product. An internal-degree-minus-three symbol W has sole block (3,0). The frozen HPL convention gives

    (d_End W)_(3,1)=gamma W,   (d_End W)_(2,0)=W ev,

both with plus signs, since d_End W=dW-(-1)^(-3)Wd. The supplied structural-A proof gives ev(Y,Z)=(sum_k v_k Y_k,sum_k v_k Z_k), while gamma_ia=sum_k g_iak v_k. Therefore (7) applied to d_End W is

    sum_(a,k) g_iak v_k W_ab
      -sum_(a,k) g_iak W_ab v_k = 0.                       (9)

This is a universal commuting-symbol identity, not evaluation of frozen gamma, ev or W. It proves the precise bottom chain-row relation, though (9) is not needed for the domain proof and does not kill L G J2.

## 3. Reference transport and the regular local connection are different

Let r=v-u and let a prime mean V_d in the stated frame. The supplied common-split theorem gives, in the fixed original reference,

    G_ref,d(M)=B_d^v M-M B_d^u+2r beta_m,d M,
    beta_m,d=-m_d y_d/(2x_d).                              (10)

Thus its scalar action is -r m_d(y_d/x_d)M. This includes the old connection; it is not omitted. On a standard chart A put

    a_A=V_d(log tau_A),
    a_A=0 if d is finite, a_A=-u_d/t_d if d is at infinity.

The actual transformed connection on degree-r coefficients is

    G_A,d(M_A)=tau_A^r G_ref,d(M_ref)-r a_A M_A.            (11)

Equivalently, after expressing the reference operator on local input coefficients,

    G_A,d=G_ref,d-r a_A Id.

Equation (11) is forced by (V+G_A)(tau_A^r M_ref)=tau_A^r(V+G_ref)M_ref. It uses the genuine even-Hom transition and does not require a frame of N on Y. The raw B term is tensorial by its accepted split-minus-old interpretation; the common derivative term belongs to the connection transformation, not to B.

The scalar coefficient of (11), after extracting the one odd factor z_d, is

    -r m_d/x_d       on a finite factor,
     r(1-m_d)/t_d    on an infinity factor.               (12)

For the source degree r=-2 this is 2m_d/x_d and 2(m_d-1)/t_d respectively. The locally represented G_A has a safe OWN-owner 5/1 coefficient clearance on each standard chart (not a separate tensorial gluing assertion):

- finite m_d=0: (12) vanishes;
- finite m_d=1: its 1/x_d denominator is paid by that owner's x_d(x_d^2-1);
- infinity m_d=0: its 1/t_d denominator is paid by that owner's t_d;
- infinity m_d=1: (12) vanishes.

The raw left and right B terms each add 5/1 at this same leading occurrence. Their sum and (12) are therefore padded by 5/1, with no new standard divisor. The proof applies to every internal degree r; for a character containing d, multiplication by z_d contributes z_d^2=f_d, a regular polynomial, rather than division by a branch factor. Here J2(Z_d) has missing-d character, so no such extra factor is needed.

This local bound does NOT confer the same domain on the transported reference action. If r=-2 and d is infinite, define

    A_src,A=-tau_A^-2 L G_ref,d J2,ref(Z_d),
    A_loc,A=-L G_A,d J2,A(Z_d),
    F_A=LJ2,A(Z_d).

Then (11) gives exactly

    A_loc,A=A_src,A-2 a_A F_A=A_src,A+2(u_d/t_d)F_A.       (13)

In particular the reference scalar at a bit-one leading owner contributes -2(u_d/t_d)F_A to A_src,A, whereas the same scalar part of A_loc,A vanishes. Confusing these conventions is precisely the apparent unowned infinity pole.

## 4. Exact cancellation with the retained test derivative

The transported source derivative of the test is

    D_src p_A:=tau_A^2 V_d(p_ref)=V_d p_A-2 a_A p_A.       (14)

The right side is NOT merely V_d p_A. Its extra term is +2(u_d/t_d)p_A at infinity. Equations (13)-(14) prove

    p_A A_src,A+(D_src p_A)F_A
      =p_A A_loc,A+(V_d p_A)F_A.                         (15)

Both source terms in (1) must be transported this way, with every other-coordinate multiplier included in tau_A. Those other-coordinate multipliers have zero V_d derivative. No sign or line factor has been suppressed by stripping.

For an arbitrary A-to-B overlap let lambda=tau_B/tau_A. Then

    F_B=lambda^-2 F_A,   p_B=lambda^2 p_A,
    A_loc,B=lambda^-2 A_loc,A+V_d(lambda^-2)F_A.

Substituting into the right side of (15) gives Q_d,B=Q_d,A as an unstripped scalar on X: the two cross terms are p_A F_A times

    lambda^2 V_d(lambda^-2)+lambda^-2 V_d(lambda^2)=0.

Thus Q_d is a genuine scalar family, although its two local summands need not glue separately. Its character is full: G_d and V_d each flip exactly bit d; p is deck invariant and F has missing-d character. By the free character projection, its coefficient is consequently in

    O_Y(-2,-2,-2,-2)=K_Y.                                (16)

One can see the degree accounting before this last derivative. The coefficient f_d of F lies in O(-2 e_d-4 sum_(r!=d)e_r). Multiplication by p puts p f_d in O(-2 sum_(r!=d)e_r), of degree ZERO in d. Its complementary derivative has the canonical degree in every direction. The test is never regarded as an ordinary global function by itself.

Explicitly on the switched d factor, write F_A=z_(S_d),A f_A. Transported reference differentiation of F contributes stripped coefficient

    -partial_t f_A-2 f_A/t,

whereas transported reference differentiation of p contributes

    -partial_t p_A+2 p_A/t.

Their plus-signed product-rule sum is -partial_t(p_A f_A). These are coefficient formulas in the same local canonical frame, and their opposite 2/t terms are the coefficient versions of (15).

## 8. Exact failure mode for a mismatched grouping

Suppose d is infinite and all available direction-d legal bits are one. Then their legal sections are 1-t_d^2, so none supplies t_d. On a locus where the legitimate carrier factors are units at t_d=0, the source-reference A_src has principal stripped term

    -2 f_A/t_d.                                         (21)

The other local contribution is regular after its existing owned clearance. Thus separate regularity of the reference term requires f_A|_(t_d=0)=0 after that clearance. More generally, if C is the fixed common clearing product for the regular terms, the precise condition is t_d divides C f_A in the regular base coefficient ring (localized in any remaining permitted units). This statement does not assume C is a unit if a carrier actually vanishes there.

If one pairs A_src with only the LOCAL derivative V_d p_A, omitting the correction in (14), the result differs from the correct family by

    -2(u_d/t_d)p_A F_A,

with stripped residual -2p_A f_A/t_d. Its precise regularity condition is correspondingly t_d divides C p_A f_A. A particular retained test can vanish there; that possibility is not ruled out. If regularity is demanded uniformly for test sections nonzero on the divisor, the condition reduces to the condition on f_A. No actual f_A or retained p is evaluated here.

Conversely pairing A_loc with D_src p_A overcounts the opposite +2p_A f_A/t_d. Changing the plus sign in (1) to a minus doubles rather than cancels the source-reference anomaly. At branch points these are genuine coefficient issues: t has order two and u order one on C, and u/t is not regular. The free finite-flat coefficient projection explains why a regular full-character section cannot be justified by treating u as an invertible denominator.
