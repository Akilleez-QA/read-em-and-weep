## 1. Endpoint saturation from a vanishing sequence

For a basepoint-free ten-dimensional subsystem of \(H^0(\mathcal O(18))\),
let \(\mathcal A_k\) be the image of the complete order-\(k\) osculating
bundle \(U_k^{18}\) in \(H_{\mathrm{fin}}\mathcal O_X\), and let \(C_k\)
be its saturation. For \(k\leq9\), this projection is generically injective.
Locally, after choosing a coordinate and a line frame, \(\mathcal A_k\) is
generated by \(c,c',\ldots,c^{(k)}\). The coordinate and frame changes are
triangular and invertible, so this is an intrinsic sheaf.

If \(a_0<\cdots<a_9\) is the vanishing sequence at a point, then
\[
\operatorname{length}_p(C_k/\mathcal A_k)
=\sum_{i=0}^k(a_i-i).
\tag{14}
\]
Indeed, in a section basis adapted to the vanishing filtration, every maximal
minor of the local derivative matrix has order at least the right-hand side.
The minor from the first \(k+1\) basis sections has precisely that order:
its leading determinant is the nonzero Vandermonde determinant of their
distinct leading exponents, times nonzero leading coefficients. The ideal
of maximal minors over the local discrete valuation ring therefore has that
valuation, which is the determinant-index length of the image lattice in
its saturation. This is a universal local calculation, not an evaluation of
selected source data.

Under (2), the lengths in (14) are one at every \(D_{12}\) point for \(k=5\),
and two there for \(k=6\); elsewhere they are zero for these values of \(k\).
Consequently
\[
\deg C_5=\deg U_5^{18}+12=6(-13)+12=-66,
\]
\[
\deg C_6=\deg U_6^{18}+24=7(-12)+24=-60.
\tag{15}
\]
The saturated bundles form the actual successive forward osculating flag:
\[
C_k=\operatorname{sat}(C_{k-1}+T_X\nabla C_{k-1}).
\tag{16}
\]
To see this without differentiating a bundle splitting, work over the
function field. The span of \(c,\ldots,c^{(k-1)}\), together with derivatives
of its rational sections, is exactly the span of
\(c,\ldots,c^{(k)}\). Saturation in the fixed ambient constant bundle then
gives (16).

In particular, \(C_5/C_4\) and \(C_6/C_5\) are line bundles. Their degrees
from (15) and \(\deg C_4=5(-14)=-70\) identify them as
\[
C_5/C_4\simeq\mathcal O(4),\qquad
C_6/C_5\simeq\mathcal O(6).
\tag{17}
\]
All line-bundle identifications in this note can be made equivariant. The
binary group has no nontrivial one-dimensional characters, so there is no
unrecorded character twist.

It is important to distinguish two sixth-step statements. The image of the
original \(U_6^{18}\) has total saturation defect 24. Nevertheless the
forward derivative of the already saturated \(C_5\) has no new defect. In
fact its induced nonzero map
\[
C_5/C_4\longrightarrow(C_6/C_5)\otimes\Omega_X^1
\tag{18}
\]
is a map \(\mathcal O(4)\to\mathcal O(4)\), hence an isomorphism. Thus
\[
C_5+T_X\nabla C_5=C_6
\tag{19}
\]
as sheaves, before any additional saturation.

## 2. The fifth-step obstruction map is intrinsic

The point requiring proof is the transfer of endpoint saturation while the
vertical kernel is only \(U_7^8\), not all of \(V_1\mathcal O_X\).

Form the actual sum
\[
S=R_4+U_7^8\subset\overline H\mathcal O_X.
\tag{20}
\]
It is a saturated subbundle. For example, a local frame of \(U_7^8\), followed
by local lifts in \(R_4\) of a frame of \(C_4\), is fiberwise independent,
since the projected frame is a frame of the subbundle \(C_4\). This gives
\[
0\longrightarrow U_7^8\longrightarrow S\longrightarrow C_4\longrightarrow0.
\tag{21}
\]
In particular \(S\cap V_1\mathcal O_X=U_7^8\), and
\[
B=\overline H\mathcal O_X/S
\]
is locally free. Its actual endpoint projection fits into
\[
0\longrightarrow K\longrightarrow B
\longrightarrow H_{\mathrm{fin}}\mathcal O_X/C_4\longrightarrow0,
\quad
K=V_1\mathcal O_X/U_7^8\simeq\mathcal O(8).
\tag{22}
\]

The second fundamental map of the actual constant connection, taken modulo
\(S\), is an \(\mathcal O_X\)-linear map
\[
R_4\longrightarrow B\otimes\Omega_X^1.
\tag{23}
\]
It kills \(R_3\), because differentiating \(R_3\) lands in \(R_4\); it also
kills \(U_6^8\), because differentiating \(U_6^8\) lands in \(U_7^8\).
Hence (23), after tensoring with \(T_X\), factors canonically as
\[
\phi:L\longrightarrow B,\qquad
L=(R_4/(R_3+U_6^8))\otimes T_X
\simeq(C_4/C_3)\otimes T_X\simeq\mathcal O(-8).
\tag{24}
\]
Here \(R_3+U_6^8\) is exactly the inverse image of \(C_3\) inside \(R_4\),
so all quotients in (24) are honest locally free quotients. The map is
equivariant and does not depend on a coordinate, frame, lift, or bundle
splitting.

The complete degree-eight vertical flag satisfies
\((U_6^8)^+=U_7^8\) without saturation. Thus the raw fifth derivative sheaf
contains \(S\), and (24) gives
\[
\mathcal G_5/S=\operatorname{im}\phi.
\tag{25}
\]
Let \(\psi\) be the composition of \(\phi\) with the last map in (22).
It is the endpoint fifth fundamental map
\[
\psi:\mathcal O(-8)\longrightarrow H_{\mathrm{fin}}\mathcal O_X/C_4.
\tag{26}
\]
Under (2), \(\psi\) has one simple zero at each point of \(D_{12}\), and no
other zeros. This follows directly from (14) for \(k=5\), since \(C_4\)
is already a subbundle. Equivalently, locally its image modulo \(C_4\) is
the fifth derivative column after elimination of the first five columns;
its determinant index is exactly one.

## 3. Stabilizer characters force the whole obstruction to vanish

Fix an arbitrary point \(p\in D_{12}\), not a selected source-instance
evaluation. Let a generator of its binary stabilizer have eigenvalue
\(\lambda\) on the tautological line, with \(\lambda\) a primitive tenth
root, and set \(\zeta=\lambda^2\).

The fiber character of \(\mathcal O(d)\) at \(p\) is \(\lambda^{-d}\).
Since \(\psi_p=0\), the fiber map \(\phi_p\) lands in \(K_p\). But its
source and possible target characters are
\[
L_p:\lambda^8=\zeta^4,\qquad
K_p:\lambda^{-8}=\zeta.
\tag{27}
\]
They are distinct. An equivariant linear map between them is zero, so
\[
\phi_p=0\qquad\text{for every }p\in D_{12}.
\tag{28}
\]
This excludes vertical repair of the missing endpoint derivative. Merely
knowing that the endpoint map vanishes would not have implied (28).

More strongly, (28) proves the precise regular normalization needed here.
As a morphism from a line bundle to a locally free bundle, \(\phi\) factors
uniquely through division by the reduced divisor \(D=D_{12}\):
\[
\widetilde\phi:L(D)\longrightarrow B,
\qquad \phi=\widetilde\phi\circ(L\hookrightarrow L(D)).
\tag{29}
\]
In a local parameter \(t\), every component of \(\phi\) is divisible by
\(t\), because its fiber map is zero. Thus division is regular in \(B\),
not merely in the endpoint quotient. The projected map
\(\widetilde\psi\) is nonzero on every fiber, because the zeros of \(\psi\)
are exactly simple. It follows that \(\widetilde\phi\) also is nonzero on
every fiber, and its image is a line subbundle of \(B\).

There is no hidden choice of a differentiated splitting in this argument.
Locally a representative of \(\phi\) can be lifted from \(B\) to the
ambient constant bundle; its value at \(p\) is in \(S_p\). Subtract a
regular local section of \(S\) with this value, then divide by \(t\).
Such a regular ambient lift exists precisely because (28) holds. Different
choices change the lift by a section of \(S\), so the inverse-image
subbundle below is unchanged. A change of coordinate changes a local
equation of the divisor by a unit, and a change of frame changes (29)
by the corresponding line transition. These are the intrinsic transitions
of \(L(D)\).

The inverse image of \(\operatorname{im}\widetilde\phi\) under the quotient
\(\overline H\mathcal O_X\to B\) is saturated, has the same generic span
as \(\mathcal G_5\), and therefore is exactly \(R_5\). This proves
\[
R_5/S\simeq L(D)=\mathcal O(4),\qquad
R_5/\mathcal G_5\simeq L(D)/L.
\tag{30}
\]
The latter quotient has length one at each point of \(D\). Its projection
onto the endpoint quotient is the corresponding normalization of \(\psi\),
so it maps onto \(C_5/C_4\). As the projected normalized line is fiberwise
nonzero, it introduces no additional vertical kernel. Together with (21),
this proves the sheaf-surjective exact sequence (4), including
\[
R_5\cap V_1\mathcal O_X=U_7^8.
\tag{31}
\]

The degree can be computed without assuming any splitting:
\[
\deg\mathcal G_5=\deg S+\deg L=(-8-70)-8=-86,
\]
\[
\deg R_5=-86+12=-74,
\quad
\operatorname{rk}R_5=8+6=14.
\tag{32}
\]
This proves all fifth-step saturation assertions.

## 4. The sixth derivative contains the full constant kernel

Let \(\mathcal G_6=R_5+T_X\nabla R_5\) be the raw sixth forward sheaf.
It contains \(U_7^8+T_X\nabla U_7^8=U_8^8=V_1\mathcal O_X\) as a sheaf,
without saturation. The universal degree-eight leading coefficient for
this last vertical step is \(8-7=1\), so it never vanishes.

Because (4) is sheaf-surjective and \(q\) is constant,
\[
q(\mathcal G_6)=C_5+T_X\nabla C_5=C_6
\tag{33}
\]
by (19). Since the entire constant kernel is already contained in
\(\mathcal G_6\), (33) implies the sheaf equality
\[
\mathcal G_6=q^{-1}(C_6).
\tag{34}
\]
This is saturated, since the ambient quotient is
\(H_{\mathrm{fin}}\mathcal O_X/C_6\), a vector bundle. Thus
\(R_6=\mathcal G_6\), proving (5) and the second equality in (7).

For clarity, this zero new defect refers to differentiating the already
saturated \(R_5\). The sheaf generated instead by derivatives of the
original \(R\) only through order six contains the full \(V_1\) but projects
to \(\mathcal A_6\); it is \(q^{-1}(\mathcal A_6)\) and has total saturation
defect 24. There is no conflict between that statement and (34).

## 5. Consecutive quotients and the primal flag

Compare (1) for \(k=4\) with (4). Exactness and the actual inclusions give
\[
0\longrightarrow U_7^8/U_6^8
\longrightarrow R_5/R_4
\longrightarrow C_5/C_4\longrightarrow0,
\]
or
\[
0\longrightarrow\mathcal O(6)
\longrightarrow R_5/R_4
\longrightarrow\mathcal O(4)\longrightarrow0.
\tag{35}
\]
Similarly, (4) and (5) give
\[
0\longrightarrow V_1\mathcal O_X/U_7^8
\longrightarrow R_6/R_5
\longrightarrow C_6/C_5\longrightarrow0,
\]
or
\[
0\longrightarrow\mathcal O(8)
\longrightarrow R_6/R_5
\longrightarrow\mathcal O(6)\longrightarrow0.
\tag{36}
\]
Both extension groups are \(H^1(\mathcal O(2))=0\), so the sequences split
as bundles, and equivariantly after averaging a splitting. These splittings
are used only to state isomorphism types, never to differentiate.

Let \(p:H^*\mathcal O_X\to\overline H\mathcal O_X\) be the genuine
constant quotient by the 11-dimensional \(V_0\). PR784, as used in PR785,
established \((Q^*)^{++}=p^{-1}(R_2)\). For a full inverse image under a
constant quotient, saturated forward growth commutes with inverse image.
Consequently
\[
(Q^*)^{(k)+}=p^{-1}(R_k)\qquad(k\geq2).
\tag{37}
\]
This general full-kernel statement is applicable to \(p\) at every step;
it was not assumed for the non-full-kernel quotient \(q\) at step five.

Annihilator duality in the original constant pairing identifies
\(E_k=\operatorname{ann}(p^{-1}(R_k))\). Hence
\[
\operatorname{rk}E_k=30-(11+\operatorname{rk}R_k),\qquad
\deg E_k=\deg R_k,
\]
\[
J_k\simeq(R_k/R_{k-1})^*.
\tag{38}
\]
Equations (32), (34), (35), and (36) prove (8) and (9).

The fifth Gauss map can also be identified with its defect, rather than
only its numerical degree. Dualizing the actual forward second fundamental
map gives (10). On the vertical quotient of the dual filtration it is the
nonzero scalar map
\(\mathcal O(-6)\to\mathcal O(-6)\), dual to the complete degree-eight
step with coefficient \(8-6=2\). On the endpoint subline it is
\[
\mathcal O(-4)\longrightarrow\mathcal O(8),
\tag{39}
\]
whose zero divisor is exactly \(D_{12}\), by (29). The snake lemma for these
two filtrations identifies the cokernel of (10) with the cokernel of (39),
namely \(\mathcal O(8)|_{D_{12}}\). Thus its length is exactly 12 and the
torsion is reduced. No choice of a diagonal Gauss matrix is needed.

Finally the next actual Gauss map is a sheaf injection
\(J_6\to J_5(-2)\), as for successive backward transforms generally.
Both bundles have rank two and degree \(-14\); its determinant is a
nonzero section of \(\mathcal O_X\), and therefore never vanishes.
This proves (11).

## 6. The everywhere-spanning fifth-jet alternative

Suppose instead that endpoint jets through order five span everywhere.
Then \(C_5=U_5^{18}\) and the map \(\psi\) in (26) is nowhere zero.
Therefore \(\phi\) is nowhere zero, without needing a character argument.
Its image is a line subbundle of \(B\), and (25) is already saturated.
The same construction gives (12), with
\[
\deg R_5=\deg U_7^8+\deg U_5^{18}=-8-78=-86.
\]
The actual consecutive quotient sequence is
\[
0\longrightarrow\mathcal O(6)
\longrightarrow R_5/R_4
\longrightarrow\mathcal O(-8)\longrightarrow0.
\tag{40}
\]
It splits since \(H^1(\mathcal O(14))=0\). Apply (38) to obtain (13).
The injection \(J_5\to J_4(-2)\) is then between rank-two bundles of the
same degree and is an isomorphism by the same determinant argument.

### 6.1. Full fifth jets and a simple sixth defect on \(D_{12}\)

Assume in addition that the sixth endpoint derivative has a simple defect
at every point of \(D_{12}\), and none elsewhere. For example, this follows
from the vanishing sequence
\[
0,1,2,3,4,5,7,8,9,11
\tag{41}
\]
on \(D_{12}\), with ordinary orders through six at all other points.
The pinned PR786 classification establishes precisely these hypotheses for
\(t=t_1\), and no other parameter with full fifth jets.
Formula (14) gives
\[
\deg C_6=\deg U_6^{18}+12=-72,\qquad
C_6/C_5\simeq\mathcal O(6).
\tag{42}
\]
Here \(C_5=U_5^{18}\), of degree \(-78\).

The raw sixth residual contains all of \(V_1\), by precisely the argument
in Section 4, and its projection is now \(\mathcal A_6\). Thus
\[
\mathcal G_6=q^{-1}(\mathcal A_6),\qquad
R_6=q^{-1}(C_6),
\tag{43}
\]
and \(R_6/\mathcal G_6\) has reduced length 12 on \(D_{12}\). This transfer
uses a genuinely full constant kernel, so it requires no additional
vertical character argument. Equation (38) yields
\[
(\operatorname{rk}E_6,\deg E_6)=(3,-72).
\tag{44}
\]
The consecutive quotient is an extension of \(\mathcal O(6)\) by
\(\mathcal O(8)\), split because \(H^1(\mathcal O(2))=0\). Hence
\[
J_6=\mathcal O(-8)\oplus\mathcal O(-6).
\tag{45}
\]
In the actual map
\[
J_6\longrightarrow J_5(-2)=\mathcal O(-8)\oplus\mathcal O(6),
\tag{46}
\]
the vertical quotient map is an isomorphism, while the endpoint subline
map \(\mathcal O(-6)\to\mathcal O(6)\) vanishes simply along \(D_{12}\).
Its cokernel is therefore \(\mathcal O(6)|_{D_{12}}\), of length 12.

### 6.2. Full sixth jets everywhere

If endpoint sixth jets span everywhere, then
\(C_6=U_6^{18}\), of degree \(-84\). Again the raw sixth residual contains
the full \(V_1\) and already equals its inverse image, so there is no
defect at either step five or step six. Now
\[
C_6/C_5=\mathcal O(-6),\qquad
0\to\mathcal O(8)\to R_6/R_5\to\mathcal O(-6)\to0.
\tag{47}
\]
The latter sequence splits because \(H^1(\mathcal O(14))=0\). Consequently
\[
(\operatorname{rk}E_6,\deg E_6)=(3,-84),\qquad
J_6=\mathcal O(-8)\oplus\mathcal O(6),
\tag{48}
\]
and \(J_6\to J_5(-2)\) is an isomorphism. This completes the three
conditional rows of the table.

## 7. Balancedness cannot eliminate both exceptional branches

[PR754, Sections 3--5](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/044add76ebb14043a0edf73171517c90b9ef0c13/research/hm/order-four-quadratic-load/ABSTRACT-DEGREE-EIGHTEEN-BALANCED-SYSTEMS.md)
identifies exactly two distinct nonbalanced parameters, the zeros of the
nonzero multiplicity-space linear forms \(a\) and \(b\). At the unique
\(b\)-zero \(t_b\), the variable copy is \(P_t=f_{12}P_6\), and the fixed copy is
\(V_{18}=f_{12}V_6\). Since \(\operatorname{Sym}^6W=V_6\oplus P_6\), the
endpoint system there is
\[
\mathcal H_b=A_{18}\oplus f_{12}H^0(\mathcal O(6)).
\tag{49}
\]

The nonzero invariant form \(f_{12}\) has reduced zero divisor \(D_{12}\):
its invariant zero divisor has degree twelve, and the unique projective
orbit of the minimum size twelve is \(D_{12}\). At any point of that orbit,
the complete degree-six system has an adapted basis of orders
\(0,1,\ldots,6\). Multiplication by \(f_{12}\) gives seven independent
sections of orders \(1,2,\ldots,7\). All-parameter basepoint-freeness from
PR786 supplies a section of \(\mathcal H_b\) of order zero. These eight
sections have distinct initial orders \(0,1,\ldots,7\), so their order-seven
jet images are independent and \(\mathcal H_b\) spans seventh jets at every
point of \(D_{12}\).

The PR786 parameters \(t_0,t_1,t_2\) fail respectively fifth-, sixth-, and
seventh-jet spanning on \(D_{12}\). Consequently
\[
t_b\notin\{t_0,t_1,t_2\}.
\tag{50}
\]
Among the two distinct parameters \(t_0,t_1\), only the single remaining
\(a\)-zero can be nonbalanced. At least one of \(t_0,t_1\) is therefore
balanced. In particular, the balanced presentation by itself does not
force global sixth-jet spanning and cannot eliminate both exceptional
rows of the table.

This does not prove that \(t_0\) itself is balanced: it may be the
\(a\)-zero. Nor does it identify any endpoint parameter with an actual
source. It is a structural comparison of previously characterized abstract
parameters, with no coordinate assignment or source-instance evaluation.

## Dependency boundary

The actual residual conclusions are consequences of the pinned
PR784/PR785 actual-source inputs and the pinned PR786 endpoint
classification, conditional only on the stated branch of the still-unknown
actual parameter. This note does not identify that actual parameter and
does not assert that any branch is realized. Section 7 excludes the
\(b\)-zero from \(t_0,t_1,t_2\), but does not identify the \(a\)-zero relative
to \(t_0,t_1\), decide which exceptional parameters are balanced individually,
or select the actual branch. The first exceptional proof in fact only needs
the fifth- and sixth-order endpoint
