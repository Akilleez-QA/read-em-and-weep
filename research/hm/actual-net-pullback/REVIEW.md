## Checked interfaces

1. **Actual/BHM dictionary.** The universal identity F_1(v_k*)=e_(3k+2)* wedge e_(3k+3)* and F_2(v_k*)=e_(3k+4)* wedge e_(3k+1)* gives F_1=f^+, F_2=-f^- after r_i=p_(2i). Thus the decomposability incidence is in P(V*) times P(W*), not silently in P(A). BHM Section 3.4 Lemma 4 makes it smooth. Its finite birational projection and the finite birational map in Section 4.5 Proposition 9 identify it with the normalization S(5) of S_15. The first hyperplane class is D=I+2F and the second is F. Finiteness of the pencil projection can also be seen from BHM equation (8): its mixed wedge coefficient never vanishes identically at a nonzero parameter, so an entire parameter P^1 cannot be a fiber.

2. **Upper bound.** A rank-two k determines a traceless hyperplane in a two-by-two tensor space. Its rank-one locus is a smooth spanning (1,1) graph conic. Zero wedge implies this graph is in the decomposability surface. Hence D.C=F.C=1 and I.C=-1. BHM Section 4.3 Lemma 9, 5I=sum L_ab, then forces C to be one of the twenty-five sections, by nonnegative intersection of distinct irreducible curves on a smooth surface. The inverse nondegenerate hyperplane pairing recovers projective k. This proves the upper bound without the false claim that a decomposable conic automatically has a decomposable plane span.

3. **Full-group interface.** I independently read research/hm/order-four-quadratic-load/PRIMARY-INJECTION-AND-FAILURE.md at commit 7bf725bcd857a2deb975f9ecb80fca32e7833ae0, Section 1. It explicitly supplies the actual N-module identity Lambda^2 A=V tensor W, with A=V_3 and W=W', and the irreducible adjoint quotient. Thus the stronger proof does not substitute an inequivalent Galois multiplicity representation. BHM Section 3.1 Lemma 2 supplies the Weil extension; its central reflection has eigenspace dimensions three and two, unchanged by the relevant Galois conjugation. A multiplicity-space basis change does not change the intrinsic traceless-orbit construction.

4. **Stabilizer without coordinate alignment.** The full normalizer preserves the decomposability incidence and both projection degrees. Its action therefore permutes the twenty-five section graphs. The translation group F_5^2 acts simply transitively, by BHM Section 4.2. The central involution of any chosen SL_2(F_5) complement acts on this torsor as affine inversion, with one fixed point. Its centrality forces the whole complement to fix that section. This avoids assuming that a particular coordinate complement fixes a named modular zero section.

5. **Exact beta classification.** Every section has degrees (1,1), so gives a unique rank-two candidate k. Perfectness makes an invariant candidate line pointwise invariant under its complement. Consequently N_k is the irreducible, centrally even adjoint three-dimensional module. Its projection to the one-dimensional summand in the even exterior square Lambda^2 E_3 direct-sum Lambda^2 E_2 is zero, so N_k=Lambda^2 E_3. All twenty-five candidates are therefore beta nets, by translation. No tensor-rank-two alpha net occurs. The elementary irreducibility proof in the text is valid: A_5 has no nontrivial representations of dimensions one or two, and Schur's lemma excludes a trivial summand in sl(W).

6. **Actual rank-nine lines.** At an established rank-eight support-drop point, the missing V-covector p0 gives p0 tensor W* inside the two-dimensional annihilator kernel, hence equality. All its nonzero tensors have rank one. Thus each rank-two beta candidate's radical line avoids every rank-eight point and consists entirely of rank-nine points. Distinct lines cannot intersect: two independent candidate annihilators at an intersection would force rank at most eight. Uniqueness of the rank-nine annihilator gives exactly the asserted union of twenty-five disjoint lines as its zero-wedge locus.

## Limits retained

The classification concerns geometric projective k and geometric points of the primary parameter space. It does not assert that a zero-wedge scheme has reduced length twenty-five, that an actual determinantal pullback is reduced or transverse, or that universal normality descends. No higher-order lifting or algebraicity claim follows from it. Away from the twenty-five lines within the rank-nine locus, the rank-four-form/Pfaffian bridge applies; scheme behavior on the lines remains a separate problem.
