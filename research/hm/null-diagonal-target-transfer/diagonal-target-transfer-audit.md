# Independent audit: diagonal pure-source transfer and quotient

20 October 2026. Formal result and existing review record; actual HM/source values are not evaluated here.

## Verdict

The proposed deck-even, D8-invariant finite transfer and ranks pass. The upper differential is injective for a coefficient-specific reason, not merely because the total Ext dimensions happen to fit. The correct second-obstruction input is the transferred COMPLETE pure SUM; replacing its projection by its global component is invalid in general. The quotient has dimension 32. The accepted cusp/branch-family theorem further confines the actual class to its six-dimensional A² eigenspace, split 2+4. These are quotient and primitive criteria, not obstruction values or a proof of their vanishing.

## 1. Cochain dimensions and coefficient descent

Let L=[5N^-1 → 15N → 10N³ → 2N⁵], N=O(1,1,1,1), and let J be the trivial deck character and trivial representation of the factor-permutation subgroup D8 generated by the two pair swaps and block exchange. End cancels N's root factor. Its internal-degree q line multiplicities are m0=354, m1=245, m2=80, m3=10, with m(-q)=mq.

In lower-row internal degree q, deck-even sections have one-factor dimension q+1. Pair symmetry followed by block symmetry gives Sym²(Sym²(C^(q+1))). Thus the lower row is

C0 → C1 → C2 → C3, dimensions 354 → 1470 → 1680 → 550.

The upper row has one-factor deck-even H¹ dimension r-1 for internal degree -r. Each internal pair swap carries the Künneth minus sign, so invariance selects Λ²(C^(r-1)); block exchange has positive sign (it exchanges two degree-two blocks). Its invariant multiplicity is Sym²(Λ²(C^(r-1))). Only r=3 contributes, giving dimension 10 in (internal,sheaf)=(-3,4), total degree one. There are no other upper spaces in J.

Finite quotient, projection formula, and the accepted REGULAR coefficient splitting identify this sector with derived sections of End(L_P4) tensored with

Acoef = O ⊕ W O(-1) ⊕ W² O(-2).

W is a fixed coefficient line; it does not change numerical dimensions. This splitting is an O-module splitting compatible with the base differential, not a claim that the algebra multiplication is componentwise. It uses no inverse discriminant and remains regular on branch loci. The imported cohomology vectors are (1,24,2,0,0), (0,5,10,0,0), and (0,0,20,0,0), respectively. Their sum is (1,29,32,0,0).

## 2. The coefficient-specific proof of injectivity

In the O and O(-1) coefficient summands, negative internal degrees down to -3 produce line twists only down to -3 and -4. Their H⁴ on P4 is zero. In the O(-2) coefficient summand the sole upper space is H⁴(O(-5))^10 at internal -3. This is exactly the entire upper ten-dimensional space.

Moreover, in that O(-2) summand the lower degree-one space is zero: its line twist is -1. Its finite transferred complex is therefore 10 → 80 → 50 in total degrees 1,2,3. The imported H¹(End H(-2))=0 forces the upper d5 to be injective. This is a proof using the specific coefficient summand. Total Ext¹=29 alone would not suffice, since surviving upper classes could otherwise contribute to those 29.

Consequently the spectral map from the upper ten into lower-row H² is injective, and its image has zero intersection with lower-row boundaries after lifting to C2. More precisely, for any permitted contraction, if T:U10→C2 denotes its representative and A1:C1→C2 the lower differential, then

Tz ∈ im(A1) implies z=0.

A contraction not respecting the coefficient splitting can change T by lower-boundary terms. This does not invalidate the assertion: it is the injectivity of the induced map into H² of the lower row that establishes it. The particular representative need not literally occupy only the chosen O(-2) coordinate block unless the contraction is chosen compatibly.

## 3. Exact ranks

Write A0:C0→C1, A1:C1→C2, B:C2→C3. Ext0=1 gives rank A0=353. Upper injectivity leaves all Ext¹ in the lower row; hence rank A1=1470-353-29=1088. There is no upper contribution to total degree three and no upper source at internal -2; Ext³=0 forces B surjective, of rank 550.

The full finite transferred complex is

K0=C354 → K1=C1470 ⊕ U10 → K2=C1680 → K3=C550,

with d1=F=[A1,T]. Its ranks are 353,1098,550. In particular ker F has dimension 382=353+29, and ker B has dimension 1130. Thus

0 → im F (dimension 1098) → ker B (dimension 1130) → Ext²_J (dimension 32) → 0.

The lower-row H² before d5 has dimension 42, not 32. Removing its ten-dimensional upper image produces the final quotient. All statements follow from accepted cohomology and formal descent, not fresh source computations.

## 4. Project the full second source

Use a normalized equivariant SDR for the Čech differential δ onto the two sheaf-cohomology rows: δh+hδ=1-ip, pi=1, hi=ph=h²=0. Put Δ=(-1)^(Čech degree)[d,-], D=δ+Δ. With the sign conventions checked in ../null-first-staircase-ranks/first-staircase-ranks-audit.md, the finite HPL maps are

I=(1+hΔ)^-1 i,
P=p(1+Δh)^-1,
H=h(1+Δh)^-1,
dK=pΔ(1+hΔ)^-1 i.

They satisfy DI=I dK, PD=dK P, PI=1, and DH+HD=1-IP. The series are finite by Čech degree, and the operators act on regular finite-support chart cochains. No discriminant inversion is involved. The cross-row term is T=pΔ(hΔ)^4 i, with positive fourth-order sign.

Let R be the complete D-closed J-valued PURE SUM residual R_PP+R_QQ obtained from the prescribed first boundary after the accepted comparison. If a representative is only invariant up to coboundary, take the finite-group projector on its closed sum; this retains the class. Individual pure terms need not be block-invariant. The separate mixed residual is handled only through the accepted mixed-vanishing premise.

Set r=P R. It belongs to K2=C1680 and Br=0. The relevant obstruction is [r] in ker B/im F. In total degree two the original R can have (Čech,internal) components (j,2-j), j=0,...,4. Consequently its lower projection is the sum

r = Σ_(j=0)^4 (-1)^j p(Δh)^j R_(j,2-j).

The totalized signs are already included in Δ. Keeping only R_(0,2), or merely pR, generally omits real transfer terms. This is why the full prescribed residual, including transported boundary and all ambient/root/frame contributions before its established global-Moyal comparison, must be used. Dimension statements cannot supply r.

## 5. A valid quotient projector and primitive contract

Choose Hermitian inner products on finite K. Let F† be the Moore–Penrose inverse, equivalently invert F only from (ker F)^perp onto im F. Let B†=B*(BB*)^-1, which exists because B is surjective. Then

Π2 = 1 - F F† - B† B

is the orthogonal projection onto ker B ∩ ker F*, of dimension 32: BF=0 makes im F orthogonal to im B*. For the closed r above, Π2 r=0 iff r∈im F. One must NOT write (F*F)^-1 F* on all K1: F has the 382-dimensional kernel established above. A restricted inverse or pseudoinverse is essential.

If Π2 r=0, let u=F†r. Then Fu=r, and the actual total-degree-one cochain

b = -Iu - HR

satisfies Db=-R, since DR=0 and DH R=R-I P R. Conversely any primitive Db=-R gives dK(Pb)=-r and therefore Π2r=0. This proves the necessary-and-sufficient contract. The HR term is essential for a general full source; -Iu alone has differential -IPR, not generally -R.

If Π2r is not zero, this contract does not produce a primitive. No numerical inverse, coefficient vector u, residual r, or value Π2r has been evaluated. The nonuniqueness of a primitive is expected: K has nonzero Ext¹ of dimension 29 and gauge boundaries of dimension 353.

## 6. Actual six-coordinate interpretation and scope

The quotient identification above is the compact target M=H²(End0 H⊗Acoef), dimension 32=2+10+20; scalar H² contributes nothing. The coefficient projections are the accepted regular projections, not arbitrary coordinates on the 1680-dimensional cochain space.

For the normalized cusp problem, use the accepted branch-family covariance and faithful normalization theorem: the ACTUAL prescribed diagonal pure SUM represents v=Q(1,0,0), the A² coefficient. Here A² denotes the square of the branch-family parameter, not the coefficient algebra Acoef. The class v lies in one nontrivial rotation eigenspace, dimension six, with coefficient decomposition 0+2+4. This is an additional theorem about the actual class; it is not a consequence of the 32-dimensional rank calculation. In particular it does not imply that Π2 has rank six on arbitrary closed sources.

The original elliptic class is v+Rv, and the accepted map v↦v+Rv is injective because the two nontrivial rotation eigenspaces are distinct. Thus testing those six coordinates of v is equivalent to the original remaining diagonal condition. This does not test R_PP and R_QQ separately, and must not be promoted to a full two-parameter lifting assertion.

A cochain realization of the six coordinates requires composing the quotient class [PR] with the established compact-target/normalization identification and its coefficient/eigenspace projections. An arbitrary 32-dimensional harmonic basis does not itself label the six physical coordinates. Vanishing all six is equivalent, on the accepted actual source, to Π2PR=0 and therefore to existence of the primitive above, together with the separately accepted mixed result. None of those six values is established here.
