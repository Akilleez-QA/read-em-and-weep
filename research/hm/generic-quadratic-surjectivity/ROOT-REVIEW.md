# Second independent review of generic quadratic surjectivity

20 October 2026. Scoped mathematical verdict: PASS. This is a structural mathematical review, not formal verification or an evaluation of a fixed HM multiplication map.

The rank-at-most-one linear-space dichotomy is proved directly by comparing two rank-one operators and their sum. Degree-zero generation ensures that all quadratic images span M3 and that a nonzero common functional is nonzero on some z m. In the common-kernel case, associativity and commutativity then give cG(xy)=lambda(xm)G(yz) and cG(yz)=lambda(ym)G(z²). All images must lie in one line, contradicting dim M3=2. Both alternatives are therefore excluded, establishing an actually nonempty surjectivity locus, not merely a conditional determinantal description.

The locus is Zariski open by maximal minors. Its intersection with nondegenerate quadrics is nonempty because both are nonempty opens in irreducible projective quadratic space. The primary resolution genuinely supplies generation in degree zero as well as the two-dimensional top piece. The commuting natural surjections from PR440 then prove surjectivity on H¹B(4)→H¹B(6); the extra four summands land in M4=0. This suffices to kill Q(q,r) for every cubic r without evaluating the remaining resolution map.

The universal binary-quartic quadratic is nondegenerate from its paired coefficient blocks and nonzero middle square. The geometric consequence correctly uses q_g=g∘q_0 and u_g=(g^{-1})*u_0, with HM fixed on the target and its ordered-root pullback varying. The PGL orbit of a nondegenerate quadratic is all nondegenerate quadrics over C. Pushing the existing filtered identity through the target isomorphism transports the ideals, while the cohomology inputs remain those of the fixed B. Thus whole-coefficient order-six vanishing holds on a nonempty open of this translated family.

No identity-coordinate membership in that open is proved. The abstract bad-nondegenerate-quadric example correctly excludes upgrading the module lemma to every nondegenerate quadratic under the stated module hypotheses. It is not an HM example. The theorem changes the relative position of the root quotient and the fixed bundle; it does not assert projective invariance of HM, settle the original Q, or produce a source representative or primitive. Pure conditions, module lifting and algebraicity remain separate.
