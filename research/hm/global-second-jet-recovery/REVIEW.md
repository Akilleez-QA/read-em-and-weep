## 2. Exact coefficient sequence, determinant and physical twists

For a weight-four matrix, entry degrees are `(4,2,6)`. The equation

`2fgx+g^2 y-f^2 z=0`

first forces `f|y` by regularity modulo `f`; since `deg y=2<3`, `y=0`. The remaining equality forces `x=f ell`, `z=2g ell`, with `deg ell=1`. The retained unit `u` changes neither this kernel nor surjectivity. The square-ideal resolution of a regular `(3,5)` pair has quotient ending in degree eleven, so degree twelve is surjective fiberwise and relatively. This establishes the correct rank-two kernel of the rank-fifteen-to-rank-thirteen coefficient map without an actual rank calculation.

The physical-twist-four entry lines are respectively `O(2),O(10),O(-6)`. They are presentations under triangular transitions, not a global splitting. The trace has physical twist six and binary degree twelve, giving coefficient line `O(0)`. The tangent preimage lies in `G(2)` and its surviving component has coefficient line

`O(-5/2+2-1/2)=O(-1)`.

The displayed determinant transport is consistent: if `A=PD`, then `det A=z^-2` and `v_x=z^4 A v_y`. The rank-two identity for the bilinear tangent map consequently produces the overall factor `z^4 det A=z^2`, exactly as in the proof. The lower triangular entry does not create a first component when `v1=0`. No square root of a determinant unit is required.
