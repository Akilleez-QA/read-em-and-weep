## 8. Contribution and limits

The proof identifies a genuine constant splitting of the original ambient, rather than postulating that a Birkhoff decomposition is horizontal. That actual splitting is the missing bridge identifying the original rank-four defect with a small-ambient rank-three defect. It then forces the defect to twelve, retaining all five possible stabilizer characters, and reduces the actual small dual endpoint to two explicit rank-two forms.

No actual coefficient, parameter, point, jet, tensor, matrix or instance rank is selected or evaluated. The only calculations are fixed representation restrictions, the seven and four symbolic rank-two degree partitions, and rank/degree inequalities. No broad quotient search, historical enumeration or unproved connection on a quotient is used.

The two primal quotients in (1) and the two dual endpoints in (3) are necessary configurations, not realization claims. Neither primal quotient is excluded by this proof. The separate triplet-positive form, higher top degrees, the full rank-four maximal profile, global rank-five bounds, square surjectivity, full-adjoint invertibility, smoothing, algebraicity and the original repair remain outside its conclusion.
