# A complete relation-degree bound for the adjoint module

Work over C. Put S=Sym(T), T=V*, dim V=5, B=End_0(F), and K_j=H^1(B(j-1)). All modules and maps are the actual HM ones. Write A_HM,j=H^1(F(j)) for primary cohomology; A and A* below instead denote the five-dimensional normalizer representations V_1 and V_3. Let V_s=H^0(F(3))=T_4. For the Rao module of the twenty-five disjoint transverse evaluation-degeneracy lines C, put P_j=H^1(I_C(6-j))* and N=SP_0.

We prove that K has no minimal relations above degree four. We also identify the quartic relations as a cokernel with only three multiplicity-one scalar channels. This converts the degree-five and degree-six fixed-pair presentations into consequences of a finite list of relation degrees, without evaluating the fixed polynomials.

## 1. Exact dependencies

PR475 at f741df08eb8556f03cde518558c1c67c86f2b120 gives

0 -> N -> P -> R_6 tensor k(-1) -> 0.                       (1)

Here R_6 is the six-dimensional quotient representation, not a relation module. P has a complete minimal presentation with five degree-zero generators, six degree-one generators, one degree-one relation and forty-five degree-two relations. In particular Tor_1(P,k) has support only in degrees one and two.

PR476 at 3aa461bc3581fb72fbf923fbe869ddee023b435a proves K is generated by K_0=V, with one Euler relation epsilon in degree one and fifteen minimal quadratic relations of type T tensor U'. PR477 at ff87076794e97238150485b12183c3cd0f4711e4 constructs the actual exact sequence

0 -> L=V_s tensor A_HM(-3) -> K -> N -> 0.                  (2)

PR478 at 142b0b52f4ad0ddea526ae044774e4f1dd599aef, section 3, identifies the normalized dual resolution of the line ideal through internal degree seven. In particular

Tor_3(P,k)_4=0,    Tor_2(P,k)_4=A tensor W'.                (3)

Indeed its first free terms toward P are S V+S(-1)R_6, S(-1)+45S(-2), 40S(-3)+10S(-4), and 10S(-5)+6S(-6). The degree-four ten-dimensional term is the dual of the ten minimal septic syzygies A* tensor W'. The intervening canonical-module cohomology first occurs in internal degree eight, so it does not alter (3). This is not an assertion that the dual complex is exact in all degrees.

PR479 at b57cf851bb244706acfe6c6485d4dfa54ceb6519 proves that the minimal cubic relation space of K is either zero or A* tensor U', of dimension fifteen. Its scalar remains undetermined.

## 2. The primary module has a complete linear presentation

The normalized monad is

A tensor O(-1) -> W tensor Omega^2(2) -> A* tensor O.

Let Z be the kernel of its right map. Splitting the monad into its two short exact sequences and using H^1(O(d-1))=H^2(O(d-1))=0 on P^4 gives, naturally in every integer d,

A_HM,d = coker[W tensor H^0(Omega^2(d+2)) -> A* tensor S_d]. (4)

For this cokernel identification, H^1(Omega^2(d+2))=0 for every d; the only intermediate Bott cohomology of Omega^2(t) is H^2 at t=0. These are sheaf cohomology statements and commute with polynomial multiplication.

The exterior Euler complex shows that H^0_*(Omega^2(2)) is generated in degree one by Lambda^3 T. More explicitly it is the second Koszul cycle module shifted by two: exactness of the polynomial Koszul complex makes its elements the images of Lambda^3 T tensor S(-1). Thus the image in (4), which is the entire relation module of the five degree-zero primary generators, is generated in degree one. No higher minimal relation can occur.

At d=1 the source has dimension twenty. Its kernel is H^0(Z(1)), and 0 -> A tensor O -> Z(1) -> F(1) -> 0 identifies it with A because H^0(F(1))=0. Consequently the relation space has dimension fifteen. Equivariantly T tensor A*=A tensor (U+W'), while A_HM,1=A tensor W'. The surjection onto primary cohomology therefore leaves exactly A tensor U as its kernel. Hence

Tor_1(A_HM,k)_j=0 for j != 1,
Tor_1(A_HM,k)_1=A tensor U.                                (5)

This is a complete presentation assertion, not just the first-degree dimension count. Generation of the full image in (4) is essential.

## 3. No relation of K can first occur after degree four

The residue module in (1) is killed by S_+, so Tor_i(R_6 tensor k(-1),k) is Lambda^i T tensor R_6 in internal degree i+1 and zero elsewhere. The Tor sequence of (1) contains

Tor_2(R_6 tensor k(-1),k)_j -> Tor_1(N,k)_j
   -> Tor_1(P,k)_j.

For j>=4 both outer terms vanish. Therefore

Tor_1(N,k)_j=0 for j>=4.                                   (6)

By (5), Tor_1(L,k) is supported in degree four. The sequence of (2) contains

Tor_1(L,k)_j -> Tor_1(K,k)_j -> Tor_1(N,k)_j.

For j>=5 the outer terms again vanish, proving

Tor_1(K,k)_j=0 for every j>=5.                              (7)

Since K is generated in degree zero, (7) says its full defining relation module is generated in degrees one through four. It does not say higher syzygies vanish, nor does it determine the remaining cubic or quartic maps.

## 4. The quartic relation space has three scalar channels

In degree four, (2) and (6) give the exact cokernel formula

Tor_1(K,k)_4 = coker[Tor_2(N,k)_4 --delta--> Tor_1(L,k)_4].   (8)

The target is V_s tensor A tensor U. The normalizer tensor rules give T_4 tensor U=U'+T_4+J_5, so

Tor_1(L,k)_4=A tensor (U'+T_4+J_5).                         (9)

All sums here are direct sums of representations. Apply (1) one step earlier, using (3). Since Tor_2(R_6 tensor k(-1),k)_4=0, its exact sequence gives

0 -> Lambda^3 T tensor R_6 -> Tor_2(N,k)_4
  -> A tensor W' -> 0.                                    (10)

The injection on the left uses Tor_3(P,k)_4=0; omitting that check would not justify the source character. Since Lambda^3 T=A tensor W and W tensor R_6=U'+T_4+J_5, finite-group semisimplicity splits (10) as representations and yields

Tor_2(N,k)_4 = A tensor (U'+T_4+J_5+W').                    (11)

No graded S-module splitting is claimed. The actual connecting map delta is equivariant. The four summands in (11) are pairwise inequivalent irreducibles, and each of the three target summands occurs once. Thus delta is zero on the W' channel and is a scalar on each matching U', T_4 and J_5 channel. Formula (8) says that the minimal quartic relations are precisely the sum of those target channels whose scalar is zero. Their possible component dimensions are fifteen, twenty and twenty-five. In particular there are at most sixty quartic relations. No scalar value, surjectivity, or independence among their possible vanishing conditions is asserted.

## 5. Finite presentations for the fixed-pair degrees

Let R=ker(S tensor V -> K), and choose homogeneous lifts E_i in R_i of its minimal relation spaces. Take E_1=C epsilon, E_2 of type T tensor U', E_3 either zero or A* tensor U', and E_4 the cokernel representation in (8), lifted into R_4. Choices of lifts do not change the generated submodule. Graded Nakayama and (7) now give the complete equality

R=S E_1+S E_2+S E_3+S E_4.

In particular, define

D_5=S_4 epsilon+S_3 E_2+S_2 E_3+S_1 E_4,
D_6=S_5 epsilon+S_4 E_2+S_3 E_3+S_2 E_4.

Then R_5=D_5 and R_6=D_6. These sums need not be direct. For any quadratic u and cubic v, the three coefficient quotients from PR476 consequently have the exact presentations

Q_4=(S_5 tensor V)/(D_5+u S_3 tensor V+v S_2 tensor V),
Q_5=(S_6 tensor V)/(D_6+u S_4 tensor V+v S_3 tensor V),
P_5=(S_6 tensor V)/(D_6+u^2 S_2 tensor V+v S_3 tensor V).

Here P_5 denotes the squared-quadratic coefficient quotient, not degree five of the dual Rao module P. These formulas hold without regularity or genericity assumptions on u,v; they follow directly from a graded presentation. They do not evaluate the original fixed pair. The actual embeddings of the relation lifts and the surviving scalar channels remain needed for such an evaluation. In particular this finite presentation does not discard the Tor correction of PR477 when separately analyzing its primary submodule.
