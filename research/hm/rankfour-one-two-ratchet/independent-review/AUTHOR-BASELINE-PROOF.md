The source has maximum ordinary degree at most−2. Its actual derivative kernel K has maximum degree at most−3. ## 3. Three-column endpoint capacity and actual cohomology

For a negative saturated subbundle F with derivative kernel A of rankn, preceding quotient C=F/A of rank4, and derivative rank(A)≤3, let λ1≥λ2≥λ3 be the three largest ordinary degrees of C. Iterating actual derivative kernels gives graded quotients Gj of nonincreasing positive ranks qj≤3, and regular coherent-image injections

    Gj → G(j−1)(−2) → C(−2j).

The iteration terminates because a nonzero horizontal negative saturated subbundle is impossible. Thus sum qj=n. Determinants of these injections imply

    degreeA ≤ max Σi=1..3 [hi λi−hi(hi+1)],
    h1≥h2≥h3≥0, sum hi=n.

This is the conjugate-partition form of the actual endpoint argument, not a splitting of the connection. The checker independently compares the height maximum with all nonincreasing rank strings. It applies first with A=K,C=B. On the dual side L3* injects into B*(−4), regardless of torsion; replacing the actual preceding quotient by this larger bundle only weakens the upper capacity bound, so it applies safely to Z0 with n=5.

The actual cohomology characters are

    H1(E(1))=W′+3T,

    χ(E(3))=W′+2T.

The rank-independent cubic-channel theorem supplies W′+2T⊂H0(B(3)). All of its graph-cancellation hypotheses concern this actual quotient; no coefficient-presentation ambient is substituted. ## 4. Iterated section envelopes

The successive quotients Gj=Kj/K(j+1) inject into B(−2j), with G0=B. Exact cohomology and semisimplicity therefore imply, for every t,

    H0(E(t)) ≤ Σj=0..J−1 H0(B(t−2j)),
    J=max(0,t+maxdegree(E)+1).

The finite cutoff is justified by H0(KJ(t))=0, not by dropping a tail. This is a necessary character inequality; no connection-compatible Birkhoff splitting is asserted. ## 6. Torsion-safe horizontal-subbundle test

The actual reversed endpoint derivative has target L3*(−2), which injects into B*(−6). Let S be any irreducible direct summand of the abstract equivariant splitting of Z0. If

    HomG(S,B*(−6))=0,
    HomG(S,(Z0/S)(−2))=0,

its actual derivative in the constant ambient is zero. Since S is saturated in that ambient, it must be constant, contradicting its negative degree. The second vanishing is essential; no splitting is presumed horizontal.

The exact Hom formula is multiplicity of R in R′Sym^(f−e)W for blocks RO(e),R′O(f), with zero if f<e. Applying this obstruction rejects11 tuples with explicit witnesses, including every P,V,Z tuple. Seven source-D tuples survive, one with δ2=12.
