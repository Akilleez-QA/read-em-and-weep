## Evaluation cokernel

On C, write M for the image line bundle, L for the quotient line bundle and K0 for the rank-three source kernel. The normal derivative gives K0 ≅ L⊗N*_(C/P4). On each line, if deg L=d and deg M=m, the source determinant and target determinant give 3d−3+m=0 and m+d=5. Thus d=−1 and T=coker(ev)=⊕i_*O_line(−1). All cohomology of T vanishes.

## Symmetric defect

The map V⊗T→Q defined by multiplying ev(v) with a lift of t is well defined and canonical. Locally Q=(R/I)ab ⊕ (R/I²)b², and the four source basis elements map to ab, xb², yb², zb². Regularity gives independence in I/I². The cokernel is Sym²T=i_*L². Therefore

0→V⊗T→Q→Sym²T→0

is exact. In particular H⁰Q=0, h¹Q=25 and HʲQ=0 for j≥2. Q need not be annihilated by I: it is generally a sheaf on a first infinitesimal neighborhood, even though the subobject and quotient above are annihilated by I.

## Symmetric-kernel complex

Exactness at Sym²V requires the complete-intersection ideal I to be of linear type, so Sym²I→I² is an isomorphism. This establishes that the quadratic relations are generated by products of source syzygies with source vectors.

For the left kernel, split the source as Re0⊕W, with K⊂W the syzygies of (x,y,z). A tensor in K⊗V killed by symmetric multiplication has no e0 entries and is an alternating 3×3 matrix A with (x,y,z)A=0. Writing its entries as A12, A13, A23, regularity gives A12=zf, A13=−yf, A23=xf for a unique f∈R. Thus the kernel is free of rank one, generated by the complementary-minor bivector.

Globally this line is (det V)⊗(det E)^−1=(det V)⊗O(−5), giving the asserted exact complex. The literal Λ²K should not be substituted for this line at C: its image in the free kernel is I times the free generator. It is the extension of det K from the complement, or equivalently its reflexive determinant, that is the line bundle.

The global-section proof follows: H⁰K=H¹K=0; splitting the complex into short exact sequences and using the intermediate cohomology vanishing of O(−5) gives H⁰J=Sym²V. H⁰Q=0 then gives H⁰Sym²E=Sym²V and dimension 10.

## Stronger naturality and primary multiplication audit

Put A=im(ev), D=im(K⊗V→Sym²V⊗O), and P=(det V)⊗O(−5). There is a commutative diagram of short exact rows:

Top: 0→K⊗V→V⊗V⊗O→A⊗V→0.
Bottom: 0→D→Sym²V⊗O→J→0.

The vertical maps are respectively the defining quotient, symmetric multiplication, and induced multiplication. Naturality of connecting homomorphisms identifies H¹(A⊗V)→H¹J with H²(K⊗V)→H²D. The latter is an isomorphism by 0→P→K⊗V→D→0 and H²P=H³P=0.

Since T is acyclic, H¹A→H¹E is an isomorphism. The composite with J→Sym²E is exactly the multiplication map V⊗H¹E→H¹Sym²E, by the sheaf-level commuting multiplication square. Finally H¹J→H¹Sym²E is injective because H⁰Q=0. Consequently the primary multiplication map is injective. If h¹E=2, its rank and h¹J are both 8.

With the ordinary symmetric-algebra multiplication convention the diagrams commute exactly. A convention inserting an overall nonzero scalar does not alter injectivity. No ambiguous sign can turn this result into a zero map.
