## Result

    z0,...,z7, w0,...,w3.

For these conventions the four actual detector values are

    h=2z6-2w1,
    j=2z7,

    r=2z3-2w3.                                    (1)

`interface.json` contains the exact4-by12 matrix, all twelve sparse homogeneous dual-cycle representatives, basis keys, and hashes binding the two component certificates. This is an exact cohomological coordinate interface. **None of z,w,h,j,p,r is evaluated here.** A coordinate is a pairing with the actual source class, not a known value furnished by a basis construction.

The four detectors remain independent on this invariant target. In fact the eight original and inversion-transported detector classes are independent on the full32-dimensional epsilon target. Thus the original four-dimensional detector span is proved **not** inversion-stable modulo the actual dual boundaries. This resolves the earlier open raw-row/nonclosure question; a four-by-four inversion matrix on that original span cannot exist. The span of the eight original/transported classes is inversion-stable, with inversion exchanging each pair.

The representation and coordinate construction alone prove no source-specific vanishing. They do show that inversion covariance by itself cannot force an original-only linear relation among h,j,p,r.
