# Minor clarification to the previous five-space proof

The current working copy of https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/59a2aa5db85baeb0da1ff467c4f72d77a41ae488/research/hm/universal-polar/full-support-rank-five-dimension-bound/PROOF.md, Section 4, was clarified to handle possible geometric reducibility/monodromy of general plane fibers over a factor curve. An integral total space need not have geometrically integral general fibers. The corrected text uses an irreducible curve in the relative Grassmannian parametrizing compatible plane components and dominating the factor curve generically finitely. Fixing one general plane component and varying the other still forces the whole factor curve into the fixed plane, giving the same forbidden square. Every prior theorem and bound is unchanged.
