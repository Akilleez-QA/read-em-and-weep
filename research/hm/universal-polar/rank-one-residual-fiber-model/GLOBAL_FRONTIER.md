# Exact remaining source of affine dimension eight

Retain the established trilinear setup and the separately proved hypothesis (H): the projective residual rank-drop middle locus has dimension at most two.

The universal K8 theorem and earlier higher-rank bounds imply that every independent-zero component generically of residual rank at least two has affine dimension at most seven. Residual rank zero is already impossible by ../rank-one-boundary-support/PROOF.md, Section7: a correction on all Sym²(C⁴) would induce a nowhere-zero section of Q(2)=T_P³(1), but its top Chern class is 15h³.

The universal K9 bound in PROOF.md gives projective fiber dimension at most three at residual rank one. Therefore, if an independent-zero irreducible component has affine dimension eight, its generic middle has residual rank exactly one, its projective middle image has dimension exactly two, and its generic separately projectivized fiber has dimension exactly three. Indeed three nonzero input scales contribute exactly three dimensions, so the remaining projective dimension is five; under (H) the only way to attain five with a fiber bound of three is 2+3.

This locates the equality case in the previously established global bound eight; it does not claim that the global upper bound itself is new. The older independent-zero dimension-eight theorem is in ../nilpotent-kernel-parameter-locus/PROOF.md, with its own source hypotheses.

The isolated synthetic model in PROOF.md has only one exceptional middle and affine independent-zero dimension six. It proves sharpness of the pointwise fiber bound and eliminates an attempted universal improvement to two, but neither realizes nor excludes the moving middle surface required for affine dimension eight. Its additional source-equivariance hypotheses are not asserted.
