# Independent audit: rank-one trace quadrics and nilpotent curves

Verdict: PASS for Sections 1–6, with no required corrections.

The trace pairing on sl(3) is nondegenerate. The rank-one traceless projective flag locus has dimension three and lies on its null quadric. OG(3,8) has dimension nine and is irreducible; the incidence of its planes containing a point of the flag locus has fibers OG(2,6) of dimension five and total dimension eight. Properness makes its image a proper closed subset. The resulting totally isotropic rank-one-free three-spaces therefore exist.

For such R, the quadratic space R-perp/R is nondegenerate of dimension two. The inverse image of a nonisotropic line is a four-space with radical R and quadratic rank one. Every traceless rank-one matrix is isotropic and would have to lie in R, so the extension remains rank-one-free. This is a rigorous universal existence argument, not an actual structural-kernel construction.

The determinant divisor in the smooth null quadric is smooth along regular nilpotents: the trace-pairing differentials 2A and A² are independent there. Generic transversality under the connected orthogonal group's transitive action gives smooth intersections with general isotropic projective planes, and the earlier incidence excludes the rank-one singular locus. The determinant restriction is nonzero by the imported no-projective-surface theorem. Thus these intersections are smooth irreducible plane cubics. The local equations (h,ell²) give the stated doubled complete-intersection nilpotent scheme, with no claim of a global split ribbon.

The regular-nilpotent filtration has line factors L, LM, LM²; trivial determinant gives (LM)^3 trivial. If T=LM were trivial, the kernel inclusion would extend from the cubic to a vector of linear forms on the plane. Its quadratic kernel identity would then vanish on the entire plane, because the cubic ideal contains no nonzero quadratics. Invertibility on the dense determinant-nonzero open forces the linear vector to vanish identically, a contradiction. Hence T has exact order three. The degree-three assertion for the two flag maps is consistent with the degrees of their tautological lines.

Finally, any linear factor of the determinant cubic on K would supply a three-dimensional determinant-zero rank-one-free net. The audited net theorem would force trace-square rank three on that subspace, contradicting rank one on K. Thus the determinant cubic is irreducible.

The result rules out universal quadratic-rank-at-least-two claims under rank-one-freeness alone. It does not realize an actual K_W or decide its geometry.
