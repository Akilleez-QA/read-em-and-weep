# Independent review: quadratic-generator ambiguity of C

## Verdict

The derivation in `PROOF.md` is correct over the complex numbers, conditional on the PR498 module, the stated normalizer-equivariant identification exterior^2 V = A tensor W, multiplicity one of its contraction multiplication, and the stated transitive action on the 24 nontrivial Heisenberg quotient characters. This is an independent mathematical review, not a formal proof-assistant check or a selected-source computation.

The conclusion is precisely beta_(1,2)(C) = beta_(2,2)(C) in {0,24}. It does not select one value and does not prove purity.

## Checks

1. In internal degree 2, the Koszul complex has the relevant terms exterior^2 T tensor C_0 and T tensor C_1, each of dimension 50. There is neither an incoming term exterior^3 T tensor C_(-1) nor an outgoing term C_2. Therefore the second homology is the kernel and the first homology is the cokernel of D. Their dimensions agree. Minimal quadratic generators of the relation module are Tor_1(C,C_ground)_2, so their number is exactly this common dimension.

2. With contraction i_s(v wedge u) = s(v)u - s(u)v, the trace of t tensor i_s(v wedge u) - s tensor i_t(v wedge u) is 2[s(v)t(u)-s(u)t(v)]. This is twice the natural exterior pairing, with no dimension or sign ambiguity. If p is fixed and alpha_i(eta) is defined by alpha_i(eta)(p(a tensor w)) = a_i*(a)eta(w), the sum over five dual basis vectors is exactly 10 eta(w). Scaling the identification or identifying W* with W only conjugates/scales the nonzero invariant-channel map; injectivity is unaffected.

3. The source is A* tensor A tensor W* as a Heisenberg representation. The invariant subspace is exactly the scalar endomorphism tensor W*, of dimension 2, by irreducibility of A. Thus the displayed k(eta) exhausts the invariant source, rather than merely testing a proper invariant subspace. Nonzero trace on every nonzero k(eta) proves that the invariant kernel is zero.

4. The center acts trivially on the source, and the quotient is finite abelian. Its characters therefore decompose every subrepresentation over C. Each nontrivial character has multiplicity 2 in the source, and normalizer stability plus transitivity forces a common kernel multiplicity r in {0,1,2}. The kernel dimension is 24r. No irreducibility assertion for the full 48-dimensional nontrivial part is needed.

5. Choose t,a with mu(t,a) nonzero. Then t and a are nonzero. For four representatives t_i forming a basis modulo t, the four domain vectors (t wedge t_i) tensor a are independent. Their images modulo the target subspace t tensor C_1 are t_i tensor mu(t,a), which are independent. Consequently rank D is at least 4, contradicting the rank 2 that a 48-dimensional kernel would require. This is a universal argument and contains no selected-instance rank evaluation.

## Minimal-presentation consequence and boundary

Since dim(T tensor A) - dim C_1 = 15, there are exactly 15 minimal linear relations. Since C is supported in degrees 0 and 1, the relation module is generated in degrees at most 2: its entire degree-2 part is S_2 tensor A and all higher parts are its polynomial multiples. Thus the minimal first free module is 15 S(-1) plus b S(-2), with b either 0 or 24. The quadratic summand records relations not generated by the linear ones; it is not legitimate to count all imposed degree-2 relations as minimal.

If b=0, the vanishing of beta_(2,2) rules out all subsequent terms of the linear strand by minimality and exactness of a graded free resolution. Together with the Hilbert numerator and regularity 1 this gives the conditional pure resolution with ranks 5,15,50,75,45,10 and shifts 0,1,3,4,5,6. This remains conditional on b=0. If b=24, this audit establishes no value for the later linear-strand dimensions.
