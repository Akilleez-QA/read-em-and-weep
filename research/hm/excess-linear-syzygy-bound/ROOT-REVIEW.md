It covers the entire two-dimensional Heisenberg-invariant source and therefore excludes every invariant kernel vector.

The remaining source characters occur twice in a transitive orbit of twenty-four characters. The kernel dimension is thus 0, 24 or 48. This proves the stated two alternatives without computing an actual matrix.

Fifteen linear relations and relations generated at most quadratically give the exact first free module.
