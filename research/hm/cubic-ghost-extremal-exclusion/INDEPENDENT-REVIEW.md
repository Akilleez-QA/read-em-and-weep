# Independent review: exclusion of the cubic ghost

Status: independently reviewed mathematical argument, conditional on the pinned representation and presentation inputs. Not formally checked. No selected source-instance evaluation or numerical multiplication matrix was used.

## Inputs checked in the argument

Work over C with S = Sym(T), dim T = 5. Write W for W′ and E = A*. The relevant inputs are S₂ = E ⊗ Sym²W, L₂ = E ⊗ W, and T ⊗ E = A ⊗ (U ⊕ W), with U ⊗ W = R₆ and W ⊗ W = 1 ⊕ Sym²W. Consequently A ⊗ Sym²W occurs exactly once in T ⊗ L₂, through the A ⊗ W channel in T ⊗ E. The universal relation map has columns (-β, α) and (-γ, β), where α, β, γ are the E-valued quadratic columns corresponding to x², xy, y². Each column is injective on E, and their fifteen coefficients together form a basis of S₂.

## Coefficient audit

Assume the optional cubic second-syzygy module is A ⊗ Sym²W. Let F: A ⊗ W → T ⊗ E be the summand inclusion, and fix a ∈ A. Set u = F(a ⊗ x) and v = F(a ⊗ y). Multiplicity one identifies its three symmetric tensors with u ⊗ x, u ⊗ y + v ⊗ x, v ⊗ y, up to a common nonzero scalar. Their images under multiplication into S₃ ⊗ W vanish.

The first and last give uβ = uα = vγ = vβ = 0. The mixed tensor gives uγ + vβ = 0 and uβ + vα = 0. Thus uγ = vα = 0 as well. As a varies, all ten dimensions of image F are therefore linear syzygies of each individual column α, β, γ. These are polynomial identities deduced from the representation inclusion, not evaluations of selected coefficients.

## Extremal-growth audit

For any five-dimensional subspace Q ⊂ S₂, let J = (Q). Then h₂(S/J) = 10 = binom(5,2). Macaulay gives h₃(S/J) ≤ binom(6,3) = 20, so dim(TQ) ≥ 15 and dim ker(T ⊗ Q → S₃) ≤ 10.

If there are ten independent linear syzygies, equality holds. Since J is generated in degree two, Gotzmann persistence applies and gives h_d(S/J) = binom(d+3,3) for every d ≥ 2. Hence dim(S/J) = 4 and ht J = 1.

Choose a homogeneous height-one minimal prime of J. Since S is a polynomial UFD, it is generated by a homogeneous irreducible polynomial f. All elements of Q are divisible by f. Thus deg f ≤ 2. If deg f = 2, then Q ⊂ C f, contrary to dim Q = 5. Therefore f is linear and Q = f S₁ by dimension. This proof requires no claim about saturation, embedded components, or classification of degree-one schemes.

Applying this lemma to the three columns yields Qα = ℓα S₁, Qβ = ℓβ S₁, Qγ = ℓγ S₁. Their sum lies in B S₁ for B = span(ℓα,ℓβ,ℓγ), dim B ≤ 3. The largest possible degree-two dimension of B S₁ is 15 - binom(3,2) = 12. It therefore cannot equal the fifteen-dimensional S₂. This contradiction excludes the optional cubic syzygy module.

## Conclusion and limits

Given the established binary alternative r ∈ {0,15}, the argument forces r = 0. The H presentation is then purely quadratic. In the established Fitting construction its maximal minors are quartics, so no independent quintic Fitting generator remains. This does not determine the separate middle binary parameter s, nor assert higher-order deformation or algebraicity conclusions.
