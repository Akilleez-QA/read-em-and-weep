# Independent addendum audit: relative rank 27 and rank 12

## Verdict

The proposed relative ranks and inner-swap sign are correct for the canonical geometric endomorphism linearization. On the good base, the both-odd fiber-deck summand of R¹r_*End₀F has rank 27 and splits under the inner swap into ranks 15 (even) and 12 (odd). The rank-12 odd summand is a possible recipient for restricted retained classes through Leray; its rank is not the dimension of its H¹ or a count of nonzero obstruction values.

## 1. Scalar trace and Riemann–Roch

Let E be a stable normalized HM plane restriction, with c₁(E)=-H and c₂(E)=4H². Write A=End(E), A₀=End₀(E). Then ch(A)=4-15H² and

χ(A(t))=2t²+6t-11.

Stability and Serre duality give, for t=0,-1,-2,-3,

h¹(A(t))=(12,15,15,12).

At t=0, h⁰(A)=1; at t=-3, h²(A(-3))=1. Both endpoint scalar classes must be retained when deriving these H¹ values from Euler characteristics. For A₀ the H¹ values are the same, since O, O(-1), O(-2), O(-3) all have vanishing H¹ on P². All four A₀ twists have h⁰=h²=0.

The Euler sequence gives χ(A⊗Ω¹)=3χ(A(-1))-χ(A)=-34. Its h⁰ vanishes by Ω¹↪O(-1)³; its h² also vanishes because (Ω¹)*⊗O(-3)≅Ω¹ and A*=A. Hence h¹(A⊗Ω¹)=34. Trace removes the scalar Ω¹ summand, whose h¹ is 1, so h¹(A₀⊗Ω¹)=33.

Using the degree-eight pushforward and ordering fiber deck characters (++),(-+),(+-),(--), one obtains

ranks R¹End(F): (27,34,34,27), total 122;
ranks R¹End₀(F): (27,33,33,27), total 120.

This agrees with χ(End₀(F|fiber))=-120 and h⁰=h²=0. The two scalar H¹ classes occur in the two single-odd deck sectors, as expected from H¹(O_{C²}).

## 2. Inner-swap linearization

The both-odd deck summand before the symmetric quotient is the external product L=O(-2)⊠O(-2). Its natural factor-swap action is positive on the product y₁y₂ of the two odd generators. These generators are degree-zero functions; there is no graded/Koszul minus sign in their interchange.

With σ:P¹×P¹→P², identify L=σ*O(-2) with this natural linearization. The invariant/anti-invariant decomposition σ_*O=O_+⊕O(-1)_- therefore gives

σ_*L=O(-2)_+⊕O(-3)_-.

The plane restriction E, and hence End₀(E), has the canonical trivial action under this swap because σ is invariant. The line twist N in F cancels canonically in End(F); an equivariant line-linearization scalar cannot reverse the resulting conjugation action. Finite pushforward is exact and introduces no extra cohomological sign. Thus on fiber H¹ the both-odd sector splits into

swap-even: H¹(P²,A₀(-2)), dimension 15;
swap-odd: H¹(P²,A₀(-3)), dimension 12.

The same distinction holds for full End because the scalar line twists have no H¹. Confusing this finite-pushforward calculation with the swap sign on a Künneth product of two H¹(O_C) classes would give the wrong reasoning; that latter product concerns degree two, not the present H¹ summands.

## 3. Relative eigensubbundles and Leray

On B°, R⁰r_*G=R²r_*G=0 and R¹r_*G is locally free and commutes with base change. The vertical subgroup generated by the two fiber deck involutions and their factor swap acts over the identity on B°. Character idempotents in characteristic zero therefore give actual subbundles. Denote by W the both-odd, swap-odd subbundle; it has rank 12.

For any open U⊂B°, one has the natural equivariant identification

H²(r⁻¹U,G)≅H¹(U,(R¹r_*G)|U).

Consequently a class whose restriction is both-odd under the fiber decks and odd under the inner swap maps into H¹(U,W|U). In particular this applies to the stipulated full-sign retained sector when the supplied D₈ character ε really restricts to − on that inner swap. This last character identification is an input from the stipulated representation data, not something inferable from RR alone.

Any further character conditions acting nontrivially on the base still have to be imposed on H¹(B°,W). A permutation exchanging the two factor blocks exchanges the two fibrations rather than acting vertically on a single one. The rank-12 calculation does not alone implement those additional constraints or identify the dimension of the fully retained global space.

If U is affine, H¹(U,W|U)=0; indeed H²(r⁻¹U,G)=0 for every tracefree class, regardless of its character. Over non-affine B° the H¹ group can be nonzero and is not determined by the bundle rank. Restricted classes may also vanish on B° yet remain nonzero globally because of the excluded locus. No value of any retained obstruction has been computed.

## 4. Premise and scope

All assertions concern the actual geometric bundle F=N⊗f*HM with canonical induced endomorphism action. A merely numerical/index-model identification cannot replace this premise. The affine-good-open localization applies to every actual global tracefree H² class without requiring a vertical quantization origin. It does not establish scalar compensation, all-orders module existence, gluing, or global vanishing.

This audit uses universal RR, standard coherent cohomology, the already checked pushforward, and equivariant bookkeeping only. No actual HM/source/jet evaluation or experiment was performed.
