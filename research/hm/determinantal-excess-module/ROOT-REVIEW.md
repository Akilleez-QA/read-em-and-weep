# Root mathematical review

The exact module identification follows from the unique copy of V tensor T_4 in T tensor A tensor W′. Both multiplication maps are nonzero, so target rescaling identifies the two two-step modules. The ordinary decomposition into two copies is not confused with a normalizer-equivariant decomposition.

The linear annihilator is zero by irreducibility of T and nonzero multiplication. The relation-invariance proof of degree-zero rigidity was independently checked: if every endomorphism of A extended, matrix units would make each coefficient of a nonzero linear relation annihilate all generators. This contradicts the zero linear annihilator. The scalar/full dichotomy follows from the multiplicity-one Heisenberg character spaces and transitivity on the twenty-four nontrivial characters.

Generation and support exclude all endomorphism degrees except zero and one. Every degree-one map from A to V tensor W extends, because products of two variables act as zero. Its square vanishes. The resulting fifty-one-dimensional local algebra has no nontrivial idempotents, proving ungraded indecomposability as well as graded indecomposability. Homogeneous decomposition of any ungraded map is valid for these finite graded modules. Passing to two copies gives the full matrix algebra and dimension 204.

This proof is conditional on the pinned inputs and has independent mathematical review, not formal proof-assistant checking. It makes no claim about a Galois-twisted primary-module identification or a full minimal resolution, and does not evaluate the original fixed pair or source.
