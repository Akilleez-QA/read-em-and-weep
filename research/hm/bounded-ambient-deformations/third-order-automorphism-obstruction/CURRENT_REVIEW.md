## Fixed-product third order (04)

Expansion at degrees two and three yields d_H T=0 and d_H S=L_T mu1 with exactly the displayed signs. On a commutative algebra the first expression in the latter equation is symmetric and the second alternating. Characteristic zero therefore forces both to vanish. A common change of mu3 cancels, while different chart products introduce their stated difference. The conjugation calculation gives the antisymmetric mu2 contribution; the b-squared terms cancel by Leibniz. The scalar unit primitive is an additional premise of the descent corollary. Closedness of the Poisson defect is not pointwise vanishing.
