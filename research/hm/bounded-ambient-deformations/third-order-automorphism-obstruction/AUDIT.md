# Independent audit

**PASS.** Independently reviewed PROOF.md Sections 1–4 against the pinned source conventions and the new public scalar-unit comment. No outstanding correction.

For the fixed scalar star product, direct expansion gives d_H T=0 and d_H S=L_T mu1 with the stated Hochschild sign. The left-hand side of the second equation is symmetric for any linear S on a commutative algebra; its right-hand side is antisymmetric. In characteristic zero both vanish. Thus the fixed ansatz extends as an algebra automorphism exactly when T preserves the Poisson bracket and S is a derivation. The inverse and unitality statements are correct. This argument would not apply unchanged to a matrix classical algebra, and the proof explicitly limits it to the scalar algebra.

For distinct local products, the differences mu2^j-mu2^i and mu3^j-mu3^i enter with the stated signs. The antisymmetric compensation and remaining symmetric Hochschild equation are necessary separate conditions; the text correctly avoids asserting existence of compatible changed products.

The inner-conjugation expansion is correct. The h³ term is the antisymmetric part of mu2 on (b,f), plus {a,f}-b{b,f}; it contains neither the third-order unit coefficient nor mu3. Taking a=b²/2 cancels the latter bracket terms. The specified descent orientation therefore gives delta S=C2(b,-). Scalar Moyal mu2 is symmetric under the two index exchanges, so delta S=0.

Consequently, conditional on the separately stated regular solution of the scalar unit equation, the fixed displayed two-jet extends modulo h⁴ with the same Moyal products if and only if all prescribed T_ij preserve pi. Sufficiency uses S=0 and that scalar correction. Necessity is a cochain-level condition, not merely vanishing of a cohomology class. The closedness of L_T pi follows from delta T Hamiltonian and Jacobi, but is correctly distinguished from pointwise vanishing.
