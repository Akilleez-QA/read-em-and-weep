# Exact antecedent closure for the bounded joint Čech model

## Decision and scope

The missing *public theorem bindings* for the geometric applications of the secondary-class and actual-joint-model manuscripts are now closed by the editions below. This is a composition of published mathematical theorems, with their stated source, localization, marking and characteristic-zero hypotheses retained. It is not a source execution, a verification of an arbitrary implementation, a construction of a mixed module, or a new value/vanishing claim. The conditional strict-DGLA realization remains conditional. The direct bounded equation model does not require that realization.

The new atlas refinement is [PR404, proof](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/831c290c4d5b212232e4b332e6c96cd8cd667a91/research/hm/coarse-atlas-refinement/PROOF.md). The separate negative-inertia Tor calculation in PR405 is a category guard, not an additional premise of this closure. It must not be used to assert descent or derived comparison for all ramified equivariant modules.

Every exact repository path, commit and blob read for this closure is in JOINT-PUBLIC-PINS.json. An inherited logical source title is resolved by the claim-level mapping here, not by asserting that a different public proof has identical bytes.

## 6. Publication and mathematical boundary

The former unresolved aliases for atlas effectiveness, marked full ambient, prescribed pure boundary, complete same-source ten-term residual, normalized obstruction transport and retained projectors have exact published theorem bindings above. They need not remain a missing-navigation hold for either manuscript. All mathematical hypotheses explicitly retained by those theorems remain hypotheses; no general ramified descent, unmarked mixed classification or source-runtime certification is newly claimed.

- Strict-DGLA rectification or a family-level secondary pairing beyond the manuscripts' stated hypotheses.

- Deletion of the ambient correction, coefficient-input terms, overlap terms or scalar-gerbe terms.

- A fresh run of any historical checker or actual HM/source evaluation.
