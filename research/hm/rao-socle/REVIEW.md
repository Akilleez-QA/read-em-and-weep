## Checks of the new argument

1. **Untwisted adjoint cohomology.** Twisting the symmetric-square monad by one computes B. PR455's two repeated Bott weights make the nonconstant central Schur summands acyclic, and every other term has no positive cohomology. Thus only the complex of global sections matters and H^i(B)=0 for i>=3. Its Heisenberg-invariant terms in degrees zero, one and two are respectively 1+U, 1+U and W'. Simplicity of F makes H^0(B)=0, so the first invariant map is an isomorphism. The second is zero because W' is inequivalent to 1 and U. The independent upper bound h^2(B)<=2 from PR455 then implies H^2(B)=W', not merely a lower bound of two. The Euler characteristic is -22: ch(B)=3-15h^2+(225/12)h^4 and td_2(P^4)=35h^2/12. Therefore H^1(B) has dimension 24 and no Heisenberg invariants. Transitivity on the 24 nontrivial characters makes it an irreducible normalizer representation, each character occurring once.

2. **Rao groups and equivariant twists.** In the degree-five Eagon–Northcott sequence, equality dim I_5=6 forces H^1 of the intermediate kernel sheaf to vanish. Consequently the two-dimensional H^2(B(-5))=W' injects into Lambda^3(V_s) tensor H^2(F(-2))=T_4 tensor W=W'+R_6. The quotient is R_6, and the remaining quotient is H^3(B(-5))=H^1(B)^*, the irreducible 24-dimensional representation. Thus the degree-five Rao group is R_6 plus that 24-dimensional summand. At degree six, the Rao group is H^3(B(-4))=H^1(B(-1))^*=V*. The last identification follows from the Bott weight (1,1,1,1,0), which represents Lambda^4(V*)=V. Equivariant Serre duality introduces no determinant factor because the normalizer lies in SL(V); the section-space determinant line is trivial.

3. **Actual multiplication, not an assumed scalar.** The nonnegative section module of the disjoint union of lines is the direct sum of their homogeneous coordinate rings and is generated in degree zero. Its quotient by the image of S is exactly the Rao module, also generated in degree zero. Therefore multiplication from degree five to degree six is onto. Its adjoint has target V tensor V*=1 plus an irreducible 24-dimensional representation. Equivariance kills the R_6 summand of the source. Surjectivity of multiplication makes the adjoint nonzero, so its restriction to the remaining irreducible 24-dimensional summand is injective. The degree-five socle is consequently exactly R_6, of dimension six. The degree-six Rao group is wholly socle because the degree-seven group vanishes.

4. **Minimal-resolution indexing.** For R=S/I, graded local duality identifies Ext^4_S(R,S(-5)) with the graded dual of the Rao module. Since pd R=4, this Ext module is the cokernel of the final dual differential. Minimality puts the image in the homogeneous maximal ideal times the last dual free module. Accordingly its minimal generators in degree -5 count beta_{4,10}(R)=beta_{3,10}(I), and duality identifies their number with the degree-five Rao socle dimension. Hence beta_{3,10}(I)=6. Comparing with PR470's independently established 5+b gives b=1. Likewise the five-dimensional degree-six socle gives beta_{3,11}(I)=5. This argument does not cancel equal shifts in different homological degrees.

5. **Rank-one exclusion of degree-nine first syzygies.** After b=1, the degree -5 part of the final dual map has one-dimensional source and 31-dimensional target, with 30-dimensional cokernel M_5^*. Its restriction to the rank-one S(5) summand is therefore nonzero. Because S is a domain and the target is free, that restriction is injective as an S-module map, not merely on its constant generator. A degree -4 generator from S(4)^a in the preceding dual term cannot map to S(4) summands by minimality; it could only map into this S(5). The complex identity and injectivity force that image to vanish. The previous dual term has zero degree -4 part, so these a generators would survive in Ext^2. Graded local duality and dim(S/I)=2 give Ext^2=0, forcing a=0. All dual shifts and the dimensions 31 and 30 have been checked. This rules out a ghost pair structurally and does not assume cancellation.

## Scope and residual dependency

The argument imports the pinned monad, cohomology upper bound, Eagon–Northcott sequence, quintic saturation, Hilbert function and constrained resolution. It proves b=1 and a=0, determining the full minimal graded Betti numbers. It does not provide explicit coefficient matrices for the minimal differentials.
