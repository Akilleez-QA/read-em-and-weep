2. **Root centrality.** Pure root triple units reduce to 1, so belong to 1+εO. Such units are central because the product has only one square-zero parameter. Thus the root maps form an ordinary algebra cocycle. The actual root group multipliers are constant signs by PR126, so conjugation is unchanged and the strict group action survives. This inference is not made for arbitrary higher-order units.
3. **Regular existence.** On each actual affine inverse image the finite affine-intersection Čech complex computes zero positive coherent cohomology. Hence both β and ℓ are regular sections on the actual covers. Averaging leaves their coboundaries unchanged. The displayed denominator-clearing sign is δβ=c, from c_ijk−c_ljk+c_lik−c_lij=0.
4. **Direction and band sign.** With b-to-a transport, T^a=(Q^a)⁻¹Q^bT^b, hence d^ab=β^b−β^a. The section equation for 1+ℓ is equivalent to ℓ_i−ℓ_j=d_ij. The triple coefficient is γ=δ_aℓ. In total differential D=δ_a+(−1)^pδ_i, Dβ=d+c and Dℓ=γ+d, so c+Dℓ−Dβ=γ. No inverse gerbe is silently substituted.
5. **Trace scale.** Finite duality's unnormalized evaluation trace differs from inverse pullback by the degree 8. The payload explicitly uses PR128's inverse-pullback marking (1/8)Tr, and hence product marking (1/64)Tr. This resolves the scale ambiguity of an abstract canonical-line isomorphism without evaluating a module operation.
6. **Mixed coefficient square.** On a chosen product affine chart take arbitrary C-linear complements A_z=J_z⊕V_z and A_h=J_h⊕V_h. The four tensor summands directly identify the intersection of the two pure ideals with J_z tensor J_h. Multiplication both lands in and spans this summand. This is the full mixed line; it is killed only upon passing to the pure-axis fiber product. It is not a statement about arbitrary products of invariant ideals.
7. **Module lifting.** In a supplied module, the nilpotent layers are locally finite sums of the quasi-coherent coefficient layers. H¹ of the filtered kernel vanishes on each affine inverse image. Averaged frame lifts are frames by matrix invertibility modulo a nilpotent ideal, also with left-module/opposite-ring conventions. This proves the actual invariant-frame hypothesis, not the existence of an unknown module.
8. **Derived lemma.** The comparison is checked on those frames: both derived reductions become (A/J)^r in degree zero, and the map is the identity. The identification is natural under matrices with entries in A and therefore glues. The right-hand expression uses ordinary pushforward of a derived object already concentrated in degree zero, not an unjustified exactness claim for all deformed sheaves. D need not be flat over A.
9. **Negative example.** The companion ramified calculation has precisely the excluded nontrivial inertia. Its positive Tor tail is R/(x); in the transverse two-factor case the intersection multiplicity in homological degree n is n+1.
