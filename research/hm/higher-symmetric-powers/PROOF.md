# All higher symmetric powers of the dual-cohomology module

Work over C. Put S=Sym(T), dim T=5, and let H_j=H²(End_0(F)(j−5)), W=H_0=W′, dim W=2. Symmetric powers below are over S, with their original polynomial grading; p denotes symmetric-power degree, not polynomial degree. Write m=S_+.

## Inputs and conclusion

Use degree-zero generation, no linear relations, and the quadratic presentation from PR482; the scalar-annihilator identification R_2=S_2=A*⊗Sym²W and compatible perfect symmetric pairing b of total degree five from PR488; the universal quadratic relation inclusion from PR490; and End_S(H)_d=R_d for d=0,1,2 from PR491. Determinant factors of W are trivial for the normalizer under discussion. The proof does not use or choose the unresolved cubic and middle Betti parameters.

The complete Hilbert functions are

- Sym³_S H: (4,20,30,10), supported in polynomial degrees 0 through 3;
- Sym⁴_S H: (5,25,35,5), supported in polynomial degrees 0 through 3;
- for every p≥5, Sym^p_S H: (p+1,5(p+1),5(p+3)), supported in polynomial degrees 0 through 2.

In particular m⁴ Sym³H=m⁴ Sym⁴H=0 and m³ Sym^pH=0 for p≥5. Their last displayed components are nonzero, so these annihilation exponents are sharp. Moreover Ann_S(Sym³H)=Ann_S(Sym⁴H)=I_3+m⁴, and Ann_S(Sym^p H)=m³ exactly for every p≥5, where I_3+m⁴ denotes the homogeneous ideal generated by I_3 and m⁴. The assertions concern symmetric powers of the actual S-module H, not higher deformation obstructions or an evaluation of the original source instance.

## Degrees zero, one and two

Set L=ker(S⊗W→H). The presentation of Sym^p H has relations equal to the image of L⊗Sym^(p−1)W in S⊗Sym^p W. Thus the first two components are Sym^p W and T⊗Sym^p W, of dimensions p+1 and 5(p+1).

In degree two, L_2=A*⊗W is included in S_2⊗W=A*⊗Sym²W⊗W by the lower Clebsch–Gordan inclusion

r(x)=x²⊗y−xy⊗x,
r(y)=xy⊗y−y²⊗x.

This universal inclusion is pinned in PR490. It is not a choice or evaluation of actual HM polynomial coefficients. After tensoring by Sym^(p−1)W and multiplying in the final W-factor, the map W⊗Sym^(p−1)W→Sym²W⊗Sym^pW is injective: for inputs r(x)a+r(y)b, the coefficients of x²,xy,y² in its first factor are ya, yb−xa, −xb. The first and last vanish only when a=b=0, because multiplication by x or y in the polynomial algebra Sym(W) is injective.

Multiplication Sym²W⊗Sym^pW→Sym^(p+2)W kills the image and is surjective. The domain has dimension 3(p+1), the injected relation space dimension 2p, and the target dimension p+3. Hence its kernel is exactly that relation image. Consequently

(Sym^p_S H)_2 = A*⊗Sym^(p+2)W,

of dimension 5(p+3), for every p≥1. Higher-degree defining relations of H cannot alter this degree-two calculation.

## Reduction of higher-degree forms to scalars

Every Sym^p H is generated in degree zero and vanishes in polynomial degrees greater than five, because H does. For p≥3 and 3≤n≤5, let f be a linear functional on (Sym^p H)_n. Equivalently, f is a symmetric S-balanced p-linear form supported on total polynomial degree n.

Fix p−2 arguments in W. The remaining balanced symmetric bilinear form, together with b, defines a degree d=5−n endomorphism of H. By PR491, d=0,1,2 implies this endomorphism is multiplication by a unique scalar q in R_d. Varying the fixed arguments gives a symmetric multilinear map

q: W^(p−2) → R_d.

Symmetry between one of these arguments and the next argument is precisely

q(w_1,...,w_(p−3),a)b = q(w_1,...,w_(p−3),b)a in H_d

for all generators a,b in W. This equality has degree d, since W=H_0.

Conversely, every such q determines f. Here is the necessary relation check, which cannot be replaced merely by dimensions. For a relation Σ a_i w_i=0 in H and fixed other generator arguments, let c=Σ a_i q(...,w_i) in R. For every z in W, the displayed compatibility gives

cz=Σ a_i q(...,w_i)z=q(...,z)Σ a_i w_i=0.

Since W generates H and R=S/Ann(H), c=0 in R. Thus q extends S-balancedly in each argument through H, and

f(h_1,...,h_p)=b(q(h_1,...,h_(p−2))h_(p−1),h_p)

is well-defined. Compatibility and symmetry of b make it symmetric in all arguments. These constructions are mutually inverse, because H is generated by W. This proves the reduction without any uncomputed scalar-map rank assumption.

## Vanishing in degrees four and five

For n=4 or 5, d=1 or 0. The map S_d⊗W→H_d is injective because H has no defining relations below degree two. Fix p−3 generator arguments. The compatibility q(...,x)y=q(...,y)x therefore holds already in S_d⊗W. Independence of x and y forces both scalars to vanish. Multilinearity then gives q=0, hence (Sym^p H)_4=(Sym^p H)_5=0 for p≥3. Together with the degree-five support bound, this proves all degree≥4 vanish.

## The cubic component and its two successive exclusions

Now n=3 and d=2. The degree-two quotient S_2⊗W→H_2 is, after removing the A* factor, multiplication Sym²W⊗W→Sym³W: its kernel is exactly the displayed r(W). Compatibility therefore becomes an identity of ordinary binary cubic forms. Let m=p−2. For each A* coefficient, q is a symmetric m-linear map W^m→Sym²W satisfying

q(w_1,...,w_(m−1),x)y=q(w_1,...,w_(m−1),y)x.

For m=1, coprimality of x and y implies q(x)=xℓ and q(y)=yℓ for a unique ℓ∈W. Thus the functional space is A*⊗W, of dimension10, proving dim(Sym³H)_3=10.

For m=2, write q(x,x)=xℓ_x, q(x,y)=yℓ_x=xℓ_y, q(y,y)=yℓ_y. Coprimality gives ℓ_x=cx and ℓ_y=cy, so

q(x,x)=cx², q(x,y)=cxy, q(y,y)=cy².

Thus the functional space is A*, of dimension5, proving dim(Sym⁴H)_3=5.

For m=3, fixing the first input to x or y reduces to the preceding m=2 case. Hence q(x,x,y)=c_x xy but also q(y,x,x)=c_y x². Symmetry forces c_x y=c_y x, so c_x=c_y=0. Therefore q=0. For m>3, fix m−3 generator arguments and apply the m=3 exclusion; every value is zero. This proves (Sym^p H)_3=0 for every p≥5.

## Exact annihilators

For p≥5 the proved support gives m³⊆Ann_S(Sym^p H). Conversely no nonzero degree-zero or degree-one scalar annihilates the module, since its first two components have no relations. A degree-two scalar a∈S_2=A*⊗Sym²W acts on the generating space Sym^pW by binary multiplication into A*⊗Sym^(p+2)W. If a≠0, a linear functional on A* extracts a nonzero binary quadratic. Its product with any nonzero binary form of degree p is nonzero. Therefore a does not annihilate the generators. The annihilator is homogeneous, so there are no additional elements below degree three. Thus

Ann_S(Sym^p H)=m³ for every p≥5.

For p=3,4, the same low-degree argument excludes annihilators of degree at most two, and all degrees at least four annihilate. Since I=Ann(H) annihilates every symmetric power, the degree-three scalar action factors through R_3=S_3/I_3=A⊗J_5, an irreducible normalizer representation from PR488. This action is not zero: Sym^pH is generated in degree zero and its degree-three component is nonzero, so that component equals S_3 times the generating space. Its kernel in R_3 is normalizer-stable, hence zero by irreducibility. Thus the degree-three annihilator is exactly I_3 and

Ann_S(Sym³H)=Ann_S(Sym⁴H)=I_3+m⁴.

Here I_3+m⁴ means the ideal with degree-three part I_3, all components of degrees at least four, and zero components below degree three. The representation argument uses no selected scalar evaluation.

No assertion is made about choosing the remaining Betti parameters, realizing every possible Betti table, the original fixed-pair quotient, selected source values, higher-order lifting, or algebraicity. The new result determines the complete higher symmetric-power Hilbert functions and gives sharp uniform nilpotence bounds independent of those unresolved choices.
