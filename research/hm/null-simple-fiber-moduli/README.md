## Open boundary and corrections

Deck equivariance suffices; the Alexander–Whitney cup representative need not be literally swap-odd. Fiberwise existence is not gluing, not fixed all-order Moyal existence and not a mixed-value calculation. Endomorphism-sheaf equality does not identify root-twisted module lifting problems. The cited general moduli theorems do not establish the missing coefficient-valued theorem.
