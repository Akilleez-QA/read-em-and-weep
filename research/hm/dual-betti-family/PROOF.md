# A two-parameter minimal Betti family for dual HM cohomology

Work over C and let H_j=H^2(End_0(F)(j-5)), K_j=H^1(End_0(F)(j-1)), and S=Sym(T), where dim T=5. All multiplications are the actual cohomological actions. PR482 at 30c2f32418bbe68ad5b2635ee424d698f329fe8d proves that H is generated by W' in degree zero, is self-dual with complementary degree five, and has a complete presentation with ten quadratic relations and r cubic relations, where r is zero or fifteen. PR479 identifies the cubic relation and second-syzygy spaces with the same optional A tensor U' representation.

We derive the following full family of minimal graded free modules in a resolution of H, with an additional parameter s in {0,1}:

F_0=2S,
F_1=10S(-2)+rS(-3),
F_2=rS(-3)+20S(-4)+sS(-5),
F_3=sS(-5)+20S(-6)+rS(-7),
F_4=rS(-7)+10S(-8),
F_5=2S(-10).

The r and s alternatives are not claimed independent or realized. This theorem bounds the full Betti table of the actual module to this family; it does not determine its differentials or choose its parameters. Equal-degree summands in adjacent free modules are genuine minimal-resolution possibilities, not units available for cancellation.

## 1. Self-duality and the possible second syzygy degrees

The actual Serre-compatible perfect pairing gives

Tor_i(H,C)_d = Tor_(5-i)(H,C)_(10-d)* tensor det T.

The determinant factor is trivial for the HM normalizer. PR482 determines F_0 and F_1, so it also determines F_5 and F_4. H has no linear relations. Minimality therefore makes F_2 start no earlier than degree three and F_3 no earlier than degree four. By duality, F_2 can have generators only in degrees three through six. Its degree-three part is the optional A tensor U', of dimension r, by PR479.

It remains to determine its degree-four dimension and bound degree five; degree six will then follow from the Hilbert function.

## 2. A twenty-dimensional degree-four second syzygy space

Tensor the exact coordinate Koszul sheaf complex by B(1), where B=End_0(F). Its hypercohomology spectral sequence has

E_1^(-i,q)=Lambda^i T tensor H^q(B(1-i)),

with d_a of bidegree (a,1-a). The q=1 row has internal K degree two; the q=2 row has internal H degree six. The three relevant E_2 positions are

E_2^(-5,3)=T,
E_2^(-3,2)=Tor_3(H,C)_6,
E_2^(-1,1)=Tor_1(K,C)_2=T tensor U'.

For the first equality, Lambda^5 T is trivial and H^3(B(-4))=H^1(B(-1))*=V*=T. Its next horizontal term has H^3(B(-3))=H^1(B(-2))*=0, so it survives to E_2 unchanged. The last equality is PR476's fifteen quadratic relations of K.

The first term has no incoming differential. Its only possible outgoing maps besides the indicated d_2 are d_3 to Tor_2(K,C)_2=0 and d_4 to a subquotient of T tensor H^0(B)=0. The first vanishing follows from the injective Euler relation and independent quadratic lifts of PR476, as explicitly checked in PR482; the second follows from simplicity.

The middle term has no other incoming differential, since any source for d_a with a>=3 lies outside the complex. Its only other outgoing map is d_3 to H^0(B(1))=0. That positive-twist vanishing is audited in PR479 using the pinned cohomology dimensions and Riemann–Roch.

The final term has no outgoing differential. Its other possible incoming maps start at (-4,3) and (-5,4). These E_1 terms vanish respectively by H^3(B(-3))=0 and H^4(B(-4))=H^0(B(-1))*=0. All later sources are outside the complex.

The zero abutment consequently gives an exact sequence

0 -> T -> Tor_3(H,C)_6 -> T tensor U' -> 0.

Finite-group semisimplicity and duality identify

Tor_3(H,C)_6=T tensor (1+U'),
Tor_2(H,C)_4=V tensor (1+U').

In particular the degree-four second syzygy space has dimension twenty. This computes an actual Koszul homology representation; it does not infer a multiplication map from a dimension coincidence.

## 3. The possible scalar middle ghost

Now use the coordinate Koszul complex for B(0). The q=1 row has internal K degree one, and the q=2 row has internal H degree five. The left end of its q=3 row is

H^3(B(-5)) -> Lambda^4 T tensor H^3(B(-4)) -> 0.

The spaces have dimensions twenty-four and twenty-five. Under Serre duality and Lambda^4 T=T*, its first map is dual to the actual surjection T tensor K_0 -> K_1. Thus it is injective. Its cokernel is the dual of the one-dimensional Euler relation and is the trivial normalizer representation. Consequently

E_2^(-5,3)=0,    E_2^(-4,3)=C.

Consider E_2^(-3,2)=Tor_3(H,C)_5. Its incoming d_2 has the zero source just computed; all higher incoming sources are outside the complex. Its outgoing d_2 lands in E_2^(-1,1)=Tor_1(K,C)_1=C. Its only other possible outgoing map is d_3 to H^0(B)=0. The zero abutment forces this d_2 to be injective. Hence

s=dim Tor_3(H,C)_5=dim Tor_2(H,C)_5 is either zero or one.

When nonzero, both spaces are trivial representations. The same spectral sequence describes the unresolved alternative precisely. The one-dimensional term E_2^(-4,3) may map by d_2 to Tor_2(H,C)_5 at (-2,2); the latter has no other incoming differential and no outgoing differential to a nonzero term. Explicitly, its outgoing d_2 target is Tor_0(K,C)_1=0 because K is generated in degree zero; its possible incoming d_3 source is a subquotient of H^4(B(-5))=H^0(B)*=0 by simplicity; later sources or targets lie outside the complex. Thus this d_2 is onto. If s=1, both indicated d_2 maps are isomorphisms and these four terms disappear at E_3. If s=0, the two outer scalar terms survive, and d_3 from (-4,3) to (-1,1) must be an isomorphism to achieve zero abutment.

In particular the middle ghost is not forced to occur merely because the Euler relation exists. A surviving third differential can kill the Euler class. No value for this transgression is asserted here.

## 4. Elimination of every other Betti entry

The Hilbert function of H is 2(1+t)^5. Its Hilbert numerator over the five-variable ring S is therefore 2(1-t^2)^5. The coefficient of t^4 is twenty.

At degree four, F_0, F_1, F_4 and F_5 have no contribution. Section 2 gives dim Tor_2(H,C)_4=20. Thus the coefficient equation is

20 = 20 - dim Tor_3(H,C)_4.

Therefore Tor_3(H,C)_4=0. By duality Tor_2(H,C)_6=0. Section 1 already excludes all further degrees of F_2, and duality excludes the corresponding degrees of F_3. This proves the displayed entire Betti family.

The degree-four and degree-six twenty-dimensional terms are respectively V tensor (1+U') and T tensor (1+U'). The optional cubic terms are A tensor U', and their complementary degree-seven terms are A* tensor U'. The possible two middle degree-five terms are trivial. These refinements concern minimal generators, not a direct-sum decomposition of the module.
