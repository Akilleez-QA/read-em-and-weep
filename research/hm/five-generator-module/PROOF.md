# Five generators for the full HM adjoint cohomology module

Work over C on P(V)=P^4. Let F be the normalized Horrocks--Mumford bundle, B=End_0(F), T=V*, S=Sym(T), and dim V=5. Put K_j=H^1(B(j-1)), with its actual cohomological S-action. We prove that K is generated by K_0=V, and determine its minimal relations through degree two: one linear Euler relation and fifteen quadratic relations of type V* tensor U'. Higher-degree relations are not determined.

## 1. The actual H^2 multiplication

Use the normalizer notation of PR471: A=V_1, A*=V_3, W,W' the two-dimensional quotient representations, U'=Sym^2W'. Let L=Omega^2(2). The graded symmetric square J of the normalized monad A O(-1) -> W tensor L -> A* O computes B(-1). Its rightmost term in degree two is Lambda^2A* tensor O.

PR464 identifies H^2(B(-1)) canonically with this rightmost edge Lambda^2A*. PR455 proves that every term of J(1) has vanishing positive cohomology. Thus H^2(B) is exactly the cokernel of the last global-section map into Lambda^2A* tensor T. Multiplication J tensor T -> J(1), and naturality of the edge identifications, identify

T tensor H^2(B(-1)) -> H^2(B)

with the quotient map from that last target. In particular this ACTUAL multiplication is surjective. PR471 gives Lambda^2A*=V tensor W' and H^2(B)=W'. The Heisenberg-invariant part of T tensor V is the one-dimensional identity representation, so equivariance forces this map to be a scalar multiple of evaluation tensored with identity on W'. Surjectivity makes the scalar nonzero. After a compatible normalization the map is

T tensor V tensor W' -> W',  x tensor v tensor w -> x(v)w.

This conclusion uses the edge map, not merely a coincidence of source and target representations.

## 2. Koszul contraction closes the twist-two gap

Tensor the exact coordinate Koszul complex on P^4 by B(2). Its term at position p=-i is Lambda^iT tensor B(2-i), for 0<=i<=5. The hypercohomology spectral sequence has

E_1^(p,q)=H^q(Lambda^(-p)T tensor B(2+p)),

d_r:(p,q) -> (p+r,q-r+1), and abuts zero. At its right boundary,

E_2^(0,1)=coker[T tensor H^1(B(1)) -> H^1(B(2))].

The possible d_2 source is E_2^(-2,2). The preceding d_1 in that row is

Lambda^3T tensor H^2(B(-1)) -> Lambda^2T tensor H^2(B).

By section 1 it is the universal contraction Lambda^3T tensor V -> Lambda^2T, tensored with W'. This is onto: for any basis wedge x_i wedge x_j choose k distinct from i,j and contract x_k wedge x_i wedge x_j with the dual vector v_k. Hence E_2^(-2,2)=0. This is a universal exterior-algebra identity, not an evaluation of an HM matrix.

The d_3 source vanishes already on E_1, since it is Lambda^3T tensor H^3(B(-1)), zero by PR455. The d_4 source is Lambda^4T tensor H^4(B(-2)), zero by Serre duality and H^0(B(-3))=0. The latter follows from stability of F: a section would give a map F -> F(-3), impossible between stable bundles with decreasing slope. There are no q>=5 groups, and there is no outgoing differential from p=0. Thus E_2^(0,1) survives to the zero abutment and must be zero. We have proved

T H^1(B(1))=H^1(B(2)).

## 3. All degrees and the five-generator cover

We check the negative tail rather than assuming it. PR466 proves H^1(B(-2))=H^3(B(-2))=0. For every s>=-1, J(s+1) gives H^3(B(s))=0 as follows. Write r=s+1>=0. The possible total-degree-three terms are H^4(L(r-1)), the central H^3(Sym^2(W tensor L)(r)), H^2(L(r)), and H^1(O(r)), together with an H^5 term. All vanish. For the central term use the exterior Euler resolution tensored by L(r):

0 -> L tensor L(r) -> Lambda^2T tensor L(r) -> T tensor L(r+1) -> L(r+2) -> 0.

Bott gives no positive cohomology for L(a) with a>=0. Splitting this resolution into short exact sequences shows H^i(L tensor L(r))=0 for i>=3; the central symmetric square is a direct summand of copies of this tensor square in characteristic zero. Also L(-1)=Omega^2(1) is acyclic. This proves the asserted H^3 vanishing. Self-duality and Serre duality now give H^1(B(t))=H^3(B(-t-5))*=0 for every t<=-3. Together with PR466 this covers all t<=-2.

PR471 identifies H^1(B(-1))=V and actual T H^1(B(-1))=H^1(B): dualize its natural top Rao multiplication and the Eagon--Northcott identifications. PR475 proves T H^1(B)=H^1(B(1)). Section 2 supplies the next multiplication. PR455 proves multiplication onto in twists three, four and five, with the last two imported there from PR454. PR450 proves H^1(B(6))=0, and the structural regularity bound PR167 gives H^1(B(t))=0 for t>=7. Thus every nonzero degree of K is generated from K_0, and there is a minimal graded cover

S tensor V -> K -> 0.

The five generators are all in degree zero of K, equivalently twist minus one of B. No conclusion about one fixed polynomial follows from generation by all polynomials.

## 4. The first two relation degrees

Let R be the kernel of this cover. The actual top Rao map of PR471 identifies K_1=End_0(V), so R_1 is the one-dimensional Euler subspace spanned by epsilon=sum x_i tensor v_i in T tensor V. Multiplication by epsilon into the free module is injective, so T epsilon has dimension five.

PR475 decomposes S_2 tensor V as

V* tensor (1 direct-sum U' direct-sum J_5 direct-sum R_6),

where T epsilon is the V* tensor 1 summand. Its actual map to K_2=V* tensor (J_5 direct-sum R_6) is an isomorphism on each of the J_5 and R_6 channels: the second is precisely the graph scalar proved nonzero there by finite length. Therefore

R_2=(T epsilon) direct-sum (V* tensor U').

Consequently the minimal relation spaces through degree two are R_1=C epsilon and (R/S_+R)_2=V* tensor U', of dimensions one and fifteen. This is a statement about actual relations in the five-generator cover. It does not import the forty-five quadratic relations of PR475's larger eleven-generator presentation as relations of K; that presentation has six additional generators and a different kernel. Nor does it assert that these sixteen relations generate all of R.

## 5. Fixed-pair consequences and the remaining dependency

For a fixed quadratic u and cubic v, the quotients relevant to the original problem have the exact presentations

Q_4=(S_5 tensor V)/(R_5+u(S_3 tensor V)+v(S_2 tensor V)),
Q_5=(S_6 tensor V)/(R_6+u(S_4 tensor V)+v(S_3 tensor V)),
P_5^(2,1)=(S_6 tensor V)/(R_6+u^2(S_2 tensor V)+v(S_3 tensor V)).

Here R_j always means degree j of the relation module R, not the quotient-group representation R_6 used in section 4. These formulas follow by taking the indicated homogeneous pieces of the five-generator cover and then the quotient by (u,v), respectively (u^2,v). In particular Q_4 and Q_5 are degrees five and six of K/(u,v)K, a quotient of five copies of S/(u,v). The new content enabling these reductions is full five-generator generation, together with the exact low relation spaces above. Higher-degree R_j and its interaction with the fixed forms remain necessary; neither these presentations nor aggregate generation prove a fixed-form map is onto. No selected u,v or source instance is evaluated.
