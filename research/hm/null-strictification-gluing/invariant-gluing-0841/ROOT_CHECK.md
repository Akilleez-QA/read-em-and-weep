# Independent check of the invariant-generator argument

Let D and E be local rings with finite genuine G actions fixing their residue fields, with |G| invertible. Let P be an invertible (D,E)-bimodule, free of rank one on the left, with compatible genuine semilinear G action and trivial residue representation. The averaged-basis lemma gives a G-fixed left generator p. Write p e = φ(e)p. Invertibility makes φ:E→D a ring isomorphism. Applying g to this equality shows φ(g e)=g φ(e), so φ restricts to E^G≅D^G. Consequently P^G=D^G p is an invertible invariant bimodule.

For a second compatible bimodule Q over E and F, choose an invariant left generator q. Then p⊗q is an invariant left generator of P⊗_E Q. The natural map P^G⊗_(E^G)Q^G→(P⊗_E Q)^G sends generators to generators and respects the right action through the composed ring isomorphism. It is an isomorphism. This proves the tensor assertion for these specific bimodules, not arbitrary invariant tensor products.

Suppose charts have weak group actions (F_i,A_i), and overlaps have operators σ_ij,g with σ_gσ_h(p)=A_i(g,h)σ_gh(p)A_j(g,h)^−1. With local gauges c_i,c_j, set σ'_g(p)=c_i(g)σ_g(p)c_j(g)^−1. Direct substitution gives the same law with A_i',A_j'. Thus strictifying both chart coherences gives a genuine overlap action. A coherent composition θ(P_ij⊗P_jk)→P_ik stays equivariant since the adjacent c_j factors cancel in the balanced tensor. Hence the original equivariant composition and pentagon descend through the proved tensor comparison.

For a sheaf-level conclusion these data and invariant bases must exist locally in an actual compatible quotient atlas, with restriction-compatible maps. A theorem on isolated completions does not prove this effectiveness. Independent gauges need not coincide across charts: their effects are included in the transformed overlap operators. The outcome is a Morita algebroid; it need not be an ordinary sheaf of algebras or the prescribed physical corrected model.

No assertion is made that D⊗_(D^G)P^G⊗_(E^G)E≅P. It is false already for P=D=E and nontrivial finite extension D/D^G. No physical coefficients or matrices are evaluated.
