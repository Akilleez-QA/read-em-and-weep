## Open boundary and corrections

Strictification does not remove a nontrivial classical projective cocycle. Actual compatible sheaf/atlas data are required: isolated completions alone do not prove effectiveness. The outcome may be a Morita algebroid, not an ordinary prescribed algebra; physical comparison and mixed values remain outside this packet.
