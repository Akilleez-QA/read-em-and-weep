# Excluding the scalar degree-six Betti ghost

Work over C, with S=Sym(T), dim T=5, and the normalizer representations of PR492 and PR497. Write W=W′, E=A*, and write W0 for the other binary representation. Assume the established conclusion r=0 of PR497. This argument does not assume s=0.

The actual presentation is P=S⊗W→H, with relation module L generated by L₂=E⊗W and no cubic first syzygies: T⊗L₂→L₃ is injective. Write I=Ann(H), N=Sym²_S(H), and B=ker(S⊗Sym²W→N). The actual sequence 0→I⊗detW→L⊗W→B→0 sends a to a(x⊗y−y⊗x). The determinant line is trivial. PR492 identifies the remaining parameter v∈{0,10} as the kernel dimension of

τ: Tor₁(I)₄=V⊗W → Tor₂(H)₄⊗W.

We prove τ is nonzero, hence injective and v=0.

## 1. The unique cubic lift

Let D=E⊗W denote the quadratic relation generators. The map S(−2)⊗D→L has no kernel in degree three. Therefore the inclusion I₃→(L⊗W)₃ has a unique lift

f:I₃=A⊗W → T⊗E⊗W⊗W.

The two last W factors are respectively the relation-generator factor and the exterior tensor factor. The universal decompositions are

T⊗E=A⊗(U⊕W),  W⊗W=1⊕U′,
U⊗U′=T₄⊕J₅,  W⊗U′=W⊕Qspin.

Thus every equivariant f factors through the A⊗W summand of T⊗E; that is, through an embedding F:A⊗W↪T⊗E tensored with W⊗W. There are two possible intertwiners, and the actual antisymmetric inclusion fixes their relative coefficient.

Choose binary basis x,y. Under S₂=E⊗Sym²W, the quadratic relation is

r(x)=x²⊗y−xy⊗x,  r(y)=xy⊗y−y²⊗x.

The multiplication S₁⊗S₂→S₃, restricted to F(A⊗W)⊗Sym²W, has image in the unique A⊗W summand of S₃=A⊗(J₅⊕W). Since W⊗Sym²W=W⊕Qspin, it is a scalar κ times binary contraction c. Normalize c by

c(x,x²)=0, c(x,xy)=x, c(x,y²)=2y,
c(y,x²)=−2x, c(y,xy)=−y, c(y,y²)=0.

Here κ≠0. Otherwise the entire nonzero subspace F(A⊗W)⊗W of T⊗L₂ would map to zero in S₃⊗W, contradicting injectivity of T⊗L₂→L₃.

Composing c with r gives a map K:W⊗W→W⊗W. Directly on the four binary tensors,

K(xx)=−xx, K(yy)=−yy,
K(xy)=xy−2yx, K(yx)=yx−2xy.

Equivalently K=−P_sym+3P_alt, so K⁻¹=−(1/3)(id+2 swap). Put ε=x⊗y−y⊗x. After using F and the identification I₃=A⊗W, the unique cubic lift is consequently

f(a⊗z)=−(1/(3κ))(id_A⊗[id+2 swap₁₂])(a⊗z⊗ε₂₃).

Projecting the last two factors onto Λ²W gives

(id⊗P_alt,23)f(a⊗z)=−2/(3κ) F(a⊗z)⊗ε.

In particular this projection is nonzero. The displayed computation is a universal binary tensor identity, not a selected-instance matrix evaluation.

## 2. A zero Tor map would force an impossible alternating lift

Let G:V⊗W↪T⊗I₃ be the inclusion of the cubic linear syzygies, established in PR489. Lift these syzygies by f into the free cover S(−2)⊗D⊗W. Their degree-four images lie in its kernel onto L⊗W and represent exactly τ. That kernel has no elements below degree four. Thus if τ were zero, these degree-four images themselves would be zero: there are no lower-degree kernel elements whose multiples could represent a trivial minimal relation.

Projecting to the alternating pair of W factors and using section 1 would then give

sym₁₂ ∘ (id_T⊗F) ∘ G = 0

as a map V⊗W→Sym²T⊗E. Hence the injective map (id_T⊗F)∘G would have image in Λ²T⊗E.

But the universal tensor identities yield

Λ²T=E⊗W0,
E⊗E=V⊗(U⊕W),
U⊗W0=W0⊕Qspin, W⊗W0=T₄.

Therefore

Λ²T⊗E=V⊗(W0⊕Qspin⊕T₄),

which contains no irreducible V⊗W. Equivariance and semisimplicity make every map V⊗W→Λ²T⊗E zero. This contradicts injectivity of G and id_T⊗F. Thus τ≠0. Its source is irreducible, so τ is injective and v=0.

## Contribution and limits

The scalar algebra R=S/I has no degree-six second Betti ghost: β₂,₆(R)=0 and β₃,₆(R)=45. This proof uses r=0 but does not select s or u. It gives the exact two-channel cubic lift and a structural nonvanishing proof, rather than an assumption that a multiplicity-one map is nonzero. It does not address selected HM/source evaluation, higher-order lifting, or algebraicity.
