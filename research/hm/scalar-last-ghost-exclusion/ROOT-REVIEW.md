The universal binary calculation was independently expanded. K acts as minus identity on the symmetric pair and three times identity on the alternating pair. Applying its inverse to z tensor epsilon and projecting the last pair onto its alternating component gives minus two over three κ times F(z) tensor epsilon. The result is nonzero; its exact normalization is harmless but is not guessed.

Projecting to the alternating binary pair would then place the injective composite TF G inside exterior-square T tensor E. The latter is V tensor (W0 plus Qspin plus T_4), which has no V tensor W′ constituent. This gives the contradiction and proves v=0.
