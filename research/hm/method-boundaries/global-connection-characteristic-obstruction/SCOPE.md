# Scope and status

## Reused antecedent and precise addition

The characteristic expansion, global holomorphic/PGL connection obstruction, line-twist invariance and finite étale persistence already appear in [the public characteristic-class discussion](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/pull/1#issuecomment-6257575487) and [PR105's proof edition](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/1b2f14625a5ae47d94953e31eec2e3faed607e76/research/hm/null-global-connection-obstruction/RESULT.md). They are reused here with an independent current mathematical audit, not claimed as newly discovered results.

The additional statement is persistence for every proper generically finite surjective holomorphic map from a smooth compact Kähler fourfold, including ramified covers and modifications satisfying those assumptions. The degree pairing remains 1440 deg(q), even if the pulled-back test class is not Kähler. The trace-free Atiyah obstruction also persists. No extension of the separate CM-Hom exclusion is asserted.

## Limits and later results

Ordinary smooth nonflat, local, open-set and meromorphic connections are not excluded. No local current, detector or module-obstruction value is identified. Euler-characteristic scaling by deg(q) is not asserted for a general source Y, because its Todd class need not be 1.

Historical unknown-value sentences in the preserved proof state what that argument did not establish. They do not override the later [conditional dihedral mixed-vanishing theorem](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/43194f29b0f1b01dac1ef1cc92742359da5e6e34/research/hm/null-dihedral-mixed-vanishing/PROOF.md), which proves vanishing of the canonical corrected mixed obstruction under its geometric-source, algebraic-sheaf, cohomology-rank and pure-boundary premises. That theorem supplies neither evaluated primitive coefficients nor pure-square or all-order lifting. The present result neither contradicts nor strengthens it.
