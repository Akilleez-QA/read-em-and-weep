# Exact boundary of pointwise rank-two primitive-linear identities

## Result and scope

There is a bounded exclusion of a possible shortcut to reducing the four eligible HM components. After the adjugate-compatible normalization of pair primitives, **all constant-coefficient permutation identities that hold pointwise for the trace-free Massey expression by universal rank-two matrix algebra are generated by outer reversal**. In particular, neither the cyclic-zero identity nor vanishing of the middle ordering is such a pointwise identity.

Adding the already-established cohomological cyclic-zero relation leaves precisely the usual two-dimensional argument-ordering quotient for a fixed triple of labeled inputs. Thus this specified class of pointwise Cayley–Hamilton/trace manipulations removes none of the four equivariant parameters in the mixed HM operation. This is not a claim that all differential, sheaf-theoretic, Azumaya, higher-degree, or geometric identities have been exhausted. No assertion of geometric realization, nonvanishing, or any actual Massey value follows.

## 1. Normalized primitive variables

Work in characteristic zero. Locally trivialize a rank-two bundle only to discuss universal identities; no trivialization of the global bundle or cohomology is assumed. The coefficient algebra is graded commutative. Let a,b,c be independent traceless endomorphism-valued forms of degree one. For each unordered pair choose a traceless degree-one variable q_ab=q_ba and a scalar degree-one variable t_ab=-t_ba. Put

    h_ab=q_ab+t_ab I.

For actual normalized primitives, this is exactly the decomposition of h_ab under

    tau(h_ab)=-h_ba,
    tau(x)=tr(x)I-x.

The primitive equations are dh_ab=ab, but **the pointwise identity test in this note imposes no differential equations**. Its purpose is precisely to identify which proposed conclusions follow from the fiberwise algebra alone. Differential equations may give further cohomological information, outside this test.

All variables are formal universal variables; none is an actual HM representative, resolution matrix, primitive, or evaluated polynomial instance.

## 2. Polarized Cayley–Hamilton with the exterior signs

For degree-one traceless forms x,y, polarized rank-two Cayley–Hamilton gives

    xy-yx=tr(xy)I.

Here the minus sign, rather than the familiar plus sign for ordinary degree-zero matrices, is caused by interchanging the exterior coefficients. Consequently the trace-free product is

    B(x,y):=(xy)_0=(xy+yx)/2,
    B(x,y)=B(y,x).

As a universal bilinear operation B is nonzero: its coefficient tensor is one half of the Lie bracket tensor of sl2, combined with the exterior product. The Lie algebra sl2 is nonabelian. This is a structural statement about its bracket, not the evaluation of any pair of matrices.

Scalar degree-one variables anticommute with degree-one endomorphism-valued forms. Therefore the trace-free part of the usual defining expression is

    T(a,b,c):=(a h_bc+h_ab c)_0
            =B(a,q_bc)+B(q_ab,c)-t_bc a+t_ab c.       (1)

Formula (1) proves outer reversal pointwise:

    T(a,b,c)=T(c,b,a).                               (2)

It also shows explicitly why treating every pair primitive as traceless would be unjustified. Their antisymmetric part is scalar, not necessarily zero or closed.

## 3. Exact classification of the remaining permutation relations

After (2), the six permutations are represented by

    X=T(a,b,c), Y=T(b,c,a), Z=T(c,a,b).

Introduce formal expressions

    P_a=B(a,q_bc), P_b=B(b,q_ca), P_c=B(c,q_ab).

Then

    X=P_a+P_c-t_bc a+t_ab c,
    Y=P_b+P_a-t_ca b+t_bc a,
    Z=P_c+P_b-t_ab c+t_ca b.                         (3)

The three P expressions belong to distinct multihomogeneous components of the free graded-commutative coefficient algebra: they use respectively the independent variable families (a,q_bc), (b,q_ca), and (c,q_ab). Each is nonzero because the universal B tensor is nonzero. Scalar-primitive terms belong to different multihomogeneous components again.

Suppose a universal constant-coefficient relation lambda X+mu Y+nu Z=0 held. The respective P components in (3) require

    lambda+mu=0, mu+nu=0, nu+lambda=0.

In characteristic zero these imply lambda=mu=nu=0. This is symbolic coefficient comparison in a universal algebra, not a rank computation, matrix evaluation, or selection of a polynomial instance.

Thus X,Y,Z are linearly independent as universal primitive-linear expressions. Since the original six expressions are identified in pairs by reversal, the kernel of their map to universal expressions consists exactly of the three pairwise reversal differences. This establishes the asserted classification for every constant-coefficient permutation identity in this specified pointwise primitive-linear sector, not merely for a selected list of Cayley–Hamilton manipulations. All valid universal polynomial matrix identities are already satisfied in the universal algebra used for this test.

## 4. The cyclic relation has a different source

Summing (3) cancels all scalar-primitive terms and gives

    X+Y+Z=2(P_a+P_b+P_c).                           (4)

The right side is not the zero universal polynomial. For the actual defining system, however, the canonical-Massey proof shows its cohomology class vanishes using a third-order lift in the smooth geometric family. Equation (4) is compatible with that theorem: an expression may represent zero cohomology without vanishing pointwise.

Likewise no individual ordering in (3) is forced pointwise to vanish. Imposing cyclic zero only after taking cohomology leaves the quotient of the three-dimensional ordering span by its sum, of dimension two. There is no additional pointwise relation to impose on that quotient.

The trace-free projection used here is legitimate for the actual cohomological operation when the target scalar H2 vanishes: it is a chain map, and the discarded scalar cocycle is exact. On the downstairs mixed weight-two target this scalar group is H2(P4,O(-2))=0. The existence of this projection does not assert that arbitrary universal q,t choices satisfy the defining-system differential equations or arise globally.

## 5. Consequence for the four-component certificate

Write F(a,b,c)=m011(a,b,c), with a in U0 and b,c in U1. The established cohomological identities give

    m110(b,c,a)=F(a,c,b),
    m101(b,a,c)=-F(a,b,c)-F(a,c,b)=-2 F_sym(a,b,c).

The equivariant multiplicity proof identifies two symmetric and two alternating components of F. The pointwise rank-two identity test above supplies only the reversal already used in these formulas. Thus it supplies no additional linear equation on either pair. In particular a proposed deduction m101=0 solely by pointwise rank-two trace/Cayley–Hamilton simplification cannot succeed in this sector; some further differential or geometric input would be required.

This conclusion must not be enlarged to say that arbitrary four-component choices are realizable by rank-two Azumaya derived endomorphism DGAs. The present universal polynomial test neither constructs such DGAs nor analyzes their global descent or differential homotopies. It is distinct from an additive square-zero-algebra counterexample and preserves the genuine rank-two fiberwise multiplication throughout its stated test.
