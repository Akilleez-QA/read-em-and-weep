# Universal mixed Atiyah channel and its symmetry obstruction

## Result and scope

Let X=P²_z×P²_h, m:X→P⁴ multiply binary quadratics, E=m*HM, and A=At(E). Let x and y denote the two hyperplane classes, in H¹(Ω¹_X). Write A_z and A_h for the projections of A onto the two holomorphic cotangent summands. Put

    B_z=A_z+(x/2)Id,       B_h=A_h+(y/2)Id,
    C=B_z B_h−B_h B_z.

Products throughout mean cohomological cup product and exterior multiplication of holomorphic forms, with the total differential-form convention fixed below. The following is a bounded classification and exclusion, not a mixed module-obstruction calculation:

1. The space of coefficient-free gauge-natural bilinear maps sl₂×sl₂→sl₂ is one-dimensional, generated by the commutator. There is no symmetric such map. Consequently the only possible trace-free quadratic mixed polynomial in the projected Atiyah classes is a scalar multiple of C.
2. C is block-odd under τ(z,h)=(h,z). Any block-equivariant linear coefficient transformation carrying its cohomology group into the retained block-even target kills its retained projection. An odd auxiliary coefficient or an odd coefficient transformation is necessary to evade this particular exclusion.
3. The surviving universal commutator channel has the identity

    C²=[tr(B_z B_h)²−tr(B_z²)tr(B_h²)]Id.

   For this pullback family the right side is zero in cohomology. Thus C²=0, without asserting C=0. Also tr(CB_z)=tr(CB_h)=0.

The classification and symmetry test concern the entire coefficient-free quadratic class, together with its discriminant constraint. Polarized Cayley–Hamilton is an input rather than a new claim.

## 1. Coefficient convention and polarization

Represent A by normalized Chern curvature on the compact complex product. For pointwise algebra use the genuinely trace-free representatives B_i=A_i−(tr A_i/2)Id, for i=z,h. Their cohomology classes are exactly the displayed B_z and B_h, because [tr A_z]=−x and [tr A_h]=−y. No equality with independently chosen harmonic representatives for x,y is assumed. Projection acts only on the holomorphic cotangent index; antiholomorphic indices may lie in either factor. Thus the entries of each B_z and B_h are scalar forms of total degree two. These entries commute, even when they involve antiholomorphic base directions. This is sufficient for ordinary rank-two Cayley–Hamilton and its polarization over the commutative even-form coefficient ring. Identities are frame-independent, descend to Dolbeault cohomology, and by GAGA give identities in algebraic coherent cohomology. They may then be restricted to opens.

For any two traceless endomorphisms U,V over such a coefficient ring, put p=tr(U²), q=tr(UV), r=tr(V²). Then

    U²=(p/2)Id,  V²=(r/2)Id,  UV+VU=q Id,
    tf(UV)=[U,V]/2.

In particular tf(B_zB_h+B_hB_z)=0. This alone is a polarization of the old square theorem, and is not offered as the new conclusion.

The intrinsic coefficient bundle for C is the mixed summand

    M=pr_z*Ω¹_P² ⊗ pr_h*Ω¹_P² ⊂ Ω²_X,
    C∈H²(X,End₀(E)⊗M).

It is not H²(X,End₀(E)⊗ω_X). No identification between these bundles or their cohomology groups is assumed. All product signs above are realized by differential-form wedge; if tensor-first Čech conventions are used, the corresponding standard comparison signs must be inserted consistently.

## 2. Complete bilinear gauge-natural classification

Here “coefficient-free gauge-natural bilinear” means a complex-bilinear map F:sl₂×sl₂→sl₂ commuting with simultaneous change of frame by GL₂, using no chosen vector, covector, connection, derivative, or additional endomorphism. For sheaf-valued inputs, the scalar coefficient factors are simply carried along and multiplied. This excludes additional coefficient operations until the separate step in §3.

The conjugation action factors through PGL₂. For its double cover SL₂, sl₂ is the irreducible highest-weight-two representation V₂. In characteristic zero,

    V₂⊗V₂ = V₄ ⊕ V₂ ⊕ V₀,
    Sym² V₂ = V₄ ⊕ V₀,       Λ² V₂ = V₂.

For completeness, the highest-weight-four vector in the symmetric square generates a five-dimensional irreducible submodule, and the invariant nondegenerate trace form supplies the remaining one-dimensional summand. The alternating square has dimension three, and the nonzero equivariant Lie bracket identifies it with sl₂. These account for all nine dimensions and establish the displayed decomposition. Schur's lemma gives

    Hom_GL₂(sl₂⊗sl₂,sl₂)=C·[−,−],
    Hom_GL₂(Sym²sl₂,sl₂)=0.

Thus every F is λ[U,V]. This classifies all such natural bilinear maps, not just word expressions aUV+bVU. General homogeneous quadratic maps in two labeled inputs are sums of the two pure and one mixed polarization pieces; both pure trace-free pieces vanish, leaving the same channel. Scalar-valued bilinear terms, such as tr(UV)Id, disappear under trace-free projection.

## 3. Block-exchange obstruction and the exact escape condition

Because mτ=m, E has its canonical block-exchange identification. Naturality of At and the cotangent splitting give τ*B_z=B_h and τ*B_h=B_z. Geometric pullback respects wedge and matrix multiplication. Therefore

    τ*C=B_hB_z−B_zB_h=−C.

This includes the geometric action on M embedded in Ω²_X; no extra sign is subsequently inserted for reordering its two holomorphic one-form factors.

Let L be any linear map from H²(X,End₀(E)⊗M) into H²(X,End₀(E)⊗ω_X) that commutes with τ. It may arise from a specified coefficient operation, if such an operation exists. Then L(C) is block-odd. The canonical retained projector here means projection first to the block-even sector, followed by its canonical removal of the alternating two-space, as specified by the source. It is block-equivariant and annihilates the odd sector; merely having even image would not suffice. Hence

    P_ret L(C)=0.

Combining with §2 proves this for every coefficient-free quadratic expression in B_z,B_h, after any such equivariant coefficient transformation. This statement is conditional on the transformation's existence and equivariance; it does not manufacture one.

More generally if L is odd, τL=−Lτ, its value on C is even. Likewise tensoring C with an odd auxiliary class makes the combined parity even. These observations identify necessary additional representation support for evading this particular no-go theorem. Even parity alone does not show a nonzero retained component, exclude the alternating two-space, or produce an actual source. A non-equivariant choice is also outside the theorem and requires tracking its equivariance defect.

## 4. Discriminant and trace-pair constraints

Set D=[U,V]=2UV−q Id. Using VU=q Id−UV gives

    UVUV=qUV−U²V²=qUV−(pr/4)Id,
    D²=(q²−pr)Id.

Cyclic trace and U²=(p/2)Id imply

    tr(DU)=tr(UVU−VU²)=0,
    tr(DV)=0.

These are formal algebra identities with even scalar coefficients, not evaluations on a chosen matrix instance.

For the pullback family, tr A=−(x+y) and tr A²=−7(x+y)². Hence, for B=A+(x+y)Id/2,

    tr(B²)=−(15/2)(x+y)².

The decomposition by holomorphic z/h degree is a direct sum decomposition of Ω²_X and is respected by cohomology. Projecting the last equality gives

    p=tr(B_z²)=−(15/2)x²,
    q=tr(B_zB_h)=−(15/2)xy,
    r=tr(B_h²)=−(15/2)y².

Thus q²−pr=0 and C²=0 in H⁴(X,End(E)⊗ω_X). The identities are cohomological; these p,q,r need not equal the displayed harmonic representatives pointwise. No nilpotence assertion about arbitrary uncorrected Čech representatives is made. The result is neither a nonvanishing assertion for C nor a deduction that a square-zero cohomology class itself vanishes.

## 5. Boundary of applicability

The actual mixed module problem involves compatible first-order boundary module choices, a two-direction ambient extension, and potentially higher products or homotopies. Its endomorphism-valued class in H²(End₀(E)⊗ω_X), or the good-locus relative recipient H¹(U,V⊗ω_h), has not been identified with a coefficient transformation of a quadratic polynomial in B_z,B_h. The source explicitly leaves that identification open.

Consequently this result excludes a precisely defined universal shortcut, and states the extra odd data needed for its sole commutator channel to reach the even target. It does not eliminate a genuine block-even higher obstruction, settle exceptional extension data, or calculate any of the thirty retained values. The square-zero identity cannot be used as a formality or Massey-product theorem.
