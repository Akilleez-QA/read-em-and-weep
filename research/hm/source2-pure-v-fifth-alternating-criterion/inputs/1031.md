# A unique rank-three member of the pure-V quadratic lift pencil

## Theorem and boundary

Let A=Sym2 W, D=Sym4 W and P=Sym6 W/V6 for the binary icosahedral group.
The conditional pure-V quotient-third theorem PR1025 places its unique
quadratic lift in the two-dimensional space

    H=Hom_G(P O_X(-2),D O_X),       X=P(A).

Use invariant complex BILINEAR pairings, not Hermitian adjoints. Let
L_a:D->P be multiplication by a followed by projection, and let L_a* be
its bilinear adjoint. Let rho_D(a) be the usual binary Hamiltonian action
on quartics, and rho_P(a) the projected action on the invariant P summand
of Sym6 W. Then

    R1(a)=rho_D(a)L_a*,       R2(a)=L_a*rho_P(a)                (1)

form a basis of H. In this normalization:

1. Every nonzero alpha R1+beta R2 has generic rank4, except the UNIQUE
   projective member alpha+beta=0. The coefficient ideal of its universal
   four-by-four minors is EXACTLY

       (alpha+beta)(alpha,beta)^3.

   Its projective saturation is the reduced ideal (alpha+beta), including
   the coefficient point at infinity. No actual coefficient is selected.
2. Rminus=R1-R2 factors canonically through V6 by two linear families

       P --H_a--> V6 --J_a--> D.

   J is a subbundle injection for every nonzero quadratic a. H has rank3
   away from the six projective C5 axes and rank2 at precisely those axes.
   Therefore Rminus has exactly those ranks, and its maximal-rank
   degeneracy scheme is the SIX REDUCED points, not the ten-point L locus
   or the length34 third-symbol target scheme.
3. The sheaf kernel of Rminus:P O(-2)->D O is O(-5), generated by the
   cubic P-covariant cP(a)=Pi(a^3). Its fiber vector vanishes at the six
   axes; no claim identifies that zero vector with the larger specialized
   kernel there. On every other direction it spans the one-dimensional
   kernel.

This classifies the UNIVERSAL lift pencil, not the actual coefficients of
the conditional lift. It does not prove that any nonzero lift occurs,
select the exceptional member, evaluate the original fourth symbol, or
exclude a source or catalogue pair.

## 2. Fixed universal conventions and geometric basis

Write a=p x^2+q xy+t y^2, retaining all three independent variables. In the
monomial basis of Sym6 W use the authenticated projection

    Pi=[ sqrt5  0       1     0  -sqrt5/3  0       5 ]
       [ 0      sqrt5/3 0    -1   0       -sqrt5  0 ]
       [ 0     -sqrt5   0    -1   0        sqrt5/3 0 ]
       [ 5      0      -sqrt5/3 0 1        0       sqrt5 ].

For even n, the invariant apolar Gram matrix Gn has anti-diagonal entries
(-1)^i/binomial(n,i), indexed from zero. Write G4=GD and define the
canonical orthogonal equivariant inclusion

    E=G6^(-1) Pi^T (Pi G6^(-1) Pi^T)^(-1):P->Sym6 W,
    GP=E^T G6 E.

The letter E here denotes an INCLUSION matrix, not the quotient bundle
from earlier packets. Let M_a:D->Sym6 W be full binary multiplication.
Then L=Pi M, L*=GD^(-1)L^T GP=M_a*E. All pairings are nondegenerate
complex bilinear pairings. The formulas are representation normalizations,
not selected actual endpoint matrices.

Define rho_n(a)f=a_x f_y-a_y f_x. It is equivariant, linear in a, and
rho_n is the infinitesimal sl2 action. Put rho_P=Pi rho_6 E. It is a
nonzero generator of the unique A tensor P -> P channel. In this basis

    rho_P(a)=[2q  0  -4p  0]
             [0  2q   0 -4p]
             [4t 0  -2q  0]
             [0  4t   0 -2q],

It is skew for GP. Thus R1,R2 in(1) are equivariant quadratic families.

## 3. The exceptional factorization through V6

    rho_6(a) M_a = M_a rho_D(a).

gives rho_D(a) M_a*=M_a* rho_6(a). Consequently

    Rminus = M_a* pr_V rho_6(a) E.                            (2)

This is the asserted factorization, where

    H_a=pr_V rho_6(a) E :P->V6,

## 4. Exact projective coefficient-ideal certificate

Each maximal minor of R=alpha R1+beta R2 is homogeneous of degree eight
in (p,q,t) and degree four in (alpha,beta). Collect all its direction
coefficients into an ideal I in C[alpha,beta]. A coefficient point has
generic rank at most three exactly when all these coefficient polynomials
vanish. This tests the whole universal direction space, not a chosen
quadratic direction.

Every coefficient is divisible by alpha+beta by(2). Dividing by that
linear factor leaves homogeneous cubics. The exact coefficient vectors of
these residual cubics span the ENTIRE four-dimensional binary-cubic space.
Thus their ideal is (alpha,beta)^3 and

    I=(alpha+beta)(alpha,beta)^3.                              (3)

verify.py verifies this by exact linear algebra over Q(sqrt5), retaining
all coefficients. For efficiency it may dehomogenize p=1 when computing
the minor coefficients: homogeneity of degree eight recovers each missing
p exponent uniquely, so this loses no coefficient, direction, or rank
condition. In particular this is NOT a substitution of an actual instance.

Two compact coefficient witnesses from the minor omitting row zero are

    [q^8] minor = (9sqrt5/80000) beta(alpha+beta)
                                   (alpha^2-7alpha beta+beta^2),
    [q^6 t^2] minor = -(3/8000)(alpha+beta)
                                   (4alpha^3-90alpha^2 beta
                                      +15alpha beta^2+beta^3).

Their gcd is alpha+beta. The stronger full-span check proves(3), including
scheme multiplicity rather than only support. Saturating by the irrelevant
ideal(alpha,beta) removes its cubic power and gives precisely(alpha+beta).
This is a reduced single point of the coefficient projective line; no
extra point at alpha=0 or beta=0 is missed. Hence all other nonzero members
have generic rank four. Section5 proves exact generic rank three for the
remaining member.

## 5. All direction ranks of the exceptional member

Compute the ten three-by-three minors of J, a five-by-three matrix linear
in (p,q,t). On EACH symbolic projective chart p=1, q=1 and t=1, their
Groebner basis over Q(sqrt5) is the unit ideal. The variables not normalized
remain independent. These are three exact emptiness certificates covering
P2, so J has rank three at EVERY nonzero direction. It is therefore a
subbundle injection V6 O(-1)->D O. This is not inferred from generic rank
or an absence of numerically found points.

For H, the vector of signed three-by-three cofactors is exactly

    cof(H)=(-243sqrt5/2560)cP(a),       cP(a)=Pi(a^3).           (4)

The nonzero constant and the polynomial identity are checked over all
three direction variables. The four cubic components of cP are the
nonzero unique P-covariant used in PR1005, so their common-zero scheme is
precisely its six reduced C5 axes. Thus the maximal-minor ideal of H is
that exact reduced scheme, and H has generic rank three. It has rank two
at each of the six points: if rank were at most one there, every 3-minor
would lie in the square of the maximal ideal, contradicting that the
minor ideal is the reduced maximal ideal.

Because J is a subbundle injection, local row operations identify JH
with H followed by three coordinate rows and two zeros. Accordingly the
ideals of three-by-three minors of JH and H agree locally. This proves
both the rank statement and exact degeneracy scheme for Rminus.

The kernel sheaf has rank one and is generated by cP. One can see this
from cofactors on the full-rank locus and extend: the kernel of a map
between locally free sheaves with torsion-free image on a smooth surface
is reflexive by the depth lemma, hence a line bundle. The cofactor vector
has no divisorial common factor, since its zero scheme is finite, so its
map O(-5)->P O(-2) identifies that line bundle with the kernel globally.
At each exceptional point the specialized linear map has a two-dimensional
kernel, while the cofactor vector specializes to zero. Sheaf kernels and
specialized kernels are intentionally distinguished.

## 6. Conditional meaning and verification limits

For an actual compatible pure-V source, the unique lift from PR1025 is
one point of this two-dimensional space, possibly zero. If it is nonzero,
its generic rank is either four or three, the latter only on the identified
single universal line. Choosing which alternative actually holds remains
an unsolved coefficient gate. Rank of R is not rank of the original fourth
row: PR1025's compositions and quotient must still be applied.

The verifier authenticates all exact sources, rechecks the P6 generator
identities, constructs the adjoint basis and V factorization, proves the
whole coefficient ideal, and verifies the all-direction J certificates
and H cofactor identity. All calculations are universal polynomial or
representation identities. No actual HM coefficient, source point,
parameter, jet, obstruction matrix or instance rank is evaluated.
