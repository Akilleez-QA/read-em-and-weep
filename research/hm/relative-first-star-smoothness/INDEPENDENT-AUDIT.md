## Detailed checks

1. **Finite flatness.** Ampleness excludes positive-dimensional fibers of each generating tuple map. Proper quasi-finite then implies finite, and the four-dimensional closed image is all of P4. The intersection number is 24. The smooth source is Cohen–Macaulay, the smooth target is regular, and the finite equal-dimension criterion gives flatness. Pushforward of a line bundle is therefore a rank-24 vector bundle. No symmetry hypothesis enters.

2. **The Omega summand really splits.** For a proper nonempty subset of size r, the Kunneth table is correct for every integral twist. The resolution ending in O with successive terms sums of O(-i), for 1 <= i <= r, yields a surjection Hom(Omega^r,B_S) -> H^r(B_S): the necessary positive-degree groups of B_S(i) vanish. Hence a map inducing the nonzero generator exists. Finite duality gives B_S^*(-5)=B_complement, not an unjustified self-duality. The complementary lift, dualized, gives B_S -> Omega^r. Serre duality identifies its induced H^r map with the nonzero complementary map. The composite is a nonzero scalar because End(Omega^r)=C, so the summand splits. Removing it kills every intermediate cohomology group in every twist, exactly the input Horrocks requires.

3. **The line bounds and fixed type are correct.** Vanishing of H0(B_S(1)) forces a >= 2 for a line summand O(-a). Applying complement duality and the same vanishing forces a <= 3. Rank and Hilbert polynomial then uniquely determine the two multiplicities. As an additional arithmetic check, the respective splittings for r=1,2,3 are Omega^1 plus O(-2)^17 plus O(-3)^3; Omega^2 plus O(-2)^9 plus O(-3)^9; and Omega^3 plus O(-2)^3 plus O(-3)^17. The empty-subset splitting has multiplicities 1,11,11,1 in degrees 0,-1,-2,-3, agreeing with rank 24 and (n+1)^4. The full-subset case follows by duality. These are underlying vector-bundle statements only.

4. **Relative base change is genuinely proved, not assumed from h1 alone.** Each character cohomology dimension in every degree is constant because F is fixed and the corresponding B_S has fixed underlying type. Proper smooth pushforward of the vector bundle is perfect, and derived base change computes each fiber. In a bounded finite-free presentation, constancy of all fiber cohomology dimensions recursively forces constant ranks of all differentials. Over the reduced finite-type complex parameter base, vanishing of all larger minors at closed points implies their actual vanishing. An invertible rank minor allows local elimination and splitting of a contractible block. Iteration produces a finite-free zero-differential cohomology complex locally. Arbitrary, including nonflat and nonreduced, pullback therefore commutes with its cohomology. Proper perfect base change identifies this with actual pulled-back End cohomology.
