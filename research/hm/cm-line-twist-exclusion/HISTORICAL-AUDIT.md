# Historical mathematical audit: line-twist scope

This is the section-6 mathematical assessment extracted from the source audit, not a new independent review of this edited packet. The full original proof and audit are hash-bound in SOURCE-BINDINGS.json. Earlier coefficient and ordinary-Hom portions are superseded by the exact predecessors cited in PROOF.md.

For a nontrivial P in Pic⁰(C⁴), the product description of Pic⁰ and elliptic-curve cohomology give H*(P)=0. The twisted Hom spectral sequence consequently has no total-zero entries. Torsion-freeness of H²(C⁴,Z) places every two-torsion line bundle in Pic⁰; [GAGA](https://www.numdam.org/item/AIF_1956__6__1_0/) transfers the analytic statements to algebraic ones. The matrix-unit proof that an O_X-algebra isomorphism End(E)≅End(F) forces F≅E⊗P is valid. From the four-term presentation, det H=O(−1), so the [carrier identifications](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/3bdc6cb28ecd75cfefb3cb0d85f42d5bcde8cc7f/research/hm-carrier-and-atlas.md) give det E=det τ*E=O_X and hence P²=O_X. Tensoring E⊗P≅F by P⁻¹ gives exactly the map forbidden by the twisted vanishing. Projective-bundle isomorphisms induce the associated matrix-algebra isomorphisms, completing that exclusion. Extension of scalars gives the stated nonexistence over Q(i).
