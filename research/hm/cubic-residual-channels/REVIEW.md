## Findings

1. **Normalization channel.** The intrinsic derivative formula is well-defined because every section in the cubic kernel vanishes at the centers. Local vector-field extensions of the center velocities give both the tangent-restriction connecting cocycle and the transition cocycle of the moving-center blowup. This supplies the Kodaira–Spencer identification; it is not merely an equality of dimensions. The moving-center cubic equation then identifies the induced map with the normalization-section obstruction, up to the consistent obstruction sign. Projective vector fields contribute zero in the cokernel of restriction.

2. **Residual local line and its twist.** The two affine tangent spans have a nine-dimensional sum. Its annihilator is exactly the one-dimensional space of sections with zero value and zero first jet at both normalization marks. Restriction of `Hom(S,Q_sigma)` to this line has kernel equal to the differential-operator image. Thus the quotient is `Hom(K_sigma,Q_sigma)`, including the necessary fiber factor. The separate descent map is canonical only after imposing normalization compatibility; the proof maintains that distinction.

3. **Missing weight and opposite pairing.** The nine tangent weights in the normalized case omit exactly `(1,3)`. Its two repeated-index representatives are `(1,4)` and `(3,0)`, so the dual cubic is supported exactly on `x_1^2 x_4` and `x_3^2 x_0`. Both coefficients are nonzero by nonvanishing of the corresponding evaluation tensors, without calculating either coefficient. The general opposite-pair formula is well-defined under switching representatives and affine relabeling, squares to the original paired value, and has no fixed paired value.

4. **Heisenberg multiplicities.** Each center orbit contributes every character nontrivial on its stabilizer once. This gives five copies of the augmentation representation in center motions; subtracting the injective projective-vector-field copy gives four. Each local residual line has trivial stabilizer character because its missing and value tensors have the same first weight coordinate. Twelve five-point residual orbits therefore give twelve invariant lines and two copies of each nontrivial character. The faithful common central character on sections and normalization cohomology gives two copies of the regular representation in the normalization target. The target multiplicities and the fourteen forced zero invariant conditions follow exactly.

5. **Normalizer and common rank.** The two independent intrinsic shears generate all upper and lower elementary transformations of the projective Heisenberg group, hence an `SL_2(F_5)` action on its character labels. These are actual automorphisms of the entire center configuration and complete cubic morphism; functoriality therefore applies to the obstruction map, not just to an abstract matching representation. Transitivity on nontrivial characters makes their twenty-four four-dimensional block ranks equal. The rank and dimension formulas follow; the initial five possibilities are reduced to four by the strict exclusion below.

6. **Four one-dimensional gates.** Independently checked the final refinement. For a nontrivial character with kernel `G`, the shear cycles the five contributing center-direction lines; the projective-vector-field line removed from them is shear-invariant. The resulting source has precisely shear weights `1,2,3,4`. The residual weights are `2d^2`, giving `2,3`.
