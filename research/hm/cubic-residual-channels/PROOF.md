## 2. The normalization channel is the derivative of postulation

Set P=H0(P(A),O(3)), T_Z=direct-sum_z T_z P(A), and F_Z=H0(Z,O_Z(3)). Let rho:P->F_Z be restriction. Then ker rho=S, dim im rho=25, and coker rho=H1(D). For v=(v_z) in T_Z define

       delta(v)(s) = [(d s_z(v_z))_z] in F_Z/im rho,  s in S.       (1)

The derivative of a section vanishing at z is canonically an element of Hom(T_z P(A),O(3)_z); it is independent of a local frame because the frame-change correction is multiplied by s(z)=0. Thus (1) is intrinsic, without choosing a matrix for rho.

A first-order motion z_epsilon=z+epsilon v_z allows a cubic s+epsilon t to continue vanishing precisely when t(z)+ds_z(v_z)=0 for every z. Therefore all ten sections lift precisely when delta(v)=0. This is the usual tangent-to-rank-stratum formula for the evaluation map, but follows directly from that equation and needs no smoothness of a determinantal locus. The first-order blowup of the moving centers carries 3H-E, whose pushforward is the moving ideal twisted by3, so its section-lifting obstruction is exactly this equation. The tangent quotient of PR614 identifies its Kodaira–Spencer class with the quotient of v by pgl(A): explicitly, extend the center velocities to local vector fields on ambient open sets. Differences on overlaps vanish at the centers and lift to blowup vector fields. These differences are both the connecting cocycle for the tangent restriction sequence and the transition cocycle of the moving-center blowup deformation. This proves delta is b_N, up to the uniform harmless Cech obstruction sign.

For a projective vector field a, moving the centers by a is compensated by the induced first-order change of the cubic under the projective action. Equivalently, Lie differentiation of s supplies a global cubic whose evaluation is ds_z(a(z)), up to sign. Consequently delta annihilates pgl(A) and descends to H1(Theta_M). The scalar ambiguity in lifting a to gl(A) changes that differentiated cubic by a multiple of s, whose restriction is zero. No claim of independence of the fifty linear conditions follows.

## 3. The residual descent line is a simultaneous singular section

Let ell_sigma subset S* be the common value line. Define

       K_sigma=(U_p+U_q)^perp subset S.                           (2)

Since the affine spans have dimensions5,5 and intersect in ell_sigma, K_sigma is a line. In local common frames a section lies in K_sigma precisely when its value and its four first derivatives vanish at each of p and q. This describes a section with a zero of order at least two on each smooth normalization branch; it does not assert that the zero divisor is reduced or has an isolated singularity.

The local operator formula from the polarized proof is

       e_sigma(u,v,a)(s)=df_p(u)-df_q(v)+a s(sigma).

Its image is (U_p+U_q) tensor Q_sigma inside S* tensor Q_sigma. Hence restriction to K_sigma gives the canonical quotient isomorphism

       J_sigma = coker e_sigma = Hom(K_sigma,Q_sigma).           (3)

Thus the sixty residual descent conditions are scalar conditions only after choosing generators of the sixty lines in (3).

They are not in general a separately defined map from all of H1(Theta_M): E has a filtration, not a prescribed splitting. On ker b_N there is a canonical map b_J:ker b_N->J. Concretely lift a normalization deformation with its lifted sections, choose any pair lift and compute its section obstruction. Its projection to N vanishes, so its class is in J; the local residual class is restriction of a representative local discrepancy to K_sigma. Changing mark motions or fiber gluing changes that discrepancy by e_sigma and therefore does not change (3). Changing choices of the pair lift changes only the eliminated image B(F). This is the intrinsic scalar descent obstruction, with the normalization compatibility imposed first.

## 4. Explicit missing weights and an opposite-pair involution

For a fixed Heisenberg direction choose the abstract eigenspaces L_i, i in F5, as in PR610. A tensor L_a L_b L_c has weight (a+b+c,a^2+b^2+c^2). The twenty ordered repeated-index pairs have ten weights, each occurring once in S*. Normalize a paired value to (i,j)=(0,1), whose partner is(4,3). PR611 lists the tangent support union as

       (1,1), (2,4), (3,4), (4,1), (2,2),
       (3,2), (4,3), (0,1), (0,4).

The remaining weight is (1,3), represented by (1,4) and (3,0). These are distinct from all the displayed nine weights and exhaust the last one-dimensional summand. Therefore K_sigma is the dual character line of weight (1,3).

Inside Sym^3(A*) its only monomial supports are x_1^2 x_4 and x_3^2 x_0. Indeed the corresponding Sym^3(A) weight space has exactly the two tensor lines by PR610; the cubic kernel line is dual to its quotient. Both coefficients are nonzero because each of the two tensor lines has nonzero image in that quotient. Their ratio is not calculated or needed.

Undoing the affine character relabeling, the omitted weight is represented by

       (j,2i-j), with partner (3j-2i,i).                         (4)

The two possible representatives of the original value give exactly these two representatives in (4), so this is well-defined on paired values. Applying (4) twice gives (2i-j,3i-2j), the original partner. It has no fixed value: normalized original weight(1,1) differs from missing weight(1,3). Thus for each of the six directions it pairs its ten double values into five opposite pairs. This construction is independent of affine relabeling and compatible with the Heisenberg normalizer. It pairs an exceptional image weight with its simultaneous branch-annihilator section line, not a claimed canonical self-duality of the full10-dimensional representation.

In particular the first weight coordinate of the missing tensor equals that of the original value:

       2j+(2i-j)=2i+j.                                          (5)

This equality, not the unevaluated coefficient ratio, controls the local residual stabilizer representation.

## 5. Heisenberg representation of the source

All the constructions are equivariant for the finite Heisenberg group H_5 acting on A. Its center acts trivially on the projective geometry, on Theta_M, and on the obstruction spaces Hom(S,H1(L)) because its scalar characters on source sections and target line-bundle cohomology cancel. Thus b and its filtration carry honest representations of U=H_5/Z(H_5).

For each direction G subset U, the five eigenlines form one U-orbit with stabilizer G. At any such line L_i, the tangent representation Hom(L_i,A/L_i) of G contains each of the four nontrivial characters once. Inducing to U shows that this five-center orbit contributes each character chi of U with chi|G nontrivial once, and no others. A nontrivial chi has one kernel direction among the six and is nontrivial on the other five. Therefore

       T_Z = 5 Reg_0(U).

Conjugation on End(A) is Reg(U). For completeness the twenty-five Heisenberg operators indexed by U span its twenty-five distinct commutator-character lines: their weights are distinct by the nondegenerate commutator pairing and each is nonzero, hence they form a basis. Removing scalars gives pgl(A)=Reg_0(U). The injective tangent evaluation map of PR614 therefore proves

       H1(Theta_M)=T_Z/pgl(A)=4 Reg_0(U).                        (6)

## 6. Heisenberg representations of the two targets

Fix G and its ten paired values. An element outside G translates the character indices i,j simultaneously; G fixes them. Under pairing, d=j-i is identified with -d. Thus the two nonzero classes d modulo sign each give a U-orbit of five paired values. Their stabilizer is exactly G. The sixty values are the resulting twelve orbits, two for each direction.

For the local line (3), choose the order-five lift g used in PR610. The value tensor has g-weight 2i+j; hence L_sigma, the O(1) fiber, has weight -(2i+j). The quotient Q_sigma is this line tensored with the residue-difference line; g fixes both branches individually and acts trivially on that residue-difference factor. The section K_sigma has weight -(2i+j) as well by (5). Consequently Hom(K_sigma,Q_sigma) has trivial G-character. Choice of the lift cannot affect this ratio. Thus each five-point orbit contributes Ind_G^U trivial. The trivial character occurs in all12 such induced representations. A nontrivial chi occurs only for G=ker chi, twice. Therefore

       J=12 trivial + 2 Reg_0(U).                              (7)

For N, the center of H_5 acts on both S and H1(D) through the same faithful character, namely the O(3) character c^(-3) under the usual convention. The exceptional ideal factor is canonically linearized with trivial central action. Every irreducible representation with a fixed nontrivial central character has dimension5 and is unique: diagonalizing one order-five generator and applying the other cycles its five eigencharacters transitively, giving the standard five-dimensional block; iteration and semisimplicity give the general assertion. Let R be this irreducible. Dimensions imply S is two copies of R and H1(D) is one copy. Therefore

       N=Hom(S,H1(D))=2 End(R)=2 Reg(U).                       (8)

The last equality follows by the same commutator-character basis argument as for End(A). It does not choose a shear phase for the5-dimensional cohomology or an identification of its normalizer action.

Representations of the finite group U over C are semisimple. Applying character projections to the target filtration and using (7)–(8) gives

       E=14 trivial + 4 Reg_0(U).                              (9)

No canonical splitting of J from N is implied. The averaging projector does canonically split E into its invariant14-space and its nontrivial96-space E_nt. Equation(6) forces b to land in E_nt. Likewise b_N has no invariant component, so rank b_N<=48 and dim ker b_N>=48. These vanishings are genuine universal exclusions, not assertions that either channel attains its remaining possible rank.

## 7. Normalizer symmetry and the one-block rank gate

The intrinsic normalizer elements h of PR610 exist for every direction G, preserve the whole set Z, and therefore induce automorphisms of M,D and of its complete cubic image V,L. They preserve the normalization marks and their pairing because these are fibers of the canonical cubic morphism. Every differential-operator sequence, cohomological map, and quotient used to define b is functorial under these automorphisms. Thus b is equivariant for the group generated by H_5 and these h. Possible scalar choices of linearization cancel in Hom and do not affect this assertion.

For a chosen pair of independent directions, the two quadratic h constructions induce nontrivial transvections fixing the respective directions of U. In a symplectic basis they give upper and lower elementary unipotents with nonzero parameters (PR610 explicitly gives parameter2 for one). Their powers give all parameters in F5. Upper and lower elementary unipotents generate SL2(F5), so the induced normalizer action contains SL2(F5). This group is transitive on the24 nonzero elements of the dual U, equivalently the24 nontrivial complex characters of U.

Let chi!=1. Both H1(Theta_M)_chi and E_chi have dimension4, and b restricts to

       b_chi:H1(Theta_M)_chi -> E_chi.                          (10)

An element of the normalizer carrying chi to psi gives invertible maps on these domain and target spaces commuting with b. Hence all24 maps (10) have the same rank r. This identifies ranks, not preferred matrices or bases. Consequently

       rank b=24r,
       dim ker b=24(4-r),
       dim coker b=14+24(4-r),  r in {0,1,2,3,4}.              (11)

The same argument applies to b_N, whose nontrivial character targets have dimension2. Its rank is24q for q in {0,1,2}. On ker b_N the descent map b_J is canonical. Its character-block rank is r-q, with 0<=r-q<=2, by the elementary rank formula for a filtered linear map. In particular r=q+(r-q), not a rank obtained by freely choosing a splitting. These are universal structural constraints on the exact two-channel obstruction.

Before the further exclusion in Section9, the embedded tangent dimension is accordingly one of

       99, 123, 147, 171, 195.                                (12)

Determining which value holds is exactly the rank of one four-dimensional character map (10), with its two-dimensional target filtration. No rank of that map, determinant, coefficient ratio, selected point value, smoothness of the Hilbert locus, or higher lifting is inferred. ## 8. Further resolution: four scalar gates, two in each channel

Fix a nontrivial U-character chi and let G=ker chi. Use the order-five intrinsic shear h of PR610 fixing G, with h|L_i=zeta^(i^2). Its induced action fixes chi, so it acts on the four-dimensional source and target chi spaces. All weights in this section are exponents modulo5 for this specified h; they are not numerical evaluations of the residual map.

First consider the source. In T_Z, the chi-isotypic summands come from the five directions other than G, one dimension from each. The shear cycles these five directions. Since h^5=1, their direct sum is the regular representation of <h>: choose a nonzero vector in one summand and apply h repeatedly. The pgl(A)_chi line is fixed by h. Indeed it is the Heisenberg operator whose label is symplectically dual to chi, which lies in G; h commutes with g and therefore conjugates this operator to itself. Thus

       H1(Theta_M)_chi has h-weights 1,2,3,4, each once.          (13)

On J, only the two five-point orbits associated to G contribute to chi. The shear fixes each of their paired values and each normalization branch. Formula(3) gives its local weight as missing tensor weight minus value tensor weight, namely

       [2j^2+(2i-j)^2] - [2i^2+j^2] = 2(j-i)^2.                (14)

The two orbits are labeled by d=j-i modulo sign, so d^2=1 or4. The weights are respectively2 and3. Translation of both indices leaves(14) unchanged. Hence J_chi has h-weights2 and3, each once.

For N there is an exact intrinsic identification requiring no unknown shear phase. Write Z_G for the five G-eigenlines and Z' for the other twenty-five centers. PR610 gives a surjection P->H0(O_Z'(3)) with kernel S, which also vanishes on Z_G. Therefore the full restriction image in H0(O_Z(3)) projects isomorphically onto H0(O_Z'(3)). Its intersection with H0(O_ZG(3)) is zero. The inclusion of the latter five-dimensional space consequently induces a D=<g,h>-equivariant isomorphism

       H0(O_ZG(3)) -> H1(D).                                  (15)

The five lines on the left have joint weights (-3a,-3a^2), a in F5. In Hom(S,H1(D)), combine such a line with a repeated-index weight of S*, say (2i+j,2i^2+j^2). The g-invariant condition is 3a=2i+j, or a=2(2i+j). Its h-weight is

       2i^2+j^2 - 3a^2 = -(j-i)^2.                            (16)

The ten repeated-index weights divide into two classes d^2=1 and4, each containing five weights. They yield h-weights4 and1, respectively. On either of these five-dimensional subspaces, the second Heisenberg generator cyclically translates i,j,a simultaneously. Its fifth power is the identity on Hom, so each is a regular representation of U/G. Thus each character of U trivial on G, in particular the chosen chi, occurs once in each h-weight. Consequently N_chi has weights1 and4, each once.

Since the weights of J_chi and N_chi are disjoint, the filtration of E_chi splits canonically under the h-eigenspace projections: its weight2,3 lines equal J_chi; its weight1,4 lines project isomorphically onto N_chi. In particular E_chi also has weights1,2,3,4, each once. Equivariance of b therefore makes (10) the direct sum of four maps between one-dimensional lines,

       beta_t: H1(Theta_M)_(chi,h=t) -> E_(chi,h=t),
       t=1,2,3,4.                                             (17)

Calling these scalar gates requires bases; their zero/nonzero status and their ranks do not. The normalization rank q in Section7 is the number of nonzero maps beta_1,beta_4. The residual descent contribution r-q is the number of nonzero maps beta_2,beta_3. Source directions of weights2,3 automatically satisfy normalization compatibility; a weight1 or4 source direction surviving normalization has zero full obstruction, because no descent target of that weight exists. Thus this character refinement actually separates the two channels, without asserting a globally preferred splitting of the original exact sequence.

Every other nontrivial U-character gate is carried to this one by the normalizer as in Section7. It is therefore enough to establish the vanishing or nonvanishing of these four one-dimensional maps for one chosen nontrivial chi. The present proof supplies their intrinsic geometry, representation lines, and universal cross-channel exclusions, not their values. The exact embedded tangent dimension, and any higher-order or original-source claim, remain unresolved.

## 9. A strict exclusion from moving one eigenline orbit

The normalization scalar gates cannot both vanish. Fix any direction G and restrict the center motion v to its five eigenlines Z_G, setting all other velocities to zero. Formula(1) then takes values in Hom(S,F_G), followed by the canonical isomorphism F_G->H1(D) of(15). At a center z in Z_G, the map

       T_z P(A) -> Hom(S,O(3)_z),  v_z -> [s -> ds_z(v_z)]

is injective. Indeed its projectivization is precisely the exceptional-divisor map P(T_z P(A))->P(S*) of the resolved complete cubic system, which is a closed immersion by PR604. Equivalently the first-jet linear map has no kernel; a kernel vector would give an exceptional base point. The five output fibers are distinct direct summands of F_G. Their direct sum is therefore injective on the twenty-dimensional space of these supported motions.

Consequently rank b_N>=20. Since rank b_N=24q, Section7 gives q in {1,2}, and hence r>=1. This argument also proves that these twenty supported motions meet the ambient pgl(A) subspace trivially, consistently with delta descending to the96-dimensional quotient. No selected derivative is evaluated: only the proved exceptional generation is used.

Combining this exclusion with(11)–(12), the sharpened possibilities are

       rank b_N in {24,48},
       dim ker b in {0,24,48,72},
       dim T_embedded in {99,123,147,171}.                      (18)

Thus the old195 upper bound is strictly improved to171. The surviving exact gate remains the four one-dimensional maps(17), with at least one of the two normalization maps nonzero. The proof does not decide which one or whether both are nonzero; no actual96-to110 rank is asserted.
