## 3. Independent check of the exact kernel formula

For nonzero a let S_a be the G-submodule generated by a^3. Each central
isotypic projector is an element of C[G]. Consequently its image on a^3
belongs to S_a. When that image is nonzero it generates the whole relevant
irreducible; when it is zero, every orbit vector has zero projection to
that irreducible. Multiplicity-freeness therefore gives exactly

    S_a = direct-sum of R with pi_R(a^3) nonzero.

There is no inference that a nonzero vector would generate every copy
of an irreducible in a representation with multiplicity greater than one.
That generally invalid inference is avoided by the explicit hypothesis.

Let F:S tensor D -> T be G-equivariant. If its complete h-family at a
vanishes, then for every g and every h,

    F((g a)^3 tensor h)=g F(a^3 tensor g^-1 h)=0.

Thus F vanishes on S_a tensor D. Conversely that vanishing immediately
implies the complete observation at a is zero. Exactness of tensoring
over C and the canonical isotypic direct sum give

    ker Ev_a = Hom_G((S/S_a) tensor D,T)
             = direct-sum over missing R of Hom_G(R tensor D,T).

All maps here are maps on the entire source D. The proof cannot be
replaced by one fixed h, since g^-1 h varies in the displayed equation.
It also excludes cancellation between distinct isotypic channels after
evaluation: if cancellation were to give zero full-source data, the
equivariance identity would force vanishing on the whole orbit-generated
submodule, including each independently projected summand.

The weights 3, 3, 4 follow independently from the dependency's decompositions

    A tensor D=A+V+P4+D5,
    V tensor D=A+V+P4+D5,
    P4 tensor D=A+V+P4+2D5

by taking Hom into 1+V+P4+D5. Rank-nullity then gives (G3)-(G4).
These are universal image dimensions, not ranks of a selected physical
instance. The note correctly makes no assertion that every formal support
pattern is realized by a pure cube.

## 4. Independent check of the generic open

Each map a -> pi_R(a^3) is homogeneous cubic and equivariant. If it were
identically zero, its underlying linear projection would vanish on every
pure cube. Polarization in characteristic zero says that pure cubes span
Sym^3 A, contradicting the nonzero summand R. Hence its projective zero
locus is proper and closed.

Projective A is irreducible. Its three nonempty open complements therefore
have nonempty intersection. This proves that Omega is nonempty, open,
and G-stable without choosing a point. At each of its points S_a=S, so
the exact kernel formula makes Ev_a injective on H.

The argument establishes generic existence in the full abstract parameter
space. It does not certify membership in Omega for any supplied point,
nor intersection with a further physically admissible locus not identified
with an appropriate nonempty open. The note states this application
boundary and does not claim an actual admissible generic observation.

## 5. Special-orbit and fiber-product audit

The primal/dual distinction in Section 1 is compatible with the imported
vanishing-polynomial arguments. In general the cubics vanishing on the
projective orbit of a are precisely the annihilator of S_a in S*. The
natural pairing between S and S* is nondegenerate on the matching dual
isotypic summands. Here A, V and P4 are self-dual and inequivalent.
Accordingly:

- Axis vanishing cubics equal the P4 summand of S*, so the primal orbit
  span is A+V and the missing channel is P4.
- Square-orbit vanishing cubics equal delta A*, the unique A summand of
  S*, so the primal orbit span is V+P4 and the missing channel is A.

This needs no arbitrary identification of a polynomial with a primal
tensor. It proves the exact patterns asserted in Section 4, rather than
only extracting them from dimension counts. The imported axis calculation
retains the normalizer sign on O(3); the square calculation uses twelve
zeros of a restricted binary sextic, not an ideal-sheaf jet theorem.

Writing H=H_A direct-sum H_V direct-sum H_P, let X and Y be the images
of the axis and square observations. The observations induce isomorphisms

    H/H_P -> X,       H/H_A -> Y.

Use their inverses followed by the H_V projection to define q_X:X->H_V
and q_Y:Y->H_V. A joint image has equal q_X and q_Y because both recover
the same H_V component. Conversely, if x and y have equal such components,
their H_A and H_P components together with that common H_V component give
a unique F producing both. Therefore the joint image is exactly

    X fiber-product over H_V Y,

of dimension 6+7-3=10. The maps are induced through the specified
evaluation identifications, with their line fibers, not by equating raw
binary coefficients at distinct parameter points. The text explicitly
preserves this distinction.

## 6. Physical multiplier and normalization audit

Under the stated gates, the error formula is

    error(a,h;x)=-a(x)^3 h F(a^3 tensor h).

The quadratic a is a nonzero section on the negative base line, so there
is a nonempty open set of x with a(x) nonzero. After choosing any eligible
fiber, its value is a nonzero scalar in the appropriate one-dimensional
fiber trivialization. No globally invertible a or cancellation at a zero
of a is asserted.

For L:D->Sym^12 Wprime, the map L -> [h -> h L(h)] is injective. Indeed
let h=sum H_j m_j be the formal universal quartic. Both h and L(h) are
polynomials in H_j and the positive binary variables. This polynomial
ring is an integral domain and h is nonzero, so h L(h)=0 forces L(h)=0,
hence L=0. This uses complete binary-vector equality as a polynomial
family in h, not one scalar binary evaluation and not division by each
chosen source value.

Thus zero complete error-family data at an eligible base fiber and a
parameter in Omega imply Ev_a(F)=0 and therefore F=0. The converse is
immediate. The base constancy used here comes from prior globality;
the theorem does not derive globality from the calibration equation.

The source-dependent multiplier is retained throughout. Its presence
makes the norm-error family quadratic in h and degree sixteen in the
positive binary variables. The complete corrected source and its domain
are still required: the fact that the full-domain comparison operator
can be applied to S does not permit evaluating the source response row
outside its certified domain on S-qW.
