# The residual smoothing map is nonzero and has even generic rank

## Theorem

Let F be the actual normalized Horrocks–Mumford bundle, B=End_0(F), and let q0 be the invariant smooth conic equation in the positive fixed plane R. For the negative quadratic parameter plane P=P(Sym^2 W), the actual residual invariant-positive multiplication map is

    m_res: W tensor S6* -> K8,                       (1)

with source rank four and target rank eight, as in NONZERO-INVARIANT-SMOOTHING-CHANNEL-BY-FLAG-FILTRATION.md. Then:

1. The actual map (1) is nonzero. More precisely, the actual relative plane boundary does not annihilate q0 times the genuine four-dimensional summand in the Cartan Sym^8 W subset H0(B_R(3)).
2. The generic rank of (1) is even. It is consequently either two or four, not zero, one, or three.
3. On a general smooth inversion-even quadric Q,

       h0(B_Q(4))_- <= 2.

   Its generic value is either zero or two. This does not decide between these alternatives and does not prove complete full-adjoint maximal rank.

The proof uses the actual exterior-symmetric evaluation resolution, equivariant lifting, and the geometry of a generated rank-two bundle on the invariant conic. It does not evaluate any actual HM coefficient or matrix. Sections 6–7 give the actual residual root and double-root formulas, including the nonlinear variation already contained in the original bundle family.

## 1. Notation and established actual maps

Write G=SL2(F5), with center generated by HM inversion. The negative two-dimensional representation is W, and A=Sym^2 W. The positive-plane representation V is the other genuine three-dimensional representation. Let P4 denote the genuine irreducible four-dimensional representation, and D=Sym^4 W the five-dimensional one. In particular

    Sym^8 W = P4 directsum D.

Let T=P(W*) be the negative hyperplane pencil. Its actual square-section bundle is A O_T(-6), coming from Y=W O_T(-3). Every hyperplane has precisely these three twist-three adjoints, and no odd ones. The actual boundary is the everywhere-surjective map

    delta5: H0(B_R(5)) tensor O_T -> C_lin(2),        (2)

where C_lin is the rank-five degree-26 hyperplane cokernel bundle.

Let Z subset T x P be the universal length-two root scheme, with eta:Z->T and nu:Z->P. Put S_n=nu_*eta*O_T(n), K=S_-5, and

    C_quad=nu_*eta*(C_lin(1)).

The invariant-channel theorem proves that Hom_G(C_lin,O_T(2)) has dimension one, and every nonzero map rho in it is surjective. Set

    F4=ker(rho),        K8=ker(C_quad -> S3).

The same theorem identifies the projected actual multiplication by q0 with a nonzero scalar times Disc times psi(-2), where

    0 -> W tensor S6* -> A tensor K(1)
      --psi(-2)--> S3(-2) -> 0.                    (3)

Restriction of the actual multiplication to the kernel in (3) is (1). Off the discriminant its rank adds to the rank-two projected part. The equality of kernel sheaves holds on all of P, but this rank-additivity statement is only made off the discriminant.

## 2. The ambient twist-five section space is the actual symmetric square

This step is not inferred from an Euler characteristic. Put U=H0(P4,F(3)) and d=det U. Equation (6) of the actual-primary-image-extension proof at the immutable source below is

    0 -> d O_P4(-5) -> wedge^2 U tensor O_P4
      -> U tensor F(3) -> B(5) -> i_*ell^2 -> 0,    (4)

where i embeds the twenty-five evaluation-degeneracy lines and ell restricts to O_line(-1) on each line. The map U tensor F(3)->B(5)=Sym^2(F(3)) is actual symmetric multiplication.

Let R1 be the image of the second term in the third, and R0 the image of the third term in B(5). Since H0(O(-5))=H1(O(-5))=H2(O(-5))=0, the first short exact sequence gives

    H0(R1)=wedge^2 U,       H1(R1)=0.

The next short exact sequence therefore gives

    H0(R0)=coker[wedge^2 U -> U tensor U].

The displayed map is the actual antisymmetrization u wedge v |-> u tensor v-v tensor u, up to one harmless common normalization. Finally H0(ell^2)=0, since its restrictions have degree -2. Thus restriction from R0 to B(5) gives the canonical multiplication identification

    H0(P4,B(5)) = Sym^2 U.                          (5)

The original HM character calculation identifies U with the genuine P4, not the center-odd four-dimensional representation. Its evaluation is onto on R, hence on the invariant conic C0=(q0=0). Both of these actual inputs are separately sourced below.

## 3. The invariant conic permits at most one quadratic relation among U

The invariant conic is balanced, with the actual equivariant restriction

    F_C0 = W tensor O_P1(-1).

Here O_R(1)|C0=O_P1(2), so F_C0(3)=W tensor O_P1(5). In particular the degree is five, not two. Only generation and rank two, rather than this exact degree, are needed in the following argument.

The restriction map U->H0(F_C0(3)) is nonzero because evaluation is onto, and it is injective because U is irreducible. Use the quotient convention for the projective bundle of F_C0(3). Its generated evaluation defines a morphism

    P_C0(F_C0(3)) -> P(U*).

Each projective-line fiber maps isomorphically to a line, because the evaluation onto the two-dimensional fiber is surjective. The image is irreducible and nondegenerate: a hyperplane containing it would be a nonzero element of U restricting to zero on C0.

Its image has dimension two. Indeed, if the image were an irreducible curve, the line image of every fiber would have to be that whole curve; it would then be a single line, contradicting nondegeneracy. Give the image its reduced induced structure and call it Sigma. Thus Sigma is an integral nondegenerate surface in P3.

A homogeneous prime ideal of an integral surface in P3 has height one, so is generated by one irreducible homogeneous polynomial. Nondegeneracy forces its degree to be at least two. Consequently its space of quadratic equations has dimension at most one. Via the projective-bundle formula this says exactly

    dim ker[Sym^2 U -> H0(Sym^2 F_C0(3))] <= 1.
                                                               (6)

Under (5), the target is H0(B_C0(5)), and the arrow is restriction of actual ambient adjoint sections. No statement about the degree or defining equation of Sigma is required.

## 4. A genuine four-space cannot disappear under the actual plane boundary

Let X=Bl_R(P4) be the universal negative hyperplane, with p:X->P4 and pi:X->T. Set E5,+=(pi_*p*B(5))_+. The actual relative restriction sequence and the twist-four odd square-section bundle give

    0 -> A O_T(-4) -> E5,+ -> G5 -> 0,
    G5=ker(delta5).                                  (7)

This is a sequence of the actual image sheaves. In particular it is not an arbitrarily chosen extension with the same ranks and degrees.

Suppose for contradiction that delta5 annihilates q0 P4, where

    P4 subset Sym^8 W subset Sym^2 D=H0(B_R(3)).

Multiplication by the nonzero conic q0 is injective on sections of the locally free sheaf B_R(3). Thus q0 P4 is a genuine four-dimensional subrepresentation of H0(G5). Its obstruction to lifting through (7) lies in

    H1(A O_T(-4)) = A tensor Sym^2 W
                  = 1 directsum A directsum D.      (8)

There is no P4 summand in (8). The connecting map consequently vanishes on q0 P4. Since H0(A O_T(-4))=0, it has a unique lift to H0(E5,+), and that lift is equivariant.

The blowup satisfies p_*O_X=O_P4, so

    H0(T,E5,+)=H0(P4,B(5))_+.

The lifted four-space therefore consists of actual ambient sections of B(5). Their restriction to R is q0 P4, so they restrict to zero on C0. By (5) they give four independent quadrics in the kernel appearing in (6), a contradiction. We have proved

    delta5(q0 P4) is not the zero bundle map.         (9)

The argument does not assert the rank of that four-space under delta5 at a particular parameter.

## 5. Nonzero residual map, even rank, and the improved bound

Under D=Sym^4 W, the actual square map at a root ell sends c in A to the corresponding product of elements of Y_ell=ell^3 W. The residual two-space c in ell W has image

    b(ell)w = (ell^4) symmetric-product (ell^3 w),
    w in W.                                         (10)

These sections lie in the Cartan Sym^8 W summand of Sym^2 D and span that summand as ell varies. One way to check the first assertion is that their second and fourth transvectants vanish. For the spanning assertion take w=ell: the resulting pure eighth powers span Sym^8 W in characteristic zero.

In affine root coordinates, the actual cross block at distinct roots r,s is

    delta_r(q0 [b(s)w-b(r)w]/(s-r)).                 (11)

The term delta_r(q0 b(r)w) is zero because b(r)w is an actual hyperplane square section and its q0 multiple extends on that same hyperplane. If the residual map were identically zero, (11) would therefore imply

    delta_r(q0 b(s)W)=0

for every pair of distinct roots. Polynomial continuation and the spanning statement after (10) would force delta5(q0 Sym^8 W)=0, contrary to (9). Thus m_res is nonzero.

Here is the parity obstruction to generic rank one or three. The center of G acts trivially on the base P=P(Sym^2 W). Its action on the standard O_P(1) is also trivial. Every G-linearized line bundle on P is O_P(d) with this linearization: two choices differ by a character of G, and G is perfect. Hence the center acts trivially on every such line bundle.

The center acts as -1 on both the source and target of (1). It therefore acts as (-1)^r on the determinant line of the saturated image, where r is the generic rank. The determinant is the double dual of the top exterior power of a torsion-free image sheaf, so is indeed a G-linearized line bundle on P. The preceding paragraph forces r to be even. Since the map is nonzero and its source has rank four,

    generic rank(m_res) is 2 or 4.                  (12)

This is a generic rank constraint, not a claim that every individual fiber has even rank.

Off the discriminant, (3) and the nonzero invariant projected channel give generic rank of the full first smoothing obstruction equal to 2+r, hence at least four. Its six-dimensional source has kernel dimension at most two. For a general nondegenerate negative quadratic q_-, consider the actual family

    q_- + s q0 = 0.

Every s!=0 gives a smooth even quadric. Proper cohomology and the first-order lifting condition bound the general nearby odd section space by the kernel of this actual first obstruction. Thus h0(B_Q(4))_-<=2 on a nonempty open of smooth even quadrics, and upper semicontinuity gives the asserted generic bound on the irreducible smooth-even family.

For completeness, the generic value cannot be one. The entire actual odd multiplication pencil over the even parameter space P(Sym^2 V* directsum Sym^2 W*) is a map from the center-odd ambient 44-space tensored by O(-1) to the center-odd ambient 48-space. The base and its standard O(1) are center-even. Applying the same determinant-line argument to its saturated image makes its generic rank even, and therefore its generic kernel dimension even. In conjunction with the bound just proved, the generic odd section dimension is zero or two. Higher smoothing compatibility could still remove the remaining two directions; no assertion is made that they survive.

## 6. Exact residual cross and diagonal formulas

This section fixes the actual operator, rather than just its source and target. Choose an affine root coordinate z with the negative hyperplane equation ell(z)=u+zv. Let

    a(z):A -> H0(B_R(3)),
    k(z):A -> H1(B(2))_-

be the actual plane square restriction and ambient boundary, of degrees six and five respectively. The latter satisfies multiplication by ell(z) times k(z)=0. Use the apolar identification of the residual source with ell(z) W and define

    b(z)w=a(z)(ell(z)w),
    kappa(z)w=k(z)(ell(z)w).                         (13)

Their degrees are seven and six. Formula (10) is precisely b(z), not a separate abstract model.

Let delta_r:H0(B_R(5))->H1(B_Hr(4))_- denote the actual plane boundary, with its parameter O_T(1) factor suppressed only in this affine frame. Restricting the ambient cohomology class q0 kappa(s)w to H_r gives (11) for r!=s. At r=s its value is

    delta_r(q0 b'(r)w).                              (14)

To verify the diagonal term, differentiate the actual relation ell(z)k(z)=0, or apply the existing Hermite formula before making the substitution c=ell(z)w. The extra derivative term a(r)(ell'(r)w) is a square section on H_r; after multiplication by q0 it extends and is annihilated by delta_r. Thus (14) does not discard a correction or assume any higher formal-neighborhood splitting.

The q0 projection of (11) and (14) to O_T(4) is zero, because the transvectant theorem shows that the projected boundary annihilates q0 Sym^8 W. Accordingly all these actual values lie in F4(2), and after the root transform in K8.

These formulas use ambient boundary coordinates at the source. Natural root-value coordinates introduce the complementary-root factors recorded next. They must not be interchanged without those factors. Positive multiplication has the sign used here; the signed deformation Bockstein for q_-+s q0=0 is its negative.

## 7. Scheme-theoretic division and double roots

Let P(z)=z^2-sigma1 z+sigma2 represent the root scheme, so q_-=u^2+sigma1 uv+sigma2 v^2. In the local root presentation of the residual source take w(z) in W tensor C[z]/(P). Divide

    kappa(z)w(z)=P(z) G(z)+r0+z r1,
    b(z)w(z)=P(z) J(z)+j0+z j1.                     (15)

The actual ambient boundary is -r1, and its positive q0 multiplication is

    -delta_z(q0 J(z)) modulo P(z).                  (16)

The boundary in (16) is the actual relative morphism evaluated over the length-two scheme. Thus its variation in z is retained, even at a double root. Replacing a representative w by w+P h adds b h to J, which is annihilated by delta_z(q0 -); hence (16) is independent of that choice. The ambient remainder in (15) is exactly unchanged, so the actual root-transform identification is unchanged.

For P(z)=(z-t)^2 write h=z-t and w=w0+h w1. The ambient boundary in (15) is

    -kappa'(t)w0-kappa(t)w1.

The quotient needed in (16), modulo h^2, is

    J = b''(t)w0/2+b'(t)w1
      +h [b'''(t)w0/6+b''(t)w1/2].                 (17)

In particular the nilpotent coefficient of (16) contains the derivative of delta_z as well as the derivative of J. Nothing is computed by independently restricting to the reduced root twice.

Globally the residual source equals

    nu_*eta*(W O_T(-6)) tensor O_P(1)
      = W tensor S6*.

Its restriction to the discriminant, identified with T by q=2t, is

    W tensor J^1(O_T(-6)) tensor O_T(2).

It has the canonical exact filtration

    0 -> W O_T(-6) -> (W tensor S6*)|Delta
      -> W O_T(-4) -> 0.                            (18)

Its underlying splitting is W O_T(-5)^2, but this does not canonically split (18), nor is (18) asserted to be preserved by the actual map. Along the discriminant the projected full six-space map vanishes, so the residual four-bundle is not the whole fiberwise kernel of that projected map. Formula (17) is an actual residual restriction, not a replacement for the full double-root problem.
