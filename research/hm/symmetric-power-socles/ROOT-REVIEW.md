# Root mathematical review

The common-radical exclusion was checked separately from the character arguments. Evaluation on any nonzero binary covector is equivariant for the Heisenberg subgroup, because that subgroup acts trivially on W. The resulting bilinear map is nonzero, and its common radical is an invariant subspace of the irreducible variable representation. Thus it has zero radical. Evaluating a putative degree-one socle element at every covector then makes the element zero.

The binary character identities were checked against the Horrocks–Mumford Appendix: Sym⁵W′=R_6 and Sym⁶W′=U plus T_4, with the ordinary and spin four-dimensional labels kept distinct. For the third symmetric power the degree-two source is irreducible; for the fourth, precisely the T_4 constituent is absent in the joint-multiplication target. Generation and the nonzero last component supply the required nonvanishing, rather than a representation match alone.

The final free modules follow from top Koszul homology. Independently comparing the upper Hilbert-numerator coefficients gives the penultimate entries 20p+70 for p at least five, 20 for p=3, and 10 for p=4, in the degrees stated in the proof. No other entries of a full minimal resolution are inferred.

This is conditional mathematical review, not formal proof-assistant checking. It concerns the exact socles of module symmetric powers, not higher-order deformation obstructions or the original fixed source.
