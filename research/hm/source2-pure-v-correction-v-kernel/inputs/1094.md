# The symmetric fourth lift is detected by an actual terminal incidence

## Theorem

Retain the conditional actual source2 nonzero pure-V compatible-radical
hypotheses. Write Q2:M2→M4 and Q3:M3→M5 for the same invariant quadratic,
I2=D, I3=T, I4=P for the actual radical images, and F for the negative
module generated by im h=I2⊕P_h. Put

    J3=ker j3,    K3=ker Q3∩ker j3,
    V5=im Q3=(M5)_T≅3T.

There is a canonical NONZERO equivariant map

    ell:V5→I3,      C=ker ell⊂V5,      C≅2T.           (1)

It is defined by actual maps, not by selecting a two-dimensional plane
in a multiplicity space. The unique quadratic fourth lift R of PR1069
satisfies the exact incidence criterion

    R=0  if and only if  ell(F5)=0.                    (2)

In the residual-large mechanism, PR1079 identifies F5 with the specific
NR5=T, so (2) is equivalent to NR5⊂C. In the nonzero-N0 mechanism,
PR1081 gives F5≅2T, so (2) is equivalent to F5=C as actual subspaces.
Here F5 may be a graph in the genuine terminal separation; it is not
replaced by an equal-character separated subspace.

Neither incidence is selected. In particular this theorem does not
prove R nonzero, exclude either remaining mechanism, or complete the
original proof repair. It links the actual invariant quadratic and
homotopy to the two-dimensional lift gate, not to an evaluated instance.

## 2. Canonical factorization through the odd quadratic image

For z∈J3 define

    theta(z)=X_x hY_y z−X_y hY_x z.                    (4)

The outputs hY_n z belong to I2: Q2hY_nz=j4Y_nz=Y_nj3z=0,
and im h∩ker Q2=I2. Thus theta:J3→I3 is well-defined. Each hY_n
annihilates F3, so theta(F3)=0. By PR1089 the T-isotypical part of K3
is exactly F3_T. Equivariance also kills its other constituents W′,S
when mapping to I3=T. Consequently

    theta(K3)=0.                                      (5)

The restriction Q3:J3→V5 is onto. Given any q∈V5, choose z∈M3 with
Q3z=q. Since j3(ker Q3)=I3, choose k∈ker Q3 with j3k=j3z. Then
z−k∈J3 is another lift of q. Its kernel is exactly K3. Thus (5) gives
one and only one factorization

    theta=ell Q3 on J3,       ell:V5→I3.              (6)

This factorization uses only actual maps and is canonical. It does not
depend on a choice of G or on the lifts just used to prove surjectivity.
The genuine inclusion NR3⊂J3 from PR795 and the surjectivity
theta(NR3)=I3 from PR1089 imply ell is onto. Therefore C in (1) is an
actual kernel, with character2T and dimension12.

The plane has a second actual description using the full middle pairing
of PR1091:

    A_h:J3→W*⊗I2,       A_h(z)(n)=hY_nz,
    C=Q3(ker A_h).                                    (6a)

Indeed theta is determinant rotation followed by multiplication applied
to A_h, so theta kills ker A_h. By (6) its Q3 image lies in C.
PR1091 gives ker A_h=3W′+2S+4T, dimension38. Its intersection with
ker Q3 is exactly the kernel of A_h restricted to K3. PR1091 identifies
that kernel as an extension of F3 by the pairing radical2W′+2S, hence
with character3W′+2S+2T and dimension26. The actual image under Q3
therefore has dimension12 and character2T, proving equality with C.
This is an equality of embedded images, not an arbitrary isomorphism
between two representations of the same character.

## 3. Exact cubic comparison with the symmetric transfer

    A4:Sym4 W⊗I4→I2,       B(a,b)p=A4(ab,p).          (8)

## 4. The universal operator loses only scalar endomorphisms

Define the fixed-variable linear operator

    D:End(Sym4 W)→Hom(Sym3 W,Sym5 W),
    D(U)(c)=x U(yc)−y U(xc).                          (10)

It is equivariant for SL2 under the fixed determinant convention, hence
for G. Its matrix has size24×25. Its exact kernel is C Id and its rank
is24. Here is a direct recurrence proof, independent of the matrix check.
Let e_j=x^(4−j)y^j for 0≤j≤4 and f_i=x^(3−i)y^i for 0≤i≤3.
If D(U)=0, then

    x U(e_(i+1))=y U(e_i),        0≤i≤3.              (11)

In the fraction field these imply U(e_j)=(y/x)^j U(e_0). Since U(e_4)
is polynomial, x^4 divides U(e_0): x,y are coprime. Degree four then
gives U(e_0)=lambda x^4, and (11) gives U(e_j)=lambda e_j for every j.
Conversely scalar Id satisfies (11). This proves the kernel assertion.
The source and target dimensions are25 and24, so (10) is onto as well.
The checker constructs the entire matrix and verifies rank24 and its
exact scalar kernel, using only universal binary-form coefficients.

The family p↦U_p in (8) is G-equivariant. If D(U_p)=0 for all p,
then p↦U_p takes values in the trivial scalar subrepresentation.
Hom_G(P,1)=0 forces the family to be zero. Thus D is injective on
this family space. In fact exactness and complete reducibility imply

    Hom_G(P,End(Sym4 W))
       → Hom_G(P,Hom(Sym3 W,Sym5 W))                 (12)

is an isomorphism: its kernel and the possible obstruction from the
scalar term vanish. Both spaces have dimension two. The first is
precisely the symmetric transfer space in (8), which has dimension two
by PR1069. This is a universal channel isomorphism, not a rank claim
about an actual source matrix.

## 5. Vanishing is exactly terminal incidence

The actual generated F5 is spanned by cubic descendants of P_h. Indeed
F2=I2⊕P_h and W²I2⊂I4, while W I4=0 by the retained full joint-kernel
identity. Thus W³I2=0 and

    F5=image[Sym3 W⊗P_h→M5].                         (13)

Equation (7) already puts every such descendant in V5, so ell(F5) is
defined. By (9), ell(F5)=0 exactly when D(U_p)=0 for every p. By the
injectivity in (12), this holds exactly when A4=0, equivalently B=0.
Finally B is symmetric and R is its diagonal, so polarization gives
B=0 exactly when R=0. This proves (2) in both directions.

In the residual-large case PR1079 gives the actual equality F5=NR5;
therefore (2) says that this marked T line in the multiplicity space
lies in C. In the nonzero-N0 case F5 and C both have character2T and
same dimension12. Thus containment F5⊂C is equivalent to equality.
Neither consequence selects the incidence or replaces a terminal graph
by its separated projection.
