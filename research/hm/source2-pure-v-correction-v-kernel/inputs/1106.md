# A canonical invariant-quadratic terminal line and its forced first-block image

## Result

Retain the conditional actual source2 nonzero pure-V compatible-radical
hypotheses. Let V_C be the UNIQUE actual V in NC2, let Q_d denote the
same invariant quadratic from degree d to d+2, and define

    V_Q=Q2(V_C)⊂M4,     T_Q=Q3(NC3)⊂M5.

Then the actual multiplication map gives

    W⊗V_Q ≅ T_Q,        T_Q=W V_Q≅T.                   (1)

In particular Q3|NC3 has rank six and kernel character W′+S+T.
The induced actual negative subpencil is saturated, with exact sequence
on P(W*) in the fixed determinant convention

    0→V_Q O(-1)→T_Q O→V_Q O(1)→0.                    (2)

This notation T_Q is distinct from the earlier U_Q=Q2(NC2), a
12-dimensional degree-four image.

Let ell:im Q3→I3 be the canonical terminal row of PR1094, C=ker ell.
Then

    h|NC4=0  iff  T_Q⊂C.                              (3)

If h|NC4≠0, ell|T_Q is an isomorphism onto I3. In particular it is
an isomorphism in the residual-large mechanism.

In that residual-large mechanism, suppose additionally that
 tau=ell|NR5=0. For the genuine negative separation N0⊕NR, with
projector e0, one necessarily has

    e0|T_Q injective,
    e0(V_Q)=(N0,4)_V,      e0|V_Q an isomorphism,
    e0(T_Q)=W (N0,4)_V,
    ell0|e0(T_Q):e0(T_Q)→I3 an isomorphism,            (4)

where ell0=ell|(N0,5)_T. Thus vanishing tau forces a specific nonzero
cross-block Q2 channel from the marked V_C, not just some unspecified
Q3 leakage. Neither tau nor the mechanism is selected. No fifth-symbol
value, positive cyclicity conclusion, or source occurrence is claimed.

## 2. The terminal image is exactly one nonzero T

First Q3 kills I3: I3=W I2, Q2(I2)=0, and actual multiplication commutes
with Q. Therefore Q3|NC3 factors through

    NC3/I3=W′+S+T.

Its image lies in im Q3=3T, so equivariance permits at most one T.
We now show its nonvanishing with an actual embedded source.

The unique V_C⊂NC2 meets I2 trivially. Hence Q2 injects V_C and
V_Q≅V. Suppose W V_Q were zero. Then V_Q would lie in the FULL
joint-negative kernel NC4=P+D by PR1068. That is impossible because
V_Q is a nonzero irreducible V and Hom_G(V,P+D)=0. Thus W V_Q≠0.

Actual negative multiplication W V_C lies in NC3, so commutation gives

    W V_Q=Q3(W V_C)⊂Q3(NC3)=T_Q.

Since W⊗V≅T is irreducible, its nonzero image W V_Q is a T. The upper
bound just proved is one T. Hence equality holds and multiplication
W⊗V_Q→T_Q is an isomorphism, proving (1). It follows that the kernel
of Q3|NC3 has character (W′+S+2T)−T=W′+S+T and dimension12.
This is subtraction after an actual image proof, not selection of an
arbitrary T in the ambient multiplicity space.

## 3. The actual saturated subpencil

Use the actual coefficient isomorphism in (1) to identify the target
with W⊗V_Q. The restricted parameter arrow in (2) then becomes

    V_Q O(-1)→(W⊗V_Q)O,       v↦l⊗v,

where l is the universal tautological negative linear form. Tensor the
binary Euler sequence 0→O(-1)→W O→O(1)→0 by V_Q. This gives (2),
including its locally free cokernel. Thus the image is saturated and
the arrow is injective at every projective parameter. This statement
concerns the actual subpencil identified by multiplication; it does not
evaluate a source direction or assert that the full pencil splits.
The quotient V_Q O(1) has rank3 and degree3, consistent with source
degree−3 and constant target degree0.

## 5. Specific leakage if residual tau vanishes

Assume residual-large and tau=0. Then NR5⊂C, while (3) says T_Q∩C=0.
Thus T_Q∩NR5=0 and the actual projector e0 is injective on T_Q.
Moreover ell|T_Q=ell0 e0|T_Q, so ell0 is an isomorphism on e0(T_Q).

If e0 vanished on V_Q, then V_Q⊂NR4. Genuine negative-module stability
and projector commutation from PR1072 would put W V_Q=T_Q inside NR5,
a contradiction. Therefore e0|V_Q is nonzero. Its domain is irreducible
V and the separated target N0,4=V+2P+D contains exactly one V.
Equivariance proves its image is the entire unique (N0,4)_V and that
it is an isomorphism onto this actual constituent. Finally projector
commutation and (1) give

    e0(T_Q)=e0(W V_Q)=W e0(V_Q)=W (N0,4)_V.

This proves every statement in (4). In particular the actual map
 e0 Q2|V_C is nonzero, and the marked terminal T it generates is detected
by ell0. One cannot discard that Q2 cross-block under the hypothesis
tau=0. Conversely nothing here forces tau=0, forces this channel to
vanish, or says that a nonzero channel suffices for tau=0.

## 6. Scope of the positive-coordinate and higher-symbol questions

The deduction uses only actual negative commutation and the full joint
kernel. It does not infer that joint positive-coordinate injectivity
forces an invariant-quadratic projection to vanish or be nonzero.
Such a claim requires the actual two-step positive products and their
projection onto (N0,4)_V or the marked terminal T in (4), with the
intermediate parity retained. The negative projector is not assumed
to extend to those intermediate positive-coordinate spaces.

Likewise no corrected fifth class is computed by (4). A fifth symbol
requires an additional actual Q-preimage and its correction-choice
quotient; the terminal image and the row detected here do not identify
that lift. The result supplies a specific embedded cross-block target
for a future positive-map comparison, not a substitute for one.
