# The exact Heisenberg obstruction to linearizing the cubic polarization

Work over C. Let V=V51 be the canonical cubic image, L0=O_V(1), and U=(Z/5)^2 its projective Heisenberg subgroup. All group actions fix the base. A linearization means a coherent action on the line bundle, not merely invariance of its isomorphism class.

## 1. An actual central extension and its scalar on L0

Choose generators g,s in the five-dimensional Heisenberg representation with

    g^5=s^5=1,   gs=z sg,   z=zeta Id_A,

where zeta is a primitive fifth root. The group H generated by g,s,z has order125, center <z>, and quotient U. These are universal presentation choices for the already specified irreducible Heisenberg module; no selected geometric evaluation is involved.

Use the usual action on polynomial functions, (h.f)(a)=f(h^(-1)a). The scalar z acts on Sym^3(A*) by zeta^(-3). Since S is the actual stable cubic subspace, the same scalar acts on S. Thus H acts on S*, preserves V in P(S*), and acts on the restriction L0 of the tautological O(1). On that bundle z acts by zeta^(-3): it acts by zeta^3 on the tautological O(-1) in S*, and dually by zeta^(-3) on O(1). This supplies an actual H-linearization of L0 and recovers its actual projective U-action. Reversing conventions replaces the exponent by its negative, without changing any assertion below.

The induced lifts of g and s to L0^m, for an integer m, therefore have scalar commutator

    zeta^(-3m).                                                   (1)

This includes negative tensor powers by duality and m=0 by the trivial bundle. The central exponent has not been inferred from a dimension or a character matching argument: it follows from the given inclusion S in the cubic polynomial representation and the actual complete embedding.

## 2. Why changing lifts cannot remove the obstruction

Any two bundle lifts of the same scheme automorphism differ by an automorphism of the line bundle over the identity of V. Such an automorphism is multiplication by a global unit, hence by a constant in C*. Therefore replacing either of the chosen lifts of g,s by any other lift leaves their commutator unchanged. In particular an honest U-linearization, whose two generator lifts must commute, can exist only if zeta^(-3m)=1. Since 3 is invertible modulo5, this is equivalent to 5 dividing m.

Conversely, if 5 divides m, the H-linearization of L0^m constructed above has trivial central action. It descends along H -> U to an honest U-linearization. We have proved the exact criterion

    L0^m is U-linearizable  if and only if  5 divides m.           (2)

Equivalently, the obstruction class of L0 in H^2(U,C*) has exact order5. For completeness, taking tensor powers adds the scalar two-cocycle classes. The fifth power is zero by the explicit descent, and the first power is nonzero by (1); since 5 is prime, the order is exactly5.

More generally, the commutator pairing for the theta extension of L0^m is

    ((a,b),(c,d)) |-> zeta^(-3m(ad-bc)),                         (3)

when (a,b) denotes the image of g^a s^b. This follows by moving the powers of g and s past each other using the presentation. If 5 does not divide m the pairing is nondegenerate. In particular its restriction to every cyclic subgroup is trivial, but the lifts for two independent cyclic directions cannot be made compatible. This explains concretely why the cyclic linearizations in PR637 do not combine to a U-linearization.

When 5 divides m, the set of isomorphism classes of U-linearizations on the fixed line bundle is a torsor under Hom(U,C*), which has25 elements. Indeed the quotient of two coherent systems of lifts is a scalar character, and conversely twisting by any character produces a new system. Scalar bundle automorphisms commute with all lifts, so they do not identify distinct characters. Scalar automorphisms still remain within each linearized object.

## 3. The obstruction persists throughout the twelve-dimensional fixed germ

Let R be a local Artin C-algebra and let (X_R,L_R) represent any U-fixed marked pair class specializing to (V,L0). Its underlying U-action is the unique one supplied by PR637. Every u individually preserves L_R, hence also L_R^m. Choices of lifts on L_R^m have a scalar cocycle in R*, because H0(X_R,O)=R. Its reduction is the obstruction of L0^m computed above.

The decomposition R*=C* times (1+m_R), followed by logarithm on the second factor and averaging over U, shows that reduction

    H^2(U,R*) -> H^2(U,C*)

is an isomorphism. Thus no infinitesimal choice can change the central obstruction, and (2) strengthens to

    L_R^m is U-linearizable  if and only if  5 divides m.         (4)

For 5 dividing m, a specified central U-linearization lifts, uniquely up to isomorphism with that fixed central identification: the relative obstruction and ambiguity groups are H^2(U,1+m_R) and H^1(U,1+m_R), both zero. There are still scalar automorphisms; no uniqueness of an isomorphism is claimed. Without specifying the central linearization, there are again25 classes, since Hom(U,1+m_R)=0.

Consequently the entire twelve-dimensional U-fixed pair-class germ has no honestly U-linearized original polarization. Its fifth-power polarization does admit a linearization at every Artin order. In particular an equivariant fifth root of a linearized L_R^5 with underlying root specializing to L0 cannot exist, although the ordinary marked root exists and is unique as a class by PR632. This is a genuine equivariance obstruction, not an ordinary Picard or root-existence obstruction.

For the full group Gamma, the restriction to U already excludes a Gamma-linearization of L0^m whenever 5 does not divide m. We do not infer sufficiency for Gamma from sufficiency for U.

## 4. The Heisenberg cover still linearizes every fixed class

Inflate the underlying U-action on X_R to H. The actual central H-linearization from Section1 has zero central obstruction as an H-linearization. The same logarithm and averaging argument for the finite group H therefore lifts it to an H-linearization on L_R. Its center acts by exactly the prescribed scalar zeta^(-3), not a nilpotent perturbation: an element of 1+m_R whose fifth power is1 is1, as logarithm shows. With the central linearization specified, the lift is unique up to isomorphism.

These lifts can be made compatible along a system R_(n+1) -> R_n: after choosing a lift at the next order, its reduction and the chosen previous lift differ by a relative character, which is trivial by H^1(H,1+m)=0. Thus the distinction is exact: U acts on the scheme and fixes the line-bundle class; its nontrivial Heisenberg central extension acts honestly on the line bundle.

For any finite-dimensional representation E of H over a field containing C on which z acts by zeta^(-3m), with 5 not dividing m, dim E is divisible by5. Diagonalize g, which satisfies g^5=1 with distinct roots. The relation gs=z sg sends the g-eigenspace of eigenvalue lambda by s isomorphically onto the eigenspace of eigenvalue zeta^(-3m)lambda. These five eigenspaces form a full cycle, all of equal dimension. This proves the divisibility without invoking a classification of Heisenberg representations.

## 5. A section-loss consequence for nonconstant U-fixed polarization arcs

Let a nonconstant compatible marked pair-class arc lie in the twelve-dimensional germ F^U, over R_n=C[t]/(t^(n+1)). Such arcs exist by the formal coordinates of PR637; nonconstant means at least one finite-order pair class differs from the constant one. PR628 makes the system an effective projective polarized family (X,L) over R=C[[t]]. Put K=C((t)). Then

    dim_K H0(X_K,L_K) belongs to {0,5}.                         (5)

First, the compatible H-linearizations of Section4 act on the inverse limit of the finite-order section modules. Proper formal functions identifies this limit with H0(X,L); equivalently, full faithfulness for coherent sheaves identifies the compatible line-bundle isomorphisms with actual ones. Thus H0(X,L) and its localization H0(X_K,L_K) carry the H-action with central weight -3. Section4 proves that the generic dimension d is divisible by5.

The R-module H0(X,L) is finite and torsion-free: flatness of X/R and invertibility of L make multiplication by t injective on L and hence on its global sections. It is therefore free of rank d. From 0 -> L --t--> L -> L0 -> 0, reduction gives an injection

    H0(X,L)/t H0(X,L) -> H0(V,L0).

The target has dimension10, so d<=10.

If d=10, this injection is an isomorphism. Choose a global basis lifting a basis of H0(V,L0). Its ten sections generate L: the cokernel of evaluation vanishes on the special fiber; if its support were nonempty, properness over the local DVR would force its image to contain the closed point, a contradiction. The associated morphism X -> P9_R reduces to the original closed immersion. It is a closed immersion over every R_n: it is proper and quasi-finite because its topological fibers are those of the special closed immersion, hence finite, and the cokernel of the corresponding finite algebra map is zero by Nakayama. Its image is a flat embedded deformation of V at each order.

PR622 makes each such embedded deformation projectively equivalent to the constant V, with the equivalence reducing to identity at the special fiber. Pulling back O(1) then makes each marked pair class constant. This contradicts the chosen nonconstant arc. Hence d cannot equal10. The only nonnegative multiples of5 below10 are0 and5, proving (5).

This argument permits arbitrarily delayed first nonzero polarization terms; it does not assume a nonzero first-order abstract deformation. Indeed the underlying abstract class of every U-fixed arc is constant. It proves a sharper section-loss bound here than the general bound d<=9, while not deciding whether the value0, the value5, or both occur.

## Contribution and scope

The new result computes, rather than leaves open, the central U-linearization obstruction of the actual cubic polarization: it has order5 and persists over every Artin fixed class. The fifth-power remedy is exact for U, and cyclic linearizations are proved incompatible across independent directions for the original polarization. The next consequence is a representation-theoretic quantization of section loss along nonconstant U-fixed polarized arcs: generic h0 is0 or5.

These are statements about V51 and its polarized deformation theory, conditional on the pinned inputs. They do not settle null's higher adjoint Bocksteins, selected actual source-instance gates, generic Weil-class algebraicity, or a full-Gamma sufficiency criterion. No selected point, coefficient, matrix, derivative, rank evaluation, or large computation was performed. The proof is mathematical, not proof-assistant checked.
