## 2. Functorial continuation through the actual Koszul cokernel

Let e=(e2,e3,e4) be an arbitrary endomorphism of the actual degree2–4 negative system. Write the Koszul map formally as

      delta(s)=x tensor m_y(s)-y tensor m_x(s).

Since e4 m_l=m_l e3 for both negative variables l, one has

      (1_W tensor e4) delta = delta e3.            (4)

Thus 1_W tensor e4 preserves the actual image of delta. Exactness of (2) gives a unique induced endomorphism e5 of M5 satisfying

      e5 mu = mu (1_W tensor e4).                  (5)

Equation (5) is exactly e5 m_l=m_l e4 for both actual next multiplications. The construction is G-equivariant and involves no arbitrary quotient splitting. Its uniqueness follows from surjectivity of mu, not merely generation at a generic parameter.

Conversely any degree2–5 endomorphism restricts to e, and (5) forces its last component to be this unique quotient action. Addition, identity and composition descend functorially through the same cokernel. Therefore restriction and continuation are inverse algebra homomorphisms:

      End_G,negative(M2 through M5)
          -> End_G,negative(M2 through M4)         (6)

is an isomorphism. This also proves the analogous Hom statement for any two actual systems with the natural exact sequence (2): a triple of intertwining maps extends uniquely by the cokernel. No essential-surjectivity claim for arbitrary pencil systems is needed.

Since the entire next cohomology H1(B(6)) is zero, adjoining its zero negative arrows imposes no condition. All further negative descendants of these spaces are zero by associativity. This last observation concerns the negative module generated by the displayed degrees, not an assertion about new independent generators in unspecified degrees.

In particular endomorphism extension existence, invertibility, products and radical identities give no new negative-continuation obstruction. Units extend to units because their inverses extend; conversely an invertible extension restricts to an invertible triple.

## 4. Exact annihilator ideal, with the dual orientation retained

Write the actual pencil product in the same labels by reversing (1):

      (a0,a1,a2,b) star (a0',a1',a2',b')
        =(a0a0',a1a1',a2a2',a0' b+a2 b').         (8)

For nonzero residual coupling set a1=a2. Equation (7) shows immediately that the kernel of terminal evaluation is exactly J in this case. Thus the faithful terminal image is the semisimple quotient of the full algebra.

In zero coupling the kernel consists precisely of

      I5={(0,0,a2,b): a2 in C, b in J}
         =C e_endpoint plus J.                    (9)

This is a three-dimensional two-sided ideal. Its definition as the terminal kernel is intrinsic; the displayed endpoint idempotent may change by a radical element under a change of allowed zero-coupling gauge. Let j_b=(0,0,0,b), and e_endpoint=(0,0,1,0). Formula (8) gives

      e_endpoint star j_b=j_b,
      j_b star e_endpoint=0,
      e_endpoint squared=e_endpoint.              (10)

Hence e_endpoint is noncentral whenever b is nonzero. It must not be confused with the separate central core idempotent e_core=(0,1,0,0), which acts as identity on U5 and therefore survives at degree5. In particular the zero-coupling terminal kernel is NOT just the radical and is NOT obtained by discarding the separate central C factor of ALG's abstract C times T(J).

In both branches there is an exact algebra quotient

      0 -> I5 -> End_G,negative(M2 through M5)
          -> C times C -> 0,                      (11)

where I5=J or (9) as specified. This is an exact statement about actual terminal evaluation, not an assertion that the terminal action is faithful on the whole endomorphism algebra. The square-zero radical is always invisible there; in zero coupling the endpoint idempotent is invisible as well.

## 5. Consequence for the obstruction strategy and scope boundary

The terminal nonvanishing and quotient theorems give real new information about the actual marked string. They do not, however, supply a new endomorphism-extension test: the entire extension problem is already forced by the actual Koszul cokernel. Even requiring invertibility or all algebra products at degree5 cannot select a proper subalgebra of the old fixed-negative algebra, because (6) is an isomorphism, not a dimension comparison.

Moreover the actual terminal image C times C is the same in both residual-coupling branches. Its action alone cannot recover the coupling branch, endpoint scalar, or radical element. A further exclusion must use genuinely additional data, for example positive-coordinate intertwining or a new degree-shifting compatibility relation, rather than asking again whether an old negative-pencil endomorphism extends. No such extra relation is assumed here. In particular the homotopy h:M4->M2 is degree-shifting, not an element of (6), and none of this proves that h extends as a graded module map.
