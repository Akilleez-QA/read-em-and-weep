# Root independent verification and integration

The pencil image is a globally generated locally free quotient of O4 and a subsheaf of O(1)^5, so its summand degrees are zero or one. The product-span argument rules out generic image rank zero or one. Rank two gives only the four kernel splittings. In degree two, coefficient vectors contained in a fixed three-space would produce O(2) plus O as image, impossible; hence all four coefficient vectors are independent. Six pre-symmetric coefficient tensors can lose at most the one-dimensional wedge-square X under multiplication, so the quartic family spans the full K5. This verifies the spanning hypothesis used in the quartic classification.

The rank-jump issue is resolved before contact: K is codimension two in XE, and degree minus two forces surjectivity of O4 to O(1)^2. Every fiber of the kernel bundle equals the actual product kernel. At an isolated square, fixed-ruling and transverse-base support limits intersect only in the square line. This forces a forbidden compatible square, including higher-order contact. The all-square section is incompatible with the splitting or span five. Thus K is squarefree.

After K intersect Sym²X is normalized to Cxy, the two nonzero complement functionals are independent or proportional. These are exactly the two displayed quartic normal forms. Matching the repeated product coefficient gives precisely four compatible correction parameters, all contractions. The no-two-by-two-product-rectangle argument follows by projection to Sym²(E/X) and would otherwise force a constant kernel bundle of degree zero.

The global quotient realization checks independently: L=B0_u(K) has dimension five and second-factor support at least two for every nonzero element. It contains no repeated tensor and meets no F_a for a different middle. The bad Grassmannian incidence has dimension at most 297 versus 300; a quotient avoiding its closure and the fixed-middle bad locus gives repeated injection everywhere and exactly the asserted residual ranks. Since K is contained in XE, every factorized tensor has a factor in X, so the ruled-family image exhausts the reduced rank-two locus. Ordered components embed as projective subbundle incidences; their only shared ordered points are (x,y) and (y,x). No scheme-transversality claim is made.

Integration with earlier results: in the global trilinear application, degree-zero complementary-product surfaces belong to the finite C_(2,2) incidence, and degree-one type III surfaces belong to the now finite rank-one (2,3) rectangular incidence. Therefore, within the full-support rank-five stratum whose factor projection is a line, a component requiring a positive-dimensional middle family must use one of the two quartic alternatives. In particular this local classification cannot eliminate a possible seven-dimensional component by routing every case through the old finite incidences. The new isolated quartic constructions have affine independent-zero dimension five and do not supply such a seven-dimensional component.
