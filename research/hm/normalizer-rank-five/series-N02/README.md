# Eigenpoint rank and canonical lines

## Scientific scope

The eigenpoint rank-six conclusion requires full normalizer equivariance, repeated-input injectivity, and at least a twenty-dimensional linear image. Universal rank-four statements have their own explicitly weaker hypotheses. Canonical-line results are imported from the rank-four series.

## Dependencies

Canonical-operation applications retain the explicit source/target representation, honest normalizer linearization, repeated-input injectivity, image-dimension and geometric premises of the supplied proofs. Abstract group arguments use only their stated complex Schrödinger-normalizer hypotheses. Historical audits are retained as mathematical evidence, not as formal-checker execution. No selected HM/source instance is evaluated.
