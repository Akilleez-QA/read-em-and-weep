# Scientific dependencies and closure

Give two distinct additive countermodels: a filtered formal DGA with the prescribed transgression, and a square-zero sheaf algebra with the exact underlying HM derived object. Neither retains actual Azumaya multiplication or perfect cyclic trace.

Only the explicitly specified additive cohomology/coordinate-module and equivariant data are preserved. Actual multiplication, Azumaya realization, and the perfect multiplication trace pairing are deliberately absent. These arguments alone do not determine actual-HM nonvanishing.

No checker or selected-source execution is claimed. Historical public links retained in SOURCES.md or the proof are mathematical references, not a statement that current connector inspection was repeated.

Current scope: these countermodels exclude deductions from additive data alone. They are not counterexamples to the canonical cubic nonvanishing theorem in PR194, commit e81dd9f12296353d175451908c168e5d0e752500, under its stated actual-presentation and transfer hypotheses. Historical statements that actual HM nonvanishing is undecided by these arguments do not reopen that theorem. Neither countermodel supplies a pure-six solution.
