# Scope and publication boundary

Give two distinct additive countermodels: a filtered formal DGA with the prescribed transgression, and a square-zero sheaf algebra with the exact underlying HM derived object. Neither retains actual Azumaya multiplication or perfect cyclic trace.

These are prior mathematical manuscripts. Original authorship dates are not inferred from archive or review dates.

Historical reviews and control receipts refer to their source editions and source hashes, not to a fresh rerun. Source-relative commands, input-copy statements, source-pins.json and manifest.json references inside retained manuscripts describe those original editions. Only explicitly included standalone controls are provided as runnable files. No controls were executed during staging.

Complete derivations and historical mathematical audits are retained. SOURCE-BINDINGS.json records original and public bytes. A current independent review of these final public bytes is still required. See DEPENDENCIES.md for unresolved prerequisite closure. No actual source value, higher-order existence, or algebraicity follows beyond the explicitly stated theorem.

Current scope: these countermodels exclude deductions from additive data alone. They are not counterexamples to the canonical cubic nonvanishing theorem in PR194, commit e81dd9f12296353d175451908c168e5d0e752500, under its stated actual-presentation and transfer hypotheses. Historical statements that actual HM nonvanishing is undecided by these arguments do not reopen that theorem. Neither countermodel supplies a pure-six solution. Public reference: https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/pull/194
