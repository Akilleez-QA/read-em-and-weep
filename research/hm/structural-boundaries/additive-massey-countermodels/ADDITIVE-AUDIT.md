# Independent audit of the additive reconstruction argument

The argument in ADDITIVE-RECONSTRUCTION.md is valid, subject to its stated characteristic-zero and equivariance assumptions. No substantive flaw was found.

1. Trace splits the actual underlying bundle as O direct-sum End_0 H. Giving the tracefree summand square-zero multiplication preserves that bundle, its twists, and its entire underlying graded S-derived section object, while changing the algebra structure.
2. H1(O(n)) vanishes for every twist on P4. Hence every degree-one class admits a tracefree closed representative. In the replacement algebra any pair of these representatives has strictly zero product. Choosing zero primitives gives zero triple representatives, and all degree-one product indeterminacy also vanishes. The Massey sets are therefore exactly {0}.
3. The adjugation statement correctly distinguishes the sheaf algebra and commutative Dolbeault/Thom–Whitney resolutions from ordinary ordered Cech cup multiplication. The degenerate multiplication trace pairing and the absence of an Azumaya realization are essential limitations, accurately identified in the text.
4. The saturation argument is valid using the standard derived adjunction between exact graded sheafification and derived graded sections. Higher graded cohomology of a coherent sheaf is irrelevant-ideal torsion by Serre vanishing: each homogeneous class is annihilated by a sufficiently high power of the irrelevant ideal. Thus sheafification of the derived section object recovers the original sheaf. The essential image is closed under retracts by naturality of the adjunction unit.
5. A nonzero torsion cohomology module cannot be a derived-saturated direct summand, because its sheafification is zero. A formal decomposition of the bounded derived section object would create precisely such a summand. Consequently its underlying S-complex is nonformal whenever higher graded cohomology is nonzero. This applies equally to the actual and square-zero algebras, and cannot distinguish their degree-one ternary products.

The theorem excludes additive reconstruction and underlying S-complex nonformality as sufficient arguments for the specified Massey nonvanishing. It does not decide actual HM nonvanishing or exclude a proof using actual multiplication, its perfect trace pairing, or the endomorphism/linear-resolution realization. The imported HM representation dimensions and linearizations were not independently recomputed in this formal audit. No actual HM matrix or Massey value was evaluated.
