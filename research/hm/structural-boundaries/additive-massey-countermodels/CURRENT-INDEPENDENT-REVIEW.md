## Ordered atlas exclusion

The K-theory signs in degrees minus one through two give rank two, c1=-H and ch2=-7H²/2, hence c2=4H². Pullback to (P1)^4 has c2 coefficient eight on each independent e_i e_j. Every pair of hypothetical summand multidegrees therefore satisfies (2a_i+1)(2a_j+1)=-15. Three indices force a real integer square to equal -15. This excludes all line-bundle splittings on that base, not just symmetric multidegrees. A globally ordered regular diagonal atlas glues exactly such line summands. Refinements and algebraic regaugings do not evade the contradiction; unordered, meromorphic and physical-X gauges remain outside it.
