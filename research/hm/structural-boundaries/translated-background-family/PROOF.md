# A compatible background-source translation family

## Result and scope

There is a concrete one-parameter family of the source-defining geometric data through the pinned source. Its parameter derivative commutes with the physical vector fields and deck projector, and with finite coefficient extraction. The source map has nonzero incidence. This is conditional on the SAME universal homogeneous primitive/contraction identities and determinant normalization used by the pinned source, not a new verification of their historical evidence.

The parameter **s varies background source data**. It is not the star/deformation-order parameter epsilon. Nothing here repairs an excluded epsilon-squared scalar-twist prescription or proves higher-order liftability, algebraicity of a formal deformation, a nontrivial bundle deformation class, independent H24 freedom, cancellation, nonvanishing of a detector. Existing pole, cutoff, staircase, standard-target and global comparison certificates are not automatically inherited.

## 1. Family, horizontal derivation, and physical directions

Let k have characteristic zero. Over S=Spec k[s], use four smooth elliptic curves with coordinates z_i,y_i satisfying y_i^2=z_i^3-z_i. Put x_1=z_1+s and x_i=z_i for i>1. Equivalently the first equation is y_1^2=(x_1-s)^3-(x_1-s). Infinity O_i extends as a section. The family of elliptic products is trivial in z,y coordinates.

Define V_i(z_i)=y_i, V_i(y_i)=(3z_i^2-1)/2 and V_i(s)=0, with other axes fixed. These are the usual global regular vector fields, transported to the family. Set sigma(s)=1 and sigma(z_i)=sigma(y_i)=0. Thus sigma(x_1)=1. Directly on generators, [sigma,V_i]=0 and the V_i commute. Independent deck signs fix s,z,x and change only the corresponding y. Hence sigma commutes with every deck action and with their full-sign projector P.

Write b=product_i y_i. Since sigma(b)=0, finite full-sign coefficient extraction E=P/b also commutes with sigma. This P is the deck projector, not a primitive matrix P_i(v), which generally varies.

## 2. Actual homogeneous map and root-line matching

Let N be the external tensor product of O(O_i). Use the map f_s to P^4 whose finite coordinates are v=(1,e_1(x),...,e_4(x)). Each x_i has polar divisor 2O_i, also when x_1=z_1+s. If x_i=a_i/b_i, the pair (a_i,b_i) is a generating pair of sections of O(2O_i). Replacing z_1 by z_1+s replaces a_1 by a_1+s b_1.

The homogeneous coordinates of f_s are the five coefficients of product_i(b_i T+a_i U). These are sections of N^2. They have no common zero: at any geometric point each factor is a nonzero linear form, and their product is nonzero. Therefore f_s^*O_P4(1) is N^2. Root frames depending on z and complex coefficients depending on x consequently have matching line bundles; this is not an incompatible mixture of two line conventions.

Pull back the SAME homogeneous four-term complex and its rational primitive recipes by f_s. Every universal algebraic primitive identity and determinant normalization remains an identity after this substitution wherever its denominators are units. Their truth is an inherited premise. No model polynomial, matrix, coefficient, point, jet or residual is evaluated in this argument.

## 3. The actual moving cover and localization

On each curve use U_0=C minus O and U_1=C minus the three points z=0,1,-1. For N_i=O(O_i), the rational sections e_0=1 and e_1=y/z are frames on U_0 and U_1 respectively. This follows from div(z)=2P_0-2O and div(y)=P_0+P_1+P_-1-3O. Their rational-function poles do not contradict their being frames of O(O).

The family common cover consists of intersections of these root legal opens, ACTUAL carrier/primitive denominator opens pulled back at v(x), and the desired standard coordinate opens. Use the full pulled-back carrier atlas with its inherited covering premise. The root and standard covers cover independently; their common refinement covers. Root masks alone do not guarantee that moving primitive denominators are invertible. Merely replacing every old carrier expression by v(z) would define a different family.

On every resulting intersection, localize at all denominators actually used. Derivations extend by sigma(f^-1)=-f^-2 sigma(f), and likewise for V_i. They need not preserve denominator ideals. Restrictions between these localizations commute with the derivations, in the sheaf sense. This does not establish commutation with a finite cutoff, staircase projection, or coordinate-dependent coefficient normalization.

## 4. Root line, old connection, and common split complex

For owner o with mask m, set nu_o=product_i(y_i/z_i)^{m_i} and D_o=diag(nu_o,nu_o^-1). In one direction, put

alpha=m y/[z(z^2-1)],
rho=V(log nu)=m y(z^2+1)/[2z(z^2-1)],
beta=alpha-rho=-m y/(2z),
q_ij=(m_j-m_i)y/z.

These formulas follow from the same root-line proof, now in z. In particular the old square frames are ell_0=1, ell_1=z, and e_1^2/ell_1=1-z^-2. The local old connection is flat in ell_m on N^2; in e_1 its coefficient is half the logarithmic derivative of 1-z^-2. Differences of old connections on N^k are -(k/2)(m_j-m_i)d_X log z, with contraction -k q_ij/2. These are differences of local connections, not a global flat connection on a positive-degree line.

Keep the source ranks (5,15,10,2), line weights (-1,1,3,5), and primitive matrices evaluated at v(x). In a common original-resolution reference, the split frames are

The primitive identities make the differential constant on its contractible pairs: the paired exponents agree (-1,3,5). The cohomology inclusion/projection remain I D and D^-1 P. ## 5. Infinity charts and the square-frame bridge

For the shifted factor on the inverse-x chart let t=1/x, u=y/x^2, A=1-s t and B=A^2-t^2. Then

u^2=t A B, z=A/t, r=y/z=u/(t A).

On the genuine bit-one inverse-x intersection, A and B are units. With the local root frame n_infinity=1/u, the relation e_1=B n_infinity follows from the curve equation. It remains a valid root frame at O because B(O)=1. The inverse-x chart does not cover all of U_1 for nonzero s: x=0 can lie in U_1. Retain the finite-x chart or use the invariant inverse-z chart as well.

The OLD square frame is ell_old=z, whereas the target homogeneous square frame is ell_target=x. They obey ell_old=A ell_target. With the convention nabla ell=c_ell ell,

c_old=c_target+d_X log A.

The old connection flat in ell_old therefore has coefficient c_target=-d_X log A. On N^k the corresponding correction is -(k/2)d_X log A. Explicitly,

h_old=e_1^2/ell_old=B/A^2,
h_target=e_1^2/ell_target=B/A,
alpha_form=(1/2)d_X log h_old
          =(1/2)d_X log h_target-(1/2)d_X log A
          =-t dt/(A B).

Here d_X is relative differentiation along the curve, with s fixed. These gauge formulas apply only on the old/target frame intersection where A is a unit. They do not authorize extending this particular conversion across A=0. Root-specific frames and the target homogeneous frames are allowed to differ, but their conversion cannot discard the logarithmic term.

The horizontal derivative satisfies sigma(t)=-t^2, sigma(u)=-2tu, sigma(A)=-tA and sigma(B)=-2tB. Hence sigma(r)=0, while the infinity odd basis u moves. Let c_u extract the odd coefficient in f=f_0+u f_1, with sigma_coeff the induced derivative on invariant coefficients. Then

sigma_coeff c_u-c_u sigma=2t c_u.

The analogous product-basis correction sums the shifted-factor terms. Thus finite E commutation is not unqualified all-chart extraction commutation. An invariant inverse-z chart has an invariant odd basis, but replacing the target atlas with it is a separate comparison. No old infinity factor 1-t^2, pole certificate or terminal truncation rule is reused without proof.

## 6. Nonzero incidence and the limited nontriviality statement

At s=0 all the original defining formulas recover. In the fixed z,y trivialization, the elliptic geometry, physical V_i, deck action and root data are constant, but v_1=e_1(z)+s, so sigma(v_1)=1. The evaluation differential containing v_1 consequently has nonzero marked source-map incidence. This is a symbolic coordinate identity, not a numerical source evaluation.

Under the restricted equivalence of identity-central-fiber base reparametrizations preserving each V_i and the deck full-sign projector, the marked map is infinitesimally nontrivial. Such a reparametrization has generator D in Der_k(k(E^4)) commuting with all those operations. The pinned centralizer theorem forces D=0. Trivializing v_1 with fixed target would require D(e_1(z))+1=0, which is impossible.

This restricted marking statement allows neither target automorphisms nor complex/frame gauge transformations. It does not establish nontriviality of an underlying bundle or derived deformation class. In particular nonzero incidence of one matrix entry is not a sensitivity theorem for the final scalar.

## 7. Coupled source response now has an actual family

In the finite common root reference sigma(nu_i)=0, hence sigma(D_i)=sigma(D_i^-1)=0. The literal row transition is g_ij=D_j(P_i I_j)^t D_i^-1. Its derivative is therefore

C_ij:=sigma(g_ij)
     =D_j[(sigma P_i)I_j+P_i(sigma I_j)]^t D_i^-1.

Both primitive terms remain. Their parameter dependence is induced by the SAME map: sigma(v_k)=e_{k-1}(x_2,x_3,x_4) for the single first-factor shift. They are not independently chosen leaves.

For H24=tf(g^-1 V_2 V_4 g), commutation with V_i and the fixed tracefree projection gives

sigma(H24)=tf(-g^-1 C g^-1 V_2 V_4 g+g^-1 V_2 V_4 C).

The physical mixed derivative acts on the complete products in C. Every other source, primitive, raw block/scalar, HPL, kernel, weight, test and chart-extraction variation must be retained, except factors actually proved sigma-fixed. This supplies a concrete application of the coupled response identity. It proves neither sigma(H24) nonzero nor freedom to vary H24 while freezing its ancestors and the rest of the source.
