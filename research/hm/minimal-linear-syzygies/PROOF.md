# The first minimal syzygies of the HM line ideal

Work over C. Let S=Sym(T), T=S_1, dim T=5, and let I be the saturated ideal of the twenty-five transverse HM evaluation-degeneracy lines in P^4. Betti indices below refer to a minimal graded free resolution of I itself, not S/I.

## 1. Imported premises and the actual minimal presentation

PR469 proves that I is minimally generated by H=I_5, dim H=6, and the canonical complement U_6 in I_6, dim U_6=10. Thus its minimal free cover is

F_0=(S(-5) tensor H) direct-sum (S(-6) tensor U_6).

Write Z=ker(F_0 -> I). PR466 and PR468 prove that S_2 H -> I_7 and S_3 H -> I_8 are injective, and that

X=ker(T tensor U_6 -> I_7/(S_2 H)) = A* tensor W', dim X=10.

Projection Z_7 -> T tensor U_6 is injective by the first injectivity above, and its image is exactly X: every element of X has a unique compensating coefficient in S_2 tensor H. Thus Z_7 is canonically isomorphic to X. There are no Z_d for d<=6, since the quintics have no linear relations and U_6 is a direct complement. Therefore these ten degree-seven relations are the ten minimal first syzygies.

Choose an N-equivariant identification U_6=T tensor W'. Then

T tensor U_6=(T tensor T) tensor W'.

The exterior summand Lambda^2 T tensor W' is the irreducible A* tensor T_4, whereas X is A* tensor W'. These irreducibles are inequivalent. The equivariant alternating projection therefore kills X. Consequently X lies in Sym^2 T tensor W', viewed inside T tensor T tensor W'. This placement, not a dimension count, controls the next differential.

## 2. A universal symmetrization exclusion

For any complex vector space T, consider

sigma: T tensor Sym^2 T -> Sym^2 T tensor T,

obtained by symmetrizing the first two slots in T tensor T tensor T; the source is symmetric in the last two slots. This map is injective, hence an isomorphism since source and target have the same dimension.

Indeed suppose v is in its kernel. Put s=(12) and t=(23), acting on the three tensor slots. The source condition gives tv=v; the kernel condition gives sv=-v. Hence sts(v)=v, whereas tst(v)=-v. The braid identity sts=tst implies v=-v, so v=0 in characteristic zero. Tensoring this argument with any auxiliary vector space preserves injectivity.

Apply the lemma with auxiliary W'. Restriction yields an injective map

T tensor X -> Sym^2 T tensor U_6.

It is precisely the U_6-component of multiplication T tensor Z_7 -> Z_8 under section 1's identification. Therefore multiplication T tensor Z_7 -> Z_8 is injective. In particular

beta_{2,8}(I)=0.

This proof never evaluates the HM syzygy coefficients. It uses the actual embedding of X into the symmetric summand supplied by its inequivalent normalizer type.

## 3. Exactly forty minimal octic first syzygies

The imported Hilbert function gives dim I_8=270. Since I is generated in degrees five and six,

dim Z_8 = 6 dim S_3 + 10 dim S_2 - dim I_8
          = 6(35)+10(15)-270 = 90.

Section 2 shows that the products of the degree-seven syzygies form a fifty-dimensional subspace. There are no lower syzygies. Thus

beta_{1,8}(I)=dim(Z_8/T Z_7)=90-50=40.

Together with section 1, beta_{1,7}(I)=10. The seventy-dimensional kernel of T tensor I_6 -> I_7 studied in PR468 is a different presentation: its sixty old-product Koszul relations do not count as minimal first syzygies of I.

## 4. A constrained remaining resolution, not an inferred Betti table

The established Hilbert function of S/I is

1,5,15,35,70,120,170,200,225,...,

with value 25(d+1) for d>=7. Taking fifth finite differences gives

(1-t)^5 H_I(t)=6t^5+10t^6-10t^7-40t^8+45t^9-5t^10-5t^11.

For example the coefficients in degrees 7 through 11, using I_5=6,I_6=40,I_7=130,I_8=270,I_9=465,I_10=726,I_11=1065, are respectively

130-5(40)+10(6)=-10,
270-5(130)+10(40)-10(6)=-40,
465-5(270)+10(130)-10(40)+5(6)=45,
726-5(465)+10(270)-10(130)+5(40)-6=-5,
1065-5(726)+10(465)-10(270)+5(130)-40=-5.

There are no later coefficients because from degree seven onward the quotient Hilbert function is linear, and the subsequent fifth differences vanish.

For completeness the module I has projective dimension three. Saturation gives H^0_m(S/I)=0, while the nonzero restriction deficiency H^1(I_C(6))=5 gives H^1_m(S/I)_6 !=0. Thus depth(S/I)=1; the exact sequence 0 -> I -> S -> S/I -> 0 gives depth I=2. Auslander--Buchsbaum over the polynomial ring gives pd I=3. The established reg I=8 bounds beta_{i,j} by j-i<=8.

Minimality, the least first-syzygy degree seven, and beta_{2,8}=0 imply that second syzygies begin in degree nine and third syzygies begin in degree ten. Therefore the remaining free modules necessarily have the form

F_1 = S(-7)^10 direct-sum S(-8)^40 direct-sum S(-9)^a,
F_2 = S(-9)^(45+a) direct-sum S(-10)^b,
F_3 = S(-10)^(5+b) direct-sum S(-11)^5,

for some nonnegative integers a,b. These identities follow by comparing the remaining coefficients only after the structural degree restrictions have been proved. Neither a nor b is asserted to vanish. Determining them, and determining the remaining differentials, remain open.
