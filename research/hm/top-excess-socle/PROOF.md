# The top derived excess is a single residue-field layer

Work over C, with the original S-grading and the Veronese grading distinguished throughout. Let S=Sym(T), dim T=5, dim E=5, dim W=2, and use the actual quadratic presentation of H pinned in PR490 and PR499. Let P=Sym(E tensor Sym^2 W), with its variables of Veronese degree one, and put A=S^(2) and B=direct sum_d Sym^d E tensor Sym^(2d) W. Shifts mean M(s)_d=M_(d+s). All canonical-module statements below are non-equivariant; equivariant versions retain the canonical determinant coefficient lines.

The conclusion strengthens PR540:

Tor_4^P(A,B) is isomorphic to (P/P_+)(-7)^{45}.

In particular it has length45, is supported in exactly Veronese degree7, and is annihilated by P_+. This is not an identification with the unresolved parameter k of PR503.

## 1. A universal binary module, with a genuine presentation

Choose x,y in W and a basis e_i of E. Write z_(i,0)=a_i, z_(i,1)=b_i, z_(i,2)=c_i for the P variables corresponding to e_i tensor x^2, e_i tensor xy, e_i tensor y^2. Define the P-module N by the presentation with generators x,y in degree zero and relations

 a_i y-b_i x=0,   b_i y-c_i x=0.

For each p>=1, write N_p=Sym_P^p N. Its presentation has generating space Sym^p W and relation space generated by the displayed relations multiplied by every element of Sym^(p-1) W. Define

 M_p = direct sum_(d>=0) Sym^d E tensor Sym^(2d+p) W,

with P-action given by ordinary multiplication in both factors. The formula sending a polynomial monomial times a binary generator to the product in these two factors defines a surjection N_p -> M_p. We prove injectivity at the level of actual relations.

A degree-d monomial of the source is specified by an E-index multiset, numbers q_1,...,q_d in {0,1,2} attached to its d occurrences, and a generator x^(p-t)y^t with 0<=t<=p. Its image is determined exactly by that index multiset and the total binary exponent q_1+...+q_d+t. Occurrences may temporarily be labeled even when their E indices coincide; forgetting the labels recovers the original commutative monomial.

The presentation relations permit transferring one unit between any q_j and t whenever both stay in their allowed intervals. For example (q_j,t)=(0,t) can be replaced by (1,t-1) for t>=1, using a_i y=b_i x; similarly (1,t) can be replaced by (2,t-1), using b_i y=c_i x. All reverse transfers are allowed as well. These moves are precisely multiples of defining module relations.

Every fiber of the total exponent is connected by these moves. Indeed compare an assignment with a target assignment. If t is below the target t, some q_j exceeds its target value, and units can be moved from such entries into t until its target is reached. If t is above target, use deficient q_j in the reverse direction. Now keep t fixed and choose an excessive q_i and a deficient q_j. If t<p, first move a unit from q_i into t and then from t into q_j. If t=p, first move a unit from t into q_j and then from q_i into t. The latter move is possible because p>=1. Each pair reduces the total discrepancy of the q entries and preserves t. Iteration reaches the target assignment.

Thus the quotient has at most one spanning monomial for each index multiset and total exponent. The corresponding images are distinct standard monomials in M_p, and every such standard monomial is reached. They are therefore linearly independent. This proves the P-module isomorphism

 N_p = M_p  for every p>=1.

The p>=1 restriction matters: the binary generator is the buffer in this straightening proof. No analogous assertion is being made for N_0=P and M_0=B.

## 2. Base change and parity are actual module operations

Give S its original grading, so that P variables act in original degree two. Pure quadratic presentation gives S tensor_P N=H. Symmetric powers commute with this base change, so

 S tensor_P M_p = S tensor_P Sym_P^p N = Sym_S^p H.

This is an actual isomorphism induced by the generators and relation columns, not an equality inferred from Hilbert functions.

Let O be the odd Veronese module, O_d=S_(2d+1). As ungraded P-modules S=A direct sum O; this direct sum respects parity and the regrading just specified. Consequently the preceding isomorphism splits into

 A tensor_P M_p = (Sym_S^p H)^even,
 O tensor_P M_p = (Sym_S^p H)^odd,

where the dth pieces on the right mean original degrees 2d and 2d+1, respectively. These are ordinary tensor products; no flatness is asserted or needed. The parity splitting is a direct-sum decomposition of the module being tensored.

## 3. Canonical modules identify the top excess

PR540 computes

 omega_A=O(-3),    omega_B=M_8(-5),

since their degree-d pieces are S_(2d-5) and Sym^(d-5) E tensor Sym^(2d-2) W. Thus

 omega_A tensor_P omega_B
   = (O tensor_P M_8)(-8)
   = ((Sym_S^8 H)^odd)(-8).

PR494 proves that Sym_S^8 H has original Hilbert function (9,45,55), supported in degrees0,1,2 only. Moreover its original degree-one component is T tensor Sym^8 W, and it has no component in any odd original degree>=3. Hence its odd Veronese part is the 45-dimensional residue-field module in Veronese degree zero. It follows, with the actual action included, that

 omega_A tensor_P omega_B = (P/P_+)(-8)^{45}.

Applying the canonical top-excess duality from PR540,

 (Tor_4^P(A,B))^vee = (omega_A tensor_P omega_B)(15),

gives the claimed Tor_4=(P/P_+)(-7)^{45}. The shift (15) takes the canonical product's degree8 to degree-7; graded duality then places Tor_4 in degree7.

As a second explicit consequence, ordinary base change of the canonical module of B has Hilbert function9 in Veronese degree5 and55 in degree6, and zero elsewhere:

 A tensor_P omega_B = ((Sym_S^8 H)^even)(-5).

Its nonzero action is the actual multiplication

 (E tensor Sym^2 W) tensor Sym^8 W -> E tensor Sym^10 W,

and every product of two positive-degree P elements kills it. This follows from the same presentation isomorphism, not just from the dimensions9 and55.

## 4. Scope and verification

This determines the full highest derived-intersection module, not merely its socle. It supplies no claim about the separate unresolved S-linear channels of PR503, individual nonvanishing of Tor1 through Tor3, the original fixed-pair residual, higher-order lifting, or algebraicity. No selected actual HM/source-instance evaluation, numerical multiplication matrix or rank computation, coefficient search, or large computation was performed.
