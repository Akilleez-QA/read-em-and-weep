## Checks

1. The octic character subtraction uses the actual injective old-product and linear-syzygy maps, not a numerical inference of their rank. Expanding (U+W') tensor (W'+Q_4) gives 1+2U'+W+Q_4+2R_6+J_5. The Eagon--Northcott quotient removes W+R_6+Q_4; multiplication of the septic syzygies removes 1+U'+R_6. The result is U'+J_5. The identity W tensor R_6=U'+T_4+J_5 agrees with the SL_2(F_5) character table, including the two order-five classes.

2. The dual resolution shifts are consistent. Ext^4(S/I_C,S(-5)) is the graded dual Rao module. Normalizing by six gives free-generator degrees 0 and 1, relation degrees 1 and 2, and next free-generator degrees 3 and 4. The only earlier nonzero cohomology relevant to this complex is Ext^3, the canonical module. By graded local duality and Serre duality its degree n part is H^0(omega_C(n)); disjoint lines make its first nonzero degree two. It therefore starts in normalized degree eight. Ext^i for i<3 vanishes because S/I_C has dimension two. This justifies computing Tor in degree three from the displayed minimal dual complex without assuming global exactness of that complex.

3. The Tor long exact sequence from P/N_0=R_6 tensor k(-1) has target Lambda^2 T tensor R_6 in internal degree three, followed by Tor_1(N_0,k)_3. The next term Tor_1(P,k)_3 vanishes because P has a complete presentation in degrees one and two. Multiplicity one and inequivalent normalizer types yield precisely the two scalar channels. PR477's twenty-dimensional quotient is the unique T_4 isotypic summand, so its map is an isomorphism on that summand. The remaining cubic relations are exactly the zero-scalar channels.

The added canonical-kernel paragraph also passes: Tor_3(k(-1) tensor R_6,k) has no degree-three part, so Tor_2(N_0,k)_3 injects with image ker f. The primary submodule starts in degree three, so its positive Tor groups have no degree-three part. Its extension therefore identifies Tor_2(K,k)_3 with Tor_2(N_0,k)_3 canonically.

4. The cubic ghost count is valid. The lower-relation free cover contributes ninety degree-three vectors; the entire degree-three kernel of the five-generator cover has dimension ninety. There are no syzygies below degree three. Adding r minimal cubic relations therefore leaves r minimal degree-three second syzygies, so beta_{1,3}(K)=beta_{2,3}(K)=r.

5. The rank obstruction is intrinsic. D=S R_2 is generated in degree two but contains the Euler line sheafwise. A rank-one quadratic image in the Euler quotient would have saturated reflexive hull O(a). Its inclusion in the locally free quotient extends across codimension two. The Euler sequence excludes a>=1, while fifteen independent quadratic sections exclude a<=-1. The resulting O line gives a normalizer-invariant line in V, contrary to irreducibility. Thus rank D>=3. Equivalently, saturation itself is reflexive by the depth lemma, since its quotient in the locally free bundle is torsion-free.

6. The lex argument uses the correct degree-two-only convention for D. The extremal shadow would be fifty-dimensional. The lex module then starts with m^2 e_1 plus x_0 m e_2 and has no cubic generators. This particular truncation has a two-linear resolution; no assertion that arbitrary lex ideals have linear resolutions is needed. A first later generator of degree e>=4 has no adjacent degree-e first-syzygy Betti entry to cancel, and cannot be removed in the consecutive-cancellation passage to D. Thus there are no later generators, forcing rank two and contradicting the preceding rank obstruction. No unknown quotient rank is inserted into a rank-adjusted persistence theorem.

The precise cancellation source was checked directly in Roger Dellaca, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1511.07859, Lemmas 6.3, 6.6, 6.7 and the proof following them on printed pages 11--12. Lemma 6.6 supplies preservation of Betti numbers under the polarization/specialization operation; the combination supplies cancellation from the lex module. The special truncation's linear resolution and the ensuing application are explicit in the proof rather than borrowed from an overbroad formulation.

## Boundaries

The proof forces at least one scalar to be nonzero; it does not determine which scalar survives, or show that both are nonzero. The irreducible alternatives and the canonical twenty-dimensional primary channel are stronger than a dimension bound, but they do not settle any original fixed quadratic/cubic pair. No selected HM/source-instance evaluation, numerical matrix/rank calculation, coefficient search, generator/checker or large computation was performed. Actual-source membership, higher-order lifting and algebraicity remain separate unresolved dependencies.
