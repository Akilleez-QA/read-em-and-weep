# Remaining rank-five curve support has degree at most seventy-five

Under the hypotheses of PROOF.md, let Γ5 be the reduced union of the curve components of Z5. The same complete intersection of degrees(10,14,14) constructed there contains Γ5. At a generic point of any such curve, rank b≤9, so its local equations have orders at least(1,5,5).

In the regular local ring of dimension three, replace the first parameter f by f⁵. The three resulting equations have order at least five. The equal-order Hilbert–Samuel comparison gives colength at least125; the power filtration multiplies the original colength by five. Thus each curve's generic multiplicity in the auxiliary complete intersection is at least25.

It follows that

    25 deg Γ5≤1960,
    deg Γ5≤78.

The reduced support Γ5 is normalizer-invariant. The Heisenberg Hilbert congruence, also proved for reduced curves in ../rank-three-curve-constraints/PROOF.md, Section2, gives5|deg Γ5. Hence

    deg Γ5≤75.

After GLOBAL_EMPTINESS.md, every point of Z5 has residual rank exactly five. Neither existence of these curves nor exclusion of isolated rank-five points follows. The bound concerns reduced curve support only, not the length or degree of the full determinantal scheme.
