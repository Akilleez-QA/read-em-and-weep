# Independent audit: small invariant curve cycles

Status: PASS, including the optional strengthening to degree at most twenty-four. No outstanding corrections.

For total degree below twenty-five, a component cannot have H-orbit size twenty-five. An orbit of size five forces six different stabilizer lines and hence thirty distinct components. Thus every component is H-stable and spans P4 by irreducibility. Projective transformations fixing an integral spanning curve pointwise are scalar, so all claimed actions on the normalization are faithful. A degree-four component would have rational normalization, on which C5 times C5 cannot act faithfully. Each component therefore has degree at least five, leaving at most four components in the stated range. Perfectness of SL2(F5) makes their permutation action trivial.

The low-genus lemma is correct. A5 has no faithful elliptic action because its map to origin-preserving automorphisms is trivial and translations are abelian. In genus two the hyperelliptic branch set has six points, whereas every orbit of faithful A5 on P1 has at least twelve by cyclic point stabilizers. For a faithful SL2(F5) action in genus at most four, quotienting by the central involution gives a faithful A5 action on a curve of genus at most two. The positive-genus cases are excluded as above; the rational case has a nonempty branch set of size at most ten and is impossible. This also treats unramified double covers.

The normalization's embedding linear series is simple and base-point-free. Independently checked Castelnuovo's cited formulation in Korchmaros--Torres, Section 2.1, equation (2.1) and Lemma 2.1: https://www.ime.unicamp.br/sites/default/files/pesquisa/relatorios/rp-2000-20.pdf . It gives genus at most fifty-one at degree twenty and seventy-seven at degree twenty-four. Riemann--Hurwitz for the faithful degree-twenty-five H quotient then gives quotient genus at most three or four respectively. The induced SL2(F5) action is faithful because automorphisms over the invariant function field are exactly H. This produces the required contradiction.

The application requires invariance of an actual effective cycle, not merely its class. That requirement is met by the top-dimensional cycle of the established equivariant Ext1 singularity sheaf. Combining with the separately audited third-Chern bound excludes c3=10 and 20, leaving c3=0 or 30. No degree-thirty exclusion or rank-eight realization follows. No actual operation or HM instance was evaluated.
