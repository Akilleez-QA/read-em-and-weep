# Pointwise strengthening: residual rank at least five on every canonical conic

## Statement

Under the actual target representation and repeated-input hypotheses used in PROOF.md, every point on every canonical invariant conic has residual middle rank at least five. In particular, these conics are disjoint from Z4, not merely excluded as components.

This strengthening uses the actual target's parity dimensions, derived below from the already established abstract representation identities. It does not evaluate the operation.

## 1. The actual odd target has dimension twelve

Use distinct notation W_h for the historical two-dimensional representation in the identities

    Λ²V = A⊗W_h,    J3=A*⊗Sym³W_h.

These identities, including the spinorial central action on W_h, are established in `../full-hm-image-symmetry/PROOF.md` and `../cubic-massey-module-reduction/PROOF.md`. The canonical central involution z acts positively on the three-dimensional T⊂V and negatively on the two-dimensional W⊂V. Hence Λ²V has positive dimension

    dim Λ²T + dim Λ²W = 3+1=4

and negative dimension dim(T⊗W)=6. Since z acts negatively on W_h, the identity Λ²V=A⊗W_h gives

    dim A+ = 3,    dim A− = 2.

The four-dimensional Sym³W_h also has negative central action. Therefore

    dim J3,− = 4 dim A*+ = 12.

All G-linearizations here agree with the induced one on the cubic operation: G is perfect, so it has no scalar character allowing a different twist. The target is generated by operation values because the nonzero operation is normalizer-equivariant and J3 is irreducible.

## 2. Odd rank three at one conic point would force it everywhere

The existing canonical-plane addendum proves odd rank at least three at every point of C. Suppose its rank is three at one point p. Every four-minor of the odd block is a homogeneous quartic on P(T); on C≅P1 it restricts to a section of O(8).

The G-orbit of p has at least twelve distinct points: the effective A5 action on C is faithful and its point stabilizers are cyclic of order at most five. Equivariance makes every four-minor vanish at all those orbit points. A section of O(8) with at least twelve zeros is zero. Thus the odd block has rank at most three everywhere on C, and the existing lower bound makes it constant rank three.

Sections 1–2 of PROOF.md apply using only this odd constant-rank hypothesis, irreducibility of E=W⊗T, and cyclic zero. They prove that the span of all odd operation coefficients has dimension at most eight. Since the operation generates J3, this contradicts dim J3,−=12.

Therefore the odd rank is at least four at every point of C. The existing canonical-plane addendum gives even rank at least five there. The parity images are complementary, so

    rank b ≥ 9,    rank Q = rank b - 4 ≥ 5.

Normalizer equivariance propagates this bound to every canonical conic. The total degree of their union is fifty, but no point of that union belongs to Z4.

## Scope

This pointwise strengthening uses the actual twenty-dimensional target representation, not merely the greater-than-sixteen image-dimension hypothesis sufficient for PROOF.md. It does not address other rank-four components or isolated points and does not improve ranks away from the canonical conics.
