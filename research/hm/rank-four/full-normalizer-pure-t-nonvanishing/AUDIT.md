# Independent audit: the pure-T channel is nonzero

## Verdict

The proof in PROOF.md is correct under its stated normalizer-equivariance and actual-target identification. It proves alpha is nonzero without evaluating the actual operation, a source coefficient, or a rank instance. In particular the quaternion coupling theorem excludes residual rank five on O375. No substantive algebraic or normalization gap was found.

The audit used only static local proof reads and exact universal tensor/finite-group reasoning. No actual source-instance evaluation is claimed.

## 1. Universal Schur realization and the actual quotient

Over C, the wedge map V tensor Lambda²V to Lambda³V is surjective and split, with kernel U of dimension 5·10−10=40. The map

    F(a,b,c)=a tensor (b wedge c)+c tensor (b wedge a)

lands in this kernel, is symmetric in its first and third inputs, and has zero cyclic sum. Its span is all U. One direct verification of spanning is multidegree by multidegree: a repeated-index component is generated by F(a,a,b)=a tensor (a wedge b); on three distinct indices the kernel is the two-dimensional plane of the single wedge relation, and two F-values span it. These are exactly the outer-symmetric cyclic-zero relations: for each multiset of three distinct indices they leave two dimensions, for a multiset with precisely two equal indices one dimension, and for three equal indices zero. Thus any trilinear f with the stated symmetries factors uniquely through F.

The nonzero N-map q:U to J is surjective because J is the specified irreducible twenty-dimensional N-module. Its kernel therefore has dimension twenty. It matters that this is the actual cubic target J=A* tensor Sym³W (as established in cubic-massey-module-reduction), rather than an unidentified abstract twenty-dimensional representation.

## 2. Reflection class, phases, and centrality

With X e_i=e_(i+1), Z e_i=zeta^i e_i, and R e_i=e_(-i), direct composition gives

    X^a Z^b R Z^(-b) X^(-a)e_i
      =zeta^(-2b(i−a)) e_(2a−i).

There are exactly twenty-five such conjugates: the centralizer of R in H5 is its center, since R acts by negation on H5/Z(H5), and 2 is invertible in F5. The complement K fixes R under conjugation and normalizes H5. Therefore the displayed full H5-conjugacy set is stable under both H5 and K. Its sum C is central in C[N]. No projective scalar is being discarded: the operators are the honest normalized lifts from canonical-line-parity-exclusion.

On a cubic tensor the phase is zeta^(-2b(i+j+k−3a)). Its sum over b is five exactly when i+j+k=3a and zero otherwise. Since 3 is invertible in F5, precisely one a survives. This proves C=5L with the precise factor five, not twenty-five and not its negative.

L preserves the sum of the three indices, and reflects each about a=(i+j+k)/3. Thus L²=1. Exterior orientations produce the usual signs, but squaring restores those also. The basis tensors with j<k are indeed permuted up to sign: reflection never makes unequal j,k equal. U is stable since each summand of C is a group action commuting with wedge.

## 3. Spectrum and identification of the kernel

R has three positive and two negative eigenvalues on V. Consequently its traces on V, Lambda²V, Lambda³V are respectively 1, −2, −2. The split exact wedge sequence gives trace_U(R)=0; conjugacy then gives trace_U(C)=0. Together with C²=25I and dim U=40, this gives multiplicities twenty for both eigenvalues +5 and −5.

For the actual J, trace_J(R)=8−12=−4, so Schur's lemma gives C|J=25(−4)/20=−5. Hence U_(+5) is contained in ker q, and their equal dimension gives equality. Equivalently q(t)=0 if and only if L(t)=t.

There is a further independent way to check the target sign from the recorded representation identities. In Lambda²V=A tensor W the complement involution is −I on the two-dimensional W. Since trace_R(Lambda²V)=−2, trace_R(A)=1; an involution has the same trace on its dual A*. On S=Sym³W it acts as −I, so trace_R(J)=trace_R(A*)·trace_R(S)=1·(−4)=−4. This verifies that cubic dualization has not accidentally reversed the reflection sign. It also recovers the 8/12 parity dimensions from dimension twenty.

No decomposition of W' tensor W, nor its irreducibility, is needed for this spectral argument.

## 4. Metric contraction is precisely the pure-T summand

The basis of the positive eigenspace is e0,s1=e1+e4,s2=e2+e3. D acts on these with weights 1,zeta,zeta^4. Hence any invariant symmetric inverse-metric tensor is

    Q=A e0 tensor e0+B(s1 tensor s2+s2 tensor s1).

All other symmetric entries have nontrivial D-weight. Nondegeneracy gives A≠0 and B≠0: the determinant of its coefficient matrix is −AB². This requires no Fourier normalization or numerical value of A/B.

Invariant contraction in the outer slots of F defines a K-map i:T to U. The contraction with v=e0 is exactly

    i(e0)=2B[s1 tensor (e0 wedge s2)+s2 tensor (e0 wedge s1)].

The factor two comes from the two terms of F and the symmetric entries of Q; the A-term vanishes. This vector is nonzero, so the map is injective by irreducibility of T. It lies in the Schur module of T. Since that module is T plus P5, with multiplicity one, this is exactly the pure-T T-summand. For completeness this decomposition follows from Lambda²T≅T, T tensor T=1 plus T plus P5, and Lambda³T=1.

## 5. Single universal coefficient detects the negative eigenspace

Use the ambient basis e_i tensor (e_j wedge e_k), j<k. The coordinate of i(e0) at e0 tensor (e1 wedge e2) is zero, since its first factors lie in span(s1,s2).

The target ordered triple (0,1,2) has mean 1 in F5. Its unique L-preimage is (2,1,0); in increasing wedge order this is minus e2 tensor (e0 wedge e1). The latter vector has coefficient 2B in i(e0). Therefore the target coordinate in L(i(e0)) is −2B, nonzero. There is no second preimage that could cancel it because L is an involutive signed permutation of the ambient basis.

It follows that L(i(e0)) differs from i(e0), hence q(i(e0)) is nonzero. Since q is K-equivariant and i(T) is positive under R, its image is the unique T in J+=T plus P5. This is precisely alpha≠0.

## 6. Scope of the rank consequence

The independent quaternion coupling argument gives even rank at least six and odd rank at least four once alpha≠0. Thus the total middle rank on the quaternion-axis orbit is at least ten, and the residual rank is at least six after subtracting four. This excludes O375 from residual rank five.
