## 1. Honest group action and representation identities

The inverse image of the projective stabilizer in SL(V) is a finite central extension by mu_5. For d(g)=det(g|W), d^5 descends to a character of the perfect quotient and therefore equals 1. The normalization rho(g)=d(g)^2 g is multiplicative, kills scalar c because (c^2)^2 c=c^5, and has determinant d^5=1 on W. The normalized central involution is exactly the trace-one reflection R, so its signs on W and T are as claimed.

The target construction is legitimate: replace J by the span of tensor values, identify it with the quotient of the cubic tensor space by the invariant kernel of f, and use the normalized action on that quotient. Projective equivariance gives kernel invariance, which is all this construction needs. It neither assumes an irreducible J nor imports a guessed target decomposition. The quotient action makes f exactly equivariant.

The prior no-invariant-line argument and complete reducibility prove irreducibility of both W and T. Sym^2 W is an irreducible A_5 three-space. For the stated D, its eigenvalues on W are zeta and zeta^4, with determinant 1, and its eigenvalues on T are 1, zeta, zeta^4. Thus the trace comparison correctly distinguishes T from T'=Sym^2 W. The displayed A_5 character products, Sym^2 T=1+P_5, and W tensor W tensor T=T+P_4+P_5 all check.

The endomorphism calculation proves W tensor T is irreducible: End(W)=1+T' and End(T)=1+T+P_5 have precisely one common irreducible constituent. Likewise Hom(W tensor Sym^2 T,W) has dimension one. Its contraction form q is nondegenerate by irreducibility of T.

## 2. Constant pencils and the even block

The parity decomposition has source dimensions nine and six. A generic-rank r minor has degree r, so any nonempty finite rank-drop set has at most r distinct points. An invariant nonempty set has at least twelve points; both blocks therefore have constant rank. Rank-zero blocks are correctly treated separately.

For a constant-rank pencil, the image is simultaneously a quotient of O(-1)^n and a subbundle of a trivial bundle. Global generation of I(1) and I* forces every splitting degree to be -1 or 0. This proves the claimed splitting without any equivariant splitting assumption.

If the even block has rank three, repeated-input injectivity supplies its rank-three O(-2) tensor T injection. For its rank-three kernel K, H^0(K(1)) is a proper invariant subspace of the irreducible W tensor T, hence zero. Therefore deg K is at most -6; the identity deg K=-6+a forces a=0. Its image is a fixed three-space H. Because H is generated by coefficients, it is a quotient of W tensor W tensor T, and the verified decomposition forces H to be T. The induced map Sym^2 W tensor T to H is then zero by the character products. This contradicts repeated-input injectivity and proves rank at least four.

## 3. Odd block

Outer symmetry and cyclic zero eliminate Sym^3 W and leave exactly S_(2,1)W, isomorphic to W. Repeated-input injectivity makes its map nonzero. The displayed determinant formula verifies that every middle u in W has the entire resulting two-space H in its image.

If the odd block has rank at most three, its constant-section space has dimension two or three. Dimension three is impossible because the central involution acts as minus the identity, giving determinant -1, whereas the group is perfect. Thus its constant sections are precisely H. Its quotient by H is zero or O(-1).

The quotient embeds in an invariant complement J' of H. Uniqueness of the line-bundle linearization follows because two such linearizations differ by a character and the group is perfect. Consequently the inclusion corresponds to a map W to J', producing another copy of W when nonzero. All T,W,T values consequently land in at most two copies of W. The one-dimensional Hom calculation then forces f(t,u,t)=q(t)h(u). A nonzero isotropic t exists over C, and cyclic zero converts this vanishing into f(t,t,u)=0, contradicting repeated-input injectivity since W and T intersect trivially. This establishes the second rank-at-least-four bound.
