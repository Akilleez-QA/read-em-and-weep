# Canonical conics have residual rank at least six pointwise

## Theorem and imported hypotheses

For the actual operation and representation conventions of
`../rank-four-canonical-conic-exclusion/POINTWISE_ADDENDUM.md`, every point on every canonical invariant conic has

    rank b_odd ≥ 5,
    rank b_even ≥ 5,
    rank Q ≥ 6.

In particular, the union of the twenty-five canonical conics is disjoint from Z5.

The proof uses these already established inputs:

- G=SL2(F5) acts honestly, V=T⊕W_v with dimensions three and two, and its center has signs + on T and − on W_v.
- C⊂P(T) is the canonical smooth G-invariant conic. On C≅P1, the effective group is A5 and every point orbit has at least twelve points.
- E=W_v⊗T is an irreducible six-dimensional G-representation; see `../canonical-line-parity-exclusion/PROOF.md`, Section 2.
- The actual target J is generated by the values of the operation and dim J−=12; see the cited pointwise addendum. This is the actual induced target action, not an unidentified projective twist.
- The operation is outer-symmetric and cyclic-zero. Its pure-W_v cubic coefficient image has dimension at most two, since it factors through S_(2,1)W_v.
- On C the odd block has rank at least four and the even block has rank at least five everywhere, by the cited pointwise addendum and its dependencies.
- rank Q=rank b−4.

The notation W_v denotes the negative eigenspace in V. No identification with the historical spinorial representation W_h used to present J is made or needed.

## 1. A single odd rank-four point would imply constant rank four

Assume the odd block has rank four at p∈C. For each five-minor, restriction to C is a section of O_P1(10): matrix entries are linear on P(T), and O_P(T)(1)|C=O_P1(2).

Equivariance implies rank four at every point of the G-orbit of p. Hence every five-minor vanishes at at least twelve distinct points. A section of O(10) with that many distinct zeros is identically zero. All five-minors therefore vanish throughout C, and the existing lower bound makes the odd rank identically four.

## 2. Bundle bounds are forced to equality

Let I be the image bundle of this constant-rank-four map:

    O_C(-2)⊗E → J−⊗O_C.

Constant rank makes I both a quotient bundle of the source and a subbundle of the target. Thus I(2) and I* are globally generated. Write its Birkhoff–Grothendieck splitting as

    I = ⊕_(i=1)^4 O(-d_i),     0≤d_i≤2,
    s = Σ_i d_i.

The induced G-map E→H⁰(I(2)) is nonzero, and therefore injective by irreducibility of E. Consequently

    6 ≤ h⁰(I(2)) = 12−s,
    s ≤ 6.

Let M⊂J− be the fixed vector space spanned by the image fibers over C. Restriction of linear functionals gives a map J−*→H⁰(I*) whose kernel is exactly M's annihilator. It follows that

    dim M ≤ h⁰(I*) = 4+s ≤ 10.

On the other hand M is exactly the span of f(W_v,T,T). Indeed the fibers are the spans of f(w,u,t) with [u]∈C, and C spans T. Outer symmetry and cyclic zero express every placement with one W_v input and two T inputs in this same span; explicitly,

    f(t1,w,t2) = −f(w,t2,t1)−f(w,t1,t2).

The only other odd coefficients are those of f|_(W_v)^3, spanning a space H of dimension at most two. Since all operation values generate J,

    J− = M+H,
    12 = dim J− ≤ dim M+2 ≤ 6+s.

Together with s≤6, this forces

    s=6,   dim M=10,   h⁰(I(2))=6.

In particular the injection E→H⁰(I(2)) is an isomorphism of G-representations. Hence H⁰(I(2)) must be irreducible.

## 3. The forced splitting contradicts irreducibility

Four integers d_i∈{0,1,2} summing to six have, up to permutation, only two possibilities:

    (2,2,2,0),   (2,2,1,1).

Thus F=I(2) has one of the splittings

    F ≅ O(2) ⊕ O^3,
    F ≅ O(1)^2 ⊕ O^2.

In either case F has an intrinsic maximal-degree subbundle F_max: it is the first nonzero term of the Harder–Narasimhan filtration. In the two cases it is respectively O(2) or O(1)^2. This subbundle is preserved by every equivariant bundle isomorphism covering a G-action on P1: degrees, slopes, and the unique maximal-slope subbundle are preserved under automorphisms of the base. Therefore H⁰(F_max) is a G-invariant subspace of H⁰(F).

Its dimension is respectively three or four, whereas h⁰(F)=6. It is a nonzero proper invariant subspace. This contradicts H⁰(F)≅E and the irreducibility of E.

This argument needs neither an equivariant choice of a Birkhoff–Grothendieck splitting nor a classification of its multiplicity representations. Only the canonical maximal-degree subbundle is used.

## 4. Pointwise conclusion

The contradiction excludes odd rank four at any point of C. The existing odd rank lower bound then gives odd rank at least five everywhere on C. Together with the even rank lower bound,

    rank b ≥ 5+5=10,
    rank Q ≥ 6.

Normalizer equivariance propagates this to all twenty-five canonical conics.

## Scope and checks

This is a new pointwise exclusion of Z5 on those conics, stronger than excluding them as components. It does not establish the absence of other Z5 points or higher-degree components, nor does it identify the rank at any selected point. It uses the actual twelve-dimensional odd target and its generation by operation values. For arbitrary weaker abstract target hypotheses the conclusion is not asserted.

No source tensor, source coefficient, operation value, selected input, actual-operation matrix, rank instance, jet, or residue has been evaluated.
