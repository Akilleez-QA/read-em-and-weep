# Independent audit: pointwise rank-five canonical-conic exclusion

## Verdict

**PASS, conditional on the explicitly imported actual-target, equivariance, repeated-input-injectivity, and earlier pointwise rank hypotheses. No logical gap found in the new argument.**

The proof establishes that every point of each of the twenty-five canonical conics has odd middle-block rank at least five and total residual rank at least six. Consequently these conics are disjoint from Z5. It does not establish that Z5 is empty elsewhere.

## 1. Actual representation and notation dependencies

The argument needs the actual odd target to have dimension twelve and to be generated by operation coefficients. These are stronger assumptions than a generic abstract target or a bound on total image dimension.

The imported identities are Λ²V=A⊗W_h and J=A*⊗Sym³W_h, where W_h is the historical two-dimensional spinorial representation. For the canonical central involution, V=T⊕W_v has signs positive on T and negative on W_v. Thus Λ²V has positive dimension four and negative dimension six. Since W_h has negative sign and dimension two, A has positive dimension three and negative dimension two. The four-dimensional Sym³W_h also has negative sign, so J has negative dimension 3·4=12 and positive dimension 2·4=8. Dualization preserves the two central signs.

The target action must be the induced honest action compatible with the operation. The cited proofs supply this compatibility; the normalizer irreducibility of J, equivariance, and nonvanishing supplied by repeated-input injectivity give generation of all of J. Target irreducibility alone would give only a zero-or-full-image alternative. This nonvanishing dependence is properly retained among the inherited hypotheses.

There is no identification of W_h with W_v in the new proof. The irreducible source E is specifically W_v⊗T. The prior representation argument proves its irreducibility using End(W_v)=1⊕T', End(T)=1⊕T⊕P5, and T' not isomorphic to T. Their only common constituent is the trivial representation, so dim End_G(E)=1. Complete reducibility over C implies E is irreducible of dimension six. In particular, one must not replace T by Sym²W_v; the supplied proof does not make that mistake.

## 2. From one rank-four point to constant rank four

On the canonical conic C≅P¹, the odd block is the restriction of a matrix linear in the coordinates on P(T). A five-minor therefore restricts to a section of O_P¹(10), not O(5), because the plane hyperplane bundle has degree two on C.

The faithful effective A5 action on C has point stabilizers cyclic of order at most five, hence every orbit has at least twelve distinct points. Equivariance preserves rank. If rank is four at one point, every five-minor vanishes at all the points in its orbit. Each such section has more than ten distinct zeros, so it vanishes identically. This works minor by minor; no assertion that an individual minor is invariant is needed.

The imported lower bound odd rank at least four then makes the odd rank exactly four everywhere. This lower bound is essential for introducing the ordinary image subbundle without a saturation correction. Under constant rank, both the image and the quotient by the image in the target are locally free. Equivariance of the map gives a canonical equivariant structure on its image I.

## 3. Bundle estimates and their directions

On P¹ the map is O(-2)⊗E→I→J−⊗O, with the first arrow surjective and the second a subbundle inclusion. Hence I(2) is globally generated, and dualizing the second arrow makes I* globally generated. Birkhoff–Grothendieck therefore gives

    I≅⊕_(i=1)^4 O(-d_i), with 0≤d_i≤2.

These are statements about the ordinary splitting; no equivariant splitting is assumed. Put s=Σd_i. Twisting by the dual of the restricted tautological line cancels the source line, so the map on global sections is genuinely an equivariant map E→H⁰(I(2)). It is nonzero because the bundle surjection is nonzero. Equivalently, morphisms O⊗E→I(2) are exactly linear maps E→H⁰(I(2)). Irreducibility gives injectivity, without requiring surjectivity on global sections at this stage. Consequently

    6≤h⁰(I(2))=Σ(3-d_i)=12-s,
    s≤6.

If M is the span in J− of all fibers of I, the map J−*→H⁰(I*) has kernel exactly the annihilator of M: a constant linear functional restricts to the zero section precisely when it annihilates every image fiber. Its image therefore has dimension dim M. It need not be surjective, but this yields the required inequality

    dim M≤h⁰(I*)=Σ(d_i+1)=4+s≤10.

No inequality is reversed, and no unjustified section-lifting assertion is used.

## 4. The coefficient-span lower bound covers every odd input type

The fibers of I are the spans of f(w,u,t) with w∈W_v, t∈T, and [u]∈C. The conic spans T, so trilinearity makes their total span exactly the span of f(W_v,T,T). This is an equality of coefficient spans, not an assertion that each individual image fiber contains all mixed coefficients.

Outer symmetry identifies f(T,T,W_v) with this same span. Cyclic zero together with outer symmetry gives

    f(t1,w,t2)=−f(w,t2,t1)−f(w,t1,t2),

so the remaining placement f(T,W_v,T) also lies in M. No symmetry of the two T inputs is silently assumed.

By central parity, an odd output coefficient has either one or three W_v inputs. The three-W_v part factors through the outer-symmetric cyclic-zero quotient S_(2,1)W_v, of dimension two. Call its image H. Thus all odd coefficients span M+H, and generation of the full actual target gives

    J−=M+H,  dim H≤2,
    12≤dim M+2≤6+s.

Potential intersection between M and H cannot invalidate this upper bound. Together with s≤6 it forces s=6, dim M=10, dim H=2, and M∩H=0. Only the first two consequences are needed. In particular h⁰(I(2))=6, so the already proved injection E→H⁰(I(2)) is now an equivariant isomorphism.

## 5. The equality case and the canonical destabilizing subbundle

Four integers between zero and two with sum six have exactly the unordered possibilities (2,2,2,0) and (2,2,1,1). Equivalently, their deficits from two are either (2,0,0,0) or (1,1,0,0). Thus F=I(2) has splitting

    O(2)⊕O³, or O(1)²⊕O².

The first nonzero term F_max of its Harder–Narasimhan filtration is respectively O(2) or O(1)². This subbundle is intrinsic even though a full Birkhoff–Grothendieck splitting is not canonical. For every g∈G, pullback by the base automorphism preserves degrees, ranks, and slopes, so it takes the Harder–Narasimhan filtration to that of g*F. The equivariant isomorphism g*F≅F therefore identifies the first terms by uniqueness. This establishes invariance of F_max under the full action covering base automorphisms, not merely under automorphisms over the identity.

Global sections are left exact, so H⁰(F_max) injects equivariantly into H⁰(F). Its dimension is three in the first splitting and four in the second, while h⁰(F)=6. It is a nonzero proper G-invariant subspace, contradicting H⁰(F)≅E with E irreducible. There is no need to choose equivariant summands, to classify their multiplicity representations, or to identify the binary representation parametrizing the conic with W_v.

## 6. Conclusion and precise scope

The contradiction rules out odd rank four at every conic point. The imported pointwise lower bound four therefore strengthens to five. The separately imported even bound five concerns the same middle points, and the two target parity eigenspaces are complementary. Hence rank b≥10 and the standing identity rank Q=rank b−4 gives rank Q≥6. Normalizer equivariance transports the conclusion to the other twenty-four canonical conics.

The new exclusion does not depend on the conic-component classification's global degree budget or on the assertion that a conic component must be canonical: it directly treats the canonical conics, pointwise. The classification can be combined with it only in settings where that classification's own hypotheses and degree bound apply. No assertion about off-conic points, other curves, isolated Z5 points, or determinantal scheme structure follows from this proof alone.

**Gaps found: none in the new argument.** The material dependencies are the actual target and induced-action identities, nonvanishing/generation, source irreducibility, and the previously established pointwise odd/even lower bounds. They remain explicit conditional inputs rather than being reproved by a source evaluation.
