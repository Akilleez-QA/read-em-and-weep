# Root review and public-source check

The finite-thickening induction and the dimension contradiction were independently checked against the final proof interfaces. The global annihilator incidence is the zero locus of a rank-twelve vector-bundle section on the smooth thirteen-dimensional product. This provides a genuine global error bundle at each finite step; no global nonlinear Schur residual is assumed.

The square-zero lifting ideal is O(5n). Both the ambient-map gluing obstruction H1(TY|L tensor O(5n)) and the correction obstruction H1(ker(ds) tensor O(5n)) vanish. Equivariant lifts and corrections exist by averaging affine torsors and linear solutions over the finite stabilizer. The residual class therefore lies in the claimed invariant H0(O(5n+1)), for the chosen preceding embedding. Perfectness fixes the line-bundle linearizations, and the binary action has only projective orbit sizes 12,20,30,60. These facts rigorously exclude orders two through six; order seven is allowed but not evaluated.

The finite source remains a closed subscheme: its first tangent map is injective, yielding a surjection of completed local rings; properness and finite fibers give a finite map, for which completion detects the closed-immersion assertion. The rank-nine pivot transfers the embedding to the actual degeneracy scheme. The resulting generic length-seven quotient is sufficient for the degree contradiction; an infinite formal solution or convergence is not needed.

For the hypothetical dimension-one locus, the transpose has ranks ten and twelve and expected maximal-minor codimension three. Its natural cycle is c3(E*)=105H^3. The twenty-five distinct line components would each have multiplicity at least seven, forcing degree at least175. The contradiction, together with the established upper bound two, proves dimension exactly two. The 105 is not the actual surface degree.
