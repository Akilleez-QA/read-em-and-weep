The proof correctly uses the global annihilator incidence as the zero scheme of a rank-twelve bundle section on a smooth thirteen-dimensional product. Its induction accounts for both gluing of ambient maps and correction of section errors. In particular, vanishing of H1(TY|L(5n)) alone would not suffice; the additional H1(T0(5n)) vanishing in the proof is essential and is valid for n>=1. Finite-group averaging acts on affine lift torsors and on the affine fibers of the correction map, so equivariance follows in characteristic zero without averaging nonlinear maps themselves.

The obstruction is independent of the ambient lift with the previous embedding fixed. The document correctly does not claim independence from changing that previous embedding. The center excludes n=2,4,6; the A5 orbit divisor semigroup excludes n=3,5. Thus the sixth thickening exists. The order-seven invariant space is indeed spanned by F12 cubed; no assertion about its actual scalar follows.

For the closed-immersion step, one may expand the stated argument as follows. The source is proper and the reduced map is an embedding, so the map to the separated ambient variety is proper with finite fibers and hence finite. At each closed point the map on completed local rings is surjective: it is surjective on residue fields and cotangent spaces, and the target complete local ring is generated by lifts of a cotangent basis. The finite cokernel therefore vanishes by faithful flatness of completion. The resulting sheaf map is surjective, proving a closed immersion. The rank-nine pivot gives a scheme isomorphism between the annihilator incidence and D on the open set of rank at least nine. All of the thickening lies over that open set, since its underlying support is L.

The local elimination argument is also valid. Eliminate eleven independent derivatives of the twelve section components; the completed local incidence becomes C[[t,z]]/(h). The closed embedding of the sixth thickening induces a surjection C[[t,z]] onto C[[u,v]]/(v^7) with invertible linear part. Lift the images of t,z to C[[u,v]]; the formal inverse-function theorem makes this lift an automorphism. Consequently the kernel is generated by a seventh power of a smooth parameter, and h belongs to that ideal. No pre-existing smooth surface or global Schur equation is assumed.

## Further theorem: the actual rank-drop scheme has dimension two

Let D be the maximal-minor degeneracy scheme of e:E -> 10O on P4, where E=3Q(-1). Suppose, toward contradiction, that dim D<=1. The twenty-five lines already in D show that dim D=1. Thus the maximal-minor ideal has expected codimension three. Since P4 is regular, its grade is three. The maximal-minor perfection theorem (Eagon--Northcott) gives a Cohen--Macaulay determinantal curve, and the expected-codimension Thom--Porteous formula applies to its fundamental cycle.

For H the hyperplane class, the twisted tautological sequence gives

c(E)=(1-H)^15/(1-2H)^3=1-9H+39H^2-105H^3+terms of degree four.

Thus c(10O-E)=1+9H+42H^2+132H^3+terms of degree four. The expected-codimension maximal-minor class is

[D]=Delta_1^3(c(10O-E))=(9^3-2*9*42+132)H^3=105H^3.

Equivalently, transpose the map and use c3(E*)=105H^3. In particular degree D=105.

For each of the twenty-five distinct lines L_i, the sixth thickening closed immersion gives a local surjection at its generic point

O_{D,eta_i} -> C(L_i)[v]/(v^7).

Under the curve hypothesis the source is Artinian, so its length is at least seven. Each line is an irreducible component under this hypothesis. The fundamental one-cycle therefore has degree at least 25*7=175. All remaining components contribute nonnegative degree. This contradicts degree D=105.

Consequently dim D>=2. The independently established upper bound dim D<=2 in PR555, commit ff5a3164337016e96e3a29a9ad37891130a66e29, then yields dim D=2. If that upper bound is not imported, the unconditional consequence here is only dim D>=2.

This argument proves existence of a surface component, not that every exceptional line lies on a surface component, and not reducedness, normality, smoothness, or algebraicity of the original target. It does not evaluate any actual selected matrix or coefficient. The degree 105 is used only under the contradicted expected-codimension curve hypothesis; it must not be reported as the degree of the actual excess-dimensional D.
