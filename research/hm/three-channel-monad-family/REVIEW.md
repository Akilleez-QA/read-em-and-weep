The exterior Euler identification uses H as coordinate functions and P(H*) as projective space. Accordingly, evaluation of a coefficient three-form is contraction by x in H*. This verifies the positive-plane and negative-line restrictions with the stated channel conventions. The three equivariant channels are exhaustive. The c=0 and b=0 exclusions follow immediately from source kernels; when a=0, the b-channel contracts to the unique nonzero map V* to sl(W′). Its determinant has a nonempty projective zero locus, which contradicts fiber injectivity without choosing or evaluating an actual instance.

The source-gauge chain isomorphism has components g^(-1), identity, and g^t, consistently with the adjoint right arrow. The block weights (λ³, λμ², λ²μ) follow from the three exterior summands. The explicit choices λ=1, μ²=(b′/a′)/(b/a), s=a′/a and r=c′/(μc) do send one arbitrary nonzero triple to another. Keeping the determinant-valued pairing makes this a transformation of the full self-dual monad, not merely its left arrow.

There is no circular appeal to universal isotropy: the existence of the actual subbundle monad, the structural exclusion of all coordinate hyperplanes, and torus transitivity first establish isotropy and subbundle exactness on the entire nonzero torus. Polynomial density then establishes isotropy on the boundary, but not fiber injectivity there. The latter distinction is explicitly and correctly retained. The rank-two middle quotient is locally free because the rank-five isotropic source is a subbundle of the kernel of the surjective adjoint.

The concluding a/b transformation is a source-gauge invariant, not an invariant under arbitrary coordinate pullbacks. The proof correctly reserves bundle-isomorphism separation for the separate strong-exceptional reconstruction argument. It does not by itself establish nonvanishing of κ or of the positive cubic obstruction.

This review used symbolic representation, contraction, and bundle arguments. No selected actual HM coefficients, fibers, matrices, ranks, or point evaluations were used; no repository computation was run. No external publication was performed by the reviewer.
