# A sharp sextic-channel apolar codimension obstruction

## Statement and scope

Work over C with G=SL2(F5), its defining two-dimensional representation W, and the unique nonstandard three-dimensional summand V in Sym6 W. Fix the determinant self-duality of W and its induced invariant apolar forms. Let d>=2, n=d+6, and k=d-1=n-7.

Let H be a finite-dimensional G-representation of dimension M. Let F be an equivariant saturated subbundle of H tensor O on P1, with all ordinary splitting degrees strictly negative. Put S=(H tensor O/F)* inside H* tensor O, and assume H0(S)=0. Suppose:

1. There is an equivariant saturated line C=O(-n) inside S, with the standard linearization.
2. The actual osculating directions of C through order k, formed using the constant connection on H* tensor O, lie generically in S. This is an additional geometric hypothesis, not a consequence of degrees or a chosen bundle splitting.
3. As an equivariant bundle F=F_high direct-sum V O(-d), where every ordinary splitting degree of F_high is greater than -d. This splitting is not assumed horizontal.

Then its actual coefficient map Sym^n W -> H* is injective, and

    codim(F,H tensor O) >= n-2.

If F_high is nonzero, the stronger inequality holds:

    codim(F,H tensor O) >= n-1.                     (1)

Both bounds are sharp for abstract equivariant saturated polynomial inclusions satisfying the stated hypotheses. Sharpness is not an assertion of actual HM realization. No selected actual source coefficient, point, parameter, matrix, jet or instance rank is computed.

No parity restriction on n is needed for arbitrary H. If the center acts trivially on H, existence of C forces n even. The sharp examples below also respect that optional center-even restriction when n is even.

## Public inputs

The balanced-sextic theorem is [PR1147](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/176645bb2f022b68f86414ced87667a45e4930e2/research/hm/balanced-sextic-syzygies/PROOF.md). It proves the exact sequence

    0 -> W' O(-3) -> V O -> O(6) -> 0,

hence multiplication V tensor Sym^d W -> Sym^(d+6) W is onto for every d>=2. Pure dth powers span Sym^d W in characteristic zero, so the constant span of l^d V over all lines l is the entire Sym^(d+6) W. Its full proof is retained and immutable-pinned in INPUTS.json. The actual partial-system method used in [PR1145](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/2e2da6c1226b7671c79e55b3c9f8bb14a0f8719b/research/hm/profile11-apolar-exclusion/PROOF.md) is retained as application context, not as a general profile-coverage claim.

## 1. Allow a partial coefficient system first

Write q:Sym^n W -> R subset H* for the coefficient map of the actual line C, onto its constant image R. A priori q can have a kernel. The inclusion of C is nonzero, so R is nonzero. Duality gives an injection

    R* -> (Sym^n W)*.

Compose the actual constant projection H -> R* with F -> H tensor O and this dual injection. This gives

    a:F -> (Sym^n W)* tensor O.                    (2)

The universal kth osculating fibers of the full degree-n line are l^(n-k) Sym^k W=l^7 Sym^(d-1) W. Their images under q are the actual osculating directions of C. This remains true for a partial or ramified coefficient quotient: differentiation commutes with the constant map q, and no independence or completeness is presumed. Assumption 2 therefore says that (2) annihilates these universal fibers generically. Its pairing with the universal osculating bundle is a bundle morphism and vanishes identically once it vanishes generically.

Under apolarity the annihilator of l^7 Sym^(d-1) W in Sym^(d+6) W is l^d Sym6 W. Indeed at a line generated by x, the first space is spanned by monomials with x-exponent at least seven. Its apolar orthogonal is spanned by monomials with x-exponent at least d; the dimensions are complementary. Equivariance under SL2 moves this calculation to every line. Thus the universal annihilator is the saturated subbundle

    Sym6 W tensor O(-d) -> (Sym^n W)* tensor O,

embedded by multiplication by l^d. Consequently a factors through this bundle, not through a fictitious full polynomial system inside R.

## 2. The sextic channel forces completeness

Every map F_high -> Sym6 W O(-d) is zero by ordinary degree: all source degrees exceed -d. On V O(-d) the equivariant map into Sym6 W O(-d) is scalar on the unique V summand of Sym6 W=V+P. This scalar is nonzero. Otherwise (2) is zero; because R* -> (Sym^n W)* is injective, F projects to zero in R* tensor O. This would put the nonzero constant R tensor O inside S, contrary to H0(S)=0.

The scalar channel is therefore the multiplication embedding l^d V on every fiber. Since (2) factors through R* tensor O, the constant R* contains l^d V for every l. The balanced-sextic span theorem makes their span all of (Sym^n W)*. Hence dim R=n+1 and q is an isomorphism onto its image. This proves completeness rather than assuming it.

## 3. Rank and saturation give the bounds

The constant projection H -> R* now has kernel N of dimension M-(n+1). The actual embedded F_high lies in N tensor O. If r=rank(F_high), rank alone gives

    r <= M-(n+1).

Since rank(F)=r+3, this is exactly codim(F)>=n-2.

For r>0 equality is impossible. F_high is a direct summand as a bundle of F, and F is saturated in H tensor O. Thus H tensor O/F_high is locally free. The quotient N tensor O/F_high injects into it, so it is torsion-free and hence locally free on P1. If r=dim N, this quotient has rank zero and is zero. Therefore F_high=N tensor O, contradicting the strictly negative degrees of the nonzero F_high. Hence r<dim N and the integer bound improves to codim(F)>=n-1.

This improvement uses actual saturation, not just a determinant degree inequality. It does not require the displayed abstract decomposition of F to commute with differentiation.

## 4. Sharp abstract models

For every d>=2 put n=d+6, H0=Sym^n W, and let

    F0=V O(-d) -> H0 tensor O

be the universal multiplication inclusion with fiber l^d V. Multiplication by a nonzero polynomial is injective, so this map has constant fiber rank three; it is a saturated subbundle. Its coefficient span is all H0 by the balanced-sextic theorem, so H0((H0 tensor O/F0)*)=0. The complete rational-normal line C=O(-n) with fiber l^n and its derivatives through order k=d-1 lie in the annihilator: l^7 Sym^(d-1) is apolar-orthogonal to l^d Sym6, which contains l^d V. Thus every hypothesis holds, F_high=0, and codim(F0)=n-2. This realizes the first bound.

For the nonzero-high-block bound, take the direct sum of this model with

    J=W O(-1) -> Sym2 W tensor O,

whose fiber map is multiplication by l. This is fiberwise injective and saturated, and its coefficient span is Sym2 W. Put F=F0 direct-sum J in H=Sym^n W direct-sum Sym2 W. The high block J has degree -1>-d. Both factors span their constant ambients, so the dual quotient still has no constant sections. The same C and its derivatives lie in the first dual summand. Here M=n+4 and rank(F)=5, hence codim(F)=n-1. The second bound is therefore sharp. When n is even, both ambient representations have trivial central action; this also supplies sharp center-even examples.

These are universal model inclusions, not selected actual HM/source instances. They establish sharpness of the stated hypotheses only; extra conditions in an application can give stronger bounds.

## 5. Application boundary

The residual configuration in PR1145 has n=10, d=4, rank(F)=16 and M=21, with a nonzero rank13 high block. Its codimension5 violates even the weak bound8 and the stronger bound9. PR1145 already excludes that exact configuration; this corollary is not counted as another actual shape exclusion. Its new content is a uniform sharp codimension criterion for all d>=2, including the stronger saturation bound and explicit abstract boundary models. Establishing the actual osculating-containment hypotheses for another source remains a separate obligation. No full profile11 closure or original repair follows.
