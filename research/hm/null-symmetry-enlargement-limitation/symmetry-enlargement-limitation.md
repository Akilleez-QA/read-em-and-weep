# The existing D5 symmetry cannot extend to an icosahedral carrier action

20 October 2026. Formal result and existing review record; actual HM/source values are not evaluated here.

## Useful result

The proposed enlargement from the actual binary-projective D5 to A5 cannot act by pullback automorphisms of this fixed HM bundle. This is obstructed already by the canonical untwisted group H²(P4,End H), independently of the six unknown pure coordinates and without a classification of the full HM automorphism group.

Consequently one cannot reduce the six coordinates using an abstract A5 extension of the combined 32-dimensional target representation. An abstract extension would have to respect the canonical constant-coefficient summand, and that summand cannot extend.

## Proof that projective linearization does not evade the test

Suppose a projective-base subgroup G≅A5 contains this actual D5 and preserves the isomorphism class of H. For each g choose an isomorphism φ_g:g*H→H. Stability gives Aut(H)=C*. Thus φ_g composed with g*φ_h differs from φ_(gh) only by a scalar. Conjugation on End H cancels that scalar, giving an honest G action on End H and hence an ordinary two-dimensional complex representation on V. No G-linearization of H itself is required.

The action restricted to the existing D5 is the already calculated one: the two choices of an H-isomorphism for a fixed base element differ by scalar and induce the same End action. Tensoring by a line does not introduce an escape here: on P4 an isomorphism g*H≅H⊗L forces L²=O by determinants, and Pic(P4)=Z has no two-torsion.

Every two-dimensional complex representation of A5 is trivial. An elementary proof avoids character tables. Its kernel is normal, so simplicity makes any nontrivial representation faithful. Its determinant is trivial, since A5 is perfect. Any nonidentity involution in a faithful representation would therefore be a determinant-one involution in GL2, hence −Id. Two distinct involutions would have the same image, contradicting faithfulness. Thus the representation must be trivial.

But R is an involution in D5 and acts on V as −Id, a contradiction. The binary icosahedral cover is no escape: its central element acts trivially on the base and its scalar action on H cancels on End, so the cohomology representation again factors through A5.

## Exact implication for the proposed six-value route

The compact target has the canonical constant summand M0=V, selected by the normalized algebra trace. Any symmetry coming from binary projective transformations and preserving the actual carrier must preserve the unit, trace, and this summand. It cannot mix M0 with the ten- and twenty-dimensional coefficients to conceal its failure to extend. Therefore matching the total D5 character with restrictions of A5 representations does not construct a usable geometric action.

This excludes an icosahedral strengthening of the existing covariance argument for this carrier. It does not show that D5 is the entire binary-projective stabilizer, exclude all larger dihedral groups, exclude changing the carrier, or evaluate any of the six coordinates. In particular it is not a new vanishing theorem.
