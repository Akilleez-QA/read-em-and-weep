# Four distinct geometric lines in the pure-V lift pencil

## Theorem and boundary

Retain the exact quadratic lift basis of PR1031,

    R1(a)=rho_D(a)L_a*,       R2(a)=L_a*rho_P(a),

and the two intrinsic jet-exceptional lines ell5 and ell3 of PR1030.
Under the canonical lift/gate isomorphism of PR1025, their exact equations
in the coefficient pencil R=alpha R1+beta R2 are

    ell5: 2alpha-beta=0,      ell3: 2alpha+beta=0.              (1)

Equivalently they are spanned by R1+2R2 and R1-2R2. The unique generic
rank-three lift line of PR1031 is spanned by R1-R2. It lies on NEITHER
jet-exceptional line. Therefore its nonic base ideal agrees locally with
the fixed target ideal I_Z at every one of the sixteen prescribed axes:
six length-four C5-axis schemes and ten reduced C3 axes. This does not
exclude additional basepoints elsewhere.

The unique line ellDelta whose nonic base scheme meets the square conic
is spanned by R1, with equation beta=0. Thus ell5, ell3, ellDelta and the
rank-three line are FOUR pairwise distinct projective lines. On ellDelta
the scheme-theoretic intersection with the conic is its reduced invariant
twelve-point orbit; the other three lines have no conic basepoints. No
ambient local thickness is inferred from that intersection statement.

Conversely, both jet-exceptional gate lines have generic lift rank four.
Their previously proved length lower bounds, at least46 for ell5 and54
for ell3, are retained from PR1030, not newly claimed exact lengths.
These are universal pencil identifications. No actual gate coefficients,
source occurrence, fourth-Bockstein value, or additional base locus is
selected. In particular rank of the lift is not rank of the original
fourth row.

## 2. Symbolic ideals for the entire two axis orbits

Work in the affine chart p=1, retaining q,t algebraically independent.
This is a coordinate chart, not a selected quadratic direction. The
reduced C5-axis ideal I5 is generated by the four components of
cP(a)=Pi(a^3). The reduced C3-axis ideal I3 is generated by the five
maximal minors of L(a). Their exact radical/global-scheme identifications
are retained through PR1030 and PR1031 from the original universal inputs.

In lexicographic order q>t, their reduced Groebner bases are

    I5:
    q^2 + 5t^3 + t + 2sqrt5,
    q t^2 - q,
    t^4 - (2sqrt5/5)t^3 + (2sqrt5/5)t - 1;

    I3:
    q^2 + (27/2)t^5 + (27sqrt5/10)t^4 + 6t^3
        - 3sqrt5 t^2 - (11/2)t - 57sqrt5/10,
    q t^4 - q,
    t^6 + (2sqrt5/3)t^5 + t^4 - t^2 - (2sqrt5/3)t - 1.

The leading ideals are respectively

    (q^2,q t^2,t^4),       (q^2,q t^4,t^6).

Their affine quotient lengths are6 and10. Those equal the entire known
reduced orbit lengths, so this one chart contains ALL sixteen relevant

Because these ideals are radical and zero-dimensional, their powers are
the intersections of the corresponding powers of their pairwise-comaximal
maximal ideals. Therefore the ell5 condition is exactly componentwise
membership in I5^3, and the ell3 condition is exactly componentwise
membership in I3^2. There is no saturation or symbolic-power shortcut
at this step: ordinary affine powers already impose the desired jets at
every point in the orbit.

## 4. The conic-exceptional line in the same basis

PR1033 characterizes the unique conic-exceptional line intrinsically:
under the formal square-family substitution

    (p,q,t)=(u^2,2uv,v^2),

all four restricted nonic components are divisible by the invariant binary
form f12. The latter has its reduced twelve-point orbit as zero divisor.
In the pinned universal normalization, take the Wronskian of a basis of
ker Pi, homogenized to degree twelve. The result is

    f12(u,v)=-2u^12-(44sqrt5/5)u^10v^2+66u^8v^4
              +(88sqrt5/5)u^6v^6+66u^4v^8
              -(44sqrt5/5)u^2v^10-2v^12.

The verifier derives this normalization from the kernel basis rather than
assuming a new actual invariant coefficient. In this pinned polynomial, every exponent is even and the coefficients
are symmetric under u and v interchange. Therefore the fixed symplectic
square-factor/zero-point conversion satisfies the EXACT equality
f12(v,-u)=f12(u,v), equivalently f12(-v,u)=f12(u,v). This explicit identity,
also checked by the verifier, identifies the same divisor without invoking
an arbitrary coordinate change.

Polynomial division on the whole symbolic chart u=1 gives

    rem_f12(F1(1,2v,v^2))=0,
    [v^10] rem_f12(F2(1,2v,v^2))_0=13539328/45 != 0.

The remainder identities hold componentwise, and homogeneity recovers
full binary divisibility: each original component has degree eighteen,
f12 has degree twelve, and the quotient has degree at most six. No
particular square or orbit point is substituted. Consequently the kernel
of this restriction-remainder map is precisely beta=0, proving
ellDelta=span(R1).

## 5. Consequences and limits

At the rank-three coefficient line beta=-alpha, the two jet functionals
are nonzero multiples of 3alpha and alpha. Thus a nonzero member of that
line is off both ell5 and ell3. PR1030's Nakayama argument consequently
gives I_B,p=I_Z,p at every prescribed axis. This is a LOCAL statement on
that fixed support; no assertion about all other points follows from it.

On ell5 or ell3, alpha+beta is nonzero for a nonzero member. PR1031 thus
gives generic lift rank four on each jet-exceptional line. These three lines, and the conic line beta=0,
are pairwise distinct. Their special properties concern different objects:
the matrix rank of R, and the component-ideal jets of its nonic row NLR.
No implication in the reverse direction beyond these exact line relations
is asserted.

The two old lower bounds remain lower bounds. This packet neither computes
additional zeros nor proves a finite base scheme has exactly46 or54 points.
It also leaves whether the actual gate is zero entirely open.
