## 3. Actual maximal plateau and its exhaustive bounds

Suppose beta_E has generic rank three. Its first saturated negative kernel and forward transform have ranks14 and20. Outward derivative increments cannot increase: on the forward side differentiation modulo the new stage vanishes on the preceding stage and factors through its increment; on the backward side the derivative takes values in the preceding quotient twisted by O(-2). Thus each successive outward increment is at most its predecessor.

Continue on each side while the increment is three. Ranks strictly change by three and remain between0 and30, so this constructs the maximal consecutive rank-three plateau

    F0 < ... < Fm, E=Fk, m=k+l,
    rank Fi=17+3(i-k), 1<=k<=5, 1<=l<=4.

At every interior stage, differentiation gives Fi^-=F(i-1) and Fi^+=F(i+1): the inclusions have equal generic ranks and saturation in the same ambient identifies the sheaves. This remains valid across ramification and does not identify sheaf kernels with enlarged pointwise kernels. Endpoint derivatives have rank at most two, not assumed three or one.

Every Fi with i<m contains no nonzero constant subbundle. Below E this follows by inclusion. Above E any constant subbundle lies in the interior derivative kernel, propagating back into E. Dually a proper constant span of a lower stage propagates forward to a proper constant span of E. These are assertions about the actual connection.

Here is the section-length argument explicitly. A nonzero section of E(d) saturates to L=O(-e) inside E and then H O, with1<=e<=d. In an affine coordinate its inclusion is a polynomial vector f(z)=sum v_j z^j, j=0,...,e. In characteristic zero the derivatives f,...,f^(e) span the constant coefficient space of the v_j: the change of generators is triangular with nonzero factorial diagonal, even if the coefficients are dependent. Saturating reaches this constant span after at most e forward transforms. All these transforms lie in F(k+j). If l>d this supplies a constant subbundle in an interior stage, a contradiction. Therefore H0(E(d)) nonzero implies l<=d. Apply the identical argument to Q*=E^perp in the reversed dual plateau to obtain H0(Q*(d)) nonzero implies k<=d.

The actual cubic sections in A2 give

These are ordered directions. Exchanging k,l also exchanges E,Q*, of different ranks:12 cannot be silently replaced by21. ACTUAL additionally says l=3 forces Z, but absence of quadratic sections does not conversely force l=3.
