# Full saturation excludes the entire (1,3) Gauss profile

## Result and new point

The complete maximal rank-three Gauss profile (k,l)=(1,3) is impossible. The earlier rank-three cubic-image argument extends to the FULL saturated derivative image, without assuming the cubic image has rank three. The quartic source adds no eligible splitting block except V O(-1), which is impossible for the full saturation because its cubic sections contain U=Wprime+2T6.

Combined with the already proved exclusions of (3,3),(2,3),(3,2),(3,1), this leaves only

    (1,1), (1,2), (2,1), (2,2).

In particular both lengths are at most two. This does not exclude all generic rank-three SF, prove SF>=4, improve square rank>=27, or evaluate a fourth Bockstein.

## 1. Use the full image, not the cubic subimage

Keep the actual constant-bundle setup and the l=3 splitting

    E=U O(-3)+V O(-4), U=Wprime+2T6,
    rank E=17, deg E=-54.

Put K=E^-, I=(E/K)(3), and let S be the saturation of I in Q(1). The full actual image I and S have rank three. The cubic subimage J of U O may have any rank; it is never substituted for I in this proof.

Persistence gives H0(K(3))=0. Consequently U injects into H0(I) and H0(S). Also every degree of K is at most -4, so deg K<=-56 and

    deg I=-45-deg K>=11.                              (1)

The source E(4)=U O(1)+V O is globally generated and maps generically onto S(1). Hence every ordinary degree of S is at least -1. This is generic generation of S(1), not an unproved assertion that S is generated by the cubic source.

## 2. The saturation cannot have a negative summand

S has center-odd linearization and rank three. A degree -1 summand has coefficient representation R that is center-even of dimension at most three. The possible irreducible R are 1,A,V. No map from U O to R O(-1) exists by degree. A map from V O(-1) to R O(-1) is possible only for R=V.

But that rank-three summand would be ALL of S, giving S=V O(-1) and H0(S)=0, contrary to the injection of U. Thus S has no negative summand at all.

This exclusion is intrinsic to the full source. It remains valid even if the actual map from U O has generic rank one or two.

## 3. The correct dual envelope and finite range

In the reversed dual plateau the central Q* has rank13, its preceding kernels have ranks10,7,4, and the lowest rank-four stage has no cubic sections. Negative-transpose duality identifies S=(Q*/Kd)*(1), where Kd has rank10. Therefore

    chi_G(Kd(3))=T6-2W-S4-chi_G(S*(4)),
    chi(Kd(3))=deg S-17.

The two successive dual derivatives and persistence give

    H0(Kd(3)) <= H0(S*(2))+H0(S*).                    (2)

The actual intervening derivative image embeds in the preceding quotient twisted by -2; no equality with a saturation is presumed in deriving (2).

Since S has nonnegative degrees, the right dimension is at most 9+3=12. Thus

    11<=deg S<=29.                                   (3)

Each irreducible splitting block R O(e) has e>=0, dim R<=3, central parity (-1)^(e+1), and receives a nonzero projection from at least one of U O or V O(-1). Its necessary source condition is

    Hom_G(U,R Sym^e W) + Hom_G(V,R Sym^(e+1)W) != 0.   (4)

This is the FULL source condition. The exact finite character check shows that within 0<=e<=29 every block satisfying (4) already has Hom_G(U,R Sym^e W)!=0. The only quartic-only block in the preliminary range e>=-1 is V O(-1), already excluded geometrically. We do not infer that the actual cubic projection to each block is nonzero; only eligibility is used.

Enumerating rank-three sums under (2)-(4), the section inclusion U<=H0(S), and (3), gives exactly

    Wprime O+O(15), Wprime O+O(17),
    Wprime O+O(19), Wprime O(2)+O(7).                 (5)

The first has no equivariant embedding into the actual Q(1), by the positive Q* target constraints in the three-image reduction. The other three have degree at most19.

## 4. The full actual image is saturated

The determinant of I⊂S defines an invariant effective divisor. By (1),(5) its degree is at most19-11=8. Every positive invariant divisor degree is at least12. Therefore I=S.

This step uses the degree of the FULL image I, not a degree formula involving rank J. We have proved that E/K is one of

    Wprime O(-3)+O(14),
    Wprime O(-3)+O(16),
    Wprime O(-1)+O(4).                               (6)

The three exact quotients are thus forced independently of cubic-image rank.

## 5. Apply the verified top-multiplicity obstruction

The two-twist splitting calculation in EXTREMAL-13-RANK-THREE-CUBIC-IMAGE-EXCLUSION.md uses only E, H0(K(3))=0, and the exact quotients (6). It does not use the rank of J once (6) is known. It gives respectively

    K=(2V+D) O(-4)+Wprime O(-5)+O(-8);

    K=(2V+P4) O(-4)+Wprime O(-5)+O(-6)+O(-8), or
      (2V+P4) O(-4)+Wprime O(-5)+W O(-7);

    K=(2V+2P4) O(-4).

In every case K has two top copies of V O(-4), while the space of equivariant maps from one such copy to (E/K)(-2) has dimension one. The actual second fundamental form of K therefore kills a direct V O(-4) multiplicity summand C.

Its internal second fundamental form lands in (K/C)(-2), all of whose degrees are at most -6. Hom(C,(K/C)(-2))=0. Thus C is a saturated horizontal subbundle of the original constant bundle and must be constant, contradicting its negative degree. No rank assumption on this endpoint derivative is used.

All cases in (6) are impossible. This proves the whole profile exclusion, not merely the earlier conditional rank-three subcase.
