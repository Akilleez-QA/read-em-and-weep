# All three quartic connecting scalars are nonzero

Work over C, with the actual normalized HM bundle F on P(V)=P^4, B=End_0(F), T=V*, and S=Sym(T). Write K_j=H^1(B(j-1)) and H_j=H^2(B(j-5)). We prove Tor_1^S(K,C)_4=0. Consequently every quartic channel of the connecting map identified in PR480 is nonzero, and K is presented by relations of degrees at most three.

## 1. Actual cohomology inputs

PR479 records the S-compatible perfect Serre pairing

H_j tensor H_(5-j) -> C,

with H supported in degrees zero through five, and proves that the actual multiplication T tensor H_0 -> H_1 is an isomorphism. The latter follows by duality from the evaluation map in PR476; it is not inferred just from matching dimensions. In particular H has no linear relation among its degree-zero generators.

PR476 proves H^3(B(t))=0 for t>=-1 and H^1(B(t))=0 for t<=-2. Negative-twist H^0(B(t)) vanishes by stability. We will use only H^3(B(-1))=0 and H^0(B(-3))=0.

## 2. A low Koszul group of H vanishes

In internal degree two the beginning of the Koszul complex of H is

0 -> Lambda^2 T tensor H_0 -> T tensor H_1 -> H_2.

There is no preceding term, since H_-1=0. Identify H_1 with T tensor H_0 by the actual isomorphism above. The left differential becomes

(a wedge b) tensor h -> b tensor (a tensor h) - a tensor (b tensor h),

up to the common Koszul sign convention. This is the usual alternating inclusion and is injective in characteristic zero. Therefore

Tor_2^S(H,C)_2=0.                                         (1)

This conclusion does not require a complete presentation of H or any later multiplication statement.

The S-compatible perfect pairing on H and exterior duality pair its Koszul complexes in complementary homological degrees and total internal degrees summing to ten: the five-dimensional variable space contributes five and the self-duality shift contributes five. Thus

Tor_3^S(H,C)_8 = Tor_2^S(H,C)_2* tensor det T = 0.           (2)

The determinant factor is trivial for the HM normalizer in SL(V); its value does not affect this vanishing.

## 3. The acyclic sheaf Koszul complex forces the quartic vanishing

Tensor the exact coordinate Koszul complex on P^4 by B(3). Its cohomology spectral sequence has

E_1^(-i,q)=Lambda^i T tensor H^q(B(3-i)),

and d_r has bidegree (r,1-r). The q=1 row is the internal-degree-four Koszul complex of K, while the q=2 row is the internal-degree-eight Koszul complex of H. Therefore

E_2^(-1,1)=Tor_1^S(K,C)_4,
E_2^(-3,2)=Tor_3^S(H,C)_8=0.

We check every possible differential affecting E_2^(-1,1).

- Its incoming d_2 source is the zero term E_2^(-3,2) from (2).
- Its incoming d_3 source E_3^(-4,3) is zero already on the first page, since its sheaf cohomology factor is H^3(B(-1))=0.
- Its incoming d_4 source E_4^(-5,4) is zero already on the first page, since H^4(B(-2))=H^0(B(-3))*=0 by self-duality and Serre duality.
- For r>=5 an incoming source lies outside the five-step Koszul complex, and also above the cohomology range.
- For every r>=2 an outgoing target has first coordinate -1+r>0, outside the complex.

Thus E_2^(-1,1) survives unchanged to E_infinity. The sheaf Koszul complex is exact, so its hypercohomology is zero. Hence

Tor_1^S(K,C)_4=0.                                         (3)

No H^0(B(3)) vanishing is needed. In particular the positive-twist row is not discarded wholesale.

## 4. Consequences for the actual quartic scalars and finite presentation

PR480 proves that the degree-four minimal relation space is the cokernel of the actual equivariant connecting map

A tensor (U' + T_4 + J_5 + W') -> A tensor (U' + T_4 + J_5),

where each indicated summand is an inequivalent irreducible, each with multiplicity one. The W' source summand maps to zero by character mismatch. Equation (3) proves that the map is onto. Therefore all three matching scalar channels, of dimensions fifteen, twenty and twenty-five, are nonzero, and its kernel is exactly A tensor W'. This proves nonvanishing rather than selecting or evaluating their scalar values.

Together with PR480's absence of relations above degree four, the full relation module R=ker(S tensor V -> K) is generated by the Euler relation epsilon, the fifteen quadratic minimal relations E_2 of type T tensor U', and a possible cubic space E_3 of type A* tensor U'. By PR479, E_3 is either zero or that entire fifteen-dimensional irreducible. Choose homogeneous lifts of E_2 and E_3. Then

R=S epsilon+S E_2+S E_3,
R_5=S_4 epsilon+S_3 E_2+S_2 E_3,
R_6=S_5 epsilon+S_4 E_2+S_3 E_3.

These are submodule and subspace sums, not direct sums. In the fixed-pair presentations of PR480 the quartic relation contributions may therefore be removed entirely. The remaining cubic scalar and the actual relation embeddings are not determined here; no original fixed quadratic or cubic is evaluated. This does not replace the primary Tor correction in PR477 by zero.
