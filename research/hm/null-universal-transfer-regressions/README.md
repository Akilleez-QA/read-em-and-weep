# Preserve universal transfer and line-quantization controls

Two unchanged, standalone standard-library scripts accompany their existing reports and independent audit records. The recorded results are 2553 exact transfer checks and 636 exact line-quantization checks in ordinary and optimized Python. The three transfer mutants and five line mutants have recorded exit status 1 in both modes. These receipts are preserved, not rerun for this publication record. All inputs are synthetic rational examples; no physical-source matrix, rank, curvature or obstruction value is tested. Finite testing does not prove the general algebraic theorem.

## Shared boundary

These records give conditional formal deductions, not evaluated HM matrices or obstruction coordinates. Classical source identification, accepted HM cohomology, actual first-order existence, ambient normalization and boundary comparison remain imported where invoked. Operators and inverse prescriptions remain unevaluated. No six obstruction values or second-order lift are established.

The diagonal criterion concerns the complete pure sum, with the separately imported mixed result. Separate PP and QQ exactness is stronger and is not asserted. The 60-dimensional first-staircase input is not 60 Ext¹ classes. The diagonal quotient is 32-dimensional; confinement to a six-dimensional 2+4 subspace requires the actual-source covariance theorem. The diagonal differential has a 382-dimensional kernel, so its primitive prescription uses a Moore–Penrose inverse, not an inverse of its full Gram matrix.
