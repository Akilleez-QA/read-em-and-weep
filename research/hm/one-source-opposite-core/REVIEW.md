## Whole-proof mathematical checks

1. The projection of the actual U into B4 cannot vanish. If it vanished, the restriction of the fiberwise-injective source map would factor as an everywhere rank-two injection U into F. Such an equal-rank injection is an isomorphism, incompatible with degrees -2 and 10. This forces nonvanishing without an actual coefficient calculation.

2. The relevant equivariant Hom space is one-dimensional because S tensor A=W+S+T6. The universal cubic-polarization sequence is correct: at an arbitrary quotient W to a line with kernel generated by l, polarization followed by two evaluations kills l cubed and l squared times m, and takes l times m squared and m cubed to independent nonzero W directions. This identifies the kernel as l squared times W, globally giving 0 to W O(-1) to S O(1) to W O(3) to 0. It is a uniform proof over every line, not a selected HM-point evaluation. Consequently the actual nonzero projection is saturated and its actual quotient R has the asserted type.

3. Hom_G(R,F)=0 follows from W' tensor A=T6 and the absence of W in T6; Ext1_G(R,F)=0 follows from H1(O(2))=0. The exact Hom/Ext sequence therefore makes restriction Hom_G(B4,F) to Hom_G(U,F) an isomorphism. Since the original target extension splits equivariantly by H1(O(4))=0, there is exactly one correction of any initial section whose restriction agrees with the actual source. Thus the resulting section is independent of the initial choice. The proof correctly limits uniqueness to equivariant splittings: ordinary Hom(R,F) is not zero.

4. Quotienting by the actual U subbundle and its actual image yields the original Q3, not a replacement bundle. The target is the intrinsic direct sum F+R selected by the compatible section, and the remaining source is the inherited L8. The two projected maps alpha and beta include the entire remaining actual source map, including its graph contribution. Representation identifications can change scalar coordinates but not either zero locus. The inherited map F to Q3 is retained.

5. The two Hom channels have dimension one: Sym11 W=W+S+T6 and Sym13 W=W+W'+S+T6. For the degree-eleven system, the minimal nonempty projective orbit size twelve rules out any common-zero divisor. For the degree-thirteen system, every such divisor would have degree twelve, since projective orbit sizes are 12, 20, 30 and 60. Its defining binary form spans a one-dimensional representation, trivial because SL2(F5) is perfect. Division would then give an impossible equivariant inclusion W' into Sym1 W=W. Both systems are therefore basepoint-free on every fiber, rather than only generically. Their line quotients have degrees 18 and 14; perfectness excludes an additional nontrivial character linearization.

6. Simultaneous vanishing is excluded by the inherited line-source injection. If alpha vanishes, the quotient is F plus coker beta, hence W' O(5)+O(14). If beta vanishes, the quotient is coker alpha plus R, hence O(18)+W O(3). No choice of an old row is needed for either deduction.

7. When both channels are nonzero, saturation of beta gives the actual extension 0 to F to Q3 to O(14) to 0. Its class is the connecting image of alpha, with a possible global sign according to the pushout convention. Hom_G(R,F)=Ext1_G(R,F)=0 makes this connecting map an isomorphism, so the class is nonzero. Independently, equivariant Serre duality and Sym7 W=W'+T6 give dimension one for Ext1_G(O(14),W' O(5)). Twisting the authenticated sextic sequence by O(8) gives a nonsplit representative with middle bundle V O(8); it cannot split since Hom(O(14),V O(8))=0. All nonzero classes in the one-dimensional space have isomorphic middle bundle after nonzero rescaling of an endpoint. This proves the both-nonzero type without merely matching its character to a list.

8. The three resulting equivariant splitting types are pairwise distinct. The authenticated TYPES list, combined with SMALL's equivalence of mu=0 and lambda=0 through the same actual Q, identifies them with exactly rows17, 18 and 20. This justifies both directions of the three stated criteria. It does not assert that each row or channel pattern is realized by actual HM data.

9. All bundle balances agree. The cubic sequence has source (rank,degree)=(2,-2), middle (4,4), and quotient (2,6). The one-source core has source (1,-8), target (4,16), and quotient (3,24). The nonzero-channel line quotients and the sextic extension have the stated degrees. These numerical checks supplement, rather than replace, the exact-sequence arguments.
