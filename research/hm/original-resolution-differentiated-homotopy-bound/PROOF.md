# An explicit commutator for one genuine homotopy derivative

## Result and exact limit

The main new result is an exact **own-owner commutator identity** for the accepted actual original-resolution homotopy:

    [nabla_V,H_o]=[ad_(S_o),H_o].

Section 7 constructs S_o explicitly from the differentiated contraction. For the retained old connection S_o has carrier/legal bound **5/1**. For the literal reference derivative its bound is **5/0 plus one explicit standard infinity factor**. For a word of different homotopy owners, the exact formula retains every internal ad_(S_i-S_(i+1)) insertion. This supplies a concrete reorganization of differentiated inner-kernel factors, without making them constant or claiming they cancel.

For the accepted actual product/root-line construction and frozen original-resolution End homotopy, one invariant derivative has the following **different** meanings and bounds. Write the accepted whole operator as

    H_r(F)=hF+(-1)^r EFh,     E=Id-gamma H0-H1 delta,
    h <= carrier/legal 2/0,  E <= 2/0,  H <= 4/0.

1. Differentiating the coefficients in a **regular standard square-line frame** gives K_A=[V,H_A] with bound **5/0**. These local derivatives have an inhomogeneous frame-change law, so they are not the coefficients of a global bundle map on their own.
2. The **literal original-reference coefficient derivative**, defined by the frozen source derivative convention when applied to H, is represented on chart A by

       K_ref,A=K_A+(V log tau_A)H_A.

   It has carrier/legal **5/0**, together with **one named standard factor t_r** when V=V_r and r is an infinity coordinate of A. There is no new legal-mask dependence, but the standard correction cannot be suppressed. This is not an unqualified all-chart 5/0 extension theorem in the original Hom line.
3. The derivative for the **retained owner-m old connection** is

       K_old,A=K_A-a_m,A H_A,
       a_m,A=-sum_i m_i V log x_i-V log tau_A.

   It has bound **5/1** when the connection and homotopy have the same owner. Here a is the connection coefficient on the **square line N^2**, twice the root-line connection after frame conversion. Its entire pole allowance is owned by the legal factor of that connection owner; no separate standard denominator is required.
4. The whole projector has degree zero. Its genuine derivative **VE has bound 3/0 on every standard chart**, for all the above scalar line connections.

The nontrivial differentiated contraction is

    partial K+K partial = -dotPi-QH-HQ,                       (R)
    Pi(F)=EFE,       Q(F)=(nabla_V d)F-(-1)^r F(nabla_V d),
    K=[nabla_V,H],   dotPi(F)=(nabla_V E)FE+EF(nabla_V E).

The two Q terms record the variation of the actual polynomial differential. Neither the contraction identity nor idempotence permits deleting them, deleting the derivative of H from a scalar kernel, or declaring the complete inner kernel constant. An explicit three-step normalization yields K=[ad_S,H], with S_old<=5/1, while retaining every meromorphic correction and every difference between owners in a multi-owner word; see section 7.

All statements are mathematical consequences for the exact rational maps, conditional on the accepted contraction, homogeneous clearance, frame identification, and genuine derivation semantics. No physical/source/model evaluation, source import, source polynomial product, matrix product, detector, raw-F value, new kernel construction is performed. No source scalar cancellation is concluded.

## 2. Three coefficient derivatives must not be conflated

On a standard product chart A, use the regular square-line frame

    ell_A=tau_A^-1 ell_ref,      tau_A=product_(i in A)t_i,
    t_i=1/x_i,                  u_i=y_i/x_i^2.

On an overlap put lambda=tau_B/tau_A. A degree-r End coefficient obeys

    F_B=lambda^r F_A,     h_B=lambda^-1 h_A,     E_B=E_A.

The operator coefficients H therefore have degree -1:

    H_B=lambda^-1 H_A.                                       (2.1)

This shorthand compares the coefficient tensors; equivalently
H_B(lambda^r F_A)=lambda^(r-1)H_A(F_A).

Let V=V_r be the genuine regular invariant field. On its curve factor,

    V(x)=y,       V(y)=(3x^2-1)/2,
    V(t)=-u,      V(u)=(3t^2-1)/2.                           (2.2)

It preserves the regular local coordinate rings, including branch and infinity points. Define the local coefficient derivative

    K_A(F)=V(H_A(F))-H_A(VF)
          =(Vh_A)F+(-1)^r[(VE_A)Fh_A+E_AF(Vh_A)].             (2.3)

Differentiating (2.1), with V acting on the frame ratio as well, gives

    K_B=lambda^-1[K_A-(V log lambda)H_A].                    (2.4)

Thus the regular local coefficient derivatives do **not** themselves glue as a degree-minus-one morphism.

Now specify an even connection along V. Its induced action on degree-r End coefficients in a standard square-line frame is

    nabla_V F_A=VF_A+r a_A F_A,
    a_B=a_A-V log lambda.                                   (2.5)

The common root twist cancels in End. Consequently the induced derivative of H is

    K^nabla_A=[nabla_V,H]_A=K_A-a_A H_A.                     (2.6)

Equations (2.4)-(2.5) show K^nabla_B=lambda^-1 K^nabla_A, exactly. The minus sign comes from output degree r-1 minus input degree r. E has degree zero, hence nabla_V E=VE without a scalar connection term.

### 2.1 The literal reference derivative

Choose the meromorphic connection whose square-line coefficient is zero in ell_ref. Since ell_A=tau_A^-1 ell_ref, its coefficient is

    a_ref,A=-V log tau_A.

The source's bare derivative of the original-reference H coefficient is exactly this covariant derivative, expressed on A:

    (V H_ref)_A=K_A+(V log tau_A)H_A.                        (2.7)

This is a statement about differentiation of the operator coefficients. A derivative of H(F) additionally contains H(VF), or its corresponding covariant term.

For V=V_r, the correction in (2.7) is zero if r is finite on A, and is -(u_r/t_r)H_A if r is an infinity coordinate. Treating tau_A as a fixed pointwise normalization would delete this term incorrectly. The original rational reference is a valid comparison frame, not a frame regular across all infinity divisors.

An invented local check isolates the problem without using any model: let a degree-minus-one local coefficient on an infinity chart be the constant 1. Its reference coefficient is t. The transported derivative of t is -u/t, while the derivative of the local coefficient 1 is zero. Since u is a uniformizer and t has order two at O, -u/t has a genuine simple pole. This does not assert that every actual source entry realizes the maximal bound; it proves that the frame correction is not dispensable by a general derivative rule.

### 2.2 The retained old connection and the factor of two

For actual legal root frame e_m, the accepted bridge gives

    e_m^2=h_m ell_m,
    h_m=product_i(1-x_i^-2)^(m_i),
    alpha_m=(1/2)V log h_m.

Alpha is the root-line connection coefficient in e_m. Its square-line coefficient in e_m^2 is **2 alpha_m**. With

    c_m,A=e_m^2/ell_A=nu_m^2 tau_A,

the square-line coefficient in ell_A is

    a_m,A=2 alpha_m-V log c_m,A
         =-sum_i m_i V log x_i-V log tau_A.                 (2.8)

For one differentiated coordinate the full table is

    finite,  bit 0: 0;          finite,  bit 1: -y/x;
    infinity,bit 0: u/t;        infinity,bit 1: 0.            (2.9)

This is the connection retained by the source; setting alpha_m to zero in the actual e_m frame would change it. In particular the old derivative and the literal source reference derivative are generally different operators:

    K_old,A=K_A-a_m,A H_A,
    K_old,A-K_ref,A=(sum_i m_i V log x_i)H_A.                (2.10)

The bare derivative in the actual legal frame is a third coefficient operation. Since H_m=c_m,A H_A,

    c_m,A^-1 V(H_m)=K_A+(V log c_m,A)H_A                     (2.11)

as an operator-coefficient identity. Its covariant correction subtracts 2 alpha_m H_A and recovers (2.10). Bare reference, bare legal, and covariant derivatives cannot be exchanged merely because the undifferentiated maps agree.

## 3. The exact differentiated contraction and projector equations

Let nabla=nabla_V be any of the induced connections above, or any compatible even connection on the graded complex. Write dots for induced covariant derivatives of bundle maps. Leibniz applied to (1.1) gives

    d dot h+dot h d=-dot E-dot d h-h dot d.                  (3.1)

For the actual degree-zero block, this is explicitly

    dot E=-(dot gamma)H0-gamma(dot H0)
          -(dot H1)delta-H1(dot delta).                    (3.2)

The differential is the source's varying homogeneous polynomial differential. A coefficient derivation of it is not declared zero. In a square-line frame dot d=Vd+a_A d, whereas dot h=Vh-a_A h. The a terms in (3.2) cancel between degree +1 and degree -1 factors. Thus the whole projector derivative is exactly VE, including for the old connection.

The derivative of E^2=E is

    dot E E+E dot E=dot E.

Multiplying by E and by 1-E yields

    E dot E E=0,       (1-E)dot E(1-E)=0,
    dot E=E dot E(1-E)+(1-E)dot E E.                        (3.3)

This says the derivative is off-diagonal relative to the moving projector, not that it is zero. Differentiating dE=Ed=0 also gives

    dot d E+d dot E=0,     dot E d+E dot d=0.               (3.4)

Now Q=[nabla,partial] has degree +1 and acts as

    Q_r(F)=dot d F-(-1)^r F dot d.                          (3.5)

The derivative K=[nabla,H] has degree -1 and is exactly

    K_r(F)=dot h F+(-1)^r[dot E Fh+EF dot h].                (3.6)

Differentiate (1.2) as an operator equality. Since nabla has degree zero, its commutator obeys the ordinary Leibniz rule for operator composition. Therefore

    QH+partial K+K partial+HQ=-dotPi,
    dotPi_r(F)=dot E F E+E F dot E.                         (3.7)

Rearranging proves (R), with its two minus signs and all degree indices supplied by composition. No extra graded sign is generated by the parameter derivative. A homogeneous chain-contraction identity for K alone would require additional vanishing of the entire right side, not just Pi=0 in the current degree.

For example, for a closed F, one obtains

    partial K(F)=-dotPi(F)-QH(F)-HQ(F).                     (3.8)

Closedness removes K partial(F) but does not remove HQ(F). A restriction of the operator to a scalar terminal row does not automatically annihilate any term in (3.8).

## 5. Distinct owners and an already meromorphic input

The derivative of H is an operator coefficient; it does not by itself differentiate the input F. For a full derivative, retain

    nabla(HF)=K(F)+H(nabla F).                              (5.1)

If a meromorphic input has several owner factors, differentiating it may raise each participating denominator power by one. That is a separate term in (5.1), not included in the selected homotopy owner's increment 5.

If the homotopy owner is o and the old connection owner is n, the sharper two-term statement is

    K_old,A=K_A-a_n,A H_A:
    first term: carrier5 at o, no new legal factor;
    second term: carrier4 at o, legal1 at n.                 (5.2)

Their sum can be cleared by carrier5 at o and legal1 at n. These become the single-owner pair 5/1 only when the occurrences themselves are identified. Equal masks, carriers, or polynomial factors do not identify them. If n is not an admitted owner of a proposed kernel, its legal factor cannot silently be supplied by another label. The old connection must be specified before using (5.2).

The reference derivative instead has the named chart correction (4.3). Replacing its standard factor by an owner's legal factor requires an actual divisibility/domain argument, and is not automatic for bit-one owners at infinity.

## 6. Why a scalar adjoint kernel must retain K

Let ell be a dual output row for H, with all pairings and line factors correctly typed. Write H* for the algebraic transpose/dual under that pairing. The dual-connection product rule is the exact identity

    nabla^vee(H* ell)=K* ell+H*(nabla^vee ell).               (6.1)

With an additional scalar weight w,

    nabla^vee(w H* ell)
       =(Vw)H*ell+w K*ell+w H*(nabla^vee ell).               (6.2)

Thus a transposed solver or terminal contraction does not make its H factor constant. A kernel with several H factors contains a corresponding sum of differentiated-factor terms. They can cancel only after a proof for the complete ordered, weighted, frame-correct kernel. Equations (R), (3.3), determinant one, and flatness alone do not provide that proof.

For a certified finite Laurent coefficient functional Lambda_e=[x^e], the normalized coordinate derivative has the elementary adjoint formula

    Lambda_e(kappa partial_r f)
      =Lambda_e(( -partial_r kappa+(e_r+1)x_r^-1 kappa ) f). (6.3)

Equivalently put w=x^(-e-1) into the residue multiplier and differentiate the whole w kappa; the weight correction is then already included and must not be added twice. For the historical exponent (-1,-1,-3,-3), (6.3) has the -2/x_r correction in directions 3 and 4.

Equation (6.3) is only used when the complete expression has the prescribed finite Laurent or equivalent proved residue domain. It does not select a rational expansion for an uncleared carrier denominator. Nor does the bound for genuine V transfer unchanged to a stripped operation V/y=partial_x: dividing by y or changing to the infinity odd basis u requires its own regularity and line calculation.

In fact the genuine V changes the deck character in its differentiated coordinate: V sends an even coefficient to an odd one and conversely. On the finite free basis (1,y),

    V(a(x)+b(x)y)=y a'(x)+(x^3-x)b'(x)+(3x^2-1)b(x)/2.

This formula has no division by y and explains why genuine V is regular at branch points. A normalized coefficient or a full-sign projection has additional bookkeeping; the undifferentiated homotopy's character-preservation statement is not a differentiated-kernel statement.

The available older kernel analysis is copied only as scoped background. It explicitly lacks authenticated complete current kernels and retains kernel and weight derivatives. This packet supplies an exact required derivative identity and bounded local factor; it does **not** authenticate a complete current scalar kernel, assign its final ownerwise bound, establish its Ward cancellation, or certify normalized-jet implementation correctness.

## 7. An explicit own-owner commutator for the differentiated homotopy

The following algebraic normalization is useful for locating complete-kernel terms. It changes neither the frozen source derivative nor its connection. All corrections are retained explicitly.

### 7.1 First make E parallel

Put B=1-E and define the degree-zero endomorphism

    A=[dot E,E]=dot E E-E dot E.

By (3.3), [A,E]=dot E. Thus the analytically corrected connection nabla^sharp=nabla-A on K, with induced End action nabla^sharp F=nabla F-[A,F], satisfies

    nabla^sharp E=0.

Equations (3.4) give

    A d=E dot d,       d A=-dot d E,
    [A,d]=E dot d+dot d E,       E dot d E=0,
    q:=nabla^sharp d=dot d-E dot d-dot d E=B dot d B.         (7.1)

For Q^sharp=[q,-] with the usual degree sign and K^sharp=[nabla^sharp,H], the differentiated contraction becomes

    partial K^sharp+K^sharp partial=-Q^sharp H-HQ^sharp.     (7.2)

Making E parallel alone therefore does not remove the defect.

### 7.2 Then make d parallel without disturbing E

Differentiating d^2=0 gives d q+q d=0, and Eq=qE=0. Put

    U=-h q=-h dot d B.

The accepted contraction identities give

    [U,d]=-h q d+d h q=(h d+d h)q=q,     [U,E]=0.

Consequently nabla^b=nabla^sharp-U on K has

    nabla^b d=0,       nabla^b E=0.                         (7.3)

The superscript b labels this intermediate connection; no flatness or vanishing of its curvature is asserted.

### 7.3 Finally make h parallel

Set k=nabla^b h. Differentiate the accepted identities using (7.3):

    d k+k d=0,       h k+k h=0,       E k=k E=0.             (7.4)

For C=-d k, direct substitution gives

    [C,d]=0,          [C,E]=0,
    [C,h]=-d k h+h d k=(d h+h d)k=k.                        (7.5)

With

    S=A+U+C,         nabla^natural=nabla-S,

one obtains, on this owner's graded complex,

    nabla^natural d=0,   nabla^natural E=0,
    nabla^natural h=0,   [nabla^natural,H]=0.                (7.6)

Write ad_S(F)=[S,F]. Since nabla=nabla^natural+ad_S on End, the exact operator identity is

    K=[nabla,H]=[ad_S,H].                                  (7.7)

This is a concrete source-bound replacement identity for one differentiated H factor. The auxiliary S is explicit in dot d, dot E, dot h and the accepted contraction. It is not asserted to be flat in multiple directions, constant, identical across owners, or cheaper to construct. In particular S itself contains derivatives; (7.7) reorganizes them rather than declaring their arithmetic unnecessary.

### 7.4 All-chart carrier/legal bound for the correction

For the scalar-line connections of sections 2-5, the auxiliary k has a useful connection-free standard-chart expression. Differentiating Eh=hE=0 and using h^2=0 gives

    [A,h]=E dot h+dot h E,        [U,h]=-h dot d h.

Therefore

    k=dot h-E dot h-dot h E+h dot d h
     =Vh-E(Vh)-(Vh)E+h(Vd)h.                              (7.8)

In the last equality the scalar a_A terms cancel because hdh=h and Eh=hE=0. Formula (7.8) transforms tensorially: under a frame change, Vh gains -(Vlog lambda)h and h(Vd)h gains +(Vlog lambda)hdh; they cancel. Thus k is an actual degree-minus-one map with global carrier/legal bound **5/0**, not merely a collection of local coefficient derivatives. The four local charges are 3, 2+3, 3+2, and 2+0+2.

C=-dk has degree zero and global bound5/0. The first correction A has bound5/0 because dot E=VE<=3/0 and E<=2/0. For the old connection, dot d<=0/1; U=-h dot d(1-E)<=4/1. Hence

    S_old <=5/1                                              (7.9)

for the common owner. For the source reference derivative S_ref has carrier/legal5/0 with the same one explicit standard t_r factor as (4.3). If the initial old connection has a different owner, its legal1 charge remains there. No new clearance owner is supplied by this normalization.

A naive pole estimate of the commutator product (7.7) can be weaker than the direct K<=5/1 estimate. The identity does not authorize replacing a controlled whole expression by an uncontrolled intermediate arithmetic schedule.

### 7.5 Owner differences survive in a word

Let H_i be homotopies of possibly different owner occurrences, all differentiated with the same initial connection, and let L_i=ad_(S_i). For the pure operator word P=H_n ... H_1, (7.7) and the product rule give

    nabla(P)=L_n P-P L_1
       +sum_(i=1)^(n-1) H_n ... H_(i+1) (L_i-L_(i+1))
                          H_i ... H_1.                     (7.10)

Here nabla(P) means the induced operator derivative [nabla,P]. This is an exact telescoping identity with the internal owner differences retained. Different contractions generally give different S_i. For identical carrier contractions differentiated by the same initial connection, the accepted legal-free source identification does give S_i=S_j as rational maps; this does not merge their clearance occurrences. If owner-specific old connections are used instead, first convert to a common connection and retain their connection differences before applying (7.10).

A complete kernel has additional inclusion/projection, row, transport, scalar, partition and possibly normalized-derivative factors, whose derivatives remain as in section 6. Even if all S_i agree for a special word, a terminal scalar contraction is not automatically a trace of a commutator in the required representation. No actual inner-kernel term is discarded by (7.10).

The earlier contraction-transgression proof concerns theta/chi descent and its scalar edge ansatz. It does not supply vanishing of the correction terms in (7.7) or (7.10).
