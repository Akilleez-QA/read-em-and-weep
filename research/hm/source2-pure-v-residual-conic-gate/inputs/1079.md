# The full actual residual-large negative module

## Conditional theorem

Retain the actual source2 nonzero pure-V Q-compatible radical hypotheses.
Fix a genuine first-block separation N=N0 plus NR. Assume the ACTUAL N0
projection of P_h is zero and its actual residual quotient projection
pi2(P_h) in N8,2 is nonzero. These are precisely the residual-large
hypotheses, not the general sixteen-dimensional endpoint alternative.

Let R=Sym W be the ring in the same two negative coordinates and put
G_h=R·im h inside the actual cohomology module. Then

    (G_h)_2=P+D,       (G_h)_3=W'+2T,
    (G_h)_4=K_R=V+2P+D, (G_h)_5=NR5=U5=T,
    (G_h)_d=0 for d>=6.                               (1)

The equality in degree five is with the specific genuine residual block
and marked terminal sextet of PR1072, not an arbitrary copy of T. Its
negative socle is the actual embedded I4=P in degree four plus the
actual NR5=T in degree five, with no earlier component.

Its minimal G-equivariant free resolution over R, in ORIGINAL degrees,
is

    0 -> P tensor R(-6) plus T tensor R(-7)
      -> S tensor R(-3) plus V tensor R(-4)
                        plus (W'+S+T) tensor R(-5)
      -> (P+D) tensor R(-2) -> G_h -> 0.              (2)

Its length is45 and Ann_R(G_h)=(W)^4. It is not level: its socle occupies
two different degrees. No nonzero-N0 branch, full Rao resolution,
positive-coordinate stability, or selection of an actual source or
coefficient is asserted.

## 2. The terminal generated image is the whole residual sextet

Because im h=I2 plus P_h is contained in NR2 and the first two actual
pencils preserve NR, its generated endpoint F4 lies in NR4. PR1072
extends this preservation to the next actual multiplication: W F4 is
contained in the specified subspace NR5=T of M5.

If W F4 were zero, the entire F4 would lie in the joint-negative
kernel NC4 of PR1068. But (3) says its intersection with NC4 is only
I4 of dimension four, whereas F4 has dimension sixteen. Thus W F4 is
nonzero. It is G-stable, since it is the image of the full equivariant
map W tensor F4, and NR5 is irreducible. Consequently

    W F4=NR5=U5=T.                                   (5)

This is the actual degree-five piece of R·im h, since the module is
generated in degree two. The ambient H1(B(6)) vanishes, so every
negative descendant of (5) is zero; associativity kills all further
ones. This proves the full support and actual spaces in (1).

## 3. The precise socle, including absence of earlier classes

The degree-four joint annihilator in G_h is the intersection of its
actual F4 with the ambient joint-negative kernel. By (3) and PR1068
this is exactly I4, not the entire abstract P-isotypical component
of F4. The degree-five space is all socle by the ambient degree-six
vanishing.

There is no degree-two socle. On I2, multiplication by a nonzero
negative variable is injective on the binary quartics; on P_h, the
first coefficient map W tensor P_h is injective. The two actual first
images are disjoint by PR1054. Thus a generator killed by both
coordinates is zero.

There is no degree-three socle. If s in F3 is killed by both negative
coordinates, delta(s)=0 in (4). The actual ambient injection delta
then gives s=0. Restricting it to F3 is legitimate because the actual
second arrows take F3 into F4; no additional quotient has been made.
It follows that

    Soc_R(G_h)=I4 in degree4 plus NR5 in degree5.      (6)

This identifies the actual socle subspaces, not just their characters.

## 4. Exact minimal resolution

For this paragraph shift generators to degree zero, writing M_d=(G_h)_(d+2).
Its character-valued Hilbert series has coefficients

    M0=P+D, M1=W'+2T, M2=V+2P+D, M3=T.

The two-variable Koszul complex identifies Tor_2^R(M,C) with its socle
shifted by two; Lambda²W is trivial. Hence (6) gives precisely

    Tor_2^R(M,C)=P in degree4 plus T in degree5.       (7)

A finite-length module over C[x,y] has projective dimension two; no
higher Tor occurs. There are only the degree-zero minimal generators
P+D. Multiply the character-valued Hilbert series by 1-[W]t+t².
The tensor rules needed are

    W P=W'+T, W D=S+T, W V=T,
    W W'=P, W T=V+P+D.

The resulting numerator is

    (P+D) - S t - V t² - (W'+S+T)t³ + P t⁴ + T t⁵. (8)

Subtracting the known generator and Tor_2 terms in (7) determines all
Tor_1 terms: S in degree1, V in degree2 and W'+S+T in degree3, and none
in other degrees. This is not an inference from a dimension numerator
alone: the exact absence of lower socle in Section3 is essential.
Shifting back by two yields (2). Finite-group averaging makes the
minimal resolution equivariant; its differentials have positive degree.
The numerical numerator is9-4t-3t²-12t³+4t⁴+6t⁵, and the Hilbert
function sums to9+14+16+6=45.

## 5. Exact negative annihilator and remaining boundary

Put m=(W). Equation(1) gives m⁴ G_h=0. For each d=1,2,3, the kernel of

    Sym^d W -> Hom((G_h)_2,(G_h)_(d+2))

is a G-subrepresentation. Each domain is irreducible (W,A,S), and its
action is nonzero because its images generate the nonzero degree d+2
piece in (1). The kernel is therefore zero. Constants also have no
nonzero annihilator. The annihilator of a graded module is homogeneous:
apply any candidate to homogeneous generators and separate output
degrees. Thus Ann_R(G_h)=m⁴ exactly.

This argument computes no rank at a chosen negative direction. In
particular it does not say that each map from a generator to a target
is injective. It excludes a polynomial that kills ALL generators.

The zero N0 projection is essential: without it the terminal image
need not lie in the irreducible NR5, and (3) does not identify F4 with
K_R. No conclusion here is transferred to that other large branch.
The module and annihilator concern only the negative two-variable ring;
positive-coordinate actions, the entire Rao module, actual parameter
values and realization of either residual branch remain unselected.
