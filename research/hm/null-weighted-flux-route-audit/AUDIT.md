No genuinely new constraint on the retained ten- or twenty-dimensional classes, and no primitive of either actual weighted source, was obtained in this historical pass. The original thirty-unknown frontier was subsequently superseded by the separate dihedral argument; this audit is not the current project verdict.

## 1. Exact additional chain-rule checks

Retain the complete published eight-term source error

    H(f)=Σ_v(D_v f)Z_v,

where each Z_v is the actual two-endpoint flux cochain, not a replacement current. Work in the same fixed local coefficient frame as the weighted-remainder proof. H is a derivation on scalar weights into that cochain module.

Set d=3(σ²−I)=3(τ+I). Differentiating the actual cubic and centering identities gives, without division,

    d H(σ)=3σ H(I)+H(J),
    H(τ)=2σ H(σ)−2H(I),
    d H(τ)=6I H(I)+2σ H(J).

Proof: apply H to σ³−3Iσ−J=0 and τ=σ²−2I; then multiply the second identity by d and substitute the first. No actual coefficients or matrices are evaluated.

These equations expose a precise error in any new ansatz that treats I and J as differentiation-constant: that ansatz must retain H(I),H(J), or prove the needed contractions cancel. Being base invariants does not make them constant for product-factor differentiation.

However, the equations are local common-frame identities. The separate derivative errors are not independently asserted to descend. They do not constrain the two intrinsic degree-two classes, establish divisibility of a proposed primitive, or supply a primitive of any term. The published dual reconstruction already avoids d and remains stronger for its own purpose.

## 2. Candidate closure gate rejected as duplicate

In the fixed source row frames, L_v,ij=g_ij^{-1}D_vg_ij obeys δ_row L_v=0. With row cup products and δs_q=F_q, the actual fluxes for p=(a,b) satisfy

    Z_a=(L_b∪s_q−s_q∪L_b)/2,
    Z_b=−(L_a∪s_q−s_q∪L_a)/2,

and hence

    δZ_a=−(L_b∪F_q+F_q∪L_b)/2,
    δZ_b= +(L_a∪F_q+F_q∪L_a)/2.

Therefore

    δH_p(f)=1/2[(D_b f)(L_a∪F_q+F_q∪L_a)
                        −(D_a f)(L_b∪F_q+F_q∪L_b)],

plus the complementary q contribution. A separate primitive of the projected H(f) would require the projected displayed degree-three term to vanish. Closure of the complete weighted source does not imply closure of H(f) separately.

But this is exactly substitution into the existing endpoint-defect theorem. The weighted-remainder report already identifies Hσ with these same nonclosed fluxes. Thus no novelty claim survives this audit; f=σ or τ merely specializes existing formulas.

## 3. Useful bounded next question, not a promise of progress

If this derivative route is revisited, the only worthwhile next step is to decide, formally and with full transports, whether the projected combination Σ(D_vσ)δZ_v or Σ(D_vτ)δZ_v vanishes for the ACTUAL F_r=b_r−c_r. Do not derive the generic endpoint formulas again. A nonzero projected combination would rule out a separated absorption/error-primitive ansatz, not show the intrinsic weighted class is nonzero. A zero combination would only make the error a cocycle; its primitive would still be missing.

This pass did not decide those actual-source cancellations. It also did not reopen a pure-gauge search, which the canonical gauge formula has already shown cannot change the compact obstruction class.
