## 1. A different, smooth genus-ten curve

Put A=O_L(4), and form the finite flat spectral cover

    pi:S=Spec_L(O_L plus A^(-1) plus A^(-2)) -> L,
    lambda^3=d.

Multiplication in the displayed algebra uses d:A^(-3)->O_L. The tautological lambda is a section of pi^*A, not a global function without a line trivialization. This construction is intrinsic and has degree three.

The cover is smooth. Away from Omega, the polynomial's derivative 3lambda^2 is nonzero at every point of the cover, so pi is etale there. At a point of Omega, a local equation is lambda^3=t u(t), with u a unit. Its derivative with respect to t at the origin is nonzero, so its total space is smooth, with ramification index three. The same reasoning works in every trivialization of A, including a chart containing infinity. A simple zero of d prevents d from being a cube in the function field of L. Thus lambda^3-d is irreducible there, and S is integral and connected. Finite over the projective line, it is projective.

There are twelve totally ramified points. Riemann--Hurwitz gives

    2g(S)-2=3(-2)+12(3-1)=18,  hence g(S)=10.

In particular S is not D, whose genus is four and whose trigonal fibers above Omega have profile (2,1). Nor is it a normalization or a reinterpretation of the same degree-three extension: its ramification profiles and genera differ. The cyclic deck action lambda -> zeta_3 lambda exists on S, independently of the original trigonal geometry. No lift of this deck action to the spectral line bundle below is asserted.

The canonical bundle and relative dualizing bundle are

    omega_(S/L)=pi^*A^2=pi^*O_L(8),
    omega_S=pi^*O_L(6).

One can verify the first formula locally from the monogenic algebra. The functional extracting the coefficient of lambda^2 generates Hom_O(pi_*O_S,O_L) as a module over pi_*O_S, and transforms by A^2. Equivalently the ramification divisor is twice div(lambda), with O_S(div(lambda))=pi^*A.

## 2. The actual Gram operator defines a spectral line bundle

The identity M^3=d Id makes E into a module over the spectral algebra: the A^(-1) summand acts through the contraction of M. At the generic point of L, the spectral algebra is the cubic field just proved, and E has dimension three over the base field. Thus it has dimension one over that cubic field.

This module is torsion-free over O_S. Indeed a nonzero local spectral element b has nonzero norm in the base function field. Its multiplication adjugate shows that if b annihilates a module section, then a nonzero base element annihilates that section. This cannot happen in the locally free base module E. Since S is smooth, a finite torsion-free rank-one module is an invertible sheaf. Denote it by B. Thus

    pi_*B=E=V tensor O_L.

Euler characteristic gives

    3=chi(E)=chi(B)=deg B+1-10,
    deg B=12,  h0(B)=3.

The identification retains the actual M-module structure; this is not merely a line bundle chosen to reproduce the same dimensions.

## 3. Self-adjointness produces a nontrivial two-torsion class

The symmetric perfect pairing on E identifies E with its O_L-dual. Because M is self-adjoint, this identification is linear for the entire spectral algebra, generated by M. Finite duality identifies the latter module with

    pi_*(B^(-1) tensor omega_(S/L)).

For completeness, this is the module identity

    Hom_O(E,O)=Hom_(pi_*O_S)(E,Hom_O(pi_*O_S,O)),

and the coefficient-of-lambda^2 description above identifies its dualizing factor. Therefore the pairing gives an isomorphism of line bundles

    B^2 = omega_(S/L) = pi^*O_L(8).

Set T=B tensor pi^*O_L(-4). Then T^2=O_S and deg T=0. It is NONTRIVIAL: if B were pi^*O_L(4), the projection formula would give

    pi_*B=O_L(4) plus O_L plus O_L(-4),

contrary to pi_*B=O_L^3. For example, the former has six global sections and the latter has three. Consequently T has exact order two in Pic(S).

There is also an actual ineffective theta characteristic

    Theta=B tensor pi^*O_L(-1),
    Theta^2=omega_S,  deg Theta=9,
    pi_*Theta=V tensor O_L(-1),  h0(Theta)=0.

Thus Theta is even, where parity means h0 modulo two. This is a structural invariant of the actual self-adjoint Gram operator. It is separate from the exact five-torsion class on D and does not transfer a two-torsion class to D.

## 4. The exact centralizer and a low-degree exclusion

A local endomorphism of E commutes with M if and only if it is linear over the spectral algebra. Since B is a line bundle, its endomorphism sheaf on S is O_S. Consequently

    Cent(M)=pi_*O_S=O_L plus O_L(-4) plus O_L(-8)

as an O_L-algebra. Under its embedding in End(E), the three terms act by Id, M, and M^2 with their line twists. This remains true at the twelve nilpotent fibers: it is a statement of the entire sheaf, not a claim obtained only on the etale locus.

More generally, an O_L-linear twisted operator C:E->E(m) satisfying

    M(m) C = C(4) M

has a UNIQUE expression

    C=b0 Id+b1 M+b2 M^2,
    bj in H0(O_L(m-4j)),  j=0,1,2.

Uniqueness holds because the cubic minimal polynomial has degree three; existence is the line-bundle module argument. Since tr M=tr M^2=0 and tr Id=3, trace-free commuting operators have b0=0. Hence

    dim H0(Cent(M)(m))=sum_(j=0)^2 max(m-4j+1,0),
    dim H0(Cent_0(M)(m))=max(m-3,0)+max(m-7,0).

In particular there is no nonzero trace-free commuting operator of degree m<4; at m=4 the space is precisely C M; and below degree eight every trace-free commuting operator is a scalar polynomial multiple of M. No equivariance hypothesis is needed for these exclusions. If equivariance is imposed, it further restricts the coefficient sections to their compatible invariant parts.

Every local commuting endomorphism is self-adjoint, since it is a polynomial of degree at most two in the self-adjoint M with scalar coefficients and appropriate line twists. In characteristic zero, a commuting endomorphism that is also skew-adjoint must therefore be zero. In particular the Lie algebra sheaf of orthogonal frame transformations commuting with M vanishes, not merely its global sections. This excludes an infinitesimal orthogonal commuting frame ambiguity; it imposes no restriction on noncommuting frame changes or on the unresolved normal cochain.

## Contribution and scope

The Gram identity now supplies more than exceptional-fiber Jordan flags: it determines a smooth genus-ten spectral curve, a canonical spectral module with an ineffective theta characteristic, and the complete commuting-operator sheaf. In particular, low-degree trace-free corrections cannot centralize this Gram factor unless they vanish; degree-four ones are forced to its single line. These are usable structural constraints on proposed commuting corrections, not an assertion that the actual remaining cochain correction commutes with M.
