## Result

The single-line realization theorem in `PROOF.md` is correct under its stated geometric inputs: the marked ramification/source identities, the nonzero channel scalars, and fiberwise injectivity of the left monad arrow. The proof establishes exact six-dimensional unrestricted linewise image, exact five-dimensional vertex-zero linewise image, and realization of all three center-compatible coefficients by global vertex-zero skew operators. It does not establish simultaneous twelve-line interpolation or realization by the Gamma-equivariant source family.

This review independently checked the bundle arguments and formulas, not just dimension counts. No selected actual HM point, source instance, coefficient matrix, or rank was evaluated. No scan or large computation was performed.

## Checks

1. **Constant and moving Lagrangians.** The splitting of the Euler kernel follows directly from `H=K0+span(ev,ew)` and the equation `t v+s w=0` on its moving kernel. Its exterior square gives `E|L=N O+S O(-1)`. Both summands are isotropic, and nondegeneracy of the marked wedge pairing makes their mutual pairing perfect. After the determinant markings, this identifies `S` with `N*` by a constant pairing, without a rational coordinate factor.

2. **Nonzero regularized source and independence.** Applying the full left arrow to `(-s u/a_m,t z0/c_m)` gives `t^2 C-c s^2 B`, since the two VV terms cancel and `M(v)u=0`. The source is nonzero at every point of the parameter line, including both endpoints. Consequently its image is nowhere zero. In particular `C` and `B` are nonzero and independent: nonzero proportional vectors would produce a mixed zero of that quadratic pair over C. The resulting quotient `N O/O(-2)` is `O(2)+O^4`. On `span(C,B)`, the exact sequence is the basepoint-free pair `(t^2,-c s^2):O(-2)->O^2`, with quotient `O(2)`.

3. **Annihilation of the whole constant Lagrangian.** The mixed part of `N` is killed by the VV projection. Its VV part is exactly `A(v,U)=U tensor Lambda^2 ker(v)`, not merely the line generated by the special vector `u`. The cited ramification identity kills this entire image. Independently, the expression `F(r,w)=lambda_w(r^3)` and vanishing of the positive derivative at ramification yield `lambda_w(r^2 u')=0` for every `u'`, using the tangent derivative and Euler homogeneity. The row `ell` is nonzero because the nonzero functional on `U tensor Lambda^2 V` vanishes on `U tensor Lambda^2 ker(v)` and must therefore be nonzero on its `ev wedge ker(v)` complement.

4. **Endpoint factor and target degree.** The moving factor satisfies `lambda0(e wedge k)=s ell(k)`. The input contributes `s^(-1)Ytilde`, and the cubic evaluation functional contributes `s^3`; therefore the normalized output is exactly `ell(D Ytilde)`, of degree five, and the actual output is `s^3 ell(D Ytilde)`. This also defines a regular bundle map to `O(5)` at both endpoints.

5. **Exact kernel splitting.** The constant map `Sym^2 N*->N*`, obtained by inserting the nonzero vector represented by `ell`, is split surjective and has kernel dimension 15. The remaining row map is `N* O(3)->O(5)`, insertion of `t^2 C-c s^2 B`. It splits into four zero directions `O(3)^4` and the map `(t^2,-c s^2):O(3)^2->O(5)`. Its kernel is `O(1)`, generated by `(c s^2,t^2)`. Including the two invisible symplectic blocks gives exactly

       O(3)^19 + O(1) + O(4)^36 + O(5)^21.

   The kernel has rank 77 and degree 307, consistent with a rank-78, degree-312 source and quotient `O(5)`. Its global section dimension is `19*4+2+36*5+21*6=384`. All summands have zero first cohomology, giving the exact six-dimensional image in the 390-dimensional line-operator space.

6. **Pure-S global lifts.** These are explicit, not an unproved extension assumption: `i_x(u tensor ev wedge ew wedge k)=-u tensor e wedge k` for `k` in `K0`. The resulting global Koszul sections restrict to pure moving sections. Their symmetric transvections restrict solely to the symmetric `N->S` block. Cubic scalar multipliers therefore realize every such cubic block globally. A cubic divisible by `t` lifts using a positive linear form restricting to `t`; the lift vanishes along the entire vertex `Z`, not just its intersection with the chosen line. The identity `(t^2,s^2) H0(O(2))=H0(O(4))` then produces all `t H0(O(4))`. Conversely every vertex-zero operator has normalized output zero at `(0:1)`, so this five-dimensional image is exact.

7. **Center action, including the fixed source data.** There is no need to transform or reselect the fixed vectors `u,z0` to justify averaging. In the fixed ambient coordinates, `JY(t,s)=Y(t,s)` because the mixed sector is center-even, whereas `Y(t,-s)=-Y(t,s)`. Also `lambda0 J=-lambda0`. Thus for the actual functional `F_T`, direct substitution gives `F_(J.T)(t,s)=-F_T(t,-s)`. Dividing by `s^3` gives `q_(J.T)(t,s)=q_T(t,-s)`. Center averaging consequently projects exactly onto the even-in-s quintics `t s^4,t^3 s^2,t^5` and preserves vertex vanishing. This corrects the potentially misleading shortcut that would regard `Y` itself as unchanged under the coordinate substitution `s->-s`.

8. **Explicit right inverse.** With the displayed dual rows `a,b`, the row `r=f a+g b` satisfies `r(Ytilde)=alpha t s^4+beta t^3 s^2+gamma t^5`. Inserting `n_ell` into `theta tensor r+r tensor theta-r(n_ell)theta tensor theta` returns `r` exactly. All cubic coefficients are divisible by `t`, so the construction lifts by the preceding pure-S argument and remains unchanged after center averaging.
