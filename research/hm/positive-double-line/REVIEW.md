## 1. The ordinary squared-ideal resolution and its augmentation

The mapping-cone derivation is valid. The Koszul resolution of I and the tensor Koszul resolution of I/I² carry the stated comparison map. With the usual Koszul differential, its alternating coefficient inclusions can be given the signs (−1)^i in homological degree i, so the degree-zero map is the identity. Exterior multiplication supplies a splitting after division by a nonzero integer. The comparison maps are consequently split injections on coefficient spaces in characteristic zero. Their contractible constant summands can be cancelled equivariantly in the shifted cone. The remaining coefficient quotients are exactly those displayed in the proof.

Crucially, the cone resolves the kernel of the actual quotient I→I/I². Its augmentation is therefore the ordinary multiplication map Sym²V⊗O(−2)→I². The derivation does not substitute an unspecified map having the same coefficient characters. Sheafification and tensoring with the locally free B preserve exactness. ## 7. Rank-open and formal-lifting corollary

On this open set the kernel is locally free of rank 41−33=8 and the cokernel is canonically the constant quotient N₄/I. This is an algebraic openness argument; it does not select or evaluate an actual minor.

Thus the proposed lift k(t)=k−s(id_I+E(t))⁻¹M(t)k is killed by M(t) and reduces to k modulo that ideal.
