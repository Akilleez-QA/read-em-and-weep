# Source2 invariant-compatible algebra

The proof derives two new restrictions conditional on actual source2:

1. Every nontrivial invariant-Q-compatible idempotent is impossible.
   The unique rank-compatible block is ruled out by the actual second
   Bockstein, not by a dimension-only proxy.
2. The whole two-dimensional radical cannot remain Q-compatible, because
   its annihilator would be a surjective block with the same forbidden
   vanishing second Bockstein.

Thus D_Q=C·1⊕J_Q with J_Q²=0 and dim J_Q≤1. This leaves scalars or an
abstract dual-number algebra and does not exclude the source or its pair.
