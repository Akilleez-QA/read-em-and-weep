# Rank-four reverse-adjacent plateau exclusion

Conditional theorem: the actual maximal ordered rank-four plateau (3,2)
is impossible under the four retained actual-geometric inputs.

The order is fixed: E has rank 17 and Q* has rank 13. The proof does not
assume source Z, interchange these bundles, or enumerate rank-four
quotient splittings. It gives no generic-rank-five or original-repair claim.

## Contents

- `PROOF.md`: full geometric proof, intrinsic finite domain, and all fourteen endpoint-pair contradictions.
- `verify.py`, `RESULT.json`: author arithmetic checker and exact output.
- `INPUTS.json`, `inputs/`: four complete immutable public originals.
- `independent/`: whole-proof review, distinct arithmetic implementation, nine executable mutation controls, and frozen reviewed author files.
- `verify_publication.py`: byte authentication, portable replays, and cross-comparison of every endpoint and final character.
- `PUBLICATION.md`: reproduction commands and precise claim boundaries.
- `MANIFEST.json`: sizes, SHA256 hashes, and Git blob identities of every other publication file.

The proof forces endpoint degrees (-36,-26) or (-30,-30), with every
ramification defect zero. Complete rank-five endpoint domains contain
44, 15, and 4 splittings at degrees -36, -30, and -26. Of 401 ordered
endpoint pairs, fourteen satisfy two necessary exact Euler identities,
and all fourteen fail a cohomological character-positivity condition.

No actual coefficients, parameters, jets, matrices, or instance ranks are
evaluated. The written proof supplies the geometric steps. The checker
is universal arithmetic, not proof-assistant certification of those steps.
