# The unique scalar generates an actual twelve-dimensional rectangular module

Work over C with the actual normalized Horrocks--Mumford bundle F on
P4, B=End_0(F), G=SL2(F5), and actual coordinate space T=V direct-sum W.
Here V is the positive three-space, W the negative two-space,
A=Sym2 W, D=Sym4 W, P is the even four-dimensional irreducible,
and T6=Sym5 W. Put R=Sym(T). All actions below are actual ordinary
cohomological polynomial multiplication, not freely selected equivariant maps.

Let H be the unique scalar in H1(B(3))_+, and let C=R H be its actual
cyclic submodule. Then multiplication gives the canonical isomorphism

    [R / ((V)^2+(W)^2)] tensor H  -> C,                 (1)

where H is placed in B-twist degree3. The ideals (V)^2 and (W)^2 are
generated by ALL pure positive and ALL pure negative quadratics,
respectively. Thus C has exactly the following nonzero graded pieces:

    C3=H=1,
    C4=V H direct-sum W H = V+W,
    C5=V W H = T6.                                    (2)

Its Hilbert function is (1,5,6), and its total dimension is12.
No nonzero mixed quadratic kills H, whereas every pure quadratic does.
Here Q3 denotes the canonical invariant positive quadratic acting from
B-degree3 to B-degree5; the D channel below is D in Sym2 V=1+D.
In particular

    Q3(H)=0,  D H=0,  Sym2(V) H=0.                    (3)

The positive cyclic submodule is exactly 1 -> V -> 0, with annihilator
(V)^2 in Sym(V). The D descendant is ZERO, contrary to the proposed
nonvanishing route. Nevertheless the negative descendant W(VH) is
nonzero and injectively parameterized by W tensor V, not annihilated.

The proof also locates VH and VWH inside the ACTUAL primary--adjoint
submodule: they are its unique V in B-degree4 and its unique T6 in
B-degree5. It does not identify either with a chosen negative-pencil
summand on the basis of its character.

## 3. Locate the scalar using the actual injection, not a character copy

Equation (4) says L_j=U tensor Aprim_(j-3). Since K_j=H1(B(j-1)),
the relevant grading translation is

    B-degree3: K4 contains L4=P tensor Aprim_1;
    B-degree4: K5 contains L5=P tensor Aprim_2;
    B-degree5: K6 contains L6=P tensor Aprim_3.     (8)

In L4 the unique scalar is in P tensor P; (6)--(7) show that it
occurs exactly once. The injection in (4) is G-equivariant, so that
scalar has a nonzero scalar image in K4. PR1118 says the scalar of K4
is unique. Hence its image is exactly the given H, proving H subset L4
as an ACTUAL embedded subspace. This uses injectivity and uniqueness,
not a choice between repeated characters.

Every polynomial descendant of H stays in L because (4) is an
R-module map. This is essential: unrelated equivariant embeddings in
three degrees would not permit this conclusion. More explicitly the
maps in PR477 are connecting maps and Serre transposes of actual
evaluation-resolution maps. Multiplication by a homogeneous polynomial
commutes with those sheaf maps; duality transposes multiplication by
that same polynomial. Thus the original inclusion is R-linear in
all the displayed degrees, with its fixed determinant trivializations.

By (6), L6=P tensor W=W'+T6 is entirely physical negative. Both
Sym2 V and Sym2 W are positive, while H is positive. Their products
with H lie simultaneously in L6 and the positive part of K6, hence
are zero. This proves all pure quadratic annihilations in (3), as
well as Sym2 W H=0. It does NOT assert that the whole positive part
of H1(B(5)) vanishes; only this primary terminal subspace is negative.

## 4. Levelness forces the mixed product and both linear channels

PR1118 gives B0=VH as an actual embedded V in K5. All positive
coordinates kill B0 because Sym2 V H=0.

Suppose all negative coordinates killed B0 too. Then B0 would be a
nonzero subspace of Soc_R(K) in degree5. PR486 locates this socle in
degree6 only. The supposition is therefore impossible. Thus

    W tensor B0 -> K6                              (9)

is nonzero. Its domain is the irreducible T6 by (7), so it is
injective. Under the actual isomorphism V tensor H -> B0, (9) is
precisely mixed multiplication V tensor W tensor H -> K6. Define
Tprim=VWH to be its image.

If WH were zero, commutativity would give W(VH)=V(WH)=0,
contradicting (9). Thus W tensor H -> K5 is nonzero and injective
because W is irreducible. Write C0=WH. The actual subspaces B0 and C0
have different central parities and disjoint intersection. Therefore
T tensor H -> K5 is injective, with image B0 direct-sum C0.

There is additional actual location information. By (6)--(7),

    (L5)_+=P tensor (1+A)=V+2P+D,
    L6=W'+T6.                                     (10)

Since B0 is a nonzero V subspace of the first space and that space
has V multiplicity one, B0=(L5)_V. Likewise Tprim=(L6)_T6.
Both equalities are relative to the actual inclusion (4); no
negative projector or source splitting has been chosen.

## 5. No further relation: the complete actual annihilator

Let J=(V)^2+(W)^2 in R. Section3 gives J H=0, so multiplication
defines the canonical surjection in (1). The quotient R/J is the
tensor product

    [Sym(V)/(V)^2] tensor [Sym(W)/(W)^2].

Its graded pieces are C, V+W, V tensor W in degrees0,1,2.
Every monomial of total degree at least3 has at least two factors
from V or at least two from W, hence lies in J. There is no linear
relation in R/J and its quadratic relations are precisely
Sym2 V direct-sum Sym2 W.

The map in (1) is injective in relative degree0 because H is a line,
in degree1 by Section4, and in degree2 by the irreducible injection
(9). Its domain is zero in all higher degrees. Hence it is an
isomorphism and the full actual annihilator of H is exactly J.
Equivalently the socle of this cyclic submodule is its terminal
T6, fully compatible with the global levelness theorem.
