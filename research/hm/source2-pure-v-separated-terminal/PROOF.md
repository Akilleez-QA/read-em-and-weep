# Nonzero first-block projection forces twelve terminal descendants

## Theorem and scope

Retain the conditional actual source2 pure-V compatible-radical hypotheses.
Fix a genuine separated negative block N0, and assume the projection
of the canonical P_h in degree two into N0,2 is nonzero. Let F_infty be
the actual negative-coordinate module generated by im h. Then

    dim (F2,F3,F4,F5) = (9,14,16,12),
    F5 has character 2T,       F_d=0 for d>=6,
    length F_infty=51.

Its negative-coordinate socle is exactly

    I4=P in degree four, plus F5=2T in degree five.

Writing R=Sym W with the original cohomological grading, its minimal
G-equivariant free resolution is

    0 -> P tensor R(-6) plus 2T tensor R(-7)
      -> S tensor R(-3) plus V tensor R(-4)
           plus (W'+S) tensor R(-5) plus (V+P+D) tensor R(-6)
      -> (P+D) tensor R(-2) -> F_infty -> 0.             (0)

Its exact negative-coordinate annihilator is Ann_R(F_infty)=(W)^4.
The quartic relations relative to the generators, namely the R(-6)
term in (0), must not be discarded.

The actual projection F5->N0,5 is an isomorphism onto the full
T-isotypical component of N0,5=W'+2T. This does NOT identify F5 with
that separated subspace: F5 may be a graph into N_R,5=T. No residual
horizontal splitting, Q-stability, actual coefficient value, or existence
of this branch is asserted. The residual-projection branch is not treated.

The new input is an exact rank-twelve UNIVERSAL cubic descendant map,
not an actual HM instance rank.

## 6. Minimal resolution and exact annihilator

Shift the generators to degree zero, solely for Tor bookkeeping. The
representation-valued Hilbert series has coefficients

    F0=P+D, F1=W'+2T, F2=V+2P+D, F3=2T.

The equivariant Koszul numerator is (1-W t+t^2) times that series.
Its successive coefficients from degree zero through five are

    P+D, -S, -V, -(W'+S), -(V+D), 2T.                  (11)

The module is finite length over the two-variable polynomial ring R.
Its minimal free resolution has length two. The second Koszul homology
is the socle tensored with Lambda2 W in degree shifted by two.
Since Lambda2 W is trivial, Section5 gives

    Tor2 = P in degree4, plus 2T in degree5.           (12)

Minimal generators are precisely F0=P+D. Therefore (11) and (12)
determine Tor1, degree by degree, as

    S in degree1; V in degree2; W'+S in degree3;
    V+P+D in degree4.                                (13)

In particular the hidden P in degree4 is compulsory: its contribution
cancels the P in Tor2 in the Hilbert numerator, not in a minimal
resolution. Restoring the original generator degree two gives (0).
Minimality means every differential entry has positive degree; no
constant cancellation between the two R(-6) coefficient terms is
permitted; the component between equal shifts is zero. Equivariant
minimal resolutions exist by averaging the
choices of minimal homogeneous generators and syzygy lifts over G.
This specifies the exact free terms, not unexplained numerical Betti
ranks. The actual maps are the syzygy maps of the actual module.

Because F5 is the terminal nonzero degree and generators lie in degree2,
m^4 F_infty=0 for m=(W). For d=1,2,3, the degree-d annihilator is the
kernel of the equivariant action map

    Sym^d W -> Hom_C(F_infty,F_infty).

Each Sym^d W is irreducible (W,A,S respectively), and the map is nonzero:
its products with F2 span the nonzero F_(2+d). Thus each kernel is zero.
The scalar annihilator is zero since the module is nonzero. Finally the
annihilator is homogeneous: applying any sum of homogeneous operators
to a homogeneous vector separates the target degrees. It follows that
Ann_R(F_infty)=m^4 exactly. This is a negative-coordinate statement only,
not an assertion concerning positive-coordinate annihilation.
