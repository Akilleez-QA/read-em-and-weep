## Checks

1. The action on marked pair classes is well-defined without an honest Gamma-linearization of L0. Scalar differences between central identifications are absorbed by constant scalar isomorphisms between the differently marked objects. Composition discrepancies therefore disappear on classes, although not on the line-bundle groupoid. The forgetful transformation and its tangent sequence are equivariant.

2. The compatible parameter construction respects the given ring map R_G -> R_F. The cotangent injection has an equivariant complement by finite-group semisimplicity. Averaged lifts of that complement together with images of equivariant parameters for R_G form a full cotangent basis of R_F. The formal inverse function theorem consequently gives the stated relative product, rather than merely two unrelated linearizations. Setting the additional parameters to zero is indeed an equivariant section.

3. The conductor quotient is a difference of residue-field values. Its stabilizer character is therefore only the branch-exchange sign, not an arbitrary character. The actual paired-flag formula from PR610 and translation action from PR626 give exactly two length-five node orbits per eigenbasis pentad. An odd-order stabilizer cannot exchange two branches. Induction from its trivial fiber representation gives 12 trivial characters plus two copies of Reg0(U), dimension sixty. The independent character count yields pair tangent module 12 trivial characters plus 26 copies of Reg0(U), dimension 636.

4. Fixed class functors are represented by fixed-point quotients, not invariant rings. Compatible linearization promotes the character counts to all Artin orders. Each cyclic subgroup fixes 116 parameters, U fixes twelve, and distinct cyclic fixed germs meet exactly in the U-fixed germ. The decomposition into a common twelve-dimensional subspace and six 104-dimensional character sectors is consistent with all dimensions.

5. PR627's involution conjugates U by inversion and therefore preserves every pentad. Its affine action on labels has the form i -> a-i. Direct comparison with tau shows that translation by a-2i+d identifies the involution's image of each ordered branch pair with the reversed ordered pair. Hence it acts by -1 on each of the twelve orbit-invariant conductor lines. This proves P^Gamma=0 and, together with T^U=0, constant fixed pair-class functor for Gamma and already for the subgroup generated by U and the involution. No assumption that the full conductor representation is an unsigned permutation module enters this argument.

6. The scalar-defect obstruction belongs to H^2(H,A*) because base-preserving automorphisms act trivially on global units A*. The canonical decomposition of A* and the nilpotent logarithm reduce its infinitesimal part to finite-group cohomology of a complex vector space, which vanishes by averaging. Thus only the central obstruction survives. For cyclic order-five H the central degree-two group vanishes by fifth-root surjectivity. Fixing a central linearization kills the remaining infinitesimal ambiguity via degree-one vanishing, without eliminating scalar automorphisms. The distinction between existence, uniqueness up to isomorphism, and uniqueness of an isomorphism is correctly preserved.
