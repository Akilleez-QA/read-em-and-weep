# Complete-source adjoint remainder for the rational-residue route

## Result

The existing scalar-adjoint lemmas do not certify a retained test as zero. There is, however, an exact source-bound reduction that removes all explicit derivatives of the two raw scalar leaves. It expresses the SAME complete extracted scalar as a rational divergence plus a specified remainder. The remainder contains the entire scalar-free baseline and derivatives of the full outer/selector/current kernels. Nothing in the cited adjoint, closure, or selector identities makes that remainder zero.

This is a symbolic identity, not an obstruction value, a sparse-certificate existence claim, or permission to evaluate production. No physical model, source, owner, jet, polynomial matrix product, or retained coefficient was evaluated. Only inert source, ASTs, abstract mask labels, and invented free differential symbols are used.

## 2. Exact physical-to-rational derivative bridge

Let K=k(x_0,...,x_3), A=K[y_0,...,y_3]/(y_i^2-C_i(x_i)), in characteristic zero. Write b_m for the squarefree mask basis and pi for coefficient of b_15, WITHOUT another division by the deck product. The source has C_i=x_i^3-x_i, but the identity below is universal for any nonzero C_i of one coordinate. Let V_i x_j=delta_ij y_i and V_i y_j=delta_ij C_i'/2.

For EVERY Z in A,

    pi(V_i Z) = partial_i pi(y_i Z).                         (A)

Proof: in both sides only Z's mask 15 XOR 2^i contributes. That mask does not contain i. V_i differentiates its rational coefficient and adds y_i, with no basis-derivative contribution; multiplication by y_i also adds that bit without C_i. All other masks contribute zero to both sides. This is a linear identity on arbitrary rational coefficients, not a statement that pi commutes with V_i.

Consequently, for arbitrary a,s in A,

    pi(a V_i s) = partial_i pi(y_i a s) - pi((V_i a)s).       (B)

This follows from Leibniz and (A). It is valid with arbitrary moving-frame parity and arbitrary rational poles. It neither assumes that a is rational-x nor requires termwise Laurentness. It introduces no extra deck division. The derivative on the right is exactly the ordinary rational coordinate derivative required by the residue theorem. In particular, the apparent physical-versus-rational mismatch does not prevent integration by parts after complete extraction.

## 4. Explicit divergence and exact non-derivative remainder

Define four rational functions and one rational remainder:

    G_i = sum_(lambda,e: axis_e=i) pi[y_i p a_(lambda,e) s_e],

    B_p = pi[ p sum_lambda w_lambda ell_lambda(R_alg)
              - sum_(lambda,e) V_(axis_e)(p a_(lambda,e)) s_e ].    (D)

Then the exact identity in K is

    p F_spec = sum_i partial_i G_i + B_p.                         (E)

Proof: p is in K, so it pulls through pi; apply (B) to each of the eight signed E branches of each linear residual occurrence and sum. This proof never differentiates the scalar solver or assumes it commutes with a cover map. It includes the derivative of p, every outer weight, each surviving H/E/I/P factor inside ell, and the current itself. Expanded differentiation is unnecessary for the identity, but omitting any such contribution changes B_p.

The exact residual operator on raw scalar leaves is therefore

    s_e |-> -pi[V_(axis_e)(p a_(lambda,e)) s_e],

alongside the complete algebraic contribution. This is the sought non-derivative remainder: there is no explicit derivative acting on s_raw, although derivatives of transition/current/selector/weight functions remain. It is an adjoint reduction, not a proof of its cancellation.

If one uses an already normalized rational coefficient form C partial_i t from the earlier compiler, (B) becomes the familiar remainder -partial_i(p C)t. The earlier normal order on numerator P/D,

    C partial_i(P/D) = -(C partial_i D)/D^2 P + C/D partial_i P,

integrates by parts to -partial_i(C)P/D: the two D-derivative terms cancel exactly. This is consistent with the earlier compiler; that cancellation does NOT cancel the derivative of the outer/current kernel. For C(alpha partial+beta)(cP/D), the residual is

    [p C beta - partial_i(p C alpha)] cP/D,

with flux p C alpha cP/D. Thus conversion and denominator derivative terms are handled, not discarded.

## 5. Relation to the logarithmic certificate and exact stopping point

Under the existing geometric/same-source/finite-Laurent/trace hypotheses, the retained pairing equals the fixed iterated residue of pF_spec. Rational divergences have zero such residue, so (E) gives

    lambda_p(F_spec) = Res(B_p).

B_p itself is not asserted Laurent; neither are the individual G_i or circuit terms. The equality uses the fixed formal iterated-residue extension already supplied by the residue packet. No analytic boundary argument is needed.

A proof that B_p=0 is a sufficient complete rational-divergence certificate. More generally an exact rational-divergence certificate for B_p completes (E). Conversely, if the authenticated Laurent pF_spec has zero residue, its complete Laurent divergence from the published theorem, minus the displayed G, is a rational divergence for B_p. Thus, WITH the Laurent hypothesis on F_spec, the remaining problem is exactly whether this specific B_p is a rational divergence. This does not assert the false converse for arbitrary rational functions merely having zero iterated residue.

If F_spec=N/D, a successful complete certificate may be recast as the published polynomial logarithmic equation

    pN = div A - A(D)/D,       A(D) in (D).

The G_i in (D) need not have denominator D. They are explicit candidate fluxes for the general rational certificate, not evidence that their numerators are already elements of the denominator-D logarithmic module. Obtaining the complete denominator-one form uses the published existence theorem after vanishing is proved, or a new exact construction. No factorization or common-denominator support has been calculated here.

The known selector relation L d_End=0 and same-raw Cech closure govern different differentials and do not imply B_p=0. The old partial-h intertwining only handles the tensor scalar homotopy with its twist change; it does not annihilate V_i(p a). The older quartic-current formal survivor likewise excludes a universal cancellation of the isolated algebraic quartic sector, but is NOT evidence that this complete B_p is nonzero: E-derived remainders and actual source relations can still cancel it.

The precisely bounded next mathematical step is an identity for the COMPLETE summed B_p using actual pinned source relations, or a further exact rational/logarithmic divergence decomposition of B_p. A failure of a sparse ansatz remains inconclusive. Thirty test polynomials still give thirty unknown targets; this common operator construction can be reused without equating them.

## 6. Invented moving-kernel countercontrol

Let i=0, s=x_0, and a=y_1 y_2 y_3/(x_0 x_1 x_2 x_3). This is an invented algebra element, not a physical current, owner, or scalar source. Then

    pi(a V_0 s)=1/(x_0 x_1 x_2 x_3),
    pi(y_0 a s)=1/(x_1 x_2 x_3),
    -pi((V_0 a)s)=1/(x_0 x_1 x_2 x_3).

The flux derivative is zero and the remainder has formal residue one. Treating a as constant would falsely declare this expression a divergence with zero residue. No particular curve polynomial is needed because a has no y_0 factor. This demonstrates the precise discarded-kernel-derivative failure; it says nothing about the actual B_p value.
