# References and dependencies

## Repository prerequisite

- [Abstract trace-free evaluation and discriminant](../universal-cofactor-normal-form/PROOF.md), with its [audit](../universal-cofactor-normal-form/AUDIT.md).
- PROOF.md establishes all-corank dimensions and transversality. INCIDENCES.md Part C uses these results. Parts A–B retain the direct corank-two derivation and do not depend on Part C.

## Public mathematical inputs

- Winfried Bruns and Udo Vetter, [Determinantal Rings](https://www.home.uni-osnabrueck.de/wbruns/brunsw/detrings.pdf), Lecture Notes in Mathematics 1327 (1988): Corollary 5.17, printed p. 60 (PDF p. 68), for Cohen–Macaulayness; Theorem 6.3, printed p. 65 (PDF p. 73), for normality and integrality. Corollary 8.9, printed p. 98 (PDF p. 106), is the square-matrix Gorenstein cross-check. Used in INCIDENCES.md Part C, Section 5.
- János Kollár, [Canonical Models, Chapter 2](https://web.math.princeton.edu/~kollar/book/chap2.pdf): Proposition 3, printed p. 5; Lemma 12, printed p. 8; Corollary 16, printed p. 10. These supply smooth discrepancy positivity, crepant invariance, and the terminal criterion used for D₁ in PROOF.md Section 6.
- Mihnea Popa, [Hodge theory and singularities, Math 265 notes](https://people.math.harvard.edu/~mpopa/265/Math265-notes.pdf#page=11), Exercise 1.2.5(ii), printed p. 7 (PDF p. 11): Grauert–Riemenschneider vanishing, used for rational singularities of D₁ by the projection formula.

No geometric-module identification is a dependency. The claims of terminal and rational singularities in this packet are made for D₁; the all-corank incidence component proves normality, Cohen–Macaulayness, Gorensteinness and crepancy for each D_c without claiming those stronger extensions.
