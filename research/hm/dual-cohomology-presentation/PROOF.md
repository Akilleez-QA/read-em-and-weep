# The dual cohomology module has a complete quadratic–cubic presentation

Work over C with the normalized Horrocks–Mumford bundle F on P(V)=P^4, B=End_0(F), T=V*, S=Sym(T), and the actual graded cohomology modules

K_j=H^1(B(j-1)),   H_j=H^2(B(j-5)).

The module H has Hilbert function (2,10,20,20,10,2), supported in degrees zero through five. We prove that it is generated in degree zero, has socle only in degree five, and has no minimal defining relations after degree three. Its complete minimal presentation has two degree-zero generators, ten quadratic relations, and either zero or fifteen cubic relations. The remaining cubic scalar is not determined.

## 1. Actual multiplication and all-degree generation

Use the normalizer notation of PR479. The six graded pieces of H, in increasing degree, are

W', T tensor W', A* tensor Q_4, A tensor Q_4, V tensor W', W'.

Here W' has dimension two, Q_4 is the four-dimensional spin representation, and A and A* are the five-dimensional representations V_1 and V_3. In particular every displayed graded piece is irreducible for the normalizer. This does not identify Q_4 with the ordinary four-dimensional section representation.

PR479, section 1, supplies the actual surjections

T tensor H_0 -> H_1,   T tensor H_1 -> H_2,   T tensor H_2 -> H_3.

The first is an isomorphism. These maps are proved there using PR476, PR467 and PR469, respectively; no arbitrary equivariant map is substituted for multiplication.

Self-duality of B and Serre duality give a perfect pairing H_j tensor H_(5-j) -> C. It is compatible with multiplication: for x in T, the pairing of x a with b equals that of a with x b. Therefore multiplication T tensor H_3 -> H_4 is nonzero because its paired map T tensor H_1 -> H_2 is nonzero. Its target H_4 is irreducible, so it is onto. Likewise T tensor H_4 -> H_5 is nonzero by the paired map T tensor H_0 -> H_1, and is onto its irreducible target H_5.

Consequently H is generated by H_0=W' in every degree. Dualizing generation through the perfect pairing identifies the socle with the dual of H/S_+H, in complementary degree. Thus Soc(H)=H_5=W', and H is level of socle degree five and type two. This uses the actual compatible pairing, not only symmetry of its Hilbert function.

## 2. The low relation spaces and the self-dual Tor pairing

Let R=ker(S tensor W' -> H). Since the first multiplication is an isomorphism, R_1=0. PR479 identifies

R_2=A* tensor W',   dim R_2=10,

and its two adjacent Koszul identifications show

Tor_1(H,C)_3 = 0 or A tensor U',
Tor_2(H,C)_3 = 0 or A tensor U',

with the same alternative as the remaining cubic ghost of K. Both have dimension r in {0,15}. No choice between these alternatives is made here.

The perfect pairing of H pairs its polynomial Koszul complexes in complementary exterior degrees. Since dim T=5 and the complementary H degrees sum to five, it gives

Tor_i(H,C)_d = Tor_(5-i)(H,C)_(10-d)* tensor det T.          (1)

The normalizer lies in SL(V), so det T is trivial. In particular a minimal relation in degree j is dual to Tor_4(H,C)_(10-j). This formula is valid for the actual finite-length module, whether or not its resolution is pure.

## 3. Excluding quartic and quintic defining relations

For j=4 or 5, set t=5-j, so t=1 or 0. Tensor the exact sheaf coordinate Koszul complex by B(t). Its hypercohomology spectral sequence has

E_1^(-i,q)=Lambda^i T tensor H^q(B(t-i)),

and differential d_a of bidegree (a,1-a). Its q=1 row is the polynomial Koszul complex of K in internal degree t+1, while its q=2 row is that of H in internal degree t+5. Therefore

E_2^(-4,2)=Tor_4(H,C)_(t+5)=Tor_4(H,C)_(10-j).

There is no incoming differential d_a for a>=2 to this position: every such source has p=-4-a<-5, outside the five-variable complex. The only possible outgoing maps are d_2 to (-2,1) and d_3 to (-1,0).

The d_2 target is Tor_2(K,C)_(t+1), which is zero for t=0,1. To check the nontrivial endpoint explicitly, PR476 proves that K has one degree-one Euler relation epsilon and fifteen independent minimal quadratic relations. In internal degree two, the presentation of its relation module consists of T epsilon and chosen lifts of those fifteen minimal relations. The Euler map is injective and the chosen lifts are independent modulo T epsilon. Hence there is no degree-two syzygy among them, so Tor_2(K,C)_2=0. The degree-one group vanishes for degree reasons.

The d_3 target is a subquotient of E_1^(-1,0)=T tensor H^0(B(t-1)). For t=1, H^0(B)=0 because F is simple and B is its trace-free endomorphism bundle. For t=0, H^0(B(-1))=0 by stability. Thus this target is zero as well. Later outgoing differentials have negative q. The source survives to E_infinity, but the sheaf Koszul complex is exact and its hypercohomology is zero. It follows that

Tor_4(H,C)_6=Tor_4(H,C)_5=0.

Equation (1) consequently proves Tor_1(H,C)_4=Tor_1(H,C)_5=0. This argument does not require the later quartic relation theorem for K; it uses only K's established first two relation degrees.

## 4. Excluding every still higher defining relation

Since H is generated in degree zero and has no linear relations, the first module of a minimal resolution starts in degree two. Minimality over the standard graded polynomial ring forces each successive free generator to have degree at least one larger than the preceding minimum. In particular Tor_4(H,C)_d=0 for d<5.

For j>=6, the complementary degree 10-j is at most four, so (1) gives Tor_1(H,C)_j=0. Together with section 3, this proves the complete presentation bound

Tor_1(H,C)_j=0 unless j=2 or j=3.

Choose homogeneous cubic lifts of the possible space A tensor U' into R_3. Graded Nakayama then says that R is generated by its ten quadratic relations and these r cubic lifts. Equivalently H has a complete minimal presentation

S(-2) tensor (A* tensor W') + S(-3) tensor G
  -> S tensor W' -> H -> 0,

where G is either zero or A tensor U'. The plus sign denotes a direct sum of free modules in this presentation; their images inside the relation module need not be independent. The dimensions are (10,r) with r in {0,15}.

Thus the unresolved cubic channel is the only possible extra defining-relation channel of H. There is no independent quartic or higher relation dependency in this dual module. This does not resolve that cubic scalar, determine higher syzygies, or identify the original fixed-pair quotient of K.
