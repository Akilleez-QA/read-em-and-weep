## Endomorphism exclusion

The space E_d consists of all degree-d S-linear endomorphisms. Restriction is injective because H is generated by W=H_0. Normalizer conjugation preserves S-linearity, even though the normalizer acts nontrivially on S; hence E_d is an invariant subspace of Hom(W,H_d). It is unnecessary and would be incorrect to require each individual endomorphism to be equivariant. The scalar subspace is the image of S_d with kernel I_d. For d=0,1,2 this is the stated full scalar summand, since I_2=0 also forces I_0=I_1=0. Each ambient quotient by that summand is irreducible under the pinned representation inputs. Therefore a larger E_d must be the whole ambient Hom-space.

Assuming that whole-space alternative, every map w -> q A(w) extends. Applied to a quadratic relation r, this gives q[(id tensor A)r]=0 for every q in S_d. The bracket lies in H_2. A nonzero z in H_2 pairs nontrivially with some k in H_3. Writing k as a sum of cubic multiples of W shows that some cubic p has pz nonzero in H_5. Since every cubic is a sum of products from S_d S_(3-d), this excludes S_d z=0 for d=0,1,2. Thus L_2 is invariant under all coefficient-factor matrices A. Applying four matrix units to a tensor a tensor x + b tensor y gives ax=ay=bx=by=0. Generation then gives a,b in I_2=0. This contradicts a nonzero quadratic relation. The exclusion does not presume the exact Clebsch-Gordan embedding and does not evaluate any selected source-instance polynomial.

## Duality and degrees

For total tensor degree n, a balanced functional identifies H_i with a functional on H_(n-i). The perfect pairing of total degree 5 identifies the latter dual with H_(5-n+i). Thus the endomorphism degree is exactly 5-n. The balance relation and compatibility of b prove S-linearity, including zero graded components. Symmetry of b identifies tensor swap with adjoint, with no sign from polynomial degrees. Consequently symmetric tensors dualize to self-adjoint endomorphisms and exterior tensors to skew-adjoint ones.

Scalar multiplication is self-adjoint. Endomorphism rigidity for degrees 0,1,2 therefore gives symmetric-tensor dimensions 1,5,15 in degrees 5,4,3. The existing surjection to N has precisely these target dimensions, so it is injective in those degrees. PR490 supplies degrees 0,1,2. Generation in degree zero writes any tensor of degree n as a sum of p(w tensor w'); support through degree 5 kills these for n>5. Negative degrees also vanish. This establishes the global isomorphism, not merely equality of Hilbert functions.

## Endomorphism algebra consequence

The dual map of Phi sends q exactly to h -> qh, since its associated balanced functional is (h,k) -> b(qh,k). Hence the self-adjoint part is the actual central scalar copy of R. The exterior result gives the skew part M=(S/m^2) dual with grading reversed around 5: dimensions 5 and 1 in degrees 4 and 5. The duality is compatible with S-action: multiplying an endomorphism by x corresponds to precomposing the functional with multiplication by x in a tensor factor. Thus M has the claimed dual evaluation action and m^2 M=0.

There are no negative-degree endomorphisms because restriction to H_0 is injective, and no degree greater than 5 because the target of that restriction is zero. Products of skew endomorphisms therefore vanish, being of degree at least 8. Scalars commute with every S-linear map and preserve skew-adjointness. The direct sum R plus M is accordingly the commutative square-zero extension R semidirect M as claimed. Trivializing det W is the same normalization already used in PR490; without choosing it, retain the determinant line in the exterior summand.

The resulting symmetric, tensor and endomorphism Hilbert functions are respectively (3,15,25,15,5,1), (4,20,25,15,5,1), and (1,5,15,25,20,4). The last sums to 70. None of these statements resolves the remaining Betti parameters or implies higher-order lifting or algebraicity.
