## 2. A field of definition for the canonical cubic geometry

Put K=Q(zeta), where zeta is a primitive fifth root of unity. Choose the universal Schrödinger model A_K with basis e_i, i in F5, and operators

    g e_i=zeta^i e_i,       s e_i=e_(i+1).

Any irreducible complex five-dimensional Heisenberg representation with faithful central character is projectively equivalent to such a model, after replacing a generator or zeta if necessary. This follows by diagonalizing one generator and cycling its eigenspaces with the other. Consequently the given eigenline configuration and its complete cubic construction may be transported to this model without changing any tangent or obstruction assertion.

Every noncentral projective Heisenberg direction has an order-five lift over K. Its minimal polynomial divides X^5-1, which splits with distinct roots over K; its five eigenspaces are one-dimensional. Thus all thirty eigenlines, their reduced union Z_K in P(A_K), and its ideal are defined over K. In fact coefficientwise sigma:zeta->zeta^2 preserves Z_K: it sends g to g^2 and s to s, hence permutes the projective Heisenberg group and its six directions and eigenlines. No selected eigenvector is calculated here.

The permutation P e_i=e_(2i) also preserves Z_K, because it normalizes the projective Heisenberg group. Hence both sigma and P preserve the entire configuration, not merely an abstract character decomposition.

Form M_K=Bl_(Z_K)P(A_K), D_K=3H-E and S_K=H0(M_K,D_K). Base change to C gives the objects of PR616. The complete cubic morphism and its scheme-theoretic image V_K are defined over K. The normalization map, paired fibers, normalization quotient, line bundle L_K=O_(V_K)(1), differential-operator sequences and their section-evaluation maps are all functorial under the induced semilinear maps. Equivalently these constructions may be base-changed first and their natural maps then transported. Coherent cohomology commutes with the field extension K->C here.

In particular the intrinsic residual obstruction b, its filtration and the canonical map b_J are K-defined and functorial for J=P sigma. This uses only the canonical cubic construction from Z. It makes no assertion that an independently chosen HM bundle, the original fixed source, or any of its coefficients descends to Q or K. Nor does it select a splitting of the obstruction filtration or a branch ordering.

## 3. The semilinear operator and its exact conjugations

Use the intrinsic shear

    h e_i=zeta^(i^2)e_i.

It has order five and fixes the direction generated by g. Coefficientwise sigma gives

    sigma g sigma^(-1)=g^2,
    sigma s sigma^(-1)=s,
    sigma h sigma^(-1)=h^2.

The rational permutation P gives

    P g P^(-1)=g^3,
    P s P^(-1)=s^2,
    P h P^(-1)=h^4.

All exponents are in F5; for example P h P^(-1)e_i=zeta^((3i)^2)e_i=zeta^(4i^2)e_i. Therefore the sigma-semilinear operator J=P sigma satisfies exactly

    J g J^(-1)=g,
    J s J^(-1)=s^2,
    J h J^(-1)=h^3.                                  (1)

These equalities hold on the universal linear representation and consequently on all functorial geometric constructions. On the source and target obstruction spaces the Heisenberg center is trivial, so no scalar lift ambiguity enters their U-actions.

## 4. Character and shear transport

The following character-space calculations are performed over K; zero/nonzero status and dimensions are then preserved under extension to C. No extension of sigma to an automorphism of C is needed.

Fix a nontrivial U-character chi with kernel the g-direction. Write chi(g)=1 and chi(s)=zeta^k, with k nonzero. For v in a chi-isotypic space, (1) gives

    g Jv = J gv = Jv,
    s Jv = J s^3 v = sigma(zeta^(3k)) Jv = zeta^k Jv.

Thus J preserves the chosen chi-isotypic space itself. This is why it is necessary to include the permutation P: coefficientwise sigma alone changes the character.

If in addition h v=zeta^t v, then (1) gives

    h Jv = J h^2 v = sigma(zeta^(2t)) Jv = zeta^(4t) Jv. (2)

Hence J exchanges the weight1 and weight4 lines, and exchanges the weight2 and weight3 lines, in both domain and target of b_chi. These calculations use semilinearity at the scalar step; treating J as a complex-linear normalizer would give an incorrect conclusion.

The semilinear map is invertible. Naturality of b means b(Jv)=J(b(v)), with the corresponding J-actions on its source and target. Therefore b is zero on one line exactly when it is zero on its partner. We obtain

    beta_1=0 iff beta_4=0,
    beta_2=0 iff beta_3=0.                             (3)

The statement is about zero/nonzero intrinsic line maps, not equality of their scalar representatives in arbitrarily chosen bases. By the normalizer transport in PR616–617, it controls all twenty-four nontrivial character blocks.

Complex conjugation is not a substitute for this argument: it corresponds to the square -1=4 in F5 and does not supply the nonsquare transport used in (2). The proof depends on the explicit coefficient automorphism zeta->zeta^2 and the explicit character-returning permutation.

## 5. The remaining descent decision is all or nothing

PR618 gives q=2. Alternatively, PR616 proves q>=1, and the first equivalence in (3) already forces q=2. Thus both normalization channels are nonzero.

The source of the residual descent map is the sum of the two twenty-four-dimensional irreducibles of shear weights2 and3, and its nontrivial target J_nt is the matching sum. Each channel is either zero or an isomorphism on its corresponding irreducible, by PR617. The second equivalence in (3) forces them to have the same status. Accordingly

    b_J=0, or b_J:ker b_N -> J_nt is an isomorphism;
    rank b=48 or96;
    dim ker b=48 or0;
    dim T_embedded=147 or99.                          (4)

This is a new exclusion of the intermediate rank72 and tangent dimension123. It does not prove the residual map is nonzero. Symmetry over C alone allowed its two independent statuses; the additional semilinear symmetry of the actual canonical configuration rules out the unequal statuses.

## 6. Scope and next geometric dependency

Only one yes/no question remains: is the actual descent map nonzero? A geometric proof that any one of its intrinsic local discrepancies is nonzero would, by (3) and normalizer transport, prove it is an isomorphism and eliminate all non-projective first-order embedded deformation directions. Conversely an identically zero descent map would leave a forty-eight-dimensional first-order kernel. Neither is established here.

No selected map value, source-instance evaluation, determinant, or rank experiment is used. The theorem concerns first-order deformations of the canonical cubic image. It neither proves higher-order integrability in the147 alternative nor changes the full-adjoint, prescribed-source or algebraicity boundaries of PR1.
