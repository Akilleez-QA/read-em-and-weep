# Blowing up the universal Picard defect does not split the family

Status: authored structural proof; independently mathematically reviewed (PASS); not formally checked. No selected actual HM/source-instance evaluation or large computation is used.

Work over \(\mathbf C\). Complexes are cohomologically indexed. The argument is a structural continuation of the universal Picard residue complex; its geometric input is the actual residue factorization, not a model inferred from fiber dimensions.

## 1. Inputs and statement

The inputs are [the universal Picard family, PR583](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/ce046d10a03a609aee3d0125b3264dd36bb9d598/research/hm/universal-picard/PROOF.md) and [the defect and its exact Postnikov annihilator, PR584](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/df1e09bb456974ec43ab598709bd60d0378cfedf/research/hm/picard-defect-singularity/PROOF.md), with their pinned geometric dependencies.

Fix one residual node. Let

\[
 A=\mathbf C[t_z^{\pm1}:z\in Z],\quad P=\operatorname{Spec}A,
 \quad \delta=(t_2-t_1,t_3-t_1,t_4-t_1,t_5-t_1)^T,
 \quad I=(\delta_1,\ldots,\delta_4).
\]

The remaining twenty-five ratios are independent units. Set \(Z_0=V(I)\). The finite defect has the resolution

\[
 0\longrightarrow A\xrightarrow{\delta} A^4\longrightarrow K\longrightarrow0. \tag{1}
\]

Changing the harmless overall sign of the differential, PR584 identifies the actual node complex with

\[
 C=[E_A\xrightarrow{\delta\beta}A^4],\qquad
 0\longrightarrow\mathfrak m_A\longrightarrow E_A
 \xrightarrow{\beta}A\longrightarrow0. \tag{2}
\]

Here \(R=\mathbf C[[s,t]]/(st)\), \(\bar R=\mathbf C[[s]]\oplus\mathbf C[[t]]\), \(\mathfrak m=(s,t)\), \(T_A=R\otimes_{\mathbf C} A\), and

\[
 E_A=\{(f,g)\in\bar R\otimes A:f(0)=t_1g(0)\},\qquad \beta(f,g)=g(0).
\]

Thus \(E_A\simeq T_A\), with its specified residue map and inherited conductor inclusion. The complex is in degrees zero and one. Its Postnikov class is the splice of (2) with (1).

Let \(f:X=\operatorname{Bl}_{I}P\to P\), let \(D\subset X\) be the exceptional divisor, and write \(i:D\hookrightarrow X\). We prove:

1. \(X\) is smooth and \(D\simeq Z_0\times\mathbf P^3\).
2. There is a canonical exact sequence
   \[
   0\longrightarrow i_*\mathcal O_D(D)
   \longrightarrow f^*K\longrightarrow Q\longrightarrow0, \tag{3}
   \]
   where \(Q\) is a rank-three vector bundle and \(\mathcal O_D(D)\) is the relative tautological line \(\mathcal O_D(-1)\). The first term is exactly the parameter torsion of \(f^*K\).
3. \(Lf^*K\simeq f^*K\), but this does not remove the torsion in (3). The recomputed cohomology of \(Lf^*C\) is \(\mathfrak m_X\) in degree zero and \(f^*K\) in degree one.
4. On every blowup chart the actual complex contains a residue block \([E_B\xrightarrow{e\beta}B]\), whose Postnikov class has exact parameter annihilator \((e)\). Consequently the actual family is not cohomologically split on any parameter neighborhood meeting \(D\), even though its defect modulo torsion is locally free.

Statements about \(C\) are nodewise. They concern the ordinary cohomology splitting, not a multiplicative structure or any higher-order assertion.

## 2. Blowup charts and smoothness

Use \(\tau=t_1\) and \(\delta_j=t_{j+1}-t_1\) as coordinates. Then \(A\) is a localization of

\[
 \mathbf C[\tau^{\pm1},\delta_1,\ldots,\delta_4,
             t_z^{\pm1}\ (z\notin Z_q)]
\]

obtained by inverting the four \(\tau+\delta_j\). The center is the coordinate subspace \(\delta=0\). On the chart \(U_a\) where the homogeneous coordinate corresponding to \(\delta_a\) is nonzero, put

\[
 e=\delta_a,\quad u_a=1,\quad \delta_j=e u_j\ (j\ne a).
\]

Its coordinate ring is

\[
 B_a=\mathbf C[\tau^{\pm1},e,(u_j)_{j\ne a},
             t_z^{\pm1}\ (z\notin Z_q)]
       [ (\tau+e u_j)^{-1}:1\le j\le4]. \tag{4}
\]

In particular each chart is a smooth integral affine scheme, \(e\) is a nonzerodivisor, and \(D\cap U_a=V(e)\). The chart description follows either directly from the Rees algebra or from the incidence equations \(\delta_j u_a=\delta_a u_j\); (4) also shows there are no extra chart components. These charts prove smoothness without a general resolution theorem. Their exceptional charts glue to \(Z_0\times\mathbf P^3\), since the conormal basis given by the four \(\delta_j\) globally trivializes \(I/I^2\). Standard blowup conventions are recorded in [Stacks, blowing up](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01OF) and [the smooth-center lemma](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0FUT).

## 3. The global exceptional torsion line

The invertible ideal \(I\mathcal O_X=\mathcal O_X(-D)\) is generated by \(e\) on \(U_a\). Division of the pulled-back column \(\delta\) by this local equation defines a subbundle

\[
 L=\mathcal O_X(D)\hookrightarrow\mathcal O_X^4,
 \qquad 1/e\longmapsto v_a=(u_1,\ldots,u_4)^T. \tag{5}
\]

Indeed its \(a\)-th coordinate is one. If \(e_b=u_b e_a\) on an overlap, then \(v_b=v_a/u_b\), exactly the transition law of the frame \(1/e\). This verifies the twist and global compatibility directly. Define

\[
 Q=\mathcal O_X^4/L.
\]

It is locally free of rank three. Under the direction morphism \(X\to\mathbf P^3\), (5) is the pullback of the tautological subline, and \(Q\) is the corresponding tautological quotient bundle. In particular

\[
 L|_D=\mathcal O_D(D)=\mathcal O_D(-1),
\]

where the last notation is relative to \(D\to Z_0\).

The pulled-back map in (1) factors as

\[
 \mathcal O_X\hookrightarrow L\hookrightarrow\mathcal O_X^4,
\]

with the first arrow multiplication by \(e\) in the frame \(1/e\). It is injective. Taking successive quotients gives (3), because the divisor sequence tensored by \(L\) is

\[
 0\longrightarrow\mathcal O_X\longrightarrow L
 \longrightarrow i_*(L|_D)\longrightarrow0. \tag{6}
\]

Locally a complement to \(B_a v_a\) gives

\[
 f^*K|_{U_a}\simeq B_a^3\oplus B_a/(e). \tag{7}
\]

Since \(X\) is integral and \(Q\) is locally free, every parameter-torsion section of \(f^*K\) maps to zero in \(Q\). Conversely the kernel in (3) is killed locally by \(e\). Thus it is exactly the torsion subsheaf, and the torsion-free pullback is \(Q\). Formula (7) is only a local decomposition. In fact (3) does not split globally. Restrict to an exceptional fiber \(F\simeq\mathbf P^3\). Since \(Q\) is locally free, restriction preserves the injectivity of its kernel sequence, and the restricted presentation of \(f^*K\) has zero differential. The resulting sequence is

\[
 0\longrightarrow\mathcal O_F(-1)\longrightarrow\mathcal O_F^4
 \longrightarrow Q|_F\longrightarrow0.
\]

A splitting would give a retraction \(\mathcal O_F^4\to\mathcal O_F(-1)\), but every such map is zero because \(H^0(F,\mathcal O_F(-1))=0\). Thus the global extension of \(Q\) by the torsion line retains additional gluing information, even though it splits on each blowup chart.

## 4. Derived pullback versus ordinary pullback

All derived pullbacks in this proof act in the parameter direction; node modules carry the induced action of \(R\otimes\mathcal O_X\). The free resolution (1) computes \(Lf^*K\). Its pulled-back differential is injective on every chart, since it has a coordinate equal to the nonzerodivisor \(e\). Therefore

\[
 \operatorname{Tor}^{A}_j(K,B_a)=0\quad(j>0),
 \qquad Lf^*K\simeq f^*K. \tag{8}
\]

This is a statement about this particular module and morphism, not flatness of the blowup morphism.

Both terms of (2) are flat over \(A\): \(E_A\simeq R\otimes A\), and the other term is free. Hence the derived pullback of the actual complex is its termwise pullback

\[
 C_X=Lf^*C=[E_X\xrightarrow{\delta\beta}\mathcal O_X^4]. \tag{9}
\]

The residue extension remains exact after pullback, since its quotient \(A\) is free over \(A\). On (4), the column \(e v_a\) is injective, so

\[
 H^0(C_X)=\mathfrak m_X,\qquad H^1(C_X)=f^*K. \tag{10}
\]

Thus in this blowup the ordinary pullback of each original cohomology module happens to agree with the recomputed cohomology. Agreement (10) does not imply cohomological splitting.

## 5. The actual residue block survives

Globally the image of (9) lies in \(L\). Let

\[
 C_{\mathrm{res}}=[E_X\longrightarrow L],
\]

where locally the differential is \(e\beta\). There is a short exact sequence of complexes

\[
 0\longrightarrow C_{\mathrm{res}}\longrightarrow C_X
 \longrightarrow Q[-1]\longrightarrow0. \tag{11}
\]

On each chart the target sequence \(0\to L\to\mathcal O_X^4\to Q\to0\) splits and gives an isomorphism of complexes

\[
 C_X|_{U_a}\simeq [E_{B_a}\xrightarrow{e\beta}B_a]
                    \oplus B_a^3[-1]. \tag{12}
\]

This is an actual change of target basis applied to the given residue map. In particular it is not an arbitrary extension chosen to reproduce the cohomology. The residue block has the two-extension

\[
 0\longrightarrow\mathfrak m_B\longrightarrow E_B
 \xrightarrow{e\beta}B\longrightarrow B/(e)\longrightarrow0. \tag{13}
\]

Its degree-one cohomology is the local presentation of the global exceptional torsion line in (3).

## 6. Exact annihilator on the blowup

Fix a chart, write \(B=B_a\) and \(T_B=R\otimes B\), and let \(\eta\in\operatorname{Ext}^1_{T_B}(B,\mathfrak m_B)\) denote the residue extension. The nodal free resolution of the residue module, or the explicit calculation of PR584, gives

\[
 V:=\operatorname{Ext}^1_{T_B}(B,\mathfrak m_B)\simeq B^2,
 \qquad \eta=(\tau,1). \tag{14}
\]

For clarity, this does not use flatness of \(B\) over \(A\). Tensor the finite-rank free resolution of the \(R\)-module \(\mathbf C\) over \(\mathbf C\) with \(B\); its Hom complex computes (14). The two coordinates come from the two branches \(\mathfrak m\simeq R/(t)\oplus R/(s)\). Pulling the normalization residue extension back along \(b\mapsto(\tau b,b)\) gives the displayed class.

Apply \(\operatorname{Ext}_{T_B}(-,\mathfrak m_B)\) to

\[
 0\longrightarrow B\xrightarrow{e}B\longrightarrow B/(e)\longrightarrow0.
\]

The pertinent long exact sequence provides a canonical injection

\[
 V/eV\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Ext}^2_{T_B}(B/(e),\mathfrak m_B). \tag{15}
\]

The image of \(\eta\bmod e\) is the class \(\kappa_{\mathrm{res}}\) of (13), by Yoneda splicing. Its second coordinate is one, so

\[
 \operatorname{Ann}_B(\kappa_{\mathrm{res}})=(e). \tag{16}
\]

By (12), the Postnikov class of \(C_X|_{U_a}\) is \((\kappa_{\mathrm{res}},0)\) in the additive Ext decomposition for \(B/(e)\oplus B^3\). It too has exact annihilator \((e)\). These local statements glue: the annihilator ideal sheaf of the local Postnikov classes is precisely \(\mathcal O_X(-D)\), and their cyclic subsheaf is isomorphic to \(\mathcal O_D\). The latter is a statement about the cyclic obstruction class, not the torsion module, which is \(i_*\mathcal O_D(D)\).

At every parameter point of \(D\), (15) remains injective after localization and \((\tau,1)\bmod e\) remains nonzero. Hence no neighborhood of that point admits a splitting of the actual complex into its cohomology. Outside \(D\), \(e\) is invertible, (13) is quasi-isomorphic to \(\mathfrak m_B\), and (12) supplies a parameter-local splitting. This is an exact parameter-local assertion; it does not assert a global splitting on \(X\setminus D\).

## 7. Restriction to the exceptional divisor is a different operation

Write \(j:D\hookrightarrow X\). On \(D\), all four \(\delta_j\) vanish. Termwise restriction of the parameter-flat complex (9) therefore gives

\[
 Lj^*C_X=[E_D\xrightarrow{0}\mathcal O_D^4]
        \simeq E_D\oplus\mathcal O_D^4[-1]. \tag{17}
\]

Its degree-zero cohomology is \(E_D\), not \(\mathfrak m_D\). The exact comparison is the residue extension

\[
 0\longrightarrow\mathfrak m_D\longrightarrow E_D
 \xrightarrow{\beta}\mathcal O_D\longrightarrow0. \tag{18}
\]

Similarly, pulling back the locally free resolution
\(0\to\mathcal O_X\xrightarrow{\delta}\mathcal O_X^4\to f^*K\to0\)
shows

\[
 H^{-1}(Lj^*f^*K)=\operatorname{Tor}^{\mathcal O_X}_1(f^*K,\mathcal O_D)
    =\mathcal O_D,\qquad
 H^0(Lj^*f^*K)=\mathcal O_D^4. \tag{19}
\]

The Tor line in (19) is untwisted. Equivalently, the torsion line \(\mathcal O_D(D)\) contributes its tensor product with the conormal line \(\mathcal O_D(-D)\). This cancels the twist. Ordinary restriction of \(Q\), in contrast, gives the rank-three quotient in the tautological sequence

\[
 0\longrightarrow\mathcal O_D(-1)\longrightarrow\mathcal O_D^4
 \longrightarrow Q|_D\longrightarrow0. \tag{20}
\]

Thus the four operations must not be conflated: \(f^*K\) contains exceptional torsion; \(Lf^*K\) happens to coincide with it; its torsion-free quotient is \(Q\); and restriction of the actual derived family to \(D\) recomputes cohomology as in (17)–(19). In particular the new Postnikov class of (17) is zero. This does not contradict (16): derived restriction changes the degree-zero cohomology via Tor, and is not an exact functor on the two-extension with unchanged endpoints.

## 8. Conclusion

Blowing up principalizes the defect ideal and makes the torsion-free transform a vector bundle. It simultaneously concentrates the original relative obstruction into a Cartier residue block with exact exceptional annihilator. Smoothness of the modified parameter space and local freeness of \(Q\) therefore do not split the pulled-back Picard family.

The distinction is already visible in the chart normal form

\[
 f^*K=B^3\oplus B/(e),\qquad
 C_X=[E_B\xrightarrow{e\beta}B]\oplus B^3[-1].
\]

Discarding the torsion of the first expression does not constitute a quasi-isomorphism of the second. It removes genuine degree-one cohomology carrying the surviving two-extension. The conclusions remain conditional on the pinned actual Picard residue complex and concern this one-node structural continuation.
