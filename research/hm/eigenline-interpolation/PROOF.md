## 2. Cubics interpolate the twenty-five chirps

Let x_i be the linear coordinate dual to e_i. For a cubic monomial x_i x_j x_k, evaluation on the normalized vector v_(a,b) is the character

    chi_(Q,S)(a,b)=zeta^(aQ+bS),
    Q=i^2+j^2+k^2,  S=i+j+k.

For S=0 the seven unordered triples

    000, 014, 023, 113, 122, 244, 334

have respective Q-values

    0, 2, 3, 1, 4, 1, 4.

In particular every Q in F_5 occurs. This displayed arithmetic is an identity of index residues in the universal representation, not an evaluation of a selected geometric instance.

Shifting all three indices by t sends

    (Q,S) to (Q+2tS+3t^2, S+3t).

Starting with S=0, choose t=S'/3 for any desired S'. As Q varies over all residues, Q+3t^2 also varies over all residues. Hence every pair (Q',S') occurs for at least one cubic monomial. Choose one such monomial m_(Q,S) for each pair.

The character-orthogonality identity is

    sum_(a,b in F_5) chi_(Q,S)(a,b) chi_(-Q',-S')(a,b)
      = 25 if (Q,S)=(Q',S'), and 0 otherwise.

Indeed each sum factors into two geometric series, and a nontrivial fifth-root geometric series sums to zero. Therefore for arbitrary prescribed values f(a,b), the cubic

    C_f = (1/25) sum_(Q,S) [sum_(a,b) f(a,b) zeta^(-aQ-bS)] m_(Q,S)

satisfies C_f(v_(a,b))=f(a,b). Thus restriction of ambient cubics to the twenty-five chirps is surjective. No evaluation matrix or rank test is needed.

## 3. Quartics interpolate all thirty eigenlines

Define five universal quartics

    F_i = x_i^4 - product_(j != i) x_j.

The identities sum_(j in F_5) j=0 and sum_(j in F_5) j^2=0 hold in F_5. At a chirp the exponent of the second term of F_i is

    a sum_(j != i) j^2 + b sum_(j != i) j
      = -a i^2-b i = 4a i^2+4b i.

This is exactly the exponent of its first term, so F_i vanishes on every chirp. On the coordinate representatives, F_i(e_j)=delta_(i,j).

Given arbitrary target values f(a,b) at the chirps and c_i at the coordinate points, take the cubic C_f from Section 2. Since x_0(v_(a,b))=1, the quartic P=x_0 C_f has exactly the desired chirp values. Then

    P + sum_i (c_i-P(e_i)) F_i

has all thirty prescribed values. Equivalently the sheaf restriction map

    H^0(P(A),O(4)) -> H^0(O_Omega(4))

is onto. Normalizing representatives merely trivializes the thirty one-dimensional fibers and does not affect this assertion. In every degree d>=4 it remains onto: choose any linear form ell avoiding all thirty points and multiply the interpolating quartics by ell^(d-4), adjusting the prescribed values by its nonzero fiber values.

## 4. The actual nodes are this exact configuration

For clarity, the singular-node set of the primary surface is not inferred merely from a matching number of points. PR553 gives rank e_z=8 at every z in Omega. In a local trivialization of e, the defining equations of D_9(e) are its 10 by 10 maximal minors. The first derivative of each such minor is a sum of differentiated entries times 9 by 9 cofactors, all of which vanish at a rank-eight point. Thus all local defining equations have zero linear term there. The Zariski tangent space of D_9(e) at z is the full four-dimensional ambient tangent space.

By the scheme equality D_9(e)=S of PR573, this is also the tangent space of S. Since S has dimension two, each of the thirty distinct eigenlines is singular on S. PR573 says S has exactly thirty singular points, all transverse double points. Hence its node support Z equals Omega. Both supports are reduced; the normalization quotient T has length one at each point by PR578's normalization-sequence input. Thus the quartic interpolation theorem applies to the actual conductor support in its actual ambient coordinates.

## 5. Conductor surjectivity in degree seven

Write nu:X->S for normalization, H=nu^*O_S(1), and

    0 -> O_S -> nu_*O_X -> T -> 0,
    rho_n:H^0(X,nH) -> H^0(T(n)).

For each node z the coordinate functional rho_(3,z) is nonzero. Indeed complete cubic very ampleness separates the two distinct normalization points over z: a section can vanish at one and not at the other, so its branch difference is nonzero. The kernel of rho_(3,z) is therefore a proper linear subspace of H^0(X,3H). Over the infinite field C a finite union of proper linear subspaces cannot exhaust this vector space. Choose one section s outside the thirty kernels. Its difference rho_3(s) is nonzero at every node. This is an existence argument, not a chosen-coefficient search.

Multiplication of a normalization section by a pulled-back ambient polynomial obeys

    rho_(3+d)((nu^*p)s) = (p|_Z) rho_3(s).

This follows either from O_S-linearity of the normalization quotient or by taking the difference of the branch values: p has the same residue on both branches. Since T is reduced of length one at each node and rho_3(s) has no zero coordinate, multiplication by rho_3(s) gives an isomorphism

    H^0(O_Z(d)) -> H^0(T(3+d)).

For d=4, the ambient restriction onto the left side is surjective by Section 3. Hence rho_7 is surjective. Multiplication by a linear form avoiding Z, or the degree-d interpolation assertion above, then gives

    rank(rho_n)=30 for every n>=7.

For d=3, Section 2 supplies arbitrary cubic values on the twenty-five chirps, which are a subset of Z. The same product identity shows that rho_6 projects surjectively onto the corresponding twenty-five summands of T(6). Therefore

    rank(rho_6)>=25.

Nothing here asserts rank(rho_6)=30: the other five coordinates are not independently corrected by a cubic in this argument.

## 6. Cohomology and regularity consequences

For n>=1, the normalization long exact sequence and H^1(X,nH)=0 give

    H^1(S,O_S(n)) = coker(rho_n).

Consequently

    h^1(S,O_S(6))<=5,
    H^1(S,O_S(n))=0 for every n>=7.

The primitive central-character divisibility already proved in PR578 further implies rank(rho_6) is either 25 or 30, so the degree-six defect is either 5 or 0. The argument does not choose between them.

Also H^2(S,O_S(6))=H^2(X,6H), since T is zero-dimensional and nu finite. Serre duality identifies the latter with H^0(X,K_X-6H)^*. The fiber class F is nef, and

    (K_X-6H).F = (3F-6(I+2F)).F = -30.

No effective divisor can have negative intersection with a nef divisor, so H^0(X,K_X-6H)=0. This establishes the needed degree-six H^2 vanishing directly from the pinned geometry.

It follows that O_S, regarded as a coherent sheaf on P^4, is 8-regular: H^1(O_S(7)) and H^2(O_S(6)) vanish, and cohomology in degrees above two vanishes by support dimension. In particular complete-section multiplication

    H^0(O_S(n)) tensor H^0(P^4,O(1)) -> H^0(O_S(n+1))

is surjective for n>=8 by Castelnuovo-Mumford regularity.

## 7. Scope and verification limits

The new universal representation result is quartic interpolation on all thirty eigenlines, together with cubic interpolation on twenty-five of them. Combined with the pinned actual-geometry inputs, this improves the prior n>=9 conductor-defect vanishing to n>=7 and improves sheaf regularity from 10 to 8. It also improves the degree-six conductor rank lower bound to 25.

This is not projective normality, ambient polynomial surjectivity onto all sections of O_S(n), regularity or generation of I_S, full degree-six conductor surjectivity, a full-adjoint or prescribed-source membership theorem, higher lifting, or algebraicity. No degree-two conductor generation or relative Pfaffian claim is used. The proof is universal symbolic mathematics relative to the explicitly pinned geometric inputs, not formal proof-assistant verification.
