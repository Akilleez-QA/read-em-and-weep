# Preserve the CM obstruction under finite étale pullback

For every finite isogeny q:Y→X over C and every M in Pic⁰(Y), Hom(q*F,q*σ*F tensor M)=0. The conditional projective obstruction therefore persists on every connected finite étale cover; a CM lift upstairs is excluded when a compatible automorphism is specified.

## Unresolved boundary

Untwisted Hom vanishing alone is insufficient: all Pic⁰ twists are essential. Both pullbacks use the same finite cover. No existence of a lift of σ on an arbitrary cover is asserted. Arbitrary or ramified base change, rational/meromorphic maps and unrelated automorphisms are outside scope.
