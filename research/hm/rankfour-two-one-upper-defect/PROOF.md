## 4. Torsion characters and the exact K0 identities

Let Dn denote the special orbit of size n. Its stabilizer in G is cyclic of order120/n. The center acts trivially on Ti. At length12 or20, the sheaf has length one at each point and a center-even stabilizer character. We can represent these factors as

    O_D12(r), r=0,2,4,6,8;
    O_D20(r), r=0,2,4.

At length24, support must be the size12 orbit, with local length2. The module need not be reduced or a direct sum of two reduced factors. Filter it by equivariant simple composition factors. Its K0 class is one of the15 unordered pairs

    O_D12(r)+O_D12(s), 0<=r<=s<=8, r,s even.

This is an overlist of composition-factor classes, not a realization or splitting assertion. It includes every nonreduced local length-two module, and retains even factors that a later source-fiber compatibility test might exclude. No descriptor is interpreted as an actual direct-sum local torsion module.

Because G has no nontrivial characters, the equation of Dn is invariant. For every integer t,

    τ(n,r;t):=χ(O_Dn(r+t))
             =χ(O(r+t))-χ(O(r+t-n)).        (4.1)

The twist r is taken modulo10 for n12 and modulo6 for n20. The checker also tests the analogous order4 periodicity for n30, without using it to narrow the slice.

From the two actual graded sequences, in equivariant K0(T),

    [E]-[F0]=[B(-2)]+[B]-[T1],
    [Q]-[D0*]=[B(2)]+[T2(2)].              (4.2)

Shift the second identity by -2 and -4 and eliminate B:

    [F0]-[D0*(-2)]-[D0*(-4)]
      =[E]-[Q(-2)]-[Q(-4)]+[T1]+[T2]+[T2(-2)].   (4.3)

Equivalently, using equivariant Serre duality,

    χF0(t)=χE(t)-χB(t)-χB(t-2)+χT1(t),
    χD0(t)=χQ*(t)+χB(-t)+χT2(-t).          (4.4)

The upper argument is -t, not -t-4. T2 has not been omitted or assigned the wrong sign under duality. Two adjacent Euler characters at t=0,1 determine these K0 equalities: K0^G(P1) is free over R(G) on O,O(-1), and χ0,χ1 recover the two coefficients. Equivalently the tautological recurrence χ(t+1)=Wχ(t)-χ(t-1) propagates every equality. The five final tuples are additionally checked at every t from -12 to12, including the full torsion-retaining elimination (4.3).

## 5. Valid cohomology inequalities and finite domains

Every inequality in this proof is coefficientwise domination of genuine G-representations. Semisimplicity is used only after an actual cohomology map is proved surjective.

First, E->B is a bundle quotient, so

    H1(B(1)) <= W'+3T6.                    (5.1)

Second, for every t<=1 the persistence vanishing gives H0(F1(t))=0. The exact sequence 0->F1->E->B->0 therefore gives

    χB(t)-χE(t)=H1(F1(t)).

The quotient F1->L1 followed by L1->B(-2), whose cokernel is torsion, induces surjections on H1. Hence

    χB(t)-χE(t) >= H1(B(t-2)) for all t<=1. (5.2)

The finite checker uses t=-2,-1,0,1. Negative twists require no new persistence theorem, since all slopes of F1 are at most -2.

Third, 0->D0->Q*->L3*->0 and the dual of the upper adjacent sequence give surjections

    H1(Q*(1)) -> H1(L3*(1)) -> H1(B*(-1)).

Serre duality gives H1(B*(-1))=H0(B(-1))*; all irreducibles are self-dual. Thus

    H0(B(-1)) <= W+2S4+3T6.                (5.3)

Finally H0(D0(2))=0, so (4.4) at t2 implies that

    -χB(-2)-χT2(-2)-χQ*(2)=H1(D0(2))       (5.4)

is genuine. No stronger domination by H1(Q*(2)) is claimed. In particular the invalid shortcut H1(D0(t))>=H1(Q*(t)) for an arbitrary subbundle is NOT used: the quotient can have nonzero H1.

For a block R O(-j) in rank4 B, (5.1) bounds j<=22 when j>=3. Indeed dimR*(j-2)<=20, so j<=22. Exhausting this short character interval with dimR<=4 and physical parity leaves exactly

    W'O(-3), PO(-4), VO(-4), W'O(-5), O(-8), WO(-9), O(-10).

Thus every degree of B is at least -10. Since B has rank4 and degree d, every degree is at most d+30. Each endpoint has rank9, degree a or b, and cap -3, so its lower degree is a+24 or b+24. These are finite domains derived from the actual inequalities, not imported historical lists.

The verifier constructs each domain twice: once as multisets of irreducible coefficient blocks and once as ordinary degree partitions followed by independent decompositions of each equal-degree multiplicity. It requires exact equality of the two resulting domains. It then applies (5.1)-(5.4) and the two-twist identities (4.4), keeping all torsion descriptors in the stated composition-factor overlists.

## 6. Torsion-safe horizontal subbundle exclusions

The endpoint derivative F0->L1(-2) factors through the majorant B(-4). The reversed endpoint D0 differentiates into L3*(-2), which injects into B*(-4) by dualizing the upper adjacent sequence. These are honest injections of sheaves, valid even with nonzero T1,T2.

Let A be either endpoint and S an equivariant direct summand in an equivariant Birkhoff splitting of A. If both

    Hom_G(S, outer majorant)=0,
    Hom_G(S,(A/S)(-2))=0,                  (6.1)

then S is horizontal in the actual constant ambient bundle. To see this, the first vanishing kills the composition of dS with the quotient by A. Thus dS lies in A(-2). Modulo S(-2), differentiation is O-linear; the second vanishing makes it zero. Therefore dS lies in S(-2). The saturated derivative-invariant subbundle theorem makes S constant. But all degrees of A, hence of S, are negative, a contradiction.

This does NOT treat a chosen Birkhoff projection as horizontal and does NOT posit a splitting of the ambient connection. The verifier needs only single irreducible-block choices for S. It computes each Hom dimension exactly via

    dim Hom_G(R O(e), R' O(f))
      = multiplicity of R in R' Sym^(f-e)W if f>=e,
      =0 otherwise.

Every discarded tuple has a printed direct witness with both dimensions zero in RESULT.json.

## 9. Complete rank-one flag excludes the fifth row

Here δ1=0, so L1=B(-2). The endpoint derivative lands in

    L1(-2)=B(-4)=O(-12)+W'O(-7)+O(2).

Every map from F0=VO(-4)+T6O(-3) to the first two summands vanishes by ordinary degree. Thus the generic second fundamental rank of F0 is at most one. It cannot be zero: F0 is saturated in the constant ambient, and a horizontal saturated subbundle is constant, contradicting its negative degree. Its rank is exactly one.

Let U be the minimal constant span of F0 in H and n=dimU. This U is G-invariant, since the span is intrinsic. Apply the pinned complete rank-one theorem inside U O. Its two nondegeneracy conditions hold: H0(F0)=0, and minimality of U excludes a proper constant subspace containing F0. Equivalently a nonzero constant covector annihilating F0 would contradict this minimality.

For clarity, the resulting complete flag has no skipped ranks. Every positive-rank backward stage lies inside F0 and cannot become horizontal because H0(F0)=0. Every proper forward stage cannot become horizontal before reaching U because of minimality. Each rank increment and decrement therefore remains one. All stages are saturated and all adjacent line derivatives are regular and nonzero; possible ramification contributes effective invariant divisors, never poles.

Write this flag as C0<...<Cn=U O with rankCi=i and C9=F0. Its first and second forward stages satisfy

    C10 subset F1, C11 subset F2=E.         (9.1)

Indeed dF0 lies in F1(-2); saturation in the ambient preserves the containment. Next dC10 lies in dF1, hence F2(-2), and saturation again preserves containment. If n-9 were1 or2, the constant endpoint U O would lie in F1 or E, both of which have H0=0. Hence n>=12.

The universal rank-one degree bound gives

    30 >= 9(n-9).

Thus n=12. Put εj for the degree of the invariant zero divisor of the j-th adjacent line derivative of the complete flag. Each εj lies in the invariant-divisor semigroup and is zero or at least12. The exact complete-flag identity, with rank9 and complementary rank3, gives

    12(30-9*3)=36
      =Σ(j=1..11) wj εj,
    (w1,...,w11)=(3,6,9,12,15,18,21,24,27,18,9).

Every term is nonnegative. Any positive term other than j1 is at least72; at j1 the only allowed contribution at most36 is3*12. Therefore ε1=12 and every other εj=0.

If dj=deg(Cj/C(j-1)), the recurrence d(j+1)=dj+2+εj and Σdj=0 now force

    (d1,...,d12)=(-22,-8,-6,-4,-2,0,2,4,6,8,10,12).

Consequently rankC11=11 and degC11=-12. But (9.1) and H0(E(1))=0 imply H0(C11(1))=0, so every ordinary degree of C11 is at most -2 and degC11<=-22. This contradicts -12.

As an optional consistency check, the complete flag determines the constant-span character

    [U]=Σj χ(O(dj))=A+D+P.

Indeed equivariant line bundles have unique linearization because G is perfect. The complement H-U=A+D+P+2V is genuine, so this character alone is not a contradiction and is not used as one.

All five tuples are impossible, proving the theorem of Section1.
