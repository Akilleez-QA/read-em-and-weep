# One cubic operation controls the lower and upper mixed channels

## Result

With the audited HM conventions, let V=U1, Z=U0, J_r=H²(End H(-r)), A=V1, and S=Sym³W. There is a canonical ordered operation f=m111:V³→J3. It obeys outer reversal and cyclic zero. Its symmetry-eligible N-equivariant parameter space is one-dimensional:

    Hom_N(Schur_(2,1)V,J3)=C,   J3=A*⊗S.

The actual mixed operations satisfy

    m011(ℓu,v,w)=ℓ f(u,v,w),
    m001(ℓu,mv,w)=ℓm f(u,v,w),

with corresponding identities in every ordering. Here ℓ,m are genuine linear homogeneous sections and their action on J is genuine coherent-cohomology module multiplication. Since Z is generated by linear multiples of V, the formerly four- and eight-dimensional eligible families are constrained to images of this common one-dimensional space. These images can be zero. No actual cubic scalar or target-multiplication nonvanishing is established.

## 1. Canonical operation and scalar linearity

Use the twist-graded Dolbeault DGA C_r=A^(0,*)(End H(-r)). The audited ordered product V·V→J2 vanishes. For closed degree-one representatives a,b,c in C1, choose dh_ab=ab and dh_bc=bc in C2. Then

    f(a,b,c)=[a h_bc+h_ab c]∈J3.

Its indeterminacy is a H¹(End H(-2))+H¹(End H(-2))c=0. The usual representative-boundary correction identities respect twists. Thus f is canonical, trilinear and natural.

A global homogeneous polynomial is a closed degree-zero central scalar in this graded collection. Replacing the three inputs by p a,q b,r c, use primitives pq h_ab and qr h_bc. The resulting cocycle is pqr times the original. Whenever both relevant canonical operations are defined, uniqueness proves equality of their cohomology classes. This proves the displayed formulas without choosing a module-linear contraction or an A-infinity model, and without any algebra splitting of the coefficient cover. Degrees of ℓ,m are one, lowering the negative twist by one per multiplication.

The audited Euler/KS calculation identifies V*⊗V→Z with the quotient by the scalar identity. Consequently the displayed formulas determine every input of the mixed operations by linearity.

## 2. Reversal and cyclic zero

The canonical generation filtration on B=σ_*O_(P1)^4 has associated graded coefficient algebra C=R/S_+R. This is standard graded with Hilbert function (1,11,11,1). Therefore C1³→C3 is surjective and nonzero. Every triple of upstairs classes of filtration at most one admits adjacent primitives in filtration at most two. Its degree-three leading Massey symbol is

    abc ⊗ f(u,v,w),    a,b,c∈C1.

This follows by projecting a filtered defining system and comparing with the factorized downstairs system; H¹(End H(-2))=0 eliminates any primitive discrepancy. As in the canonical-Massey proof, the quotient of V³ by these argument-permutation relations is one Schur_(2,1)V. It has dimension forty. The quotient need not carry a residual action of every argument permutation.

## 3. Target and eligible multiplicity

Equivariant trace identifies (End H)* with End H. For N⊂SL(V), the equivariant canonical bundle is O(-5) without a determinant character. Serre duality thus gives J3=J2*. The audited J2=A⊗S, and S is self-dual, so J3=A*⊗S.

    V⊗Λ²V = Schur_(2,1)V ⊕ Λ³V.

The G-central involution acts as -1 on S and +1 on W′⊗W. Hence Hom_G(W′⊗W,S)=0, while S is irreducible. Heisenberg-isotypic tensor-Hom adjunction proves the asserted one-dimensional Hom space. This argument needs neither a decomposition nor an irreducibility assertion for W′⊗W.

## 4. Euler balancing does not kill this line

Let f be any N-equivariant element of this cubic Hom space, and μ:V*⊗J3→J2 the actual cohomology multiplication. For each of the three input slots, contracting that slot with the invariant Euler tensor Σ_i x_i⊗e_i defines an N-map

    V⊗V→J2.

But V²=A⊗(Sym²W′⊕W) and J2=A⊗S, so this Hom space is zero. Therefore every Euler contraction Σ_i x_i f(...,e_i,...) vanishes. The formula with one multiplied input consequently descends through V*⊗V→Z in every slot. Multiplying these identities proves descent in two multiplied slots as well. There are no further degree-one kernel relations because the Euler/KS quotient has exactly the scalar kernel.

These balancing identities are universal equivariance consequences. They do not identify μ with a freely chosen bilinear map or prove it nonzero. Nor do they prove that a nonzero cubic scalar survives one or two coordinate multiplications. Accordingly, the rigorous conclusion is a common at-most-one-dimensional family for both mixed channels, not an asserted nonzero one-dimensional image in either older parameter space.
