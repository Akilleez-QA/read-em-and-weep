# Independent audit: coordinate-component multiplicities

Verdict: PASS for all six sections, with no required corrections.

## Rank strata and local scheme structure

For each omitted slot, trilinearity gives the actual scheme equation f=L_i(b)u. An invertible five-minor supplies a local left inverse, so the full ideal equals the five omitted-slot coordinates on that ambient open. This is stronger than a tangent-space or reduced-support assertion.

For independent complementary inputs, the two-plane theorem excludes every nonzero kernel vector in their span. Thus kernel dimension is at most three, and every nonzero kernel vector gives an independent zero triple. On each reduced exact-rank stratum the kernel is a vector bundle by minor charts, so its punctured total space has dimension dim(stratum)+kernel dimension. The imported bound dim Z^(3)≤9 therefore yields all stated bounds 9−k. Taking the union of exact-rank strata preserves these inequalities and proves nonemptiness of the injectivity open.

For nonzero proportional complementary inputs, outer reversal and cyclic zero reduce every omitted-slot ordering to a nonzero scalar multiple of the repeated-input map, with the middle-slot factor −2. Structural nonvanishing gives its exact one-dimensional kernel. If either complementary input is zero, the map vanishes identically. Thus the global defect bounds are 8,7,6,5,5 for kernel thresholds 1 through 5, and kernel dimension four never occurs. The last two loci are the zero-input axes set-theoretically, as correctly qualified.

## Singular and component-crossing statements

At a coordinate-plane point all differential blocks other than the omitted-slot block vanish, giving tangent dimension 10+dim kernel. At every closed point there, the coordinate ten-plane gives local dimension at least ten and the global dimension theorem gives at most ten. Hence the exact rank-drop/singularity criterion follows. The five-minor scheme is indeed the restriction of Fitt_10(Omega_Z); the proof carefully avoids identifying that Fitting scheme with a universal singular scheme on the whole non-equidimensional space.

The new component-crossing claim is also correct. A nonzero kernel vector yields an algebraic line through the coordinate-plane point, contained in Z but not in that plane. Its irreducibility places it in another irreducible component. Conversely a full ambient neighborhood equal to the coordinate plane cannot meet a distinct component, since such an intersection would be a nonempty open subset of that component contained in the plane. For independent complementary inputs, the line's nonzero points are independent triples, establishing the stronger degeneration statement. None of this implies reducedness of the defect scheme.

## Multiplicity and Hilbert consequences

The ambient ideal equality on a nonempty open proves that the local ring at the generic point of each coordinate component is its function field and has length one. Since these are exactly the three dimension-ten components, the top-dimensional cycle is their sum. Projectivizing gives three degree-one P^9 components, hence top degree three and Hilbert polynomial leading term 3n^9/9!.

For the standard graded ring, localization at the vertex does not change the finite-length quotients by powers of the homogeneous maximal ideal. Summing graded dimensions therefore gives leading length term 3N^10/10!, proving Hilbert–Samuel multiplicity three. Lower-dimensional components and embedded structure do not alter this leading coefficient. No Cohen–Macaulayness or equidimensionality assumption is needed or claimed.
