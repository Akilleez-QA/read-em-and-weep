# Scope and successive sharpening

The earlier propositions remain valid at their stated strength; the final component theorem supplies stronger conclusions, not contradictory alternative claims.

- The initial10–14 dimension interval is sharpened to exactly10. The twenty minimal cubic generators and failure of local complete intersection remain valid.
- Fixed two-plane generic double multiplicity belongs to that restriction, not to the full cubic scheme. The AP classification remains restricted to those fixed three-planes.
- The projective-three-space constant-rank obstruction and the totally-isotropic four-kernel exclusion are retained proven exclusions.
- The initial possible sources for a ten-dimensional independent component were kernel dimension4 or a singular three-dimensional net. The net-locus Chern relation bounds the latter base by4. The empty image-line incidence then bounds the entire four-kernel locus by4. Consequently no independent ten-dimensional component remains.
- Reducible-determinant normal crossings and constant-rank-net frame geometry remain meaningful fixed-kernel theorems; they are not discarded because their strata cannot give a top-dimensional component.
- The degree27 divisibility statement is a necessary cycle constraint for a four-dimensional four-kernel subvariety, not an existence claim.
- Final conclusions: exactly three ten-dimensional coordinate components, each generically of multiplicity1; at least one smaller component; top-dimensional degree3 and origin Hilbert–Samuel multiplicity3. Global reducedness, exact smaller-component dimensions/counts, and their multiplicities are not determined.

The reduced repeated-input blowup is not a replacement for the original nonreduced extension functor. Its doubled exceptional total transform and the distinction between birationally small and semismall remain part of the separate slice theorem.
