# Actual cubic application of four kernel empty incidence

The preceding universal arguments are not duplicated. The universal dimension/Chern arguments are proved in unit03, using units01–02. Unit09 verifies their hypotheses for actual cubic kernels. The following retained conclusions include the earlier frontier exclusions and their final sharpening.

## 5. Consequences for the full cubic zero locus

The imported full-cubic-locus-dimension proof bounds all independent-triple strata over kernel dimensions zero, one, or two by nine. Over kernel dimension three with nonzero restricted determinant, its spectral fibers have dimension at most one and its base has dimension at most five, giving frame dimension at most nine. The exceptional kernel-three determinant-zero locus has dimension at most four by [the exceptional net locus bound theorem](../exceptional-net-locus-bound/PROOF.md), with frame fibers of dimension five, again giving at most nine. Finally G4 now has dimension at most four, with frame fibers at most five. Hence the entire independent-triple locus has dimension at most nine.

The dependent locus is the union of the three coordinate-zero affine ten-planes and the rank-at-most-one locus of dimension seven. The full zero locus has dimension ten, and each coordinate-zero plane is already an irreducible component. Any other ten-dimensional irreducible component would either have a dense independent open, contradicting its bound of nine, or be contained in the dependent locus, which has no other ten-dimensional irreducible component. Thus the coordinate-zero planes are exactly the ten-dimensional components.

The dimension, singularity, and twenty-minimal-generator conclusions in [the full cubic locus dimension theorem](../../../global-kernel-incidence/full-cubic-locus-dimension/PROOF.md) remain valid. The present result settles the formerly open top-dimensional independent-component question; it does not classify smaller components or their scheme structures.

## 6. Non-equidimensionality

The previously established structural AP-frame theorem in [the ap three plane cubic frames theorem](../../../two-and-three-plane-zeros/ap-three-plane-cubic-frames/PROOF.md) supplies a nonempty independent zero-frame locus. We import this existence theorem without evaluating any new input or reconstructing a source calculation. Choose an irreducible component of the full zero locus containing an independent zero. Its intersection with the independent open is nonempty and dense, so its dimension is at most nine by Section 5. It cannot equal any of the coordinate-zero ten-planes, which contain no independent triple. Thus there is at least one additional, smaller-dimensional irreducible component. In particular the full cubic zero scheme is not equidimensional, and its underlying space has at least four irreducible components. This does not determine how many smaller components there are or their exact dimensions.
