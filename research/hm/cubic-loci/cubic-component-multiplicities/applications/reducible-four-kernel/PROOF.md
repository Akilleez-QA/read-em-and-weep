# Actual cubic application of reducible four kernel

The preceding universal arguments are not duplicated. Sections1–4 are proved in unit02, using the net theorem and P³ obstruction of unit01. The actual rank-one-free kernel and spectral incidence are supplied by unit09. The existence corollary uses the preserved constant-rank-net application§7 in this packet.

## Imported fixed-kernel notation

Assume K⊂sl(W) is four-dimensional and rank-one-free with reducible determinant. The universal reducible theorem in unit02 gives K=so(3)⊕CS with S symmetric, traceless and invertible; write A=tS+C(x), Q=(det S)t²+xᵀSx, q=tr(A²), and X={tQ=0,q≠0}⊂P(K). Its two smooth components meet transversely along D°={t=Q=0,q≠0}, a nonempty smooth curve. These are exactly the hypotheses and notation used below.

## 5. Conditional implication for independent zero frames

If an actual K_W satisfies these hypotheses, the imported independent-zero-frame theorem applies to this X. The oriented spectral cover is finite étale of degree two, and the frame scheme is a Zariski locally trivial principal (G_m)³-bundle over that cover. Thus the fixed-W frame scheme is nonempty, reduced, and pure of dimension five. It is singular precisely over D°, with ordinary transverse double-crossing singularities after étale localization and multiplication by smooth torus directions; its singular locus is smooth of pure dimension four.

Indeed finite étale maps and smooth torus bundles preserve the displayed local normal-crossing structure, and every component of the cover dominates a component of X. No assertion here counts irreducible components of the oriented cover or frame scheme: a degree-two étale cover of an individual smooth open component may or may not split, and the universal component count for X alone does not decide that question.

There is no assertion about the global relative scheme over a varying family of W, whose base can have additional geometry or singularities. In particular this theorem supplies neither existence of the actual reducible determinant branch nor a new dimension or component count for the full trilinear zero scheme.
