# Independent audit

The corrected proof is sound over C, under its explicitly imported representation and canonical-operation hypotheses.

1. The map Psi is well defined on the symmetric quotient and is N-equivariant. If it vanishes, antisymmetry in slots 1 and 2 together with outer reversal forces f=-f. Thus a nonzero f gives a nonzero, hence injective, map from the irreducible module Sym^2 V. In particular its value on the nonzero square D_0^2 cannot vanish. This does not assume symmetry of f itself.

2. Differentiating the formal chain identity T_G d(v)=d(Gv)T_G gives T_G D_w T_G^-1=D_(Gw). Consequently the opposite cochain action sends D_w to D_(G^-1 w). For the specified row-index permutation, P_2^-1 e_i=e_(2i). The determinant-corrected generator is -P_2, and it sends D_i to -D_(2i). Its minus sign on D_0 is retained in the proof. The diagonal subgroup has determinant one and fixes D_0. The four other distinct diagonal lines form one multiplier orbit, so their span is irreducible under the chosen subgroup. The covariance formula for L establishes kernel stability; cyclic zero kills D_0, proving the claimed injectivity on its four-dimensional complement.

3. In the opposite convention the selected value has weight (4,4). The invariant Serre trace pairs it only with weight (1,1) in J2. In the recorded quotient basis this is a=1, i=2, delta=4, hence precisely the nonzero class e_(+,V,2). Both weight spaces are one-dimensional and pair perfectly. The corrected SL5 lift introduces no extra determinant character; its scalar minus sign affects odd twists and does not alter this diagonal labeling.

4. The geometric residue formula is valid after the explicit specialization f=m111 now made in Section 4. For general symmetry-eligible f, the theorem supplies the abstract pairing criterion only. The residue uses the ordinary cubic defining cocycle, the TW product before simplex integration, and the existing normalized ordinary trace, without a further factor 1/2. Selecting a resolution-Hom quotient class does not construct a regular TW representative of it. The text correctly leaves that representative and adjacent primitives unsupplied.

The audit identified and the author incorporated the specialization in item 4. The final corrected text was reread. No claim of actual cubic nonvanishing or nonzero mixed-channel multiplication follows from this certificate selection.
