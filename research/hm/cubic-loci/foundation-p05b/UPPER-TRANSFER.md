# Corrected central lifts and canonical upper image

The literal N action and target identity (5), U2=V1⊗Sym³W irreducible, are proved in the preceding pairing normalizer foundation. The canonical downstairs operation and coefficient transfer are proved in foundationP04. This excerpt supplies the remaining lift convention and actual-image arguments.

## 4. Central cover and relation to the previous monomial sectors

In the usual left action used above, O(−r) has central character c^r, so U2 has c², as does (5). The earlier literal cochain convention is pullback f(v)↦T⁻¹f(gv)T; it is the corresponding opposite-group action and has central character c^(−r). Converting by g↦g⁻¹ reconciles the conventions, without altering invariant subspaces or irreducibility. U0, U1, U2 have central characters 1,c⁻¹,c⁻² in that convention; the source and target of m011 match. An honest action on twist−2 is asserted for N (or N^op), not for the projective order-3000 quotient.

There is a small but important matrix-lift distinction. The [existing monomial group](../foundation-p05/WEIGHT-SECTORS.md) has order 2500 in GL5; its multiplier-2 permutation has determinant −1. HM's corresponding SL5 generator is its negative. Every matrix in that monomial group has determinant ±1, and

    g ↦ det(g)g

identifies it with the SL5 monomial subgroup of N. Their projective actions agree. The latter maps onto a Borel subgroup of order 20 in SL2(F5), so its index in N is six; projectively the orders are 500 and 3000. The change of representative is a scalar ±1. Conjugation on End H ignores bundle-scalar ambiguity, and scalar −1 acts trivially on O(−2). Hence the two lifts give the same action on U2.

Thus the prior irreducible ten-dimensional sectors S and N_sector are the restriction of (5) to this monomial subgroup. Neither is invariant under the full group. We do not claim a particular Fourier matrix sends S exactly onto N_sector: irreducibility implies full-group mixing, which is all the image argument needs.

## 5. Canonical image and the retained quotient

The canonical zero-indeterminacy downstairs Massey operation is natural under automorphisms of the twisted End differential graded algebra. Pulling back a defining system gives one for the transformed triple; uniqueness of its cohomology class proves equivariance. The coherent N action just constructed supplies these automorphisms and respects twist addition. Therefore its linear image is N-stable in (5), and must be zero or all of U2.

The previously established reversal-positive dimension is dim U2,+ = 8. The already proved coefficient transfer identifies the retained leading-symbol image with L⊗I+ for a nonzero coefficient line L. Consequently its dimension is 0 or 8. This is a stronger invariant-image restriction than 0,4,8, but still not nonvanishing or a statement that lower-weight contributions vanish. The same reasoning applies separately to the other canonical orderings, if their stated naturality and zero-indeterminacy hypotheses hold.
