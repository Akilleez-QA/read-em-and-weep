## 1. Primitive and repeated-input checks

The supplied transgression theorem gives a regular total-degree-one primitive h_phi of twist -2, with top Cech-degree-four/internal-degree-minus-three term phi/(v0 v1 v2 v3 v4). The injective exterior-product embedding ensures D_u D_w is nonzero for independent u,w, so its unique transgression antecedent phi_uw is nonzero. This is an abstract consequence of injectivity, not an inversion or evaluation of the transgression.

D_u squared is strictly zero. Hence h_uu=0 is an allowed primitive, and F=D_u h_uw is closed by the total Leibniz rule. The imported H1(End H(-2)) vanishing removes the defining-system indeterminacy. The resulting class is the canonical ordered cubic f(u,u,w).

## 2. Both product orders and all trace signs

Use the proof's coefficient notation K^(-1)=A, K^0=C, K^1=E, K^2=W, with the indicated line twists suppressed. Let eta_B have its two closed degree-two blocks A to E and B:C to W. At Cech degree p, F has internal degree 2-p; multiplying by eta_B gives internal degree 4-p. Supertrace therefore annihilates every component except p=4.

For the proof's order F eta_B, the Cech cup sign is (-1)^(4 times 2)=+1. The only diagonal block is on K^0, namely gamma_u phi_uw B:C to C. Its supertrace sign is +1. The other block of eta_B cannot compose into a diagonal term.

For the reverse order eta_B F, eta_B is global and the cup sign is again +1. The only diagonal block is on K^2, namely B gamma_u phi_uw:W to W. Its supertrace sign is (-1)^2=+1. Thus both orders give the same scalar by the rectangular trace identity

    tr_C(gamma_u phi_uw B)=tr_W(B gamma_u phi_uw).

There is no missing opposite-sign block and no possible cancellation from the lower staircase. The output cochain is precisely that scalar times the standard H4(O(-5)) generator.

## 3. Derived trace and quotient compatibility

Supertrace on a bounded locally free complex is a morphism from its endomorphism complex to the structure sheaf complex: it is zero on nonzero internal degree and annihilates graded commutator boundaries. Extending it over the stated Cech totalization preserves this property. The locally split resolution decomposes locally into H in degree zero and contractible summands; consequently its induced trace is ordinary trace on End H. This verifies the derived-trace comparison used by the proof. No nonmultiplicative Cech-to-form integration is being substituted for composition.

Every B extends to a closed eta_B by the independently proved coefficient isomorphism. Some such eta_B are total boundaries, because the global thirty-dimensional cycle space has the ten-dimensional transgression relation subspace. This is not a flaw: if eta_B=delta k, then, since F is closed of total degree two, F eta_B=delta(F k). Its trace class must vanish. Accordingly the displayed linear trace functional automatically kills those relations. Conversely a B producing nonzero trace cannot represent a boundary. The proof never assumes the thirty-dimensional cycle space injects into the twenty-dimensional cohomology quotient.

The full perfection theorem for Serre duality is more than is needed here. A nonzero traced product class in H4(O(-5)) already prevents F from being a boundary; only the elementary perfect pairing between Hom(W,C) and Hom(C,W) is needed to find such a class abstractly.

## 4. The geometric injection is sufficient

The left map gamma is a subbundle inclusion, not merely a sheaf monomorphism. Pullback to an arbitrary line generated by nonzero u therefore gives an injective gamma_u. Since phi_uw is nonzero, gamma_u phi_uw is nonzero. Perfect ordinary trace pairing then supplies a B with nonzero trace. No particular B, projective point, derivative matrix, rank, or residue value is selected or calculated.

Changes of homogeneous coordinates, coefficient bases, and orientation transport these maps and the residue generator compatibly. Such changes cannot affect the nonvanishing conclusion.
