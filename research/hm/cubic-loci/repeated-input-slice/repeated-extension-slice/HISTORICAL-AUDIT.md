# Independent audit

Verdict: sound, relative to the stated imported structural nonvanishing and graded-framed filtered-extension representation theorems. No correction is required.

Reviewed `PROOF.md` and the imported proofs in the cubic structural nonvanishing manuscript (PROOF.md) and [the cubic filtered extension obstruction manuscript](../../canonical-cubic-extension/cubic-filtered-extension-obstruction/PROOF.md). This audit checks the new universal algebra and its use of those imports, not their complete upstream geometric dependencies.

- **Subbundle and arbitrary base change.** The universal vector generates a line subbundle on the punctured affine space. The quotient has rank four. The failure locus for injectivity of the induced quotient map is closed and has no complex closed point, hence is empty. Invertible maximal minors give local left inverses and locally free cokernels. These splittings persist under arbitrary base change; fiberwise injectivity alone would not have sufficed without this argument.
- **Zero scheme.** A local left inverse identifies the component ideal of the bundle map with the four quotient fiber coordinates. Its zero scheme is therefore exactly the total space of the kernel line. The actual vector canonically trivializes that line over the punctured space, giving the unique regular scalar. Base change can introduce nilpotents from the base, but introduces no additional thickening beyond this pulled-back line bundle.
- **Determinantal kernel.** The monomial argument is valid. Fix total index multiplicities and total u-degree. A monomial is specified by the allocation of that degree among the index multiplicities. If two allocations differ, select an index with excess u-allocation and another with deficient u-allocation. A minor relation transfers one allocation unit between them; repeating reaches the other allocation. Distinct allocation classes have distinct exponent vectors after the stated monomial substitution. Thus the kernel is exactly the minor ideal, and the image subring is a domain. Its indicated nonempty localization has transcendence degree six.
- **Radical and saturation.** Structural nonvanishing and diagonal vanishing identify all complex closed zeros, including the zero-u axis. Nullstellensatz then gives the determinantal radical. Localization equality on every principal u-chart and primality give the asserted saturation. The equality between ideal saturation and the intersection of these localization contractions follows from the finite pigeonhole argument stated in the proof.
- **Exact nonreduced locus.** The origin comparison uses orders two versus at least three. At every other closed point of the zero-u axis, the universal determinantal ideal has a nonzero first-order term, whereas the cubic ideal lies in the square of the local maximal ideal. Consequently the finite nilradical module is nonzero there. Its support is closed; the ambient finite-type complex scheme is Jacobson, so equality of its closed-point support with the axis proves equality of the entire support with the axis. Reducedness on the complementary open follows independently from the zero-scheme identification over the reduced punctured space. The reduced scheme-theoretic fiber on the axis is compatible with this nonreduced total space.
- **Filtered-extension interpretation.** Pullback of the imported affine-base representing scheme along the repeated-input slice gives precisely the cubic ideal, together with the actual universal filtered bundle. Its uniqueness statement is valid with the specified graded framing and the imported automorphism vanishings. Over a base mapping to the punctured space, the unique scalar description holds even with nilpotents. Over bases meeting the axis, replacing the cubic ideal by its radical would change the represented functor.
