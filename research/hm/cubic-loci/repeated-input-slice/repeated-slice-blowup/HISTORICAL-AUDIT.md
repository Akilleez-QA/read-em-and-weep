This checks the new universal geometry and its use of those imports, without re-auditing every upstream dependency. The terminology links are ancillary; none of the conclusions below relies on a new external calculation.

- **Tautological injection and ideal factorization.** The diagonal identity makes the morphism L²⊗Q→J well-defined. Its pullback along the faithfully flat punctured-vector-space torsor is the already established split quotient injection. Consequently its cokernel is locally free, and local left inverses recover exactly the quotient coordinates of w. The universal identity u=tv therefore gives I·O_B=t²K on each chart and glues to e²K. This is equality of ideals, including scheme structure, rather than only equality of zero sets. The quotient-coordinate ideal is not divisible by t, so the exceptional order is exactly two.
- **Strict and weak transforms.** Locally O_B/K is a polynomial algebra in t and the remaining free coordinates over the integral projective-space chart. Thus t is a nonzerodivisor modulo K. Saturating t²K by t gives precisely K, so the scheme-theoretic strict transform of the original nonreduced cubic scheme is the smooth total space Tot(L⊕L). Dividing by e² gives the same weak transform. The proof correctly avoids claiming that arbitrary nonflat base change commutes with scheme-theoretic closure.
- **Total inverse image and associated components.** The same regularity proves (t²)∩K=t²K. In the displayed polynomial charts, (t) is prime and t is a nonzerodivisor, so (t²) is (t)-primary; K is prime. Their radicals are incomparable, and this is an irredundant primary decomposition. Accordingly the full inverse image has exactly the doubled ambient exceptional component and the strict-transform component, with no additional embedded associated component along their intersection. This makes no assertion about a primary decomposition before blowing up. Their intersection is the Cartier double cut out by t² on Y.
- **Contraction and smallness.** The explicit inverse obtained from either nonzero column proves an isomorphism onto the reduced target away from the total origin, including the nonzero w-axis. The origin fiber is scheme-theoretically the reduced zero section P(V), of codimension two in Y. This is a projective birational resolution without exceptional divisors. The divisor E∩Y=Tot(L) maps onto the entire w-axis, and only its zero section is contracted. The final qualification is essential: this map is not small, or even semismall, in the intersection-cohomological sense, since twice the exceptional-fiber dimension is eight while the origin has codimension six.
- **Canonical bundle.** The cotangent sequence gives ω_Y=p*(ω_P⊗det(L⊕L)⁻¹)=p*O_P(-3). The proof appropriately leaves target Q-Gorenstein and crepancy questions separate.
- **Actual filtered family.** The imported family is an actual algebraic filtered vector bundle on the fourfold times the full cubic scheme, so ordinary pullback along Y→Z and W→Z preserves algebraicity, filtration, graded framings, and nonreduced test-base compatibility. No division or choice of primitives is needed. Affine-local uniqueness permits framed descent when desired. The zero-section family is constant because its classifying map factors through the origin. Additional tautological line data do not replace or represent the original cubic extension functor.
