# Independent audit: exceptional net locus

Verdict: PASS, with no required corrections.

The singular-net normal form is correct. Subtracting the skew part gives a symmetric traceless extra generator. If it were singular, factorization of its rank-at-most-two symmetric quadratic supplies a nonzero traceless rank-one matrix differing from it by a skew matrix. This contradicts rank-one-freeness. The universal determinant identity t(x^T Bx+t²det B) has a nondegenerate irreducible quadratic factor, so its unique linear factor gives the unique singular net.

The incidence is a closed subscheme of a relative Grassmannian, hence proper over G. Every geometric fiber has at most one underlying point by that uniqueness and the uniform kernel bound. Proper plus quasi-finite therefore implies finite, even if fibers have nilpotents. Taking its reduction preserves finiteness and dimension; it is not incorrectly asserted to become an isomorphism with the image.

On the reduced incidence, untwisting by det S gives a rank-three net subbundle L' of sl(S). Its trace pairing is fiberwise nondegenerate, hence an actual self-duality. The universal kernel line R over P(L') restricts to O(−1) on each projective-plane fiber. Cohomology and base change applied to R(1) yields a line bundle M with R=O(−1) tensor p*M. Adjunction gives L' tensor M→S, and the fiberwise kernel-parameter identification makes it an isomorphism. This correctly glues the conformal structure without requiring local normal forms to glue. Line twists and projective-space conventions are consistent.

Self-duality makes the odd Chern classes of L' vanish rationally. The three displayed Chern formulas for S=L' tensor M therefore imply 27c3−9c1c2+2c1³=0 after pullback. Pulling to smooth projective resolutions avoids any difficulty with Chow products on singular components.

The Schubert calculations in the 3-by-2 rectangle are correct: integrals H^6=5, H^4 sigma_(1,1)=3, H^3 sigma_(1,1,1)=1 give integral R_G H^3=−10. A finite five-dimensional component pushes forward to a positive divisor class dH, and the projection formula after multiplying by H² contradicts −10d. A six-dimensional component similarly gives a positive multiple of [G] and a contradiction after H³. These prove dimension at most four, without needing the AP example.

The resulting elimination of the net branch from ten-dimensional independent components follows from the previously audited five-dimensional fiber theorem. No bound for the entire four-kernel locus is inferred. Only local proof reads and this local audit write were used; no actual HM/source/model evaluations or external writes occurred.

The added Section 7 also passes. Reducibility of the nonzero cubic is equivalent to a singular-net hyperplane, so the claimed equality of underlying loci is exact. No scheme equality is asserted. The reducible four-kernel branch consequently has dimension at most four and contributes at most nine dimensions of independent triples. The earlier exact criterion therefore reduces correctly to dimension five of the irreducible-determinant four-kernel locus.
