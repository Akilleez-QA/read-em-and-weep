# A degree-divisibility constraint on four-dimensional kernel strata

## Statement

Under the hypotheses of EMPTY-INCIDENCE.md, every integral four-dimensional closed subvariety A of G4, if any exists, has Grassmannian cycle class

    [A]=3n(3 sigma_(2)+sigma_(1,1))

for a positive integer n. In particular its Plucker degree is 27n.

This does not assert that G4 has dimension four or that such A exists. It is a necessary condition only, involving no actual source instances.

## 1. The cycle class from the cubic Chern relation

Write H=c1(S*) and b=c2(S)=sigma_(1,1). The codimension-two Chow group of G=Gr(3,C^5) is freely generated by sigma_(2), sigma_(1,1). Write

    [A]=alpha sigma_(2)+beta sigma_(1,1),

with integer coefficients. The relation R=27c3(S)-9c1(S)c2(S)+2c1(S)^3 pulls back to zero on a smooth projective resolution g:X -> A by EMPTY-INCIDENCE.md. The projection formula gives

    0=integral_G R H [A]=5alpha-15beta,

where the intersections are obtained by Pieri's rule. Thus alpha=3beta and

    [A]=m(3 sigma_(2)+sigma_(1,1)),  m=beta.

The same Schubert calculation gives

    integral_G H^4[A]=9m,
    integral_G H^2 b[A]=5m,
    integral_G b^2[A]=4m.

The first is the positive degree of A, so m>0.

For transparent verification of these intersections, put x1,x2,x3 for formal roots of S*, H=sum x_i, b=sum_(i<j) x_i x_j, and e=x1 x2 x3. Then sigma_(2)=H^2-b and R=-27e+9Hb-2H^3. For any symmetric homogeneous degree-six polynomial F, its integral over G is the coefficient of x1^4 x2^3 x3^2 in

    F(x1,x2,x3)(x1-x2)(x1-x3)(x2-x3).

This is the standard alternant form of the Schur integration rule. It gives respectively 5,-15,9,5,4 for the five intersections used above.

## 2. Integrality of the image bundle's fourth Chern number

On the reduced G4 there is an exact sequence of vector bundles

    0 -> K -> E -> I -> 0,

where E=sl(S) tensor det S has rank eight and K,I have rank four. The bundle I is the image of T in its fixed trivial target. Put d=c1(S) and a=b-d^2/3. EMPTY-INCIDENCE.md gives c(K')=1+a, where K'=K tensor(det S)^(-1), and hence

    c1(K)=4d,
    c2(K)=a+6d^2,
    c3(K)=2ad+4d^3,
    c4(K)=ad^2+d^4.

If x_i are now formal roots of S, E has the eight formal roots

    d,d, and d+x_i-x_j for i unequal j.

Expanding c(E)/c(K) through degree four gives

    c4(I*)=c4(I)=-2d^4/9+7d^2b/3+4b^2.

The formal-root expansion is a universal polynomial identity, not a calculation of any operation instance.

Pull back this actual rank-four vector bundle to the smooth projective fourfold X. Its top Chern number is an integer. By the projection formula and d=-H it equals

    integral_X c4(g*I*)
       =(-2/9)(9m)+(7/3)(5m)+4(4m)
       =77m/3.

Since m is an integer and 77 is coprime to three, it follows that 3 divides m. Writing m=3n proves the claim. The argument uses an actual bundle on the resolution, so integrality does not require A itself to be smooth.
