## 1. The exact operator and its typing

The compiler's block indices 0,1,2,3 denote complex degrees -1,0,1,2, with ranks
5,15,10,2. A block indexed (u,v) maps the u term to the v term, so its End-degree
is r=v-u. `Lift.H(source,owner)` is the relevant H method and returns the bare
H0, H1 or eta leaf with shape (dims[source-1],dims[source]). The raw engine has
no corresponding physical H wrapper: its generic leaf branch returns the
primitive selected by the carrier. The frozen leaf is explicitly tagged as an
original fixed-resolution leaf.

The left branch hF exists for v>0 and maps (u,v) to (u,v-1). The right branch
(-1)^r EFh exists exactly for v=1 and u<3 and maps (u,1) to (u+1,1).
Every output has degree r-1 and the specified matrix shape. The guard v=1 is
essential because the projector E acts only in K^0. There are 15 admissible
typed branches over the 16 possible input block positions.

The static independent checker matches the exact AST of `Lift.leaf`, `IP`, `H`,
`homotopy`, `delta`, `at`, and the raw `leaf`/`Dframe` contracts. It does not
import any of them. In particular it authenticates both factors belonging to
the same selected owner, the actual Koszul sign, original owner indices, the
alternating face restriction, and the transposed residual inclusion.

The accepted contraction gives dh+hd=1-E, h^2=0, hE=Eh=0, and dE=Ed=0. On
homogeneous degree-r endomorphisms, d_End(F)=dF-(-1)^r Fd. Direct formal
substitution into H(F)=hF+(-1)^r EFh gives

    d_End H(F)+H d_End(F)=F-EFE.

Thus the typed frozen formula has the expected End-contraction sign and
ordering. This algebraic identity does not establish that a particular raw

## 2. Original versus actual legal frames

This is the decisive geometric point, and the producer handles it correctly.
The actual bundle is K^j=N^(2j+1), equivalently f*L tensor N. Therefore

    Hom(K^u,K^v)=f*O(v-u)

with the common N twist canonically canceled. This does not claim every
strict endomorphism entry is a scalar function; its residual square-line
factor is still present.

On a standard product chart A, let ell_A=tau_A^-1 ell_ref and v_A=tau_A v_ref.
On an actual legal open use e_m=nu_m e_ref and
c=e_m^2/ell_A=nu_m^2 tau_A. The accepted root bridge establishes c as a regular
unit on the legal/standard intersection, including infinity and branch points
permitted by that legal open. The actual e_m-frame homotopy is c h_A. It is
incorrect to identify this coefficient with the bare h_A.

Converting the actual map from degree j to degree j-1 back to the common
rational reference multiplies by nu_m^(2j-1) on the target and
nu_m^-(2j+1) on the source. The result is

    nu_m^-2 c h_A=tau_A h_A=h_ref.

The final equality uses the proved degree -1 homogeneous chart law. The
frozen bare H leaf consequently is the actual geometric homotopy in the
original reference frame. The rational reference is not misrepresented as a
regular frame at infinity.

For a degree-r input, the actual legal coefficient is F_m=c^-r F_A. Both terms
of the operator have legal coefficient c^(1-r) H_A(F_A), precisely the
coefficient law of a degree-(r-1) endomorphism. Across standard charts the
same statement is F_B=lambda^r F_A, h_B=lambda^-1 h_A and E_B=E_A, hence
H_B(F_B)=lambda^(r-1)H_A(F_A). No square root of lambda is required.

The accepted homogeneous clearance is essential. Carrier-square multiplication
turns h_A into a global regular section of its assigned negative square-line
bundle, including the auxiliary v0=0 divisor. Thus no legal divisor is needed
to clear h. Finite mask independence alone would not prove this conclusion.
The argument respects the actual ramification of t at the origin of C.

For a previously established full-sign input on Y, the coefficient line has
degree r-2 in each factor. The even primitive h lowers this to r-3, preserving
deck character. This validates the subsequent J_s coefficient twist -s-2
conditional on the retained initial membership; it supplies no new proof of

## 3. Projector cancellation and carrier improvement

The frozen leaves are I_phys=I D and P_phys=D^-1 P, where
D=diag(nu,nu^-1). Their composite is therefore exactly IP. This cancels the
intermediate quotient-frame factors; it does not cancel unrelated owner
factors, use transition cocycle identities, or assume a finite identity
extends to infinity without further proof. The actual legal coefficients
I_A diag(1,c^-1) and diag(1,c)P_A yield the same projector.

Each primitive I or P has carrier bound 2, so the literal composite bound for
E is 4/0. Along with h<=2/0 this gives the unconditional-in-A 6/0 homotopy
estimate.

For the sharper result, the accepted homogeneous A identity gives

    E=Id-gamma H0-H1 delta.

Gamma and delta are globally polynomial homogeneous maps, with carrier cost
zero. H0 and H1 have the same selected owner's carrier-square clearance. The
whole E thus has bound 2/0. Combining it with h<=2/0 yields 4/0 for the right
branch; the left branch costs only 2/0. This is a proved equality of exact
rational sections, not an unproved denominator cancellation or a modification
of the compiler's IP representation.

No differentiated primitive, q, logarithmic frame derivative, or old
connection occurs in this operator. The root bridge's nonzero old connection
has not been set to zero. Its discrepancy from an old squared flat frame
matters for differentiated operators, but this operator changes coefficients
only by tensor/Hom frame ratios. Assigning the same bound to derivatives of h,
Ward expressions, or differentiated HPL kernels would require a new argument.
