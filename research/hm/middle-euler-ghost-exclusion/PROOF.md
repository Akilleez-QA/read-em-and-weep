# Exclusion of the scalar middle ghost by Euler contraction

Work over C with S=Sym(T), dim T=5, and the actual graded dual HM cohomology module H of PR483. Write N for the finite HM normalizer and Gamma for its Heisenberg subgroup. The variables are T=V*, E=A*, and W' is the two-dimensional generator representation of H. All representations and maps below use this fixed variable convention.

PR497 proves r=0. PR483 consequently leaves precisely the family

F0=2S,
F1=(E tensor W') S(-2),
F2=[V tensor (1+U')] S(-4) + s S(-5),
F3=s S(-5) + [T tensor (1+U')] S(-6),
F4=10S(-8),
F5=2S(-10),

where s is zero or one and each degree-five summand is the trivial representation. We prove s=0. Thus H has pure minimal resolution with ranks (2,10,20,20,10,2) and shifts (0,2,4,6,8,10).

## 1. A universal multiplication kernel

Use U=Sym^2 W, U'=Sym^2 W', the ordinary four-dimensional quotient representation T4=W tensor W', the four-dimensional spin representation Q4, and the five-dimensional quotient representation J5. The pinned identities in PR479 and PR488 are

S2=E tensor U',
T tensor E=A tensor (U+W'),
S3=A tensor (J5+W').

The quotient tensor products are

U tensor U'=T4+J5,
W' tensor U'=W'+Q4.

For completeness the first identity follows directly from the Horrocks--Mumford appendix character table: in its class order 1,-1,4,3,6,5a,5b,10a,10b, both sides have character (9,9,1,0,0,-1,-1,-1,-1). The second is the usual two-dimensional Clebsch--Gordan decomposition, also used in PR479. The distinction between T4 and Q4 is essential.

It follows that

T tensor S2=A tensor (T4+J5+W'+Q4).

The ordinary polynomial multiplication mu:T tensor S2 -> S3 is surjective. Each displayed normalizer summand is irreducible, and they are pairwise nonisomorphic. Semisimplicity therefore identifies its kernel without evaluating any source-instance map:

ker(mu)=A tensor (T4+Q4).                         (1)

In particular

Hom_N((E tensor W')*, ker(mu))=0,                (2)

because (E tensor W')*=A tensor W' and this summand is absent in (1).

## 2. The only possible degree-five column is Euler

Choose an N-equivariant minimal graded free resolution of H. Such a choice exists by taking equivariant lifts of minimal generators at each stage, using finite-group semisimplicity over C. Suppose s=1, and let z be the invariant degree-five basis element of F3.

Minimality makes the component of d3(z) into the degree-five part of F2 zero: it would have constant coefficients. The remaining component lies in

(T tensor V tensor (1+U'))^N.

Since V is irreducible under Gamma, (T tensor V)^Gamma=End_Gamma(V)=C, with trivial N action. U' is trivial under Gamma and has no N/Gamma invariant vectors. Consequently this invariant space is one-dimensional, generated by the Euler tensor in T tensor V, with zero component in T tensor V tensor U'.

Moreover d3(z) is nonzero. If it were zero, exactness would put the free basis element z in im(d4), whereas minimality gives im(d4) contained in the homogeneous maximal ideal times F3. This is impossible. We may therefore rescale z and write

d3(z)=sum_i x_i v_i,                            (3)

where x_i is any basis of T and v_i the dual basis of the V summand of F2's degree-four generators. No actual matrix or chosen HM coefficients are used in (3).

## 3. Euler contraction cannot vanish

Restrict d2 to the V summand of F2's degree-four generators. Its coefficient map is an N-equivariant map

f:V -> S2 tensor E tensor W'.

Via the canonical identification V*=T, its tensor is

tau_f=sum_i x_i tensor f(v_i)
       in (T tensor S2 tensor E tensor W')^N.

The complex identity d2 d3=0 and (3) say precisely

(mu tensor identity)(tau_f)=sum_i x_i f(v_i)=0.

Thus tau_f lies in (ker(mu) tensor E tensor W')^N. By (2) this invariant space is zero. Hence tau_f=0 and f=0.

This is impossible in a minimal exact free resolution: any nonzero basis vector v of this V summand now belongs to ker(d2)=im(d3), but im(d3) is contained in the homogeneous maximal ideal times F2, while v is not. The contradiction proves s=0.

The argument uses the universal polynomial multiplication kernel, not an assertion that the Euler class alone forces either spectral-sequence alternative. In particular PR483's B(0) spectral sequence allows s=0: its two outer scalar terms must then be killed by the nonzero d3 isomorphism. Our exclusion now selects that alternative; it does not discard that differential.

## 4. Consequences and remaining boundary

Together with PR497, the result determines the entire minimal graded free-module resolution of H. It does not give its differentials explicitly. In PR492's scalar-annihilator Betti family, the bound u<=2s now forces u=0. Since r=0 also gives q=0, the only remaining parameter there is v in {0,10}. Explicitly R=S/Ann(H) has the remaining family

F0=S,
F1=10S(-3)+15S(-4),
F2=10S(-4)+52S(-5)+vS(-6),
F3=(45+v)S(-6)+10S(-7),
F4=20S(-8),
F5=3S(-10).

Nothing here chooses v. These statements concern first-order cohomology modules and their syzygies; they do not establish higher-order lifting, source membership, fixed-pair values, or algebraicity.
