# Structural nonvanishing of the HM quintic pairing

## Result

Using the independently proved vanishing H⁰B(1)=0 from https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/872aca45943d3758e261a900e5984b981c18b3c4/research/hm/twist-one-vanishing/PROOF.md and AUDIT.md, the HM quintic pairing scalar satisfies

    c≠0.

Thus the normalizer-equivariant quintic pairing Φ=cΨ is nonzero and surjective, and the entire middle-cohomology module M is generated by M₋₅. The proof uses a small universal Koszul tensor argument and no selected HM matrix or multiplication rank evaluation. Sections 1–6 isolate the exact middle edge; Sections 7–9 prove it is nonzero.

## Intermediate dichotomy, resolved below

For the literal normalized HM bundle F on P⁴, put B=End₀F and M_t=H²B(t). Let c be the scalar in the audited normalizer-equivariant quintic pairing Φ=cΨ, with U=M₋₅. Then

    S₂U=M₋₃,
    S₁M₋₂=M₋₁,
    S₁M₋₁=M₀.

After the outer propagation, the single edge to settle is

    ν:S₁⊗M₋₃ → M₋₂.                         (1)

The following conditions are equivalent:

1. c≠0.
2. ν≠0.
3. ν is surjective.
4. Soc(M)₋₃=0.
5. M is generated by M₋₅ as a graded S-module.

If these conditions fail, ν=0, Soc(M)₋₃=M₋₃, and M is a direct sum of its three lowest and three highest graded pieces as S-modules. Thus this intermediate test is linear multiplication between two actual irreducible twenty-dimensional modules, not an unspecified chain of five propagation steps. The zero alternative will be excluded in Sections 7–9 without evaluating a value of (1).

## 1. Imported audited representations

Use the ordinary N⊂SL₅ linearization from the original HM monad, with H₅ normal and quotient SL₂(F₅). The following are established in the indicated local proofs:

- ../quintic-pairing-representation/PROOF.md and AUDIT.md: M₀≅W′ and U≅(W′)*, where W′ is the irreducible two-dimensional conjugate HM representation; Φ=cΨ and c=0 iff S₅U=0.
- ../lowest-degree-generation/PROOF.md: μ:S₁⊗U→M₋₄ is an isomorphism; Soc(M)₋₅=Soc(M)₋₁=0. The coordinate representation S₁ is irreducible on H₅, whereas U is H₅-trivial.
- ../full-hm-image-symmetry/PROOF.md and its AUDIT.md: M₋₂≅V₁⊗Sym³W is irreducible of dimension twenty for N. This is an actual cohomology representation derived from the equivariant Euler coresolution, not inferred from dimensions. Sym³W is the irreducible spinorial four-dimensional representation.
- Trace/Serre duality gives M_t*≅M₋₅₋t, compatibly with N and multiplication.
- ../hm-low-degree-dependency/PROOF.md: M_t=0 outside −5≤t≤0.
- H¹B(t)=0 for t≤−2 and H⁰B(t)=0 for t≤0.
- https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/872aca45943d3758e261a900e5984b981c18b3c4/research/hm/twist-one-vanishing/PROOF.md and AUDIT.md: H⁰B(1)=0.
- The established negative-twist table gives h¹B(−1)=5, and h¹B=24; see historical-source:triple-component-primary-reduction/PROOF.md and ../pairing-foundation-inputs/COHOMOLOGY.md.

The primary representation source used by those audited proofs is Horrocks–Mumford, Topology 12 (1973), 63–81, especially §§1–2 and the appendix:
https://www.dam.brown.edu/people/mumford/alg_geom/papers/1973a--Rk2BdleP%5E4-NC.pdf

Thus M₋₃≅(V₁⊗Sym³W)* is irreducible. Also M₋₄=S₁⊗U is irreducible for N: any H₅-submodule is S₁⊗U′, and N-stability makes U′ an SL₂(F₅)-subrepresentation of the irreducible U. Its dual M₋₁ is irreducible. Hence every nonzero M_t in the six supported degrees is an irreducible N-module.

## 2. The next lowest socle vanishes

Apply the exact coordinate cochain Koszul complex to B(−4):

    K^p=Λ^p(S₁*)⊗B(p−4),   0≤p≤5.

Its hypercohomology is zero. The spectral sequence has

    E₂^(0,2)=Soc(M)₋₄.

There are no incoming differentials because the complex has no negative column. The d₂ target is a subquotient of Λ²(S₁*)⊗H¹B(−2)=0; the d₃ target is a subquotient of Λ³(S₁*)⊗H⁰B(−1)=0. Later targets have negative cohomology row. Therefore Soc(M)₋₄=0.

This argument uses neither H¹B(−1) nor the unproved positive-twist section vanishings.

## 3. All outer edges propagate

Write a_t:S₁⊗M_t→M_(t+1) for multiplication. Its image and socle kernel are N-stable.

Because M₋₄ is nonzero and Soc(M)₋₄=0, a₋₄ is nonzero. Its target M₋₃ is irreducible, so it is surjective. Composing with μ and using S₂ spanned by products of linear forms proves S₂U=M₋₃. In particular this is strictly stronger than the earlier degree-one generation theorem.

By adjointness, a₋₄ is nonzero iff a₋₂ is nonzero: for m∈M₋₄,n∈M₋₂ and ℓ∈S₁,

    ⟨ℓm,n⟩=⟨m,ℓn⟩.

Since M₋₁ is irreducible, a₋₂ is surjective. Likewise the nonzero map a₋₅=μ is dual, in this sense, to a₋₁. Since its target M₀ is irreducible, a₋₁ is surjective. Thus S₂M₋₂=M₀ as well.

The same adjointness and surjectivity show Soc(M)₋₂=0. Consequently the complete socle list is now known apart from the middle alternative:

    Soc(M)₋₅=Soc(M)₋₄=Soc(M)₋₂=Soc(M)₋₁=0;
    Soc(M)₀=M₀;
    Soc(M)₋₃ is either 0 or M₋₃.

## 4. Equivalence and exact failure mode

The image of ν is N-stable in the irreducible M₋₂, so ν is either zero or surjective. The socle in degree −3 is an N-submodule of the irreducible M₋₃; if ν is nonzero this submodule cannot be all, hence is zero. Conversely a zero ν annihilates all M₋₃ by all linear forms. This proves the equivalence of (2)–(4).

If ν is surjective, Section 3 gives

    S₃U=M₋₂,  S₄U=M₋₁,  S₅U=M₀.

Because M₀≠0, the audited criterion c=0 iff S₅U=0 proves c≠0. The same identities, μ, and the support theorem prove generation of the entire module.

If ν=0, every product of three linear forms kills U because S₂U=M₋₃. Hence S₃U=0 and in particular S₅U=0, proving c=0. More generally every positive-degree multiplication crossing the cut between −3 and −2 is zero: each monomial factors into linear forms and crosses that edge. Therefore

    M = (M₋₅⊕M₋₄⊕M₋₃) ⊕ (M₋₂⊕M₋₁⊕M₀)

as graded S-modules. The first summand has dimensions (2,10,20), generated at −5; the second has dimensions (20,10,2), generated at −2. Serre duality exchanges the two summands. A vector-space or graded-module splitting here is not a splitting assertion about the vector bundle B.

## 5. Smaller symmetric linear-form detector

Set A=M₋₃. Perfect duality identifies M₋₂=A*. The central multiplication map can equivalently be written

    Θ:S₁→Sym²A*,   Θ(ℓ)(a,b)=⟨a,ℓb⟩.       (2)

The trace/Serre pairing is symmetric on the two cohomological-degree-two arguments, and multiplication is self-adjoint, so (2) really is symmetric. It is N-equivariant. The equivalent conditions above hold precisely when Θ≠0. Since S₁ is irreducible, any nonzero Θ is injective, although no claim is made that any one symmetric form Θ(ℓ) is nonsingular.

This is a linear-degree detector in Sym² of an actual twenty-dimensional representation, replacing quintic propagation from the bottom. Its multiplicity can also be determined without evaluating Θ.

## 6. The middle Hom space has multiplicity one

Put Q=Sym³W=χ₄#, so A*=M₋₂=V₁⊗Q and S₁=V*. The original primary paper, Lemma 3.3(v), gives Sym²V=V₁⊗χ₃′. Its construction also gives Λ²V=V₁⊗W. Apply the Galois automorphism ζ↦ζ², which sends V to V₁ and V₁ to V₂=V*: it exchanges W,W′ and χ₃,χ₃′. Hence

    Sym²V₁=V*⊗χ₃,    Λ²V₁=V*⊗W′.           (3)

The appendix table gives the character of Q as

    4,−4,0,1,−1,−1,−1,1,1

in its nine-class order. The symmetric/exterior square character formulas (χ(g)²±χ(g²))/2 yield respectively

    Sym²Q: 10,10,−2,1,1,0,0,0,0;
    Λ²Q:    6, 6, 2,0,0,1,1,1,1.

Comparing those lists with the same appendix gives

    Sym²Q=χ₃⊕χ₃′⊕χ₄,     Λ²Q=1⊕χ₅,        (4)

where χ₄ is the nonspinorial four-dimensional character, distinct from Q. This is a finite character calculation, not an actual multiplication computation.

The natural tensor-square decompositions and (3) give

    Sym²A* = V*⊗[χ₃⊗Sym²Q ⊕ W′⊗Λ²Q],
    Λ²A*   = V*⊗[χ₃⊗Λ²Q   ⊕ W′⊗Sym²Q].

For every representation R inflated from N/H₅, the H₅-isotypic evaluation equivalence gives Hom_N(V*,V*⊗R)=R^(N/H₅). The irreducibles in (4) are self-dual, and W′ is self-dual too. Exactly one trivial representation occurs in the first bracket, from χ₃⊗χ₃, and none in the second. Therefore

    dim Hom_N(S₁,Sym²A*)=1,
    Hom_N(S₁,Λ²A*)=0,
    dim Hom_N(S₁⊗M₋₃,M₋₂)=1.               (5)

Thus ν is itself an undetermined scalar multiple of a fixed nonzero intertwiner, automatically symmetric after Serre identification. The new missing scalar is zero exactly when the old quintic scalar c is zero. We do not assert equality of their numerical normalizations. Sections 7–9 prove both scalars nonzero structurally.

## 7. The middle socle is an H¹ Koszul kernel

At twist −3, the coordinate complex has E₂^(0,2)=Soc(M)₋₃. Its d₂ target is

    K=ker[Λ²V⊗H¹B(−1) → Λ³V⊗H¹B],          (6)

because the preceding H¹B(−2) term is zero. The displayed map is induced by actual H¹ multiplication. The d₃ target from column zero is H⁰B=0 and all other outgoing or incoming possibilities vanish. Consequently d₂:Soc(M)₋₃→K is injective.

The outgoing d₂ from K has target a subquotient of Λ⁴V⊗H⁰B(1)=0 by the new twisted-endomorphism exclusion. There are no later incoming or outgoing differentials at (2,1). Convergence to zero therefore gives the N-equivariant isomorphism

    Soc(M)₋₃ ≅ K.                              (7)

We now prove K=0 using the actual low H¹ representations and a universal tensor identity.

## 8. The actual H¹ multiplication is a traceless projection

Put T_t=H¹B(t). First T₋₁≅V. To see this, use the equivariant complex K∨⊗F(−1) from the original monad, with columns

    −2: W*⊗F(−3),
    −1: V⊗W*⊗F(−2),
     0: V₁⊗Sym²W*⊗F(−1),
     1: V₃⊗F.

F(−3) is acyclic: its H² vanishes by the primary support theorem, and duality H^iF(t)*=H^(4−i)F(−t−4) reduces its possible H³,H⁴ to the zero H¹F(−1),H⁰F(−1); its H⁰,H¹ vanish by the established tables. F(−1) is also acyclic. The only E₁ entries are therefore

    (−1,2): V⊗W*⊗W=V⊗(1⊕χ₃),
    ( 1,1): V₃⊗V₃.

The only possible differential is d₂ between these entries. Total degree one is its kernel. Trace splitting does not change H¹, and the established dimension is dim T₋₁=5. Both V and V⊗χ₃ are irreducible N-modules, of dimensions five and fifteen, by the H₅-isotypic evaluation argument. Consequently that kernel is the unique V summand. This determines the actual module T₋₁≅V without evaluating d₂.

Second, T₀ has no H₅-invariants. The invariant spectral sequence at twist zero was already computed in ../quintic-pairing-representation/PROOF.md: its entries are (−2,2):1⊕χ₃, (0,1):χ₃, and (1,1):W′. The last d₁ is zero by inequivalence. Simplicity gives H⁰EndF=1, so d₂:1⊕χ₃→χ₃ has kernel exactly the trivial line, and is surjective. Thus no invariant total-degree-one class survives, proving T₀^H₅=0.

Third, the multiplication

    b:S₁⊗T₋₁ → T₀

is nonzero. In the coordinate Koszul spectral sequence for B(−1), E₂^(0,1) is the simultaneous kernel of multiplication by all linear forms on T₋₁. It has no incoming differential. Its sole possible outgoing differential is d₂ to a subquotient of Λ²V⊗H⁰B(1), which is zero. Hence E₂^(0,1)=0. Since T₋₁ is nonzero, b cannot be zero.

Now S₁⊗T₋₁≅V*⊗V=1⊕Z, with Z the actual irreducible twenty-four-dimensional trace-zero N-module from HM §1. Since T₀^H₅=0, b kills the trivial line. Since b≠0, its restriction to Z is injective, and h¹B=24 makes it an isomorphism Z→T₀. Therefore, after these N-equivariant identifications and an irrelevant nonzero scale, b is the canonical quotient

    V*⊗V → (V*⊗V)/C·id = Z.                 (8)

This is a structural determination of b, not a selected matrix computation.

## 9. A universal injectivity lemma kills the last socle

For any five-dimensional vector space V, the map induced by (8),

    D:Λ²V⊗V → Λ³V⊗[(V*⊗V)/C·id],
    D(ω⊗v)=Σ_i(e_i∧ω)⊗[x_i⊗v],             (9)

is injective. Here e_i and x_i are dual bases, and the sum is canonical.

Indeed write T=Σ_j T_j⊗e_j with T_j∈Λ²V. If D(T)=0, then before quotienting its image belongs to Λ³V⊗C·id. Thus there is R∈Λ³V with

    e_i∧T_j=δ_ij R    for every i,j.

For fixed j, the two-form T_j satisfies e_i∧T_j=0 for all four indices i≠j. A two-form killed by wedging with a nonzero vector is divisible by that vector; equivalently, expand in basis wedges. Being divisible by three independent basis vectors already forces a two-form to be zero. Hence each T_j=0 and T=0.

Under the identifications in Section 8, (6) is exactly the kernel of (9), up to a nonzero scale. Thus K=0, and (7) gives Soc(M)₋₃=0. Section 4 now proves ν is surjective and

    S₃U=M₋₂,  S₄U=M₋₁,  S₅U=M₀.

The nondegenerate trace/Serre pairing and M₀≠0 finally give c≠0.

## Consequences and evidence boundary

The original Φ=cΨ is surjective onto Sym²U*, and its restriction to S₅^H₅ has kernel exactly the χ₃ summand. Its overall scalar remains normalization-dependent but is nonzero. In the cofactor notation of ../quintic-pairing-representation/PROOF.md, αμ=cA_Ψ therefore gives rank α=rank A_Ψ. No rank of A_Ψ or α is evaluated or asserted.

The zero-middle-edge and split-module alternatives in Sections 3–4 are rigorously excluded for the actual HM bundle by the additional H⁰B(1)=0 input. They remain the exact conditional failure mode if that geometric input is omitted.

This proof uses existing audited geometric inputs, finite representations, coordinate Koszul exactness, trace/Serre adjointness, and the universal five-dimensional identity (9). No HM, source, conductor-cofactor, physical-source, or obstruction matrix was selected or evaluated. No source or obstruction nonvanishing is inferred merely from the nonvanishing of this different quintic pairing.
