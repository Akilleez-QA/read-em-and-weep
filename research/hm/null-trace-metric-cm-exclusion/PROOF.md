# Conditional exclusion of trace-metric CM lifts

## Result and evidence boundary

Let X=C^4 be the elliptic product in the two input proofs, work over the complex numbers, let sigma be their simultaneous CM automorphism, and put G=sigma*F. Retain the historical A-identity premise and physical-bundle identification exactly as stated there. The input projective-lift theorem then says

    P(F) is not isomorphic over X to P(G).

Consequently there is no global regular vector-bundle isomorphism

    T: End_0(F) -> End_0(G)

satisfying

    tr(T(A)T(B)) = lambda tr(AB)

for any nonzero global scalar lambda. In particular there is no trace-pairing isometry, including orientation-reversing ones. Here End_0(G)=sigma*End_0(F) canonically, and its trace pairing is the pulled-back pairing. The condition is an identity of bundle-valued bilinear forms for all local sections A,B, not merely an equality on chosen currents.

This is a formal rank-two deduction from the conditional projective obstruction. It adds no model-dependent computational premise, and does not remove either inherited premise. It excludes neither arbitrary vector-bundle isomorphisms that fail to preserve the trace pairing up to scale, nor accidental covariance of selected or complete scalar observables. No physical source or jet values are evaluated.

## 1. Canonical trace tensors and universal identities

For a rank-two bundle V, set E_V=End_0(V). In characteristic different from two,

    End(V)=O_X Id direct_sum E_V,
    q_V(A,B)=tr(AB),
    tau_V(A,B,C)=tr(A[B,C]).

The trace and commutator are intrinsic, so these definitions glue under every change of frame. Cyclicity of trace shows that tau is alternating. In a local frame use

    h=diag(1,-1), e=E_12, f=E_21.

Then

    [h,e]=2e, [h,f]=-2f, [e,f]=h,
    matrix(q) in (h,e,f) = [[2,0,0],[0,0,1],[0,1,0]],
    tau(h,e,f)=2.

Thus q is nondegenerate and tau trivializes det(E_V)^*. There is a canonical orientation; no trivialization of det(V) is needed. For any three sections A_1,A_2,A_3, the preceding basis calculation and change-of-basis formula give

    tau(A_1,A_2,A_3)^2 = -2 det(q(A_i,A_j)).                 (1)

Two further universal rank-two identities are

    q(A,A)=-2 det(A),
    AB = (1/2)q(A,B) Id + (1/2)[A,B]                       (2)

for traceless A,B. The latter follows by polarizing A^2=(tr(A^2)/2)Id, or directly from 2x2 matrices. These identities involve abstract 2x2 matrices only, not the pinned model.

## 2. Short proof through the intrinsic projective null conic

To keep projectivization conventions explicit, write P_lines(W)=P_quot(W^*) for the bundle of line subbundles. Let

    C_V = { [A] in P_lines(E_V) : q_V(A,A)=0 }.

There is a canonical X-isomorphism

    P_quot(V) -> C_V.                                      (3)

Indeed, a rank-one quotient V -> Q has a rank-one subbundle L as kernel. The line subbundle Hom(Q,L) of End(V), inserted by the composite V -> Q -> L -> V, consists of traceless square-zero endomorphisms. It defines the map (3). Equivalently, in the line convention it sends L to the nilpotent line Hom(V/L,L). This is independent of any chosen frame, so it glues and commutes with base change.

Here is a scheme-theoretic local verification, rather than a bijection of complex points. Trivialize V and write

    A=[[a,b],[c,-a]],  C_V: a^2+bc=0 in P^2.

For a line L generated by (s,t), its nilpotent endomorphism line is generated by

    [[-st,s^2],[-t^2,st]],

so the morphism is the Veronese parametrization

    [s:t] -> [-st:s^2:-t^2].

The opens D_+(b) and D_+(c) cover the conic: a homogeneous prime containing b,c and a^2 also contains a and hence cannot be a projective point. On D_+(b), normalizing b=1 gives c=-a^2; the inverse line is [1:-a]. On D_+(c), normalizing c=1 gives b=-a^2; the inverse line is [a:1]. The two expressions agree on the overlap and inverse the displayed parametrization. Thus (3) is an isomorphism of schemes over each trivializing open, and its intrinsic construction glues globally. Geometrically its inverse is the common image/kernel line of a nonzero rank-one nilpotent.

A similarity T:E_F -> E_G induces an isomorphism P_lines(E_F) -> P_lines(E_G) taking C_F onto C_G, since its multiplier is invertible. Restriction followed by (3) gives P_quot(F) -> P_quot(G), contradicting the imported theorem. This already proves the result, without orientation choices or an appeal to a classification of Lie algebra automorphisms.

For the quotient convention and its rank-one quotient functor, see the primary reference [Stacks Project, Projective bundles](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01OA). The conic identification above is proved explicitly here.

## 3. Independent orientation/Lie-algebra proof

Let T:E_F -> E_G be a global regular similarity, with multiplier lambda in Gamma(X,O_X)^*. Since both canonical volume forms are nowhere vanishing, there is a unique unit d with

    tau_G(TA,TB,TC)=d tau_F(A,B,C).

Taking determinants in the metric identity and using (1) yields

    d^2=lambda^3.                                         (4)

In particular a similarity admits the canonical normalization

    U=(lambda/d)T.

Indeed,

    q_G(UA,UB)=q_F(A,B),
    tau_G(UA,UB,UC)=tau_F(A,B,C).

This works without choosing a square root: the similarity itself supplies a square root d/lambda of lambda. On the present connected projective complex X, all global regular functions are constants, so lambda,d are nonzero complex numbers. The usual alternative argument also works: divide T by a chosen square root of lambda, and if the resulting isometry reverses the canonical orientation, multiply it by -Id. An isometry has d^2=1, and connectedness gives d=+1 or -1. Multiplication by -Id changes the volume sign in rank three and preserves the metric.

The bracket is uniquely recovered from metric and volume by

    q(A,[B,C])=tau(A,B,C).

Nondegeneracy therefore proves

    U[B,C]=[UB,UC].

Explicitly, pair both sides with every UA and apply the two tensor identities. Thus U is a Lie-algebra bundle isomorphism.

Using (2), extend U to

    Phi: End(F) -> End(G),   Phi(a Id+A)=a Id+U(A).

It is a unital associative algebra isomorphism, since (2) recovers the product on traceless elements and scalar products are automatic.

### Local innerness and gluing, with no unproved lifting assumption

Trivialize F and G on an open set. Let e_ij denote source matrix units and p_ij=Phi(e_ij). The idempotent p_11 splits G into image(p_11) and image(p_22). The p_12,p_21 relations make these two summands isomorphic; since G has rank two, each has rank one. After shrinking, choose a generator v_1 of image(p_11) and put v_2=p_21(v_1). The identities p_12 p_21=p_11 and p_21 p_12=p_22 show that v_2 generates image(p_22). Thus v_1,v_2 form a local frame and p_ij act on it as ordinary matrix units. The local linear isomorphism g:F -> G carrying the source basis to this frame satisfies

    Phi(A)=g A g^{-1}.

Two local choices g and g' implementing Phi differ by a scalar unit: g^{-1}g' commutes with all 2x2 matrices, and commuting with the matrix units forces a matrix to be scalar. Their projectivizations therefore agree on overlaps. They glue to the forbidden X-isomorphism of projective bundles. In the quotient convention the map is Q:F -> L mapped to Q g^{-1}:G -> L; scalar changes do not alter its quotient point.

This proves the needed content of Aut(sl_2)=PGL_2 directly in this setting. Conversely, projective bundle isomorphisms locally give projective linear maps, whose conjugation actions glue and preserve q, tau, and bracket. Equivalently one can use the input proof's classification G≅F tensor M and the canonical End(F tensor M)≅End(F). Lie-algebra isomorphisms also preserve q because their Killing form is 4q, as the displayed h,e,f brackets verify. Hence over C the existence of a projective-bundle isomorphism, a Lie-algebra-bundle isomorphism, a canonical-orientation-preserving trace isometry, any trace isometry, or a trace similarity is equivalent.

## 4. Duality and line-valued conformal twists

There is no rank-two duality loophole. The canonical wedge pairing gives

    V ≅ V^* tensor det(V),

so projectivization does not distinguish V and V^* in rank two. Also transpose is an associative anti-isomorphism and a Lie anti-isomorphism, not a new orientation-preserving Lie symmetry. The map -transpose is a Lie isomorphism. In a frame, if J=[[0,1],[-1,0]], then for traceless A,

    J A J^{-1}=-A^t.

Thus the possible sign/duality operation is already accounted for by the projective adjoint mechanism. Frame-dependent J does not claim an unchosen global linear identification V≅V^*: the determinant-line formula states the intrinsic version.

The conic proof even gives this precise strengthening. Let M be any line bundle, suppose T:E_F -> E_G tensor M is an isomorphism, and suppose there is an isomorphism kappa:O_X -> M^2 with

    (q_G tensor multiplication)(TA,TB)=kappa(q_F(A,B)).

Local trivializations of M make this a similarity with a unit multiplier. Projectivization removes M canonically, and the null conics are preserved and glue. Therefore such a line-valued conformal similarity is also excluded. More generally, any X-isomorphism of the ambient projective adjoint bundles carrying their intrinsic null conics onto one another is excluded. No claim is made for a line twist without this conformal/null-conic condition.

## 5. Hypotheses and strict scope

- Rank two is essential to the nilpotent conic and the product reconstruction used here. No higher-rank analogue is asserted.
- Characteristic different from two is sufficient for these intrinsic rank-two arguments. In characteristic two the trace pairing on the traceless space is degenerate, Id is traceless, and the argument as stated fails. The inherited model theorem is only being used over C.
- Connectedness and projectivity justify the simple global constant/sign discussion on this X. They are not needed for the conic argument with a unit-valued multiplier, nor for the canonical normalization U=(lambda/d)T. Disconnected bases allow componentwise signs.
- The actual obstruction concerns global regular O_X-linear isomorphisms. Local or rational/meromorphic changes of variables remain possible. A semilinear lift covering sigma is represented here by the corresponding O_X-linear map to sigma*E; there is no extra escape in that notation.
- An arbitrary isomorphism E_F≅E_G need not preserve the specified trace forms up to scalar. This deduction neither proves nor disproves the existence of such an isomorphism in the pinned example. Even on a point, scaling h by 2 while fixing e,f gives a linear isomorphism of sl_2 that is not a q-similarity: q(h,h) changes by a factor 4 while q(e,f) does not.
- Equality/covariance of scalar expressions alone does not manufacture a global invertible map on the whole adjoint bundle or prove its compatibility with q. Thus this result cannot settle accidental complete scalar covariance or any detector value. Trace-current reformulation is obstructed only when it requires the specified global trace-metric/conformal bundle lift. Nonlinear transformations, selected sections, jets, and differential operators require separate hypotheses and are not ruled out here.
