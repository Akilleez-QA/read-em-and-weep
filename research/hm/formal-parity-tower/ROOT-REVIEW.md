20 October 2026. Verdict: no blocking finding within the marked, reversal-compatible ambient category and retained literal small-extension hypotheses of the proof. This is mathematical review, not formal verification.

The gauge lifting lemma correctly lifts coordinate data without claiming an equivalence between arbitrarily prescribed higher endpoints. For a lifted unit with constant term one, reversal fixes its norm. Thus an odd top norm defect is zero; at even order adding minus one half of that defect corrects the norm. Even normalized differential chart maps lift coefficientwise, and their formal inverses preserve coefficientwise finite differential order.

The finite relative automorphism induction addresses the necessary lower-automorphism quotient. A lower chart two-unit lifts in the higher source product and modifies an equivalence without changing its endpoints. The remaining top automorphism is a global invariant vector field at even order, hence zero, or an invariant Čech one-cocycle of functions at odd order, hence a coboundary. The induction uses both inversion vanishings and produces two-triviality, not literal absence of edge automorphisms.

The proof then distinguishes existence of an equivalence from compatibility of its reduction. Once an equivalence of higher endpoints exists, the same chart-unit correction gives any specified lower equivalence as its exact reduction. This justifies injectivity of formal classes into the inverse limit. Conversely lifting coordinate gauges strictifies representatives of compatible finite classes. Coefficientwise completeness supplies both formal limits without a convergence assertion.

For the fiber torsor, the common lift of an edge unit is unitary for two top products since their difference is h^n u and normalization gives u(1,1)=0. All other positive gauge terms disappear at that top order. The induced translation space is therefore unchanged. Torsor origins still require noncanonical choices, so the conclusion is a product of sets compatible with truncation, not a natural affine structure, moduli functor or period map.

Formal relative automorphisms are trivialized by compatible chart units, not merely by a claim that all finite automorphism classes vanish. Identity two-automorphisms have global scalar coefficients inductively because every overlap map is unital. Their logarithms have only odd powers in the unitary convention; ordinary inner two-units permit all powers. These scalar towers have surjective restrictions. No hidden inverse-limit quotient is assumed away.

The fixed literal base, gauges identity on that base, adjustable higher products, invariant acyclic cover and original literal lifting premise are essential. No fixed-product continuation, unmarked classification, module lifting, pure-condition vanishing, algebraicity or analytic convergence follows.
