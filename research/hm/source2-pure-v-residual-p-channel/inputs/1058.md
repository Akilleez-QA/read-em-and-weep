## 4. Transport through the actual separated projector

Assume e2|P_h is nonzero. Equivariance and the multiplicity-one P in N0,2
make it an isomorphism P_h→P8. Because the projectors commute with both
actual negative arrows, they also commute with every negative quadratic:

    e4 T_a=T_a^0 e2.

Consider the actual composite

    A tensor P_h→F4→N0,4.

By the preceding identity, it is theta composed with A tensor(e2|P_h),
and is therefore injective. On the other hand e4 annihilates I4 because
I lies inside the genuine residual block NR. It follows that

    A tensor P_h→F4/I4

is injective. PR1054 already proves it onto, so it is an isomorphism.
This proves (1), with every V,P,D channel present. It also shows that the
actual quadratic image T_A P_h is disjoint from I4; no residual P graph
relation survives in this branch.

The generated P submodule has degree-two space P_h, degree-three space
X_W P_h, and degree-four space T_A P_h. PR1054 and the new injectivity
identify these, through the ACTUAL multiplication maps, with
P_h, W tensor P_h, A tensor P_h. Associativity gives precisely ordinary
polynomial arrows. The respective intersections with I2,I3,I4 are zero,
so F is their direct sum as a fixed negative module, proving (2).

The decomposition does not commute with Q. The invariant map has
Q(D)=0 and Q(P_h)=I4, with Q|P_h an isomorphism. Thus there is no claimed
Q-stable splitting, acyclic cancellation, or Bockstein equivalence from
(2). The full generated endpoint complex remains the one in PR1054.

Taking the contrapositive of (1) gives dim F4<16⇒e2(P_h)=0. Since I2=D
lies in NR2, that is the stated actual-embedding constraint im h⊂NR2.
The converse need not hold: this proof makes no rank assertion about a
P_h whose separated projection vanishes.
