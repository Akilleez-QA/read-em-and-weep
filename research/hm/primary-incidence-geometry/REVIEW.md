## Dimension, regular sequence, and integrality

The ten equations in the smooth eighteen-dimensional product give a lower bound of eight for every irreducible component. The rank-nine and rank-eight inverse images have dimensions at most seven and six, respectively. Thus every component meets the smooth irreducible eight-dimensional projective bundle over the rank-ten open. This gives a unique component of dimension eight. At each closed point the ideal has height ten and ten generators in a regular local ring. The Cohen-Macaulay complete-intersection criterion applies to these generators. Consequently the incidence is Cohen-Macaulay, with no embedded associated primes; its smooth dense open makes it generically reduced. These statements together imply that it is reduced and integral. Properness and birationality then follow from projectivity and the scheme-level kernel graph on the nonempty rank-four locus of PR555.

## Adjunction and cohomology

The regular section has bundle M tensor O(1,1), of rank ten. Adjunction gives the ordinary dualizing line bundle O(5,-5), since the ambient canonical line bundle is O(-5,-15). If linearizations are tracked under the convention that P(A) parametrizes lines, the constant determinant factor is det(A*) tensor det(S*) tensor det(M).

The Koszul terms are exterior^j M* tensor O(-j,-j). Taking Euler characteristics gives the stated alternating binomial polynomial for every integral twist. For j from one through ten, O_P14(-j) is acyclic, so every negative Koszul term is acyclic by the product formula. Hypercohomology therefore gives H^0(O_I)=C and H^i(O_I)=0 for i>0. This does not require normality or rational singularities.

## Rank-eight fiber calculation

The four distinct nonzero Heisenberg weights make the evaluation kernel the direct sum of a three-dimensional D_0 summand and four one-dimensional summands generated by N_b, where N_b kills w_b and has image Cw_b. Passing from affine parameter directions to projective tangent directions does not shrink the eight-dimensional evaluation image, because q itself lies in the evaluation kernel.

Modulo that image, the x-direction e_c contributes exactly t_(-c) N_(-c) w_(2c). The vectors w_(2c) and w_(-c) are independent for every nonzero c in F5, so its coefficient is a nonzero multiple of t_(-c), in the line Cw_(-c). The residual two-by-four matrix therefore has two columns in each of two independent lines. Its rank is two, one, or zero according as both parameter pairs, exactly one parameter pair, or neither parameter pair is nonzero. The full tangent dimensions are correspondingly 8, 9, and 10.

The stated singular set is consequently exactly the union of the two P4s, meeting in P2. The proof expressly makes a set-theoretic statement; it does not inadvertently identify a potentially thicker singular subscheme. No claim of normality or smooth resolution follows from these fiber calculations, and none is made.
