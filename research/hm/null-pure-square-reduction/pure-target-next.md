## 4. Actual reciprocal reflection, with the root-line correction

On C the algebraic automorphism

   r(x,y)=(1/x, i y/x²)

extends over the smooth projective completion. Direct pullback gives r*(dx/y)=i dx/y, so each pair bivector transforms by−1, and r² is the elliptic deck involution. Apply r on all four factors. On Y and Z it induces binary coefficient reversal projectively, and on P⁴ it induces exactly the HM matrix R above. The negative quadratic lift and positive quartic lift are compatible with multiplication.

The infinity point is sent to the branch point p₀=(0,0). Since div(x)=2p₀−2∞,

   L=r*N⊗N⁻¹ = ⊗_i p_i*O_C(p₀−∞)

is a two-torsion line bundle. The HM R isomorphism therefore identifies r*E with E⊗L, not with E directly. Omitting L would be a genuine error.

The torsion correction is available algebraically and to the required differential-algebroid order. Choose L²≅O. Its μ₂-torsor is finite étale and trivializes L by frames whose transition functions lie in μ₂, hence are locally constant ±1. Differential star operators kill their derivatives, and unary algebra maps fix them. Tensoring module transitions by those constants preserves every product, overlap map and triple equation. Thus tensoring by L⁻¹ gives an exact module equivalence; this is not an unsupported assertion that arbitrary line bundles quantize. Étale descent brings the construction back to the original algebraic setting. Scalar changes of the chosen HM R lift or flat torsion frames act trivially on End and do not alter the class.

Now combine r with (s,t)↦(−s,−t), or h↦−h on the diagonal. The first-order compensated class is restored: π changes sign, At(N⊗L)=At(N) because L is torsion, and B consequently changes sign. Identify first-order presentations, tensor by L⁻¹, and use pair-character uniqueness to compare the transported boundary. Any remaining valid second-order ambient difference has zero invariant characteristic image. Functoriality of the small-extension obstruction yields

   R[C_PP]=[C_PP], R[C_QQ]=[C_QQ], hence R(o₀)=o₀.

Although r has order4, its square is a deck action, hence acts trivially on the invariant obstruction target. The induced coefficient action is exactly the order-two R, with no h² sign. This gives the 12-dimensional reduction and annihilates M₀.

This reciprocal map is not the bare CM automorphism (x,y)↦(−x,iy). Indeed r is translation by p₀ composed with that CM map. The reciprocal map induces quartic REVERSAL, an established HM symmetry; the bare CM map induces a different quartic matrix. A no-lift theorem for the bare CM matrix does not exclude this construction. Conversely the present proof makes no claim that the bare CM matrix lifts.

## 5. Why full D5 vanishing still does not follow

For a quadratic q=aX²+bXY+cY² the fixed branch square is

   Δ(q)=(b²−4ac)ac[(a+c)²−b²].

It is reflection-invariant, consistently with §4. Under T its three bracket pieces have weights3,0,2 modulo5; the fixed Δ is not T-invariant. The torus generally changes the branch cover and is not an automorphism of the fixed upstairs curve. Thus M has a D5 representation but the actual fixed-source o₀ has only the proven reflection constraint. No T(o₀)=o₀ assertion, branch-linearity theorem, or replacement of the full source by Δ-multiplication is supplied here.
