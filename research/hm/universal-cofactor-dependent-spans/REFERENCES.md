# References and dependencies

- [Abstract trace-free evaluation](../universal-cofactor-normal-form/PROOF.md) fixes the operator model.
- [Higher-corank transversality](../universal-cofactor-higher-coranks/PROOF.md), Section 4A, gives the square-case argument. The rectangular proof is included in full here, so it can be checked without a black-box extension.
- [Audit](AUDIT.md) independently checks the trace-kernel formula, support incidences, rank ranges, orbit refinements and ordered-tuple exact sequence.

The elementary Grassmannian and generic-determinantal dimension arguments are supplied in the proof. No identification with effective classes of any geometric module or multiplication-adjointness theorem is used. Kernel and cokernel statements refer only to the abstract evaluation operator.
