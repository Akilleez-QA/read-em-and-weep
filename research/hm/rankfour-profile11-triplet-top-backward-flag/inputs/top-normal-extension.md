## 2. Shared row lemma: the common F type forces its actual backward kernel

Let B=(D+P)(-6) be the actual canonical top of F. The actual inclusion Z4=D(-6) has zero component to O(-10) by ordinary degree and zero component to the same-degree P block by inequivalence. Thus actual Z4 is exactly the D block of B.

The lower projection of beta_F onto the coherent O(-10) vanishes on B by degree. Because beta_F is onto (5), its complementary O(-10) row is an isomorphism onto that coherent lower line. Therefore

    B=ker(lower beta_F).

The actual upper row on this kernel is everywhere onto:

    u:B -> O(10), F^-=ker u,
    rank F^-=8, deg F^-=-64.                         (8)

Indeed a local preimage of any section of the upper summand of (5) with lower component zero belongs to B. This is surjectivity onto the genuine coherent image, not an assertion about internal derivative projection from B to F/B.

The actual u on Z4 kills Z3, since derivatives of Z3 lie in Z4 subset F. Its induced row on Z4/Z3=O(-2) is nonzero: otherwise Z4 would lie in F^- and its actual forward span Z5 would lie in F, contrary to (6). That nonzero scalar row O(-2)->O(10) has invariant zero divisor D12. Thus it is a nonzero unspecified multiple of the universal invariant f12.

Write q:P(-6)->O(10) for the remaining row. It is nonzero, since u is onto and the D row vanishes on D12. In fact q itself is onto. Its entries have degree sixteen. A nonempty common zero divisor would be invariant, of degree at most sixteen, hence D12. Dividing the row by its common f12 factor would give a nonzero equivariant map

    P(-6) -> O(-2),

which is impossible because Sym4 W=D contains no P. Hence q has no common zero.

Quotient the actual kernel (8) by actual Z3 and write Q=F^-/Z3. This is locally free because Z3 is a subbundle of the constant ambient and F^-/Z3 is torsion-free. It has rank four and degree -36, with exact sequence

    0 -> Q -> O(-2)+P(-6) --(f12,q)--> O(10) -> 0.  (9)

No actual scalar representative of either row is chosen. The following three twists determine Q.

At twist six the source section representation is D+P, while the target is

    Sym16 W=2D+V_alt+P.

The D row is nonzero multiplication by f12; the P row is nonzero q. Each is injective on its irreducible coefficient module, and their images cannot cancel because D and P are inequivalent. Thus H0(Q(6))=0 and top Q<=-7.

At twist seven the source is

and the target is Sym17 W=S4+2T6+W'. Its W' component is nonzero. Otherwise q would kill the canonical Euler W'(-7) inside P(-6), in the exact sequence

    0 -> W'(-7) -> P(-6) -> W'(-5) -> 0,

and factor through W'(-5)->O(10). That Hom space is zero because Sym15 W=S4+2T6 contains no W'. Therefore the W' part of the section kernel is zero. Any nonzero kernel on the 2T6 part would contain a full T6. But top Q<=-7 implies h0(Q(7))<=rank Q=4, excluding a six-dimensional T6. Hence H0(Q(7))=0 and top Q<=-8.

At twist eight the source and target are respectively

The kernel must contain V_alt by its multiplicities two and one. Since top Q<=-8, h0(Q(8))<=4. The source has no scalar or two-dimensional constituent; adding any further source constituent to V_alt would exceed this bound. Consequently H0(Q(8))=V_alt.

## 6. The required scalar quotient forces E=W(-1)

Suppose E has unequal ordinary splitting degrees. Equivariant Birkhoff splitting and the absence of nontrivial scalar characters give

    E=O(a)+O(-a-2), a>=0.

Its center-evenness makes a even, though parity is unnecessary for the following argument. Consider the required everywhere-surjective quotient q_E:E->O(10) in (16). Its top component belongs to H0(O(10-a))^G, which is zero unless a=10: for a>10 the degree is negative, and for 0<=a<10 it is positive and at most ten, below the invariant-divisor minimum.

If a differs from ten, q_E must factor through O(-a-2). A scalar map from that line to O(10) has degree a+12>0 and cannot be everywhere onto. This contradicts (16).

For a=10, q_E on the top O(10) must be a nonzero constant isomorphism; the lower-only map again cannot be onto. Thus the extension splits, and E=O(10)+O(-12). But every map from B=(D+P)(-6) to O(-12) vanishes by ordinary degree. This contradicts the everywhere-surjective beta_B in (17).

All unequal splitting degrees are excluded, so E=R O(-1) for a two-dimensional coefficient representation R. Twisting (16) by one and taking sections gives an equivariant injection

    R=H0(E(1)) -> H0(O(11))=Sym11 W=W+S4+T6.

The only two-dimensional subrepresentation is W. Hence

    E=W(-1).                                        (18)

In particular (16) is nontrivial. The proof identifies its middle type from its actual quotient and actual derivative surjectivity, not from the mere dimension of an Ext space.

The correct exclusion is the required quotient E->O(10): its O(0) component is zero by invariant degree ten, and its O(-2) component has a degree-twelve zero divisor. It cannot be onto. The preceding argument applies this same obstruction to every unequal type.
