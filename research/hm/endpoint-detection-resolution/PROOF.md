# Exact ideal image, residue obstruction, and division-free channel reconstruction

## Inputs and statement

Work over C. Let G=A5, let A be its three-dimensional rotation representation, A' the other three-dimensional irreducible, D the five-dimensional irreducible, and P the four-dimensional irreducible. Set X=P(A). Use the canonical endpoint interface

0 → K → D⊗O_X → A⊗O_X(1) → 0,
F=K*⊗P⊗O_X(2),   E:Q⊗O_X → F,   dim Q=2.

All elements of Q are equivariant families, so Q is a trivial G-module. The pinned inputs are:

- PR661, commit 1325647fd916bf2daa71b11efeae6d52cfefa2cd: the kernel interface and two-dimensional family space.
- PR668, commit ca3c34c40363bde6315a0797eb66a5d3ea68c104: reducedness of the detection scheme and distinction of the C5 and C3 kernel channels.
- PR669, commit 8c17fe9c5736d657cf07f894b24917ec301e09af: the rank-at-most-one scheme of E is exactly the reduced disjoint union Σ5∐Σ3 of six C5 axes and ten C3 axes; E has rank one there and rank two elsewhere. Its two exceptional channel lines ℓ5,ℓ3⊂Q have respective zero schemes Σ5,Σ3, and Q=ℓ5⊕ℓ3.

The new result is an exact, orbit-separated ideal image and a canonical residue test for lifting from the reflexive quotient. It also gives a division-free test for polynomial channel reconstruction, valid across every exceptional axis. No selected actual HM/source-instance coefficients, points or ranks are evaluated, and no large computation is used.

## 1. The dual image is the direct sum of the two axis ideals

Write I_i=I_{Σ_i}. Under Q*=ℓ5*⊕ℓ3*, there is equality of subsheaves of Q*⊗O_X:

                 im(E*)=J:=(ℓ5*⊗I_5)⊕(ℓ3*⊗I_3).       (1)

Separate surjectivity to each ideal would not alone prove (1). Here is the joint local argument. At p∈Σ5 let R=O_{X,p}, m=m_p, and choose generators q5,q3 of the two channel lines. Every entry of the q5 column lies in m; the q3 column has a unit entry. Change a local frame of F to make the q3 column e0. Write the other column as a e0+v, with v in the span of the seven remaining basis vectors. Then a∈m, and the entries of v generate the maximal-minor ideal of E. Reducedness of the detection scheme says precisely that this ideal is m.

The rows of E therefore generate all (m,0), as well as (a,1). Subtracting (a,0) supplies (0,1). The image is m⊕R. At Σ3 the argument is identical with the summands reversed. Off the two orbits E* is surjective. These stalk identities prove (1), without altering the intrinsic decomposition of Q.

Put H=ker(E*). There are canonical exact sequences

0 → H → F* → J → 0,                                      (2)
0 → J → Q*⊗O_X → B → 0,
B=(ℓ5*⊗O_{Σ5})⊕(ℓ3*⊗O_{Σ3}).                           (3)

In particular the dual cokernel consists of sixteen reduced skyscrapers, with trivial stabilizer character in each fiber.

## 2. Local normal form, torsion-free quotient, and reflexive completion

At an exceptional point, the seven entries of v generate m. Choose two whose classes form a basis of m/m². They are a regular system of parameters x,y and generate m by Nakayama. Elementary target operations replace v by (x,y,0,…,0). A local source operation removes a from the e0 component. Thus E is locally equivalent to

R² → R⊕R²⊕R⁵,    (b,c) ↦ (c,bx,by,0).                  (4)

This local source change is used only to establish the normal form; it is not a global redefinition of the exceptional channels.

The dual kernel in (4) is R⊕R⁵: the syzygies of (x,y) are generated by (−y,x). Hence H is a rank-six vector bundle everywhere. Also

T:=coker(E) ≅ R⁵⊕m locally at each exceptional point,      (5)

because the cokernel of R→R², b↦(bx,by), is identified with m by (u,v)↦yu−xv. Off the axes T is locally free of rank six. Consequently T is torsion-free, and its double dual S=T** is locally free.

The first sheaf Ext of each O_{Σ_i} against O_X vanishes, since its stalks are residue fields of regular local rings of dimension two. Dualizing (3) therefore gives J*=Q⊗O_X, and

Ext¹(J,O_X)=R_Δ:=⊕_{i=5,3} ℓ_i⊗Ext²(O_{Σ_i},O_X).

Dualizing (2) now gives the canonical exact sequence

0 → Q⊗O_X → F → H* → R_Δ → 0.                           (6)

Its first map is E. Hence T injects into H* with finite-length quotient. Since H* is locally free and agrees with T off a codimension-two subset, this identifies H* canonically with S=T**. In particular

                    0 → T → S → R_Δ → 0.                (7)

The residue morphism in (7) includes the extension information determined by E. The theorem does not assert that support and character alone specify E or its extension uniquely.

## 3. The residue characters differ from the dual-cokernel characters

For a reduced point p on a smooth surface, equivariant local Koszul duality identifies

              Ext²(O_p,O_X) ≅ det(T_pX)⊗O_p.             (8)

Equivalently Ext²(O_{Σ_i},O_X)=ω_{Σ_i}⊗ω_X^{-1}|_{Σ_i}; the intrinsic dualizing line of a reduced point is trivial, but the ambient anticanonical fiber need not be.

At a Cn axis, n=3 or5, the stabilizer is D_{2n}, and the axis line L⊂A has character ε: cyclic rotations act as +1, while the involutions outside the cyclic subgroup act as −1. Since det A is trivial,

det(T_pX)=det(Hom(L,A/L))=det A⊗L^{-3}=ε.                (9)

The residue skyscrapers thus have the reflection-sign character, not the trivial character of (3). In representations on global sections,

H⁰(R_Δ) ≅ Ind_{D10}^{A5} ε ⊕ Ind_{D6}^{A5} ε
         ≅ (A⊕A') ⊕ (A⊕A'⊕P).                         (10)

Here the channel lines remain understood as trivial one-dimensional factors. To verify (10), Frobenius reciprocity gives multiplicity of ε in R|_{D_{2n}} equal to

(1/(2n))(Σ_{h∈Cn}χ_R(h) − Σ_{h∈D_{2n}\Cn}χ_R(h)).

For either three-dimensional rotation character the sum on Cn is n and each involution has character −1, giving multiplicity one. For P the character values are 4 at identity, 0 on involutions, 1 on C3, and −1 on C5, giving multiplicity zero for D10 and one for D6. These summands already exhaust dimensions six and ten, respectively. In particular H⁰(R_Δ)^G=0.

In contrast, H⁰(B) is the sum of the two untwisted permutation representations on the six and ten axes. Confusing B with R_Δ would give an incorrect invariant obstruction space.

## 4. Exact global lifting and residue interpolation

For every integer n, H¹(X,O_X(n))=0. The sequence 0→Q O_X→F→T→0 therefore gives

H⁰(F(n))/E(Q⊗H⁰(O_X(n))) ≅ H⁰(T(n))
                         ≅ ker[H⁰(S(n))→H⁰(R_Δ(n))].    (11)

Thus a section of the reflexive quotient lifts to an actual section of F(n) if and only if its sixteen canonical residues vanish. Whenever it lifts, the set of lifts is a torsor under Q⊗H⁰(O_X(n)). This is a necessary-and-sufficient criterion, not just a count of possible obstructions.

At n=0 every residue assignment is achievable. Indeed dualizing the defining sequence for K and tensoring by P O_X(2) gives

0 → P⊗A*⊗O_X(1) → P⊗D*⊗O_X(2) → F → 0.                (12)

Standard cohomology of plane line bundles gives h⁰(F)=20·6−12·3=84 and H¹(F)=H²(F)=0. It follows that h⁰(T)=82 and H¹(T)=H²(T)=0. Sequence (7) yields

0 → H⁰(T) → H⁰(S) → H⁰(R_Δ) → 0,   h⁰(S)=98.           (13)

Therefore all sixteen sign-twisted residues interpolate freely in S. A residue-free section lifts to F, uniquely modulo the two constant channel sections.

Moreover T and S are 0-regular. Twisting (12) and 0→Q O_X→F→T→0 proves H¹(T(−1))=0 and H²(T(−2))=0. The finite support of R_Δ then gives the same vanishings for S. Consequently both are globally generated, their higher cohomology vanishes in nonnegative twists, and the residue map in (13) remains surjective after every nonnegative twist. These are consequences of the exact resolution, not an appeal to numerical Chern classes.

The reflexive completion satisfies the stronger bound: S is (−2)-regular. Indeed F*=K⊗P*⊗O_X(−2) has resolution 0→F*→D⊗P*⊗O_X(−2)→A⊗P*⊗O_X(−1)→0, so H¹(F*)=0. Also H⁰(J)=0, since each orbit is nonempty, and (2) gives H¹(H)=0. Serre duality on X now gives H¹(S(−3))=H¹(H)*=0 and H²(S(−4))=H⁰(H(1))*=0; the latter vanishing follows from H(1)⊂F*(1)⊂D⊗P*⊗O_X(−1). Thus S(−2) is globally generated. Twisting (12) by −2 gives h⁰(F(−2))=20, hence h⁰(T(−2))=20. Since H¹(T(−2))=0, (7) yields h⁰(S(−2))=36. In particular the rank-six completion already has a globally generating space of thirty-six sections after this negative twist.

There is no new equivariant ambiguity hidden in the sixteen residues. Indeed H⁰(F)^G has dimension two: (12) gives the multiplicity difference of P in D⊗Sym²A and A⊗A. The identities Sym²A=1⊕D, A⊗A=1⊕A⊕D and D⊗D=1⊕A⊕A'⊕2P⊕2D make that difference two. The injective map Q→H⁰(F)^G is therefore an isomorphism. Exactness of invariants for a finite group in characteristic zero, together with (10), proves

                         H⁰(T)^G=H⁰(S)^G=0.             (14)

## 4a. Twisting exposes precisely two odd-degree invariant obstructions

The axis line L has character ε, so O_X(n)|_p=L^{−n} has character ε^n. Thus the residue representation in arbitrary degree is

H⁰(R_Δ(n)) ≅ Ind_{D10}^{A5}(ε^{n+1}) ⊕ Ind_{D6}^{A5}(ε^{n+1}).

For even n this is the sign-induced representation (10), with no invariants. For odd n it is the untwisted permutation representation on the two orbits. It has exactly two invariant directions, one on each orbit; this follows directly because the invariant functions on a transitive finite G-set are constant.

Consequently an invariant section of S(n) always has zero residue when n is even and therefore has an invariant lift to F(n): first use (11), then average a lift over G. For odd n, exactly two scalar orbitwise residue conditions test invariant lifting. These two conditions are genuinely independent for odd n≥−1, rather than merely possible obstructions. Indeed (12) and the line-bundle cohomology calculation above give H¹(T(n))=0 for every n≥−2. Hence H⁰(S(n))→H⁰(R_Δ(n)) is onto in that range, and remains onto after taking G-invariants. This identifies the complete invariant lifting obstruction in every nonnegative degree, without evaluating the endpoint family.

## 5. Division-free reconstruction across all sixteen exceptional axes

There is a basis-free morphism

W_E:F → (det Q)*⊗Λ³F,
W_E(u)(q∧r)=E(q)∧E(r)∧u.

Then the following sequence is exact at F:

                    0 → Q⊗O_X → F →[W_E] (det Q)*⊗Λ³F. (15)

To prove the kernel assertion, let U=X\(Σ5∐Σ3). There E is a rank-two subbundle, and W_E(u)=0 exactly when u lies in its span. The coefficient section in Q⊗O_U is unique. Since X is smooth and the omitted set has codimension two, regular sections of a vector bundle extend uniquely across it. Extending the coefficients and using equality on the dense open set proves equality everywhere. The same argument applies on each open subset of X, proving sheaf exactness. Alternatively (4) reduces the assertion to the regular-sequence syzygy for x,y.

For every integer n and u∈H⁰(F(n)), this gives the explicit equivalence

W_E(u)=0  ⇔  u=E(f), for a unique f∈Q⊗H⁰(O_X(n)).       (16)

Choosing nonzero exceptional-channel generators s5,s3, the condition is simply s5∧s3∧u=0, or vanishing of all 3×3 minors of the three-column local matrix. It is independent of their nonzero rescalings. For n=0 the coefficients are constants; for n<0 the only solution is u=0. One may reconstruct coefficients on any rank-two chart, and their unique global extension requires no additional condition at the axes and no choice of a globally nonvanishing minor.

Equations (11) and (16) solve different problems: (11) tests whether a reflexive quotient datum comes from F; (16) tests whether a section already in F belongs to the polynomial channel family. Neither asserts prescribed-source membership, higher ambient lifting, identification of the actual HM channel, the fourth Bockstein, or a repair of the original theorem.

## Contribution and boundaries

The reduced rank/support classification now yields an exact orbit-separated resolution, a torsion-free quotient with canonical sixteen-residue completion, a necessary-and-sufficient global lifting criterion, free nonnegative-twist residue interpolation, and a division-free channel reconstruction identity. All proofs are structural. They leave the (1,3) Gauss, full affine R2, global monad homotopy, actual-channel identification and higher ambient-lift questions unchanged.
