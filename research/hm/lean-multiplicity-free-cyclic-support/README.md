# Multiplicity-free cyclic support — Lean checked

For three pairwise nonisomorphic simple modules over a possibly
noncommutative ring R, the submodule generated by a vector v consists
exactly of the vectors x whose coordinates vanish wherever v does:

```text
x in span_R {v}  iff  for every i, v_i = 0 implies x_i = 0.
```

Thus v generates the whole product exactly when all three coordinates are
nonzero. Zero vectors and all partial-support cases are included. The
coordinate-support conclusion is derived; it is not an input hypothesis.

The file contains **four checked declarations: two helpers and two
substantive cyclic-support results**, in `OAI.HorrocksMumford`:

| Declaration | Statement |
| --- | --- |
| `multiplicityFree_hom_eq_zero` | Linear maps between distinct factors vanish. |
| `multiplicityFree_endomorphism_coordinate` | Every endomorphism of the product acts coordinatewise. |
| `mem_cyclicSpan_iff_coordinate_support` | Membership in the cyclic submodule is exactly the support condition above. |
| `cyclicSpan_eq_top_iff` | The cyclic submodule is the whole product exactly when every coordinate is nonzero. |

The hypotheses are `[Ring R]`, a dependent product indexed by `Fin 3`,
simple left R-modules, and pairwise absence of R-linear equivalences.
Schur's lemma gives off-diagonal vanishing. Semisimplicity supplies a
complementary projection onto the cyclic submodule; its derived coordinate
behavior proves the support theorem. No scalar description of endomorphisms,
commutative-ring assumption, or assumed preservation of that cyclic submodule
by coordinate projections is used. The only direct import is
`Mathlib.RingTheory.SimpleModule.Basic`; no earlier OAI unit is imported.
