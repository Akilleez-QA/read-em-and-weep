# Only lengths five and seven remain at the rank-one component

Combine the newly proved exclusion of rank six with the earlier exclusions of ranks four, five, seven, and nine. The established generic quadratic rank is therefore either eight or ten.

Let K be the residue field of the generic point of the rank-one component, and A its transverse local Artin algebra. The previously audited even normal-form and sharpened-spectrum results identify A, noncanonically, with its quadratic graded algebra on four generators. For ranks at least eight its degree-three part vanishes. Since the quadratic relations have independent rank r, the graded dimensions are

    dim A_0=1, dim A_1=4, dim A_2=10-r, A_j=0 for j>=3.

Consequently there are precisely two remaining necessary possibilities:

- r=10: Hilbert function (1,4), length five, and the maximal ideal satisfies m^2=0.
- r=8: Hilbert function (1,4,2), length seven, m^2 has dimension two and m^3=0. In particular m^2 is nonzero.

This does not assert that the two-dimensional multiplication map in the second case has been identified, nor does it select which case occurs. It is a classification of the remaining Hilbert functions and nilpotence lengths, not an isomorphism classification of every rank-eight algebra.

In the rank-eight case the surviving ordered Chern pair is (c2(F*),c2(R))=(19,17), by the independently reviewed intermediate-sextic exclusion and its addendum. In the rank-ten case the existing exact resolution gives (69,75). These are conditional alternatives, not evaluated actual ranks or Chern instances.
