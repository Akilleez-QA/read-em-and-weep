# Independent audit

Status: PASS. Reviewed the entire frozen proof, including linkage, equality lifting, and the scope of the surviving branch. No mathematical corrections are outstanding.

1. The cubic and quartic gcd residual bounds are correct for nonreduced plane schemes. In the coprime quartic case the residual in CI(4,4) has length one. The canonical residual sequence at twist four has connecting map dual to restriction of linear forms to that residual point. This restriction is surjective, so no third quartic appears. The asserted plane bounds follow.

2. A global cubic and coprime quintic give a containment in CI(3,5). Equality of surface degrees makes the kernel of the structure-sheaf quotient supported in smaller dimension; the complete intersection is Cohen--Macaulay and unmixed, so this kernel is zero. Thus the claimed global equation dimensions in that case are justified even for nonreduced schemes. The invariant one-dimensional section space multiplies injectively by quadrics into H0(R), producing a fifteen-dimensional invariant subspace disjoint from the embedded irreducible J of dimension twenty. The contradiction with h0(R)=25 is valid without asserting irreducibility of the entire H0(R).

3. The regular flag inequalities and the nontrivial central weights correctly force z3=z4=0, z5=11, and equality in all the needed curve bounds. The curve cubic and a coprime quintic then give the same purity argument in P3, so the general curve section is CI(3,5).

4. The lifting lemma is valid. Absence of zero-dimensional subsheaves excludes closed associated points, giving stalk depth at least one. Auslander--Buchsbaum therefore kills sheaf Ext^4 on P4; at nonclosed points its vanishing follows from the smaller regular local dimension. The local-to-global Ext spectral sequence and Serre vanishing kill global Ext^4 at sufficiently negative twists. Serre duality gives the required negative H0 vanishing. The regular hyperplane sequence propagates this vanishing and, using the curve's ambient-section surjectivity, inductively gives ambient-section surjectivity for Z in every nonnegative degree. Hence H1(I_Z(t))=0 for all t and the cubic lifts. Here the property called projective normality in the lemma is precisely this surjectivity of ambient polynomial sections, which CI(3,5) has.

5. For reflexive E, the two depth-lemma applications give depth(O_Z)>=1 in codimension at least three and in particular exclude zero-dimensional torsion. The lifting contradiction excludes c2(E)=15. For merely torsion-free R that extra depth is not established, so the proof correctly retains (10,15), with h0(R)=20 and unavoidable zero-dimensional torsion in O_Z. The illustrative exterior-point construction supports this limitation and does not assert its realization by the canonical sheaf.

Conclusion: rank-six pair (15,10) is excluded; only (10,15) remains. No actual operation or HM instance was evaluated.
