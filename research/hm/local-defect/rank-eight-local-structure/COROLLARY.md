# Structural restrictions and the full length-eleven exclusion

## Universal local structure

In the established actual-rank-eight branch, all positive canonical local defect lengths at most ten have already been excluded. The universal square-zero theorem now shows that an isolated equivariantly self-dual reflexive module of rank r over a regular local fourfold has finite Ext² defect Q of length at most 5r whenever m²Q=0. Therefore every nonzero canonical rank-two defect has m²Q≠0, independently of its length: its Loewy length is at least three.

A separate general representation theorem strengthens the necessary rank bound to

    r >= β3(Q)−2β4(Q)+a_H(Tor1(D(Q),k)),

where a_H measures excess irreducible multiplicities over the dual representation. This is effective even when the previous scalar inequality is strict. The old equality-case self-duality criterion is a special case. The audited twisted wedge/residue-field families require rank at least six times their number of summands, rather than the scalar lower bound twice that number. No realization at the new lower bound is asserted.

## Full local length-eleven exclusion

The initial length-eleven theorem reduces the finite dual P=D(Q) to three radical-layer sequences: (2,4,5), (2,4,4,1), (3,4,4). The TOP_THREE addendum excludes (3,4,4) using equality-case representation self-duality and a six-versus-four quadratic dimension contradiction. The final top-two addendum excludes both remaining patterns by full-stabilizer weights and minimal relation counts. These proofs allow filtered corrections and do not assume arbitrary three-layer modules are graded.

Thus canonical local lengths one through eleven are impossible. The numerical Betti counterexample at length ten remains a valid finite module; the additional realizability and representation conditions are essential to the actual-defect exclusions.

## Global consequence

The nonzero finite sheaf T=Ext²(B,O) and saturation defect D have equal support and local lengths. A supported canonical orbit now contributes at least 30·12=360. The earlier orbit results show every supported noncanonical orbit contributes at least 600. Consequently

    length(T)=length(D) >= 360.

Below 600, support is exactly the canonical thirty-point orbit, with constant local length m∈{12,13,14,15,16,17,18,19}. The only necessary totals in that range are

    360, 390, 420, 450, 480, 510, 540, 570.

For length(T)=5q, this says q>=72 and 0<q<120 implies q∈{72,78,84,90,96,102,108,114}.
