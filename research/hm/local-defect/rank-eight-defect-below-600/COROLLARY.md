# Support below length 600 and the length-ten obstruction boundary

## Conditional global support statement

In the established actual-rank-eight branch, the nonzero finite sheaf T=Ext²(B,O) and the actual-image saturation defect D have identical support and local lengths. The audited orbit and local theorems give the following necessary contributions for each supported orbit:

- Canonical orbit, size 30: local length at least 10, contribution at least 300.

## A genuine obstruction to the proposed numerical method

The local length-ten proof constructs the natural wedge module P=W⊕Λ²W, with x.v=x∧v and x.Λ²W=0, and its finite dual Q=D(P). It proves β3(Q)−2β4(Q)=2. Therefore the universal numerical inequality alone cannot exclude all length-ten modules, even with full stabilizer equivariance. This is a rigorous counterexample to that proposed blanket method, not a realization as Ext² of the rank-two kernel.

The subsequent equivariant equality-case theorem supplies a further necessary condition. When an actual isolated determinant-trivial rank-two defect saturates the numerical inequality, Tor₃(Q,k) must be isomorphic to Tor₁(D(Q),k) equivariantly. Equivalently the latter representation must be self-dual. For the wedge example and every character twist, this fails on the canonical C5² subgroup: Sym²W is not self-dual there. Hence this particular numerical-boundary module is excluded as an actual defect by additional representation data.

These facts do not amount to an exhaustive exclusion of local length ten. Other finite modules remain to be examined, and total length 300 remains unresolved. No actual generic rank, finite defect, operation-specific value, or algebraicity conclusion is established.
