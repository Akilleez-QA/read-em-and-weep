# Local length twelve excluded; cyclic defects require at least twenty-two

In the established actual-rank-eight branch, B is a stable equivariant rank-two reflexive sheaf on P4, with equivariantly trivial determinant and isolated singularities. Its finite nonzero defect T=Ext²(B,O) and saturation defect D=F/G have equal support and local lengths.

The new noncyclic length-twelve theorem excludes every local length-twelve defect whose finite dual is noncyclic. The independent cyclic-finite-dual theorem forces a complete intersection and, at the canonical stabilizer, local length at least twenty-two. Thus length twelve is excluded completely, together with the previously excluded lengths one through eleven.

A supported canonical orbit therefore contributes at least 30·13=390. Previously audited orbit restrictions make every noncanonical contribution at least 600. Consequently

    length(T)=length(D) >= 390.

Below 600, support is exactly the canonical thirty-point orbit, with common local length m∈{13,14,15,16,17,18,19}. The only necessary totals there are

    390, 420, 450, 480, 510, 540, 570.

For length(T)=5q, q>=78 and 0<q<120 implies q∈{78,84,90,96,102,108,114}.

The cyclic theorem provides a further structural restriction: if a canonical finite-dual stalk is cyclic, its contribution alone is at least 30·22=660. Therefore every total below 600 has noncyclic finite-dual stalks at all canonical points. They also retain the previously established non-square-zero property. No cyclic or noncyclic candidate is asserted realizable.

The cyclic lower bound follows from four-generator complete-intersection rigidity, self-duality of the relation representation, filtered quadratic/cubic counts, and a socle multiplication bound. It does not require a multiplicity equality criterion. The noncyclic proof uses a fresh length-twelve enumeration, explicitly including the new three-V representation type and filtered corrections.

The actual rank-eight branch remains unresolved. No upper length bound, actual rank selection, physical operation value, or new algebraicity conclusion is obtained.
