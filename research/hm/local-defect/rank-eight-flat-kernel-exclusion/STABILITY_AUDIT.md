# Independent audit: stability and unique quadratic relation

Status: PASS. Reviewed STABILITY.md in full. No outstanding corrections.

For a semistable reflexive rank-two sheaf with trivial determinant and nonzero c2, two generically independent sections have nonzero constant wedge and trivialize the sheaf on its locally free locus. Reflexive extension then trivializes it globally, a contradiction. If the full section image has rank one, its saturation is a reflexive line bundle O(d); semistability and existence of a section force d=0. Thus the section bound h0<=1 is valid even with codimension-three singularities.

If B had its one allowed section, the globally exact square-line sequence and H1(O)=0 would give a two-dimensional equivariant submodule of End(Sym^2 V). The center is trivial on this module, and the twenty-four-character orbit argument forces the whole Heisenberg group to act trivially. The established commutant 1 plus 3 plus 5 has no two-dimensional submodule. Therefore H0(B)=0 and the canonical square is the sole global quadratic relation in H0(Ktilde(2)).

A semistable but nonstable rank-two B has a saturated slope-zero rank-one subsheaf. It is reflexive and hence O, contradicting this section vanishing. Thus B is slope-stable.

Combined with the companion audits, the conditional rank-eight kernel is stable, reflexive, has c1=0,c2=5, and is not locally free. In particular its nonempty singular locus lies in the locus where Q drops below rank eight: wherever Q has rank eight it has constant maximal rank locally and its kernel is a vector bundle. This does not prove a quadratic base point exists or exclude rank eight.
