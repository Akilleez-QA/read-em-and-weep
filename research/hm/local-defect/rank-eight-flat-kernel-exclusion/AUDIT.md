# Independent audit

Status: PASS. Reviewed the complete proof, independently checked the primary-source equality theorem, and verified its representation-theoretic application. No outstanding corrections.

Guimarães–Jardim, Definition 2.4 and Lemma 2.5, explicitly imply trivial reflexive hull for a slope-semistable sheaf with vanishing first and second Chern intersections on a good variety. Projective space meets their hypotheses. Independently verified source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.01506 . Langer's Corollary 6 also explicitly permits a reflexive semistable Higgs sheaf with only the first two stated intersections zero and gives locally free stable zero-Chern factors: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00222-014-0534-z . With zero Higgs field on simply connected projective space, the factors are O and their extensions split by H1(O)=0. Thus no unproved local-freeness or higher-Chern vanishing hypothesis is being inserted.

For a=5 this gives B=O^2, K=O(-2)^2, and the globally exact square-line extension splits as an ordinary sheaf because H1(O)=0. The intrinsic equivariant space H0(Ktilde(2)) is three-dimensional and injects into End(Sym^2 V); the canonical square maps to its identity. No equivariant splitting choice is needed.

Central triviality alone does not imply Heisenberg triviality, and the proof correctly supplies the missing argument: a nonzero character of H5/center has a full orbit of twenty-four under SL2(F5), incompatible with dimension three. Thus this module lies in the Heisenberg commutant. The established tensor decomposition and Schur's lemma identify that commutant with the multiplicity-free module 1 plus 3 plus 5. Its three-dimensional submodule cannot contain the invariant identity line. The finite-group semisimplicity argument is valid and excludes a=5.

Combined with the independently audited kernel-Chern proof, a=0 and c2(B)=5 follow. The resulting B is semistable, reflexive, and not locally free. This does not exclude rank eight or force all saturation defects to vanish. No actual operation or HM instance was evaluated.
