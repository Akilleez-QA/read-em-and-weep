# Dependencies and remaining scope

This slice is stacked on [PR274](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/pull/274), exact commit [714e2643752d9aabf52d25686501f9d0ae552b61](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/commit/714e2643752d9aabf52d25686501f9d0ae552b61). It uses total Ext1=P and total Ext2 free of rank sixteen over B, with the fixed H action.

The linear presentation and B-flat family come from [PR228](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/pull/228), exact commit [06c7e2f13e902d819bb6945c033fd455a5f28eac](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/commit/06c7e2f13e902d819bb6945c033fd455a5f28eac). The audit checks the two-monomial block directly against that presentation.

The new conclusion strengthens fieldwise separation to arbitrary C-algebra parameter recovery and proves nontriviality of the displayed tangent direction at the boundary. It does not solve the full higher-order deformation problem or the actual HM/source realization problem. The coefficient algebra carries trivial H action and coordinate changes are excluded.
