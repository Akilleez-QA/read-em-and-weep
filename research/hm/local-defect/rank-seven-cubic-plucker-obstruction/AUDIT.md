# Independent audit

Status: PASS, Sections 1–6. No corrections remain; this audit is frozen.

The Galois identification is justified at the representation level. For each nontrivial Heisenberg central character the five-dimensional irreducible restriction is unique. Two extensions of that same irreducible restriction differ by a character of the quotient group, and perfectness removes the ambiguity. Therefore the four five-dimensional modules transform exactly as displayed under zeta→zeta². The two two-dimensional quotient modules are exchanged by their explicit conjugate character rows, a separate argument correctly distinguished from Heisenberg extension uniqueness.

Applying these transformations to the imported tensor identities gives Lambda²A=V*⊗Q, Sym²A=V*⊗T_P, A⊗V*=V⊗(T_Q⊕P), and Lambda³A=V⊗Q. The Schur decompositions then give M21=Sym³Q⊕(P⊗Q) and M3⊕M21=(T_Q⊕P)⊗T_P. The latter has no invariants by inequivalence of T_Q,T_P and central-involution parity.

The fourth symmetric-power character is (5,5,1,−1,−1,0,0,0,0); using the stated class sizes, its squared norm is exactly one. Thus F is irreducible. The invariant-free four-dimensional P⊗Q contains neither F nor T_Q: a three-dimensional summand would leave a trivial one-dimensional summand. Exterior Cauchy has exactly the three displayed summands, and each multiplicity contribution to Hom_N(V,Lambda³Sym²V) vanishes by these facts or parity. The asserted Hom vanishing follows without a dimension-only representation identification.

For residual degree three, the established lifting theorem produces a degree-one Lambda³S-valued lift. Its uniqueness follows by applying that same theorem to a degree-one element annihilated by wedge with the square, where a negative-degree lift forces zero. Perfectness fixes the determinant linearization, so equivariance of the primitive Plücker vector and uniqueness make the lift equivariant. The Hom vanishing contradicts its nonzero wedge. The surviving table entry is exactly (k,d,e)=(3,0,8), with det I=O(4).

The result remains conditional on generic rank seven, and no decomposability of the lift or actual operation evaluation is inferred.
