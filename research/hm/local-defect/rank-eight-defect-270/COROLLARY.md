# Combined finite-defect lower bound 270

## Assumptions and inputs

Work in the established conditional actual-rank-eight branch. Its kernel B is a stable rank-two reflexive sheaf on P4 with equivariantly trivial determinant and only isolated singularities. The finite sheaf T=Ext²(B,O) is nonzero. The saturation defect D=F/G has the same support and local lengths as T by the already proved finite-duality identity.

Use the local exclusions of lengths one through eight at the canonical thirty-point orbit E, together with the classification and local obstruction for the unique 250-point orbit E250. All other point orbits have at least 300 points.

## Derivation

If E is contained in Supp(T), its constant local length is at least nine, so it contributes at least 30·9=270.

If E250 is contained in Supp(T), its constant local length is at least two, so it contributes at least 500.

Any other support orbit contributes at least 300. Since T is nonzero, at least one of these alternatives occurs. Therefore

    length(T)=length(D) >= 270.

If the total is below 300, the second and third types of orbit cannot occur. Support is then exactly E. Its common local length m satisfies 9<=m<10, hence m=9 and

    length(T)=length(D)=270.

In notation length(T)=5q, q>=54, and 0<q<60 forces q=54.

## What is and is not established

This excludes total length240 by the new local length-eight argument, and excludes total length250 by a separate stabilizer character obstruction. It also proves that the earlier 250-point orbit gap is attained by a unique orbit, while that orbit cannot carry simple local defects under these hypotheses.

No existence of an actual length270 defect is proved. No upper bound on total length is obtained. The actual generic rank remains eight or ten, and no operation-specific obstruction value has been evaluated. These are equivariant local and global sheaf-theoretic necessities, not a new algebraicity assertion.
