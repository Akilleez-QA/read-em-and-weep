# Refined finite-defect alternatives in the conditional rank-eight branch

Assume the actual rank-eight branch and all previously established equivariant kernel and saturation sequences. Let B be its stable rank-two reflexive kernel with trivial determinant, and let T=Ext²(B,O) and D=F/G. The finite-singularity and finite-image-defect proofs establish that T and D are nonzero finite sheaves with identical support and identical local lengths.

The refined point-orbit theorem shows that the canonical orbit E of thirty Heisenberg eigenlines is unique below size 250. Consequently, if 0<length(T)<250, its support is E and equivariance makes each stalk have the same length m; hence length(T)=30m with 1≤m≤8.

The local character obstruction excludes m=1. Its determinant hypothesis applies: N is perfect. Indeed its SL2(F5) complement is perfect, the natural module H5/Z has zero coinvariants, and the Heisenberg commutator is Z. Thus the commutator subgroup contains the complement and all of H5. Any linearization of the trivial determinant line on projective space is a character of N and must be trivial.

At each point of E an order-four stabilizer element has cotangent determinant -1. An isolated rank-two reflexive module with equivariantly trivial determinant and length-one Ext² would require δ³=χ⁻⁴. Evaluation at this element is impossible. Therefore m≥2.

It follows that length(T)=length(D) is at least 60. If it is below 250, the only remaining necessary possibilities are

    60, 90, 120, 150, 180, 210, 240.

In these cases support is exactly E and each local length is respectively 2 through 8. In the prior notation length(T)=5q, this says q≥12, and 0<q<50 implies q∈{12,18,24,30,36,42,48}. Larger lengths still obey the prior multiple-of-five condition.

A module of length at least two need not have nonreduced scheme-theoretic support (for example k²). No nonreduced-support conclusion is made. No existence of the listed alternatives is proved; no upper bound on length is asserted. Rank eight remains possible, and its actual defect is unevaluated. This is a local and global equivariant sheaf-theoretic exclusion; it does not select the physical rank or establish a new algebraicity conclusion.
