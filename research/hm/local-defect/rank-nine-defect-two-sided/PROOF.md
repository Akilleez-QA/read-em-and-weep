# Rank nine forces a saturation-defect degree between 22 and 36

## Result

Under the established hypotheses and notation in `../rank-nine-kernel-splitting/PROOF.md` and its `ADDENDUM.md`, generic rank(Q)=9 forces

    22 <= a <= 36,

where a is the degree of the codimension-two cycle of T=F/G. Here G is the actual image of b, F its saturation in J tensor O, and J is the irreducible twenty-dimensional normalizer representation. In particular neither endpoint 21 nor 37 allowed by elementary second-Chern positivity can occur.

The proof is universal sheaf geometry and the already established irreducibility of J. No actual operation, source matrix, product, input, rank instance, jet, or residue is evaluated.

## 1. Positivity with generation only in codimension one

Let M be a torsion-free coherent sheaf of rank s on P^4, generated by a finite-dimensional space of global sections on the complement of a closed set of codimension at least two. Then c2(M) is represented by an effective codimension-two cycle.

For s=1, write M=I_Z tensor det(M); the assertion follows from c2(M)=[Z]_2. For s>1 choose s-1 sufficiently general sections. On the locus where M is locally free and the evaluation map is surjective, the usual incidence dimension estimate for s-by-(s-1) matrices shows that the rank-drop locus has codimension at least two. The excluded locus also has codimension at least two. The resulting map therefore has generic rank s-1 and has no divisorial rank drop. It is a sheaf injection because its source is torsion-free:

    0 -> O^(s-1) -> M -> N -> 0.

The cokernel N has rank one and is locally free at codimension-one points. In fact it is torsion-free everywhere. At any point p of codimension d>=2, the regular local ring has depth d, the source is free, and M_p is torsion-free and hence has depth at least one. The depth lemma gives

    depth(N_p) >= min(depth(M_p), d-1) >= 1.

Thus N has no associated point of positive codimension: codimension-one torsion was excluded by the rank-drop statement, and torsion of codimension at least two would give an associated point contradicting this depth bound. Consequently

    N = I_Z tensor O(c1(M))

for a closed subscheme Z of codimension at least two (with the integer convention for c1 on P^4). Additivity of Chern characters in the displayed exact sequence gives

    c2(M) = [Z]_2.

This proves positivity without local freeness of M in codimension two and without generation at codimension-two points.

## 2. The reflexive zero-c2 splitting lemma

If E is reflexive on P^4, generated by global sections outside codimension two, and c2(E)=0, then

    E = O^(s-1) direct-sum O(c1(E)),

where s=rank(E).

For rank one this is the standard fact that a reflexive rank-one sheaf on smooth factorial P^4 is a line bundle. For s>1 use the sections and rank-one torsion-free cokernel N from Section 1. Since [Z]_2=0 and every nonempty effective surface cycle in P^4 has positive degree, Z has no codimension-two component. N is therefore locally free at all codimension-two points. At a point of codimension d>=3, reflexivity gives depth(E_p)>=2, so the depth lemma now improves to

    depth(N_p) >= min(depth(E_p), d-1) >= 2.

Together with local freeness in codimension at most two, this proves N is S2. Being torsion-free, it is reflexive. As a rank-one reflexive sheaf it equals O(c1(E)). The sequence splits because

    Ext^1(O(c1(E)), O^(s-1)) = H^1(O(-c1(E)))^(s-1) = 0

on P^4. This proves the lemma. It does not assume that E was initially a vector bundle.

## 3. Applying positivity to the saturated image and quotient

The previously proved Chern identities are

    rank(F)=13, c1(F)=-4, c2(F)=37-a,
    rank(R)=7,  c1(R)= 4, c2(R)=a-21,

where 0 -> F -> J tensor O -> R -> 0 and R is globally generated and torsion-free. In particular the positivity lemma gives a>=21.

The kernel F is reflexive: at every point of codimension d>=2, the depth lemma and torsion-freeness of R give depth(F)>=min(d,depth(R)+1)>=2. It is also torsion-free and is locally free in codimension one. Its dual E=F* is reflexive. Dualizing the inclusion F -> J tensor O gives a map

    J* tensor O -> F*.

At codimension-one points R is free over a discrete valuation ring, so the original exact sequence splits locally; thus this dual map is surjective there. It follows that F* is generated by global sections outside codimension two. Reflexive sheaves on a smooth variety are locally free in codimension two, so dualization preserves c2: c2(F*)=c2(F)=37-a. Section 1 gives a<=37.

## 4. Excluding a=37

If a=37, apply Section 2 to E=F*. It yields

    F* = O^12 direct-sum O(4),
    F  = O^12 direct-sum O(-4).

Therefore H^0(F) has dimension twelve. Since F is an equivariant subsheaf of J tensor O, H^0(F) is an N-invariant subspace of J. This contradicts the already established irreducibility of the twenty-dimensional representation J. The splitting need not be chosen equivariantly: the space of global sections and its inclusion are intrinsically equivariant.

Hence a<=36.

## 5. Excluding a=21 without assuming R locally free

Suppose a=21, so c2(R)=0. Put E=R** and D=E/R. The natural injection R -> R** has cokernel supported in codimension at least two. Write [D]_2=delta h^2, with delta>=0. Additivity of Chern characters, and equality of first Chern classes, give

    c2(E)=c2(R)-delta=-delta.

The reflexive sheaf E is generated by the global sections coming from R outside codimension two: the map R -> E is an isomorphism wherever R is locally free. Section 1 therefore gives c2(E)>=0. Thus delta=0 and c2(E)=0. Section 2 now gives

    E = O^6 direct-sum O(4).

The duals R* and (R**)* agree: both are reflexive and agree outside codimension two. Hence

    R* = O^6 direct-sum O(-4),
    dim H^0(R*)=6.

Dualizing the quotient J tensor O -> R gives the equivariant inclusion R* -> J* tensor O. Consequently H^0(R*) is a six-dimensional invariant subspace of the irreducible twenty-dimensional dual representation J*, another contradiction. No assertion R=R** was needed.

Hence a>=22, completing the two-sided bound.

## Scope

This proves necessary conditions for rank nine, not rank nine itself or its impossibility. It neither identifies nor evaluates the saturation-defect surfaces. All work uses existing representation identities, sheaf-theoretic exact sequences, depth, and Chern classes; only this local proof file was written.
