Their rank-one cokernel has no divisorial torsion. At every codimension-d point with d≥2, the depth lemma gives cokernel depth at least min(depth M,d−1)≥1, excluding all remaining torsion associated primes. Thus the cokernel is an ideal sheaf tensored with the determinant, and c2(M) is its effective codimension-two cycle.

This proves the zero-c2 splitting lemma without presupposing local freeness of E.
