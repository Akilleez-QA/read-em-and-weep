# Combined lower bound 300 and support below 375

In the established conditional actual-rank-eight branch, B is a stable rank-two reflexive sheaf on P4 with equivariantly trivial determinant and isolated singularities. Its nonzero finite defect T=Ext²(B,O) and saturation defect D=F/G have identical support and local lengths.

The local exclusions through length nine force every supported canonical-orbit point to have local length at least ten. The unique 250-point orbit has local length at least two. Both 300-point orbits also have local length at least two. All other point orbits have at least 375 points.

Consequently a support orbit contributes respectively at least 300, 500, 600, or 375 to the total. Nonzero support therefore gives

    length(T)=length(D) >= 300.

If the total is strictly below 375, support must be exactly the canonical thirty-point orbit, with constant local length m. The inequalities 10<=m<12.5 force m∈{10,11,12}. Thus the only necessary totals below 375 are

    300, 330, 360.

In notation length(T)=5q, q>=60, and 0<q<75 implies q∈{60,66,72}.

In particular, total length300 cannot arise from a simple defect at a 300-point orbit: both such orbits are excluded locally. If total300 occurs, it has thirty support points, each of local length ten.

No surviving value is realized by this argument. The actual rank remains eight or ten; no upper length bound, operation-specific evaluation, or new algebraicity assertion is claimed.
