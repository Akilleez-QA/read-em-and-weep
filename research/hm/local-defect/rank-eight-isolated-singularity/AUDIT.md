# Independent audit: isolated-singularity counterexamples

Status: PASS. Reviewed all sections, including the local duality indices, universal module, and global stable construction. No outstanding corrections.

The four short exact sequences in Section 1 give Ext1(M*)=Ext4(Ext2(M)) and Ext2(M*)=Ext4(Ext1(M)) exactly as claimed. Finite-length Ext vanishes below degree four over the regular four-dimensional local ring. Self-duality therefore makes the two defects finite-length duals; nonfreeness forces both to be nonzero, giving projective dimension and depth two. This argument is correctly restricted to genuinely isolated singular points.

For the local module, the alternating identity puts the column inside the kernel of the row. Off the maximal ideal both maps split as required, so the quotient is locally free. The depth calculations give depth at least two at the closed point and exclude finite-length torsion; hence the module is reflexive. Dualizing identifies the evaluation image with the maximal ideal, giving Ext1=Ext2=k. The determinant pairing extends to self-duality. These are universal local algebra computations unrelated to a selected operation value.

Independently checked Arrondo's Theorem 1 at https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0610015 . Its local-complete-intersection existence statement has no connectedness requirement. Six disjoint lines have the stated canonical and normal determinant data, so the nowhere-zero generating section and H2(O(-2))=0 give the required bundle. After twisting, c1=0,c2=5 and section vanishing proves stability.

The full graded section module is finite and reflexive: it is locally free on the punctured affine cone and has vanishing zeroth and first local cohomology at the vertex. Flat polynomial extension preserves the bidual isomorphism. Its projective sheaf is locally free away from the new vertex and restricts to E on any hyperplane avoiding that vertex. A free vertex stalk would force the minimal graded resolution to have no positive terms and split E, contradicting stability. Hyperplane restriction therefore gives c1=0,c2=5,c3=0 with exactly one singular point. Saturated rank-one subsheaves are line bundles; restriction to a general hyperplane proves stability on P4.

The singleton singular locus prevents equivariance under the original projective Heisenberg action. Thus this example establishes only that rank, stability, reflexivity, self-duality and the low Chern data do not force a singular curve. It neither realizes the original kernel nor excludes additional equivariant obstructions. No actual operation or HM instance was evaluated.
