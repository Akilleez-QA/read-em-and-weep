# No semistable reflexive replacement of the boundary

This continuation of [PR228](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/pull/228), exact head06c7e2f13e902d819bb6945c033fd455a5f28eac, proves that its depth-one boundary is unavoidable among slope-semistable torsion-free flat fillings of the same generic sheaf on the fixed P4. The statement survives finite ramified DVR base change.

A primitive integral Hom specializes nontrivially. Stability of the known source and semistability of the competing target force full rank; equal Hilbert polynomials eliminate the cokernel. The full families are isomorphic after scalar rescaling of the generic marking. Thus the reflexive hull and its length-one quotient cannot be removed by a different semistable filling.

This is not a claim about arbitrary unstable extensions, changed generic fibers or ambient transformations, a proper moduli compactification, or an actual HM/source deformation. Full proof and independent exact-proof audit are included. No formal checker or selected-source evaluation was used. The proof's final review request is fulfilled by INDEPENDENT-AUDIT.md.
