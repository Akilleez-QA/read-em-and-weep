The plane linkage formula is valid for arbitrary finite plane schemes inside a complete intersection: finite schemes are locally Cohen--Macaulay, the residual ideal quotient has complementary length, and the residual canonical-module sequence together with duality identifies the dimension difference with the stated H1. In the uses here the residual lengths are at most two or three, and the twists are at least length minus one, so their H1 vanishes by the explicitly proved Hilbert-function bound. No reducedness assumption enters.

The bounds for p4 and p5 are correct. In the gcd-one quintic case linkage in (5,5) gives precisely two quintics. For common-factor degrees one or three, the length bound must be equality at length twenty-two, forcing the residual to equal the corresponding complete intersection; its relevant equation space has dimension two. For degree four the residual cannot be empty without producing a quartic equation, and its linear ideal has dimension at most two. These cover all possible gcd degrees.
