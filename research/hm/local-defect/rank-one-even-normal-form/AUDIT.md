The formal implicit solution exists uniquely because its v derivative is 2I. Uniqueness makes it even in p. The quadratic and quartic terms, including their factors 1/2, −1/8 and 1/4, are correct.

At the generic point the local ring is Artinian and equals its completion. The chosen base coordinates give its coefficient field K, and the previously proved m^5=0 justifies the exact truncated presentation. The substitution map rho:W→S4 is genuinely linear on the space W of quadratic equations, so every constant relation among the quadrics also holds among the quartic corrections.

In S/n^5 an ideal element is a constant graph term q−rho(q), a cubic term from S1W, and a quartic term from S2W. Cancellation of the quadratic graph term forces q=0 and therefore rho(q)=0. There are no additional initial quartics. Thus gr A=S/(W,n^5). Four regular-sequence quadrics in W have a complete intersection quotient with Hilbert series (1+z)^4, which makes n^5 redundant. The exact equality gr A=S/(W) follows.

The stronger noncanonical algebra isomorphism is also valid. Then B4=0 and m^4=0, so the quartic corrections vanish in A; the equal-length surjection B→A is an isomorphism.
