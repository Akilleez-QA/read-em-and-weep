# Boundary duality

The total centered sheaf is reflexive and alternating self-dual, while its forced flat boundary has a unique nonzero global pairing up to scalar, with a length-one cokernel.

See PROOF.md for the total-space depth argument, ordinary-dual base change, complete Ext restriction table, classification of pairings, and the derived-dual defect. INDEPENDENT-AUDIT.md records the mathematical review. These claims are not machine-formal verification.

Dependencies: [PR228](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/pull/228) at `06c7e2f13e902d819bb6945c033fd455a5f28eac`; [PR248](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/pull/248) at `905ad4ea6000cbd7c26d01261a17ba4574e6238f`.

The centered convention is A=S(1)^4, E=S^10, A*=S(-1)^4. Uncentered and equivariant versions must retain their grading and orientation factors.
