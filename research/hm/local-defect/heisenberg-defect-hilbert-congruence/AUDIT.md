# Independent audit

Status: PASS, Sections 1–4. No corrections remain; this audit is frozen.

For nontrivial central weight, the Heisenberg commutator relation cycles eigenspaces of a finite-order generator in orbits of length five with equal dimensions. Thus every cohomology group at such a weight has dimension divisible by five. Twisting shifts the central weight by minus the twist, giving four congruence classes on which the Euler characteristic is divisible by five.

For support dimension at most three, the Euler polynomial is integer-valued of degree at most three. Its binomial expansion has integral coefficients, and multiplication by six clears all denominators. Modulo five this polynomial has four distinct roots and degree at most three, so it vanishes identically. Since six is invertible modulo five, every integer Euler characteristic is divisible by five. Finite differences then show divisibility of every binomial coefficient, including the top-dimensional cycle degree.

Central idempotents split any equivariant coherent sheaf into coherent equivariant eigensheaves because the center acts trivially on the base. Applying the argument to these pieces proves the general statement without a uniform-character or purity assumption. The exclusion of support dimension four is essential and is correctly emphasized.

The rank-nine saturation defect is equivariant and has support dimension two with positive leading degree a. Combining five-divisibility with the independently audited interval 22≤a≤36 leaves exactly {25,30,35}. The resulting Chern-number pairs (12,4),(7,9),(2,14) are correct. No actual defect surface, generic rank, or operation instance is computed.
