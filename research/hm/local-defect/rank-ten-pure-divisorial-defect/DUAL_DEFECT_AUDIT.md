# Independent audit of the dual-defect bound

Status: PASS, Sections 1–3. No corrections were recorded in this historical audit.

The fourteen displayed universal linear square syzygies give a basis for the kernel fiber on each nonzero-coordinate chart. Hence G* is globally generated. A general projective plane avoids the at-most-one-dimensional singular locus of reflexive F and is not contained in its determinant divisor. The restricted equal-rank bundle map has nonzero determinant, so it remains injective; this also verifies the needed Tor vanishing. Its dual cokernel Ext1(T_L,O_L) is a globally generated quotient of G*_L. The virtual dual identity gives degree five and second Chern-character coefficient −t, with the sign correct.

For a globally generated sheaf supported in dimension at most one on P2, removal of maximal zero-dimensional torsion preserves generation and only increases ch2 by a nonnegative length. In the pure quotient, a nonzero section has pure cyclic image O_D. On the regular surface, its unmixed height-one ideal is locally principal, so D is an effective Cartier divisor. Its degree m is positive and its ch2 is −m²/2. The generated quotient has smaller degree, allowing induction. The inequality −m²/2−(d−m)²/2≥−d²/2 proves the stated general bound, including any zero-dimensional quotients.

Application with d=5 yields t≤25/2. Combined with the previous lower bound and integrality, this gives −53/2≤t≤25/2, or 9≤c2(F)≤48 and 1≤c2(R)≤40, summing to49. These are necessary conditions for the surviving degree-five branch, not an exclusion or a computation of its defect.
