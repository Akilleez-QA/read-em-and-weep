# Independent audit

Historical mathematical audit status: PASS for the main proof, Sections 1–4. No corrections were recorded in this historical audit.

At generic quadratic rank ten, the canonical square relation is a subbundle O(−3)→O(−1)^15. Its quotient has rank fourteen, and its induced map to J is generically injective, hence sheaf-injective. This proves that the actual image G is locally free and gives c1(G)=−12h,c2(G)=69h².

The saturation F is reflexive. In 0→G→F→T→0, the depth lemma gives depth T_p≥min(c−1,2) at ambient codimension c≥2. Thus every associated point of nonzero T has codimension one; its support is pure of dimension three. Its local dimension is c−1, so the same bound proves S2. If the divisorial degree is zero, T must vanish. This rules out all hidden higher-codimension defects in the k=12 branch.

For k=2, irreducibility injects twenty constant sections into the rank-six quotient R. Five general sections and the torsion-free cokernel lemma bound h0(R) by twenty, forcing all quadrics to lie in I_Z(2). Global generation of O(2) makes Z empty; splitting then gives a forbidden five-dimensional invariant subspace of J*. Hence that branch is excluded.

For the surviving divisorial branch, Chern-character additivity gives c2(F)=k²/2−3−t and c2(R)=k²/2+3+t. The previously audited positivity and zero-c2 splitting arguments make both strictly positive. At k=7, their integrality gives exactly t=−53/2,−51/2,...,41/2 and positive second Chern numbers summing to49. At k=12, T=0 gives69 and75. The proof correctly distinguishes the divisorial ch2 coefficient from an effective codimension-two defect degree.
