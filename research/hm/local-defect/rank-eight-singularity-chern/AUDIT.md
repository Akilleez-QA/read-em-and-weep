# Independent audit: singularity sheaves and higher Chern constraints

Status: PASS. Reviewed the full frozen PROOF.md. No outstanding corrections.

Reflexive alternating duality gives B=B* as ordinary sheaves; it does not identify the derived dual with B. Auslander--Buchsbaum gives the stated projective-dimension and support bounds. Nonvanishing of the top Ext of a minimal local resolution detects the nonfree locus.

The biduality spectral-sequence indexing is correct. The only differential involving (2,0) is d2 to (4,-1), and convergence forces it to be an isomorphism. The d2 from (1,0) to (3,-1) is surjective; its kernel is carried isomorphically by d3 to (4,-2). There are no omitted possible nonzero terms or later differentials. These give the asserted Ext identities and the inclusion of T's support in S's support. Removing the maximal finite subsheaf of S leaves a pure one-dimensional Cohen--Macaulay sheaf. Local duality identifies the finite kernel with S0 and yields the Ext^3 self-duality of the pure quotient, with the ambient dualizing twist handled by the use of Ext into O.

The K-theory identity for the derived dual gives ch3(S)=c3(B) and ch4(S)=length(T), while c4(B) cancels in that comparison. The pure quotient consequently has ch4=0. RR then gives the displayed linear Hilbert polynomials and evenness of c3. Proper-support Heisenberg divisibility gives c3=10n and length(T)=5q. The rank-subtracted Euler polynomial has degree at most two, so the four nonzero residue classes force its divisibility by five in every twist. Combining chi(B)=2 mod 5 with integrality yields exactly c4=15(n-1) mod 30.

The nonemptiness consequences and projective-dimension-one case follow as stated. The finite-point orbit argument is valid: Hbar has no fixed projective point; if every orbit has size five, normalizer invariance forces all six stabilizer lines and hence at least thirty points; an orbit of size twenty-five already gives the required bound. Thus nonzero T has length at least twenty-five. No assertion of existence of a twenty-five-point orbit is needed.

These are necessary conditional constraints, not an exclusion of isolated singularities or rank eight. No actual operation or HM instance was evaluated.
