## 1. The two reduced ramification orbits

Riemann--Hurwitz gives deg R_i=2*4-2+2*3=12. Each R_i is invariant. A stabilizer on either projective line is cyclic of order at most five, so every orbit on D has at least twelve points. Therefore each effective R_i is a single reduced orbit of twelve points. Every ramification index is two.

The two ramification divisors are disjoint. Indeed D embeds smoothly into the product, and a nonzero tangent vector cannot have zero image under both projections. Equivalently in any product chart, simultaneous vanishing of both partial derivatives of a local equation would make D singular. Thus R_1 and R_2 are distinct orbits, and their point stabilizers have order five.

## 2. Quotient signature and the remaining stabilizers

The A5 action on D is faithful, since p1 is surjective and equivariant for a faithful action. A finite stabilizer on a smooth complex curve embeds faithfully into the one-dimensional tangent representation and is cyclic. Thus its possible nontrivial orders in A5 are 2,3,5.

Let Q=D/A5 and q its genus. Riemann--Hurwitz for D->Q gives

    6 = 60(2q-2) + sum_P (|Stab(P)|-1).

The two known twelve-point orbits contribute 2*12*4=96. Hence q=0, and the remaining contribution is thirty. An orbit with stabilizer 2,3,5 contributes respectively 30,40,48. Therefore there is exactly one further nonfree orbit, of size thirty and stabilizer two; no point has stabilizer three. The quotient signature is (2,5,5).

This signature is not asserted as a new classification of a familiar genus-four curve. Its role here is to identify the exact sheets supporting the actual connection defect.

## 3. Pullbacks of the exceptional orbits

Every point in R_1 or R_2 maps under either projection to a point with order-five stabilizer. Its image is therefore Omega_i. The map from either size-twelve orbit to Omega_i is bijective, by transitivity and equal cardinalities. There are no other points above Omega_i: any point above it has an orbit of size at least twelve, and the already known weighted divisors below exhaust degree36.

The ramification index of p_i on R_i is two and on the other orbit is one. Consequently, as effective Cartier divisors,

    p1^*Omega1 = 2R_1 + R_2,
    p2^*Omega2 = R_1 + 2R_2.                        (1)

In particular the two pullbacks have common zero divisor R_1+R_2, NOT disjoint support. On the smooth curve, the ideal generated by their two local equations is O_D(-R_1-R_2), so the common-zero subscheme is reduced of length24. Their multiplicity profiles, rather than their supports, distinguish the two ramification orbits.

For the actual quartic determinant det M_kappa on L, (1) gives

    div(p2^*det M_kappa)=R_1+2R_2,
    div(kappa)=R_1.                                (2)

Thus the pulled-back determinant vanishes doubly on the ramified sheet where kappa itself is a unit. Confusing determinant vanishing with pointwise vanishing of kappa loses precisely this distinction. If a reversed quartic construction is used, its determinant must be justified separately; (1) makes no assertion that such an actual reversed connection exists.

## 4. Exact exceptional-fiber algebra and kernel of h -> n_h

Fix an arbitrary ell in Omega2. Formula (1) gives

    p2^(-1)(ell)=2r+s,
    r in R_2, s in R_1.

The points r and s are distinct. At s, p2 is unramified and kappa has a simple zero. At r, p2 has simple ramification and kappa is a unit. Thus on the length-three fiber, after local line trivializations,

    O_(2r+s) = C[epsilon]/(epsilon^2) x C,
    multiplication by kappa = (multiplication by a unit, zero).

Its kernel and cokernel are the simple s summand; neither is the nilpotent direction of the double point 2r. Over the completed base DVR C[[t]], the finite algebra is C[[u]] x C[[t]], with t=u^2 on the first factor. Indeed simple ramification initially gives t=u^2 times a unit, and over C the unit has a formal square root that can be absorbed into u; no analytic retraction is assumed. Multiplication by kappa is invertible on the rank-two first factor, and is t times a unit on the second factor. Its Smith elementary divisors are therefore (1,1,t). This is an intrinsic identification of the lost sheet, not an evaluated actual matrix.

Since p1 is unramified at r and D is embedded in the product, the length-two subscheme 2r maps isomorphically onto the first infinitesimal neighborhood of the positive point x_r=p1(r). Equivalently, on the p2 fiber the positive coordinate is a parameter at r. Hence for a positive binary quartic h,

    n_h(ell)=0  iff  h vanishes on 2r
                  iff ord_(x_r)(h)>=2.             (3)

Here n_h(ell) is the entire vector in V O(6)|ell, not its value at only one point of D. The quadratic restriction isomorphism p2_*O_D(2,6)=V O(6) identifies this vector with h*kappa on the whole fiber. This is distinct from the quartic restriction surjection D5 O_L -> p2_*O_D(4,0)=V O(2), whose kernel is Wprime O(-3). Finite pushforward identifies these vector-bundle fibers with sections on the scheme-theoretic finite fiber, and tensoring the latter exact sequence with the residue field remains exact since its quotient is locally free. Thus the identification and the quartic restriction surjectivity also hold at the double-point fibers. At the simple point s no condition on h remains, because kappa vanishes there. Therefore the exceptional kernel of the five-to-three map h->n_h(ell) is the three-dimensional space

    H0(P(Wprime), O(4)(-2x_r)).                     (4)

Off Omega2, neither R_1 nor R_2 meets the fiber: it consists of three reduced points and kappa is invertible. Thus n_h(ell)=0 precisely when h is divisible by the actual fiber cubic f_ell, giving the two-dimensional space f_ell*H0(O(1)). This is polynomial divisibility as an intrinsic subspace; no coefficients of f_ell have been selected or evaluated.

For nonzero k, the actual upper connection vector A12(ell) vanishes iff a(ell)=0 or n_h(ell)=0. In particular, away from the symbol zero divisor, its exceptional-fiber zero criterion is exactly the double-root condition (3). This does not characterize vanishing of the full corrected operator: the diagonal connection, scalar correction and their cancellation in the normal cochain remain separate dependencies.

## 5. Quotient projection maps, as a consistency corollary

Each p_i descends to a degree-three map Q=P1 -> P(W_i)/A5=P1. The target quotient has stabilizer orders (2,3,5). The local ramification index of the descended map at the orbit of P is

    e_(p_i)(P) * |Stab(p_i(P))| / |Stab(P)|.

Over the target order-five branch value, (1) gives profile (2,1). Over the order-three value there are no stabilizer-three source points, so the profile is (3). Over the order-two value, the unique size-thirty source orbit contributes an unramified sheet and the remaining free orbit contributes a sheet of index two, giving profile (2,1). These account for total ramification 1+2+1=4 of a rational cubic map, so no further branch values occur. This does not make either original trigonal projection a cyclic cover.

## 6. Uniform exclusion of orbit-wide upper-entry cancellation

The map ell -> r -> x_r from Omega2 to Omega1 is a bijection: both restrictions of the projections to R_2 are bijections by Section 3. For any nonzero quartic h, criterion (3) can therefore hold at at most two exceptional base points, since each distinct x_r consumes at least two of its four zeros, counted with multiplicity. Hence n_h(ell) is nonzero at at least ten of the twelve exceptional base points.

For nonzero quadratic a, its zero divisor on L has degree two, so at most two further exceptional points can be killed by a(ell)^2. For k!=0, the actual upper entry A12 is consequently nonzero at at least eight of the twelve exceptional base points. This is uniform in the nonzero symbol and quartic, and excludes an orbit-wide disappearance of this actual connection entry without any selected evaluation. It does not exclude cancellation with other entries after passing to the normal class.

## Contribution and boundary

The existing determinant rank result says where the upper quartic map drops rank. The new statement identifies which geometric sheet is lost, proves it is disjoint from the colliding double sheet, and translates the rank-two exceptional specialization into the exact double-root kernel (3)-(4). It also corrects a possible but false inference that the two determinant pullbacks have disjoint support. No global operator nonvanishing, positive obstruction, third Bockstein value, or higher lifting result is claimed.
