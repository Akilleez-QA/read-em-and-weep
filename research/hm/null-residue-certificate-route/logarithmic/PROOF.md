# Logarithmic-vector-field extension of the audited residue route

## Scope and result

This is a symbolic extension of the public rational-divergence packet and its independent audit. Only invented symbolic examples are evaluated; no production source is executed. All geometric identification, same-source extraction, finite Laurentness, characteristic-zero, and fixed trace-orientation hypotheses remain unchanged. Ordinary coordinate derivatives only. The annihilation of divergence is by the packet's formal iterated residue, never boundary decay or dimensional regularization.

The useful cross-domain transfer is the denominator-preserving syzygy parametrization used in Feynman-integral IBP. A full logarithmic module is an exact reformulation of the complete denominator-one certificate, not a restriction. Truncating that module can lose completeness.

## Exact sparse operator

Let R=k[x1,...,xn] in characteristic zero, D nonzero, and define

    M_D = {A in R^n : A(D)=b D for some b in R},
    L_D(A) = div A - b.

Here A(D)=sum_i A_i partial_i D. Then

    div(A/D)=L_D(A)/D.

Consequently pN=L_D(A) is precisely the audited identity

    pND = D div A - A(D).

Necessity of logarithmic tangentness follows from this identity itself: A(D)=D(div A-pN). Thus full M_D parametrization loses no denominator-one certificates.

For a factorization D=c product_a q_a^m_a with distinct irreducible polynomials q_a and positive m_a, M_D is the intersection of the modules A(q_a) in (q_a). Indeed, divide the equation A(D)/D by the factors formally and localize at each prime (q_a). All terms but m_a A(q_a)/q_a are regular there; since m_a is invertible in characteristic zero, q_a divides A(q_a). The converse is the product rule. No smoothness or normal-crossing condition is needed.

If v_r(q_a)=b_ra q_a, put

    c_r=div v_r-sum_a m_a b_ra,
    A=sum_r f_r v_r.

The certificate equation is

    pN = sum_r [v_r(f_r)+c_r f_r].

Each bracket is polynomial; no squared denominators are introduced. For fixed v_r and bounded multiplier supports this is linear over k in the coefficients of f_r. The image here is a k-vector-space image of differential operators, NOT the polynomial ideal generated by the c_r. Arbitrary polynomial multiples of an already certified numerator need not remain certified without the v_r(f_r) term.

For arbitrary supplied factors, verified per-factor tangentness still suffices. Necessity of the per-factor parametrization requires the distinct-irreducible factor statement or another proved equivalent description. The unfactored syzygy A(D)-bD=0 is always exact.

## Explicit complete submodule under Laurentness

A generic syzygy computation is unnecessary for a universal baseline. Write D=x^d C, where no coordinate variable divides C. The audited Laurent hypothesis implies N=CQ with Q polynomial. Define n fields

    v_i=C x_i e_i.

They are tangent to D, and direct differentiation gives

    L_D(h_i v_i)=C [x_i partial_i h_i+(1-d_i)h_i].

Thus seek the exact polynomial identity

    pN = C sum_i [x_i partial_i h_i+(1-d_i)h_i].             (*)

This explicit submodule is complete for zero-residue Laurent inputs. To prove this, expand pQ=sum_b t_b x^b. A monomial contributes to the residue of pF=x^-d pQ only if b=d-1. If the residue vanishes, for each nonzero coefficient choose an i with b_i+1-d_i nonzero and assign

    t_b x^b/(b_i+1-d_i)

to h_i. Equation (*) follows. Set A_i=C x_i h_i to recover the audited certificate.

If pN is nonzero, the construction has deg h_i <= deg(pN)-deg C, and coordinate bounds deg_j h_i <= deg_j(pN)-deg_j C. Hence deg A_i <= deg(pN)+1 and the audited multidegree bounds hold. Zero targets use zero fields.

The proof uses Q only existentially. Verification can check (*) and A_i=C x_i h_i without constructing the full Laurent quotient. Finding h_i may nevertheless be as expensive as recovering the quotient; no sparse-size or runtime guarantee follows. General tangent generators may give smaller representations than this baseline, but that is an opportunity requiring evidence, not a theorem.

## Invented controls

Take coordinates x,y,z,w, C=1+x+yz, D=x^2 yzw C, p=1, and N=C(1+x^2). Then

    F=(x^-2+1)/(yzw), h_1=x^2-1, h_2=h_3=h_4=0,
    A_1=C x(x^2-1), G_1=(x^2-1)/(xyzw).

The weighted-Euler operator is x partial_x -1, so its value on h_1 is x^2+1. The Laurent residue of F is zero. Replacing N by C(1+x+x^2) adds 1/(xyzw), whose residue is 1; no rational divergence certificate can exist, and the displayed identity fails.

A genuinely incomplete tangent search: take D=xyzw C, N=Cy, p=1, but allow only v_1=Cx e_1. Its operator is C x partial_x, whose image has every monomial divisible by x after removing C, so it cannot produce Cy at any polynomial degree. Yet v_2=Cy e_2 with h_2=y succeeds. Failure in a selected/truncated tangent family is therefore not evidence of nonvanishing.

## Primary sources and exact transfer boundary

Verified public primary papers on 2026-10-18:

- Larsen and Zhang, Integration-by-parts reductions from unitarity cuts and algebraic geometry, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1511.01071, equations (12)-(14): polynomial syzygies cancel the terms causing dimension shifts and doubled propagators; polynomial multiples supply further relations.
- Boehm, Georgoudis, Larsen, Schulze, and Zhang, Complete sets of logarithmic vector fields for integration-by-parts identities of Feynman integrals, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1712.09737, equations (3.6), (4.8)-(4.10): logarithmic syzygies and module intersection impose compatible denominator constraints.

Only this algebraic construction is transferred. Their Gram-determinant-specific complete generators, physical integration arguments, cuts, kinematic assumptions, and performance results do not automatically apply to this denominator. No cut/sampling equality replaces the exact polynomial identities above.

The existing 30 pairings remain 30 unknown targets. Reuse of denominator operators supplies infrastructure, not vanishing values or a collapse to one scalar. This extension neither activates nor changes the guarded production implementation.
