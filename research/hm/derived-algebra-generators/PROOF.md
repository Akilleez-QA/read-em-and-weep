# Large indecomposable generators in the derived intersection algebra

Work over C. Retain P, A and B from PR540, and write T_i=Tor_i^P(A,B), with internal grading equal to P degree. Let T=direct_sum_i T_i. The product on Tor for commutative P-algebras makes T a bigraded algebra with

T_(i,d) T_(j,e) subset T_(i+j,d+e),
uv=(-1)^(ij)vu.

The sign uses homological degree, not internal degree. In characteristic zero every homologically odd element has square zero. The construction and signs are the standard product on the derived tensor of commutative algebras; see Stacks Project, Products and Tor, tag 068G. No product on an arbitrarily chosen free complex is being presumed.

Put T_0=A tensor_P B=Q^(2), let m_0=(T_0)_(>0), and let J=direct_sum_(i>0) T_i. Define relative algebra indecomposables

V=J/(m_0 J+J^2).

Thus V counts minimal homogeneous algebra generators of positive homological degree over the degree-zero algebra T_0. More precisely, every homogeneous set of T_0-algebra generators for T must span V over C. Conversely, homogeneous lifts of a basis of V generate: induction on positive internal degree removes m_0 coefficients and products. Only the necessary lower bounds will be used below.

## 1. Pinned input

PR544 proves that for two nonnegative integers x and y the complete intermediate Hilbert functions are

T_1: (10,225,x), in internal degrees (2,3,4);
T_2: (315+x,y), in internal degrees (4,5);
T_3: (91+y,85), in internal degrees (5,6).

All unlisted components vanish. PR542 identifies T_4=C(-7)^45, and T_i=0 for i>4. Its positive internal action is zero. These inputs concern modules; the deductions below concern multiplication and algebra generators, not another computation of those Hilbert functions. Neither x nor y is determined here.

## 2. At least 270+x new generators in homological degree two

Because T_2 has no component below internal degree four,

(m_0 J)_(2,4)=0.

A product of two positive-homological-degree elements of total bidegree (2,4) must have factors of bidegree (1,2) and (1,2). Every longer product has homological degree at least three. Graded commutativity therefore factors the product through

Lambda^2(T_(1,2)) -> T_(2,4).

Its source has dimension binom(10,2)=45. Consequently

dim V_(2,4) >= (315+x)-45 = 270+x.

The inequality allows all 45 possible products to be independent; no actual multiplication rank is asserted. In particular the algebra cannot be generated over T_0 by its homological degree-one part.

## 3. Exactly 91+y indecomposables in bidegree (3,5)

Again (m_0 J)_(3,5)=0 because T_3 starts in internal degree five. A product contributing to homological degree three would have degrees one and two; its internal degree is at least 2+4=6. Three factors of homological degree one likewise have internal degree at least six. Hence

(J^2)_(3,5)=0,
V_(3,5)=T_(3,5),
dim V_(3,5)=91+y.

These are indispensable generators even if every element of T_1 and T_2 is admitted as a generator. Thus T cannot be generated in homological degrees at most two. Also V_(1,2)=T_(1,2) has dimension ten. Every homogeneous algebra generating set over T_0 therefore has at least

10+(270+x)+(91+y)=371+x+y

positive-homological-degree generators. Additional bidegrees can require further generators; equality of this total lower bound is not claimed.

## 4. Product exclusions and augmentation nilpotence

The internal support imposes further structural restrictions:

- T_2 T_2=0: its internal degree is at least eight, whereas T_4 exists only in degree seven.
- J^4=0: any product of four positive-homological-degree factors either has homological degree greater than four, or consists of four T_1 factors and has internal degree at least eight, again beyond T_4.
- J^3 is supported only in bidegree (3,6). Three T_1 factors have internal degree at least six, and T_3 has no degree above six. The other possible homological degree-four triple has degrees (1,1,2), whose internal degree is at least eight and hence gives zero. Thus J^3 is the image of Lambda^3(T_(1,2)) in T_(3,6), and its dimension is at most min(binom(10,3),85)=85.

No assertion that J^3 is nonzero is made. The nonzero T_4 cannot be obtained from four T_1 factors. It might involve products of T_1 and T_3, or additional indecomposables; this argument does not decide its product presentation.

## 5. Contribution and limits

The new result is a lower bound on actual algebra indecomposables and a structural exclusion of degree-one or degree-at-most-two algebra generation. It rules out modeling the derived intersection algebra merely as an exterior algebra generated by first Tor. It does not identify a complete-intersection class, determine x or y, prove formality, or compute any product rank. Nonvanishing in intermediate homological degrees alone would not give these generator counts; the product argument uses their internal degrees and the homological sign.

This is an authored mathematical argument conditional on the pinned module inputs, awaiting independent review and not formally checked. No selected actual HM/source-instance evaluation, multiplication matrix, numerical rank experiment, coefficient search or large computation was used. Original fixed-pair vanishing, higher-order lifting and algebraicity remain separate.
