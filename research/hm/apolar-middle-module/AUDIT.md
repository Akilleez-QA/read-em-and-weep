# Independent audit: apolar reconstruction and rigidity of the middle module

## Verdict

**Pass, subject to the explicitly imported geometric and finite-normalizer inputs. No mathematical correction is required.** The proposed reconstruction recovers the entire graded module, its inverse-system model has the stated reversed grading, the scalar annihilator is exactly the apolar ideal of the three-dimensional quintic net, and the quartic-kernel duality is correct. The added assertions I_2=0 and dim(S/I)_4=15 are also valid, giving the stated partial Hilbert function (1,5,15,?,15,3) and excluding the rank-two quadratic-complete-intersection model. In particular, both stronger assertions are proved:

    End_S^gr(Q) = C,
    Q is indecomposable as an ungraded S-module.

Here End_S^gr means degree-zero endomorphisms. The ungraded endomorphism algebra can contain nonzero positive-degree maps; the proof correctly identifies it as a local algebra, rather than claiming that it too equals C.

## 2. Relation reconstruction, pairing, and numerical dimensions

The maps C_k are bilinear-pairing currying maps on S_k tensor U and S_(5-k) tensor U. For any linear form l and r in ker C_k,

    C_(k+1)(l r)(s) = C_k(r)(l s) = 0.

This proves closure under linear forms for k<5. For k=5 the product lands in degree 6, which is declared entirely relational. Since S is generated by S_1, these checks prove that R is an S-submodule in every degree.

For the actual multiplication maps pi_k, self-adjointness and associativity give exactly

    <pi_k(r), pi_(5-k)(s)> = c C_k(r)(s).

The inclusion ker pi_k into ker C_k follows immediately. Conversely, if C_k(r)=0, surjectivity of pi_(5-k) makes pi_k(r) orthogonal to all Q_(5-k). Perfectness makes pi_k(r)=0. The nonzero scalar is essential here. Consequently ker pi_k=R_k, and generation identifies the entire module with (S tensor U)/R, including its multiplication maps. This is an equality of relation spaces, not merely an equality of dimensions.

The N-equivariance of Psi makes R N-stable, and the multiplication presentation is N-equivariant, so the induced isomorphism is N-equivariant. Its restriction to U is the identity. Any S-linear map from a module generated by U is determined by its values on U, so the uniqueness statement in the specified self-dual, generated class is valid.

The source dimensions for k=0,...,5 are

    (2,10,30,70,140,252).

Subtracting the imported quotient dimensions gives precisely (0,0,10,50,130,250). These are dimensions of total homogeneous relation spaces, not minimal Betti numbers. The ranks are consequences of the presentation and imported geometry, with no evaluation of a selected catalecticant.

Symmetry gives C_(5-k)=C_k transpose. Therefore R_k and R_(5-k) are the respective left and right radicals of the same pairing, and the induced complementary-degree pairing on the quotient is perfect. Under multiplication it equals c inverse times the actual trace/Serre pairing. Scaling Psi changes this pairing's normalization but leaves R and the underlying reconstructed module unchanged.

## 3. Inverse-system realization and grading

With D_d=S_d*, the stated contraction action is intrinsically defined by

    (a contraction f)(b) = f(ab).

It satisfies a contraction (b contraction f)=(ab) contraction f. It lowers the ordinary D-degree by deg a. Thus, after setting the degree of a component in D_(5-k) tensor U* equal to k, multiplication by a homogeneous polynomial of degree e raises the reindexed degree by e, exactly as required for a graded S-module.

More explicitly, the map

    S_k tensor U -> D_(5-k) tensor U*,
    p tensor u -> p contraction F_u

sends q tensor v to Psi(pq)(u,v). It is therefore exactly C_k under the natural tensor identification, not a different catalecticant with a missing transpose or factorial. Its image is the degree-k part of the S-submodule generated by the F_u, and its kernel is R_k. The maps intertwine multiplication in every degree, including the zero action after reindexed degree 5. They establish an S-linear graded realization of Q itself.

The two vector-valued generators are independent because R_0=0. In a basis of U their entries form the asserted symmetric 2-by-2 matrix. Treating those entries as ordinary dual-coordinate polynomials requires the appropriate divided-power normalization, as the proof expressly notes. Treating the scalar apolar algebra of their span as Q would be incorrect; the proof avoids that identification.

## 4. Scalar annihilator and faithful acting algebra

Because Q is generated by U and S is commutative, a polynomial a annihilates Q if and only if it annihilates every element of U. In homogeneous degree k<=5, this gives

    a in I_k
    iff a tensor U is contained in R_k
    iff Psi(ab)(u,v)=0 for every b,u,v
    iff Psi(ab)=0 for every b in S_(5-k).

There is no need to test a separately on higher-degree generators: all module elements are S-linear combinations of U. This proves formula (5).

For t=Psi*(w), with w in Sym^2 U, contraction gives

    (a contraction t)(b) = <Psi(ab),w>.

Thus a annihilates every element of T precisely when the condition above holds. All polynomials of degree at least 6 annihilate T and Q. Since T consists of homogeneous quintics, components of different polynomial degrees contract into different dual degrees, so the equality also holds for arbitrary nonhomogeneous polynomials. Therefore I=Ann_S(T) as an ideal, not just degree by degree in the displayed range.

Surjectivity of Psi makes Psi* injective, so dim T=3. The arithmetic dim I_5=126-3=123 is correct. R_0=R_1=0 implies I_0=I_1=0. The ideal contains S_>=6 and is proper, so its radical is S_+. Finally, generation gives S_+^5 Q=Q_5 nonzero, while S_+^6 Q=0, proving Loewy length exactly six. None of these facts determines all other dimensions of I or its minimal generators, and the proof makes no such claim.

The quotient S/I acts faithfully on Q by the definition of I. It is a different object from Q: their degree-zero dimensions are 1 and 2, respectively.

### Added finite-normalizer consequences

The annihilator I is N-stable: if a kills Q, then for n in N and q in Q, equivariance gives (n a)q=n(a(n^(-1)q))=0. Dualizing the imported representation identity gives

    S_2=Sym^2 V*=V_3 tensor chi_3'.

To check its irreducibility precisely, let L be an N-submodule of V_3 tensor chi_3'. Because H_5 acts irreducibly on V_3 and trivially on chi_3', semisimplicity and Schur's lemma identify L with V_3 tensor W for a subspace W of chi_3'. Under the given tensor-product N-action, N-stability of L is equivalent to N/H_5-stability of W. Irreducibility of chi_3' gives W=0 or all of chi_3'. Thus S_2 really is an irreducible N-module; this is not a dimension-only assertion or an unjustified tensor-product irreducibility claim.

It follows that I_2 is zero or S_2. In the latter case all quadratic products with U vanish, whereas generation gives Q_2=S_2 U of dimension 20. This contradiction proves I_2=0.

The dual map Psi* identifies T with Sym^2 U. The imported U isomorphism with the self-dual W' therefore identifies T with chi_3'. Applying the same H_5-isotypic argument to S_1 tensor T makes that source an irreducible 15-dimensional N-module. The contraction map to S_4* is N-equivariant. If it vanished identically, every nonzero f in T would vanish on every product of a linear form and a quartic, and therefore on S_5 itself, a contradiction. Its kernel is either zero or the entire source; the nonzero map excludes the latter, so its kernel is zero.

The image equals I_4 perpendicular because, for a in S_4, the condition that every contraction l contraction f vanishes on a is exactly f(l a)=0 for every l in S_1 and f in T, which is the degree-four condition a in I_4. Taking annihilator complements in finite dimensions proves the equality of spaces. Hence dim(S/I)_4=15. Together with the previous dimensions, this yields exactly the claimed partial Hilbert function, leaving its degree-three entry undetermined by this proof.

Finally, any module (S/J)^2 for an ideal J generated by five nonzero independent quadrics has every element of J in its scalar annihilator. An S-module isomorphism preserves that annihilator. ## 5. Quartic-kernel duality and its generalization

The pairings identify the dual of beta:U^r -> Q_4 with a map Q_1 -> (U*)^r=(Q_5)^r. For m in Q_1 and (u_a) in U^r, self-adjointness gives

    <m, sum_a j_a u_a> = sum_a <j_a m,u_a>.

Consequently that transpose is exactly alpha. The standard dual exact sequence then yields

    ker alpha = (coker beta)* = (Q_4/JU)*,
    rank alpha = dim JU.

This works with dependent, repeated, or zero quartics. The source U^r of beta records the chosen list, but its image depends only on J.

Taking the presentation in degree 4 gives the asserted quotient by R_4+J tensor U. The restriction J tensor U -> Q_4 has kernel (J tensor U) intersect R_4 and source dimension 2 dim J. Hence

    dim ker alpha = 10 - 2 dim J + dim((J tensor U) intersect R_4).

For five quartics, alpha is square of size ten. Its invertibility requires dim J=5, since otherwise beta has image dimension at most 2 dim J<10. With dim J=5, invertibility is equivalent to the stated trivial intersection. Both necessity and sufficiency are established, without asserting either for any actual cofactors.

Since Q has no negative degrees and (J) is generated in degree 4, the degree-four part of (J)Q is precisely JU. The standard module-quotient interpretation is consequently exact. For a family in S_d acting from Q_k to Q_(k+d), the transpose has image sum_a j_a Q_(5-k-d) inside Q_(5-k); this verifies the stated generalization with exactly the claimed index range.

## 6. Graded rigidity and ungraded indecomposability

For a degree-zero S-endomorphism f, let g=f|U and let h be its action on Q_5 identified with U*. S-linearity gives h B=B g for every B arising from a quintic. Since c is nonzero and Psi is surjective, every symmetric bilinear form on U occurs. In a chosen basis, the identity symmetric matrix gives h=g as matrices in the corresponding primal and dual bases. This is a legitimate basis calculation; it does not claim that U and U* have a canonical identification.

Commuting with diag(1,0) forces both off-diagonal entries of g to vanish. Commuting with the symmetric matrix having both off-diagonal entries 1 then forces the two diagonal entries to agree. Thus g=h=lambda identity. Since U generates Q, f=lambda identity on every graded piece. No equivariance hypothesis on f is used. This proves the full degree-zero endomorphism assertion, rather than only an equivariant version.

For completeness, the homogeneous decomposition of an arbitrary ungraded endomorphism is also valid. For x in Q_k, define

    f_d(x) = projection of f(x) to Q_(k+d).

Only finitely many d occur. If a is homogeneous, taking the appropriate graded component of f(ax)=a f(x) gives f_d(ax)=a f_d(x), so each f_d is itself S-linear. If d<0, then f_d(U)=0 because Q has no negative degrees, and generation forces f_d=0. The degree-zero part is scalar by the preceding argument.

Therefore every ungraded endomorphism has a unique form lambda identity+n, where n strictly raises degree. The degree-raising maps form a two-sided ideal E_+ because there are no negative-degree endomorphisms. Any product of six such maps vanishes on Q, so E_+^6=0. For lambda nonzero the inverse is the finite geometric series

    (lambda identity+n)^(-1)
      = lambda^(-1) sum_(j=0)^5 (-lambda^(-1)n)^j.

For lambda=0 the map is nilpotent and cannot be a unit. Hence E_+ is precisely the ideal of nonunits, and End_S(Q) is a local algebra with residue field C.

If e is idempotent, its scalar part is 0 or 1. In the first case e belongs to the nilpotent ideal and is zero; in the second, 1-e is an idempotent in that ideal and is zero. An ungraded direct-sum decomposition would give a nontrivial S-linear idempotent projection. Since there are no such idempotents, Q is indecomposable even after forgetting its grading.

Finally, the composite of one graded perfect self-duality with the inverse of another is a graded automorphism of Q and therefore a nonzero scalar. The asserted uniqueness of graded perfect self-duality up to scalar follows.
