## 1. Exact lift signs and top closure

On internal degree r the two-term End homotopy is

`H_r(F)=h_K F+(-1)^r E F h_K`, with `E=IP`.

Expanding the graded commutator proves `d_End H+H d_End=1-E(-)E`. Under the source-compatible projection, the latter is `1-j pi`, with `j(R)=I R^t P`. Both H terms and the single row transpose matter. On degree zero, the projected first correction vanishes by the imported actual frame identity `pi_first delta j(R)=delta_row R` and the **complete source's** closure. It is not implied merely by local constant-matrix contraction identities.

For `D=delta+(-1)^q d_End`, J_s has bidegree `(2+s,-s)`. Thus the recurrence is exactly

`J0=jR; J1=+H_0 delta J0; J2=-H_-1 delta J1; J3=+H_-2 delta J2`.

It gives `dJ0=0`, `delta J0-dJ1=0`, `delta J1+dJ2=0`, and `delta J2-dJ3=0`. In negative internal degrees the projected cohomology term is zero. Original-frame restrictions commute with d_End, so `d_End delta J3=delta^2 J2=0`.
