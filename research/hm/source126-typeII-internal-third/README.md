# Internal cubic versus original fourth gate

This conditional source126 Type II packet constructs the global internal third Bockstein after the internal second vanishing. Third-order radical naturality confines it to a canonical two-dimensional equivariant parameter space. An exact comparison and rank-one exclusion show that its nonvanishing is equivalent to nonvanishing of the generic original fourth Bockstein.

No actual coefficient or nonvanishing alternative is selected. No source or catalogue pair is excluded.

- PROOF.md: complete argument, immutable references, gradings, and limits.
- INPUTS.json and inputs/: three exact public proofs with SHA256 and Git-blob authentication.
- verify.py and RESULT.json: 48 exact character, universal binary-form, source-authentication, and synthetic correction checks.
