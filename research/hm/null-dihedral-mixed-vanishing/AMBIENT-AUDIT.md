# Independent audit of the dihedral action on the corrected mixed obstruction

## Conclusion and scope

For the corrected external-product algebroid and its unique compact pure boundary described in the cited comments, the mixed obstruction class C is invariant under the diagonal binary dihedral action. Its canonical weighted components C10 and C20 are invariant under the corresponding actual cohomology representations. With the specified determinant-one lifts, the generator line W and its square carry the trivial character, and the identification of the canonical bundle of P4 with O(-5) has no determinant character. There is no extra reflection sign, hidden gerbe character, or scalar-gauge ambiguity to invalidate this inference.

This audit does **not** independently establish that either cohomology representation has no invariants. The conclusion concerns the admitted algebraic-sheaf model and does not construct a physical primitive or revalidate imported geometric and Ext-vanishing premises.

## 1. Exact unscaled coefficient action

Write q=u x²+v xy+w y² and r=U x²+V xy+W y², and use the unscaled product coefficients

(a,b,c,d,e)=(uU,uV+vU,uW+vV+wU,vW+wV,wW).

Let ζ be a primitive fifth root. Use binary matrices

A=diag(ζ²,ζ⁻²), B=[[0,i],[i,0]].

Both have determinant one. Substitution by A gives quadratic coefficient weights (ζ⁴,1,ζ⁻⁴). Its quartic coefficient weights are (ζ⁸,ζ⁴,1,ζ⁻⁴,ζ⁻⁸)=(ζ⁻²,ζ⁻¹,1,ζ,ζ²). Thus this is exactly T(v_j)=ζ^(j−2)v_j in the specified unscaled basis. No binomial conjugation is needed or permissible here.

Substitution by B gives q↦−(w x²+v xy+u y²). The induced quadratic matrix is −R2, and the quartic coefficient matrix is R4, ordinary reversal. Consequently multiplication m is equivariant, literally including these scalar factors. A^5=1, B²=−1, and BAB⁻¹=A⁻¹. The central binary −1 acts trivially on Sym² and Sym⁴, so these representations genuinely factor through D5, not merely through its binary extension.

Using contragredient conventions reverses A to A⁻¹ but does not change the subgroup, reflection, or invariant subspace. A character calculation must use a consistent convention across all monad terms.

## 2. Generator and centered square

The section σ=vV−2(uW+wU) is fixed by A: every summand has total weight zero. It is fixed by B: the two minus signs from the quadratic factors cancel and uW+wU is exchanged with itself. This checks the actual generator, not just its line.

For the unscaled quartic invariant I=c²−3bd+12ae, T fixes each summand and R4 exchanges a with e and b with d, so I is fixed. Therefore τ=σ²−2I is fixed. Equivalently, σ is the discriminant polarization, a GL2 relative invariant of determinant weight two, and I has determinant weight four; both become genuine invariants on SL2. Even ordinary binary swap has determinant −1 and introduces no sign in either expression.

Since W=H0(G(1)) is generated by this σ with the compatible Sym⁴ linearisation of O(1), W is the trivial D5 representation, and so is (W²)*. Multiplication, algebra trace, the centered map defining τ, and the constant trace projector are equivariant. Thus the canonical splitting G=W O(-1)⊕W² O(-2) is equivariant, not merely an abstract splitting of bundles.

## 4. Actual algebroid equivariance, not only HKR equivariance

The same applies on all higher nerve components.

## 5. Boundary transport, scalar choices and obstruction invariance

The imported compact pure-lift statement is stronger than existence: with fixed reduction, each pure lift is unique up to unique reduction-preserving isomorphism, from H1(End E⊗K_z)=H0(End E⊗K_z)=0 and the exchanged equations. These exact premises appear in comment 6260553958.

Functoriality of the mixed small-extension obstruction therefore identifies the transformed obstruction with C.

For class invariance, H1-vanishing and the explicit pure-gauge coboundary formula of comment 6271101716 already suffice: changing the chosen pure representatives changes C only by a Čech boundary. The HM isomorphisms may be projective rather than honest linearisation maps. The matrix-tracefree projection is equivariant. If an individual primitive used for trace removal is not invariant, that affects only representatives, not the resulting class. Similarly, retaining or resetting induced mixed coefficients in a pure gauge changes the normalized source by the established coboundary, not by a new character. An arbitrary change of the ambient mixed algebroid would be a different problem; none is made here.
