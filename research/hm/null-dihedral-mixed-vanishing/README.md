# Actual dihedral symmetry and mixed obstruction vanishing

## Result and scope

Over C, the actual order-ten D5 subgroup of the unscaled binary-quartic HM action has no invariants in the two retained targets H²(End₀H(−4)) and H²(End₀H(−3)). These have dimensions 10 and 20. The actual corrected external-product algebroid, canonical coefficient lines and unique pure boundary make the mixed class invariant in exactly these representations. Both retained components therefore vanish. Together with the earlier constant-component transfer, this gives zero canonical mixed class.

This is a theorem for the specified mixed small extension, with the imported geometric/source, algebraic-sheaf, cohomology and pure-boundary premises. It is not a pure-square vanishing theorem or a full total-degree-two or all-order lift. D5 is established; full SL2 equivariance of HM is not assumed.

## Original-boundary consequence and limitations

If D_raw=j(c)+δq and δH_model=c on a common acyclic algebraic refinement, then x=−j(H_model)−q solves δx=−D_raw while retaining the original reduction and pure first-order terms. The class equality and comparison boundaries are essential. Averaging in characteristic zero preserves the required finite-group character.

Existence of regular algebraic primitives does not supply their coefficients, prove equality with a frozen second-order coefficient, preserve unproved original-chart pole/resource bounds, settle pure-square obstructions or imply higher-order extension. The F3 perfect-complex statement is not promoted to this characteristic-zero geometric theorem.
