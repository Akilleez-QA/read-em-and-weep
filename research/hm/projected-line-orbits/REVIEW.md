## Checks

1. **Universal cyclic calculation.** Central rescaling changes the reversal discrepancy by twice the scalar exponent, and two is invertible modulo five. It preserves order five. A Heisenberg commutator with primitive central character cyclically permutes the five eigenspaces, forcing each to have dimension one. Reversal pairs the four nontrivial characters. Its prescribed positive and negative dimensions force the trivial-character eigenline to have positive sign. On each pair, projection of the translated negative vector has coefficient `(zeta^(jk)-zeta^(-jk))/2`, which is nonzero for j=1,2 and k=1,2,3,4. Hence the four nonzero labels in each cyclic direction have exactly the same image plane.

2. **Distinctness.** Equivariance and transitivity make the collection of image lines one orbit in the dual target plane. The dual irreducible three-dimensional representation has no orbit of size less than six: simplicity excludes sizes two through four, irreducibility excludes one, and the A4 stabilizer of a putative size-five orbit has a Klein four subgroup with invariant dimension zero. This last computation applies equally to the dual representation. Since there are six cyclic directions, all six images are distinct, with exactly four distinguished source lines per image.

3. **Generic degree without global normality.** A positive-dimensional fiber above the generic point of a target line would close to a dimension-two proper closed subset of the integral surface, impossible. Properness therefore gives finiteness near that generic point. Base change to the target discrete valuation ring gives a finite torsion-free module of generic rank d-7, hence a free module. The four disjoint source lines give four distinct rational points in the special fiber; their positive lengths force d-7 >= 4. This does not assume that the morphism is everywhere finite, that S is globally normal, or that flatness holds everywhere. Divisibility by five then excludes d=10 and gives 15 <= d <= 95 with 5 dividing d. The generic degree is consequently in {8,13,...,88}; its actual value remains undetermined.

4. **Residual divisors.** Pulling back each target line gives an effective Cartier divisor because S is integral and the dominant map does not map S into that line. Its local equation is divisible by the equation of each of the four disjoint distinguished source lines, in their regular neighborhoods. Dividing produces an effective Cartier residual divisor, including across points where other residual components meet those lines. Its class is H minus all five lines of the cyclic subgroup, since the projection bundle already subtracts the center line. Using H.L=1, L^2=-5, and pairwise disjointness gives degree d-5, square d-35, intersection six with each subgroup line, and intersection one with each other distinguished line. No nonnegative-square or moving-system conclusion follows, as the proof correctly states.

5. **Two ordinary flex contacts per projected line.** The pullback of the six-line union has degree 36. The faithful A5 action on the normalization has orbit lengths 12, 20, 30, and 60. Its unique size-12 orbit consists of the two fixed points of each of its six order-five subgroups: distinct subgroups cannot share a point because point stabilizers are cyclic. Hence the invariant divisor of degree 36 is exactly three times that orbit. A point on at least three target lines would have at least six translates, using at least eighteen distinct line-pairs although only fifteen are available. Thus there are precisely fifteen pair-intersection points. The source orbit of size twelve cannot map to the ten-node orbit by equivariance and equal fiber cardinalities; its image is therefore smooth and normalization is injective there. This image orbit of size twelve cannot be contained in the fifteen pair intersections, as the three-point invariant remainder would contradict the target small-orbit bound. Each of the twelve smooth points consequently lies on exactly one target line and has contact order exactly three with it. Bezout degree six forces exactly two such points on each line and no other intersections. These are ordinary flex contacts, not higher contacts; no claim that all sextic flexes are thereby classified is justified or made.
