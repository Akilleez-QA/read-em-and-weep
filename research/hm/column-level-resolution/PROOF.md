# Uniform column levelness and exclusion of the fourth linear strand

## Inputs and scope

Work in characteristic zero. Let S=Sym(T), dim(T)=5, and let Gamma be the level-five Heisenberg group acting on T through its irreducible five-dimensional representation with faithful central character. The binary space W is Gamma-trivial. For every nonzero binary covector lambda, let J=J_lambda be the ideal generated by the contracted quadratic presentation entries, and A=S/J.

The following pinned input is used: PR511, commit 55bc80b2cb660edd741c93747df7304ee51a061e, proves uniformly that J has ten independent quadratic generators and A has Hilbert function (1,5,5). In particular A_d=0 for d>=3 and there are fifteen linear first syzygies. See https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/55bc80b2cb660edd741c93747df7304ee51a061e/research/hm/binary-symmetric-propagation/PROOF.md .

For the representation input, see Horrocks–Mumford, “A rank 2 vector bundle on P4 with 15,000 symmetries” (Topology 12, 1973), §1, especially (1.1)–(1.2): https://www.dam.brown.edu/people/mumford/alg_geom/papers/1973a--Rk2BdleP%5E4-NC.pdf . That section defines the order-125 subgroup of SL(5), its central extension, and its four five-dimensional irreducibles and twenty-five quotient characters. Its §2.2 gives the corresponding multiplicity-space principle. In the repository notation T=V* and W=W′ is a representation inflated from the normalizer quotient, hence Gamma-trivial. The identification W=H_0=W′ and the quadratic presentation are pinned in PR490, commit 1c5ad353bb5f1808150f64cde3c29b92c6e13d91: https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/1c5ad353bb5f1808150f64cde3c29b92c6e13d91/research/hm/dual-tensor-bridge/VERIFICATION.md .

All arguments below are uniform and invariant under field extension. We may pass to an algebraic closure, prove the assertions there, and descend Betti numbers and socle dimensions by faithful flatness. No selected source-instance evaluation or multiplication-matrix rank computation is used.

## 1. Uniform levelness

Since W is Gamma-trivial, every J_lambda is Gamma-stable. Thus Soc(A)_1 is a Gamma-subrepresentation of A_1=T. Irreducibility makes this socle either zero or all of T. The latter would force T*T=0, contrary to dim(A_2)=5. Also Soc(A)_0=0, since T is nonzero. Since A_3=0, all of A_2 is socle. Consequently A is level of socle degree two and type five.

In particular the last term of its minimal S-resolution is 5S(-7); there is no S(-6) summand there.

## 2. A structural exclusion of fourth linear syzygies

We prove beta_{4,5}(A)=0. This strengthens the level conclusion and removes a possible ghost parameter not determined by the Hilbert series.

Consider the internal-degree-five part of the Koszul complex:

Lambda^5 T -> T tensor Lambda^4 T -> A_2 tensor Lambda^3 T.

The determinant character of the standard Heisenberg representation is trivial, so identify T tensor Lambda^4 T with End(T), up to the harmless common determinant line. The image of Lambda^5 T is the scalar endomorphism line. Under this identification, an endomorphism f is a Koszul cycle precisely when

x_i f(x_j) - x_j f(x_i) = 0 in A_2 for every i,j,

where x_1,...,x_5 is any basis of T. This follows directly by extracting the coefficient of the three-fold wedge complementary to i,j in the Koszul differential. The scalar endomorphism always satisfies these identities.

Under conjugation by Gamma, End(T) is the direct sum, each with multiplicity one, of the twenty-five characters of Gamma/Z(Gamma). Its trivial line is the scalar line. For completeness, the twenty-five Heisenberg operators obtained from representatives of Gamma/Z give these character lines: the commutator pairing is nondegenerate, so their conjugation characters are distinct and exhaust the twenty-five-dimensional endomorphism space.

Suppose the cycle space contained a nontrivial character. Its unique line is spanned by a noncentral Heisenberg operator f. This f has five distinct eigenvalues. Indeed, choose a Heisenberg operator h whose commutator with f is a primitive fifth root zeta. Conjugation by h sends f to zeta f. Since f is invertible and has finite order up to scalar, it is diagonalizable; its spectrum is stable under multiplication by zeta. In dimension five, this is one orbit of five distinct eigenvalues.

Choose an eigenbasis x_i with f(x_i)=a_i x_i, with all a_i distinct. The cycle identities imply (a_j-a_i)x_i x_j=0 in A_2. Hence all ten mixed monomials x_i x_j, i<j, belong to J_2. They are independent and dim(J_2)=10, so they form a basis of J_2. Since J is generated by its quadrics,

J=(x_i x_j : i<j).

But this quotient has nonzero pure cubes, for example x_1^3, contradicting A_3=0. Thus the cycle space has no nontrivial character and is precisely the scalar boundary line. This proves beta_{4,5}(A)=0 for every nonzero lambda.

The contradiction uses quadratic generation essentially. If additional cubic generators were allowed, the mixed-product argument alone would not exclude such cycles.

## 3. The remaining minimal-resolution parameter

Regularity(A)=2 and J has no linear forms. Thus, in positive homological degree i, only shifts i+1 and i+2 may occur. The preceding results and the Hilbert numerator

(1+5t+5t^2)(1-t)^5 = 1-10t^2+15t^3+5t^4-26t^5+20t^6-5t^7

give the complete numerical template

F_0 = S,
F_1 = 10S(-2),
F_2 = 15S(-3) + (5+u)S(-4),
F_3 = uS(-4) + 26S(-5),
F_4 = 20S(-6),
F_5 = 5S(-7),

where u=beta_{3,4}(A)>=0 is the only undetermined Betti number. This is a necessary exact template, not a claim that every value of u occurs.

The center acts on the internal-degree-four Koszul complex through the fourth power of its faithful character, hence nontrivially. Every irreducible Gamma representation with that central character has dimension five. Therefore u is divisible by five.

More concretely, in degree four the relevant complex is

Lambda^4 T -> T tensor Lambda^3 T -> A_2 tensor Lambda^2 T.

The first arrow is injective, of dimension five, and the middle term has dimension fifty. Hence u<=45. The last displayed arrow is nonzero, since some product in A_2 is nonzero and a three-fold wedge containing its first factor isolates that product in one wedge coordinate. Its image is a nonzero Gamma-representation with nontrivial central character, so has dimension at least five. Consequently u<=40. Thus u=5a with an integer 0<=a<=8.

Determining u requires an additional argument about the third linear strand. The Hilbert function, levelness, and the fourth-strand exclusion do not themselves prove u=0. In particular we do not assert the generic-looking template with u=0 is uniform.

## Contribution and verification boundary

The new results are uniform levelness and the structural vanishing beta_{4,5}=0, followed by reduction of all remaining numerical resolution uncertainty to the single divisible-by-five parameter u. The exclusion is a root-cause argument: a nontrivial fourth linear cycle would diagonalize the quadratic algebra into a mixed-product quotient, incompatible with its cubic vanishing.

This proof uses standard Heisenberg irreducibility and its conjugation-character decomposition, elementary Koszul homology, and the pinned PR511 Hilbert-function theorem. It is an authored mathematical proof pending independent review, not a formal proof-assistant check. It makes no first-order, higher-order, or algebraicity claim for a selected actual HM/source instance.
