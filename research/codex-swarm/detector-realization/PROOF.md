# Denotation and reduction

This is a coefficient-free implementation binding, with artificial execution
controls. Independent review must verify the literal lowering. It reports no
physical value and grants no arithmetic admission.

1. `bindings.py` authenticates the immutable pinned model/source/partition
   definitions and current reused implementations. `PhysicalModel` substitutes
   elementary symmetric v into every stored primitive rational matrix, including
   the actual auxiliary denominator, and constructs W, eta and L with the pinned
   pivot. It does not replace the auxiliary homogeneous v0 by an invented finite
   denominator. Finite representation is used throughout; PH supplies its
   physical homogeneous interpretation, rather than resampling at infinity.
2. The field relation is y_i^2=x_i^3-x_i. V_i(x_i)=y_i and
   V_i(y_i)=(3x_i^2-1)/2. Physical nu=product(y_i/x_i)^mask and
   D=diag(nu,nu^-1) are rational functions BEFORE differentiation. In particular
   V_i log nu=mask_i*y_i*(x_i^2+1)/(2*x_i*(x_i^2-1)). This physical endpoint
   current is distinct from gerbe q_ij=(mask_j-mask_i)*y_i/x_i. The four block
   connection uses gamma_i=mask_i*y_i/[x_i*(x_i^2-1)], the pinned source's
   separate line connection. Both sources of endpoint derivatives are retained.
3. The literal four-block traces, signs(-,+,-,+), K-line weights(-1,1,3,5),
   both endpoint linear terms and -15q_aq_b/8 give s_raw. The Source superclass
   then computes the seven kinetic and three gerbe baseline terms, tf M0,
   both Fk corrections and both E corrections, including mixed second jets.
   BI's independently reviewed theorem identifies this fresh raw representative's
   global obstruction class and all32 values with the accepted N14 representative.
   There is no inner N14 call or user-supplied source callback in the physical body.
4. In the original K frame, j=I D R_row^t D^-1 P: the common nu factors
   in Iphys/Pphys cancel. Original HK is bare H0/H1/eta; physical nu^2 is not
   appended again. The accepted SC Lift implements J0=jR, J1=H0 delta J0,
   J2=-H_-1 delta J1. The common schema retains J3 for closure; the terminal
   detector needs J2 only. Closure and class identification consume RS/JC, not

5. Sort the WHOLE J2 five-owner tuple once with parity, then apply face recursion
   preserving its order. Its original outer weights remain attached to their
   original full owners and tags. Primitive R is queried on these ordered faces;
   we do not use transported raw R to assert equality of distinct local original
   resolution inclusions. No repeated-physical-owner pruning is used here.
6. `outer_weight` is the exact finite first-hit B_j and endpoint Cbar/Dbar
   polynomial definition. All nonnegative multi-indices and exact chained integer
   binomials are included. For s=4, H_(4-j)=0 for j<4, so only primary4 and

7. Weights are scalar functions of twist zero on THEIR own charts, pulled back
   via t=1/x exactly once. The original-frame scalar coefficient is assembled
   fully before the y^15 component is selected. Its rational-x expression is
   subjected to exact denominator polynomial division; a remainder is rejected.
   There is no early zero from partial support or local baseline cancellation.
   This terminal division implements the Laurent polynomial coefficient when the
   accepted whole-source extension premises apply; failure to divide is a defect,
   not a zero. Source-specific identity is not inferred from support overlap.

## Artificial executable control

ArtificialFrames uses six explicit unipotent SL2 frames with independent x0/x2
and x1/x3 directions. Its transition is H_b H_a^-1. The first coefficient of
H_b star H_a^(star inverse) and the explicit gerbe primitive produce a valid tiny
scalar solver. All5 residual components are evaluated in the RF field. Its
contraction matrices are constant, so extracting a complete-source coefficient
before HPL commutes with their linear operations. This special optimization is
NOT used by the physical body. Its finite, explicit one-owner/chart weights
request that coefficient. All24 signed stairs are included; all24 contributions
are nonzero and sum0. Dropping the baseline or either Fk term changes the
source coefficient on labels(0,1,3). The E terms are retained but happen to have
zero selected coefficient in that control; no claim of omission sensitivity to
E is made. The literal four-block scalar has a separate endpoint-term control.

## Execution and aggregate boundary

The physical entry and all physical helper entry points hard refuse. Import and
pin authentication perform no field arithmetic. Generic small first-hit controls
execute independently. The code is deliberately not a new budget authority.
All object lifetimes must be counted: per-assignment model caches, source/lift
face caches, one term and the evolving total numerator/denominator, plus RF
normalization temporaries. Physical method lru caches are explicitly cleared in
finally per assignment; Mat records only counts per matrix shape. This does not
bound peak bytes. Integer binomial work is separate from RF field-product units.
No amount of hypothetical live-object accounting reconciles a missing shared
ledger. AC must establish a whole-root N/D/bit/work/storage certificate against
that ledger before any release could be considered.
