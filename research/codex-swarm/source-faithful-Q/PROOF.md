This proves the fixed formula meaning of the QH01 metadata constructors. It is coefficient-free. It does not authenticate an arbitrary callable by its name, a hash or an admission Boolean. Actual coefficient methods refuse unconditionally, including structural-zero cases. Source P identities, source-order comparisons, primitive/harmonic binding and a physical execution schedule remain separate.

## 2. Local catalog semantics

On standard chart e, substitute (X_i,Y_i)=(x_i,1) for a finite coordinate and(1,t_i) for an infinity coordinate. The coefficient v_j of product_i(Y_i+X_i z) is the sum over j-element subsets A, choosing X_i for i in A and Y_i otherwise. Therefore its expression is the product of x_i at finite i in A and t_i at infinity i outside A. This is exactly ActualCatalog.bind and QB's independently written local_reference constructor; comparison occurs in one interned expression ring without evaluating a coefficient. All16 representation charts are controlled.

The homogeneous primary generator h_j=v_j^5-product(v0,...,v4) and auxiliary a=v0 v1 v3 v4 are substituted literally. In particular the local auxiliary includes v0, even though v0=1 in the all-finite frame. Primary degree is5 and auxiliary degree4. H_k is the complete homogeneous polynomial in only the finite chart variables. Its finite composition domain includes H0=1 and gives H_k>0=0 on an empty alphabet. These are precisely the c_i=(-1)^(s-i)H_(s-i) coefficients of the chart-unit identity sum_(i=0..s)c_i v_i=1. No matrix primitive catalog, carrier relabeling or physical coefficient table is introduced.

## 3. Fixed N14 partition semantics

For N14, M27,T135 and L=(s+1)134+1, the reviewed first-hit node sums multinomial(L;n) product_i c_i^n_i v_i^(n_i-T[i=j]), over sum n=L, n_j>=T and n_i<T for i<j. All powers are nonnegative. Every composition has a unique first slot reaching T, by L>(s+1)(T-1). Thus

    sum_j B_j v_j^T = (sum_i c_i v_i)^L = 1.

Put z=v2*a, so h_j+z=v_j^5. The primary quotient is B_j Cbar_j, where Cbar_j=sum_(k=N..M) binom(M,k)h_j^(k-N)z^(M-k). The auxiliary quotient is sum_j B_j Dbar_j, where Dbar_j=sum_(k=0..N-1)binom(M,k)h_j^k v2^(M-k)a^(M-k-N). Multiplying these by their respective owned H^N gives the two disjoint binomial ranges0..M. Therefore

    h_j^N Cbar_j + a^N Dbar_j = (h_j+v2*a)^M = v_j^T.

The same formula proves the fixed carrier weights sum to1. It also proves each displayed carrier quotient is polynomial and divides the matching carrier weight, without polynomial division. Primary j>s has defined weight and quotient0. QH01 implements these exact finite index domains; QB consumes that reviewed algorithm at the pinned source hash.

For one legal coordinate the fixed weights are(1,0) finite and(t^2,1-t^2) infinity. Pair legal equations are(1,x(x^2-1)) finite and(t,1-t^2) infinity. Polynomial quotient definitions are therefore(1,0) finite and(t,1) infinity. This remains valid at zeros of the equations because the identities are polynomial; it is not pointwise division. Complementary coordinates retain their raw legal weights. Hence the constructor's local scalar quotient q obeys

    rho_owner = C_local,owner * q,
    C_local,owner = H_local,owner^14 product_(i in pair)ell_local,owner,i.

The finite and local C ASTs use exactly this same owner, carrier and pair. The ledger retains H power14 and one copy of each pair legal equation. Equal equations on two occurrences remain two ledgers.

## 4. Finite-frame identification and standard extension

Let c_i=14*d_owner+deg(ell_owner,i)[i in pair], with d=4 for auxiliary and5 for primary, legal degree1 or3. Homogeneity of the displayed generators proves

    C_local(local(x)) = product_(infinity i)x_i^(-c_i) C_finite(x).

The finite auxiliary has degree3 but this equation uses its homogeneous degree4, including the hidden local v0 factor. Since rho is scalar of twist0, the exact quotient in the global finite frame is

    Qhat(x) = q(local(x)) product_(infinity i)x_i^(-c_i),
    C_finite(x) Qhat(x) = rho(x).

Thus local exponent alpha maps to alpha in finite coordinates and -c_i-alpha_i in infinity coordinates. QB checks the exact QH shift against these c_i. No pair-character shift is added, no scalar rho frame is attached, and no derivatives or input tuple reordering occur. Since q is a polynomial on the owner's entire standard open, Qhat represents a regular section of O(-c) there. This is the local quotient premise in SW01's whole-standard extension theorem. It does not prove an arbitrary emitted source P has the corresponding exact homogeneous degree or equals the actual whole source.

## 5. API and limits

ActualQBinding(repo=None).describe(owner,pair) returns definition metadata: fixed parameters, complete owner, divisor degree and ledger, local Q AST, finite/local C AST, signed frame map and conservative finite support. constructor_semantics_checked records the literal catalog/frame comparison above; it is not a trusted admission token. The descriptor's own scope and physical flags say so. Actual coefficient/factor_terms and actual C coefficient methods always refuse, before structural-zero or exponent checks. No arbitrary catalog or status enters this actual constructor.

ArtificialBinding accepts only the explicit ArtificialCatalog and QH's small N1/N2 path. Its coefficient and finite-stream methods are artificial controls, with exact owner/pair matching. It cannot authenticate actual HM data. Actual source fibers are neither constructed nor traversed.

QH budgets are per Ring: a descriptor constructs a quotient Ring and separate finite/local C Rings. Passing its local caps does not establish an aggregate cost or peak-memory certificate across descriptors, fibers or workers. Descriptor consumers must account for every construction and retained AST in a shared prospective schedule before physical execution. No such release is supplied here.

Repository constraint: `repo` is either None or a path resolving to the canonical workspace repository containing this module. Other paths are rejected before any authentication. All construction and description therefore consume the same canonical source repository; alternate repository loading is unsupported.
