Only artificial catalogs and finite artificial quotient/fiber oracles execute. Real-model coefficient calls raise `PhysicalAdmissionMissing`, including negative or out-of-support requests. No physical capability, coefficient, source value, finished scan, or obstruction decision is supplied.

`source_program.actual_program_definition(repo)` authenticates the PR85 circuit and the separate pinned primitive catalog by SHA256. The root is node152. `polynomial_coefficients.py` implements all actual opcodes: cleared I/P catalog substitution in elementary symmetric functions, mask/legal factors, rational scales, sums, ordered matrix multiplication, transpose, trace, powers, and polynomial partials after substitution. Matrix products retain source ordering. Expression nodes and coefficients are cached under monotone bounded budgets. Products request exactly the finite coefficient convolution compatible with both per-coordinate degree enclosures; no dense polynomial or exponent box is materialized. Declared bounds are enclosures, not occupied-support claims. Small tests also exercise the real 153-op topology with an artificial identity catalog.

A `FullOwner` retains seed, occurrence, carrier, low-bit legal mask and high-bit standard tag. Source slices sort once by auxiliary-first carrier, mask and tag and apply the single permutation sign externally. Equal source `(carrier, mask)` values give zero because this F is pulled back from the source cover; this rule is not for arbitrary T/Rsharp. Clearing occurrences remain distinct even for equal polynomial factors. `RequestContext` retains every outer weight owner, the exact leading prefix, and the output edge. It records their identity; it does not apply the outer weights or certify a detector sum.

`PrismProgram` evaluates exactly

    Z sum_u Qhat_u P[u,a,b] - Ca Z sum_uv Qhat_u Qhat_v P[u,v,b].

Inserted u and v have separate occurrence IDs and tags matching a and b respectively. Powers and owned factor multiplicities are retained. It cannot differentiate a lone prism as if it were N.
