Two limitations are demonstrated by passing adversarial controls (these tests assert the limitations, not their repair):

- `PrismProgram` checks inserted tags but does not verify returned seed/occurrence, duplicate labels, or completeness. A custom provider can return seed 999 and a wrong occurrence ID, and those owners reach the source. Unlike STD's Pi adapter, INS does not enforce occurrence binding. Thus full-owner preservation is accepted for conforming providers, including the bundled synthetic provider; an unconditional claim that arbitrary providers cannot lose owners is rejected. RequestContext retains outer identities but neither applies weights nor proves a detector sum.
- Support boxes are trusted mathematical premises. `WholeNumerator` accepts `None` for prism support even with nonzero prisms; convolution then drops real terms. An understated leaf box likewise loses a genuine coefficient. Valid-box syntax does not certify an enclosure. Derivative correctness is accepted with valid complete enclosures only. Cancellation may loosen bounds; a box does not assert occupied support.

This division is honestly disclosed in the README/contract, including the unverified prism enclosure. Standard B is an executable synthetic K+AhS program, but the actual Pi inputs and witnesses remain external. Its retained harmonic projection prevents inferring universal exactness from that program.
