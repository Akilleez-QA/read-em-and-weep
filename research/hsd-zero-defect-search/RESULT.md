# Length of Q on the five-parameter formal chart

## Question

Let the formal-lift coordinates be `(a, b, c, d, λ)`. At the point `([0:1], [0:0:0:1])`, write `Q = N**/N` for the central defect of a carrier with central stalk `N`. Is there a value of `(a, b, c, d, λ)` at which this length is `0`?

## Pinned inputs

The frozen module is the cokernel of `H_0^T` in PR #1 comment 6104798062. Over `B = Q[x, y, w]` it is generated by `e1, e2` with relations

```
(-x y/2, 0),  (-w^2 x/2, 0),  ((x+y)/2, -w^2/2),
(-w^2/8, -x/2),  (-w^2/8, y/2).
```

Comment 6104803214 records the same five relations. The formal chart is `research/carrier/formal-five-parameter-chart/` at `ca9226569ddb5ed29d2809f1f4e4e1ae7c646ea8` and `6b69eefd57098d9a0c1d9f43ed993d36e1e54bcb`. Its coordinates are the classes `a ν_X`, `b ν_Y`, `c z^{-2} X E_0^2`, `d z^{-2} Y E_0^2`, and `λ z^{-1} E_0^2`.

## Fixed fiber

`fixed_length.py` encodes the five relations as an ideal in `Q[e1, e2, x, y, w]` by imposing `e1^2 = e1 e2 = e2^2 = 0`, then computes a grevlex Gröbner basis over `Q`. The leading module monomials are

```
e1 y^3, e1 x^2, e1 x y, e2 y^2, e2 w^2, e1 w^2, e2 x.
```

Each generator has a pure power of `x`, of `y`, and of `w` in the initial module, so the quotient is supported only at `(x, y, w) = 0` and is finite. The standard monomials are the eight monomials `e1, w e1, x e1, x w e1, y e1, y w e1, y^2 e1, y^2 w e1` and the four monomials `e2, w e2, y e2, y w e2`. Thus

```
length_B(Q) = 12
```

at this frozen carrier. Adding `1` to the first coefficient of the first relation produces a different finite length, so the count is read from the relations.

No coefficient of these relations lies in a polynomial ring over `(a, b, c, d, λ)`.

## What is missing

A search over the chart needs a presentation of this cokernel, or of `N`, whose coefficients lie in `Q[a, b, c, d, λ][x, y, w]` (or in `Q(a, b, c, d, λ)[x, y, w]`), together with the chart point that specializes to the five relations above.

That presentation is not in the formal chart. Section 7 of `formal-chart.md` at both pins says that the relative carrier presentation, the total-space reflexive hull, the central reduction, and the central hull are not computed, and that no family-wide defect value follows. The branch `research/carrier-defect-length-twelve` is `fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb` and adds no such presentation. No other file in the pinned chart, in `research/history/02-secant-and-monad-tests.md`, or on `research/hm-carrier-atlas` contains these relations or a parameter-dependent replacement.

## Result

**Inconclusive.** The only certified value is `length = 12` at the one frozen module. One fiber does not bound the other chart points. No semicontinuity statement is used, and no relative-support certificate for a family is available because the family module is not pinned.
