# Conditional conductor-module application

Assume the exact module, pairings, primary-defect identifications and endpoint theorem listed in unit09 INPUTS.md. The historical text below does not establish those premises merely from the conductor geometry.

## Consequences for the cofactor and lowest-degree pairing

Let M be the actual graded cohomology module with perfect symmetric pairings M_t×M_(−5−t)→C and self-adjoint polynomial multiplication. Set H₀=M_(−5), T=M_(−4), with dimensions two and ten; H₀*=M_0. Define

    μ:S_1⊗H₀→T,       μ(r⊗h)=rh,
    α:T→(M_0)^5,      α(x)_r=j_r x.

For r,s∈{a,b,c,d,e} define the block bilinear form

    C_rs(h,h′)=⟨s h,j_r h′⟩=⟨h,(s j_r)h′⟩.

Thus C is the block matrix of αμ, with each block in Sym²(H₀*). Symmetry here concerns h,h′ within each block; it does not assert C_rs=C_sr. Self-adjoint multiplication and the symmetric trace pairing prove the block symmetry.

Every displayed linear syzygy gives a tensor identity in Sym²(H₀*). Explicitly:

    C_aa+C_bb+C_cc+C_dd+C_ee=0,
    4C_ab+3C_bc+2C_cd+C_de=0,
    C_ba+2C_cb+3C_dc+4C_ed=0,
    4C_aa+2C_bb−2C_dd−4C_ee=0.

These are the zero spatial partial trace and the three zero infinitesimal-SL₂ contractions. In coordinates, for each infinitesimal coefficient matrix A, the contraction is Σ A_rs C_rs=tr(A^t C)=0. No SL₂ action on M is required: these are polynomial identities applied through its S-module structure. The four spatial coefficient tensors are independent. Since dim Sym²(H₀*)=3, they impose twelve independent linear equations on an otherwise unrestricted array of twenty-five symmetric 2×2 blocks. This last count describes the ambient linear tensor conditions only, not the dimension of the actual family of HM pairings or its rank.

Equivariantly, the multiplication map S_1⊗K_4→K_5 has kernel V_0⊕V_2, supplied respectively by Euler and the Lie columns. Since V_4⊗V_4=V_8⊕V_6⊕V_4⊕V_2⊕V_0, its image is K_5≅V_8⊕V_6⊕V_4, of dimension 21. Hence the block tensor factors through K_5 on its two spatial factors. This is a representation statement about polynomials, not about M.

There is also a direct graded-module consequence:

    (M_(−6))^4 --L→ (M_(−5))^5 --α*→ M_(−1),

is a complex, and by duality so is

    M_(−4) --α→ (M_0)^5 --L^t→ (M_1)^4.

Thus im L⊂ker α* in general. One must not assert exactness after tensoring the polynomial resolution or infer endpoint vanishing merely from the listed dimensions in degrees −5 through 0. For the actual HM module, the newly proved abelian-zero-surface theorem in prerequisite hm-low-degree-dependency, document PROOF.md (see the unit INPUTS.md) and its independent audit now establish M_1=M_(−6)=0. Therefore these endpoint complexes supply no nonzero vectors in ker α* and no nontrivial equation after α at this degree. The earlier unresolved endpoint status is superseded. The spatial block identities above remain genuine universal constraints at degree zero, and higher connecting-map ambiguities are not removed by this endpoint vanishing.

More generally all polynomial consequences of the degree-five conductor syzygies are generated by these four columns. Additional relations coming from the module M may exist. The identities constrain αμ directly; they cannot be transported to a matrix of α by inverting μ unless lowest-degree generation has separately been proved. Even if μ is an isomorphism, vanishing partial trace and Lie contractions are constraints rather than a proof of singularity.

Finally the explicit B basis makes the existing defect specialization concrete:

    W_low=Σ B_r M_(−5)=S_1 M_(−5)=im μ.

With Q=I:T→M_(−2), R=J:T→T*, C_primary=im Q*, K_primary=ker Q, the earlier exact defect remains

    c−e=dim(J K_primary ∩ Soc(M)_(−1)),

where c=rank(R|K_primary×T), e=rank(R|K_primary×W_low). Nothing in the explicit B coefficients proves W_low=T or removes this socle intersection. Likewise the remaining quadratic-image defect, the primary rank, and the secondary map σ remain unevaluated. This construction removes universal coefficient ambiguity and supplies complete universal syzygies; it does not settle any retained obstruction.
