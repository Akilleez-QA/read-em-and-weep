# The actual zero scheme determines the maximal-line lifting tower

## Scope and inputs

Work over C on W=P² with smooth conic C and X_n=nC. Assume the actual saturated section sequence

    0 → K=O_W(a) → E → I_Z(-2-a) → 0,   a∈{0,1},

where E is locally free of rank two and Z is zero-dimensional lci. Set R=Z∩C, δ=length R, and

    A_1⊂E_C,  deg A_1=2a+δ,
    Q_1=E_C/A_1,  deg Q_1=-4-2a-δ,
    d=4a+4+2δ.

All degrees on C are P¹ degrees. Write λ_n=length(Z∩X_n), using the image ideal (I_Z,q^n), not a tensor-product ideal or an ideal-square quotient. We use the established numerical inputs length Z=N_a=16+2a+a² (16 or 19), and, when applicable, d≤20, hence δ≤8−2a. The sequence itself gives length Z=c₂(E)+2a+a², so the numerical specialization uses c₂(E)=16 as an additional input.

The results below concern the actual section and its zero scheme, but evaluate no actual Z, HM presentation, detector, source, or selected rank. Local algebra examples and general deformation bounds are not claimed to be globally realized.

## 1. Exact all-order criterion

For every n≥1, the following are equivalent:

1. A_1 lifts to a line subbundle A_n⊂E|X_n.
2. The image ideal I_Z O_X_n is locally principal (equivalently invertible as an ideal).
3. λ_n=nδ.

If a lift exists it is unique as an embedded line subbundle. This does not assert that E|X_n splits.

### Necessity of the ideal criterion

Given A_n, its quotient Q_n is a line bundle. The reduced degree of Hom(K|X_n,Q_n) is −4−4a−δ. Its successive layers have degrees −4−4a−δ−4j for 0≤j<n. All have zero H⁰, so H⁰(X_n,Hom(K,Q_n))=0. The actual K map therefore factors through A_n. In a local frame of E adapted to A_n its coordinate ideal is principal. The coordinate ideal of K→E is exactly I_Z, hence its image on X_n is I_Z O_X_n.

### Sufficiency and gluing

Locally choose a generator h of the image ideal. Its reduction on C generates the ideal of R, so it is a nonzerodivisor on X_n: a function whose reduction modulo q is nonzero in the regular one-dimensional local domain remains regular modulo q^n. Write the section vector as h v. Since its coordinates generate (h), cancellation of regular h shows that the coordinates of v generate the unit ideal. Thus v is primitive and spans a line subbundle. Changing h or frames changes v by the appropriate unit and frame transformation; cancellation shows that these lines glue. Their reduction is the saturation of K_C in E_C, namely A_1.

For uniqueness, at the step X_m⊂X_{m+1} the differences of graph lifts lie in

    H⁰(C,Hom(A_1,Q_1)⊗O_C(-4m))=H⁰(O_C(-d-4m))=0.

Uniqueness on C starts the induction. This argument works also for d=4,6.

### Local length equivalence

At p∈R let S=O_W,p, I=I_Z,p, q a local equation of C, and r_p=length S/(I,q). Choose f∈I whose reduction generates I O_C,p, of order r_p in a parameter t. The reduction is nonzero, so f is regular modulo q^n for every n. Moreover

    length S/(f,q^n)=n r_p.

For example, in S/(f), multiplication by q is injective because (f,q) is a parameter sequence in the regular surface local ring; the q-adic layers through order n consequently all have length r_p. Since (f,q^n)⊂(I,q^n), local intersection length is at most n r_p, with equality exactly when these ideals are equal.

Conversely if I O_X_n,p=(h), write f=u h. Reduction modulo q shows u is a unit, since f and h both generate the same nonzero ideal on C. Thus f itself generates the image ideal, giving equality of lengths. Summing over p proves λ_n≤nδ, and global equality holds exactly when every local equality holds. Away from R the image ideal is the unit ideal. None of this local length argument requires Z to be reduced; indeed it applies to arbitrary finite-colength ideals on the smooth surface. The lci hypothesis supplies the actual rank-two regular-section presentation.

## 2. The Jordan partition and the exact stopping order

For each point p of Z supported on C, put B_p=O_Z,p. A local defining equation q_p acts nilpotently on this finite-dimensional complex vector space. Let its Jordan-block lengths be ℓ_p,1,…,ℓ_p,r_p. The number of blocks is r_p because dim(B_p/q_p B_p)=r_p. Collect these lengths as ℓ_1,…,ℓ_δ. Their multiset is unchanged by replacing q_p by a unit multiple: the ideals generated by every power, and therefore all kernel/cokernel dimensions determining the Jordan partition, are unchanged.

Let N_C=Σ_p length B_p≤N_a. Contributions supported away from C vanish from all λ_n. Then

    λ_n=Σ_{i=1}^δ min(n,ℓ_i),    N_C=Σ_i ℓ_i,
    λ_n−λ_(n−1)=#{i:ℓ_i≥n},
    nδ−λ_n=Σ_i max(n−ℓ_i,0).

These follow from the standard basis of each nilpotent Jordan block, or directly from its module C[q]/(q^ℓ).

If δ>0, set h=min_i ℓ_i. The maximal line lifts exactly for 1≤n≤h, and fails first on X_(h+1). It can never reappear at a later order, both by restriction and by the length formula. If δ=0, the section is primitive on every X_n and its line K|X_n lifts for all n. Thus a compatible maximal-line lift on the entire formal completion exists exactly when Z has no support on C. This is a statement about the formal maximal line, not an algebraization or splitting theorem.

## 3. The actual higher obstruction and its exact support

Suppose A_m exists, equivalently λ_m=mδ. Its next obstruction is

    ob_m∈H¹(C,L_m),    L_m=Hom(A_1,Q_1)⊗O_C(-4m)
                               =O_C(-d-4m).

This is the usual graph-lifting class. The actual section on X_(m+1) yields

    σ ob_m=0 in H¹(C,L_m(R)),

where σ:K_C→A_1 has divisor R. To see this without choosing a retraction, lift local frames adapted to A_m. The section has coordinates (f,q^m b) modulo q^(m+1), with f mod q a unit times t^r_p. On overlaps its lower coordinates give a coboundary for σ times the graph-lifting cocycle. Diagonal and upper transition terms multiply an already order-m lower component only through their reduced transitions, giving precisely the indicated line-bundle weights.

Since deg L_m(R)=−d−4m+δ<0, both H⁰(L_m) and H⁰(L_m(R)) vanish. Therefore the principal-parts boundary

    H⁰(L_m(R)|R) → H¹(L_m)

is injective, and ob_m has unique principal parts in this δ-dimensional subspace. Locally the principal part is, up to the uniform cocycle sign and local units,

    b_0/t^r_p,    b_0=b mod q.

Let s_p=max(0,r_p−ord_t b_0), with s_p=0 when b_0=0. Its exact pole divisor is T_m=Σs_p p≤R. In S/(f,q^(m+1)), whose length is (m+1)r_p, the q^m layer is S/(f,q) of length r_p. Imposing q^m b kills a submodule of that layer of length s_p. The actual section coordinates generate I modulo q^(m+1), hence

    s_p=(m+1)r_p−length S/(I,q^(m+1)),
    τ_m:=deg T_m=(m+1)δ−λ_(m+1).

Because λ_m=mδ, all ℓ_i≥m, and consequently

    τ_m=#{i:ℓ_i=m},    0≤τ_m≤δ.

Thus ob_m is nonzero precisely when at least one Jordan block ends at m. At its first nonzero order it has exact nonzero highest principal-part coefficient at every point of T_m and lies in the τ_m-dimensional principal-parts subspace supported on T_m. Its ambient cohomology group has dimension d+4m−1. The claimed dimension τ_m refers to this containing subspace, not the dimension of a single class or a proved family of globally realized obstructions.

The integer τ_m, support divisor, and this criterion are intrinsic despite the chosen frames. The full Jordan partition gives later intersection lengths, but ob_j for j>h is not defined in the successive embedded-line lifting problem: there is no A_j to extend. One must not call the later length deficits later obstruction classes.

At m=1 this specializes to τ_1=2δ−λ_2 and the established ribbon principal-parts description. No higher Cartan or ambient-detector rank is inferred here.

## 4. Finite bounds with the actual total lengths

For δ>0, δh≤N_C≤N_a, so

    h≤floor(N_a/δ),
    first failed thickening h+1≤floor(N_a/δ)+1.

Equivalently, a successful lift to X_n requires nδ≤N_a. This is necessary only; it does not replace the exact Jordan criterion. With δ≤8−2a, the bounds on h for positive δ are:

- a=0, N_a=16, δ=1,…,8: h≤16,8,5,4,3,2,2,2 respectively.
- a=1, N_a=19, δ=1,…,6: h≤19,9,6,4,3,3 respectively.

In particular any δ>0 fails by X_17 in the a=0 branch or X_20 in the a=1 branch. Conversely, a lift on X_17 (a=0) or X_20 (a=1) forces δ=0 and therefore maximal-line lifts at every order. A uniform version is: a lift to X_(N_a+1) forces all-order maximal-line lifting.

A reduced point of Z on C supplies a size-one block and forces h=1. Nonreduced schemes can also have h=1, so absence of reduced points is not enough to ensure a double-conic lift. For a symbolic local test, I=(t^r,q^h) has r Jordan blocks of length h and realizes the algebraic equality λ_n=r min(n,h); this does not assert global realization.

## 5. Full splitting remains separate

If A_n exists, put Q_n=E|X_n/A_n and M_n=Hom(Q_n,A_n). Then E|X_n splits exactly when its extension class ξ_n∈H¹(X_n,M_n) vanishes. Its reduced degree is d. The new layer at X_m⊂X_(m+1) is O_C(d−4m), with h¹=max(4m−d−1,0). If ξ_m=0, the new class belongs to

    H¹(O_C(d−4m))/image[H⁰(M_m)→H¹(O_C(d−4m))].

Thus a maximal-line lift through X_n guarantees full splitting when d≥4n−4 (d is even here), or n≤floor(d/4)+1. In the present variables this last guaranteed level is

    n≤a+2+floor(δ/2).

Beyond it, the zero-scheme criterion still exactly decides line lifting, but does not by itself decide ξ_n. The separate higher-conic proof supplies universal local countermodels showing that beyond this range line lifting need not imply splitting. Those models are not evaluations or existence claims for this actual global E. Likewise, δ=0 gives the actual line K at every order, but this argument alone gives no all-order direct-sum decomposition.

### A consequence combining the two bounds

For δ≥4 in either actual branch, floor(N_a/δ)≤a+2+floor(δ/2). Hence every neighborhood on which the maximal line survives lies inside the guaranteed splitting range. For these δ values the full splitting tower is therefore also exact:

    E|X_n splits ⇔ A_1 lifts to X_n ⇔ n≤h.

Indeed n≤h is in the positive-cohomology vanishing range; n>h cannot split because any decomposition would contain a line lifting the unique maximal summand on C. For δ=1,2,3, the same conclusion holds whenever the actual h is at most a+2+floor(δ/2), but the total-length bound alone does not force it. Possible additional positive extension data can matter only at orders above this threshold and at most h. This is a conditional structural statement and does not assert occurrence of any particular δ or h.
