# Result

For the actual saturated section O(a)→E with finite zero scheme Z, let δ=length(Z∩C), λ_n=length(Z∩nC), and let ℓ_1,…,ℓ_δ be the nilpotent Jordan-block lengths of a conic equation on the local Artin algebras of Z supported on C.

- The unique maximal line of E_C lifts to E|nC iff I_Z O_nC is an invertible image ideal iff λ_n=nδ.
- λ_n=Σ min(n,ℓ_i). If δ>0, the exact last surviving level is h=min ℓ_i; if δ=0 it survives at every level.
- Whenever the line exists at level m, its next obstruction has unique principal parts on T_m≤Z∩C, with deg T_m=(m+1)δ−λ_(m+1)=#{i:ℓ_i=m}. This gives an exact nonzero test, not an ambient detector evaluation.
- h≤floor(16/δ) for a=0 and h≤floor(19/δ) for a=1. Thus a line surviving to 17C or 20C, respectively, forces δ=0 and all-order survival.
- Full splitting follows from line lifting through n≤a+2+floor(δ/2). For δ≥4, the length bound places every surviving level in this range, so the full splitting tower is also exactly n≤h. Smaller δ can require separate positive extension data.
