# Mathematical history and supersession

These manuscripts are catch-up publication of existing work, not new research or a claim of priority. The original mathematical exclusions, warnings, stronger corollaries and audits are retained.

- H3(B(-9))=0 is a refuted shortcut; it is not an input.
- The +4 conductor comparison bound of five does not bound the -4 obstruction comparison.
- The original k>=13 and degree-84 detector are conservative, not a current best frontier. The separately proved sharper-HM input gives common regular unweighted source order seven and degree-30 detector support, under the same transport premises.
- Coarse triple/double pole bounds remain valid sufficient bounds. The simple-pole corollary is stronger only under its separately stated sharper cohomology hypotheses.
- Polynomial conductor syzygies do not prove lowest-degree module generation, endpoint vanishing or an actual cofactor rank. The endpoint statement is an imported theorem, not a new inference here.
- Finite detector injectivity is not restriction surjectivity. A finite 8C section-extension theorem is not a splitting theorem.
- A two-stage socle detector cannot be replaced by its first differential; the series retains the abstract counterexample.

Original audits are preserved as mathematical reviews with their original assumptions. Exact normalized-publication hashes are regenerated after edits. Only mathematical materials are included.
