# Exact fourth-power sign pushforward

## Result

Work in characteristic zero with Z=(P1)^4, Y=Sym^4(P1)=P4, q:Z→Y, and A=q*O_Y(1), with natural ungraded permutation linearizations. Let D be the reduced union of the four triple diagonals and I=I_D. Let v be the Vandermonde section of A³ carrying S4 sign. Let N⊂Y be the reduced triple-root surface, the image of (l,m)↦l³m.

Then, for ordinary ideal powers, globally including all quadruple collisions,

    (I⁴:v)=I,
    (q_*I⁴)_sign = I_N⊗O_Y(−3),
    I_N=(t₂,t₃),

where t₂=Tr_F(σ²) and t₃=Tr_F(σ³) are the degree-two and degree-three trace invariants in the established weight normalization. The second equality identifies the inclusion into (q_*O_Z)_sign=O_Y(−3); it is an equality of the corresponding subsheaves under the Vandermonde identification, not an unnormalized equality of chosen scalar generators.

In particular for E=q*B0⊗A^−2,

    (q_*(I⁴ EA))_sign = B0(−4)⊗I_N.

Its inclusion in the unrestricted sign coefficient B0(−4) is the usual nullcone ideal inclusion. This is the first nonautomatic ideal-power condition at the sheaf level. It does not assert any restriction, vanishing, or nonvanishing for the induced global H² image or for the actual source.

## 1. Inputs and line conventions

The previously audited coefficient geometry supplies these inputs:

1. I is the ideal of the reduced union D and is locally generated by a regular sequence of two weight differences, sections of A.
2. There are three pairing values σ₁,σ₂,σ₃ with sum zero. Their differences span the two-dimensional weight space, and

       (σ₁−σ₂)(σ₁−σ₃)(σ₂−σ₃)=c v,

   for a nonzero scalar c (±27 in the cited convention).
3. Every regular sign section is divisible by v, with regular invariant quotient. Thus the sign summand of q_*O_Z is O_Y(−3), including over the ramification locus.
4. With full rank-six trace, t₂=2(σ₁²+σ₂²+σ₃²) and t₃=2(σ₁³+σ₂³+σ₃³). These are global sections of O_Y(2) and O_Y(3). The cubic satisfied by all three pairing values is

       z³−(t₂/4)z−t₃/6=0.

These inputs are proved in the two local reference files listed below. All bare line-bundle formulas fix the harmless binary determinant and weight normalization lines, as in those proofs. They assert S4-equivariant geometry and do not assert an action of GL2 on HM or B0. Keeping determinant lines merely restores their one-dimensional factors and changes none of the ideal identities.

## 2. A local ordinary-power colon lemma

Let R be a local ring, J=(x,y) generated by a regular sequence, and w=u xy(y−x), where u is a unit. Then for every k≥3,

    (J^k:w)=J^(k−3).

For k≤3 the colon is R.

Proof. A regular-sequence ideal has associated graded algebra

    gr_J R ≅ (R/J)[X,Y].

The initial form of w has degree three and equals a unit times XY(Y−X). Multiplication by this polynomial is injective over every coefficient ring R/J: multiplication by X and Y is injective, while Y−X is monic in Y and therefore also a nonzerodivisor. No domain or reducedness assertion about R/J is needed.

The inclusion J^(k−3)⊂(J^k:w) is immediate. Conversely, if f∉J^(k−3), there is r with 0≤r<k−3 and f∈J^r but f∉J^(r+1). Its nonzero initial form has degree r. Multiplying by the initial cubic of w gives a nonzero class in J^(r+3)/J^(r+4). Thus wf∉J^(r+4), in particular wf∉J^k. This proves the reverse inclusion. For k=3 it is immediate from w∈J³; the lower powers follow as well. No passage to symbolic powers, saturation, localization away from intersections, or completion is used.

## 3. Applying the lemma at every point of Z

On a local trivialization of A put x=σ₁−σ₂ and y=σ₁−σ₃. Then σ₂−σ₃=y−x. The audited global weight identity gives v as a unit times xy(y−x). At every point of D the two differences are a regular sequence generating I. Section 2 therefore gives

    (I⁴:v)=I.

Away from D the ideal I is the unit ideal, so the same equality holds trivially. The identity is independent of the local frame of A, whose change only multiplies v and the generators by units. It therefore glues globally. In particular, it applies at the quadruple diagonal where the four components of D meet: smoothness of an individual triple diagonal at a generic point is not being substituted for the complete-intersection calculation there.

The same argument gives (I^k:v)=I^(k−3) for every k≥3, with the line twist understood locally. The asserted fourth-power result is its first positive colon exponent.

## 4. Exact sign pushforward

On a quotient-adapted open, write a regular sign function as v f. Its quotient f is a regular invariant section of A^−3; this is the already proved global alternant-divisibility statement. By Section 3, the condition vf∈I⁴ is exactly f∈I A^−3.

Consequently multiplication by v identifies the fourth-power sign summand with

    (q_*I)^S4 ⊗ O_Y(−3).

The invariant ideal (q_*I)^S4 is exactly I_N. Indeed, invariant functions in q_*O_Z are O_Y. Such a function lies in q_*I if and only if its pullback vanishes on the reduced D. This is equivalent to vanishing on its reduced image N. Equivalently, exactness of the characteristic-zero invariant projector identifies the kernel of O_Y→(q_*O_D)^S4 with the scheme-theoretic image ideal; the image is reduced because it embeds in the reduced ring of functions on D. Both descriptions work on every affine open of Y. This is invariant contraction, not extension of ideals: it does not claim q*I_N=I. In fact the pulled-back generators t₂,t₃ have weight orders two and three, whereas I is generated in weight order one.

This proves

    (q_*I⁴)_sign = I_N⊗O_Y(−3).

Projection formula, with EA=q*B0(−1), proves the coefficient version B0(−4)⊗I_N. The inclusion map into B0(−4) is precisely the ideal inclusion, not merely an abstract isomorphism of source bundles.

## 5. The nullcone ideal is the degree-(2,3) complete intersection

Define C⊂Y by the two sections t₂,t₃. We prove C=N as schemes.

### Set-theoretic support

Every geometric quartic admits an ordering of its roots, so q is surjective on geometric points. If t₂=t₃=0, the cubic from Section 1 becomes z³. Each σ_i is thus zero. In particular all weight differences vanish, which is precisely the condition of lying in D. Conversely, if all differences vanish, trace zero forces all three values to vanish, hence t₂=t₃=0. Therefore

    |C|=N.

This reasoning uses regular trace identities at all points, rather than distinct-root diagonalization of a possibly nonreduced fiber algebra. The σ_i are actual pullback sections on Z; their cubic identity extends globally as already proved.

### Dimension, degree, and reduced structure

The morphism

    φ:P1×P1→P4,   (l,m)↦l³m

has image N. It is generically one-to-one: a quartic with a triple root and a distinct simple root uniquely determines the ordered pair (l,m). Thus N is irreducible of dimension two. Its hyperplane pullback is O(3,1), so

    deg N = (3h₁+h₂)² = 6,

since h₁²=h₂²=0 and ∫h₁h₂=1.

As |C|=N has codimension two in P4, the two defining equations form a regular sequence in the homogeneous polynomial ring (and locally wherever their ideal is proper). Hence C is a codimension-two complete intersection of degrees two and three, with degree six, and is Cohen–Macaulay and unmixed. Its only underlying irreducible component is N. The degree formula therefore forces its generic multiplicity along N to be one. An unmixed Cohen–Macaulay scheme which is generically reduced has no nilpotents: any nonzero nilradical would have an associated prime, and with no embedded associated primes it would survive at a minimal prime, contrary to generic reducedness. Thus C is reduced.

It follows C=N as schemes, including the quadruple-root curve. In particular

    I_N=(t₂,t₃)

as an ideal sheaf. This proof establishes the complete-intersection structure directly; it does not assume the classical binary-quartic invariant-ring presentation or rely on a generic radical calculation.

## 6. Explicit resolution and regular criterion

The complete-intersection Koszul resolution is

    0 → O_Y(−5) → O_Y(−2)⊕O_Y(−3) → I_N → 0,

where the last map is (a,b)↦t₂a+t₃b and the first is c↦(−t₃c,t₂c). Therefore

    0 → B0(−9) → B0(−6)⊕B0(−7)
      → (q_*(I⁴ EA))_sign → 0.

There is also the restriction sequence

    0 → (q_*(I⁴ EA))_sign → B0(−4) → B0(−4)|_N → 0.

Thus on an affine quotient-adapted chart a sign cochain t=v f belongs to I⁴ EA if and only if its descended coefficient f vanishes on N, equivalently if and only if

    f=t₂a+t₃b

for regular local coefficient sections with the indicated twists. This is a concrete additional coefficient criterion beyond the universally automatic I³ membership. It neither guarantees closed choices a,b nor makes a claim that the cohomology map from the left sheaf has a proper image. Those are separate global questions.

## 7. The fifth power has the same sign pushforward

There is a further exact equality

    (q_*I⁵)_sign=(q_*I⁴)_sign=I_N⊗O_Y(−3),

and likewise after tensoring by EA. This does not assert I⁵=I⁴ on Z.

Indeed the complete-intersection generators identify the conormal bundle

    I/I² ≅ U⊗A^−1|_D,

where U is the two-dimensional Specht (2,2) representation (using its self-duality if needed for the generator convention). On any triple-diagonal component, the S3 permuting the three equal roots fixes that component pointwise. Its action on A is trivial, and U restricted to S3 is the standard two-dimensional representation with no invariant vectors. One can see this without a character table: the homomorphism S4→S3 permutes the three pairings, and a triple-root stabilizer maps isomorphically to this S3; U is its trace-zero permutation representation.

Every invariant conormal section therefore restricts to zero on each component. Since D is reduced and the conormal is locally free on D, that section is zero globally, including at the component intersections. Hence

    (q_*(I/I²))^S4=0.

Exact finite pushforward and invariant projection now give (q_*I²)^S4=(q_*I)^S4=I_N. Applying the already proved general colon identity at k=5 gives the displayed fifth-power equality. In particular, fourth and fifth ordinary-power membership impose the same condition on regular sign sections, even though fourth-power membership first fails to be universal.
