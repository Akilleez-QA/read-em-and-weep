# Exact sixth-power sign pushforward

## Result

Over C, retain the audited notation Z=(P1)^4, Y=Sym^4(P1)=P4, q:Z→Y, A=q*O_Y(1), I=I_D for the reduced union of triple diagonals, and the Vandermonde sign section v of A³. Let N be the reduced triple-root surface. The invariant sections t₂,t₃ have degrees 2,3 and I_N=(t₂,t₃).

For ordinary powers, globally including the quadruple-root curve,

    J₃ := (q_*I³)^S4 = (t₃,t₂²),
    (q_*I⁶)_sign = (t₃,t₂²)⊗O_Y(-3).

Here (t₃,t₂²) means the image of O_Y(-3)⊕O_Y(-4) in O_Y. Under the established Vandermonde identification the latter formula is equality of subsheaves of (q_*O_Z)_sign=O_Y(-3).

For EA=q*B0(-1) it follows that

    (q_*(I⁶ EA))_sign = B0(-4)⊗(t₃,t₂²).

This is strictly smaller as a coefficient ideal than the common fourth/fifth-power ideal (t₂,t₃). It defines the complete-intersection double structure M=V(t₃,t₂²) on N, with degrees (3,4), degree 12, and the exact layer

    0 → I_M → I_N → O_N(-2) → 0.

No strictness, rank, or vanishing of the induced H² image is inferred from this strict sheaf inclusion. No physical source or selected rank is evaluated.

## 1. Imported audited inputs

The fourth-power proof and its independent audit establish:

- I is locally generated by two weight differences forming a regular sequence, even at quadruple collisions.
- Writing x=σ₁-σ₂, y=σ₁-σ₃ in a descended local frame of A, with σ₁+σ₂+σ₃=0, gives I=(x,y).
- gr_I R=(R/I)[X,Y] for every local ring R on Z at D.
- t₂=2∑σᵢ², t₃=2∑σᵢ³ in the fixed trace normalization; both descend to Y.
- I_N=(t₂,t₃) is the reduced codimension-two complete-intersection ideal.
- (q_*I)^S4=I_N, and (I^k:v)=I^(k-3) for k≥3, with local trivializations understood.
- Every regular sign section is v times a regular invariant section of A^(-3).

These are the only geometric inputs needed for the ideal calculation. We use the same natural ungraded permutation descent linearizations and fixed harmless one-dimensional binary determinant factors. We assert no additional GL2 action on B0.

## 2. The quadratic initial form has no annihilator

The linear relations give

    σ₁=(x+y)/3,  σ₂=(y-2x)/3,  σ₃=(x-2y)/3,
    t₂=(4/3)(x²-xy+y²).

Consequently t₂∈I² and its initial class in I²/I³ is

    Q(X,Y)=(4/3)(X²-XY+Y²).

Multiplication by Q is injective in (R/I)[X,Y], over any coefficient ring R/I: regarding Q as a polynomial in X over (R/I)[Y], its leading coefficient 4/3 is a unit. Comparing the top X-degree of any nonzero polynomial proves injectivity. No integral-domain assertion for R/I is used.

In particular,

    (I³:t₂)=I

locally along D. Indeed t₂I⊂I³. Conversely, if t₂g∈I³, its degree-two initial class is Q times the degree-zero class g mod I; injectivity implies g mod I=0. Away from D the same formula is the trivial identity for the unit ideal. Local changes of descended frames multiply the quantities by units and do not affect the conclusion.

Also t₃∈I³ because each σᵢ is a linear combination of x,y. Thus the arguments apply without excluding the quadruple diagonal, where x,y vanish quadratically in root-displacement coordinates but remain degree one in the I-adic associated graded ring.

## 3. Exact contraction of the third power

Work locally on Y with O_Y(1) trivialized. Let f be a regular invariant descended function whose pullback lies in I³. Since I³⊂I,

    f∈I_N=(t₂,t₃).

After shrinking locally if necessary, choose regular descended a,b with

    f=t₂a+t₃b.

The pullback of t₃b lies in I³, so the pullback of t₂a lies in I³. Section 2 forces q*a∈I, and invariant contraction then forces

    a∈I_N=(t₂,t₃).

Write a=t₂c+t₃d. Therefore

    f=t₂²c+t₃(b+t₂d) ∈ (t₃,t₂²).

Conversely, q*t₃∈I³ and q*(t₂²)∈I⁴⊂I³, so each regular local element of (t₃,t₂²) pulls back into I³. This proves equality on local opens, hence globally as ideal sheaves:

    (q_*I³)^S4=(t₃,t₂²).

The argument uses contraction, not extension: it never identifies q*I_N with I. Twists in the local expressions glue through the images of the globally defined sections t₃ and t₂².

## 4. Sixth sign power and coefficients

Write a regular sign section as vh with h invariant and carrying the A^(-3) twist. The audited colon identity gives

    vh∈I⁶  ⇔  h∈I³ A^(-3).

Finite pushforward, the exact characteristic-zero invariant projector, and projection formula therefore identify the actual inclusion as

    (q_*I⁶)_sign = J₃⊗O_Y(-3) ⊂ O_Y(-3).

Tensoring with B0(-1) gives the coefficient statement

    (q_*(I⁶ EA))_sign=B0(-4)I_M ⊂ B0(-4).

Locally a regular sign cochain t=vf has sixth-power membership exactly when its descended coefficient satisfies

    f=t₃a+t₂²b

with regular coefficients of the appropriate twists. This is a cochain membership criterion; it does not assert that coefficient choices a,b for a closed cochain are individually closed.

## 5. Global double structure and its new layer

Because (t₂,t₃) is a codimension-two complete intersection in the smooth Y, the reordered pair (t₃,t₂) is a regular sequence locally where its ideal is proper. Squaring the second member preserves regularity, so (t₃,t₂²) is a regular sequence. Thus M is a codimension-two complete intersection of degrees 3,4, is Cohen–Macaulay and Gorenstein, and has degree 12. Its radical is I_N, so its reduced support is exactly N, including along the quadruple-root curve. Adjunction gives ω_M=O_M(2).

The map O_Y(-2)→I_N/I_M given by t₂ is surjective. Its kernel is I_N(-2). To see the annihilator locally, if a t₂∈(t₃,t₂²), reduce modulo t₃. Since t₂ is a nonzerodivisor modulo t₃, the relation implies a∈(t₂,t₃). The converse is immediate. Hence

    I_N/I_M ≅ O_N(-2).

In particular this quotient is nonzero at every point of N, so I_M is strictly smaller there than I_N. There is also an exact sequence

    0 → O_N(-2) → O_M → O_N → 0,

whose left image is square-zero, generated locally by t₂. This describes a double structure along the entire N, without assuming N is smooth or normal at quadruple roots. It does not assert a splitting of this sequence as rings or O_Y-modules.

The Koszul resolution is

    0 → O_Y(-7) → O_Y(-3)⊕O_Y(-4) → I_M → 0,

with maps (-t₂²,t₃) and (t₃,t₂²). Thus the sixth coefficient has the resolution

    0 → B0(-11) → B0(-7)⊕B0(-8) → B0(-4)I_M → 0.

Comparing it with the fourth/fifth coefficient gives

    0 → B0(-4)I_M → B0(-4)I_N → B0(-6)|_N → 0.

These are exact sheaf sequences. The connecting maps must still be accounted for before drawing a cohomological conclusion.

## 6. Optional exact cohomological criterion, with no rank evaluated

For B0=End₀(HM), use its trace self-duality B0≅B0∨. The restriction sequence for M implies

    im[H²(B0(-4)I_M)→H²(B0(-4))]
      = ker[H²(B0(-4))→H²(M,B0(-4)|M)].

Since M is a projective Gorenstein surface with ω_M=O_M(2), duality identifies the transpose of this restriction map with a two-step Koszul boundary

    δ_M:H⁰(M,B0(6)|M) → H²(Y,B0(-1)).

Specifically use the two sequences

    0 → B0(-1) → B0(3)⊕B0(2) → B0(6)I_M → 0,
    0 → B0(6)I_M → B0(6) → B0(6)|M → 0.

The first has maps (-t₂²,t₃) and (t₃,t₂²). Compatibility of Serre duality with the Koszul fundamental class gives

    im[H²(B0(-4)I_M)→H²(B0(-4))] = (im δ_M)^⊥.

The prior audited HM row gives dim H²(B0(-4))=10, hence the image dimension is 10−rank δ_M. This is a formal criterion only; no rank, positivity vanishing, or physical-source membership is asserted. Inclusion of I_M in I_N implies containment of the corresponding ambient H² images, but need not make it strict.
