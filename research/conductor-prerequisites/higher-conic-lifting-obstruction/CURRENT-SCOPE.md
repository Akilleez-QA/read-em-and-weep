# higher-conic-lifting-obstruction

This theorem distinguishes negative-root line-lifting obstructions, diagonal Picard choices, and positive extension classes modulo im δ_m. First-order vanishing does not imply third-order splitting or global algebraicity. The actual HM higher obstruction is not evaluated. Read after the ribbon classification; global-conic §5 uses this comparison but its cohomology proof does not.
