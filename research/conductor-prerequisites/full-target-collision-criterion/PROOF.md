# A common sufficient collision order for both retained targets

## Result and its conditional meaning

Over C, retain the audited ordered-root geometry and coefficient identifications listed below. The two weighted sign coefficient groups satisfy

    H²(Y,(q_*(I^k EA))_sign)=0       for k≥13,
    H²(Y,(q_*(I^k EA²))_sign)=0      for k≥14.

If a paired even class [C] admits a regular closed upstairs representative R of its transported class with

    R ∈ C²(U,I¹² E),   δR=0,   [R]=j₀([C]),

on a quotient-adapted affine cover (or an equivalent cohomology-computing regular cover), then both retained coordinates vanish:

    Tr_m^ω(σ[C])=0,    Tr_m^ω(τ[C])=0,
    τ=σ²−m*t₂/6.

It is enough, more weakly, to impose this membership and class comparison on the H-sign projection R_H=e_H S of a raw source S; see Section 4. Consequently the retained thirty-dimensional class is zero. This is an existential, sufficient representative-membership criterion. It establishes no membership of the actual source, no actual regular lift to this ideal-power complex, no algebraicity of a physical source, and no evaluated obstruction. Ordinary collision-ideal order is not deformation order, and 12 is not claimed minimal.

## 1. Geometry, linearizations, and imported inputs

Let Z=(P¹)^4, Y=Sym⁴(P¹)=P⁴, and q:Z→Y be the finite quotient by S4. Let p:Z→X=P²×P² pair the roots 12|34, and m:X→Y multiply the quadratic factors. Put A=q*O_Y(1)=p*O_X(1,1), with its natural ungraded descent permutation linearization. Let B0=End₀(F) for normalized Horrocks–Mumford F, and

    E=q*B0⊗A^(-2).

All uses of E below require this actual globally linearized coefficient sheaf, or a specified regular chain transport into its cohomology. A raw expression on an affine root space is not by itself such a transport. Harmless one-dimensional binary determinant factors are fixed as in the input proofs; restoring them changes no vanishing. No GL₂ action on B0 is assumed.

Let D be the reduced union of all four triple diagonals, I=I_D its ordinary ideal sheaf, and N⊂Y its reduced image. The union D and every I^k are S4-stable. The input proofs establish, including at quadruple collisions:

- I is locally generated by the regular sequence x=σ₁−σ₂, y=σ₁−σ₃.
- σ₁+σ₂+σ₃=0 and σ=σ₁ for the chosen pairing.
- t₂=2∑σᵢ² and t₃=2∑σᵢ³ descend with degrees 2 and 3; I_N=(t₂,t₃).
- For n≥0, K_n=(t₂^a t₃^b: 2a+3b≥n), with K_0=O_Y.
- For k≥3, (q_*I^k)_sign=O_Y(-3)K_(k−3) as actual subsheaves under Vandermonde division.
- B0 is trace self-dual and 11-regular, hence H¹(B0(t))=0 for t≥10 and H²(B0(t))=0 for t≥9.

The ideal statements are ordinary sheaf ideal statements, not symbolic powers, radical membership alone, or orders measured only on generic collision branches.

## 2. Uniform coefficient computation and effective bounds

Projection formula gives, for r=0,1,2 and k≥3,

    (q_*(I^k EA^r))_sign = B0(r−5)K_(k−3).

For n=k−3≥1 the audited adjacent-monomial resolution is

    0 → ⊕ O_Y(-e_i) → ⊕ O_Y(-d_i) → K_n → 0,

where d_i=2a_i+3b_i≥n and

    e_i=2a_i+3b_(i+1)
       =d_i+3(b_(i+1)−b_i)≥n+3.

Tensor by the locally free B0(r−5). In its long exact sequence, H² of the coefficient is controlled by H² of the generator term and H³ of the syzygy term. Serre duality on P⁴ and B0∨=B0 give

    H²(B0(r−5−d_i))∨ = H²(B0(d_i−r)),
    H³(B0(r−5−e_i))∨ = H¹(B0(e_i−r)).

If n≥r+9 then d_i−r≥9 and e_i−r≥12≥10. Both groups vanish. Thus

    H²(Y,(q_*(I^k EA^r))_sign)=0   whenever k≥r+12,

for r=0,1,2. In particular the first and second weights require respectively k≥13 and k≥14. The r=0 case, k≥12, is useful for the constant component below.

Finite pushforward and the exact characteristic-zero sign idempotent identify these groups with sign cohomology upstairs. A closed sign cochain with the displayed ideal membership is therefore a boundary in that ideal-power coefficient complex on an affine Čech cover, not merely zero after forgetting the ideal. This existence conclusion constructs no particular primitive.

## 3. The weights supply the two extra ideal orders

Solving the weight relations gives

    σ₁=(x+y)/3, σ₂=(y−2x)/3, σ₃=(x−2y)/3.

Therefore σᵢ∈IA globally. In particular σ∈IA and q*t₂∈I²A². Hence

    p*τ = σ²−q*t₂/6 ∈ I²A².

We suppress p* on τ henceforth. These assertions hold at the quadruple diagonal too. They do not require division by any weight or branch equation.

If R is a closed regular I¹²E-valued 2-cochain, multiplication by these global regular sections commutes with δ. Since I is S4-stable, averaging by e_sign=(1/24)∑sign(g)g preserves each ideal-power sheaf. Consequently

    e_sign(σR) ∈ Z²(U,I¹³EA),
    e_sign(τR) ∈ Z²(U,I¹⁴EA²).

Section 2 makes both classes zero. Neither σ nor τ is assumed S4-invariant; it is precisely the stability of the full union ideal that makes this reasoning valid. Using only the ideal of the selected single triple diagonal would not justify the projector step.

## 4. Exact transport premises for interpreting the result

Let H0=〈(12),(34)〉 be the order-four within-pair subgroup, and let H=〈(12),(34),(13)(24)〉 be the order-eight stabilizer of the unordered pairing 12|34. In the geometric End convention, the block-even paired class has H-character sign|H: the two inner swaps are negative and the block swap is positive. The chosen σ and τ are H-invariant sections. The pertinent class is in this paired even sector, not an arbitrary cohomology class with no specified relationship to X.

The following regular transport data are required, as imported in WEIGHTED-SELECTION.md:

1. An injective class inclusion j₀ from the paired finite-duality coefficient cohomology into H²(Z,E), with the above H-character convention and the correct ramification/canonical-form transports.
2. Its twists j_r into H²(Z,EA^r), r=1,2, obtained by O_Y(r); compatibility with multiplication by the pulled-back sections σ and τ.
3. The sign/constant-projector comparison, for r=0,1,2, including injectivity on the constant inclusion. Explicitly, for a paired class D of twist r,

       e_sign j_r(D) = j_r(i_const,r(Tr_m^ω D)),

   where i_const,r(η) is the finite-duality functional f↦η Tr_F(f)/6. Its ordinary duality trace is η, so this is a split constant inclusion. The divisor six belongs to this inclusion, not to the weighted trace.
4. The regular source comparison [R]=j₀([C]) in the full unweighted cohomology. Cohomological equality suffices because multiplying any regular comparison boundary by either weight and then projecting still gives a regular boundary.

With these premises, the two classes in Section 3 are respectively

    j₁(i_const,1(Tr_m^ω(σ[C]))),
    j₂(i_const,2(Tr_m^ω(τ[C]))).

Their vanishing and the stipulated injective transport imply vanishing of the two weighted traces. This is the extension of the first-weight transfer to the second weight: the same regular O_Y-linear comparison is twisted by O_Y(2) and applied after multiplying by τ. No étale-only averaging or inversion of a trace pairing is substituted for finite duality.

The required j_r maps are premises inherited from the class-transfer construction, not proved here from group characters alone. In particular, an injective map on H² at r=0 does not imply injectivity after twisting. One needs the regular split coefficient inclusions supplied by the transport construction, or must separately assume the required injectivity on the constant inclusions at r=1,2 as above. An H-character assertion alone cannot establish a regular class lift, injectivity, or compatibility with weighted duality. Likewise a representative only on a dense affine root chart, or a meromorphic representative, does not satisfy the global premise.

### A weaker, sufficient condition on the raw source

Let S be a regular closed unweighted raw cochain in E and put

    e_H=(1/8)∑_(h∈H) sign(h)h,     R_H=e_H S.

It suffices to require only

    R_H ∈ C²(U,I¹²E),     [R_H]=j₀([C]).

This formulation does not require S itself to belong to I¹²E or its other H-character pieces to transport to the paired class. Apply the theorem with R=R_H. It is therefore weaker as an assumption on a fixed raw cochain than requiring both S∈I¹²E and [S]=j₀([C]). No claim of strictness for the actual source is made.

Since σ and τ are H-invariant, multiplication by either commutes with e_H. The group-algebra identity e_sign e_H=e_sign then gives the exact cochain identities

    e_sign(σ R_H)=e_sign(σ S),
    e_sign(τ R_H)=e_sign(τ S),
    e_sign R_H=e_sign S.

Thus only the H-sign component of the raw source needs the ideal order. The class comparison [R_H]=j₀([C]) remains a separate required hypothesis; it cannot be inferred from the projector alone. If only the stronger comparison [S]=j₀([C]) is established, its H-character implies [e_H S]=j₀([C]), so S∈I¹²E is a simple sufficient corollary. Regularity and closure of S may be replaced by those of an independently supplied R_H, but no such source representative is constructed here.

A class originating on C⁴ requires the previously established regular all-four-odd finite-pushforward and coefficient identification before the projective E statement applies. A raw scalar-label permutation or a dimension count is not a substitute. Finite H or (2,2) averaging preserves I¹², but subtracting an arbitrary known sign primitive need not preserve that ideal membership.

An arbitrary R representing j₀([C]) need not have the H character at cochain level. If desired, the H-character projector makes it so, preserves I¹²E and closure, and preserves its cohomology class. This normalization is optional for the proof. No S4 action on the fixed paired deformation ambient is asserted.

In the natural canonical-form convention the equivalent statements use H-invariance and the full invariant projector; converting with the ordered top-form sign exchanges these with the End convention used throughout. These conventions must not be mixed.

## 5. Constant-zero premise and what exactly vanishes

The canonical coefficient decomposition identifies the retained part by the two coordinates

    (Tr_m^ω(σ[C]), Tr_m^ω(τ[C]))
      ∈ W*⊗H²(B0(-4)) ⊕ (W*)²⊗H²(B0(-3)).

Their joint vanishing is equivalent to vanishing of the retained class, irrespective of whether the constant component is zero. This equivalence uses the established trace-corrected canonical complement, not a choice of a noncanonical lift of the twenty-dimensional quotient.

For a general even class, the exact relation is

    Tr_m^ω(τ[C]) = Tr_m^ω(σ²[C]) − (t₂/6)Tr_m^ω([C]).

Thus the certified constant-zero premise permits replacement of τ by σ² in cohomology. It does not make a nonzero constant cochain vanish pointwise. Also, if one proves only the two retained coordinates zero, vanishing of the entire even class additionally requires the constant component to be zero.

Under the full source hypothesis of this theorem there is a further conditional implication: e_sign R is itself a closed I¹²E cochain, whose H² vanishes by the r=0 case in Section 2. The unweighted sign/constant comparison then gives Tr_m^ω([C])=0. Therefore that same global I¹² source premise, with the r=0,1,2 transports, would imply zero of the entire even class even without importing a separate constant-zero certificate. This does not assert that the premise holds for the actual source; in the original application the constant component is already certified zero independently.

This argument is universal sheaf algebra and finite-group-equivariant cohomology. No actual source membership or physical obstruction value is determined. It proves sufficient conditions, not necessity or optimality.
