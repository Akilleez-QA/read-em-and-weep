# Independent audit: actual-section constraints on the double conic

## Verdict

The statements in `PROOF.md` pass independent review. In particular, the actual plane section supplies information that is absent from a free ribbon-local deformation: it supports the maximal-line obstruction on the intersection with its zero scheme. The exact Cartan rank is

    rank C_e = min(7, τ),
    τ = 2 length(Z∩C) − length(Z∩2C).

This is a structural formula, not an evaluation of the actual intersection lengths or of an ambient map. The two lengths use the ideal I_Z, not its square. The local principal-ideal criterion and its converse in the presence of the actual section are correct.

The d=6 bounds in the original proof are valid but can be sharpened, without evaluating the diagonal class: the exact full rank in that case is τ+1. A complete independent proof is in `SUPPLEMENT.md`. Thus the a=0, δ=1 cases have full rank one when τ=0 and full rank two when τ=1. The latter includes a reduced point of Z on C.

Read directly: this directory's `PROOF.md`, `../square-detector-factorization/PROOF.md` and `SUPPLEMENT.md`, and `../ribbon-catalecticant-classification/PROOF.md` and `AUDIT.md`. No selected HM rank or ambient-source value is evaluated.

## 1. Restriction and the elementary exclusion

The quotient I_Z⊗O_W(−2−a) is torsion-free on the smooth surface. Multiplication by q² is therefore injective on it, so restriction of the actual inclusion K→E to D=2C remains injective. The coordinate ideal of the resulting section is the image ideal I_Z O_D. This is distinct from the possibly noninjective tensor-product map I_Z⊗O_D→O_D.

When E_D splits, its maximal reduced summand A_C has degree 2a+δ and its lower summand Q_C has degree −4−2a−δ. The two layers of Hom(K_D,Q) have degrees −4−4a−δ and −8−4a−δ, both negative. Thus there is no lower component of the actual K section. Its coordinate ideal is locally principal because it is the coefficient of a section of one line summand.

A reduced point of Z on C has local ideal (t,q) in a regular local surface ring. Its image in S/(q²) needs two generators. Therefore such a point excludes splitting on D. The prior ribbon classification identifies this with e≠0, which also implies ε≠0 in the small gaps: the Cartan component is injective as a function of e for every d≥4. The argument never uses the incorrect small-gap converse that a split bundle must have zero full boundary.

## 2. The local length criterion

Let r=length S/(I,q), and choose f∈I whose reduction generates the ideal (t^r) on C. Since f mod q is nonzero, q,f is a regular sequence, in either order. Consequently f is regular in S/(q²), and

    0 → S/(f,q) --q→ S/(f,q²) → S/(f,q) → 0

is exact. Hence length S/(f,q²)=2r. The natural surjection to S/(I,q²) proves ℓ≤2r, and equality is equivalent to equality of the ideals (f,q²) and (I,q²).

If I O_D is principal, say generated by h, then h mod q has order r. Writing f=uh in O_D shows that u mod q is a unit, hence u itself is a unit. Thus f already generates I O_D, proving the converse needed for

    I O_D principal ⇔ ℓ=2r.

No reducedness or transversality assumption is hidden in this test. In particular it distinguishes (t,q²), which passes, from (t²,q), which fails. These local examples do not purport to construct global bundles with a prescribed zero scheme.

## 3. Supported obstruction and its intrinsic pole divisor

Put L=O_C(−d−4), and write σ for the reduced section K_C→A_C, with divisor R. In frames lifting the reduced splitting, the lower component of the lifted section gives a Čech coboundary for σe. Thus σe=0 in H¹(L(R)). This lower-component calculation is unaffected by diagonal deformations of the reference or of K_D: those multiply a component already in the square-zero ideal and their additional products vanish.

Both L and L(R) have negative degree. The principal-parts boundary

    H⁰(L(R)|R) → H¹(L)

is therefore injective, and its image is precisely the kernel of multiplication by σ. This supplies a unique principal part for the actual e, rather than merely some representative supported on R.

Locally the lifted section is (f,qb), with f mod q=t^r up to a unit. Its lower graph coefficient is b_0/t^r in the conormal layer, where b_0=b mod q. Its pole order is

    m = r − min(r, ord_t b_0).

The q-layer of O_D/(f) is S/(f,q), of length r. The ideal generated by qb inside that layer has length m. Thus imposing the second coordinate gives

    length S/(I,q²) = 2r−m.

This proves m=2r−ℓ and, after summing, τ=2δ−ℓ. A change of a reference lift adds a regular graph coefficient; a unit change cannot alter a pole order. Independently, the intrinsic length formula proves that the divisor T=Σm_p p is well defined. It is the minimal support divisor of the unique principal part of e.

The injectivity above is essential to the global converse: e=0 forces the unique principal part to vanish, hence all m_p=0 and all coordinate ideals are principal. Conversely, local principality yields zero principal parts and e=0. A direct geometric verification divides the section by its local regular generator; the resulting primitive vectors glue to a line subbundle lifting A_C. The previous ribbon theorem then splits E_D.

Every reduced point of Z on C has r=ℓ=1 and therefore contributes exactly one to τ. The hypothesis concerns a reduced point of the zero scheme Z, not just a reduced support point of its intersection divisor.

## 4. Why the rank formula is exact

Under Serre duality, a principal part of exact pole order m becomes a functional on the length-m local algebra whose value on the socle t^(m−1) is nonzero. Its multiplication pairing is nondegenerate: if x has initial order k<m, then multiplying by t^(m−1−k) leaves a nonzero socle coefficient. This argument includes arbitrary lower-order coefficients and is not restricted to a sum of distinct point evaluations.

Summing the local pairings gives a perfect pairing between the degree-n and degree-six line-bundle fibers over T. Source restriction is surjective because

    n=d−4=4a+2δ ≥ τ−1.

The transpose of the catalecticant therefore has the same rank as the restriction of degree-six forms to T. Its kernel is H⁰(O_C(6−T)); on P¹ this has dimension max(7−τ,0). It follows that rank C_e=min(7,τ).

This argument prevents two possible mistakes. First, nonzero local contributions at several points cannot simply be declared independent without interpolation. Second, higher jets need the perfect local pairing, not a Vandermonde formula for reduced points. Both requirements are satisfied here. The resulting lower bound min(7,k) for k reduced Z points and upper bound min(7,δ) are valid.

## 5. Small gaps and the global bound

For d≥8 the prior classification has no positive-root boundary component, so the exact full rank is min(7,τ).

If δ=0, the actual section is a subbundle on D and has quotient O_D(−2−a plane). Its extension class lies in H¹(O_D(2a+2 plane)), which vanishes: the two reduced layers have degrees 4a and 4a+4. Hence E_D is the direct sum of these actual plane line restrictions, and ε=0. In particular the d=4 branch forces a=δ=0 and has zero boundary. This verifies more than abstract splitting, which alone would not have sufficed in the small gaps.

For d=6, a=0 and δ=1. The original rank interval τ≤rank ε≤τ+1 is correct, but the actual section excludes its lower endpoint. `SUPPLEMENT.md` proves rank ε=τ+1. This is a further global-section constraint, not a modification of the unconstrained ribbon-local classification.

If the independently established bound d≤20 is available, then δ≤8−2a. Therefore a=1 forces δ≤6 and

    rank ε=τ≤δ≤6.

The exclusion of rank seven in that branch is correct. The a=0 branch permits δ≤8; for δ≥2 its rank is min(7,τ), while δ=0 and δ=1 are the small cases above.

## Limits retained by the audit

Neither this argument nor the refinement selects a, δ, ℓ, τ, a zero scheme, or an actual ambient obstruction. A conditional formula for a ribbon boundary is not the ambient detector's evaluation. Any claim that these abstract constraints determine the HM source or ambient Gysin value would need additional information not supplied here.
