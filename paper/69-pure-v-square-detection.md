## 1. Conditional space, unrestricted comparison

Retain the nonzero Q-compatible pure-V source2 hypothesis, not an existence assertion. PR1025 supplies the two-dimensional quotient-third space Vgate=Hom_G(P,H⁰(J(3))), J=I_Z(6), and the corrected-lift identity ιβ₄|D=−sL. The old unrestricted fourth space Q₄ has dimension four, with a two-dimensional kernel K_δ of restriction to the square conic Δ and a two-dimensional square-restriction image. Those old dimensions are inputs, not new conclusions of PR1028.

The new theorem identifies the inherited image S_pureV of Vgate in Q₄ as a complement:

    Q₄=K_δ⊕S_pureV,   dim S_pureV=2.

This is a conditional restriction of the original-fourth gate, not an unconditional reduction of every four-channel fourth problem.

## 2. Restriction retains a nonzero connecting term

The known length-34 scheme Z misses Δ. With δ its quadratic equation, the exact divisor sequence is

    0→J(1)→J(3)→O_Δ(9)→0.

Here plane twist 9 pulls back to O_P¹(18), not O_P¹(9). The inherited resolution yields H⁰(J(1))=D, H¹(J(1))=A and H¹(J(3))=0. Therefore

    0→D→H⁰(J(3))→Sym¹⁸W→A→0.

Restriction on all sections is **not** onto: its cokernel A is nonzero. Taking Hom_G(P,−), however, kills both outer representations and gives an isomorphism onto Hom_G(P,Sym¹⁸W), of dimension two. No nonzero allowable s vanishes along the entire conic.

## 3. Composition supplies the exact complement

The canonical inclusion j:J→coker B is an isomorphism near Δ, since its quotient is coker L, supported off Δ. Also L|Δ is onto. Thus the actual-degree composition

    T(s)=−j s L(-3):D(-4)→coker B

has zero square restriction iff s has zero square restriction, hence iff s=0. The unrestricted square-image space has the same dimension two, so res∘T is an isomorphism. This proves the direct-sum complement, with its right inverse determined by the inherited maps rather than an arbitrary coordinate projection.

In old coefficient coordinates the square-invisible coefficients need not individually vanish; they may be linear functions of the two square coefficients. The statement is transversality of the new subspace, not coincidence with an earlier coordinate plane.

## 4. What detection means

For the actual conditional fourth on surviving D,

    β₄|Δ=0 ⇔ β₄=0 ⇔ s=0 ⇔ R=0.

The restriction is to the **whole family** of square directions and the whole surviving D source. This supplies neither a test at one chosen square or vector nor a claim of nonvanishing at every square. Extra specialized A-kernels are outside the comparison. The L here is source2 multiplication D(-1)→P, not the differently typed lifting correction called L in older fourth-symbol notation.

Both actual coefficients, including zero versus nonzero, remain unselected. The packet reports independent mathematical review and 29 ordinary/optimized checks of characters, twists and cohomology dimensions; the sheaf argument is written mathematics, not formal certification by those tests. No source pair is removed.

The later [PR1033 refinement](74-pure-v-conic-base.md) classifies all conic basepoints and proves that a whole-family zero at a square outside the twelve-orbit forces the gate to vanish. No such actual evaluation is supplied.
