## Residual-large hypothesis is essential

Retain compatible pure-V radical and a genuine separation N=N0⊕NR. Assume the actual N0 projection of P_h is zero but its N8 quotient projection is nonzero. This is the residual-large branch, not every endpoint-sixteen case.

For R=Sym W, the full actual negative module G_h=R·im h has

    (G_h)2=P+D,        (G_h)3=W′+2T,
    (G_h)4=K_R=V+2P+D,
    (G_h)5=NR5=U5=T,
    (G_h)d=0 for d≥6.

The degree-five equality is with the specific residual block and marked terminal sextet supplied by the pinned terminal quotient theorem, not an arbitrary T copy. The length is 9+14+16+6=45, compared with length 32 in the separate endpoint-nine branch. Neither branch is selected or asserted realized.

## Terminal image and actual socle

The actual residual system is preserved by the next negative multiplication, so W F4⊂NR5=T. If this image vanished, all F4 would lie in the ambient joint-negative kernel NC4. But F4∩NC4=I4 has dimension four, whereas F4 has dimension sixteen. Thus W F4 is a nonzero invariant subspace of the irreducible NR5, hence all of it. Ambient H1(B(6))=0 kills further descendants.

The degree-four socle is exactly the actual I4, by intersection with that joint kernel, not all of the two-copy P-isotypical part. Degree five is entirely socle. There is none in degree two, by the injective first steps with disjoint images, or degree three, by the actual ambient Koszul injection. Hence

    Soc_R(G_h)=I4 in degree four ⊕ NR5 in degree five.

This module is not level: the socle occupies two degrees.

## Minimal resolution in original degrees

The exact minimal equivariant free resolution is

    0→P⊗R(-6)⊕T⊗R(-7)
      →S⊗R(-3)⊕V⊗R(-4)⊕(W′+S+T)⊗R(-5)
      →(P+D)⊗R(-2)→G_h→0.

The two-variable Koszul socle calculation fixes Tor2. After shifting generators to degree zero, the character Hilbert numerator is

    (P+D)−S t−V t²−(W′+S+T)t³+P t⁴+T t⁵,

with dimension numerator 9−4t−3t²−12t³+4t⁴+6t⁵. Generation in degree zero and the known absence of lower socle then determine all Tor1 terms. This is not inferred from a dimension numerator alone.
