# The unique square-conic basepoint line

**Craig Ward · 23 October 2026 · PR1033**

[Immutable full proof](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/7065160df56ffa7de1bea7e02c01dd98a6219781/research/hm/source2-pure-v-conic-base/PROOF.md).

The restriction isomorphism of PR1028 and finite-base theorem of PR1030 identify the nonzero pure-V gate with a degree-eighteen binary P system. PR1033 proves that exactly one intrinsic coefficient line ℓ_Δ has square-conic basepoints:

    B_s∩Δ=E₁₂ for nonzero s∈ℓ_Δ,
    B_s∩Δ=∅ for nonzero s∉ℓ_Δ.

These are scheme-theoretic intersections. E₁₂ consists of the twelve reduced order-five fixed squares, not the six plane axes off the conic. The result determines multiplicity one **on the conic**, not ambient transverse thickness.

## The divisor argument

The four binary components span the irreducible P. Their gcd is invariant by perfectness of the binary icosahedral group. The only positive invariant degree at most eighteen is twelve, with a one-dimensional invariant space. Therefore the gcd is constant or a multiple of f₁₂. Its divisor is the reduced twelve-orbit: the minimum nonempty orbit size is twelve, so neither another support nor extra multiplicity fits its degree.

Multiplication by f₁₂ maps the one-dimensional Hom_G(P,Sym⁶W) into the two-dimensional Hom_G(P,Sym¹⁸W). Pullback of this image under square restriction defines the unique intrinsic ℓ_Δ. The residual degree-six P system has no basepoints, since a nonconstant gcd would be an invariant of degree at most six. Thus the component ideal on the conic is exactly (f₁₂) on this line and the unit ideal on every other nonzero member. This is an exact ideal computation, not just support identification.

## Refined detection and its limits

A nonzero allowable gate cannot have a whole-source-family zero at any square outside E₁₂. Consequently such a whole-family zero outside E₁₂ would force s=0. A zero at an order-five fixed square only forces membership in ℓ_Δ, not identical vanishing. Neither assertion is a test on a single source vector, and no actual square evaluation is performed.

For the original fourth T(s)=−j s L, L|Δ is a locally split surjection and j|Δ an isomorphism. Its component ideal on Δ therefore equals that of s. The same dichotomy holds on surviving D. Under this square-channel isomorphism ℓ_Δ corresponds to the older fourth C₅-invisible line; that older line and f₁₂ are not claimed anew.
