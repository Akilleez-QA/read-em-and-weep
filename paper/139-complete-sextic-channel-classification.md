# 139. Complete sextic-channel and coefficient-span classification

**Checkpoint: 23 October 2026 · Universal channel classification after PR1150**

## Contribution and scope

The marked sextic representation Sym6 W has exactly three nonzero equivariant subsystems: V, P and V+P. Their exact evaluation kernels, multiplier thresholds and finite quotients can now be stated together. The apolar application also distinguishes the source system from its active scalar channels. Absence of constant sections in the dual quotient forces the coefficient image to equal the multiplier span, including an exceptional partial system at multiplier degree one.

This classification exhausts these sextic subsystems and channel supports, not actual HM Gauss profiles. It supplies no new actual osculating-containment hypothesis and is not another count of the PR1145 exclusion. Profile (1,1), other-profile exhaustion and the original repair remain open at this checkpoint.

## The complementary four-dimensional system

[PR1150, immutable proof](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/10733b25d00ce015925eac2fa4456439a64e67cf/research/hm/complementary-sextic-syzygies/PROOF.md) treats the complementary P in Sym6 W=V+P. Its evaluation is basepoint-free by the invariant-divisor minimum twelve. Its rank-three evaluation kernel has degree −6 and no sections.

The actual linear multiplier P⊗W→Sym7 W has source and target W′+T. The W′ channel cannot vanish because full sextic multiplication is onto and V⊗W=T supplies no W′. The T channel cannot vanish either: its six-dimensional kernel would exceed the bound three on sections of the twisted evaluation kernel. Thus linear multiplication is an isomorphism, forcing the kernel to be V O(-2):

    0 → V O(-2) → P O → O(6) → 0.

Cohomology gives every higher multiplication kernel. The ideal generated by P has minimal resolution 0→V R(-8)→P R(-6)→R→R/(P)→0 and quotient Hilbert vector (1,2,3,4,5,6,3), of length 24. The linear threshold is proved for multiplication itself, not inferred merely from matching characters.

|---|---|---:|---:|
| V | W′O(-3) | 2 | 27 |

For positive multiplier degree, all systems fill Sym^(d+6) W except V at d=1. That exceptional image is exactly T inside Sym7 W=W′+T.

## Active support is not the full source by default

In the actual apolar setup let d≥1, n=d+6 and suppose a saturated negative bundle F⊂H O has dual quotient S with H0(S)=0. Assume an actual saturated line O(-n) lies in S, with constant-connection osculating containment through order d−1. Write its equivariant bundle type as

    F=F_high ⊕ U O(-d),

where every degree of F_high is greater than −d. This is not a horizontal splitting assumption.

Allow the line's coefficient map Sym^n W→R0 to be partial. Dual injection into the full polynomial dual pulls annihilation back to Sym6 W O(-d). Degree vanishing removes F_high; on U the remaining map a:U→V+P is scalar on each available irreducible. It is nonzero, but when U=V+P one channel may vanish. Define J=im(a) and retain the entire kernel

    B=F_high ⊕ ker(a) O(-d).

Inactive same-degree constituents cannot be discarded from rank or saturation arguments.

The condition H0(S)=0 says precisely that F spans the constant ambient H. After projection to R0*, its fibers are l^d J and still span the full projection. Hence

    R0* = span_l(l^d J) = image(J⊗Sym^d W→Sym^(d+6) W).

This is equality, not only containment. For d≥2 every nonzero support is complete. At d=1, P-active support is complete, but V-only support forces exactly R0*=T, dimension six, with W′ as coefficient kernel. A full eight-dimensional alternative is impossible in that stratum.

## Channel-aware codimension bounds

Put m=dim R0, r=dim J and c=dim H−rank F. The saturated negative B lies in the constant projection kernel. Rank gives c≥m−r; if B is nonzero, saturation and negativity give the strict integer improvement c≥m−r+1. For d≥2 this becomes:

| Active support J | Bound | Bound when B is nonzero |
|---|---:|---:|
| V | c≥n−2 | c≥n−1 |
| P | c≥n−3 | c≥n−2 |
| V+P | c≥n−6 | c≥n−5 |

With full source U=V+P and only one active channel, B is automatically nonzero even when F_high=0. The stronger bound is then mandatory. These necessary bounds are not asserted optimal in every representation stratum.

## Exact degree-one ambient strata

At d=1, negativity forces F_high=0 and n=7. Spanning makes H a quotient of U⊗W. The complete possibilities under the stated hypotheses are:

| Source U | Active support J | Ambient representation H | Codimension c |
|---|---|---|---:|
| V | V | T | 3 |
| P | P | W′+T | 4 |
| V+P | V | 2T or W′+2T | 5 or 7 |
| V+P | P | W′+2T | 7 |
| V+P | V+P | W′+T or W′+2T | 1 or 7 |

The theorem supplies saturated, spanning abstract polynomial models for every row and verifies the required annihilating line, rather than just checking representation dimensions. The twelve-dimensional V-only model uses multiplication on V and directional contraction on P into a separate T. The latter has full fiber rank four: a kernel would be a pure sixth power in P, contradicting basepoint-freeness of the orthogonal V net. This proves the c=5 model.

These are abstract equivariant models, not claimed HM realizations. Their center is odd. A center-even ambient forces n even, so none of these d=1 strata applies to a center-even actual ambient.
