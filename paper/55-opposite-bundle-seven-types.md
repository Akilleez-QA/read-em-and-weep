## 1. Ampleness and the physical degree-one representation

[PR994](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/48727e1f5fd5ab079c21b8b39af608f7d91fabab/research/hm/opposite-ample-exclusion/PROOF.md) proves that the actual rank-five, degree-26 opposite bundle C is ample and that its degree-one part is either absent or W O(1). Exact Koszul multiplication proves simultaneous coefficient surjectivity, not surjectivity of an individual pencil map. A nonsplit Leray filtration, the full global cohomology bound and current monad characters give the degree-one restriction without discarding transgressions.

These conditions reduce the earlier 21 necessary types to nine. Only row9, O(2)⊕O(12)⊕V O(4), permits a nonzero invariant vertex extension, through its unique O(12) summand. The top-polar obstruction has rank one exactly for row9 and rank two for the other survivors. Neither actual row nor vertex scalar is selected.

**Notation warning:** this top-polar σ belongs to the opposite-bundle/vertex problem. It is not the invariant target section σ:O(-4)→E used in the internal octic gate. No identification between them is asserted.

## 2. The seven-row bound and canonical quotient

Exactly rows **9,10,14,15,17,18,20** remain necessary possibilities; none is asserted realized. Only row9 still permits rank-one top-polar σ or a nonsplit invariant vertex extension.

Every surviving row has a unique equivariant quotient line up to scalar, C→O(2). Every nonzero such map is onto, giving a canonical rank-four, degree-24 kernel. The quotient sequence splits equivariantly in rows9,10,14,15; rows17,18,20 retain a nontrivial Euler component. The actual row and vertex scalar δ remain open.

The proof also forces H²(B(-1))^G to be one-dimensional and identifies it through Leray with this quotient line. Its invariant transgression vanishes already because its source has no invariants; that vanishing is not a new nontrivial scalar evaluation.
