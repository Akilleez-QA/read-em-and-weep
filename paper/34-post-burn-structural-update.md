## An independent cone contraction need not extend ambiently

[PR914's pinned proof](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/76a68a1b0c3e4adbcc92a6c501211659d1eb9e22/research/hm/cone-normalization-matching/PROOF.md) strengthens the normalization route following PR908 and PR909. It compares an independently authenticated contraction on the actual cone with the restriction of the ambient contraction, without assuming that the independent cone contraction extends to the ambient space.

For the canonical exact cone source s = a r(V - bZ), the proof derives the comparison directly on the cone, retaining both homotopy terms for a nonclosed degree-zero primitive. If eta is the difference of the two extracted source traces in the same physical frame, regular tensor compatibility gives eta = a xi, with xi in Sym12(U) tensor O(-2).

Regularity is required on a cover of the entire base, including every zero of a. If eta glues, multiplication by the nonzero section a is injective as a sheaf map, so the xi glue. Since O(-2) has no global sections on the projective line, eta must vanish. This is not pointwise division at roots, and a punctured-cover calculation is insufficient.

With the correctly paired ambient scalar response already global and its full ambient lower projection and affine terms held fixed, the result gives a conditional equivalence: the mixed scalar response glues on the entire base if and only if the two trace responses agree on the certified canonical source. In that case the mixed and correctly paired scalar responses are equal.

This removes an ambient-extendability assumption for the independently supplied cone contraction. It does **not** supply that contraction, authenticate its physical marking or frame alignment, recover the full ambient lower projection, or establish the required gluing. Matching on one source also does not imply equality of the entire row operator or weight-four matrix. The unrestricted thirteen-dimensional associated-graded trace space is not erased.
