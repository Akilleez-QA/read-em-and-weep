# No constant reverse c-d-f map survives all quadratic actions

**Craig Ward · 23 October 2026 · Update through PR1011**

[PR1011](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/446dbfd04f6fb689c30975174dcc23d6f4c88c64/research/hm/cdf-quadratic-reverse-map-vanishing/PROOF.md) strengthens the boundary around the one-sided c-d-f cubic argument. Under actual occurrence and Q(X_B)⊂Y_B, there is no nonzero **parameter-independent equivariant endpoint pair** from the quotient complex back to the preserved B complex that intertwines Q and every negative quadratic. This does not classify maps depending on the deformation parameter or quadratic direction, or maps for just one direction.

## Exact reconstruction, with its missing information retained

An endpoint intertwiner of all quadratic products extends uniquely to the middle linear-pencil degree when the source middle is generated by its first arrows and the target second arrows have zero joint kernel. The proof checks relations by applying every target second arrow, then uses the zero joint kernel. Both hypotheses are essential, and mixed products are recovered by polarization, not by checking only two squares.

For the actual F→B blocks, source generation is the universal multiplication W⊗Sym¹⁶W→Sym¹⁷W. The target joint kernel is H⁰(E_B(1))=H⁰((V+P)O(-3))=0. Every such endpoint map therefore extends to the full pencil, where the authenticated algebra has no F→B block. It must vanish.

The target-only L block is different: its degree-two source is zero. Thus the negative-quadratic reverse Hom space is exactly Hom_G(Y_L,Y_B), of dimension three, with zero source map and zero restriction to Y_F. Every nonzero member fails full-linear extension. This explicitly identifies the information lost by omitting the middle degree; unrestricted even-data reconstruction would be false.

## Actual Q generation kills the three remaining maps

The same actual generation theorem gives Y=im Q+im q₋. After quotienting by Y_B, one-sided preservation removes all B-source contributions. A reverse map intertwining Q kills im Q_T and already kills every negative-quadratic image. These generate the quotient target, so the reverse map is zero.

This eliminates the constant reverse-chain-map mechanism that might have supplied a nonzero D→D cohomology action and hence κ-naturality. It does not prove that every other geometric route to rκ=0 is impossible. The [raw three-dimensional cubic gate](59-cdf-preserved-b-cubic.md), its unknown actual value, and the unproved one-sided hypothesis remain unchanged. No source or pair is removed.
