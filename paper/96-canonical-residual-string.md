## Actual location of the canonical string

Under compatible pure-V radical, fix the genuine separation N=N0⊕NR and exact negative system 0→NC→NR→N8→0. The marked canonical string U=(A_can,S_can,D_can) lies in NR and projects injectively to N8 at all three vertices. Its quartic projects isomorphically to the unique D8⊂N8,4=V8+P8+D8, so D_can∩NC4=0 in both endpoint branches.

The first inclusion follows because N0,2 has no A; negative stability then retains the string in NR. Since NC2 also has no A, its initial quotient image is the unique A in A⊗Sym⁴W. In separate coefficient variables X,Y and polynomial variables x,y, that inclusion is

    e(f)=(Xy−Yx)²f(x,y),      f∈Sym²W.

For the exact two-transvectant quotient C:A⊗Sym⁶W→Sym⁶W⊕Sym⁴W, the whole polynomial identity is C(b e(f))=(0,84bf). Products bf span all quartics, proving the D8 image; nonvanishing of its descendants also proves the middle injection. The scalar 84 is a universal model normalization, not an evaluated actual coefficient.

Writing U8=π(U), there is an actual negative-module splitting

    π⁻¹(U8)=NC⊕U.

It is restricted to this inverse image, of dimension triple (20,22,14), not a splitting of the full extension NR over N8. No Q-stability is asserted.

## Residual homotopy kernel

The radical kills NR4, so NR4⊂im Q. The marked quartic in NR4 maps isomorphically under h to I2. Thus, with K_R=ker h∩NR4,

    h(NR4)=I2,       dim K_R=16,
    NR4=K_R⊕D_can.

These are actual endpoint subspaces of dimensions 21=16+5. I2, D_can and D8 are distinct embedded objects, in different degrees or quotient spaces.

## Only under zero N0 projection

Assume specifically the actual N0 projection of P_h is zero. Then F lies in NR, and two cases remain:

- If P_h⊂NC2, then F4=NC4, h(NC4)=0, and K_R=π4⁻¹(V8+P8), giving 0→NC4→K_R→V8+P8→0.
- If π2(P_h)≠0, then the endpoint has dimension sixteen. Its surjection to N8,4 has kernel I4; since F4⊂K_R and both have dimension sixteen, F4=K_R. Consequently h induces an isomorphism NC4/I4→I2 and 0→I4→F4→N8,4→0.

Thus under zero N0 projection, h|NC4 is zero exactly in the nine-dimensional case and has rank five in the residual-large case. The nonzero-N0 branch is not assigned a value of h|NC4 or identified with K_R.

No branch, scalar or source pair is selected. The theorem proves no new Bockstein vanishing, no full residual splitting and no degree-five descendant statement. Universal polynomial checks support the written transport through actual maps.
