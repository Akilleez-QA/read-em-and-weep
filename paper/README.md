The remaining (1,2) profile now has ten necessary quotient pairs, 38 endpoint records and 52 inherited pair/E records, refining the initial 25 pairs and 155 records with original defect zero; the independent bound a≥−4 applies to every maximal rank-three profile. Further endpoint/orbit constraints exclude the distinct second-dual length twenty admitted by the initial table. Original source recovery, higher lifting and algebraicity remain open.
