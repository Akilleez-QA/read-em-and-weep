## 1. Collective images, not common annihilators

These are not inferred by identifying a common kernel with a collective image. The retained geometric nonzero target D-image and upper bound two on source multiplicity force exactly two and one.

## 2. A permanent obstruction to surjectivity

The endpoint spaces have dimensions 41 and 37. Modulo collective images they have dimensions **27 and 28**. Every J-linear map induces a map between these quotients. Surjectivity upstairs would imply an impossible surjection from dimension 27 to 28. Thus every J-linear map has rank at most 36, without requiring G-equivariance and after any extension of the scalar field.

Both Q, by hypothesis, and the actual negative quadratic maps commute with J. Therefore the bound applies to the full family Q+tT_a, including over the generic Laurent-series field. This is not a selected-instance rank computation.

At Q there are 33 invertible pairs. The already established original first vanishing and generic original-second rank three produce three further pivots of valuation two. They give rank at least 36, matching the new upper bound. Consequently the original generic rank is **exactly 36**, with kernel dimension five and cokernel dimension one.

Over the generic direction's power-series ring K[[t]], the remaining 1×5 block must be identically zero: any nonzero entry would raise Laurent-series rank to 37. The Smith data are 33 unit pivots, three t² pivots and a zero residual block. The cokernel is

Thus **all original generic Bocksteins after the second vanish**. The original third vanishing was already known and is not counted as new. The fourth and all later generic vanishings are new consequences here. Exceptional directions with additional second-page kernel classes are not computed.

## 4. The internal cubic gate is zero

The quotient complex T is generically onto over the Laurent-series field because its first symbol has rank four. If PR982's internal cubic r were nonzero, that theorem would make it generically onto the rank-two internal target E. Along with the internal first rank three and internal second zero, S would then be generically onto as well. Exactness of 0→S→M→T→0 would make M onto, contradicting the permanent rank bound. Therefore **r=0 globally** under the full-radical hypotheses.

This selects the formerly unselected zero alternative in the two-dimensional cubic gate. PR984's ν=0 or r=0 remains valid but no longer restricts ν once r=0 is known.
