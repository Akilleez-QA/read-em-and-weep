## Actual marked subspaces

Retain the compatible pure-V radical, its canonical homotopy h:N4→N2 and I2=im j2. Let D_can⊂N4 be the actual canonical quartic from the degree-one negative string, not an arbitrary D copy. Then

    h|D_can:D_can→I2 is an isomorphism,
    ker h⊂im Q,
    im Q=ker h⊕D_can.

The dimensions are 33=28+5. The entire endpoint F4 generated from im h lies in ker h and is therefore disjoint from this specific D_can. This is an actual embedding comparison between the two constructions, not a character match.

## Why the secondary channel identifies the marked quartic

The canonical string's U2 is the unique A in N2=A+2V+3P+4D. It is therefore precisely the A in ker Q=A+D on which the actual secondary channel is nonzero. For coherent string embeddings f2 and f4 and an identification i_D:Sym⁴W→I2,

    T_a f2(x)=f4(ax),
    h T_a f2(x)=μ i_D(ax),       μ≠0.

The second identity is literal in N2: H0=ker Q has no boundaries. Ordinary binary multiplication Sym²W⊗Sym²W→Sym⁴W is onto, so h f4=μ i_D. This proves the isomorphism without evaluating μ. The original first-symbol vanishing also puts the span of T_a U2, namely D_can, in im Q.

For y∈ker h, j4y=Qhy=0 and ker j4=im Q, proving ker h⊂im Q. Moreover h(im Q)=im(hQ)=I2. The restriction h thus gives an exact sequence with kernel ker h, split by the inverse r=(h|D_can)⁻¹. Its intrinsic projector is

    π_can=r h|im Q.

It has image D_can and kernel ker h. Rescaling the radical and h rescales r inversely, leaving this projector unchanged. It is a projector on im Q⊂N4 only, not a horizontal or Q-stable decomposition of the full graded module.

## Generated endpoint and limits

The sandwich identity hT_a h=0 gives F4⊂ker h and π_can(F4)=0. Any D channel in F4 is therefore disjoint from the marked canonical quartic. This does not choose its position within the remaining two-dimensional D multiplicity space.

The necessary endpoint dimensions remain 9 or 16, neither selected. The theorem chooses no gate coefficient, excludes no source pair and supplies no degree-five descendant or terminal-string conclusion. In particular h is an endpoint homotopy, not a graded-module homomorphism for subsequent arrows. No cross-direction commutation is used.

The proof imports the actual string and nonzero secondary channel, then proves the comparison. Universal multiplication-span, symbolic projector and character checks support that argument without replacing the actual-map premises by synthetic normal forms.
