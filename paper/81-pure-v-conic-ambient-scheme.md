The conic line is off both prescribed-axis jet lines, so its sixteen-axis ideals equal Z. The fifteen-axis orbit occurs only on α+β=0 and is absent here. All thirty-point orbits are excluded by PR1042, and the prior support theorem exhausts every other possibility. Thus the listed local ideals determine the entire scheme, with no remaining thickness or support ambiguity on this line.
