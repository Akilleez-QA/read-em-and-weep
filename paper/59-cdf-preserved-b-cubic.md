## 1. Actual interfaces permit a global quadratic reduction

The [first-symbol theorem](57-cdf-preserved-b-interface.md) supplies the actual cohomology interfaces and connecting isomorphism: the preserved complex has kernel A+D and cokernel D, while its quotient has kernel D and cokernel P. Its internal first is multiplication on A and zero on D. On the parameter plane the resulting rank-two bundle is

    0→A O(-1)→D O→E→0.

An equivariant right inverse of restricted Q gives polynomial first corrections of every D cycle. Their quadratic residual modulo aA defines a global map s:D O(-2)→E, independent of the choices. The actual original-third comparison is −b̄_T δ⁻¹s. Imported original-third vanishing forces its image into the generic rank-one kernel of b̄_T. But every nonzero equivariant quadratic map into E has generic rank two, by the universal saturated-line exclusion. Hence s=0 globally. This is a new internal conclusion for this conditional c-d-f interface, not a new proof of original-third vanishing.

## 2. The cubic is canonical and compares with the actual fourth

The zero quadratic residual factors uniquely through the multiplication subbundle, so its correction can be normalized globally without dividing by any selected parameter coordinate. A second polynomial correction then defines r:D O(-3)→E. Changes of normalized corrections contribute only the already-zero internal quadratic or the first-symbol image. Thus r is canonical and global.

Its ambient equivariant Hom space has dimension three, and every nonzero member is generically onto E. Using the same corrections in the original complex gives, in the actual fourth-page target,

    ηβ₄=−b̄_T δ⁻¹r.

The quotient and connecting higher terms vanish after normalization because they contain the already-zero first or second restricted symbols on D. Since b̄_T has generic rank one, the universal exclusion of rank-one cubic maps proves r≠0 if and only if the generic original fourth is nonzero. It computes neither alternative, nor additional exceptional-direction classes.

## 3. Why this is not the two-dimensional source126 gate

The source126 restriction rκ=0 required a reverse chain map supplied by compatible radical action. A one-sided preserved subcomplex alone supplies no such map. In fact the authenticated full negative-linear-pencil algebra admits no nonzero reverse map from its quotient to the B vertex: that vertex is isolated from its sole radical arrow. This is a statement about that pencil category, not an exclusion of every possible quadratic-family map or future geometric relation.

Accordingly no κ restriction, secondary scalar ν, or decrement from three parameters to two is imported. Nor are all three raw coefficients claimed to be realizable. The one-sided preservation hypothesis and actual cubic value remain open, with four necessary pairs and three labels unchanged.
