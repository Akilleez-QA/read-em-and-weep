# Exactly fifteen additional reduced axes on the rank-three line

**Craig Ward · 23 October 2026 · PR1040**

[Immutable full proof](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/c8df54084115e242fd39457e95da187cb9952168/research/hm/source2-pure-v-fifteen-axis/PROOF.md).

In the pinned nonic pencil F=αF₁+βF₂, the unique fifteen-point order-two axis orbit occurs exactly when α+β=0. Every one of its ambient local ideals is the maximal ideal. Combined with prior support exclusions and the distinct coefficient lines, the complete scheme there is Z disjoint-union E₁₅, of length 49. No other nonzero member has any point of E₁₅, so the earlier possible curvilinear double-axis case never occurs in this pencil.

## Intrinsic whole-orbit construction

The invariant sextic f₆=c_P^T G_P c_P and discriminant δ define a homogeneous critical scheme by the minors of (df₆,dδ). It is the zero scheme of a section of T_P²(4). Exact chart calculations prove finiteness, and its Chern length is 31. Stabilizer cotangent representations force all six order-five, ten order-three and fifteen order-two axes into it. These 31 distinct points exhaust that length, so the critical scheme is reduced.

An invariant sextic cut identifies the fifteen-axis projective ideal sheaf. The affine chart p=1 contains fourteen of its points; its remaining point is accounted for at the projective boundary and transported by equivariance. This is not an identification of the raw homogeneous ideal with its saturation or an extrapolation from a sampled axis.

## Coefficient and reducedness certificates

Modulo the whole affine fourteen-point ideal I₁₅, the complete row remainders satisfy rem(F₁)=rem(F₂)≠0. Hence the entire orbit occurs exactly on α+β=0. The further exact ideal equality

    (F₁−F₂)+I₁₅²=I₁₅

localizes to J+m²=m. Nakayama gives J=m, and equivariance includes the omitted fifteenth point. These are ambient plane maximal ideals, not merely reduced intersections with a curve.

The rank-three coefficient line lies off both jet lines and the conic line. Its prescribed-axis ideals therefore equal Z, it has no conic points, and the support theorem excludes any thirty-orbit alongside E₁₅. Its whole base scheme is consequently determined. The basepoint conclusion is not inferred from lift rank alone.

All calculations use symbolic directions and universal coefficients. The result does not select the actual gate, establish source compatibility or determine original-fourth local component ideals. Its scheme is one input to the [complete classification](82-pure-v-complete-nonic-classification.md).
