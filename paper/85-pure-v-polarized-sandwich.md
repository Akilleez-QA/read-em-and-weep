## Statement under actual compatibility

Assume a nonzero pure-V source2 radical is Q-compatible, and let h:N4→N2 be the canonical homotopy. For negative quadratics a,b, the literal actual-map identities are

    h T_a h=0,
    K_a K_b=0,       K_a=(hT_a,T_a h).

These strengthen square-zero action on Q-cohomology to products of actual chain endomorphisms. Their span is an injectively parametrized three-dimensional square-zero space A; with the identity it forms the G-stable Q-chain algebra C⊕A with zero radical product. This is not asserted to be an algebra of endomorphisms of the full fixed negative pencil.

## Why the raw second differential matters

The sandwich H_a=hT_a h kills im Q and lands in im h∩ker Q=D. It is therefore a linear bundle map P O(-1)→D O. The quotient cycles represented by hy identify exactly the canonical P summand in H0(U), with no A mixing. A correction Qz=−T_a hy yields

    f1 β₂,U([hy])=j4 T_a z=−L_a H_a y.

The pinned quotient second differential vanishes on P in raw H1(U), not only modulo the A second image. Consequently H lands in the actual sheaf kernel of D O→P O(1), namely O(-4). The group Hom(P O(-1),O(-4))=P*⊗H0(O(-3)) is zero, proving the sandwich identity. No rank specialization or coefficient choice enters this argument.

## Middle degree: all factors remain in W

Let X_l:N2→N3 and Y_l:N3→N4 denote actual negative linear multiplication, with l in the fixed two-dimensional negative space W. For l,m,r,s all in this same W, put S_lm=X_l hY_m. Since Y_m X_r=T_mr with mr∈Sym²W=A,

    S_lm S_rs=X_l(h T_mr h)Y_s=0.

This retains mixed directions and the full middle degree. It includes no positive or arbitrary ambient linear forms, and makes no assertion that an individual S_lm extends to a fixed-pencil endomorphism.

## Cross-direction defect remains

The alternating defect C_ab=T_b hT_a−T_a hT_b is not proved zero. For D_b(t)=Q+tT_b, the chain defect of K_a is t C_ab. Actual multiplication shows that C_ab kills ker Q; the sandwich identity shows that it kills im h and lands in ker h. Its image also lies in im Q. Thus it canonically factors through

    N2/(ker Q+im h) → ker h∩im Q,
    2V+2P+3D       → 2V+3P+2D,
    dimension 29  → dimension 28.

A synthetic commuting-arrow control has nonzero defect a0 b1−a1 b0 despite the sandwich identities. It is not an actual source realization; it prevents a purely formal inference of cross-direction commutation. No scalar equation on the two actual gate coefficients, further submodule implication, source exclusion or original-proof repair follows here.

The proof uses the pinned homotopy, raw quotient second vanishing, genuine kernel sheaf and actual negative multiplication. Symbolic type, character, twist and control checks support, rather than replace, that argument.
