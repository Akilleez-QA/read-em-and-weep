## Hypothesis and exact homotopy

Assume the pinned actual source2 hypotheses and a nonzero pure-V negative-pencil radical j compatible with Q. Existence of this compatible radical is not established. Its cohomology action vanishes, and Hom_G(P,A+D)=0 makes the equivariant nullhomotopy h unique:

    hQ=j₂,  Qh=j₄,  j₂h=hj₄=0.

The embedded equalities im j₂=D, ker j₄=im Q and im j₄=P give the actual sequence 0→D→im h→P→0. Thus h has rank nine, not an instance-evaluated rank.

For a fixed negative quadratic a, K_a=(hT_a,T_ah) is a chain endomorphism of the original Q+tT_a. This is a **same-direction** assertion; no commutation with T_b for unrelated b is claimed. On H⁰=A+D it acts as (x,d)↦(0,μax), and on H¹=P it is zero. Its D action vanishes identically.

The actual endpoint j₄ induces ι:coker Q→im j₄. The restricted image map is L_a:D→P, d↦[ad] in Sym⁶W/V₆. For an original first correction Qu=−T_ax, exact identities give

    ιβ₂,a(x)=−L_a K_a⁰(x)=−μ[a²x].

The imported original-second nonvanishing forces μ≠0. No numerical value or normalization of μ is selected. Since multiplication by every nonzero quadratic is injective on A=Sym²W, K_a⁰ has rank three and kernel D for every nonzero a, including exceptional original-second directions. Those second-symbol rank drops arise only after applying L_a.
