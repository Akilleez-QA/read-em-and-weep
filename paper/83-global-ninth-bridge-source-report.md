## 1. Canonical finite/global sequences

Let C be the actual rank 5, degree 26 opposite cohomology bundle, H₉=(ker Θ)*O(8), and Jg the actual positive global B(2) cohomology bundle. The theorem gives

    0→Jg→ker Θ→C*O(8)→0,
    0→C→H₉→Jg*O(8)→0.

Jg is globally generated, of rank 13 and degree 28, and has its actual Steiner presentation

    0→H¹(B(1))₋O(-1)→H¹(B(2))₊O→Jg→0,

with coefficient dimensions 28 and 41. The second bridge is independently derived from geometric restriction and then identified with the dual twist of the first. Neither sequence is asserted split.

## 2. Why ordinary bundle duality and parity apply

Every hyperplane has three global B(3) square sections. A nonzero B(2) section would inject its four-dimensional linear-form space into those three sections, impossible. Thus global B_H(2) H⁰ vanishes uniformly. The pinned H²/H³ tails and stability give the remaining H²,H³ vanishings on every fiber, including exceptional directions. Cohomology and base change therefore justify actual vector bundles and ordinary duals, not just generic derived objects.

Relative threefold duality on the ninth restriction gives twist O(8). The odd canonical O(1) character and odd normal O(9) character cancel, so positive pairs with positive. Full-sheaf duality is applied before eigenspaces are extracted. The actual incidence sequence, with the negative tautological equation, supplies the displayed Steiner map and its parity. No arbitrary split replacement of the geometry is made.

## 3. The rank 13 bundles cannot be canceled

The earlier finite splitting selects S₅=AO(4)⊕WO(5) in ker Θ, with a complement K₁₃ of rank 13 and degree 20. The global Jg has rank 13 but degree 28, so it is **not** that residual bundle.

The actual map S₅→C*O(8) compares rank 5 bundles of degrees 22 and 14. Its determinant has degree −8 and vanishes identically. Hence S₅ has a nonzero subbundle intersection with Jg. Equivalently the determinant of Jg→K₁₃ also has degree −8, so projection cannot be a generic isomorphism. This is a genuine compatibility constraint, not permission to cancel equal-rank terms.

The result does not identify which individual A or W map vanishes or determine the precise intersection rank. C is not all of H₉; the quotient Jg*O(8) has rank 13 and degree 76, giving 102=26+76. The selected finite residual instead has degree 20 in ker Θ.
