## Actual projection criterion

Retain the conditional compatible pure-V radical and the actual module F generated by im h. Fix any genuine horizontal separation N=N0⊕NR, with projectors commuting with both negative arrows. If e2:P_h→N0,2 is nonzero, then

    F4/I4=V+P+D,       dim F4=16,
    F≅I⊕Free_≤2(P_h)

as fixed-variable negative three-space modules. Here I=(D,T,P) and the free summand has spaces P_h, W⊗P_h, Sym²W⊗P_h with ordinary polynomial arrows. The splitting is **not Q-stable**: Q kills D and maps P_h isomorphically across to I4. No cancellation or Bockstein equivalence follows.

## Universal quotient certificate

The actual separated block has spaces Sym⁸W, Sym⁸W⊗W and (Sym⁸W⊗Sym²W)/Sym¹⁰W, with the last inclusion given by complete polarization. Multiplication preserves the fixed variables. The unique P constituent P8⊂Sym⁸W has an injective universal quadratic map

    Sym²W⊗P8→(Sym²W⊗Sym⁸W)/Sym¹⁰W.

The proof constructs first and second transvectants onto Sym⁸W⊕Sym⁶W. Their combined matrix C has rank 16, kills the polarization matrix Δ, and multiplication composed with Δ is 45 Id. Thus ker C is precisely the eleven-dimensional polarization image, not just an isomorphic representation.

Using the pinned P8 projection and its apolar dual inclusion E8, the first twelve rows of C diag(E8,E8,E8) have determinant −1825478130618637221888√5/5, which is nonzero. This proves rank twelve in a universal representation calculation, not an evaluated rank of an actual source or quadratic direction.

## Transport and scope

A nonzero e2 on P_h is an isomorphism onto P8 by equivariance and multiplicity one. Actual projector naturality gives e4 T_a=T_a^0 e2, so the quadratic descendants inject into N0,4. Since I4 lies in the residual block and is killed by e4, those descendants are disjoint from I4. The already established surjection Sym²W⊗P_h→F4/I4 is therefore an isomorphism. Together with the exact first two generated spaces, this proves the negative-module splitting.

The contrapositive says dim F4<16 forces im h⊂NR2 for the chosen genuine separation. PR1058 alone did not determine the zero-projection case; [PR1059](92-pure-v-endpoint-dichotomy.md) now does.
