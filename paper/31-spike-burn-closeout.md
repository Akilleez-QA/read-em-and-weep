## 4. The construction chain is now much better specified

The new work established a useful order of operations rather than a single giant solve:

1. **Source membership before normalization.** For a prescribed full cubic W-family, canonical correction leaves a sharp27-dimensional compatibility sector. A small trace test cannot certify that sector. Allowing W to be constructed is a different problem from validating an arbitrary supplied W.
2. **Normalization pieces must be matched.** The lower projection and source row have coupled contraction-change defects. Mixing independently normalized pieces produces a precise error. Generic overlap agreement is insufficient in the abstract coefficient space.
3. **Symmetry helps only after authentication.** A coherent equivariant cubic-a/linear-h family has ten parameters, not thirteen arbitrary traces. Complete h-family observations can separate it; one scalar/source evaluation cannot.
4. **Compatible W choices do not tune the target.** Actual cone geometry kills all seven compatible W-family directions in the proved response. Norm invariance stops on4Q, not at the next normal order.
5. **Cone-first is a legitimate construction route.** The full global cone complex has the required exactness. Solve the canonical source there, lift, and recover W. The actual source row and ambient lower projection still need to be furnished and matched.
6. **A stronger realized regularity gate can remove calibration.** PR909 proves that full-divisor regularity and all-twist compatibility force a globally glued realized defect to vanish. It does not supply those hypotheses or erase the unrestricted abstract trace space.

The complete arguments, domains, diagnostic boundaries and dependency corrections are explained in [the physical-construction report](33-burn-physical-construction.md). In particular, there is no permission to use a response defined on exact sources to prove that an unknown input is exact.

## 6. Failed approaches and limits worth preserving

- Full-module Euler rigidity prevents the proper truncated projector/radical extension. That is a failed shortcut to source2 exclusion, not a contradiction eliminating source2.
- Abstract feasibility can survive all the currently imposed structural conditions; actual-source identification is indispensable in those branches.
- Global coefficient gluing, actual cone adjoint lifting and physical normalization are different statements. The thirteen-dimensional abstract coefficient space is not a space of actual compatible source variations.
- A successful small import, smoke or primitive unit does not complete a larger job. Missing terminal receipts and partial logs were corrected rather than promoted.
- Sampled RSS is not a hard address-space cap; importing checks are not an independent kernel; declared theorem counts are not proof-completion scores.
- The stopped minor-chart investigation produced no checked new contraction or marking certificate. Its unverified outline is not a result.

The cause of the final stall is not established by the inspected published evidence. This report does not infer it from silence or conflate a tool/model failure with a stopped mathematical process. Aggregate cost, total CPU, utilization and energy are unknown without a complete execution ledger.
