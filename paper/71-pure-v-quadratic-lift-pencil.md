## 1. A universal geometric basis

The unique quadratic lift of PR1025 belongs, after a common twist, to H=Hom_G(P O(-2),D O) on X=P(A), of dimension two. Use the fixed invariant **complex bilinear** apolar pairings, not Hermitian adjoints. For multiplication/projection L_a:D→P and Hamiltonian action ρ_n(a)f=a_xf_y−a_yf_x, the two families

    R₁=ρ_D L*,   R₂=L*ρ_P

form a basis of H. The projected ρ_P is the action on the invariant P summand of Sym⁶W. These are universal polynomial representation matrices, not actual HM endpoint evaluations.

## 2. Exact coefficient-rank ideal

For R=αR₁+βR₂, collect every direction coefficient of every 4×4 minor. PR1031 proves that their ideal is exactly

    (α+β)(α,β)³.

After division by α+β, the residual coefficients span the entire binary-cubic space. Projective saturation is therefore the **reduced** ideal (α+β), including the coefficient point at infinity. Thus every nonzero member has generic rank 4 except the unique projective member R₋=R₁−R₂. The affine cubic factor at the coefficient origin must not be mistaken for additional projective rank-drop members.

The exceptional line is intrinsic; its equation uses the displayed normalization. This coefficient-rank line is not identified with either intrinsic nonic jet line from PR1030.

## 3. Exceptional factorization and all direction ranks

Let E:P→Sym⁶W be the orthogonal equivariant inclusion and pr_V=1−EΠ. Infinitesimal equivariance of multiplication and bilinear skew-adjointness give

    R₋=M_a* pr_V ρ₆(a) E=J_aH_a,
    P→V₆→D.

Both factors are linear in a. J:V₆ O(-1)→D O is everywhere a rank-three subbundle injection, established by exact unit-ideal certificates on all three symbolic projective charts. H has signed maximal cofactors equal to a fixed nonzero multiple of the old cubic P-covariant Π(a³). Its maximal-minor scheme is consequently exactly the six **reduced** order-five axes.

Since J is a subbundle injection, R₋ has rank 3 off those six points and rank 2 at them, with the same scheme-theoretic rank-drop locus. This is not the ten-point L locus or the length-34 third-symbol target scheme. The old cubic's base scheme is reused; its identification with this lift's degeneracy scheme is the new consequence.

## 4. Kernel sheaf versus specialized kernel

The kernel sheaf of R₋:P O(-2)→D O is O(-5), generated by the cubic covariant. Off the six axes its value spans the one-dimensional pointwise kernel. At an axis the cofactor vector specializes to zero, while the specialized matrix has a two-dimensional kernel. These are not contradictory and must not be identified: taking a sheaf kernel does not commute with this specialization.

## 5. Conditional meaning

For an actual compatible pure-V source, its unique R could be zero, a nonzero exceptional member, or another member. None of these alternatives is selected. In particular **rank of R is not rank of the original fourth quotient map**: the composition −sL and quotient by im B are still required. Nor does generic rank 4 of another lift classify all of its individual rank-drop directions.
