## 1. The former order-four/order-six alternative is selected

[PR967](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/39c5f4fbc012f4ec57ffc56d5de18cedb3571d60/research/hm/fourth-normal-obstruction/PROOF.md) reports that the unique invariant B(4) section on the ordinary third neighborhood 3R extends uniquely to 4R and fails at the next step, **from 4R to 5R, normal order four**. Thus its invariant section space is one-dimensional on 3R and 4R and zero on every mR with m≥5. The former PR955 order-four-or-six alternative is superseded. In its polar notation α is an isomorphism and the lower β has zero target. This does not select the top σ, opposite-bundle row or vertex scalar. The proof argues via actual plane multiplication and a negative-degree first-cup kernel, not a chosen coefficient evaluation.

## 2. Secondary quotient and the nontrivial extension it does not split

[PR968](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/148e2eed1fbf50729941365c8fd3ae56a115fe62/research/hm/secondary-quotient-splitting/PROOF.md) identifies the actual rank-six quotient Q6=K/(A O(-6)) as P O(2) ⊕ W O(-1), with a unique equivariant complement to its intrinsic positive Harder–Narasimhan subbundle. Scalar identifications remain arbitrary. The original K extension is nonsplit over P O(2); quotient splitting is not K splitting.

[PR971](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/f96962f2219e096821c3dec50712cc0108abea8f/research/hm/forced-secondary-w-extension/PROOF.md) also forces the W extension component nonzero and identifies its actual preimage as D O(-4). It obtains the nonsplit sequence 0→D O(-4)→K→P O(2)→0. Its residual equivariant extension space has dimension two, and the actual class has nonzero V connecting component. The argument retains the actual extension and its maps; no projective extension direction or secondary-boundary rank is selected.

## 3. A different twist fails at order two in every hyperplane

[PR974](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/5ce277d0476224f9f3fdbc79d1750210a2169708/research/hm/uniform-third-neighborhood/PROOF.md) reports H⁰(mR_λ,B|H_λ(2))=0 for every negative hyperplane direction λ and m≥3. Here mR_λ is the ordinary thickening inside H_λ, not the ambient thickening in P⁴. All three plane sections fail **from 2R_λ to 3R_λ, normal order two**. This is B|H_λ(2), not the invariant B(4) candidate of section1; the normal orders must not be equated.

The third-neighborhood positive H¹ bundle is V O(2) ⊕ P O(2) ⊕ W O(5), and the negative part is (A+V)O(1). The source further constructs an actual rank-30 to rank-15 even cohomology gate Θ through the ninth neighborhood, with both sides of degree 120. Its rank remains unknown; splitting some cohomology bundles does not split the underlying formal bundle.

## 4. Two first-cup kernel types, still no actual selection

[PR975](https://github.com/Akilleez-QA/read-em-and-weep/blob/8170fe31a16b2028052973157dc79a06d0d77f64/research/hm/first-cup-kernel-two-types/PROOF.md) proves a nonzero P component of the residual connecting map, in addition to its known nonzero V component. It leaves exactly two equivariant possibilities for the actual rank-nine, degree-minus-twelve first-cup kernel:

- K=Sym⁵W⊗O(-1) ⊕ A⊗O(-2), when the remaining D component is nonzero;
- K=D⊗O ⊕ Sym³W⊗O(-3), when that component vanishes.

The actual D component is not selected and both types are not claimed realized. In the second case the constant D subbundle is canonical as the evaluation image, but its complement is not declared canonical. The exact square-subbundle and extension maps remain essential; an isomorphism type for K does not replace them with arbitrary split representatives.
