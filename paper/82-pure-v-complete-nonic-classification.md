## The completed question

For F=αF₁+βF₂, define its base scheme by the component **ideal sheaf**, equivalently the saturated homogeneous ideal, not the raw four-generated graded ideal. Let Z have six length-four order-five ideals (u²,v²) and ten reduced order-three axes. Let E₁₅ be the fifteen reduced order-two axes and E₁₂ the twelve reduced conic points.

Every nonzero coefficient pair belongs to exactly one of these five disjoint strata:

| Condition | Entire component scheme | Length |
|---|---|---:|
| β=−2α | Six Z ideals; m² at ten order-three axes | 54 |
| β=−α | Z disjoint-union E₁₅ | 49 |
| β=0 | Z disjoint-union E₁₂ | 46 |
| β=2α | m³ at six order-five axes; m at ten order-three axes | 46 |
| β(β+α)(β−2α)(β+2α)≠0 | Z | 34 |

The last stratum includes [α:β]=[0:1]. There are no extra points, embedded thicknesses, divisorial components or unlisted local ideals in these nonzero schemes. The two length 46 entries have different support and ideals; length alone is not the classification.

## Why the final stratum is exact everywhere

The four homogeneous coefficient forms are pairwise independent, so their nonzero kernels are four distinct projective points. The prior theorems settle each special scheme completely. Every pair in the complement lies off both jet lines, giving exact Z ideals at its sixteen axes; off β=0, excluding conic points; and off β=−α, excluding the fifteen-axis orbit. The whole-pencil thirty-orbit theorem and the exhaustive earlier support theorem exclude every other point.

Equality of local ideals on Z and the unit ideal elsewhere imply equality of ideal sheaves globally. Thus B(F)=Z for **every** pair in this open stratum, not merely a generic pair. No additional exceptional ratio is left inside it.

The special cases were established in [PR1037](78-pure-v-exact-jet-lengths.md), [PR1040](79-pure-v-fifteen-axis-scheme.md) and [PR1043](81-pure-v-conic-ambient-scheme.md), with the [PR1042 exclusion](80-pure-v-no-thirty-orbit.md). PR1044's new completion is the exhaustive assembly and exact open-stratum conclusion, not a second claim of those earlier local computations.
