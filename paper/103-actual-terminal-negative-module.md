## Why the next actual arrows vanish

The actual full Koszul injection M3→W⊗M4 restricts to NC3→W⊗NC4 because NC is a subpencil for both original negative arrows. This restriction is injective and both spaces have dimension eighteen, hence it is onto. Exactness makes subsequent negative multiplication kill all NC4 in M5.

The source also retains the independent bundle authentication: the actual kernel subbundle E_C=(P+D)O(-2) equals the evaluation image of H0(E(2))⊗O(-2), since E/E_C=W O(-9)⊕T O(-3) has no sections after twisting by two. Connecting-map naturality identifies H1(E_C) with the same embedded NC4 and joint-negative kernel. Equal characters alone would not establish that identification.

Since F4=NC4 in the nine branch, every subsequent negative descendant vanishes by associativity. Identity on the first three spaces therefore identifies F∞, shifted by two, with the prior truncated module including all negative arrows. Its resolution and absence of lower socle transfer accordingly.

## Exact annihilator, not freeness or positive stability

The annihilator in R is exactly m³, where m=(W). Cubics kill the module by terminality. There are no linear or quadratic annihilators because W and Sym²W=A are irreducible, and their actual actions are nonzero as they generate the next pieces. Grading separates any possible inhomogeneous annihilator into those homogeneous tests. Thus F∞ is faithful over R/m³; it is not asserted free over that quotient.

This is not the full Rao module, not a claim of positive-coordinate stability, and not a statement about annihilators involving positive variables. The separate canonical W-generated string can continue to degree five while this im h-generated module terminates in degree four. The endpoint branch and nonzero graph scalar remain unselected by this result. No new positive actions or repeated-D identifications are constructed.
